## ⭐ THE ANOMALY LEDGER (ranked)

**1. THE MICROSOFT SUBSIDY PASS-THROUGH — Contract #7405 "Contingency and Development Agreement, Microsoft Datacenter: 2021 Expansion," Mod #1 executed Feb 25, 2022 (Boster signature stamp). Parts 14 + 18 (duplicated).**
The City applied for a **$2,250,000 Wyoming Business Council Business Ready Community grant "for the ultimate benefit of" Microsoft**, to reimburse **100% of Microsoft's communication and electrical utility expenses — including commissioning costs**. Microsoft's return obligations: ~$50M site investment + $51,406,250 combined wages/capex within 5 years ($1,406,250 wage floor at >$24.14/hr). **THE CLAWBACK CEILING (Article III.C, corrected 8/27 red team):** repayment + 2% interest is owed only if Microsoft *ceases operations* within 5 years — and that repayment is the **exclusive remedy** for **any failure by the Company to perform**, **capped at the $2.25M grant**; the city can never recover more than it passed through, enforcement rests on the company's own annual certifications, and "temporary cessations" (server migrations, renovations, upgrades) are expressly carved out. **Say "remedy capped and self-certified" — not "no remedy."**
- Enforcement trail to pull: the 5 years of **annual payroll/investment certifications** Microsoft owed the City Grants Manager — did they file? (WBC + city grants office, WPRA.)
- Lineage frame: this is the origin instrument of the data-center era that now funds the budget — franchise fees 7.7% (FY25) → 11.8% (FY26, "+$2,652,000… because of the ongoing data center expansion") → CLFP $670,088.78/mo (Jul 2026). The City subsidied the utility bills of the customer whose consumption became its #2 revenue.
- Also in corpus: city p-cards paying **Microsoft Azure $1,604–1,665/mo (2022)** for cloud storage; "WBC Managed Data Center Grant" referred to Finance (2022 minutes).
