Task I
Map and Grid Generation
KU will provide all base map data through a combination of Global Position Systems (GPS) and UAV survey methods. Field work
will include locating existing survey control when available, and/or establishing new control of permanent nature as needed and
can be preserved. Established control will be used for subsequent surveys, as required by the RFF to generate required reports
and drawings as described in previous stated formats. Field crews will also provide temporary targets incorporated with required
grids for aerial map pin control as necessary for each stage to capture required data to map MSW and C&D items. Field crews
will also verify permitted boundary as deemed necessary, and boundaries of the MSW and C&D cells. Control for project and flight
will be points of the requested grid plans whenever possible. All critical base map points will be field verified, such as flowlines of
drainages, culverts, roads, buildings, fencing, wind turbines, and other relevant natural features or improvements within project
boundaries. As noted in the RFP the map generated from this survey will include mapping of the entire 260 acres of the site,
and the Phase 1 area will be clearly defined on any grid plans and maps produced.
Task 2
3 Air Space Utilization Calculations and Isopach Figures
TASK 2A
SWT will complete the volumetric analysis comparison for airspace (MSW and C&D) and soil usage at the City of Cheyenne
Landfill between the proposed 2021 topography information (new base mapping/survey work completed with Task 1 by KU> and
the second survey of the MSW and C&D areas. This analysis will be completed using Civil 3D AutoCAD software, which develops
two digital train models and calculates the volume in cubic yards (CV) between the two topographic maps.
Upon completion of this volume calculation, the airspace consumed will be broken out to eliminate volumes associated with
excavation and refuse fill due to refuse settlement (areas outside the MSW/C&D fill areas). These waste boundaries will be
determined by KU under Task 1. The calculated airspace utilization factor (AUF) will not include soil stockpiles or other non-
waste stockpiles created within the active landfill footprint between survey dates, SWT/KU will provide backup justifying any
volumes not included in the AUF, if applicable. For example, justification may include survey of the area prior to stockpiling in
order to calculate volume.
With this information and the tonnage information provided by the City SWT will evaluate the density of the in-place refuse
(AIJF) for both MSW and C&D. Additionally, when preparing AUF calculations, SWT typically calculates the soil-to-refuse ratio to
give the client a good understanding of soil (daily cover) to refuse ratio for their site and how it compares to published industry
standards.

BACKGROUND INFORMATION ABOUT PURPOSE OF PROPOSED PURCHASE: 
From Cheyenne Fire Rescue Department: 
In light of Boulder County and many other municipalities throughout the Western United States, 
the need for quick response/attack is crucial during these wind driven events. We have seen a 
dramatic increase in wind driven events and brush fires within municipalities and Cheyenne. 
Increased development in areas that are otherwise prairie and open in nature are prime locations 
for these type of events. Places like Whitney Ranch, Saddle Ridge, Sweetgrass, Swan Ranch, Bison 
Business Park and many others to name a few. Time is crucial and if we are capable of a quick 
response, we have a better opportunity to prevent major loss. We will still be reliant upon our 
mutual aid partners for all of these types of events and already have agreements established. 
 
An investment in alternate vehicles (i.e. brush trucks and/or mini pumpers) for these types of 
events is quite worth the investment. Current loses within Boulder and Boulder County have 
exceeded 513 million dollars. The use of the vehicle would be cross staffed when we are dispatched 
to these events. 
 
PROCUREMENT INFORMATION & PROCESS: 
The City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne Fire Rescue Department, is requesting spending authority in an 
amount not-to-exceed $150,000.00 for the purchase of a used Brush Truck.  Due to the current 
market for used vehicles and equipment, timing is of the essence when a used piece of equipment 
becomes available on the used vehicle market.

vehicles or equipment meeting or exceeding the minimum requirements set forth by the 
department/division. If the amount exceeds $35,000.00 the expenditure must be approved by the 
Governing Body.” 
 
This is a preemptive request for approval by the Governing Body, to grant spending authority when 
a used vehicle that meets all of the minimum specifications is located & identified.  In order to 
ensure that the City can make an offer on a vehicle in a timely manner, without placing a 
Consideration of Award on City Council Agenda (3-4 Week Process), the Cheyenne Fire Rescue 

Department is requesting this spending authority in advance of beginning the search for a vehicle 
that meets the needs of the Department.  This will provide the City the ability to expeditiously 
secure a used vehicle when a suitable unit is located.  In coordination with the City of Cheyenne 
Fleet Maintenance Division, minimum specifications have been established for this purchase and 
they are attached to this memorandum. 
 
It may be necessary for City Fleet Maintenance & Cheyenne Fire Rescue Staff to travel to the 
location of any vehicle(s) that meet the minimum specifications in order to allow staff the 
opportunity to test and inspect the unit.   
 
The purpose for this Request to Purchase Used Brush Truck Style Vehicle, is based on the 
significant cost savings by selecting a used vehicle, over building a new unit.  Comparable new 
units are currently being sold for $395,000.00 to $458,000.00.
