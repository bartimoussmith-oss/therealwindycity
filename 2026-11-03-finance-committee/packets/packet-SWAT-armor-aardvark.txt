To whom it may concern, 
Please allow this letter to confirm that PROJECT 7 products are sole source, made exclusively by and sold 
exclusively through AARDVARK Tactical. PROJECT 7 represents a fusion of proprietary and/or patented 
technologies and a combination of materials and design elements that are not available in any other product or 
from any other source. 
PROJECT 7 is designed to work as a single, fully integrated system that ensures all components (including 
vests, plate racks, dog vest, shields, battle belts, packs, pouches, plates, helmets, armor accessories, etc.) work 
together seamlessly to provide optimized safety, mobility, comfort, and operational effectiveness for tactical 
operators. PROJECT 7’s mission-specific armor platforms are engineered with cutting-edge materials and 
ballistic technologies to balance protection, weight, flexibility, and comfort. 
The PROJECT 7 product line includes: 
 Advanced ballistic armor platforms such as full coverage entry vests and ultralight plate racks 
designed to meet specific mission profiles and mobility needs. 
 Level III ballistic shields with ergonomic handle and padding systems and lightweight composite 
construction designed to reduce operator fatigue and improve maneuverability. 
 Integrated helmet systems combining high-performance ballistic shells and modular mounting systems 
for night vision and communications accessories. 
 Industry-leading patrol vest designs, including the Xternal Patrol Vest (XPV) with a four-piece 
platform and patented cummerbund system to provide greater coverage, adjustability, and comfort 
without compromising flexibility. 
 Tactical K9 protection vests delivering ergonomic, high-coverage ballistic protection optimized for 
operational canine units. 
 A full range of packs, pouches, accessories, and mission-specific gear that integrate with PROJECT 7 
armor systems.
