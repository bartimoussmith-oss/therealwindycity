## B. THE VERIFIED TIMELINE (all on city paper or press of record)

- **1979** — Municipal Building opens, 2101 O'Neil Ave. (city resolution, 2024).
- **Aug. 1, 1985** — Cheyenne flood (12 deaths). *Alleged settlement link to brick cracking: unverified.*
- **Mar. 5, 2024** — Governing body resolution: study the west entryway — recites rusted soffits, cracking/breaking brick, efflorescence, pebble-walkway tripping hazards, malfunctioning entry security. Framing: "public perception and experience."
- **Apr. 2, 2024** — Nemecek testifies to Public Services Committee: paint soffits, hire architectural firm for brick, quote the efflorescence work, replace the doors, fix pavement by fall 2024.
- **Jun. 4, 2024** — Follow-up resolution thanks Nemecek and Sanchez for the work.
- **Dec. 2024** — Work session on the Municipal Building announced (Wyoming News Now, Dec. 11, 2024) — *the likely venue for the engineering testimony the draft cites; record not yet pulled.*
- **2025–26** — City appropriates Nov. 2021 SPET excess to buy the **former Senior Center, 2101 Thomes Ave.** — ballot language confirms: the $22M remodel follows **"design and planning work previously approved and funded by the voters"**; industry press: the former senior center is to become a **stand-alone council building** (Plan One/Essenza concept).
- **Jan. 14, 2026** — COTW: sixth-penny list carried 8–1; **Municipal Building Remodel $22M survives** Moody's Fire Station #6 swap (0–9) and his half-funded $11M version (1–8); Nemecek's "lipstick on a pig" testimony; Collins against the partial version.
- **Ballot** — **Proposition 5: "To collect a total of $22,000,000… for completion of a full remodel of the existing Municipal Building… improvements to public service areas, office space reconfiguration, ADA accessibility upgrades, and associated furniture and equipment"** (corpus p52 — the enacted ballot language). Public Works originally requested **$27M**; council cut to $22M.
- **Calendar** — list to County Commissioners by **Apr. 6, 2026**; commissioner approval by **May 5**; early voting **Jul. 21**; election **Aug. 18, 2026**. 2021 SPET collection ends ~Q1 2026; county sales tax reverts to 5% meanwhile.
- **Aug. 18, 2026** — Election held nine days ago. **RESULTS NOT YET PULLED.**
