**1. Bundled-Tract Statutory Defect — Void Ab Initio — Two Statutes, One Ordinance**

- **15-1-403** (petition annexation): requires landowner petition, contiguity, 20 business-day certified mail to landowners [Staff p3: earliest confirmation March 16, 2026], public hearing.
- **15-1-407** (city-owned non-contiguous): allows city to annex its own non-contiguous land by ordinance, no petition, no contiguity.
- **This ordinance** merges: Sec1 = 1,252.09ac private Cox Trust under 403, Sec2 = 10.23ac city water plant under 407 admitted non-contiguous, Sec3 = single enactment of both. No Wyoming statute authorizes omnibus annexation under two distinct procedures with different notice/contiguity/jurisdictional standards in one ordinance.
- **Effect:** Violates 15-1-402 unified purpose + 15-1-407 separate-procedure requirement. Severability clause (Sec5) cannot cure because contiguity for city parcel depends entirely on private parcel per staff admission. If private fails, city fails.
