CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Girls on the Run Wyoming, an after-school program for girls that looks to enhance their social skills and health with physical activity and other programming, is ending its fall 2026 season Nov. 14 with a 5K. The public is welcome to join the run.
For 10 years, Girls on the Run Wyoming has taken girls ages 8–13 in for eight-week periods to build up their “competence, confidence, connection, character, caring, and contribution,” the organization said. A special curriculum that emphasizes physical activity is used to enhance girls’ social, psychological and physical skills and behaviors to successfully navigate life experiences.
At the end of the program, the organization hosts a celebratory 5K run.
“This closing event gives program participants of all abilities a tangible sense of accomplishment,” Girls on the Run Wyoming said. “Crossing the finish line instills confidence through completion and is a joyful moment program participants always remember! Everyone in our program receives a commemorative medal to celebrate this incredible achievement.”
The organization is inviting members of the community to join to cohort and their coaches for the occasion. The run takes place Nov. 14 at Central High School’s Riske Field. The run begins at 9 a.m. While participants can register as early as 7:30 a.m. the day of the run, registration can also be done online
