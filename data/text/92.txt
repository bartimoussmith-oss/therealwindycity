Coyote Ridge Elementary School (Coyote Ridge Elementary School Facebook)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Cheyenne’s Coyote Ridge Elementary, a participant in Special Olympics Unified Champion School programming, is getting a nod from the Special Olympics organization for its efforts to provide inclusive sports and activities for students with and without intellectual disabilities.
The school was chosen to be a National Banner School after meeting 10 national standards of excellence in the areas of inclusion, advocacy and respect and demonstrating that its work is self-sustainable. A panel of leaders from Special Olympics and the education community developed the standards, the organization said.
“The Unified Champion Schools model is a strategy for schools Pre-K through university that intentionally promotes meaningful social inclusion by bringing together students with and without intellectual disabilities to create accepting school environments, utilizing three interconnected components: Special Olympics Unified Sports, inclusive youth leadership, and whole school engagement,” Special Olympics said.
Coyote Ridge is one of 50 Wyoming schools to participate in the program, joining more than 12,000 schools across the country.
