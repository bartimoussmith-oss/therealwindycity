CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Incumbent Rep. Rob Geringer defeated Democrat challenger Jim VanDyke in Tuesday’s general election to retain his Wyoming House District 42 seat.
In Tuesday’s election, Geringer received 2,375 votes to VanDyke’s 695.
Geringer was elected to his first term in the Wyoming House of Representatives in 2024. A civil engineer and small business owner, he serves on the House Transportation, Highways & Military Affairs Committee. He also serves on the Health Insurance Affordability Task Force and the Select Committee on School Facilities.
He campaigned on easing household costs and improving healthcare access for Wyoming families. His platform paired support for traditional energy and agriculture with efforts to recruit advanced manufacturing and technology projects.
