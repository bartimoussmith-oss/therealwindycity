CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Wednesday, Nov. 11 is Veterans Day. In observance of the holiday, most city offices and facilities will be closed and the trash pickup schedule will be altered.
According to the City of Cheyenne, residential trash, recycling and yard waste pickups will slide forward one day starting Veterans Day. That means areas scheduled for Wednesday pickups will get their waste picked up on Thursday and areas scheduled for Thursday pickups will have to wait until Friday.
The
Solid Waste Transfer Station
,
Happy Jack Landfill
,
Compost Facility
and
Household Hazardous Waste
will all be closed Nov. 11. They will reopen the following morning. Like other city offices, the Sanitation Division office will also be closed.
Buses will not run Nov. 11, the city said. Some public facilities, such as the BEAST Facility and the Cheyenne Botanic Gardens, will be closed as well. Others like the Cheyenne Aquatic Center, Ice and Events Center and public golf courses will, however, remain open.
County offices, including the entirety of the Laramie County Courthouse, will not open Nov. 11 either.
