Ronald Otto Hartz, Sr
Ronald Otto Hartz, Sr.:
1937 – 2026
Ronald Hartz, 89, died peacefully at home surrounded by his family.
Ron had a lengthy career as an electrician through I.B.E.W. Once he retired, he worked for Laramie County School District #1 as an electrician. After his second retirement, he spent his time traveling with his wife until her passing.
His various hobbies included traveling, camping, fishing, gold panning and riding motorcycles. He was a proud member of I.B.E.W., Masonic Temple, Shrine Korean Clowns, Red Dale Jackalope camping club, FMCA camping club, Eagles, Elks, American Legion, Moose Lodge, Good Sam Club, Gold Wing Riders Association and the Gold Prospects Association of America.
Ron was preceded in death by his wife, Beth Hartz; his parents, Otto Hartz (Laura) and Sarah Reinheimer-Cook; siblings: Delbert (Sherry), Virginia, Patty and Duane (Diane); and his step-siblings, Ginger and Loren.
He is survived by his children, Ron Hartz, Jr. (Terry) of Cheyenne and Sherye Seckman (Eric) of Florida; grandchildren: Mike Seckman (Josie), Melissa Lolling (Brandon), Ben Hartz (Amanda), Matt Seckman (Alli) and Ashley Hartz; great-grandchildren: Jaxon, Kaylee, Kaiden, Keegan, Jaelyn, Rowan, Ryder, Lilith and Riley; step-sister, Nita; and half-sisters: Norma, Barbara and Jeanette.
A graveside service is planned for Wednesday, November 11 at 2 p.m. at Cheyenne Memorial Gardens. All are welcome to attend and help family celebrate Ron’s life. No reception to follow. In lieu of flowers, family asks donations be made in Ron’s name to the Shriner Hospital.
Genevieve Elaine Marro:
1941 – 2026
In Loving Memory of Genevieve Elaine Marro
Also known as Gonzalez, Gutierrez, and Clarke
November 14, 1941 – September 18, 2026. Age 84.
A Life Remembered
Born and named Genevieve Elaine Gonzalez on November 14, 1941, and raised in Cheyenne, Wyoming,  Genevieve married and raised her family of six in Fremont, California. She also resided in Manteca, Ripon, Hayward, Santa Clara, California. She settled in Littlefield, Texas in 2015.
On September 18, 2026, the lord collected her from the home she shared with her eldest son, Anthony Gutierrez.
Jenny treasured keepsakes and surrounded herself with family portraits. She cherished every child, grandchild and great-grandchild and did her best to be there for each one. She retired after many years at Lockheed Martin.
Jenny was a friend, daughter, sister, wife, mother, grandmother, and great-grandmother.
Both a caregiver and a prankster, she was known for her infectious humor and unmistakable laugh—her “cackle,” as we called it. She loved her shows and horror films, which is shared across generations, down to her great-grandchildren.
Although distance and circumstances kept many of her children, grandchildren, and great-grandchildren from being by her side in her final years, their love endured, and they stayed in touch. She is remembered for her unending devotion to family who needed her most, continuously setting aside her own wants and needs to care for family.
The last of her seven siblings to enter heaven, Jenny will live on in her descendants’ hearts. She is survived by her son, Anthony Gutierrez of Littlefield, Texas; her son, Nicholas Gutierrez and his wife, Karen, of Hayward; her grandson, Nicholas Stewart, and his wife, Kathy, of San Jose; her daughter, Valerie Gutierrez of Manteca, California; her daughter, Rebecca Fugit and her husband, Dennis; her granddaughter, Ronni Valladares and great-granddaughter,  Izzy Papst of Ripon; her granddaughter, Toni Glanz Moran and her husband, Anthony; great-granddaughter, Priscilla; and great-grandsons, Anthony Jr. and Alonzo, of Modesto. She’s also survived by many nieces, nephews, god-children, great-nieces and great-nephews, and more generations from her extended family.
Mom wanted a party, not a funeral. Her Life Celebration will be held on her 85th birthday:
Date and time: Saturday, November 14, 2026, at 2 p.m.
Location: 231 California Street, Ripon
Everyone is welcome! Please bring memories, laughter, and photos to share.
Roger William Radomsky, Sr.:
1941 – 2026
Roger William Radomsky, Sr., 85, of Tucson, Arizona and formerly of Cheyenne, died on Friday, November 6, 2026 in Tucson.
He was born on July 11, 1941 in East Cleveland, Ohio.
Roger was the third of four children, born to Lloyd and Lulu (Glasser) Radomsky. He grew up and lived in East Cleveland, Ohio, until 1961 when he enlisted in the US Army. He met his beloved wife, Jane, while both were working at St. Luke’s Hospital. After they were married, he served as a medic in the Army until October 1964, at Ft. Bragg in North Carolina. They moved back to his family home in East Cleveland where they began their family with a son, Jon, in 1966, followed closely by another son, Scott, in 1967.
Roger worked at a finance company in Cleveland for six years before being transferred to Dayton, Ohio, in 1970, where his third son, Roger Jr. was born. In 1971, Roger made the decision to attend mortuary college in Cincinnati. After graduating in 1972, he accepted a position as a funeral director and embalmer at Schrader’s Funeral Home in Cheyenne, Wyoming. Their family was completed with the birth of their fourth child, Laura, in 1974. Later that year he ran for and was elected as Laramie County Coroner, serving the community faithfully for 16 years. In 1978, he began working at Wiederspahn Chapel of the Chimes, eventually purchasing the company from its founder in 1987. In 1981, Roger and Jane opened the Ice Cream Corral in Frontier Mall and operated it until selling the business in 1984. Roger made all the ice cream himself in the early hours before work began at the funeral home, returning to the store in the evening to help close shop.
Roger and Jane bought a cabin near Vedauwoo, enjoying their time out in nature. They were able to treasure this haven for many years before retiring to Tucson, Arizona. Roger spent his later years enjoying multiple cruises and trips exploring the world with Jane. He was blessed with ten grandchildren and six great-grandchildren, whom he adored.
Roger was a member of the Lion’s Club, the American Legion, the Moose Lodge, and the Masons. He attended First United Methodist Church. In his retirement years, he was a member of the Sundancers, a square dance club which both he and Jane enjoyed. He loved fishing, camping, bowling, trains, watching football, and all his beloved dogs.
He is survived by his wife Jane, whom he married on September 28, 1963; his children: Jon (Bridgette), Scott (Carrie), Roger, Jr. (Debbie), and Laura Kemp (Darren); his grandchildren: Jenna, Katy, Colter, Nikki, Ellie, Logan, Lainie, Nolan, Charlotte, and Emma; and his great grandchildren: Maisyn, Harper, Forrest, Carson, Blake, and Penelope.
Roger was preceded in death by his parents, Lloyd and Lulu Radomsky; and his siblings: Lois Faflick, Paul Radomsky, and Rose Pongonis.
Services will be Tuesday, November 17, 10 a.m., at Wiederspahn-Radomsky Chapel with military honors and burial to follow at Cheyenne Memorial Gardens.
In lieu of flowers, Roger would have preferred donations to:
Tunnel to Towers Foundation 2361 Hylan Boulevard Staten Island, NY 10306
Colorado Children’s Hospital
13123 E. 16th Avenue, Box 045, Aurora, CO 80045.

Myra Jane Walradt, born on February 18, 1946, passed away on October 29, 2026.
She will be remembered with respect and affection by those who knew her. Her life spanned eighty years, and her memory remains with all who had the privilege of sharing in it.
May Myra Jane Walradt rest in peace.
Kathleen Ann Williams:
1950 – 2026
It is with deep sadness that the family of Kathleen A. Williams age 76 of Cheyenne, Wyoming would like to announce her passing on October 29, 2026, at a hospital at Loveland Colorado, surrounded by her loving family.
Kathy was born on April 4, 1950, in Ark City, Kansas to her parents Ernie and Doris Allen.
Kathy attended school in Kansas City, where she earned many honors and was proud to participate in Pep Club.
She then moved to Aurora, Colorado with her family to attend Stewardess training.
She met the love of her life, Willie Williams, in Aurora. They then were married in the Sweetheart chapel in Loveland where they later celebrated their 50th anniversary. Willie truly adored Kathy!!
Kathy and Willie were blessed with a wonderful son, Jason, in 1977 and grandson, Alexander in 2016. Both were also loves of her life.
Kathy was employed to manage several upscale apartment complexes and later she worked at a CPA firm in Cheyenne, Wyoming.
Kathy is survived by her loving husband William; her wonderful son, Jason; and her cherished 10-year-old grandson, Alexander.
Those who preceded her in death include Kathy’s parents, Ernie and Doris Allen; sister, Sue; brother, Michael; and 90-year-old grandmother, Mom Allen.
Kathy loved her family deeply, cared for them greatly and is sure to be in heaven waiting for us to arrive, hallelujah!
A memorial service will be held at 2 p.m., November 14 at CrossBridge Alliance Church, 3501 Forest Dr., Cheyenne, Wyoming. You may bring floral arrangements or donate to the college fund for Kathy’s grandson, Alex.
Dave Wilson:
1949 – 2026
In Loving Memory of Dave Wilson
Dave Wilson was born on November 16, 1949, in Lake City, Iowa. He lived a life filled with love, laughter, friendship, music, and devotion to his family.
Dave met the love of his life, Joleen Rose Golwitzer, and together they recently celebrated an incredible 57 years of marriage. They raised two daughters, Ali Wilson and Heather Wilson, of Cheyenne, Wyoming, who were among his greatest blessings.
Dave was a devoted Iowa Hawkeyes fan, especially the women’s basketball team and his favorite player, Caitlin Clark, as well as Iowa Hawkeyes football. He loved music and played in a band for numerous events, gatherings, and weddings, bringing people together through music. He also enjoyed playing horseshoes and, in his more recent years, spending time on the golf course with friends.
More than anything, Dave leaves behind a legacy of life lessons. He taught his family to go after what they want, always do their best for their family, never give up, and believe in the importance of a strong work ethic. These were values he lived by and passed on to the generations who will carry his memory forward.
Dave is survived by his beloved wife of 57 years, Joleen Wilson; daughters, Ali Wilson and Heather Wilson; granddaughter, Ashlee Montoya; grandson, Adam Montoya, and his wife, Rebecca; and great-grandchildren: Bella, Miia, Bennett, and Emmett.
He was preceded in death by his parents, Paul and Aloha Wilson; sisters, Harriett Cole and Sharon Wilson; brother, Gary Wilson; and great-grandsons, Kane and David.
Dave’s love, laughter, determination, and the lessons he taught will live on through the family he cherished. His stories will continue to be told, his favorite teams will still be cheered on, and the memories he created will forever hold a special place in the hearts of those who knew and loved him.
A life well lived, a family well loved, and a legacy that will never be forgotten.
Rest peacefully, Dad. You will be missed more than words can ever express, loved beyond measure, and remembered always.
Janet Margaret Van Kirk:
1933 – 2026
Janet Margaret Van Kirk, 93, beloved wife, mother, grandmother, passed away peacefully surrounded by her family on Friday, November 6, 2026, at Polaris Rehabilitation and Care Center in Cheyenne, Wyoming. She was a 71 year resident of Cheyenne, Wyoming and former resident of Waupaca, Wisconsin.
Janet was born on February 18, 1933, in Waupaca, Wisconsin; the daughter of William H. Johnson and Hazel E. Butterfield. She and her husband, Charles W. Van Kirk Sr., successfully ran Charlies Mobile Home Towing and Repair. She had many passions outside of the family business which included dancing, knitting, crocheting, crafting, gardening, cooking, baking and poetry. She loved the most being with family!
Janet is survived by her three sons: Steven Van Kirk of Cheyenne, Wyoming; Charles Van Kirk Jr. and wife Terry Lu Van Kirk of Cheyenne, Wyoming; Bruce Van Kirk and wife Eldeana Van Kirk of Cheyenne Wyoming. Her legacy continues through her many grandchildren, great-grandchildren, and extended family of cousins, nieces, nephews and friends.
She was preceded in death by her husband, Charles Van Kirk Sr.; her parents; two daughters, Lamarcia Michaud, Deborah Stewart; one son, Jefferey Van Kirk; and two grandsons, Christopher Graham Van Kirk and Keith William Van Kirk.
Graveside services will be held at Cheyenne Memorial Gardens, November 19, 2026, at 10 a.m.
