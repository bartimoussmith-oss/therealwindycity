Laramie County sheriff taps Wyoming DCI to investigate fatal officer-involved shooting

(Halle Sheppard, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — After an officer-involved shooting resulting in the death of a suspect off of Interstate 80 in east Laramie County on Wednesday, the Laramie County Sheriff’s Office is asking the Wyoming Division of Criminal Investigation to investigate the incident.
The shooting occurred off of milepost 377 — approximately near the TA Travel Center south of Hillsdale — at 1:42 p.m. According a release by the LCSO, the suspect is dead and no deputies were injured.
Now the LCSO is tasking DCI with investigating the incident, including reviewing body cam and patrol car footage, conducting deputy and witness interviews and examining evidence.
The process, the office said, is exhaustive and will take time. No other details can be shared at this moment, they said, as that would reportedly interfere with the investigation.
“Separately, LCSO will conduct an internal administrative review to evaluate the incident in relation to office policies, procedures and training standards to identify any areas needing improvement,” the LCSO said. “We understand the community has questions and we ask for your patience as both processes move forward. We will share additional information as it becomes available for public release.”
