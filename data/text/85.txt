CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The Cheyenne Youth Symphony’s annual fall concert is just days away. The Oct. 24 show, titled “Treasures,” will take on a pirate theme and is completely free to attend.
The show will begin at 7 p.m. Oct. 24 at LCCC’s Surbrugg/Prentice Auditorium.
The young string players, under the direction of symphony founder and director Jaylene Willhite, will play pirate-themed tunes that take families, friends and music lovers on a journey highlighting the spirit of adventure.
The Cheyenne Youth Symphony serves students in grades 3–9, helping young musicians develop their technical skills, musical expression and confidence through ensemble performance. Its programs include the Preparatory Orchestra for students in grades 3–4, the Prelude Symphony for emerging string players and the Cadenza Symphony for more experienced musicians.
