Cheyenne man issued $50K bond in child sexual abuse materials case

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The Laramie County District Court will soon hear the case of 19-year-old Brayden Lyday, who was arrested last month after a year-long investigation reportedly found that he was in possession and was involved in the distribution of child sexual abuse materials.
Police are charging Lyday with eight felonies, which could put him in jail for a combined 88 years if he is found guilty on every count and sentenced to the maximum potential penalty. Four of the charges are for sexual exploitation of a child through creating or receiving child sexual abuse materials; the other four are for possession of those materials. He is presumed innocent until pleading or being found guilty.
Lyday was arrested in late October following a criminal investigation dating back to February that reportedly discovered that he was involved with distributing child sexual abuse materials, including videos, to others online.
The Wyoming Division of Criminal Investigation first learned of suspicious activity involving Lyday from a tip sent by Discord, an online messaging platform. An account, linked to an email address associated with Lyday, was flagged by the platform for sending a two-minute video in which a child performed sexual acts on an adult man to another user.
An analysis of the email address by DCI investigators confirmed that Lyday, born in April 2007, was not a minor himself; an IP address search connected investigators to his Cheyenne address.
In late April, investigators reportedly gained access to Lyday’s Discord account via a search warrant and found conversations in which Lyday sent multiple other illegal videos to fellow Discord users. Investigators believe he had obtained the videos from platforms like X and Telegram.
On Oct. 19, investigators interviewed Lyday. He reportedly told them that he used Discord for gaming and chatting with his friends.
A search of a hidden folder on Lyday’s phone’s photo app also reportedly uncovered 17 other files containing child sexual abuse materials.
A Laramie County Circuit Court judge found sufficient probable cause to bind the case to District Court, which has not yet scheduled a plea hearing for Lyday. He was issued a $50,000 cash bond.
