Detention center inmate charged with ‘donkey kicking,’ spitting at deputies

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — An inmate at the Laramie County Detention Center was charged after “donkey kicking” a corrections officer and spitting in the eye of another late last month. According to law enforcement, the suspect is awaiting transport to the Wyoming State Hospital for psychiatric treatment.
Christopher Grant, born in 1999, was cited with two interference with a police officer causing injury charges after an Oct. 28 incident. Each charge carries a maximum potential penalty of 10 years in prison. Grant is presumed innocent until pleading or being found guilty.
According to the Laramie County Sheriff’s Office, deputies were in the booking area of the Laramie County Detention Center shortly before 6 p.m. Oct. 28 when Grant, in a nearby holding cell, reportedly began standing on his cell’s sink.
A deputy reportedly told Grant to get off the sink and told Grant that he would use pepper spray if he didn’t comply. Grant refused, the affidavit states, compelling the deputy to spray a two-second burst of the substance into the cell. Grant shortly thereafter got off the sink and allowed deputies to cuff him, deputies said.
They took Grant to the booking shower to help wash off the spray. There, before the decontamination process could begin, Grant reportedly “donkey kicked” the knee of one of the deputies standing behind him. When asked to rate the pain, the deputy reportedly gave it a 10/10 on the LCSO’s pain scale.
He and another deputy immediately brought Grant to the floor, crossing his legs so he could be put in a restraint chair. While being sat down, Grant reportedly became verbally aggressive, using curse words against deputies and, while being taken back to the shower in the restraint chair, calling one a pedophile.
He then reportedly spat at that deputy, with some of the saliva making contact with the deputy’s eye. A spit hood was placed on Grant and deputies and nursing staff were eventually able to finish decontaminating and examining him before placing him back in his cell.
The two deputies that Grant reportedly attacked were treated. The one that Grant reportedly kicked went to the hospital, where he was given a knee brace — deputies said he wore it for at least a week and a half — and ordered to take two days off of work. He did not break any bones.
As of Nov. 13, bond has not been issued to Grant and he has not been scheduled for a preliminary hearing.
