Public Hearings for Liquor License Applications
Published on November 05, 2026
Public hearings will be held on November 9, 2026 at 6:00 p.m. at the Municipal Building, City Council Chambers, 2101 O'Neil Ave, and via electronic conference meeting for a bar and grill liquor license application for Bally's Wyoming, LLC d/b/a Bally's Cheyenne, 1734 Meadowland Dr. and for a restaurant liquor license application for Shack & Co LLC d/b/a Wing Shack Yellowstone, 5719 Yellowstone Rd., Cheyenne, WY.
