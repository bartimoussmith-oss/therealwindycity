EVANSTON, Wyo. — A Utah woman was killed Oct. 24 after she reportedly entered and drove westbound in the eastbound lane of travel on Interstate 80 near Evanston and was hit by an oncoming truck. The truck driver, according to the Wyoming Highway Patrol, was uninjured.
A report made by the WHP and published by the Wyoming Department of Transportation said that 69-year-old Carol Lucas began driving westward in the eastbound lane of travel on Interstate 80 at around 9:30 a.m. Oct. 24.
Less than a mile later,
at around milepost 10.8
, a truck traveling in the correct direction was attempting to pass another semi when, entering the left lane, it struck Lucas’s Toyota Camry head-on.
Lucas was reportedly not wearing her seat belt when the crash occurred.
Investigators did not list any potential contributing factors.
This story contains preliminary information as provided by the Wyoming Highway Patrol via the Wyoming Department of Transportation Fatal Crash Summary map. The agency advises that information may be subject to change.
