BUFFALO, Wyo. — A fire is currently burning 12 miles west of Buffalo, in the Bighorn National Forest.
The Foot Creek fire, as it’s been dubbed, is currently estimated to have burnt 413 acres. According to a social media post from the U.S. Forest Service, the fire is human-caused and is burning on U.S. Forest Service land, as well as a small amount of private lands.
Dry conditions are expected throughout the weekend, with potential light, terrain-driven winds.
“The primary objective today is public and firefighter health and safety and structure protection,” the post states. “Crews are looking for opportunities to fight the fire directly and dozer lines have been put in place.”
For more information, including evacuation instructions, visit
johnsoncowy.gov/emergency-alerts
