Trio of Cheyenne students win awards at national FFA conference

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A trio of Laramie County students won awards at the National FFA Convention and Expo in Indianapolis this week, one of them taking first place in agricultural communications and a team winning at the agriscience fair in social science.
The convention is the national gathering of FFA chapters, giving students from around the country a chance to show off their knowledge of the world of agriculture. Three Cheyenne students showed that Laramie County knows its way around that world handily.
Brody Medina won first place in the proficiency award for agricultural communication. According to the FFA, agricultural proficiency awards honor FFA members who, through
Supervised Agricultural Experiences
, have developed specialized skills that they can apply toward future careers.
Medina is steeped in agricultural communications knowledge. He served as the first communications intern for Wyoming FFA and as social media coordinator for Frontier Livestock. He has published more than 23 articles in local, regional and national agricultural publications.
Teammates Samantha Duran and Emily Shuck won their division in the National FFA Agriscience Fair for social science. The fair recognizes students who gain real-world, hands-on experiences in agricultural enterprises. Students use scientific principles and emerging technologies to solve complex problems related to agriculture, food and natural resources. Students compete in one of six categories in the agriscience fair and under one of the six divisions.
One of those categories is social sciences, in which Duran and Shuck stood out and won Division 4.
