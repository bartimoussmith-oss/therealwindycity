13 dessert picks worth making while you can still eat outside without a jacket
Jenn Allen
Nov 21, 2026
Nov 21, 2026

The last mild evenings of the year are a good excuse to take dessert outside before the weather turns cold. This collection ranges from hand-held cookies and turnovers to warm fruit desserts and shareable bakes. There are chocolate and caramel treats for a richer finish, plus peaches, berries, apples, and lemon for a fruit-forward choice. Set out a few options and enjoy dessert while you can still linger outdoors without reaching for a jacket.
Chocolate Chip Skillet Cookie. Photo credit: Bake What You Love.
Flaky Apple-Cranberry Turnovers with Fresh Cranberries
Flaky Apple-Cranberry Turnovers with Fresh Cranberries. Photo credit: Food Drink Life.
Golden edges and a tart fruit center make
Flaky Apple-Cranberry Turnovers with Fresh Cranberries
easy to carry outdoors. The filling pairs apples with fresh cranberries, brown sugar, cinnamon, and nutmeg, while puff pastry forms individual hand-held portions. One batch makes eight turnovers, so guests can take one without a plate or serving spoon. Set them out after a backyard meal when the weather still invites everyone to linger outside.
Get the Recipe:
Flaky Apple-Cranberry Turnovers with Fresh Cranberries
Grilled Fruit Skewers with Cherry Dip
Grilled Fruit Skewers with Cherry Dip. Photo credit: Grill What You Love.
At a backyard cookout,
Grilled Fruit Skewers with Cherry Dip
bring a dessert that can go straight from grill to platter. Pineapple, strawberries, peaches, and bananas are threaded onto skewers, then paired with a dip of cream cheese, Greek yogurt, marshmallow fluff, and cherry juice. The recipe takes about 20 minutes and serves four. Pass the skewers around after dinner for an outdoor sweet that needs only napkins.
Get the Recipe:
Grilled Fruit Skewers with Cherry Dip
Turtle Brownies
Turtle Brownies. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Cut into squares for an easy-to-share treat,
Turtle Brownies
layer a chocolate brownie base with caramel and toasted pecans. The recipe makes 12 servings, and the brownies can be prepared ahead before the warm caramel topping is added. Their sturdy shape makes them simple to set on a dessert tray for a patio gathering, especially when guests want a rich chocolate option alongside the fruit desserts.
Get the Recipe:
Turtle Brownies
Chocolate Caramel Cookies
Chocolate Caramel Cookies. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
After a casual meal outside,
Chocolate Caramel Cookies
bring a hand-held dessert with several textures in each bite. Cocoa and chocolate chips build the cookie base, while chocolate frosting, caramel, and crunchy Skor bits finish the tops. The recipe yields about 10 cookies and bakes in 12 minutes, so the batch is easy to portion for a small group. Arrange them on a platter where guests can grab one without plates or forks.
Get the Recipe:
Chocolate Caramel Cookies
Smoked Peaches with Spiced Rum Whipped Cream
Smoked Peaches with Spiced Rum Whipped Cream. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Smoky fruit and cool whipped cream give
Smoked Peaches with Spiced Rum Whipped Cream
a place at an outdoor dinner while the smoker is already going. Fresh peach halves are smoked with turbinado sugar, then topped with cream whipped with vanilla and spiced rum. The recipe serves four and takes about 40 minutes total. Serve the peaches warm as a plated finish after grilled or smoked food.
Get the Recipe:
Smoked Peaches with Spiced Rum Whipped Cream
Strawberry Upside Down Cake
Strawberry Upside Down Cake. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
On a dessert table,
Strawberry Upside Down Cake
puts the fruit on top, where its color is easy to see when the cake is sliced. Strawberries and cake make a familiar combination that suits a late-season backyard meal, and each portion can be served with a fork. Bring it out after dinner when everyone is still sitting outside, or set the slices beside coffee for a relaxed afternoon gathering.
Get the Recipe:
Strawberry Upside Down Cake
Peaches & Cream Cookies
Peaches & Cream Cookies. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Soft cookies give
Peaches & Cream Cookies
an easy-to-carry format for a meal on the patio. The peach-and-cream pairing brings a fruit-forward choice to a dessert tray, while individual portions make passing them around simple. Set these beside richer chocolate bakes so guests can choose a lighter-tasting option. They work for an outdoor coffee break, a picnic dessert, or a casual supper that ends before the evening turns chilly.
Get the Recipe:
Peaches & Cream Cookies
Lemon Loaf
Lemon Loaf. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
When dessert needs to travel from the kitchen to the patio,
Lemon Loaf
can be sliced and served without assembling individual portions. Lemon and a glaze give the loaf its citrus focus, and slices are easy to pick up with a napkin or place on small plates. Add it to an outdoor brunch or an afternoon coffee spread, where a bright, simple bake balances the richer brownies and cookies on the table.
Get the Recipe:
Lemon Loaf
Blueberry Grunt
Blueberry Grunt. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Alongside a late-season cookout,
Blueberry Grunt
offers a warm fruit dessert to serve by the bowl. Blueberries cook beneath soft dumplings, making a spoon useful and giving the dish a different shape from the cookies and bars in this collection. Bring it to the table after the main meal, with guests seated nearby, and serve portions while the fruit is still warm. It gives an outdoor gathering a home-style finish.
Get the Recipe:
Blueberry Grunt
Salted Caramel Brown Butter Snickerdoodles
Salted Caramel Brown Butter Snickerdoodles. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
A crackled cinnamon-sugar coating sets
Salted Caramel Brown Butter Snickerdoodles
apart on a cookie platter. Brown butter flavors the dough, while a caramel center adds a soft middle and a salted finish. Each cookie is individually portioned, making them simple to pass around outdoors with coffee or cider. Let them cool fully before serving so the caramel has time to settle, especially if the platter will sit out during a gathering.
Get the Recipe:
Salted Caramel Brown Butter Snickerdoodles
Brown Butter Apple Blondies
Brown Butter Apple Blondies. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Cut into squares,
Brown Butter Apple Blondies
are easy to bring outside on a tray. Diced apples and old-fashioned oats go into the brown-butter batter, and a brown-sugar glaze finishes the top. The recipe makes 12 servings and bakes in about 35 minutes. Offer a square after a backyard meal or pack a few for a picnic, where a bar dessert is neater to serve than a cake slice.
Get the Recipe:
Brown Butter Apple Blondies
Cinnamon Roll Casserole
Cinnamon Roll Casserole. Photo credit: Bake What You Love.
Assemble
Cinnamon Roll Casserole
ahead and bake it for a group brunch on a mild morning. Bakery cinnamon rolls soak in an egg-and-milk custard with cinnamon and golden raisins, then bake into a warm dish that serves eight. Since it is best served warm with a spoon, place it on a table close to the kitchen or bring out portions just before guests sit down. It adds a brunch-style dessert to the outdoor spread.
Get the Recipe:
Cinnamon Roll Casserole
Chocolate Chip Skillet Cookie
Chocolate Chip Skillet Cookie. Photo credit: Bake What You Love.
From the skillet to the patio table,
Chocolate Chip Skillet Cookie
makes a shareable finish for a small outdoor dinner. Semisweet chocolate chips and dark chocolate chunks bake into one large cookie with crisp edges and a soft center. It serves eight and takes about 35 minutes total; vanilla ice cream is suggested for serving. Bring it out warm, then cut wedges for guests who want dessert before the evening cools.
Get the Recipe:
Chocolate Chip Skillet Cookie
