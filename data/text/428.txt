Phoned-in gun threats at Central, South debunked by Cheyenne police Monday

Central High School (file photo)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Both Cheyenne Central and Cheyenne East went into lockdown today after an individual reportedly called the schools saying that someone was outside with a gun. Following a stressful school day for students, the Cheyenne Police Department confirmed that the claims were unsubstantiated.
Central was the first to receive a threatening phone call. At around noon Monday, someone called the school and said that there was a person outside with a gun. School administration and the school’s on-site police officer were alerted and the school was “placed into a secure perimeter for the safety of the students and staff,” Cheyenne police said.
Additional officers were sent out to assist and searched the building for a potential threat.
Shortly after, Cheyenne South received a similar call, later confirmed to be from the same phone number.
“Officers cleared both buildings, and no credible threat was found,”
Cheyenne police said on Facebook
. “We have learned that similar calls were made to other schools across Wyoming. We will continue to work closely with our partners to investigate the incidents.”
