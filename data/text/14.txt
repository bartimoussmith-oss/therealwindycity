Yellowstone National Park lifts remaining fishing restrictions for Firehole, Madison, Gibbon rivers

YELLOWSTONE, Wyo. — Yellowstone National Park announced on Thursday that, effective Sept. 4, the remaining fishing restrictions on the Firehole, Madison and Gibbon (downstream of Norris Campground) rivers and all associated tributaries will be lifted due to cooling weather and water temperatures.
A release from the park says that anglers will be able to fish those rivers, in addition to most other park rivers, streams and lakes daily from sunrise to sunset, as specified in the
2026 Fishing Regulations Booklet
.
The release states that full fishing closures on the aforementioned rivers were initially implemented on July 15. They were then scaled back to partial restrictions on Aug. 28 as conditions improved.
The restrictions and closures were necessary, the release says, during a period of low water flows and water temperatures exceeding 68 degrees Fahrenheit (20 degrees Celsius), which can be lethal to fish.
“Staff will continue to monitor the water temperatures,” the release states. “Temporary restrictions or closures may be implemented depending on condition assessments.”
