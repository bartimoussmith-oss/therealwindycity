Cheyenne man arrested for reportedly inhaling air duster, crashing car at shopping center

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A man was charged in Cheyenne this week with crashing his car multiple times, destroying thousands of dollars’ worth of property, after reportedly inhaling air duster.
The suspect, Jared Talkington, born in 1984, is facing one felony charge: property destruction. The charge comes with a potential maximum penalty of 10 years in prison. He was also cited with violating a protection order and failing to fulfill his duty upon colliding with an unattended vehicle or property. Those misdemeanor charges could tack an extra six months and 20 days to his maximum potential sentence.
Until Talkington is pleads or is found guilty, he is presumed innocent.
According to Cheyenne Police Department officers in Talkington’s criminal affidavit, they were called to the shopping center at
3617 Lincolnway
at around 7:30 p.m. Monday after reports of a man crashing his Subaru car into a fence belonging to Murdoch’s. The car was caught in the fence and, upon backing out, ripped its own front bumper off.
The car reportedly drove through the plaza and left. When officers arrived, they saw multiple feeders that had been on display outside the store were damaged as well as the fence. The bumper was abandoned on the ground. Each feeder was worth approximately $500.
About an hour later, officers received another call reporting that a man was at Sportsman’s Warehouse — located right next to the Murdoch’s — huffing air duster inside the store and acting “crazy.” The caller noted that he arrived to the store in a Subaru missing its front bumper.
Officers arrived and made contact with the suspect, Talkington, as well as store employees, who showed them a can of air duster that Talkington had reportedly inhaled inside the store’s bathroom. The bathroom was also reportedly left a mess.
Talkington was placed under arrest. Officers reportedly found another can of air duster on his person while escorting him to a patrol car.
Talkington reportedly said he knew he hit something with his car, but he didn’t know what and had checked his car for damages before parking it. He insisted he never left the area, despite officers not being able to find his car the first time they were at the plaza.
While photographing Talkington’s Subaru, a woman claiming to be Talkington’s ex-girlfriend reportedly arrived on the scene and told officers that he had called her after the accident asking for help, violating a protection order she had placed against him.
Since being arrested, Talkington has been issued a $3,000 cash bond and was given an Oct. 29 preliminary hearing date in Laramie County District Court.
