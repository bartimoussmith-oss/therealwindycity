There’s No Place Like Home for the Holidays!
Historic Governors’ Mansion Celebrates Christmas Through the Ages
by

November 6, 2026

The magic of Christmas past is coming to Cheyenne, and you’re invited to celebrate!
Get ready to step back in time and experience the sights, sounds, and sparkle of the holiday season as Cheyenne’s Historic Governor’s Mansion welcomes visitors to its annual
Tinsel Through Time
Christmas exhibit!
This year’s theme,
“Home for the Holidays,”
brings the warmth and wonder of Christmases gone by to life, with the Mansion’s rooms beautifully decorated in holiday styles from different eras. From nostalgic ornaments to timeless Christmas traditions, every room offers a little holiday magic and a glimpse into how Wyoming families celebrated the season through the years.
Kick Off the Season with a Festive Opening Reception!
The holiday fun officially begins on
Saturday, November 21, from 4–7 p.m.
, when the community is invited to a special Opening Reception.
Enjoy an evening filled with festive decorations, delicious food, music, live entertainment, and plenty of Christmas cheer! It’s the perfect opportunity to gather with family and friends and soak up the holiday spirit.
Can’t Make the Opening? There’s Plenty of Time to Visit!
The Christmas magic doesn’t end after opening night!
Tinsel Through Time: Home for the Holidays
will remain on display through December 24, giving visitors plenty of opportunities to explore the Mansion’s holiday transformation.
Here’s everything you need to know:
What:
Tinsel Through Time – Home for the Holidays
Where:
Historic Governors’ Mansion, 300 E. 21st St.
Opening Reception:
Saturday, November 21 | 4–7 p.m.
Exhibit Dates:
November 21 – December 24
Regular Hours:
Tuesday–Saturday | 9 a.m.–5 p.m.
Admission:
FREE and open to the public!
Whether you’re a history lover, a Christmas enthusiast, or simply looking for a fun and festive outing with the family, this beloved Cheyenne holiday tradition is sure to put you in the Christmas spirit.
So gather your loved ones, bring your holiday cheer, and come experience a little Christmas magic through the ages!
There’s no place like home for the holidays — especially at the Historic Governors’ Mansion!
