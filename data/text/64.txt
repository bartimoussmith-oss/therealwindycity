Cooldown and chances for rain ahead for weekend in Cheyenne area

CHEYENNE, Wyo. –– Cooler temperatures and chances for some rain are ahead in Cheyenne after days of above-average fall conditions.
According to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne, Friday will see a high of 72 degrees under cloudy skies, which are expected to clear by noon. Breezy winds around 10–15 mph are likely. The overnight period will see wind gusts kick up to 30 mph, with the low dipping to 37 degrees.
Saturday will be mostly sunny and windy, with a high of 47 degrees and gusts of up to 30 mph. Overnight, temperatures will dip to 34 degrees amid calmer winds.
Sunday could see slight chances of showers under mostly cloudy skies. A nigh near 50 degrees and mild breezes are likely. Showers and a low of 35 degrees are expected overnight.
Monday will see chances of showers before noon. Skies will eventually clear, and the high will be 53 degrees.
Tuesday through Thursday are expected to see highs in the mid- to upper 50s under mostly sunny skies.
