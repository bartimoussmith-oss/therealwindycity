Microsoft annexation referendum misses November ballot due to invalid signatures

M. Lee Hasenauer collects a signature for a petition to force a referendum on Cheyenne's July 13 decision to annex 3,500 acres into the city for a Microsoft Data Center Campus July 20, 2026 (Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — An August petition to force a referendum vote on
the Cheyenne City Council’s July annexation of 3,500 acres of land to be developed into a Microsoft data center campus
did not receive the required number of signatures to appear on voters’ November ballots. Of the 3,291 signatures collected, only 1,796 were verified as belonging to eligible voters, falling well short of the 2,740-signature requirement.
Unhappy with the council’s decision to annex the large property southeast of Cheyenne that Microsoft was planning to purchase, a dedicated group of Laramie County residents got together and petitioned for a referendum. If they had collected enough signatures, Cheyenne residents would have been able to vote for or against the annexation, taking the decision out of the City Council’s hands.
They only had around three weeks to collect the requisite number of signatures — enough to represent 10% of Cheyenne’s 2024 voting population. By the end of that period, organizers estimated that they had. They sent the signatures to the Cheyenne clerk’s office to be verified and counted.
Now, a week after the passing of the 2026 election, the clerk announced that only a portion of the collected signatures were valid. 773 belonged to Laramie County residents who lived outside city limits and 539 belonged to city residents who were not registered to vote, a release by Clerk Kylie Soden announced. Another 122 signatures were illegible, did not meet address requirements or were duplicates. 61 were excluded from the beginning for not meeting petition requirements.
Only 1,796 signatures belonged to registered voters in Cheyenne, falling 944 votes short of the required amount and nearly 1,500 short of what organizers estimated they had collected.
The annexation will remain in place as the council passed it July 13.
The petition, including all signatures, can be viewed in full at the City of Cheyenne website
here
.
