Pine Bluffs Town Hall (Town of Pine Bluffs photo)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Pine Bluffs voters have chosen familiar faces for three open government seats this election.
Incumbent Mayor Dennis Michaud was challenged by Councilor Janna Kestner. Michaud earned 263 votes, around 59%, compared to Kestner’s 165 votes in the primary. Michaud also led Kestner in August’s primary election with 222 votes to 100.
Incumbent Councilors Amy Currie and David Crouse also sought reelection. They received 380 votes and 375 votes, respectively. They were the sole candidates for the two seats in August’s primary election.
