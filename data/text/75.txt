CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Wyoming Gov. Mark Gordon will attend and speak at an event highlighting the future of the trades at Laramie County Community College on Oct. 28. The event, entitled “Wyoming’s Next Generation of Skilled Talent,” will be a moderated panel discussion exploring early career exposure, clearer pathways to the trades and how education and industry can help young people pursue and succeed in skilled careers.
The event will take place from 12:30 to 2:15 p.m. at the Clay Pathfinder ANB Bank Leadership Conference Center in room 108/109. Gordon will appear around 1:30 p.m.
The event, sponsored by Bechtel, LCCC, Western Community College, the Wyoming Business Alliance and the Wyoming Innovation Partnership, will also feature remarks from  LCCC President Joe Schaffer and Bechtel Senior Vice President and Head of Construction Walker Kimball.
