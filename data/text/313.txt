CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The former WYDOT director who ousted incumbent Rep. Steve Johnson for the Republican nomination for Wyoming’s House District 8 seat secured his victory in Tuesday’s general election.
Luke Reiner received 3,120 votes to Democrat challenger Brenda J. Lyttle’s 927.
In August, Reiner, a former Wyoming Department of Transportation director, defeated Johnson
by a vote of 2,062 to 970.
In addition to his WYDOT experience, Reiner is a certified public accountant and retired major general who previously served as adjutant general of the Wyoming Military Department. He advocated for backfilling local revenue if property tax reductions pass to protect funding for schools, infrastructure and emergency services, and opposes the sale of public lands to preserve outdoor recreation.
