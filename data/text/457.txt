CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Yard waste pickups will conclude Nov. 19 as
the City of Cheyenne’s residential yard waste program
shuts down for the remainder of the year.
The last week of the yard waste program will be Nov. 16–19. The season’s final branch pickup will be Nov. 19. To schedule one last pickup, call the the Sanitation Division at
307-637-6440
.
With the off-season approaching, the city reminds homeowners to remember the following:
Store yard waste containers in a secure location during the off-season out of the right-of-way, street or alleyway.
Clean out and keep containers empty until next season.
To report damage or to have a container removed, contact the Sanitation Division office.
Do not compact yard waste in containers.
Loosen contents with a shovel, rake or broom handle to ensure contents empty completely.
Only lawn trimmings, weeds, leaves, pinecones and pine needles are allowed in yard waste containers.
No branches, tumbleweeds or other wooden material are allowed in yard waste containers.
Even though pickups are ending for the year, the city will continue to accept yard waste drop-offs at the
Compost Facility
.
