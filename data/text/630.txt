Wyoming Democrats elect leadership for 69th Legislature
SHERIDAN (WNE) — The Wyoming Democratic Legislative Caucus has elected its leadership for the 69th Wyoming Legislature, retaining the same team that led the caucus during the 68th Legislature.
In the Senate, Sen. Mike Gierau, D-Jackson, will continue as senate minority leader, and Sen. Chris Rothfuss, D-Laramie, will serve as senate minority whip.
In the House, Rep. Mike Yin, D-Jackson, will continue as house minority leader, Rep. Karlee Provenza, D- Laramie, as house minority whip and Rep. Trey Sherwood, Laramie, as house minority caucus chair.
“People in Wyoming are worried about what it costs to see a doctor, keep a roof overhead and send their kids to a good school. We will keep showing up for them, working with anyone who wants to solve those problems, and supporting all the communities of Wyoming,” Yin said.
“The people of Wyoming have delivered a legislature that’s eager to do the work,” Provenza said. “I’m grateful for the opportunity to help lead us toward solutions that serve our great state.”
The caucus also welcomed Rep.-elect Britney Tennant of Cheyenne, who won the House District 11 seat.
“Britney earned this seat one door at a time, and Cheyenne is going to have a hardworking voice in the House,” Yin said. “We are glad to have her on the team.”
For more information, see
wyolegdems.org

WYDOT adds wildlife alerts to 511 Notify subscriptions
CHEYENNE (WNE) — Subscribers to the Wyoming Department of Transportation’s 511 Notify program will now receive wildlife alerts as part of recent upgrades to the system.
If a driver reports a wildlife-involved crash to the Wyoming Highway Patrol or via the Wyoming 511 mobile app, WYDOT now sends a 511 Notify alert to subscribers of that section of highway to warn drivers of potential wildlife hazards in the area.
Seasonal Wildlife Migration Alerts have also been implemented, using historic crash data to alert subscribers traveling through high-risk corridors during peak seasonal migration periods to lower the likelihood of animal/vehicle collisions.
WYDOT regularly works with the Wyoming Game and Fish Department to identify wildlife infrastructure projects around the state, but small, low-cost innovations like these can also make a difference in reducing collisions.
Current subscribers will start getting these new alerts automatically as part of existing 511 Notify subscriptions.
511 Notify sends road and travel information via email and/or text alerts to subscribers based on preferences set by the user during the sign-up process.
Sign up for a new account or modify an existing account online at wyoroad. info/511/511NotifyAnnouncement.html.
The 511 Notify program is part of WYDOT’s road and travel information network, which can be found online at wyoroad. info or on the Wyoming 511 mobile app.

Wyoming’s average gas prices fall 20.6 cents
CHEYENNE (WNE) — Average gasoline prices in Wyoming have fallen 20.6 cents per gallon in the last week, averaging $3.85 per gallon Monday, according to
GasBuddy.com
’s survey of 494 stations in Wyoming.
Prices in Wyoming are 59.2 cents per gallon lower than a month ago, and stand $1.01 per gallon higher than a year ago. The national average price of diesel has decreased 18.7 cents per gallon compared to a week ago, and stands at $5.999 per gallon.
According to GasBuddy price reports, the cheapest station in Wyoming was priced at $3.35 per gallon Sunday, while the most expensive was $4.99, a difference of $1.64 per gallon.
The national average price of gasoline has fallen 21.3 cents per gallon in the last week, averaging $3.74 on Monday.
The national average is down 55.8 cents per gallon from a month ago, and stands 71.3 cents per gallon higher than a year ago, according to GasBuddy data.

Rep.-elect Gray elected to House Republican Policy Committee
CHEYENNE (WNE) — Wyoming Secretary of State and U.S. Rep.-elect Chuck Gray on Wednesday was elected by his freshman class to be their representative to the House Republican Policy Committee.
The House RPC is the primary advisory and policy development arm for the House Republican Conference. It serves as an internal forum for Republican members of the U.S. House of Representatives to discuss legislative proposals and develop conservative policy solutions.
“I’m greatly honored and humbled to be elected by my freshman Republican peers to the Republican Policy Committee. As we establish the policy direction for Republicans in this new Congress, I will continue to be focused on advancing our Wyoming conservative values to establish prosperity for this and future generations,” Gray said in a news release.
Gray will be sworn in to the U.S. House of Representatives on Jan. 3, 2027.

Report shows Wyoming sales, use tax collections are up
CHEYENNE (WNE) — Statewide sales and use tax collections continue to show strength, according to the Wyoming Insight, which serves as an energy index and business indicators report for the state
Total sales and use tax collections in October summed to $104 million, $9.1 million more (+9.5%) than October last year.
“Sales and use tax collections in October improved year-over-year for the ninth month in a row,” said Dylan Bainer, principal economist for Wyoming’s Economic Analysis Division, in a news release.
Collections from the retail trade sector, which is Wyoming’s largest sector in terms of sales and use tax collections, improved the most, up $5.2 million (+13.8%). The mining sector also had a strong month in October, up $3.6 million (+40.4%).
Wyoming Insight also reported that monthly oil prices surpassed $100/bbl for the third time in 2026, and natural gas prices are still under $3/MMBtu.
The West Texas Intermediate monthly average price for crude oil was $102.07 per bbl. in October, 4.9% more than a month ago and 67.6% more than October 2025. Average active oil rigs were 14 in October and the conventional gas rig count was two.
In October of last year, there were six oil rigs and five gas rigs.
The October 2026 natural gas price at the Henry Hub averaged $2.99 per million British thermal units (MMBtu), $0.04 more than last month, but $0.21 less than October 2025. The Opal Hub natural gas price averaged $2.64/MMBtu in October, $0.03 less than last month, but $0.17 more than October 2025.
The October 2026 issue of the Wyoming Insight is available at the state of Wyoming’s Economic Analysis Division website, ai.wyo. gov/divisions/economic-analysis.
