Ex-hotel employee charged after reportedly cashing six fraudulent paychecks

(Gregory Hirst, Oil City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A Cheyenne woman was arrested earlier this month on six counts of forgery after depositing multiple fraudulent checks she claimed were from her former employer, a local hotel. Over the course of a month, she reportedly defrauded the hotel of over $4,000.
According to the Cheyenne Police Department, the suspect is Sheri Gertie, born in 1969. Her six felony forgery charges — one per check — combine for a maximum potential penalty of 60 years in prison. Gertie is presumed innocent until pleading or being found guilty.
Officers were contact Sept. 25 by the owner of the Red Lion Hotel in Cheyenne after he became suspicious of his former employee, Gertie, and claimed she was cashing in multiple copies of her last paycheck, each time with slight changes.
He reportedly provided police with copies of the checks. Each was worth $715.71. The original, issued by the hotel, was real. Six others, identical to the first but with their check number changed, were reportedly copies forged to appear different.
All of them were deposited to a local bank throughout the month of September, beginning Sept. 2 and ending Sept. 23. In total, Gertie reportedly deposited $4,294.96 worth of unapproved payroll from the hotel, leading to her arrest.
Gertie was issued a $6,000 cash bond and will enter Laramie County Circuit Court for a preliminary hearing Nov. 4.
