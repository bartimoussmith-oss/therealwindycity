CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Gov. Mark Gordon has ordered the Wyoming State Flag to be lowered to half-staff at the State Capitol and in Sweetwater County on Friday, Nov. 13 to honor the life of former Wyoming State Rep. and Sen. Raymond Sarcletti.
Sarcletti represented Sweetwater County in the Wyoming House from 1993 to 1995 and the Wyoming Senate from 1995 to 1998.
He died on Oct. 30, 2026.
