CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Laramie County sheriff’s deputies detained two people early Saturday after a shooting sent one person to the hospital Friday night,
according to the sheriff’s office
.
The victim arrived at Cheyenne Regional Medical Center around 9:30 p.m. Friday and reported being shot, deputies said. The person was shot in the abdomen and was still being treated Saturday.
Deputies learned around 1 a.m. that the shooting happened on the 1500 block of West College Drive. They responded to the area, investigated the scene and detained two people, who remained detained as the investigation continued.
Deputies cleared the scene this morning after a large law enforcement presence in the area. The sheriff’s office said the incident appears to be isolated and poses no active threat to the community.
