Cheyenne in store for warm, windy Tuesday as mild weather continues this week

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Cheyenne can expect warm temperatures and gusty winds today as a dry weather pattern settles across southeast Wyoming.
Cheyenne is looking at mostly sunny skies and a high near 64 degrees today, according to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne. Winds from the west will increase from 10–20 mph to 20–30 mph this afternoon, with gusts reaching as high as 40 mph. Breezy conditions will linger tonight under mostly clear skies, with west-northwest winds gusting at up to 30 mph alongside a low around 36.
Across greater Laramie County and the adjacent plains, downslope winds will push daytime highs into the mid- to upper 60s, with a few spots possibly touching 70. Stronger winds across the county and High Plains will include gusts of up to 50 mph, while the wind corridors along Interstates 25 and 80 carry a slim chance for higher speeds.
The NWS in Cheyenne reported that the mixture of gusty winds and dry ground will create elevated fire weather concerns this afternoon, though conditions are not expected to reach red flag levels. Winds will begin to calm by this evening.
Mild and dry conditions are expected to continue for most of the next week. Breezy to windy conditions are expected today, while Wednesday will remain breezy. (National Weather Service graphic)
Veterans Day will bring sunny skies and slightly cooler conditions. Cheyenne will reach a high near 61 with west-northwest winds of 10–15 mph and gusts of up to 25 mph. The overnight low Wednesday will drop to around 37 under mostly clear skies.
Warmth builds back on Thursday and Friday as a high-pressure ridge crosses the state. Cheyenne can expect sunny skies with a high near 66 degrees Thursday and 65 degrees Friday. Overnight lows will remain in the mid-30s.
A weak weather system moving into western Wyoming will bring extra clouds and slightly cooler temperatures over the weekend. Cheyenne will see highs settle near 59 degrees Saturday through Monday under mostly sunny skies, while rain and snow chances remain limited to the Snowy and Sierra Madre mountain ranges in southern Carbon County.
Detailed Forecast
Today:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 64. Windy, with a west wind at 10–20 mph increasing to 20–30 mph in the afternoon. Winds could gust as high as 40 mph.
Tonight:
Mostly clear, with a low around 36. Breezy, with a west-northwest wind at 15–20 mph and gusts as high as 30 mph.
Veterans Day:
Sunny, with a high near 61. West-northwest wind at 10–15 mph, with gusts as high as 25 mph.
Wednesday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 37. West-northwest wind at around 10 mph.
Thursday:
Sunny, with a high near 66. Northwest wind at 5–10 mph.
Thursday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 34.
Friday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 65.
Friday Night:
Mostly cloudy, with a low around 35.
Saturday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 59.
Saturday Night:
Mostly cloudy, with a low around 34.
Sunday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 59.
Sunday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 34.
Monday:
Sunny, with a high near 59.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
