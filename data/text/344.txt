Pair of teens arrested in Cheyenne for car burglary after nighttime chase

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Two young Cheyenne men were arrested and charged last month after reportedly robbing a series of cars in the middle of the night. Officers had to catch the pair across multiple streets after they split up and ran away upon being confronted.
One of the suspects, Kyrin Arthur, born in 2008, is being charged with felony robbery and resisting arrest. The burglary charge comes with a maximum potential penalty of 10 years in prison. He and the other man involved are considered innocent until pleading or being found guilty.
According to police, officers were dispatched to the area of Custer Street and Pawnee Avenue in north Cheyenne at around 3:45 a.m. Oct. 13 to reports of two masked teens checking vehicles.
When they arrived, officers began looking around the area and soon spotted one of the teens duck behind a pickup truck a few streets from where the initial report came from, the affidavit states. The officer exited their vehicle and walked around it to find the teen crouching down. When they noticed the officer, they reportedly ran off before they could be caught.
Another officer reportedly found Arthur elsewhere in the neighborhood. He also attempted to flee, according to the affidavit, but was successfully apprehended. A third officer had also chased Arthur, but stopped when they noticed a 9mm Sig Sauer P365 handgun and a cellphone where Arthur was fleeing from. A fanny pack Arthur was wearing reportedly contained multiple 9mm rounds as well.
While officers looked for the other teen suspect, dispatch received a report about someone trespassing in a backyard nearby. According to police, an area homeowner saw the suspect in the backyard and confronted him. However, when he looked up at a drone that the police had just launched to monitor the situation, the teen ran off, dropping his backpack.
He was eventually captured multiple houses away in a backyard. The backpack he had been carrying reportedly contained multiple items, including a Garmin Inreach satellite communicator, a Leupold rangefinder and a box of ammunition. By calling a phone number listed in the Garmin, police were able to track down its owner — a man who, preparing for a hunting trip the next day, put a backpack in his car only to find it stolen soon after.
The hunter’s wife gave police doorbell camera footage of a masked teen wearing the same clothes as Arthur breaking into the car. According to police, that theft had occurred in a neighborhood nearly 3 miles away from where Arthur and the other teen were arrested.
Arthur has a preliminary hearing to decide if his case should be taken up by the Laramie County District Court on Nov. 12. He was issued a $10,000 cash bond.
