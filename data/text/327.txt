Harriet Hageman waves to a crowd of supporters during her election night party Tuesday night at the Cheyenne Frontier Days Event Center in Cheyenne. Hageman defeated her incumbent opponent, Liz Cheney, in Tuesday's primary election to become the Republican nominee for Wyoming's lone U.S. House of Representatives seat in November's general election. (Lisa Hushbeck/Oil City News)
CASPER, Wyo. — U.S. Rep. Harriet Hageman has defeated Democrat James Byrd to succeed retiring Sen. Cynthia Lummis in the U.S. Senate.
The Associated Press called the race at 7 p.m. with zero votes counted and zero percent of the votes in. Polls closed at 7 p.m.
Vote totals will be available on the live results pages
here
.
Hageman became the Republican Party’s nominee to the seat in August after defeating four challengers: Jill M. Edwards, John Holtz, Sam Mead and Jimmy Skovgard.
Hageman, a vocal ally of President Donald Trump, gained national attention in 2022 after defeating incumbent Rep. Liz Cheney in the Republican primary before winning the general election with nearly 70% of the vote. She said she aims to continue advancing Trump’s “America First” agenda in the Senate.
Results are unofficial until certified by county and state canvassing boards.
