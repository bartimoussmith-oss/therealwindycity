CHEYENNE, Wyo. — How Laramie County School District 1 uses its federal grants money doesn’t have to be mysterious. The district is hosting two community meetings Nov. 17 to demonstrate to the public how it applies for federal grants and how grant money is used.
Parents, staff and community members are invited to attend the district’s two upcoming Federal Programs Constituency meetings to learn about the district’s federal grants and provide input on the programs and services they support. The two meetings will take place Nov. 17 at the LCSD1
Administration Building
‘s room 306, the first from 7:30 to 8:30 a.m. and the second from 4 to 5 p.m.
While the community gets the opportunity to learn about how the district’s federal grant application process works, the district gets to gather feedback from residents for its Consolidated Grant Application.
Topics will include federal grants supporting students and families, school improvement, teaching and leadership development, English learners, student support and academic enrichment, special education and career and technical education, the district said.
For more information, call LCSD1 Director of School Leadership Eric Jackson at
307-771-2254
.
