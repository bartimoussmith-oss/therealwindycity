Cheyenne man charged with breaking into ex’s home, attacking other man with edged weapon

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A man was arrested in Cheyenne last week after reportedly breaking into his ex-partner’s home and physically assaulting another man. He is now facing two felonies and an additional misdemeanor charge.
The suspect, Chad Langaniere, faces an aggravated assault and battery charge and an unlawful entry into an occupied structure charge. The two felonies are punishable with a combined maximum of 20 years in prison. He was also cited with misdemeanor property destruction. Laganiere is considered innocent on all the charges until he pleads or is found guilty.
According to police, officers were dispatched to a home in east Cheyenne at around 1 a.m. Nov. 5 to reports of a forced entry and physical disturbance. Upon arrival, a woman told them that her ex, Laganiere, had broken into her home and attacked another man who was there. He was staying there, she said, because she was scared of Laganiere.
The man she was with was reportedly injured. Officers reported in Laganiere’s criminal affidavit that the man was left with a 3-inch cut across his eye. Part of his eyelid was also cut off and the entire eye area was swollen shut. The injury, officers said, looked like it came from an edged weapon.
The woman later showed officers phone messages telling Laganiere that she was scared of him, that she was planning on staying in a safe house and that she didn’t want him near her children. The texts also reportedly showed proof of physical abuse, as well as Laganiere threatening to burn her house down.
Laganiere will have a preliminary hearing in front of a Laramie County Circuit Court judge Nov. 13. As of now, his bond has been set at $20,000.
