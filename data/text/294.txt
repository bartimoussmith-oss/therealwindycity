Laramie County residents participate in early voting July 29, 2026 (Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Despite the early voting period being reduced from 45 days in 2022 to 28 in 2026, 2,000 more Laramie County voters participated in early voting this election cycle.
Early voting for the 2026 general election closed today, and the results indicate that democratic participation this year will eclipse previous years. According to Laramie County Clerk Debra Lee, 16,778 people — out of a total electorate of 45,016 — participated in advanced voting, either through early voting, an absentee ballot or military or overseas voting.
12,539 chose to vote early, which is 2,265 more than in the last midterms. 4,150 people voted with an absentee ballot via mail — 78 people fewer than in 2022. Forty-six more people in the military voted than in 2022, however.
These numbers add up to a much higher rate of participation in advanced voting than the last midterms. 30.44% of voters in Laramie County chose to cast their ballots early in 2022 compared to 37.27% in 2026.
County residents interested in voting who haven’t yet must do so the day of the election — tomorrow, Nov. 3.
Election Day polls will open Tuesday at 7 a.m. and close at 7 p.m. Unlike early voting, citizens must line up to vote at one of the following local election centers:
David R Romero Park
Storey Gym
Kiwanis Community House
North Christian Church
Laramie County Community College, CCI Building
Event Center at Archer
Pine Bluffs Community Center
Voters must bring a valid government-issued ID to be able to vote. Unregistered voters are able to register day-of.
Visit the
Laramie County clerk’s elections page on the Laramie County government website
for more information on voting.
