RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
October 26, 2026
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in City Council Chambers and via electronic conference meeting. Present were:
MAYOR – Patrick Collins COUNCIL MEMBERS – Dr. Michelle Aldrich, Dr. Kathy Emmons,
Ken Esquibel, Pete Laybourn, Mark Moody, Dr. Mark Rinne, Tom Segrave, Jeff White and
Lawrence Wolfe. Also present: Kylie Soden, City Clerk. The pledge of allegiance was recited.
Consent Agenda. (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion. There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two
other members is received. Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its
normal sequence on the agenda). Upon request by Mr. Laybourn, and pursuant to support received
by Mr. Moody and Mr. Wolfe, item #20 was removed from the Consent Agenda. Mr. White moved
to approve remaining items, seconded by Dr. Aldrich. Motion carried. Voting “yes” – all members
of the governing body.
[CA] Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on October 12, 2026. Motion (per
Consent Agenda) carried.
ORDINANCE – 2nd READING – Annexing to the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, land south
of US-30 and north of Commerce Circle. Reporting for Public Services Committee, Dr. Aldrich
moved to approve on second reading, seconded by Dr. Emmons. Motion carried. Voting “yes” –
all members of the governing body. Public comment was made by: Charles Miller.
ORDINANCE – 2nd READING – Amending the Official Zoning Map of the City of
Cheyenne, establishing the zoning classification of BP – Business Park for land annexed to the
City of Cheyenne, located south of Tate Road, north of Commerce Circle, and west of and adjacent
to Christensen Road. Dr. Aldrich moved to approve on second reading, seconded by Mr. Wolfe.
Mr. White moved to amend Section 5 by changing the established zoning classification from AG
to BP, seconded by Dr. Emmons. Motion to amend carried. Voting “yes” – all members of the
governing body. Main motion as amended carried. Voting “yes” – all members of the governing
body. Public comment was made by: Charles Miller. Comment was made by the following
member of the governing body: Mayor Collins.
ORDINANCE – 2nd READING – Annexing to the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, land south
of East Fox Farm Road, north of East Allison Road and east of and adjacent to south College
Drive. Dr. Aldrich moved to approve on second reading, seconded by Dr. Emmons. Motion
carried. Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body with the exception of Mr. Moody voting
“no”. Public comment was made by: Charles Miller. Comment was made by the following member
of the governing body: Mr. Moody.
