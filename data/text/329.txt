CHEYENNE, Wyo. — General election polls close at 7 p.m. Tuesday, Nov. 3. Results are provided by the Laramie County Clerk’s Office.
Contested races include Laramie County Board of Commissioners; Laramie County sheriff; Cheyenne City Council Wards I, II and III; Pine Bluffs mayor and Town Council; Laramie County School Districts 1 and 2; and Laramie County Community College Board of Trustees.
Also included are special district races, judicial retentions, the 2% lodging tax and the Fifth Penny tax.
Results are unofficial until certified by county and state canvassing boards.
