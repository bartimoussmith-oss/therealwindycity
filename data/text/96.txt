CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Seasonal closures of restrooms at City of Cheyenne parks and athletic facilities begin Wednesday, Oct. 28.
According to the City of Cheyenne, the closures, enforced every year since 2010, help save around $18,000 in utility costs during the winter months.
Portable restrooms will be installed at Lions Park, Holliday Park and Cahill Park until the regular restrooms reopen in the spring.
See the
City of Cheyenne Parks Department page on the city’s website
for more information.
