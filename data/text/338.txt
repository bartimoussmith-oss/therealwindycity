CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A significant police presence, which included the Cheyenne Police/Laramie County Joint SWAT Team, in a rural Cheyenne neighborhood Wednesday morning was the result of the high-stakes arrest of a strangulation suspect, Cheyenne police said.
At around 8 a.m. Wednesday,
Cowboy Road
in the far-northeast part of Cheyenne became flooded with police and sheriff’s vehicles, including heavy-duty MRAPs. Cheyenne police said on Facebook that the response was for an arrest.
Later, the department shared that 44-year-old Jacob Miller was arrested for felony strangulation of a household member. He was arrested without incident and law enforcement soon after began clearing the area.
See the Cheyenne Police Department’s Facebook page for more information.
