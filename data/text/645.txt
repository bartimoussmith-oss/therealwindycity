GREEN RIVER STAR — Sweetwater County is working on developing language to help regulate potential future construction of data centers, and the public has the opportunity to weigh in.
"The goal really is to get as much public comment as we can on this, and then take that into consideration before we adopt final regulations," County Land Use Director Eric Bingham explained.
Bingham presented the new regulatory language to the Sweetwater County Board of County Commissioners during the Nov. 17 meeting, where the commissioners voted unanimously to move forward with the notice of intent to approve the language amendments, which kicks off a 45-day public comment period.
The language amendments will add to the county's existing zoning resolutions. Bingham explained that the new language sets up a tiered system based on megawatt usage for data centers, which are referred to as "High-Intensity Computing Facilities" in the amendments.
Tier one would include facilities under five megawatts, which would be permitted under regular industrial zoning. Larger facilities would go into tiers two and three, which would require conditional use permits and would have to meet certain standards based on impact.
"We look at things such as water and power and impacts on public roads," Bingham explained regarding impacts that would have to be considered for larger data centers.
Bingham added that another major factor being considered is noise requirements, and he noted that the county is pulling from some existing regulations from wind farms. Other areas being considered include impacts on wildlife and traffic and impacts coming from lighting or glare.
"We've also brought in reclamation requirements," Bingham said, explaining that he heard concerns about facilities being abandoned due to technology changes, so people wanted something set in stone for reclamation.
The proposed regulation language has also been reviewed by two private attorneys in Cheyenne who have expertise in data center regulations, according to Bingham.
The documents presenting the specific language amendments explain that the amendments are designed to "mitigate potential adverse effects of High-Intensity Computing Facilities while allowing for development opportunities in Sweetwater County."
Copies of the proposed zoning resolutions can be found on the county's website at
sweetwatercounty.gov
under the Planning and Zoning Department webpage, or can be requested from the Planning and Zoning Department.
Members of the public can comment on the proposed regulations by emailing
landuse@sweetwatercounty.gov
, or by writing to or faxing the Sweetwater County Land Use Department.
Public comments must be received before Jan. 4.
The county commissioners are set to have a public hearing at the Tuesday, Jan. 19 meeting, during which they will take comments and consider adoption of the new regulatory language.
