YODER, Wyo. — Wyoming Senate District 3 Senator
Cheri Steinmetz
will host a community meeting in Yoder’s
Community Building
on Nov. 16. There, attendees can hear directly from different state agencies regarding community health, welfare and safety.
According to a post by Steinmetz, representatives from the Wyoming Department of Environmental Quality, Wyoming Department of Transportation and Wyoming Department of Health will be in attendance. They’ll be able to answer the community’s questions about the issues affecting the Yoder, Goshen County and Laramie County communities.
The meeting is open to the public,
Steinmetz said on Facebook
. The meeting begins at 5:30 p.m.
