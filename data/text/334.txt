Cheyenne meat processor recipient of $169K Department of Agriculture grant
by
Garrett Grochowski
November 4, 2026

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — As a part of a nationwide initiative to support meat processing operations, the United States Department of Agriculture is distributing $908,216 in grant funds to three Wyoming meat processors. One of those businesses, Cheyenne’s Home on the Range Meat Processing, gets $169,112.
More than $57 million is being distributed across 40 states through the USDA’s Meat and Poultry Expansion Program. In Wyoming, that money is going to expanding processors’ capacity and creating new and better markets, U.S. Secretary of Agriculture Brooke Rollins said in a USDA press release.
“Under President Trump, this administration is delivering for the men and women who feed our nation. Now that these funds are moving, ranchers will have more options close to home, more competition will return to the market and more of the value of American-raised beef will stay in rural communities,” Rollins said.
USDA Rural Development Wyoming State Director Tyler Harrison added that supporting local operations helps support competition. In Wyoming, that monetary support will go to Cody’s Wyoming Legacy Meats, Thayne’s Star Valley Meat Block & Cold Storage and Cheyenne’s Home on the Range Meat Processing.
Home on the Range will use its funds to purchase specialized processing equipment and expand its processing capacity.
“This investment will help increase production of nitrate-free smoked meats, including bacon, ham, smoked poultry, dehydrated dog treats and various other specialty products,” the USDA said. “This project is expected to save seven jobs and create two rural jobs.”
