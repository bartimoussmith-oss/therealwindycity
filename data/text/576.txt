The new Dunkin' location on Pershing Avenue in Cheyenne Oct. 22, 2026 (Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Cheyenne’s new freestanding Dunkin’ location on Pershing Avenue officially opens tomorrow, Nov. 17. During the coffee chain’s grand opening Nov. 21, the first 100 guests in line will be rewarded with free coffee for 100 days.
Read more about the long-awaited Pershing Avenue Dunkin’
here
.
The new restaurant marks the brand’s first traditional, stand-alone location in Wyoming. There are currently locations inside F.E. Warren Air Force Base and inside a Horse Palace Location south of Cheyenne; neither are accessible to every Cheyenne resident.
After a few days of operation, the Dunkin’ will begin celebrating its grand opening Saturday. The 9–11 a.m. celebration will feature free merchandise giveaways; free coffee for 100 days to the first 100 customers; an appearance by Dunkin’s mascot, Cuppy; face painting; music from a live DJ; and more, Dunkin’ announced. The restaurant’s official ribbon cutting will take place at 10 a.m.
Guests can also enjoy a free donut with any purchase the entire day.
“We’re thrilled to bring Dunkin’ to Cheyenne and introduce the brand’s signature coffee, donuts and breakfast favorites to the community,” Bhavesh Patel, the new store’s owner, said. “As my first Dunkin’ restaurant, this opening is especially meaningful, and we’re excited to create local jobs, welcome guests through our doors and become a part of the Cheyenne community.”
The new 1,200-square-foot restaurant employs approximately 20 people and is open daily from 5 a.m. to 8 p.m.
