CASPER, Wyo. –– A decades-long tradition between the University of Wyoming and Colorado State University appears to be heading for a hiatus in the upcoming season.
According to a
report from the Coloradoan
, the traditional fight for the “Bronze Boot” will not be played in the 2027 season after a consecutive run of 81 seasons. The previous break took place during WWII, they said.
A University of Wyoming Athletics spokesperson reached by phone was also able to confirm the pause, as was UW’s Director of Athletics Tom Burman in an email to Oil City News.
“Very disappointed that we could not get a deal done with CSU,” he said. “We were willing to adjust our home schedule to play in War Memorial Stadium in 2027, but it didn’t work with CSU scheduling goals.”
CSU departed Mountain West for the Pac-12 starting in the 2026 season. The teams were able to organize a non-conference game, which the Rams won 35–13, earlier this season. According to the Coloradoan, they have agreed to play every year from 2028 through 2036.
According to Wyonation
, the Border War was first played in 1899 when UW traveled to CSU in Fort Collins, losing 12–0. The game is the longest continuous rivalry West of the Mississippi, they said. The famous Bronze Boot trophy was introduced in 1968.
UW was able to win the Boot in 2025, only to lose it to CSU this year in Fort Collins.
