PREP CROSS-COUNTRY: Laramie girls 3rd, Jensen 7th at Nike Northwest

Laramie High proved itself as the best girls cross-country team in Wyoming.
The Plainsmen proved that regionally, too.
Dorothy Jensen finished seventh individually and led Laramie to third-place as a team in the Championship division at the Nike Cross Northwest Regional on Saturday at The Course in Spokane Valley, Washington.
Jensen finished the 5-kilometer race in 17 minutes, 5.5 seconds as Laramie finished with 137 points — 27 points behind champions Oregon Distance Project.
Weeks ago, Jensen’s third-place finish at the Class 4A state meet helped Laramie win its first state title since 2011.
On Saturday, Flora Caputo-Wilkowski helped Laramie in 42nd when she finished in 17:59.6.
Keiran Giraldo earned 54th in 18:13.8 and Kelli McNiven was 60th in 18:16.2.
Noemie Lesueur Jaunasse and June Mason finished 89th and 90th, respectively, each in 18:36.9.
Claire White concluded the Laramie contingent in 109th in 18:47.9
