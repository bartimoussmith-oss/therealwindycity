Cheyenne Frontier Days CEO Tom Hirsig stepping down after 2027 rodeo

Cheyenne Frontier Days CEO Tom Hirsig (CFD Website)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The Cheyenne Frontier Days Board of Directors announced Thursday that Tom Hirsig, CEO of the annual rodeo and events series, will be stepping down from his role after the 2027 season. A new CEO has not yet been chosen, but more information will be shared by CFD when it is available.
The board recognized Hirsig for his unwavering commitment to the organization and the event, which it said he helped improve every year.
“While no one can truly replace Tom, we are committed to building upon the strong foundation he has helped create. We are incredibly grateful that he will remain with us through the 2027 show as we work together to ensure a thoughtful and successful transition,” Tad Herz, chairman of the Board, said in a
CFD release
.
Hirsig lives and breathes rodeo, the board said. His family holds a lifelong connection to the sport and the way of life it represents. Through his time with CFD, he has “championed the values of volunteerism, community involvement and Western heritage while helping position Cheyenne Frontier Days for continued success,” the release states.
The event has continued to grow in scale and reputation since Hirsig took on the role.
The CFD Board of Directors is retaining an external executive search firm to assist in identifying candidates fit to replace Hirsig when he departs next year.
“Leaders with Tom’s experience, passion and unique connection to this organization are exceedingly rare,” Herz said. “For that reason, we are conducting a comprehensive national search to help identify the next individual who can build upon the extraordinary work that has been accomplished during Tom’s tenure.”
The board added that Hirsig will remain family.
“Tom’s love for Cheyenne Frontier Days is woven into who he is, and Cheyenne Frontier Days will always be a part of him,” the board said. “We know that his support, friendship and connection to this organization will continue long after his tenure as CEO concludes. We look forward to celebrating his contributions over the next year and honoring the tremendous legacy he leaves for future generations.”
