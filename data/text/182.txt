Leonard D Leland
Leonard D Leland:
1935 – 2026
Leonard D. Leland, 91, of Ismay, Montana passed away Oct. 28, 2026, in Gillette, Wyoming, with daughter Tanya by his side.
Born Feb. 11, 1935, in Sidney, Montana to Leonard A. and Leone Leland, he graduated from Miles City in 1953.
In 1961, Leonard married Joanne Jacobson. Hand-in-hand, they poured their hearts into their family ranch, preserving a proud multi-generational legacy. He was a true stockman, equally at home in a tractor or working cattle. A man of deep, unwavering faith, he was a lifelong member of the ELCA Lutheran Church and served on the board for years. He also served 30+ years on school boards and 27 years on the Bank of Baker Board.
Inducted into the Montana Trap Shooting Hall of Fame, he and Joanne traveled the country for shoots. A pilot for 50+ years, the cockpit was his second home—whether checking cattle, flying cowboys to rodeos, transporting family, or finding Canadian fishing spots. He also loved skiing, hunting, and creating in his wood and metal shop.
Leonard is survived by daughter, Tanya (Jim) Krummreich; sons, Steve (Heidi) and Tim (Stephanie) Leland; grandchildren: Wesley, Wendy, Whitney, Jacob, Winter, Joel, Tessa; five great-grandchildren; sisters, Sharon Lorang, Marilyn (Jim) May; brothers-in-law, Mark (Jeanne), Paul (Diane) Jacobson. He was preceded by wife, Joanne; daughter, Julie; his parents-in-law, Reverend Clarence and Deflorence Jacobson; and brother-in-law, Carroll Lorang.
A celebration to honor his life hosted by his daughter and son-in-law, grandchildren, and the Jacobson family will be held at a later time.
Kenneth Francis Dessoye:
1955 – 2026
Kenneth Francis Dessoye, known to all who loved him as Kenny, passed away on October 25, 2026, in Chyanne, Wyoming. He was born on June 28, 1955, in Staten Island, New York, and carried that name and spirit with him throughout his life.
Kenny will be remembered with love by those who knew him best and by the family circle that remains to carry his memory forward. He is survived by his brother, Frank Dessoye; his daughter, Samantha Siegel and her husband  Zack and their children Sydney, Harry, and Ayden; his brothers, Donald  and Thomas Dessoye;  his sister, Jane Dessoye; and by his sister-in-law, Tondra Shong and her children Kadie Ehlert and Kaleb Shong. He also leaves behind sister-in-law, Phyllis Dessoye and their children, Derick and Melissa Dessoye,  His memory will also be cherished by his nephew, Brandon Dessoye; his niece, Rachael Legari; and his nephew, Michael Legari.
He was preceded in death by his father, Bernard “Bud” Dessoye Sr.; by his mother, Anne (Annie Cool) Dessoye; by his brother, Bernard Dessoye Jr.; and by his sister, Barbara Legari. Their memory remained part of the family story that shaped Kenny’s own life, and now he is reunited with them.
Kenny’s passing leaves a quiet space in the lives of those who called him family, but also a lasting gratitude for the years they shared with him. May his memory bring comfort to those who mourn him and remain a source of strength, warmth, and remembrance in the days ahead.
