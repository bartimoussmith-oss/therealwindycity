89-year-old Wyoming man killed in Casper neighborhood crash Saturday
by
Garrett Grochowski
October 23, 2026

CASPER, Wyo. — The victim of a fatal Saturday crash in Casper has been identified as 89-year-old Jack Hartnett. He was the passenger of a vehicle involved in a T-bone collision at a stop sign.
The Casper Police Department
announced Oct. 17 that it was investigating three separate incidents involving deceased individuals discovered around town or involved in accidents in the same 24-hour span
. At the time, none of the names of the deceased were shared.
The Wyoming Department of Transportation, which publishes findings from Wyoming Highway Patrol investigations, recently identified the deceased from one of the two car crashes from that day as Hartnett.
According to the report, Hartnett was the passenger of a Chevy Equinox traveling through the intersection of
Knollwood Drive and Mariposa Boulevard
. The Equinox reportedly failed to stop at a stop sign and was T-boned on its passenger side by a Dodge Ram pickup also passing through the intersection.
Two others were injured in the crash. A fourth individual was uninjured.
Hartnett was wearing his seat belt.
This story contains preliminary information as provided by the Wyoming Highway Patrol via the Wyoming Department of Transportation Fatal Crash Summary map. The agency advises that information may be subject to change.
