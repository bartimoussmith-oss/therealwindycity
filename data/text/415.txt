Cheyenne Central volleyball players celebrate a play from the sidelines at the WHSAA Championships (Sydney Denekamp, Oil City News)
CASPER, Wyo. — Cheyenne Central won third place at the WHSAA State Volleyball Championships at the Ford Wyoming Center in Casper this weekend, ending their season with a record of 22-14 overall.
The team’s energy levels were intense throughout the tournament as they fought through game after game. The Indians lost their first game against Cody, but went on to win against Evanston, Sheridan and Green River.
The team played hard, keeping spirits up through each play and cheering each other on. The pressure was high coming from cheers in the stands and sidelines, but the Indians stayed focused and worked as a team to claim their third place victory.
