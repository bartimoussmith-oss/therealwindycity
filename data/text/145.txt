The importance of voting in the HD 11 race
Dear Cheyenne,
On Tuesday, Nov. 3, voters in Cheyenne will have an opportunity to bring positive change to the statehouse by electing Britney Tennant to represent House District 11.
I’ve known Britney and have followed her dedicated community involvement for many years, and I promise you no one will work harder, listen more attentively or accomplish more for the people of her district and for the people of Wyoming than her.
The community mindset that has defined Britney’s life and work will translate into real results for all of us, and the only thing that will stand in the way of the “brighter day for Wyoming” she promises is if eligible voters stay away from the polls.
Sadly, the overall turnout rate of eligible voters in Wyoming during the last midterm general election in 2022 was only 44.4%, lower than the national average that year.
Exercising our right to vote is the most important obligation we all share as Americans and it’s one of the most powerful ways we can change our lives for the better.
If you are not registered to vote, you can register by mail or by going to the Laramie County Clerk’s office. You can also register in person during early voting, which is happening now, or at one of the seven Vote Centers on election day. Click on the Register to Vote page on the Laramie County Clerk’s website to learn what documents you need to bring.
Great candidates like Britney Tennant don’t come along very often and with all of the challenges we are facing as we struggle for affordability, access to health care, and access to our public lands, there is far too much at stake for our community and for our state to sit this one out.
Get registered, help other people get registered and vote for Britney Tennant on Nov. 3.
Milward Simpson
Cheyenne
Misleading mailers are out in HD 11 race
Dear Cheyenne,
Today we received a second mailer from the Americans for Prosperity trying to portray Britney Tennant, the outstanding candidate for House District 11, as a sinister threat to all we hold dear in Wyoming — complete with a distorted photo of Britney and lies about her positions.
To me, the only thing sinister is a group funded by the billionaire Koch family living in their luxury homes somewhere outside Wyoming, telling my family and neighbors who to vote for to represent us in the Wyoming State Capitol, just down the street from the homes where we actually live with our families.
Out-of-state billionaires don’t know what we need in Wyoming. Instead they spread false information about Britney, along with meaningless platitudes about her opponent, who, of course, will be working for them if elected, not us.
Britney knows what Wyoming needs and will represent us, not the billionaires. Check out her website where you’ll quickly see how great she’d be for HD 11. As for the AFP mailers, toss them in the recycling bin.
Kevin Boyce
Cheyenne
New apartments are great but pricing is not

Geraldine Starr
