Mild, dry weather and gusty winds forecast for Cheyenne through weekend

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Mild and dry conditions will take the spotlight for the weekend across southeast Wyoming, with unseasonably warm temperatures and increasing winds expected Sunday into early next week, according to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne.
An upper-level ridge of high pressure will dominate the region Saturday, bringing partly sunny skies accompanied by scattered high clouds. Light winds and near-to-slightly-above-average temperatures are expected throughout Laramie County, where highs are projected to reach the lower 60s along and east of the Interstate 25 corridor. Clear skies Saturday night will allow temperatures to dip into the lower 30s across Cheyenne, with colder outlying pockets possibly reaching the upper 20s.
Conditions will warm further Sunday as the ridge axis settles directly overhead, drawing in warmer air. Highs in Cheyenne and surrounding areas east of Interstate 25 are forecast to peak in the mid- to upper 60s, while far-eastern Wyoming could approach 70 degrees.
Breezier conditions are forecast to develop Sunday as mid-level winds between 40 and 50 mph mix down toward the surface. Stronger gusts will focus primarily along the higher elevations of the Laramie Range and typical wind-prone corridors. Meteorologists noted that while winds will increase through Monday, fire weather conditions are not anticipated at this time.
Sunday night will bring milder conditions than previous nights, with overnight lows holding in the upper 30s to lower 40s across Cheyenne.
The persistent ridge pattern will keep southeast Wyoming largely dry through the workweek. While a series of weak low-pressure systems will pass north of the region and usher in slightly cooler air, precipitation chances remain negligible. Daytime highs through Friday are expected to level off into the more seasonable mid-50s to low 60s, with overnight lows returning to the upper 20s and lower 30s.
