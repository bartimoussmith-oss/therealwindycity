Cheyenne Happenings (10/23, 10/24): ‘Rocky Horror Picture Show,’ Boo Ball, more

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The weekend is almost upon us, which means you made it through another week. You should be proud of yourself, and you should treat yourself to the myriad of activities happening throughout Cheyenne this weekend. From “The Rocky Horror Picture Show” to the 2026 Boo Ball, this is what’s happening this weekend in Cheyenne.
Story Time at Cheyenne Botanic Gardens
Paul Smith Children’s Village is hosting a Story Time event at the Cheyenne Botanic Gardens on Friday, beginning at 11 a.m.
“Join us Fridays at 11 a.m. for a story, a fun project, and shenanigans,”
the event page states
. “Meet under the blue dome during the summer. During the winter, meet us in the classroom in the Children’s Village.”
Happening Friday at 11 a.m.
Book Babies
On Friday, the Laramie County Library is hosting Book Babies, beginning at 10:15 a.m.
“Book Babies is a shorter, less-structured Early Literacy Class just for babies (best for ages 0–24 months)!”
the event page states
. “It’s a great time to bond with your baby and inspire a love of reading. You will learn lap bounces, tickle rhymes, movement songs, and fingerplays that you can do with your baby at home, too! We will typically read 1 developmentally appropriate book and explore 1 flannel story each week. It’s also a great time for babies to start learning how to socialize with other children. After the stories, we have Stay and Play where babies can play with developmentally appropriate toys while caregivers visit.”
Happening Friday at 10:15 a.m.
Behind the Curtain: Lunch & Learn
On Friday, the Cheyenne Little Theatre is hosting a Lunch and Learn, beginning at noon.
“Dive into the world of theatre and hear about the creative process behind the Cheyenne Little Theatre’s production of ‘Misery,'”
the event page states
. “Enter a raffle to win two free tickets.
Happening Friday at noon.
Nightmare on 17th: Dinner & A Haunt
On Friday, The Metropolitan Downtown is hosting Nightmare on 17th: Dinner & A Haunt, beginning at 6 p.m.
“Planning a night out at the Nightmare on 17th Street Haunted House?”
the event page asks
. “Start your evening with us at The Metropolitan Downtown! The Deal: Receive 1 FREE Haunted House ticket for that night with every entrée purchased! How to Claim: Reserve your table at The Metropolitan Downtown for any event date. Add promo code Haunted Dinner in the reservation notes. Enjoy your meal and get ready to get spooked! Reserve your spot now — if you dare!”
Happening Friday and Saturday at 6 p.m.
Cheyenne Little Theatre Players – ‘The Rocky Horror Picture Show’
On Friday and Saturday, the Cheyenne Little Theatre Players are presenting their thrilling version of “The Rocky Horror Picture Show,” happening at 7 and 10 p.m.
“In this cult classic, sweethearts Brad and Janet, stuck with a flat tire during a storm, discover the eerie mansion of Dr. Frank-N-Furter,”
the event page states
. “As their innocence is lost, Brad and Janet meet a houseful of wild characters, including a rocking biker and a creepy butler. Elaborate dance numbers and rock songs reveal Frank-N-Furter’s latest creation: a muscular man named ‘Rocky.’”
Happening Friday and Saturday at 7 and 10 p.m.
UW Football Watch Party
On Friday, Black Tooth Brewery is hosting a UW Football Watch Party, beginning at 9 p.m.
“University of Wyoming football watch party at a local brewery, with food trucks for select games,”
the event page states
.
Happening Friday at 9 p.m.
Tunnel of Terror: Tommy’s Express Cheyenne
This weekend, Tommy’s Express Car Wash, located at 121 W. 15th St., is hosting a haunted car wash.
“Brave the wash and leave with a car that’s hauntingly spotless,”
the event page states
. “Too excited for the fun and fright? Buy your ticket ahead of the event by visiting us on-site and get a $5 discount.”
Happening all day.
Wild West Trunk or Treat
On Saturday, the Cheyenne Frontier Days Old West Museum is hosting a Wild West Trunk or Treat, beginning at 1 p.m.
“Happy Halloween!” the event page states. “Come in costume and stop by the Old West Museum for candy, costumes, and family fun! The Associated General Contractors of Wyoming will bring heavy equipment for kids to explore! Nonprofits are invited to join the fun by bringing a trunk for our Trunk-or-Treat festivities. To reserve a trunk, please call
(307) 778-7243
.”
Happening Saturday at 1 p.m.
Haunted Library
On Saturday, Laramie County Library is hosting a Haunted Library from 5 to 9 p.m.
“Enter if you dare!”
the event page states
. “We are partnering with the Cheyenne Little Theatre Players to transform the Cottonwood and Willow rooms into a FREE haunted house full of thrills and chills. Admission is free, but you’ll need to sign up in advance to secure your time slot — walk-ins are welcome too, just beware… the line might be scarier than the ghosts! Sign up for a time slot online. Space is limited.
https://lp.constantcontactpages.com/ev/reg/cqjq9mh
. ⚠️ Warning: This event may be frightening for young children. Parents, please use your discretion when deciding if your child should participate.”
Happening Saturday at 5 p.m.
Dungeons on Draught
On Saturday, Freedom’s Edge Brewing Company is hosting Dungeons on Draught, beginning at 6 p.m.
“The Dungeons on Draught event offers a fun mix of tabletop role-playing and a craft beer tasting experience,”
the event page states
. “It’s an immersive, social event that allows players to live out their fantasy adventures while enjoying the relaxed and celebratory atmosphere of a local brewery. Free to play. 21+ only.”
Happening Saturday at 6 p.m.
2026 Boo Bash
On Saturday, the Little America Hotel is hosting the 2026 Boo Bash, beginning at 6 p.m.
“BooBall is a ‘Boo-tification’ Fundraiser held by the Leadership Cheyenne class to support their beautification project,”
the event page states
. “This 21+ Halloween party promises a spook-taclular evening full of fun, games, and prizes.” Tickets are $75 per person.
Happening Saturday at 6 p.m.
