GLENROCK, Wyo. — A juvenile died in a single-vehicle rollover wreck Tuesday, Nov. 10, near Glenrock.
According to reports from the Wyoming Highway Patrol, a pickup truck was heading south along Interstate 25 when it veered off the road to the left. The driver then overcorrected to the right, lost control of the truck and rolled into a ditch.
The WHP did not release the name of the minor, who was 17, but stated that they were the driver in the incident. Driver fatigue is being listed as a possible contributing factor for the crash, which occurred at 6:47 a.m., the WHP said.
This story contains preliminary information as provided by the Wyoming Highway Patrol via the Wyoming Department of Transportation Fatal Crash Summary map. The agency advises that information may be subject to change.
