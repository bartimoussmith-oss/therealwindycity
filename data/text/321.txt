CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Cheyenne voters have elected their next City Council, sending three candidates, including one incumbent, to City Hall.
Ward I voters chose Maren Kallas, who earned 2,623 votes compared to challenger Rhianna Brand’s 1,932.
Ward II voters chose Janelle Rose with 4,466 votes over challenger Lynn A. Storey-Huylar, who received 2,875.
Ward III voters selected incumbent Councilor Ken A. Esquibel to retain his seat. He received 2,698 votes while challenger Bill J. Bryant netted 2,639.
In August’s primary, there were nine candidates for Ward I, the most in any ward.
Kallas and Brand defeated a slew of challengers
who included Jimmy Chiro, Aaron Fiore, Travis French, Cameron Karajanis, J.C. Manalo, Landon Miller and Michael Reyes.
The Ward II primary election race had the fewest challengers, with
Rose and Storey-Huylar challenged only by Christopher Camargo
.
In Ward III,
Esquibel and Bryant had been challenged by Matthew Hurt and Joshua Christopherson
.
