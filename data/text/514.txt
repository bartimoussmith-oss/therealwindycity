Danielle McCort is LCSD1 student of the week for Nov. 16, 2026 (LCSD1)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Danielle McCort, an eighth grader at McCormick Junior High, is Laramie County School District 1’s student of the week. The honors student was chosen for her work ethic, dedication to extracurriculars and thoughtfulness.
According to LCSD1, McCort has an infectious enthusiasm for learning and challenging herself academically. Besides keeping high grades in her honors classes and asking thoughtful questions in class, McCort also participates in Math Counts and Science Olympiad.
She is also a performer in the Cheyenne Youth Symphony Orchestra and volunteers at the Wyoming State Museum. Her other hobbies include reading and baking, the latter of which comes in handy when she is helping her mother with her cake business.
McCort said she hopes she’ll become a forensic scientist after she finishes school, adding that science is her favorite subject.
She chose Mikayla Morrison as her favorite teacher because Morrison makes lessons fun, inclusive and relatable, LCSD1 said. Morrison’s humor and encouragement go a long way as well.
“She is a purely kind soul and will chase her dreams until she gets them,” Morrison said of McCort.
