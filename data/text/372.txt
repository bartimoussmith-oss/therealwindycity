Canvassing board certifies election results, handing LCSD2 recount vote to Jessen

The Laramie County Canvassing Board certifies the results of the 2026 General Election on Nov. 6, 2026 (Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The Laramie County Canvassing Board met at the Historic Courthouse in Cheyenne on Friday, Nov. 6 to receive updated election results and certify them. While margins in most races were wide enough to make the winner clear on Tuesday, a seat on the Laramie County School District 2 Board of Trustees came down to just a single vote, requiring a recount.
Three LCSD2 trustee seats were up for grabs this cycle. According to Tuesday’s unofficial results, With 1,345 and 1,175 votes respectively, trustee-elects Michael Lerwick and Kendra Roeder won their seats handily.
The third seat was narrowly won out by Ashley Jessen. With 1,003 votes, she was elected to the position just barely ahead of challenger Lisa Heinert, who earned 1,002.
With the race being decided by just one vote, Laramie County Clerk Debra Lee said, a recount was in order. Wyoming law requires a recount when the margin between the winner and the loser is less than 1% of votes cast.
“This close election proves that every vote matters,” Lee said.
When the votes came back to the canvassing board for official certification, Jessen came out the winner again, this time with a slightly wider margin of victory. According to official results, Jessen won the seat with 1,007 votes to Heinert’s 1005.
The remainder of the races were certified as they were called on Election Day.
