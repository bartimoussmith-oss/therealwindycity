GILLETTE, Wyo. — A 25-year-old Wyoming woman was killed near Gillette on Oct. 24 after failing to negotiate a curve in the road and rolling the vehicle.
The deceased has been identified as Abbie Melling. She was driving west on Bypass Road in a Toyota RAV4 approaching the Highway 59 intersection at 7:05 a.m. Investigators said she failed to negotiate the turn, exited the road and rolled the vehicle.
The weather at the time was clear and roads were dry. The report notes no other contributing factors.
Per the report, Melling was wearing her seat belt.
This story contains preliminary information as provided by the Wyoming Highway Patrol via the Wyoming Department of Transportation Fatal Crash Summary map. The agency advises that information may be subject to change.
