Mild weather to hold over Cheyenne through Halloween before cold front arrives

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Cheyenne can expect mild temperatures and calm weather through Halloween before a colder storm system arrives Sunday night.
Afternoon temperatures will hover around 60 degrees through Saturday, according to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne.
Today will bring sunny skies and a high near 60, paired with light winds shifting to the south-southeast at 5–10 mph. Skies will turn partly cloudy tonight as the overnight temperature drops to around 38.
Friday will remain mild with a high near 63 under partly sunny skies. A weak disturbance crossing the area could bring an isolated sprinkle late Friday or early Saturday, but meteorologists expect dry conditions to dominate.
Halloween on Saturday looks cooperative across Laramie County, featuring mostly sunny skies and an afternoon high near 62. Overnight, the low will dip to around 38, though moisture near the surface could produce patchy fog late Saturday night and early Sunday morning.
Conditions begin to turn Sunday as cloud cover thickens across southeast Wyoming ahead of an incoming Pacific storm. Cheyenne will reach an afternoon high near 59. Rain chances increase after midnight.
A cold front will sweep across the plains Sunday night, ushering in gusty winds and much colder air to start the workweek.
Rain showers are likely in Cheyenne in the morning Monday before mixing with or changing over to snow showers. Monday’s high will reach near 48, then fall to an overnight low around 32. Breezy conditions will carry into Tuesday.
Meteorologists in Cheyenne are tracking two separate storm systems over the western United States that could merge early next week. While confidence is high for mountain snow and gusty winds along Interstate 80 and the Laramie Range, exact precipitation totals and snow levels for lower elevations are uncertain.
Detailed Forecast
Today:
Sunny, with a high near 60. West wind at 5–10 mph shifting to the south-southeast in the afternoon.
Tonight:
Increasing clouds, with a low around 38. South-southeast wind at around 5 mph shifting to the west-southwest after midnight.
Friday:
Partly sunny, with a high near 63. West-southwest wind at 5–10 mph shifting to the south-southeast in the afternoon.
Friday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 38. South wind at 5–10 mph shifting to the west-southwest after midnight.
Saturday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 62. West-southwest wind at around 5 mph shifting to the south-southeast in the afternoon.
Saturday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 38.
Sunday:
Partly sunny, with a high near 59.
Sunday Night:
Showers likely, mainly after midnight. Mostly cloudy, with a low around 37.
Monday:
Rain showers likely before noon, then a chance of rain and snow showers. Partly sunny, with a high near 48. Breezy.
Monday Night:
A chance of rain and snow showers. Mostly cloudy, with a low around 32. Breezy.
Tuesday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 51. Breezy.
Tuesday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 32.
Wednesday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 53.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
