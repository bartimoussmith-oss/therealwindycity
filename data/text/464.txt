The Mountain West’s best represent the brown and gold.
University of Wyoming fifth-year forward Alyssa Glover won Mountain West women’s soccer offensive player of the year, announced Wednesday.
Maddie Burr, Brooke Brown and Isabel Vasco also received postseason accolades.
Glover — who also gathered first team All-MW status — led a high-powered Cowgirls attack that produced a program-record 39 goals during the regular season.
Glover ended the regular season as the conference leader in goals (10) and points (28), and her eight assists shared the conference lead with Air Force’s Annika Jost.
Glover became the program’s all-time points leader (73), thanks to two goals against Air Force on Nov. 1.
She also scored the opening goal against Hawaii this past Saturday — a win that secured UW’s first Mountain West Tournament appearance since 2022.
Glover finished the regular season with 10 goals and eight assists.
Burr, a junior defender, earned second team All-MW honors. She helped UW record nine shutouts, then added four goals and six assists.
Brown, a redshirt freshman forward transfer from Connecticut, fired five goals, including a hat trick against UNLV. It marked UW’s first hat trick in 10 years.
Vasco, a freshman forward from Green River, logged five goals and five assists in the regular season.
