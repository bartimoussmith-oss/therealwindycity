LARAMIE — A plethora of heavy hitters emerged in a match where leads never appeared safe.
Whether it was a Deijah Ard-Cessna kill, Chaney Birt assist or a Ellese Klassen serve, Cheyenne Central provided options.
Southeast Quadrant No. 1 seed Cheyenne Central defeated Northeast Quadrant No. 4 seed Campbell County 24-26, 25-11, 25-23, 25-17 in the first round of the Class 4A East Regional on Friday at Laramie High.
The Indians (20-11 overall) lost to Northeast Quadrant No. 2 seed Thunder Basin, 26-24, 25-22, 23-25, 25-12 in a semifinal later Friday night. Central faces the winner of Cheyenne South-Laramie at noon Saturday in an elimination match.
Against Campbell County, Ard-Cessna charted a match-high 16 kills, while Irelyn Hixson added 10. Brooke McCarty and Josie Williams chipped in eight kills apiece.
Williams added seven block assists. Birt and Hixson logged 13 digs apiece, while Klassen added 10.
Birt also dished 39 assists.
Thin margins separated what, on paper, appeared as opposites.
Central’s regular-season sweep against Campbell County shifted into ancient history. The seesaw first set pointed in the Camels’ favor.
They clutched onto a 21-20 lead, which soon shifted into 22-22, before the Camels (10-19) clinched the opening frame.
A 7-2 opening powered Central in the second frame, which set it on cruise control in the set.
The Camels crawled out of a 6-3 third-set deficit and later evened things at 19. Klassen’s serving boosted Central in a 6-4 run in the fourth frame. She helped Central control a 22-19 lead — amid a 3-0 run.
Klassen again helped in a 5-0 run early in the fourth set, before the Camels evened things at 13.
The Indians ended the match on a 4-2 run.
Against Thunder Basin, Ard-Cessna posted 12 kills, while Birt posted 15 digs and 38 assists. Junior libero Madison Benne registered 16 digs, while Klaassen posted 10.
