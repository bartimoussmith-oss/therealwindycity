Black Hills Energy announces merger, forming Bright Horizon Energy

CHEYENNE, Wyo. —
Black Hills Energy
, one of Wyoming’s primary power companies, announced Monday that it is merging with fellow power company NorthWestern Energy Group, forming Bright Horizon Energy. Although the merger is already complete, customers using the company as their power provider should not expect any immediate branding changes or changes to how they pay their bill.
Based out of South Dakota, Black Hills Energy acts as the main utility company for some Wyoming communities like Cheyenne, where it operates as Cheyenne Light, Fuel and Power Company. The branding and user interfaces that those residents have become familiar with will not change in light of the merger, the new company announced.
“Customers should also expect no immediate changes to branding, payment processes, outage reporting, online account access or customer support at this time,” the company said.
Rate payers will continue to use the same customer service phone number, pay their energy bill the same way, access the same online account and payment options, report outages and service issues through the same channels and receive service from the same local employees.
What the merger does do, Bright Horizon said, is strengthen the service the two power companies have provided in their respective service areas.
“Today marks the beginning of an exciting new chapter as Bright Horizon Energy,” Brian Bird, president and CEO, said. “By bringing together our talented employees, complementary operations and shared commitment to safety, reliability and affordability, we are creating a stronger, more resilient company positioned to meet the growing energy needs of our 2.2 million customers across eight states. We look forward to building on the long histories of Black Hills Energy and NorthWestern Energy as we deliver long-term value for our customers, employees, shareholders and communities.”
While Black Hills Energy customers can continue using the same website they are used to, interested rate payers can learn more about the newly formed Bright Horizon Energy at its website
here
. Between the two companies, Bright Horizon now serves an estimated 2.2 million customers across eight states.
