LCCC to feature Jarred Elrod exhibition ‘Hellfighter’
by
Nick Perkins
October 29, 2026

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Laramie County Community College recently announced that it will host “Hellfighter,” an exhibition by Jarred Elrod, from Nov. 4 through Dec. 11 in the LCCC Art Gallery, located on the college’s Cheyenne campus.
A release from LCCC says that the exhibition explores the intersection of art, design, technology, education and politics through a body of work inspired by Elrod’s ongoing creative research and visual storytelling practice.
LCCC is inviting the public to attend an opening reception for the exhibition on Wednesday, Nov. 4 from 10 to 11 a.m. in the Surbrugg/Prentice Auditorium. Later that afternoon, Elrod will lead a private printmaking workshop for photography students.
“‘Hellfighter’ began as a way to explore questions that do not always have straightforward answers,” Elrod said. “I’m thrilled to share this work with the LCCC community and discuss my approach to teaching and practicing creativity.”
The release says that “Hellfighter” draws its name from Elrod’s ongoing Hellfighter project — a fictional magazine platform that he created to explore contemporary social issues through illustration, design and visual communication. The artist says that the project focuses on the intersections of art, design, technology, education and politics, providing a framework to investigate questions and ideas shaping modern cultures.
“We are excited to welcome Jarred to campus and provide students and community members with the opportunity to engage directly with a practicing artist, designer, and educator,” said Daniel Maw, LCCC art instructor. “His work encourages viewers to think critically about contemporary issues while demonstrating the power of visual communication as a tool for exploration and dialogue.”
Elrod is a citizen of the Chickasaw Nation and is a native West Texan. He’s a graphic designer, illustrator, photographer and educator. His work has been featured in HOW Magazine, the Texas Biennial and the Bolivia and Uruguay International Poster Biennials. He’s currently the visiting assistant professor of graphic design at the University of Tampa, and he presents lectures and workshops across the U.S. and abroad.
All exhibition events are free and open to the public.
To learn more about Jarred Elrod and his work, visit
jarredelrod.com
. For more information about LCCC’s art program, visit
lccc.wy.edu/art
.
