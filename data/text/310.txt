CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Tuesday’s ballot included the board member and director races for the Laramie County Conservation and Chugwater Fire districts.
Two full-term seats on the Conservation District Rural Board were up for election. Melissa Harding and Jason Crowder were the top vote-getters with 11,589 votes and 9,505 votes, respectively.
They were challenged by Kiel W. Harding, Monte Lerwick Harding and Brent Wickham. Kiel Harding received 6,782 votes while Lerwick netted 6,387. Wickham got 9,325 votes.
Ford Rundell was the sole candidate to fill a two-year term on the Conservation District Rural Board. He received 24,321 votes.
Tim Ash was the sole candidate for the sole Chugwater Fire District director seat. He received 29 votes.
