CHEYENNE, Wyo. — More than 15,000 ballots have been cast in Laramie County since early voting began Oct. 6, according to Laramie County Clerk Debra Lee. Local voters have just one day left to submit their ballots early before Election Day tomorrow, Nov. 3.
Lee told Cap City News on Monday morning that, as of Friday, 15,326 early ballots had been cast — about 38% of the total 2024 Laramie County electorate.
The clerk’s election office has also distributed nearly 4,500 absentee ballots to voters who requested them and has received and processed more than 1,200 of those ballots as of Oct. 16.
A mailed ballot must reach the clerk’s office by the close of polls on Tuesday, Nov. 3 to be counted.
The early-voting window runs 28 days, shortened from the 45 days allowed before
a 2023 change to state law
. High early-voting turnout mirrors nationwide trends, which has seen roughly 50 million people cast votes so far in a surge that is outpacing the last midterm cycle,
according to the New York Times
.
Early voting will continue through 5 p.m. today at the Laramie County Courthouse. Election Day polls will open Tuesday at 7 a.m. and close at 7 p.m. Unlike early voting, citizens must line up to vote on Election Day at one of the following local election centers:
David R Romero Park
Storey Gym
Kiwanis Community House
North Christian Church
Laramie County Community College, CCI Building
Event Center at Archer
Pine Bluffs Community Center
Voters must bring a valid government-issued ID to be able to vote. Unregistered voters are able to register day-of.
Visit the
Laramie County clerk’s elections page on the Laramie County government website
for more information on voting.
