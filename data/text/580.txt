Mild temps in Cheyenne to give way to midweek winds

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Two days of mild, sunny weather are on tap in Cheyenne before a Pacific storm system brings strong winds and cooler temperatures midweek.
Today will be mostly sunny in the capital city with a high near 60 degrees, according to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne. Winds will blow from the west-southwest at 5–10 mph before shifting to the south this afternoon.
Patchy fog was spotted early this morning near Pine Bluffs and across the Nebraska Panhandle. Meteorologists said it should lift around sunrise.
Tonight will be mostly cloudy in Cheyenne with a low around 32. Tuesday will bring mostly sunny skies and another high near 60 before conditions change late in the night.
A cold front will push across the area late Tuesday night into Wednesday, dropping Cheyenne’s high to around 50. Winds will pick up after midnight Tuesday, shifting to the west at 15–20 mph while gusting at up to 30 mph.
Wednesday will be sunny and windy in Cheyenne, with west winds at 30–35 mph and gusts reaching 50 mph before easing in the afternoon. Wednesday night will be mostly clear with a low around 25.
The National Weather Service in Cheyenne is watching Wednesday for a widespread high wind potential. Gusts at least in the 60–70 mph range are possible in the wind-prone areas and mountain peaks. (National Weather Service graphic)
The NWS in Cheyenne issued a high wind watch Tuesday evening through 10 p.m. Wednesday for wind-prone areas along Interstates 80 and 25, including Arlington, Bordeaux and the South Laramie Range foothills. Meteorologists said wind gusts of 60–70 mph are possible, with a 70% or higher chance of strong gusts through Wednesday.
Any chance for precipitation is expected to stay west of the mountains, though Carbon and Albany counties could see rain and snow showers Tuesday night into Wednesday. The Sierra Madre Range could pick up a few inches of snow.
Quiet, seasonal weather returns to Cheyenne on Thursday under mostly sunny skies and amid a high near 48. The overnight low will be around 26.
Another storm system will bring a chance of rain and snow showers to Cheyenne from 11 a.m. Friday and into Friday night, alongside a high near 49 and a low around 24. Saturday will be sunny and breezy in Cheyenne with a high near 43 and a low around 21, followed by a mostly sunny Sunday with a high near 47.
Detailed Forecast
Today:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 60. West-southwest wind at 5–10 mph shifting to the south in the afternoon.
Tonight:
Mostly cloudy, with a low around 32. South-southwest wind at around 5 mph shifting to the west-northwest after midnight.
Tuesday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 60. West wind at 5–10 mph shifting to the south in the afternoon.
Tuesday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 33. Breezy, with a south wind at 5–10 mph shifting to the west at 15–20 mph after midnight. Winds could gust as high as 30 mph.
Wednesday:
Sunny, with a high near 50. Windy, with a west wind at 30–35 mph decreasing to 20–25 mph in the afternoon. Winds could gust as high as 50 mph.
Wednesday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 25.
Thursday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 48.
Thursday Night:
Mostly cloudy, with a low around 26.
Friday:
A chance of rain and snow showers after 11 a.m. Mostly sunny, with a high near 49.
Friday Night:
A chance of rain and snow showers before 8 p.m., then a chance of snow showers until 11 p.m. Mostly cloudy, with a low around 24.
Saturday:
Sunny, with a high near 43. Breezy.
Saturday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 21.
Sunday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 47.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
