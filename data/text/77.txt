Warm end of the week to give way to weekend chill, rain, snow in Cheyenne

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Cheyenne can enjoy mild autumn weather through Friday before a weekend storm drops temperatures and brings chances for rain and snow.
The high temperature in the capital city will reach near 69 degrees today and climb to 71 on Friday under partly sunny skies, according to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne. Southwest winds will remain light today at 5–10 mph before picking up Friday night, when gusts could reach 25 mph behind an approaching cold front.
Conditions turn cooler across Laramie County on Saturday. The high will hover near 50 degrees under partly sunny skies, accompanied by breezy north winds blowing at 15–20 mph.
Rain chances increase to 60% Saturday night as the overnight low drops to around 33 degrees
.
Meteorologists expect moisture to linger into Sunday morning, bringing a chance of snow flurries or a rain–snow mix to the eastern plains and the Interstate 80 Summit. Snow accumulation is not expected.
Sunday will remain mostly cloudy with a high near 49 and a 30% chance of showers, mainly before noon. Overnight temperatures will fall near freezing at 32.
Active weather will continue into next week as several quick-moving systems from Canada cross southeast Wyoming
. Rain and snow showers remain possible Monday morning before daytime temperatures settle into the 50s through Wednesday
.
Detailed Forecast
Today:
Partly sunny, with a high near 69. West-northwest wind at 5–10 mph shifting to the south in the afternoon.
Tonight:
A 20% chance of showers. Mostly cloudy, with a low around 48. Southwest wind at 5–10 mph.
Friday:
A 20% chance of showers after noon. Mostly cloudy, then gradually becoming sunny, with a high near 71. Southwest wind at 5–10 mph shifting to the south-southeast in the afternoon.
Friday Night:
A 30% chance of showers, mainly before midnight. Partly cloudy, with a low around 39. West-northwest wind at 10–15 mph, with gusts as high as 25 mph.
Saturday:
A 20% chance of showers. Partly sunny, with a high near 50. Breezy, with a north wind at 15–20 mph shifting to the east-northeast in the afternoon.
Saturday Night:
Showers likely. Mostly cloudy, with a low around 33. Chance of precipitation is 60%.
Sunday:
A 30% chance of showers, mainly before noon. Mostly cloudy, with a high near 49.
Sunday Night:
A chance of showers. Mostly cloudy, with a low around 32.
Monday:
A chance of rain showers before 9 a.m., then a chance of rain and snow showers until noon, followed by a slight chance of rain showers. Partly sunny, with a high near 51.
Monday Night:
A slight chance of rain and snow showers before midnight. Partly cloudy, with a low around 30.
Tuesday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 58.
Tuesday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 34.
Wednesday:
Sunny, with a high near 60.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
