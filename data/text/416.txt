BUFFALO, Wyo. — The U.S. Forest Service offered an update on the still-burning Foot Creek Fire, which sparked about 12 miles west of Buffalo in the Bighorn National Forest.
A social media update from the U.S. Forest Service confirms that the fire was human caused and has currently burnt 532 acres. It said that crews completed a fire suppression line around the fire, but with unburned fuels between the fire and the fire line, additional burnouts were planned for Saturday to help secure the fire lines.
The fire is currently 41% contained.
Drones are being used to facilitate burnouts and conduct incident mapping.
The post says that smoke should be visible with the burnouts and visibility along the highway may require traffic to slow for safety.
Engines have been staffed overnight to protect structures in the fire area. Mop-up near various structures is ongoing, and the evacuation status for residences near the fire continues to be in the “SET” status.
The post notes that fire activity overnight was minimal. Dry conditions are expected through the weekend, with more critical conditions possible Sunday.
The primary objectives in the fire are public and firefighter health and safety, as well as structure protection. Minimizing fire size to contain the fire under a full suppression strategy is the focus for resources on the ground.
