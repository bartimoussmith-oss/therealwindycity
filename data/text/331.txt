CHEYENNE, Wyo. — General election polls close at 7 p.m. Tuesday, Nov. 3. Results are provided by the Associated Press.
Contested races include Wyoming Senate Districts 5, 7 and 31 and Wyoming House Districts 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 41 and 42. Uncontested races include SD 6 and HDs 12, 43, 44 and 61.
All results are unofficial until certified by county and state canvassing boards.
