Mild, dry conditions to hold across Cheyenne through the weekend

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Mild weather and dry conditions will settle over Laramie County through the weekend, according to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne.
Today brings partly sunny skies to Cheyenne with a high near 56 degrees. Winds will stay light at around 5 mph from the west-northwest before shifting to the east in the afternoon. Across the broader region, highs will hover in the mid-40s west of the Laramie Range and reach the upper 50s on the eastern plains.
Skies will clear tonight as temperatures drop to an overnight low near 32 in the capital city. Outlying rural areas across Laramie County could dip into the lower 20s before sunrise.
The warmup picks up Saturday. Cheyenne will see sunny skies and a high near 58, with northwest winds between 5 and 10 mph. Areas east of the Interstate 25 corridor will top out around 60.
Sunday will bring the warmest temperatures of the stretch. Sunny skies will push Cheyenne’s afternoon high to around 65, paired with a light southwest breeze.
Meteorologists expect the dry pattern to carry into next week across southeast Wyoming. Highs will hover in the low to mid-60s through Tuesday, with sunny conditions and a high near 60 expected for Veterans Day.
Detailed Forecast

Partly sunny, with a high near 56. West-northwest wind at around 5 mph shifting to the east in the afternoon.
Tonight:
Mostly clear, with a low around 32. Calm wind shifting to the west at around 5 mph after midnight.
Saturday:
Sunny, with a high near 58. Northwest wind at 5–10 mph.
Saturday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 35. West wind at around 5 mph.
Sunday:
Sunny, with a high near 65. Southwest wind at 5–10 mph.
Sunday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 39.
Monday:
Sunny, with a high near 64.
Monday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 36.
Tuesday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 63.

Partly cloudy, with a low around 37.

Sunny, with a high near 60.

Mostly clear, with a low around 37.
Thursday:
Sunny, with a high near 64.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
