ROCK SPRINGS, Wyo. — The Sweetwater County Sheriff’s Office recently announced that it is hosting a public auction of abandoned and court-forfeited vehicles on Tuesday, Nov. 3.
That’s according to a social media post, which states that the auction will be held at the sheriff’s office at 10 a.m. The SCSO is located at 50140B U.S. Highway 191 South in Rock Springs.
The post states that gates open at 9:30 a.m. and bidding begins at 10.
The following vehicles are up for auction:
1997 Buick Century 2G4WS52M3V1449066, $213.60
1999 Chevrolet 2500 1GCGK29J6XF093596, $100.00
2015 Hyundai Elantra 5NPDH4AE0FH612366, $198.00
2011 Hyundai Sonata 5NPEB4AC9BH098040, $546.00
2018 GMC Yukon 1GKS2AKC3JR403858, $283.80
2003 Ford Expedition 1FMPU16LX3LB88258, $276.00
“Our agency occasionally sells abandoned property,” the post states. “After trying to locate the original owner(s), we post notices in public places and publish them in newspapers before the items are auctioned to the public per Wyoming law.”
The SCSO has no history on any of the vehicles, and most auctioned vehicles have no keys. All vehicles are auctioned as-is, with no warranties expressed or implied. The SCSO reserves the right to reject any and all bids.
Pickup for the abandoned vehicles should be done by Friday, Nov. 6 during regular business hours: Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Payment is cash only.
For questions about the auction, call Kelly at
307-872-3866
.
