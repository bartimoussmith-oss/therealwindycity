The new Dunkin' location on Pershing Avenue in Cheyenne Oct. 22, 2026 (Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Cheyenne’s newest coffee shop, the Dunkin’ on Pershing Avenue, is currently slated for a November opening, though an exact date hasn’t been decided. The store’s owner, Bhavesh Patel, told Cap City News that a grand opening event is being planned as well.
Once a gas station, 3363 Pershing Ave. is just weeks away from reopening as a Dunkin’, the first Cheyenne location open to the broader public. The area’s other two locations are in F.E. Warren Air Force Base and in the Horse Palace at Swan Ranch south of town, which are inaccessible to certain demographics.
Patel said that the Pershing location is a separate venture from the other stores and, despite entering a competitive coffee scene with Dutch Bros and Dazbog nearby, is prepared to stand out as a place offering guests “something for every part of their day,” including hot and iced beverages, sandwiches and baked goods. Patel said he isn’t intimidated by the coffee competition.
“What makes Dunkin’ special is our focus on delivering quality, convenience and friendly service,” he said. “Whether guests are grabbing their morning coffee on the way to work or stopping by for an afternoon pick-me-up, our goal is to make every Dunkin’ run a great experience. We look forward to becoming part of Cheyenne residents’ daily routines and building lasting relationships with the community.”
Patel has more than 20 years in the restaurant business, but is a first-timer to franchising a Dunkin’ store. Besides the feeling of opening a new restaurant, he said, the feeling of creating local jobs, building meaningful connections and becoming part of Cheyenne’s growing business community is exciting.
That process begins in just a few weeks when the store officially opens. An exact opening date has not yet been decided, but Patel confirmed that the store will begin with a soft opening to give employees time to prepare and make sure the store is running smoothly. A grand opening is planned for a later, still-unconfirmed date.
Cap City News will share more details about the store’s opening when they become available.
