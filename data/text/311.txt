CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Incumbent Laramie County Sheriff Brian Kozak defeated Democrat challenger Robin Cozad in Tuesday’s general election.
Kozak earned 19,066 votes to retain his seat. Cozad received 10,545 votes.
In August, Kozak defeated challenger Eric Norris
in the Republican primary election. Kozak earned 11,256 votes to advance to the general election. Norris received 7,271 votes.
Per his county profile, Kozak “retired as Cheyenne’s longest serving police chief with 11 years of service. He began his law enforcement career with the Mesa, Arizona Police Department. After 22 years, he retired from Mesa to accept a position as police chief in Avon, Colorado, coming to Cheyenne four years later. Sheriff Kozak holds a master’s degree in education and a bachelor’s degree in business.”
