Warm, dry weather holds in Cheyenne through Friday before weekend cooldown

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Above-average temperatures and dry skies will hold across Laramie County through Friday before a weekend cold front drops the mercury.
Today will be sunny in the capital city with a high near 64 degrees, according to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne. Winds from the west-northwest at 5–10 mph will shift to the south-southwest this afternoon.
Skies will stay mostly clear tonight as the low dips to around 33. South-southwest winds at around 5 mph will shift to the west after midnight.
Friday will bring mostly sunny skies and a high near 61 in Cheyenne. West-southwest winds at 5–10 mph will shift to the south-southeast in the afternoon before clouds build Friday night and the low drops to around 30.
Across the rest of southeast Wyoming, afternoon temps will also reach the upper 50s to mid-60s today and Friday, meteorologists said. Patchy fog could develop early Friday morning in far eastern Wyoming and the western Nebraska Panhandle.
Laramie County will cool down Saturday as a morning cold front pushes across southeast Wyoming, dropping highs by 10–15 degrees. Cheyenne will see partly sunny skies with a high near 50 and a south wind of 5–10 mph.
The passing storm system will mostly stay south in Colorado, keeping rain and snow chances low for Cheyenne and the Interstate 80 corridor, meteorologists said. The Snowy and Sierra Madre mountains could pick up a few inches of light snow.
After a long period of warm and dry weather, the National Weather Service in Cheyenne said confidence is increasing in a pattern change around the middle part of next week. Most of southeast Wyoming will see chances for rain and snow from a series of potential storms, but the details are uncertain. (National Weather Service graphic)
Saturday night will be mostly cloudy in Cheyenne with a low around 32. Sunshine returns Sunday with a high near 53, while southeast Wyoming east of the Laramie Range will see highs in the low to mid-50s. Sunday night will be mostly clear with a low around 28.
Dry, mild weather continues into early next week across the region, with Tuesday and Wednesday highs running 5–10 degrees above average, if the forecast holds up. Cheyenne will see sunny skies Monday and mostly sunny skies Tuesday, with highs near 59 both days and overnight lows around 30.
A slight chance of showers arrives in Cheyenne on Tuesday night with a low around 31. Shower chances increase Wednesday under partly sunny skies as the high drops to around 50.
