CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Microsoft’s Outlook email services were down across the country on Thursday, leaving thousands of users without the litany of services that Outlook provides.
According to
an update from DownDetector
, more than 130,800 people have reported issues with Outlook as of noon Mountain Standard time.
Microsoft provided an update on X
acknowledging the outage and attempting to keep the community abreast about how the company is trying to solve it.
“We’re investigating reports of users experiencing issues accessing Exchange Online mailboxes through multiple connection methods in the Americas,” the X post states. “More details can be found in the Microsoft 365 admin center under EX1550543.”

Microsoft continued to update the situation, noting that it was attempting to mitigate impact. The company wrote that it has confirmed that the issue may affect Microsoft 365 service connectivity for users in any region.
Microsoft wrote that it determined that a portion of infrastructure is experiencing elevated load across multiple regions, which is likely contributing to the connectivity errors. It has begun redistributing connection traffic across it broader, worldwide infrastructure to assist in balancing the load.
“We’re aggressively pursuing all pathways to reduce impact. We’ve paused all deployments, we’re moving additional traffic off the affected infrastructure, and we’re restarting affected servers to return them to service,” Microsoft wrote on X.
Microsoft said that, as of 12:51 p.m., it was testing a configuration change to address the problem that’s causing serves to fail automated health checks, making the service unavailable. It has also initiated a restart of all affected servers and is monitoring health as it returns to service.
Microsoft has not provided an estimated time that services might be repaired. Oil City News will update this story as more information becomes available.
