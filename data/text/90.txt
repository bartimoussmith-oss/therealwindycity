Morning showers to give way to clearing skies, warmer week in Cheyenne

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Rain will taper off across Laramie County by mid-morning today, giving way to clearing skies and a steady warmup through the rest of the workweek.
Cheyenne picked up about 0.20 inches of rain early this morning, with up to 0.60 inches falling across Albany and Carbon counties. Meteorologists with the National Weather Service in Cheyenne said the moisture brings welcome relief to southeast Wyoming’s drought-stricken ground.
Cheyenne carries a 40% chance of showers mainly before 9 a.m., followed by gradual clearing and an afternoon high near 52 degrees. Light north-northwest winds at around 5 mph will shift to the south-southwest in the afternoon.
Skies will remain partly cloudy tonight as the temperature drops to an overnight low around 33. Moisture left on the ground combined with light winds could create patchy low clouds or fog east of Interstate 25 late tonight into early Tuesday morning.
Sunshine and warmer air take over on Tuesday, kicking off a dry stretch. Cheyenne will see a high near 60 degrees, accompanied by west winds at 10–15 mph.
The mild weather will hold through the rest of the workweek. Meteorologists expect sunny skies with highs near 61 on Wednesday, 62 on Thursday and 64 on Friday.
The weekend outlook shows pleasant conditions for Halloween activities on Saturday. The high will reach near 61 under mostly sunny skies, with evening temperatures falling into the upper 30s and 40s alongside a slight chance for rain showers. A colder weather pattern is expected to bring lower temperatures and unsettled conditions by Sunday.
Detailed Forecast
Today:
A 40% chance of showers, mainly before 9 a.m. Cloudy through mid-morning, then gradual clearing, with a high near 52. North-northwest wind at around 5 mph shifting to the south-southwest in the afternoon.
Tonight:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 33. South wind at around 5 mph.
Tuesday:
Sunny, with a high near 60. West wind at 10–15 mph.
Tuesday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 37. West wind at 10–15 mph.
Wednesday:
Sunny, with a high near 61. West wind at 5–15 mph.
Wednesday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 35.
Thursday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 62.
Thursday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 36.
Friday:
Partly sunny, with a high near 64.
Friday Night:
Mostly cloudy, with a low around 37.
Saturday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 61.
Saturday Night:
A slight chance of showers. Partly cloudy, with a low around 38.
Sunday:
A slight chance of showers. Partly sunny, with a high near 58.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
