Commissioners Troy Thompson, left, and Gunnar Malm sit during the regular Board of Commissioners meeting Oct. 15 inside the Commissioners Meeting Room inside the Laramie County Government Complex in Cheyenne. (Jared Gendron/Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The three Republican candidates for Laramie County’s Board of Commissioners won their seats Tuesday over two challenging Democrats.
On Tuesday, the top three vote-getters were incumbent Commissioners Gunnar Malm and Troy Thompson with 18,765 votes and 19,109 votes, respectively, and Jess E. Ketcham with 19,475 votes.
Democrat challengers included Barbara Cabot, who received 10,100 votes, and Lynne K. Davis, who netted 10,955.
Malm, Thompson and Ketcham were the top three candidates to emerge from August’s primary.
They outpolled challengers Julie Christen, M. Lee Hasenauer, Jason MacGregor, Gregory C. Pitts, Clifford Spoonemoore, Josh Tuttle and incumbent Commissioner Linda Heath.
