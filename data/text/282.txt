Strong winds, falling temps and rain-snow mix heading to Cheyenne

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A storm system sweeping across Wyoming is bringing gusty winds, dropping temperatures and rain showers that will mix with snow, according to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne.
In Cheyenne, temperatures will rise to near 44 degrees early this morning before beginning a downward slide to around 35 for the rest of the day. Scattered showers bring a 30% chance of rain through the afternoon along with patchy morning fog. Northwest winds are expected at between 25 and 35 mph, with gusts reaching up to 55 mph.
Rain will mix with snow tonight before 2 a.m. as the overnight low drops to around 31. Strong northwest winds will continue through the night, with gusts up to 55 mph and a 40% chance of precipitation.
Rain and high elevation snow showers continue today, with winds expected to ramp up quickly this morning. Snow levels will drop this evening, with accumulation possible mainly on north or northwest sides of terrain features. (National Weather Service graphic)
Across Laramie County, colder air behind the storm front will push snow levels down to around 5,000 feet this evening. Meteorologists expect light snow along the north side of the Cheyenne Ridge, with accumulations ranging from a dusting to 2 inches. Wind gusts could top 60 mph in exposed spots near the Colorado border.
Higher elevations face heavier winter conditions. Winter weather advisories are in effect through tonight for the mountains and higher stretches of Interstate 80 between Laramie and Rawlins, including the summit and the Arlington to Elk Mountain corridor. Peaks above 9,000 feet could see 6 or more inches of wet snow, while higher passes along I-80 expect a few inches of accumulation.
Valleys around Laramie, Rawlins and Saratoga could pick up an inch of snow, mainly on grass and elevated surfaces. Farther north, meteorologists said southern Niobrara County around Lusk could see 1–3 inches of snow overnight.
Showers will taper off Tuesday morning before skies clear across the region by midday. Cheyenne will see sunshine and breezy conditions Tuesday with a high near 50, though northwest winds could gust up to 40 mph.
Drier conditions and seasonal temperatures will take hold for the remainder of the week. Mostly sunny skies and highs in the mid-50s will return Wednesday and Thursday, with temperatures warming toward 60 by Sunday.
Detailed Forecast
Today
: A chance of showers before 2 p.m., then a chance of rain after 2 p.m. Patchy fog between 8 and 10 a.m. Otherwise, mostly cloudy, with a temperature rising to near 44 by 8 a.m., then falling to around 35 during the remainder of the day. Windy, with a north northwest wind 25–35 mph, with gusts as high as 50 mph. Chance of precipitation is 30%.
Tonight
: A chance of rain and snow before 2 a.m., then a slight chance of snow. Patchy fog before 2 a.m. Otherwise, mostly cloudy, with a low around 31. Windy, with a northwest wind 25–35 mph, with gusts as high as 55 mph. Chance of precipitation is 40%.
Tuesday
: Sunny, with a high near 50. Breezy, with a northwest wind 15–25 mph, with gusts as high as 40 mph.
Tuesday Night
: Mostly clear, with a low around 28. West wind 5–10 mph.
Wednesday
: Mostly sunny, with a high near 54. North wind 5–10 mph.
Wednesday Night
: Mostly cloudy, with a low around 30.
Thursday
: Sunny, with a high near 56.
Thursday Night
: Partly cloudy, with a low around 30.
Friday
: Sunny, with a high near 57.
Friday Night
: Partly cloudy, with a low around 31.
Saturday
: Sunny, with a high near 52. Breezy.
Saturday Night
: Mostly clear, with a low around 28.
Sunday
: Sunny, with a high near 59.
More on the weather is available at the National Weather Service website.
