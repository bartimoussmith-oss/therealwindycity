Debra Jean Keller
Debra Jean Keller:
1951 – 2026
In Loving Memory of Debra Jean Keller
Debra Jean Keller, lovingly known as Deb, Debbie, or Debra, was born to Charles Keller and Geraldine Keller on November 7, 1951, in Rawlins, Wyoming. She passed away peacefully in her home in Cheyenne, Wyoming on November 6, 2026, just one day before her 75th birthday.
However you knew her, Deb lived life on her own terms and lived it to the fullest. Throughout her years, she made countless memories, touched many lives, and leaves behind a legacy of love, strength, determination, and compassion.
Deb enjoyed the simple things that brought her happiness. She loved listening to her music and spending time in nature whenever she could. She wasn’t afraid to get her hands dirty, either. She helped build a derby car or a few over the years, and even proudly took home a Powder Puff Derby win of her own.
Deb was a hard worker her entire life. No matter what came her way, she had a way of figuring things out and making things happen, not only for herself, but for the people she loved. One of her greatest sources of pride was her career, where she led others with compassion, dedication, and a genuine commitment to the people around her.
But beyond anything she accomplished, Deb’s greatest legacy is the love she leaves behind.
She spent her final years surrounded by love, sharing time and building memories with those who meant the most to her. Those moments, both big and small, will forever be treasured by the family and friends who were blessed to share them with her.
Deb leaves a mark that cannot be measured simply by the years she lived. It can be found in the stories that will continue to be told, the laughter that will come when remembering her, the lessons she passed along, and the love that will continue through the generations of her family.
She is survived by her daughter, Shaun Marie; her brother, Dave Keller, and his wife, Eileen Keller; her nieces: Kelly Parker, Heidi Keller, Jen Parker, and Christine Muggleberg; and her nephew, Charles Keller; many great-nieces and nephews.
She is also survived by her grandsons, Chris Walker and Nicholas Young; her granddaughters: Harmony Jaure and her husband, Ron Jaure, Alexa Batchelor and her husband, Travaris Pou, Aubrey Batchelor, and Danni Martinez; and her great-grandchildren: Estrella Jaure, DJ Batchelor, Ainslee Pou, Anora Harris, Leon Harris, Oryan Batchelor, Cooper Pou, Harper Pou, and Paker Pou; as well as many extended family. She is preceded in death by her parents, Charles Keller, Geraldine Keller; daughter, Deva Martinez; granddaughter, Catlin.
Deb was loved deeply, and she will be missed beyond words. Her life may have come to an end, but the love she gave, the memories she made, and the pieces of herself she left in all of us will live on.
May we remember her with music playing, stories being told, laughter filling the room, and the people she loved gathered close.
A celebration of life will take place on Sunday, November 22, 2026 at the Cheyenne Depot Museum, 121 W. 15th St. Cheyenne WY from 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.
