Mark Rinne
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Longtime Cheyenne City Councilor Mark Rinne will succeed Sen. Stephan Pappas in the Wyoming Senate for District 7.
In Tuesday’s election, Rinne received 3,276 votes to Democrat challenger Jim Lee’s 2,257.
Rinne and Lee ran unopposed in the August primary elections.
Rinne, a Cheyenne dentist since 1987, has served on the Cheyenne City Council, where he was elected council president 10 times. He has worked with the Cheyenne Regional Airport Board and chaired the Legislative Leadership Committee for the Wyoming Association of Municipalities. He campaigned on easing regulatory hurdles to lower housing costs while supporting legacy industries and local economic growth. A Cheyenne resident since 1977, Rinne also previously served as a trustee for Laramie County Community College.
