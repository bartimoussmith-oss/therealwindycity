CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The Federal Aviation Administration ordered a complete grounding of all flights across the United States on Friday afternoon, citing a “communication issue” impacting all of the nation’s air traffic control centers. That stoppage, according to Cheyenne Regional Airport Deputy Director Kyle Malacina, includes Cheyenne flights.
An X post from Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy on Nov. 13 said that the ground stop is nationwide and related to communication issues. According to him, technicians with the FAA are investigating the problem and updates will be shared when available.
The @FAAnews issued a nationwide ground stop for all U.S. airports following a communication issue impacting all Air Route Traffic Control Centers. FAA technicians are investigating the issue. We will provide immediate updates as we have them.
— Secretary Sean Duffy (@SecDuffy)
November 13, 2026
The cause and severity of the communication issue is not yet known.
Cheyenne flights will be subject to the halt, Malacina told Cap City News on Friday.
“At this time, there is a nationwide ground stop,” Malacina said at around 2:40 p.m. Friday. “Outside of that, I don’t have any information to share at this time. They’ve just issued it and they have not passed any other information to me.”
He said that he found out about the ground stop at the same time that it was announced publicly.
Cap City News will share more information when it becomes available.
