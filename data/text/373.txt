Michael Eugene Stone
Michael Eugene Stone:
1950 – 2026
Michael “Mike” Eugene Stone was born on October 17, 1950, in Chattanooga, Tennessee to Harold and Betty (Rye) Stone.  He and his family moved to Colorado and Wyoming as a child.
At the age of 16, Mike took over the family business, Mike’s Water and Dozer. Mike graduated from Campbell County High School in 1969.  It was his sophomore year, when he met the love of his life, Terria Edwards, and they married on June 02, 1970.
Mike and Terria had three children: Jeani, ( JD Stetson) Toby (Jamie) and Kelly (Meli).
His real joy came with his role as “Pa” to his grandchildren: Colton, Hawkins, Avary, Emily and Ben.
On October 16, 2026, Mike, passed away at the Anschutz Medical Campus in Aurora, Colorado, after fighting metastatic melanoma cancer. Mike will be laid to rest at the Willow Grove Cemetery in Buffalo, Wyo., where he can continue to enjoy the views of the Big Horn Mountains.  In lieu of flowers, the family would appreciate cards and memories of Mike, which can be sent to Jeani Stone, P.O. Box 404, Story, Wyoming, 82842.
Donations can be sent to First Northern Bank, c/o Mike Stone Memorial Fund, P.O. Box 400, Buffalo, Wyoming 82834, where we are partnering with the Willow Grove Cemetery in Buffalo, Wyoming to create a picnic area where families can meet and reflect on their loved ones who are gone.
The family would like to thank Dr. Luke Mantle, and UC Health Anschutz team, Dr. Bergeron and Dr. Williams, and Johnson County Health, Buffalo EMTs, Brittany Miskimins, and Tanner Gillette for their care and friendship, Sally Bohart, and the Buffalo Senior Center for all their care and compassion these past couple of years.
Lauren Sue Johnson:
1959 – 2026
Lauren [Laurie] Sue Meranda Johnson, cherished wife, mother, grandmother, daughter, sister, and friend, peacefully passed away in her sleep at her home on October 20, 2026 from complications from Parkinson’s Disease. Born on April 27, 1959, in Cheyenne, Wyoming, Laurie was the beloved daughter of Jerry and Jackie [Lueras] Meranda. Her life was a testament to the power of music, love, and friendship.
Laurie’s early years were spent in the vibrant communities of Laramie, Hanna, Rawlins, Casper and Cheyenne, she graduated from Laramie High School in 1977. Her passion for music led her to the University of Wyoming, where she earned a Bachelor’s degree in Music Education in 1984. This passion for music became a lifelong career, as she dedicated 36 years to teaching music and band across all levels of education, inspiring countless students before retiring in 1999.
Laurie married the love of her life, Robert, on June 16, 1996, in a beautiful ceremony surrounded by family and friends in Cheyenne. Together, they created a loving home and raised two wonderful children, Meranda and Dominick. Laurie was a proud grandmother to five grandchildren: Michael, Emily, Olivia, Oenomaus, and Lucis, who brought immense joy to her life.
A gifted musician and actress, Laurie performed in numerous musical performances and plays, sharing her talents with the community. Her involvement extended beyond the stage as she served on the Cheyenne Frontier Days Parade committee and remained an active member of the University of Wyoming Alumni. Laurie’s compassion was boundless, extending to both her human and animal companions.
Known for her love of pets and her unparalleled dedication to friends and family, Laurie was a beacon of kindness and loyalty. Her friendship was a treasured gift to all who had the privilege of knowing her.
She is survived by her devoted husband of 30 years, Robert; her children, Meranda [Andrew] Romain, and Dominick [Cheyenne] Johnson; her grandchildren: Michael, Emily, Olivia, Oenomaus, and Lucis; and her brothers, Richard [Margaret] and John [Paul] Meranda.
She was predeceased by both sets of her grandparents, whose memories she cherished deeply.
A service to celebrate Laurie’s life will be held on November 6, 2026, at 1 p.m. at the Wiederspahn-Radomsky Chapel of the Chimes, located at 1900 East 19th Street, Cheyenne, Wyoming. Family and friends are invited to join in remembering a life filled with love and music.
In lieu of flowers, the family kindly requests donations be made to the Michael J. Fox Foundation, an organization that held special significance for Laurie. Laurie was a beautiful wife, mother, daughter, and friend who will be missed by all. Her legacy of love, music, and friendship will continue to resonate in the hearts of those who knew her.
Stephen “Steve” Allen Lee:
1950 – 2026
Stephen “Steve” Allen Lee passed away on October 25, 2026, at the age of 76. Born on August 2, 1950, Steve will be remembered as an intelligent and humorous man whose steady presence, wit, and deep interests left a lasting impression on those who knew and loved him.
Steve built a distinguished career as Manager of Steam Operating Practices for the Union Pacific Railroad. He took great pride in his work, and one of his proudest accomplishments was running a tight ship in the steam program. Trains were not only part of his profession, but also one of the interests he genuinely enjoyed throughout his life. He was also an avid reader, always drawn to learning and to the quiet pleasure of a good book.
Beyond his work, Steve was known for the personal joys that shaped his life and relationships. He enjoyed fishing, even if he seldom caught any fish. He also enjoyed cooking, and family will remember that the first meal he cooked for Karen was chicken and artichoke hearts. It was one of many expressions of care over the years they built together. Steve and Karen were married on December 17, 1977, and were just shy of celebrating 50 years of marriage.
He is survived by his wife, Karen Lee; and his children: Stephanie Lamb, Jen Lee, and Dan Lee. He is also survived by his brother, Gary Lee, and Gary’s wife, Kathy; along with their sons, Chris and Stephen, and their families. Steve is further survived by granddaughters, Kirra and Kaelynn, whose place in the family brought him pride and joy.
He was preceded in death by Deward Lee and Anna Nell Lee; as well as his uncle, Bobby Lee.
Steve will be remembered for his sharp mind, his humor, his love of trains and reading, and the many everyday moments that made him so dearly loved. His memory will remain a blessing to his family and friends, who will carry him with them always.
Clint R. Nelson:
1952 – 2026
Clint R. Nelson, 74, passed away on October 25, 2026, in Cheyenne. He was born on February 22, 1952, in Pocatello, Idaho. He served for four years in the United States Marine Corp.
Funeral services will be held on Friday, November 6, 2026, at 10:30 a.m. at The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, 3312 E Lincolnway, Cheyenne.  Viewing will be held at 9:30 a.m. at the church.   Interment will be held at the Cheyenne National Cemetery at 1 p.m. on Friday.
