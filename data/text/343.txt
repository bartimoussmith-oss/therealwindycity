CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Ward I representatives from the Cheyenne City Council are hosting a City Council and Coffee Open House on Saturday.
That’s according to a release from the City of Cheyenne, which states that the open house will take place on Saturday, Nov. 7 from 9 to 11 a.m. at Pioneer Park, located at 1331 Talbot Court.
“Discussion will include items related to Ward I and other city projects, with the opportunity for residents to voice concerns and get to know their representatives,” the release states.
