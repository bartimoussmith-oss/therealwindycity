CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A 14-year-old Cheyenne student is currently receiving treatment at the Cheyenne Regional Medical Center after being struck by a car while crossing the street near Cheyenne East High School. According to Cheyenne police, the driver of the vehicle will be cited for failure to yield.
The incident occurred at approximately 7:22 a.m. before school,
a Cheyenne Police Department Facebook release said
. The teenager was crossing Pershing Boulevard near its intersection with Windmill Road when the car hit them.
Injured, the teen was taken to the hospital. Cheyenne police did not specify the severity or extent of their injuries.
“Pick-up and drop-off times are especially dangerous for both students and drivers near school zones,” Cheyenne Police Chief Mark Francisco said. “We ask our citizens to help keep everyone safe by slowing down and eliminating distractions, especially in these areas.”
The department directed residents to the National Highway Transportation Safety Administration’s web page on
pedestrian safety
.
