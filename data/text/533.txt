By Faye Mayern
A man has been jailed for stealing a £799 ($1,075)
orangutan statue
from a garden center and cycling off with it.
Derek Lundgren was jailed for stealing an orangutan statue. (Cambridgeshire Constabulary via SWNS)
By Talker
Derek Lundgren, 61, took the statue from
Scotsdales Garden Centre
in Horningsea, Cambridgeshire, on Sept. 7.
He was
captured on security camera footage
leaving the store without paying before jumping on his bike, placing the giant animal figure across the handlebars and cycling off.
Lundgren, of Sawston in Cambridgeshire, pleaded guilty to
theft from a shop
and breaching three suspended sentences.
He was sentenced to one year and 11 months in prison at Ipswich Magistrates’ Court on Nov. 9.
PC Denzel Boachie said: “Lundgren is a
prolific thief
who has continued to offend despite being given previous opportunities by the courts.
“His repeated offending has now caught up with him and he will spend time behind bars.”
