CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Tuesday’s general election brought few surprises across Wyoming, with one notable exception: a Laramie County School District 2 board race that came down to just a single vote, prompting an official recount.
Three LCSD2 trustee seats were up for grabs this cycle. With 1,345 and 1,175 votes, respectively, trustee-elects Michael Lerwick and Kendra Roeder won their seats handily.
The third seat was narrowly won out by Ashley Jessen. With 1,003 votes, she was elected to the position just barely ahead of challenger Lisa Heinert, who earned 1,002.
With the race being decided by just one vote, Laramie County Clerk Debra Lee said, a recount is in order. Wyoming law requires a recount when the margin between the winner and the loser is less than 1% of votes cast.
“This close election proves that every vote matters,” Lee said.
Results of Laramie County’s 2026 General Election become official on Friday, Nov. 6, when the County Canvassing Board meets at 1 p.m. in the
