Cheyenne man faces 13 felonies after police find connection to gun theft conspiracy

CHEYENNE, Wyo, — A Cheyenne man is being charged with 13 felonies related to theft and illegal gun ownership after a months-long criminal investigation, including multiple searches, culminated in a joint police-ATF raid. Combined, his charges carry a maximum potential penalty of nearly 90 years in prison.
Police charged Jason Vargas, born 1992, with one count of conspiracy to commit theft, six counts of firearm theft and six counts of possession of a firearm by someone convicted of a violent felony. Each charge is a felony. Vargas is considered innocent on all charges until pleading or being found guilty.
According to his criminal affidavit, police have connected Vargas to a larger local network of firearm thefts and illegal dealing. In one April incident mentioned by police, another suspect reportedly broke into a storage unit in Cheyenne and stole 29 firearms that he would later sell. He was reportedly assisted by both his mother and his girlfriend in the stealing, transportation and sale of the guns.
Some of them, police said, ended up in the hands of Vargas. He was identified as a suspect early on in the police’s investigation — text messages from the original suspect’s girlfriend’s reportedly revealed her as a liaison between Vargas and the alleged thief — and was arrested April 14, 2026.
He was arrested in possession of multiple firearms that matched descriptions of some stolen from the storage unit a week prior, police said. Interviews with others reported to be involved in the plan to steal and sell the firearms also revealed text conversations where Vargas arranged a purchase. According to one suspect, he bought some for around $1,000.
A search of Vargas’s residence — a camper trailer parked on his brother’s property — a few weeks later turned up even more guns. According to police, the brother reported that he also owned firearms that he purchase from one of Vargas’s friends earlier, but that he had a notarized bill of sale for the transaction.
While interviewing a couple that police also suspected of purchasing some of the firearms, which they learned about through a confidential source, police were told that multiple firearms were missed during both their searches of Vargas’s truck and his trailer. The confidential source also told police around this time that Vargas had originally ordered the theft of the guns in April.
On Oct. 27, with knowledge that more guns may be inside, Cheyenne police and agents from the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco and Firearms conducted another search of Vargas’s home and did find an additional AR-15 style rifle.
By the end of their investigation, police had found in Vargas’s house and truck a Girsan Untouchable MC 1911 handgun, a Heritage Rough Rider revolver, a Ruger Single-Six revolver, a KelTec PMR-30 handgun, a GSG Firefly handgun and a Diamondback DB-15 rifle.
Vargas was issued a $50,000 cash bond and will have a preliminary hearing at the Laramie County Circuit Court on Nov. 12. If the judge finds probable cause, his 13 felonies will be taken up by the district court.
