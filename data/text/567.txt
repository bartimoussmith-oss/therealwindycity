Wyoming Senate Republicans elect new leadership team, including Cheyenne’s Tara Nethercott

CHEYENNE, Wyo. —  On Saturday, the Wyoming Senate Republican Caucus announced that it had selected its new leadership team for the 69th Wyoming Legislature.
A release from the Wyoming Senate Republican Caucus says that it has nominated Cheyenne’s own Tara Nethercott as its Senate president. It has also nominated Evanston’s Wendy Schuler as the majority floor leader and Ten Sleep’s Ed Cooper as Senate vice president.
The release notes that the team brings decades of combined legislative, professional and public service experience to the team, along with a shared commitment to “principled governance, fiscal responsibility, economic stability, and preserving Wyoming’s strong tradition of citizen leadership.”
Together, the senators intend to “advance a thoughtful and disciplined legislative agenda that promotes a strong economy, responsible stewardship of public resources, and long-term stability for Wyoming families, businesses, and communities.”
“Wyoming deserves leadership that is steady in its principles, disciplined in its decisions, and focused on the future,” Nethercott said. “Our responsibility is to strengthen the foundations of our state, protect the opportunities that make Wyoming exceptional, and ensure the decisions we make today serve generations to come.”
The incoming leadership team emphasized the importance of restoring confidence in the legislative process, encouraging meaningful debate, and ensuring that sound public policy takes precedence over political division.
“Good government requires more than good intentions. It requires experience, respect for the institution, and the willingness to work together to achieve meaningful results,” Nethercott said. “We share a commitment to a Legislature that is effective, accountable, and focused on the people of Wyoming.”
The release says that the Republican caucus’s nominations for Senate president and vice president will go before the full Senate for formal election when the 69th Wyoming Legislature convenes in January 2027.
About the Senate Republican Leadership Team
Senator Tara Nethercott — Cheyenne
Senate President-Elect
Senator Tara Nethercott has represented Senate District 4 in Laramie County since 2017. An attorney, she previously served as Senate Majority Floor Leader and chaired several legislative committees, including Appropriations, Judiciary, and Capital Financing and Investments. Her legislative work has focused on fiscal policy, economic development, financial innovation, and responsible governance. She is a graduate of the University of Wyoming College of Law.
Senator Wendy Schuler — Evanston
Senate Majority Floor Leader
Senator Wendy Schuler has represented Senate District 15 in Uinta County since 2019. A retired educator and University of Wyoming graduate, she has served as Chair of the Senate Education Committee and worked extensively on education policy, school finance, and issues affecting Wyoming families and communities. Her background in education and community service brings a practical perspective to legislative leadership.
Senator Ed Cooper — Ten Sleep
Senate Vice President-Elect
Senator Ed Cooper has represented Senate District 20 in north-central Wyoming since 2021. A longtime Wyoming businessman and oil and gas consultant, Cooper brings extensive private-sector experience in energy, natural resources, and economic development. His legislative service includes work on the Minerals, Business and Economic Development Committee and the Transportation, Highways and Military Affairs Committee. A native of the Big Horn Basin, Cooper has longstanding ties to Wyoming’s agricultural and energy industries.
