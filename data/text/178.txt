Laramie County jail inmate charged with throwing urine on deputy

Laramie County Sheriff’s Department and Detention Center (Lisa Hushbeck/Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A Laramie County Detention Center inmate was charged with aggravated assault and battery on a detention officer after reportedly throwing a cup of urine on officers from their cell Monday. The charge, a felony, could be punished with up to five years in prison if he is found guilty.
According to a criminal affidavit filed by Laramie County Sheriff’s deputies, a corrections officer was delivering meal trays to inmates the afternoon of Monday, Oct. 26. Upon opening the port to the cell of Noah Brunner, born in 1999, they were reportedly splashed with liquid from a cup.
The contents covered the deputy’s entire right side — their arm, torso and thigh — according to the affidavit.
The deputy closed the port to the cell and realized that another inmate, sitting down at Central Booking waiting to be released, was also splashed by the liquid. They reportedly said they weren’t injured but that they were deeply uncomfortable and confused. They were given an opportunity to shower before being released. Upon their exit, they reportedly told officers they intended to press charges against Brunner.
Security footage from the cell reportedly depicted Brunner covering the camera with a wet piece of toilet paper, keeping it on there for about an hour and a half before taking it off. While the camera captured the moment Brunner reportedly splashed the deputy and other inmate, he couldn’t be observed actually urinating into the cup.
Still, the deputy reported being confident the liquid was urine due to its consistency, temperature and smell. Officers later found it around the area as described.
The next day, deputies interviewed Brunner about the incident, after which he was given criminal charges, later being issued a $3,000 cash bond. He has a preliminary hearing in Laramie County Circuit Court on Nov. 6. He is presumed innocent until pleading or being found guilty.
