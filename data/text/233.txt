Major League Baseball
American League
BALTIMORE ORIOLES — Agreed to terms with OF Rece Hinds on a one-year contract.
BOSTON RED SOX — Acquired cash considerations from Detroit in exchange for INF Tsung-Che Cheng.
CLEVELAND GUARDIANS — Selected the contract of SS Milan Tolentino from Columbus (IL).
LOS ANGELES ANGELS — Reinstated RHP Jack Kochanowicz from the 60-day IL. Selected the contract of LHP Nicolo Pinazzi from Rocket City (SL).
TORONTO BLUE JAYS — Assigned OF Rudy Martin Jr. to Buffalo (IL).
National League
SAN FRANCISCO GIANTS — Sent C Andrew Knizner outright to Sacramento (PCL).
FOOTBALL
National Football League
CAROLINA PANTHERS — Fired defensive coordinator Ejiro Evero and defensive passing game coordinator Jonathan Cooley.
INDIANAPOLIS COLTS — Signed DT Kevin Givens to the active roster.
MIAMI DOLPHINS — Signed G DJ Campbell to the practice squad. Released T Gottlieb Ayedze from the practice squad. Claimed EDGE Jamree Kromah off waivers from Chicago. Waived EDGE Rob Beal Jr.
TENNESSEE TITANS — Signed DT Timmy Horne to the active roster. Signed LB Dominique Hampton to the practice squad.
HOCKEY
National Hockey League
NHL — Suspended Ottawa F Hayden Hodgson two games for charging and boarding Los Angeles D Brandt Clarke on Oct. 29.
CALGARY FLAMES — Recalled F William Strömgren and G Arsenii Sergeev from Calgary (AHL). Placed G Devin Cooley on injured reserve.
DALLAS STARS — Loaned F Matthew Seminoff to Texas (AHL).
DETROIT RED WINGS — Assigned D Axel Sandin-Pellikka to Grand Rapids (AHL). Recalled D Jacob Bernard-Docker from Grand Rapids.
MINNESOTA WILD — Assigned D Carson Lambos and F Mason Shaw to Iowa (AHL).
OTTAWA SENATORS — Placed F Warren Foegele on injured reserve.
ST. LOUIS BLUES — Recalled D Theo Lindstein from Springfield (AHL).
VANCOUVER CANUCKS — Recalled F Jacob Quillan from Abbotsford (AHL). Placed F Filip Chytil on injured reserve.
VEGAS GOLDEN KNIGHTS — Assigned G Cameron Whitehead to Henderson (AHL). Recalled F Jonas Rondbjerg from Henderson.
