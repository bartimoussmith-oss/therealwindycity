CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Lauren Schoenfeld, executive director of the Wyoming Innovation Partnership and a former Sweetwater County commissioner, has won the race for the Wyoming Senate District 5 seat.
In Tuesday’s election, Schoenfeld received 3,356 votes to Democrat challenger Ted Hanlon’s 1,389. She will be succeeding outgoing Sen. Lynn Hutchings.
In August’s primary,
Schoenfeld won the GOP’s nomination
with 1,252 votes, beating out runner-up Ben Hornok with 1,185. Meagan Herrington came third with 373 votes, while Ronald McCleary took fourth with 120.
Schoenfeld’s campaign emphasized tax reform, regulatory relief and advocacy for the state energy economy. She proposed conducting a review of the state tax system to deliver homeowner relief, removing development barriers to lower housing costs and expanding workforce training partnerships across Wyoming.
