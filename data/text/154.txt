Mild stretch to carry Cheyenne through Halloween before colder weather arrives

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Laramie County will hold on to warm, dry weather through the end of the week, giving trick-or-treaters mild conditions ahead of a storm system bringing colder air and precipitation chances to southeast Wyoming.
The National Weather Service in Cheyenne said temperatures will run 5–10 degrees above average across southeast Wyoming through Thursday. Cheyenne will see clouds clear today for a sunny afternoon with a high near 62 degrees and west wind gusts reaching 25 mph. The overnight low will fall to around 38.
Dry, calm weather continues Thursday and Friday. Afternoon highs will hover near 61 each day under mostly sunny skies, while nighttime temperatures remain in the upper 30s.
Moisture will increase across southeast Wyoming late Friday, though meteorologists expect rain showers to remain mostly east toward the Nebraska state line.
Saturday will bring another mild afternoon, reaching a high near 60. Families heading out for Halloween festivities Saturday evening will see temperatures drop into the 40s by 8 p.m. Meteorologists expect showers to hold off across the plains until late Saturday night or early Sunday morning, keeping costume walks mostly dry.
Cooler, unsettled weather begins to push into southeast Wyoming on Sunday. Cheyenne will see a high near 56 with a slight chance of showers in the afternoon. Rain showers become likely Sunday night as the low drops to around 35.
Rain and snow showers are likely Monday across Laramie County, alongside breezy winds and a high near 47. Temperatures will drop to around 30 degrees Monday night with blustery winds, followed by a slight chance of snow and a high near 49 on Tuesday.
Detailed Forecast
Today:
Mostly cloudy, then gradually becoming sunny, with a high near 62. West wind at 10–15 mph, with gusts as high as 25 mph.
Tonight:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 38. South-southwest wind at 5–10 mph calming and shifting to the west after midnight.
Thursday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 61. Light and variable wind shifting to the south-southeast at 5–10 mph in the morning.
Thursday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 38. South wind at around 5 mph.
Friday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 61. South-southeast wind at 5–10 mph.
Friday Night:
Mostly cloudy, with a low around 39.
Saturday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 60.
Saturday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 36.
Sunday:
A slight chance of showers after noon. Mostly sunny, with a high near 56.
Sunday Night:
Showers likely. Cloudy, with a low around 35.
Monday:
Rain and snow showers likely. Partly sunny, with a high near 47. Breezy.
Monday Night:
A chance of rain and snow showers. Partly cloudy, with a low around 30. Blustery.
Tuesday:
A slight chance of snow showers. Mostly sunny, with a high near 49. Breezy.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
