NCHS takes 2026 championship with 28–13 win over Cheyenne East in Laramie
by
Dan Cepeda
November 14, 2026
November 15, 2026

LARAMIE, Wyo. –– The Natrona County High School Mustangs wrapped up their undefeated 2026 season with a 28–13 win over Cheyenne East on Saturday during the 4A Wyoming High School State Football Championships at War Memorial Stadium in Laramie.
The win also likely marks the end of Steve Harshman’s unprecedented career at NC, as he is finishing up his 36th year as head coach after being elected as state superintendent of public instruction. Harshman had previously retired from teaching while continuing to coach at NCHS.
In a tearful speech to his team, Harshman praised his players for their hard work and exceptional season.
“We’ve really grown into a tight brotherhood,” he said. “Whether you win the last one or lose the last one, it’s sad because you just love each other and I really appreciate you,”
He ended his talk by proclaiming, “I am a Mustang until I die.”
