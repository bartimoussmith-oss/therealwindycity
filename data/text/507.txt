Cheyenne Happenings (11/13, 11/14): Stephen King’s ‘Misery,’ Cheyenne Artwalk, more

November 12, 2026

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The weekend is almost upon us, which means you made it through another week. You should be proud of yourself, and you should treat yourself to the myriad of activities happening throughout Cheyenne this weekend. From a stage production of Stephen King’s “Misery” to the Second Friday Cheyenne Artwalk, this is what’s happening this weekend in Cheyenne.
Story Time at Cheyenne Botanic Gardens
Paul Smith Children’s Village is hosting a Story Time event at the Cheyenne Botanic Gardens on Friday, beginning at 11 a.m.
“Join us Fridays at 11 a.m. for a story, a fun project, and shenanigans,”
the event page states
. “Meet under the blue dome during the summer. During the winter, meet us in the classroom in the Children’s Village.”
Happening Friday at 11 a.m.
Book Babies
On Friday, the Laramie County Library is hosting Book Babies from 9:15 to 10 a.m.
“Book Babies is a shorter, less-structured early literacy class just for babies (best for ages 0–24 months)!”
the event page states
. “It’s a great time to bond with your baby and inspire a love of reading. You will learn lap bounces, tickle rhymes, movement songs, and fingerplays that you can do with your baby at home, too! We will typically read one developmentally appropriate book and explore one flannel story each week. It’s also a great time for babies to start learning how to socialize with other children. After the stories, we have Stay and Play where babies can play with developmentally appropriate toys while caregivers visit.”
Happening Friday at 9:15 a.m.
November’s Second Friday Artwalk
On Friday, the Second Friday Artwalk returns to Cheyenne from 5 to 8 p.m.
“Get into the wintery spirit with us, and join us for Artwalk this November!”
the event page states
. “We’re gearing up with yummy beer at Blue Raven Brewery, local artistry on their walls, and more… Here’s what you can expect this month: Local art across downtown | Featured Venue – Blue Raven Brewery | Live music | More information about Artwalk and its other venues. Cheyenne Second Friday Artwalk is the community’s monthly celebration of artists and their work. During the Artwalk, Cheyenne galleries, businesses, restaurants and other places showcase a local or regional visual artist or musician, accompanied by light food and beverages. Artwalk patrons visit the venues on an unstructured schedule, walking, driving, or biking.”
Happening Friday at 5 p.m.
‘Antigone’ by Sophocles
On Friday, Laramie County Community College is hosting its production of Antigone, beginning at 7 p.m.
“A college theater production of Sophocles’ classic tragedy at Laramie County Community College,”
the event page states
. “The play runs for two weekends, presenting ancient Greek drama in an academic performance setting.”
Happening Friday at 7 p.m.
Stephen King’s ‘Misery’
On Friday and Saturday, the Cheyenne Little Theatre Players are hosting a production of Stephen King’s “Misery,” beginning at 7 p.m.
“This psychological thriller based on Stephen King’s novel ‘Misery’ follows successful romance novelist Paul Sheldon, who is rescued from a car crash and wakes up captive in a secluded home owned by his self-identified ‘number one fan,’ Annie Wilkes,”
the event page states
. “While Paul is convalescing, Annie reads the manuscript of his latest book and becomes enraged when she discovers the author has killed off her favorite character, Misery Chastain. Annie forces Paul to write a new Misery novel and he quickly realizes Annie has no intention of letting him go. The irate Annie has Paul writing as if his life depends on it — and it does.”
Happening Friday and Saturday at 7 p.m.
FREE Food Drive Movie: ‘The Wild Robot’
On Saturday, Capitol Cinema is hosting a FREE Food Drive Movie, “The Wild Robot,” beginning at 10 a.m.
“Mark your calendar for SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 14 for a FREE SCREENING of DreamWorks’ THE WILD ROBOT at 10 a.m.!”
the event page states
. “Admission is one nonperishable or canned food item to support our local food bank. Seating is first-come, first-served!”
Happening Saturday at 10 a.m.
Festive Layers: A Decoupage Workshop
On Saturday, Cheyenne Botanic Gardens is hosting Festive Layers: A Decoupage Workshop, beginning at 10:30 a.m.
“Looking for a Fun Holiday Craft?”
the event page asks
. “We have the perfect workshop for you and your friends! Join us for our Decoupage Wood Ornament Class on Saturday, November 14, from 10:30 a.m. to 12 p.m. Get creative, enjoy festive fun, and make a unique ornament for your holiday decorations. Grab your friends and register today!”
Happening Saturday at 10:30 a.m.
The Merry Christmas Spider
Speaking of the Cheyenne Botanic Gardens, its also hosting a class in which attendees can make The Merry Christmas Spider, beginning at 10:30 a.m.
“Bring in the holiday season by creating a unique beaded spider ornament for your Christmas tree!”
the event page states
. “For $40 per person, you’ll create your own beautiful ornament while learning about the fascinating tradition behind the Christmas spider and its special place in holiday celebrations. All supplies are provided. Join our very own Cheyenne Botanic Gardens volunteer, Yvonne Peterson, as she spins the story of this classic holiday ornament and shares the tradition behind it.”
Happening Saturday at 10:30 a.m.
Fall Wreath Drop-In Class at the Paul Smith Children’s Village
On Saturday, Paul Smith Children’s Village is hosting a wreath-making class for kids of all ages, beginning at 1 p.m.
“Join us in the Children’s Village at 1 p.m. to make a Thanksgiving wreath with handprints,”
the event page states
. “Attendance is free, and all materials are provided.”
Happening Saturday at 1 p.m.
St. Martin’s Lantern Walk
The Paul Smith Children’s Village is also hosting a St. Martin’s Lantern Walk, beginning at 5 p.m.
“St. Martin’s Day is a beloved children’s holiday in Germany with homemade lanterns, songs, and stories,”
the event page states
. “Meet darkness with light to bring an end to the harvest season and join us to learn the traditions, make a lantern, and end with a short parade around the Children’s Village. This is a fun-filled event for families. Pre-registration is required and the event is located in the Paul Smith Children’s Village on November 14 from 5 to 6 p.m.”
Happening Saturday at 5 p.m.
Shostakovich Triumphant at the Cheyenne Civic Center
On Saturday, the Cheyenne Civic Center is hosting a classical concert celebrating the music of Shostakovich.
“The Cheyenne Symphony Orchestra invites you to an unforgettable evening at Shostakovich Triumphant,”
the event page states
. “This captivating concert will showcase the vibrant Overture to Glinka’s ‘Ruslan and Ludmilla,’ setting the stage for an exhilarating performance. Experience the emotional depth of Elgar’s Cello Concerto, featuring Myra Monfort Artist-in-Residence, Zuill Bailey. The program culminates with Shostakovich’s powerful Symphony No. 5, a masterful work that resonates with triumph and resilience.”
Happening Saturday at 7:30 p.m.
