CHEYENNE – A late touchdown in the final seconds of the first half helped Pine Bluffs pull away for a 41-0 win over Big Piney in the Class 1A nine-man quarterfinals Friday in Pine Bluffs.
The Hornets led 7-0 going into the final minute, but a turnover by Big Piney gave Pine Bluffs the ball back. They scored with under 10 seconds remaining in the half to go up 14-0, and rattled off 21 points in the fourth quarter to pull away.
Senior running back Gabe Federer scored three rushing touchdowns and ran for 137 yards on 19 carries. Senior wide receiver Cade Fornstrom caught five passes for 80 yards and a touchdown. Senior Colby Lerwick had 6½ tackles and two interceptions on the defensive end.
