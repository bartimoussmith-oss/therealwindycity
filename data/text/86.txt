LCCC theater performing Neil LaBute’s ‘Reasons to Be Pretty’ this November

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Laramie County Community College’s theater program will perform “Reasons to Be Pretty,” a comedy-drama exploring relationships, personal insecurities and society’s obsession with physical appearance, multiple times next month. Tickets are on sale now.
Written by playwright
Neil LaBute
, “Reasons to Be Pretty” tells the story of a couple whose relationship begins to fall apart after an insensitive remark about appearance sets off a chain of confrontations, a release by LCCC advertising the show said. The conflict expands into their social lives, forcing the characters to face uncomfortable questions about honesty, loyalty, attraction and the expectations people place on themselves and others.
“I’m not going to lie. This is going to be a hard play to watch,” Jason Pasqua, LCCC theater instructor, said in the school release. “That’s probably not a great thing to say to a person who just sat down in a theatre, but it’s true. It’s going to be hard to watch because it’s going to challenge you in a few ways. Neil LaBute is one of America’s finest playwrights, and he writes stories that don’t shy away from the complicated things we pretend don’t exist and don’t want to think about.”
Audiences will be faced with an uncomfortable question as well: How much importance does society places on physical beauty and the consequences of judging others by their appearance?
According to LCCC, the stage design will be rather unique. The stage is placed in the middle of the theater, rotating throughout the performance to reveal different conversations and interactions on either side.
“The changing perspectives reinforce the play’s exploration of relationships, allowing the audience to see how the same conflict unfolds from different points of view,” LCCC said.
Performances are set for 7 p.m. Nov. 12–14 and 19–21 in the College Community Center Playhouse on LCCC’s Cheyenne campus. Tickets can be purchased
here
.
LCCC advises that the production contains adult content, themes and language and is not recommended for audience members under 18 years of age.
