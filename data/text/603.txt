ROCK SPRINGS -- November is the time of year that Scouting America troops and packs all over the U.S. sponsor their annual Scouting for Food Drive. Local scouts, parents, and leaders worked together on Saturday, Nov. 21 to collect 4,457 pounds of food for the Food Bank of Sweetwater County. Among those helping out were the Chatwin family. Pictured from left are Scout BSA Kaylub Chatwin, Cub Scout Exavior Chatwin, leader Kali Chatwin and Cub Scout Annalyse Chatwin.
