Nov. 3 Is National Sandwich Day — Sink Your Teeth into 11 Deals

Hold on to your hoagies, because Tuesday, Nov. 3 is National Sandwich Day. A variety of restaurants are celebrating with a spread of upper-crust
deals and discounts
.
Get a free Footlong at Subway, a free wrap at Potbelly or check with your sandwich shop of choice for a local special.
Here are some deals worth relishing:
4 Rivers Smokehouse:
Get the Pulled Pork or Pulled Chicken Sandwich for $6. Available for dine-in and online ordering with code SANDDAY.
Capriotti’s Sandwich Shop:
Rewards members can buy one sub and get a second for 50% off in-shop, online or through the Capriotti’s app.
Farmer Boys:
Very Important Farmer (VIF) loyalty members can get a Bacon Turkey Melt for $5.99 on Nov. 3 and Nov. 4. Order in-store or online.
Firehouse Subs:
Get a free medium sub when you spend $15 or more through the Firehouse Subs app or online.
Jersey Mike’s:
Get free delivery for all orders over $5. Good for app and online orders.
Kravitz Deli:
Save $2 on all 6-ounce sandwiches.
The Kroft:
Buy one sandwich, get the second sandwich 50% off. Available in-store only.
McAlister’s
:
Rewards members can grab a regular sandwich for $5 from Oct. 30 to Nov. 3.
Potbelly:
Get a free Original sandwich or wrap when you buy any BIG or Original sandwich or wrap online or through the app. Use code BOGO at checkout.
Schlotzsky’s:
Rewards members can score medium sandwiches for $5 from Oct. 31 to Nov. 3, for up to 10 sandwiches per order.
Subway:
Take them up on their BOGO Footlong deal, running through Nov. 8.
Check each retailer’s offer for full details and exclusions.
While national and regional chains tend to have the biggest platforms to advertise their deals, check in with your local favorites, too.
Social media is another great way to see if a retailer is celebrating the day with a freebie or a discount.
