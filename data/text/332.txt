CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The 90th Missile Wing at the F.E. Warren Air Force Base recently announced the death of Airman 1st Class Daniel Thornton.
A release from the Air Force Base says that Thornton passed away on Wednesday, Oct. 28. He served as a Security Forces defender, assigned to the 90th Security Forces Squadron on the base.
“On behalf of every Airman and family member across F.E. Warren, my wife and I extend our deepest condolences, thoughts, and prayers to Daniel’s family, friends, and fellow defenders during this unimaginably difficult time,” said Col. Terry Holmes, 90th Missile Wing commander. “Airman Thornton was a skilled professional who carried out his duties with dedication, discipline, and pride. His absence leaves a void in our formation.”
The release notes that Airman 1st Class Thornton served on active duty from Oct. 15, 2024, to Oct. 28, 2026.
“Out of respect for the family’s privacy during their bereavement, further details regarding the circumstances will not be released at this time,” the release states.
