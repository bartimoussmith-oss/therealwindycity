South High School junior Zoe Vazquez is the LCSD1 student of the week for Oct. 26, 2026 (LCSD1)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — South High School junior Zoe Vazquez is the Laramie County School District 1 student of the week for the week of Oct. 26. He was chosen for his leadership within the school and his academic excellence.
Vazquez wears many hats at South High School. He is the student council vice president of public relations and a member of the National Honor Society, and even helps produce the school yearbook.
He takes Advanced Placement and honors classes, maintains a high GPA, is enrolled in the Laramie County Community College Accelerate Program and is working toward his Seal of Biliteracy in English and Spanish.
The junior is also well-regarded among his peers and educators because of his positive attitude, kindness and inclusiveness. He leads by example, the district said, encouraging those around him to be themselves and challenging himself and others to grow.
That attitude doesn’t stay in the classroom. When he and his family take their annual trip to Mexico, Vasquez reportedly helps people experiencing homelessness.
He plans to attend the University of Wyoming, likely to study interior design or architecture. His favorite teacher is Paige Cathcart, who he said puts a lot of time and effort into creating engaging opportunities in her classes and through student council. He appreciates her leadership, meaningful connections and commitment to building community at South, the district said.
“Zoe doesn’t just participate in a community; he actively makes it better,” Cathcart said.
