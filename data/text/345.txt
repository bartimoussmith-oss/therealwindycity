Cheyenne woman arrested after reportedly attacking officers responding to car crash

(Gregory Hirst, Oil City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A Cheyenne woman is facing multiple criminal charges, including a felony, after reportedly attacking multiple police officers responding to a car crash. If she is convicted, she could go to jail for multiple years.
Police are charging Chaundra Barkell, born in 1986, with interference with a police officer causing bodily injury, which carries a maximum sentence of 10 years. She also faces misdemeanor reckless driving, resisting arrest and failure to stop causing damage charges, which carry less severe potential penalties.
Barkell is presumed innocent until pleading or being found guilty in court. As of midday Nov. 4, she has not had a preliminary hearing scheduled, nor has she been issued a bond.
According to her criminal affidavit, Barkell’s arrest was preceded by a Nov. 2 car accident. After Barkell was arrested and police were able to interview the driver of the Toyota pickup that she had hit and the driver of a bus that witnessed the crash, they reportedly learned that Barkell was following the bus down Campstool Road in east Cheyenne, driving erratically and honking at it.
Behind Barkell, who was driving a Jeep, was the Toyota. According to the interviewee, they were driving near Barkell normally when Barkell stopped in the middle of the road and then off onto a side street and onto the side of that road. The Toyota attempted to drive past Barkell, but she reportedly drove directly into the side of the truck. Barkell then drove away until the Toyota sped up after her and rear-ended her Jeep.
When police arrived on the scene and attempted to speak to Barkell, she reportedly refused to leave her car. She did not listen to multiple requests to roll down her window, but eventually rolled it down partially. Still, she did not comply with commands to exit the car.
One officer reportedly tried opening the door, at which point Barkell began aggressively kicking him and then punching his face and chest area, the affidavit states. She also bit him on the shoulder, breaking skin.
Another officer broke the window on the car’s passenger side to attempt to gain control of Barkell from behind, but his attempt to put her in handcuffs resulted in him getting cut multiple times by the broken glass. Barkell then reportedly became rigid to prevent officers from getting her out of the car.
She was placed in a WRAP restraint and placed in an ambulance to seek treatment at the Cheyenne Regional Medical Center. While being transported, she reportedly told officers that the bus she was following was an ambulance and that she was following it to the hospital. The Toyota, she said, turned in front of her to stop her from going to the hospital, so she hit it.
