St. Mary’s Catholic School’s Wine Fest and Casino Night set for Nov. 6

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Raising money for teachers, students and programs, St. Mary’s Catholic School will host its yearly Wine Fest on Nov. 6 at the Little America Hotel and Resort. Back by popular demand, the event’s Casino Night returns for its second year.
Fourteen wine vendors from around Wyoming will bring their beverages to Cheyenne for the event, letting participants partake in the best wine Wyoming has to offer. Attendees can also enjoy hors d’oeuvres from local Cheyenne food establishments. The school still recommends guests eat before arriving to the event, as no full meals will be served.
The Casino Night portion of the event returns for its second year after last year’s success. Players competing in the casino games will have the chance to win first, second or third place, with top finishers choosing their reward from three classroom prize baskets.
Guests can also participate in a silent auction, where up to 50 items — including more classroom baskets curated by St. Mary’s students and families — will be up for grabs. While items will be showcased live at Little America for same-night pickup, bidding will be conducted via smartphone mobile devices. Raffles will also take place throughout the night.
Doors open for VIP guests at 5:30 p.m. Nov. 6, while general admission begins at 6:30 p.m. All proceeds from the evening directly benefit the students, teachers and academic programs at
St. Mary’s Catholic School
