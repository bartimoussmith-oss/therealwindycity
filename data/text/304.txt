CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Incumbent Laramie County School District 2 Trustees Billie Wilson,  Michael Lerwick, and Kendra Roeder have retained seats on the board after Tuesday’s election. They will be joined by newcomers Kelly Greonweg and Ashley Jessen, both of whom upended incumbents Tansie Derner and Mikki Munson.
Wilson ran unchallenged for her Area D seat. Kelly Groeneweg secured 841 votes to defeat Mikki Munson with 712 votes and Arthur Botelha with 299 votes for the Area F seat.
Three At-Large seats were up for election this year. Lerwick, Roeder and Jessen were the top vote-getters from a pool of five. Lerwick received 1,345 votes, Roeder got 1,175 votes and Jessen netted 1,003 votes. Challenger Lisa Heinert and incumbent Tanise Derner received 1,002 votes and 844 votes, respectively.
