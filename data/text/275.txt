(LETTER) Chuck Gray can’t hack it

Dear Cheyenne,
It has come full circle.
Our administration has gone from arresting, deporting and occasionally murdering immigrants and American citizens — frequently under the guise of criminality — to flagrantly, willfully harboring international fugitives.
This same administration has gone from requesting states’ voter rolls while alleging impropriety to outright interfering in state elections in direct defiance of the U.S. Constitution and in spite of multiple courts’ rulings.
This same administration of “chaos and cruelty” has endorsed Chuck Gray to be Wyoming’s next congressman.
Chuck Gray, who has never held a private-sector job outside his daddy’s payroll.
Chuck Gray, who bristles at anyone who questions his earnest bootlicking of the Election Deniers and would-be meddlers with labels of “leftist,” “insider” and “victims of Trump Derangement Syndrome.”
Chuck Gray, who refuses to show up for moderated public debate, presumably because he believes he has this one in the bag.
And yet in the primary election, nearly three in every four Wyoming voters picked someone else. Why?
Because Wyoming voters can plainly see Gray isn’t up to the job. Indeed, he failed to do it when the Department of Justice came a-knocking, and he has demonstrated little interest in accountability to the same public he’s asking to elect him.
For those who still cannot read the writing on the wall, I can only hope they rely on another sense: Wyoming voters know bull when they smell it, and its only remedy is a good flush.
Anyone but Chuck 2026.
Warren Appel
Cheyenne
