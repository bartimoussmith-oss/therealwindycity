Harrisburg, Pa. — Rep. Clint Owlett, R-Pa., has been re-elected by his peers to serve as secretary of the House Republican Caucus for the 2027-28 legislative session, the House Republican caucus announced.
Owlett represents Tioga and Bradford counties. His first term as secretary was marked by a significant expansion of the office’s role in mentoring newly elected lawmakers throughout their first term, according to the caucus.
“To ensure the best outcomes for all Pennsylvanians, we want to make sure our new members are well equipped to handle the many facets of the job, from constituent service to drafting legislation to understanding the role various state agencies and row offices play,” Owlett said. “It’s been rewarding to help these public servants learn and grow in their roles. I look forward to continuing that in the next session.”
In addition to the mentoring role, the caucus secretary is responsible for recording all official legislative activities that take place in the House of Representatives.
“My goal is to always make sure the voices of my constituents in Tioga and Bradford counties, as well as the entire Northern Tier, are heard loud and clear at the capitol,” Owlett said. “We will be doing our best to combat the troubling agenda being put forth by the Shapiro administration and Democrat majorities in both the House and Senate.”
Owlett joins a House Republican leadership team that also includes Rep. David Rowe, R-Pa., who was named House Republican Policy Committee chair. Rowe’s district includes Snyder and Union counties, as well as Mifflin and Juniata counties.
