Charles "Chuck" Barhite
Charles “Chuck” Barhite:
1940 – 2026
Chuck was born on August 21, 1940, in Idaho Springs, Colorado, to Arnold and Florence (Burnett) Barhite. He graduated from Cheyenne High School in 1958 and went on to proudly serve his country in the United States Navy. Following his military service, he attended the University of Wyoming and spent many years working for the Wyoming Department of Transportation.
Chuck had a passion for life and enjoyed spending his time doing the things he loved most. Whether working on his hot rods, hunting, fishing, bowling, or playing a round of golf, he appreciated the simple pleasures of life like playing card games with family. Throughout his life he was always checking his coins for a treasure because old treasures he loved. He also loved traveling, exploring new places, and enjoying the experiences and memories that came with each adventure. Sweets always came first.
Above all, he treasured the time spent with family and friends and the memories made along the way.
Chuck is survived by his loving wife, Jaxine Barhite; his children, Kara Bowcutt (Slade) and Kent Barhite; his stepchildren, Erica Tonso (Shawn Roe) and Tyler Tonso; seven grandchildren; and his sisters, Helen Huff and Dorothy Shaffer (John) and their families.
He was preceded in death by his parents, Arnold and Florence Barhite; and his step-father Ralph Clark.
A funeral service will be held at Wiederspahn-Radomsky Chapel on December 1, 2026 at 10 a.m located at 1900 E. 19th Street in Cheyenne, Wyoming. A Celebration of Life will follow at The Gathering Place.
Chuck will be fondly remembered by those who knew and loved him. His memory will live on through his family, friends, and the many lives he touched throughout his 86 years.
Matthew Stephen Engle:
d. 2026
With profound sadness and immeasurable love, we announce the passing of Matthew Stephen Engle, affectionately known as Matt, on November 2, 2026, at the age of 31.
Born in Minneapolis, Minnesota, Matt moved to Cheyenne, Wyoming, in second grade, where he grew up and formed lifelong friendships.
In 2014, Matt enlisted in the United States Navy, beginning his career at Great Lakes, Illinois. Rising to the rank of Petty Officer First Class (E-6), he completed five deployments and served aboard several ships, including the USS George Washington, USS Ronald Reagan, USS Kidd, USNS Mercy, and USS Stethem. His Navy career took him around the world, where he built friendships and made memories that would last a lifetime.
Beyond the uniform, Matt was known for his humor, adventurous spirit, love of music, and devotion to family and friends. He loved Broncos and Nuggets games, traveling, and his dream Ford Bronco, affectionately named Bronceesha.
Matt is survived by his loving mother, Heather; aunt, Heidi; uncle, Jarod; aunt, Jenny; great-aunt, Ruby; great-uncle, Bud; his godmother, Stephanie; many cousins; and countless friends and Navy brothers and sisters.
He was preceded in death by his nana, Glenna; grandpa, Nick; grandma, Denise; and his beloved Ciena.
A funeral service with military honors will be held Friday, November 13, 2026, at 11 a.m. at Schrader, Aragon & Jacoby Funeral Home in Cheyenne, Wyoming.
A celebration of life will follow at 4:30 p.m. at The Crown Underground, with food and refreshments provided.
Burial is planned for December 4, 2026, with final arrangements to be announced.
Matt’s life was so much more than the uniform he wore or the places he traveled. His legacy lives on through the laughter he shared, the friendships he made, and the many lives he touched.
William A. “Bill” Hemenover:
1949 – 2026
William A. “Bill” Hemenover, 64, of Cheyenne passed away November 4, 2026, in Cheyenne.
He was born August 8, 1949, in Cheyenne Wyoming, to Harry and Betty Hemnover
She is survived by his daughter Cara (Chris) Johnson of Alabama; and two grandchildren, Tavia and Briley.
He was preceded in death by his wife, Celeste Hemenover; and his parents, Harry and Betty Hemenover.
Cremation services are under the care of Schrader, Aragon and Jacoby Funeral Home, 2222 Russell Ave., Cheyenne, WY 82001.
In lieu of flowers please make donations in Bill’s name to a charity of your choice.
