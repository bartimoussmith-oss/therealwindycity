Carey Junior High eighth grader Nayessa Walsh is LCSD1 student of the week for Nov. 2, 2026 (LCSD1)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Chosen for her leadership, academic performance and willingness to help others, Nayessa Walsh is Laramie County School District 1’s student of the week for the week of Nov. 2.
According to the district, Walsh is an academic achiever. She maintains a 4.0 GPA, has nearly perfect attendance and is never late to class. She is also known for helping other students as well as teachers whenever she can. Her dedication to her and others’ education makes Carey Junior High a better place, the district said.
“Her commitment to excellence extends beyond the classroom into all that she does,” Carey Principal Matt Schlaegel said.
She is a multi-sport athlete as well. She participates in volleyball, basketball and track at Carey and plays on a traveling volleyball team and two traveling soccer teams.
Walsh selected Todd Jonas as her favorite teacher because he makes learning fun and interactive.
“He is very kind, patient, advocates for all students and never judges anyone,” Walsh said.
