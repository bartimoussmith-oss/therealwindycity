Spending proposals and revised investment rules on Cheyenne City Council agenda

Garrett Grochowski

The Cheyenne City Council meets Sept. 28, 2026 (Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The Cheyenne City Council is meeting tonight and will discuss a series of proposed code amendments, potential annexations and upcoming financial decisions. Up for final consideration is an agreement with the Air Force to convert F.E. Warren’s defunct golf course into a parking lot for Cheyenne Frontier Days, the purchase of two armored vehicles for Cheyenne PD, a new city investment policy and more.
The council is set to meet at 6 p.m. in the Council Chambers of the Municipal Building, located at 2101 O’Neil Ave. The public can also access the meeting remotely
via YouTube
or telephone.
Councilors have a long agenda tonight, full of code amendments and resolutions. The agenda document is available at the bottom of this story and contains more details on each item.
Up for second reading are two annexations. One would incorporate Christensen Road and a small, triangular parcel nearby into the city, while the other would incorporate a 5-acre property on College Drive just south of Fox Farm Road. Both of these properties would also be assigned a Business Park zoning designation.
Also up for their second reading are a series of code amendments. Two proposed amendments to the Cheyenne Unified Development Code would add new definitions surrounding urban agriculture, expanding what type of agriculture and agritourism ventures City of Cheyenne residents can legally participate in, and allow gravel parking areas for certain types of residences and businesses.
The other two code amendments would, if passed, impact the City of Cheyenne Municipal Code. One revises restrictions on keeping livestock. The other permits the use of electric fencing within the city.
As all of those ordinances are on their second council read, they will return to the Public Services Committee for another look if they are approved by the council tonight. If the committee advances them, they will be ready for a final vote. Read about the agriculture code updates in more detail
here
.
The council will introduce
another UDC amendment as well, this time impacting construction sites
. As this item is on its first reading, it will be advanced to the Public Services Committee without discussion.
Last week, the Cheyenne Finance Committee voted against recommending an amendment to the city’s General Fund Balance Policy to review fiscal year-end balances and allow potential transfers of up to $10,000,000 into the Stormwater Runoff Management System Account. The city will consider approving the amendment without the committee’s support. Read about that policy
here
.
Added to the agenda without going through the committee process, an emergency procurement to be awarded to Legacy Construction to replace the fire-damaged roof structure at the Pumphouse is also on the agenda.
The $1,365,402 contract will pay to replace the roof after it was severely damaged in an arson incident in May
.
The consent agenda — the list of council decisions considered regular business and not necessary for the council to discuss in full — also features important council decisions. Councilors will consider resolutions
to purchase two Bearcat armored vehicles for the Cheyenne Police Department for $853,980
, to reappropriate the Fiscal Year 2027 Municipal Budget,
to revise the City of Cheyenne Investment Policy Statement
, to approve an interfund loan of up to $18,063,014 from the 1% Sales Tax Capital Projects Fund to the Special Purpose Option Sales Tax Fund in order to accelerate Sixth Penny projects, and more.
Councilors will also consider
an easement on F.E. Warren Air Force Base property to construct and operate a parking lot for Cheyenne Frontier Days
and
a simulcasting permit for the new gaming center replacing the Cadillac Cowboy bar on Nationway
.
