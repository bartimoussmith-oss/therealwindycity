BURNS — A 14-point swing that crossed the halftime break helped give Class 2A West No. 3-seed Mountain View enough momentum to pull away from East No. 2-seed Burns Friday night at Bronc Stadium.
The Buffalos scored with a minute left in the first half and rattled off a quick six-play drive to start the second half to go up 21-7 on Burns early in the third quarter. It was enough of a cushion for Mountain View to put the Broncs away and punch its ticket to the semifinals with a 48-13 win.
“We’re just a family,” said Mountain View senior Joseph Larson, who scored three touchdowns and ran for 103 yards in Friday’s game. “We came together, and we just worked hard. ... We just got into a rhythm, and once we got it, we were gone.”
Senior quarterback Bryce Hansen completed 20 passes for 240 yards and a touchdown, unofficially. Gage Rooney added 91 yards and a receiving touchdown on six catches. Dayne Wilshusen caught three passes for 113 yards and had a touchdown to lead the charge through the air.
Burns’ offense, which had struggled to get started on time coming into the game, scored four plays into the first quarter when Hansen connected with Rooney for a 44-yard score.
The defense forced a three-and-out its first time on the field.
“It was good to see that we could start fast,” Burns coach Travis Romsa said. “We started the game really well, we just couldn’t finish it.”
Mountain View evened the game up on the second play of the second quarter with an 11-yard run from Joseph Larson after both teams struggled to move the ball for the remainder of the first quarter.
Burns put its best drive together to close the first half, but an interception at the Mountain View five was returned 95 yards by Kolbe Webb for a touchdown. Burns had a chance to cut into the lead going into halftime, but a 35-yard field goal attempt as time expired came up just short.
The end to the half proved disastrous for Burns.
Out of the break, Mountain View rattled off 20 points in the third quarter to go up 34-7 going into the final frame. It put the game away with a touchdown on the first play of the fourth quarter to end Burns’ season.
The Buffalos scored 20 points off three turnovers, including 13 in the third quarter.
In the end, the Buffaloes rattled off 48 consecutive points from the second quarter on. Burns’ final touchdown of the game came on a 46-yard deep shot from junior quarterback Jase Franzen to Dayne Wilshusen.
“It was a little bit of both (them changing things and us getting tired),” Romsa said. “They run really hard with their quarterback and running backs, and we just didn’t tackle well.
“... It’s going to be an offseason preach of how we can get out of the first round. We have to go further.”
Mountain View faces Cokeville next week.
