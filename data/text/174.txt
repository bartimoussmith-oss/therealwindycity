Mild Halloween weekend ahead for Cheyenne before chilly storm brings wind, showers

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Laramie County residents can look forward to mild weather for Halloween this weekend before a colder storm system brings strong winds and rain showers Sunday night.
Meteorologists with the National Weather Service in Cheyenne said dense morning fog across the plains will dissipate by midday Friday, giving way to mostly sunny skies and an afternoon high near 63 degrees.
Patchy fog may return late Friday night, but conditions will remain quiet as Saturday begins. Cheyenne will see partly sunny skies on Halloween afternoon, with temperatures climbing to around 60.
Families heading out to trick-or-treat Saturday evening can expect comfortable conditions. Temperatures should hover in the mid-40s around 9 p.m. before dropping to an overnight low near 38.
Across southeast Wyoming, meteorologists expect a few light showers Saturday afternoon along and west of the Interstate 25 corridor. Most of that moisture will stay confined to Albany and Carbon counties, though residents along Interstate 25 may still want to keep an umbrella handy.
Cloud cover will increase Sunday across the region as afternoon highs reach the mid-50s. Southerly winds will pick up by Sunday afternoon, gusting at up to 25 mph in Cheyenne.
A Pacific storm system will sweep a cold front across the region Sunday night, dropping temps into the 40s and bringing a 50% chance of showers Monday. Along with the colder air, meteorologists expect strong northwest winds to develop Monday morning east of the Laramie Range, producing gusts between 40 and 55 mph through the afternoon.
Calmer conditions will return Tuesday, bringing sunshine and highs in the 50s through the middle of the week.
Detailed Forecast
Today:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 63. Light and variable wind shifting to the south-southeast at 5–10 mph in the afternoon.
Tonight:
Patchy fog after midnight. Otherwise, mostly cloudy, with a low around 39. South-southwest wind at 5–10 mph.
Saturday:
Partly sunny, with a high near 60. South wind at 5–10 mph shifting to the west in the afternoon.
Saturday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 38. Southwest wind at around 5 mph.
Sunday:
Mostly cloudy, with a high near 56. South-southeast wind at 5–10 mph increasing to 10–15 mph in the afternoon. Winds could gust as high as 25 mph.
Sunday Night:
A 30% chance of showers. Mostly cloudy, with a low around 40. Breezy.
Monday:
A 50% chance of showers. Partly sunny, with a high near 47. Windy.
Monday Night:
A slight chance of showers before midnight. Mostly cloudy, with a low around 32. Breezy.
Tuesday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 55. Breezy.
Tuesday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 33.
Wednesday:
Sunny, with a high near 53.
Wednesday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 32.
Thursday:
A slight chance of showers. Mostly sunny, with a high near 50.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
