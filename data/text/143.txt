Cheyenne approves Wyoming Downs move to former Cadillac Ranch after crime debate

INSET: Cheyenne City Council meets Oct. 26, 2026 (Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News) OUTSET: Rendering for redesign of the Cadillac Ranch building, set to become a Wyoming Downs location (City of Cheyenne)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The City of Cheyenne has approved special use and simulcasting permits for the gaming center taking over the Cadillac Ranch bar on Nationway. The Wyoming Downs location, moving from the nearby Cheyenne Plaza, has reportedly contributed little to area crime, contrary to what neighbors claim a new location would do.
Wyoming Downs, one of the state’s most popular historic horse racing and off-track betting parlor companies, currently has a location at 3617 Lincolnway, mere hundreds of feet away from the proposed location at 3307 Nationway.
“The reason why Wyoming Downs needs to move is because they do not own the plaza location,” Wyoming Downs representative Brent Rhodes told the Cheyenne City Council Monday night ahead of a vote to approve the permits. “They prefer to own their locations. There’s a number of issues with the current building and it hasn’t necessarily been a suitable fit for them. That’s the only reason why they’re moving the Cadillac Ranch.”
That would mean Cheyenne would keep the seven gaming facilities it hosts currently, rather than add an eighth.
(A Bally’s location is planned for the Cheyenne Plaza in the next few years, however.)
Even so, neighbors to the longtime bar, which was open only three days a week, said that they don’t want the increased criminal activity the location would reportedly bring.
A woman named Savannah, claiming to live near the location, said that a bar and a gaming facility are not the same. The Cadillac Ranch drew a young, sociable crowd that just wanted to drink, dance and go home. Gaming facilities, which she said she knew plenty about from years working in the industry, are open longer and later and encourage more deplorable activity.
“Through my firsthand experience here in this industry, I know exactly what happens in these establishments and their parking lots. People like to think it’s just elderly residents and young couples on date nights. It is not,” she said. “These venues attract severe gambling addicts, desperate individuals and drug activity. I have seen firsthand the theft, the drug deals, the active solicitation and the prostitution that plague these businesses.”
She also cited Cheyenne police statistics reportedly pointing to 25 calls for service to the Lincolnway location in the last year.
That number is slightly deceptive, Rhodes noted. Wyoming Downs reportedly takes security very seriously. Besides employing Wyoming Gaming Commission–mandated security cameras at all of its locations, the company actively encourages employees to call in suspicious activity whenever they see it, leading to a higher volume of calls.
“We do a lot of calls ourselves — anyone on a warrant list or anything like that because we do a substantial amount of IDing,” Rhodes said.
Cheyenne Mayor Patrick Collins had asked the Cheyenne Police Department to generate a summary of crime statistics involving gaming centers earlier in the day in anticipation for that conversation. According to him and the department, from all OTBs citywide combined over the past 12 months, officers responded to 31 criminal cases, issued 21 tickets and made 27 arrests.
Cheyenne PD Captain David James added that OTB-related incidents account for less than 1% of all police cases citywide, less than 0.5% of all logged incidents citywide, roughly 0.5% of all arrests citywide and 0.1%–0.2% of all traffic citations and tickets written in the city.
Wyoming Downs was granted its permits, facilitating a move for the parlor. Councilors Pete Laybourn and Lawrence Wolfe were the only dissenting votes.
The new location will feature 124 historic horse racing terminals across a 6,208-square-foot building. It should come equipped with all the amenities Cheyenne gamblers expect from a
Wyoming Downs
location, including food and, eventually, bar services. Wyoming Downs has not yet secured a liquor license for the location.
