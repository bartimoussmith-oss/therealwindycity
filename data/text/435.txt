Jose Del Carmen Garcia Martinez, 62 (FBI Denver)
Overview:
The FBI Denver office is announcing a $20,000 reward leading to the arrest of Jose Del Carmen Garcia Martinez.
CASPER, Wyo. — The FBI Denver office is announcing a $20,000 reward leading to the arrest of Jose Del Carmen Garcia Martinez, who is reportedly on the run after being convicted of sexually assaulting a 15-year-old girl with disabilities in Arapahoe County, Colorado.
It is believed he has friends or family in Colorado, Utah, Vancouver and Columbia, the FBI release on Tuesday said. He reportedly disabled his ankle monitor and fled on a Harley-Davidson motorcycle with Colorado Plate DEXD78, the FBI said.
He was subsequently seen in Wyoming and Utah.
Garcia Martinez was released from the Arapahoe County Jail on a $50,000 bond and appeared for the first day of his trial on March 24, 2026, but fled prior to the jury’s verdict, the FBI release said.
“The jury found him guilty on all counts despite not appearing for the remainder of his trial,” the FBI said. “Garcia Martinez is still at large.” A federal judge granted a warrant for unlawful flight to avoid prosecution earlier this year in addition to the jury convictions on two counts of sexual assault and one count of unlawful sexual contact on an at-risk child.
Garcia Martinez is 62 years old and 5-foot-8, with graying hair. He has brown eyes and weighs about 170 pounds. His primary language is Spanish.
Garcia Martinez has dual citizenship from Columbia and Spain. It is believed he has friends and/or family in Colorado, Utah, Vancouver and Columbia.
On Aug. 5, 2025, the Arapahoe County Sheriff’s Office responded to a sexual assault call at a home on the 5100 block of South Truckee Street in Centennial. The victim’s mother reported that the victim’s grandmother walked into the master bedroom of the home and witnessed Garcia Martinez fondling her 15-year-old granddaughter.
“If you harm children, be prepared for the FBI to put a target on your back. In this case, our law enforcement partners did a solid job of investigating and prosecuting the cowardly offender, who decided to flee,” said FBI Denver Special Agent in Charge Amanda Koldjeski. “The FBI is bringing our resources to bear, and we want this convicted abuser of children to know that justice will find you.”
Anyone with information is asked to call the FBI at
1-800-CALL-FBI (1-800-225-5324)
or submit a tip online at
tips.fbi.gov
