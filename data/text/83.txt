CFD Old West Museum begins new tradition with inaugural ‘Black Hat Gala’ Nov. 6

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Cheyenne Frontier Days’ Old West Museum’s first-ever Black Hat Gala is set for Nov. 6. The inaugural celebration will raise funds to support the museum’s mission to preserve and share the history and culture of the American west.
The black-tie evening will feature fine dining, a signature dessert, live and silent auctions, a whiskey pull and opportunities to connect with community members and supporters of western heritage.
Tickets are already on sale and special sponsorships are also available. An individual ticket costs $175 for museum members and $200 for guests, while a table sponsorship costs $2,500. Tickets can be purchased
here
.
Proceeds from the fundraiser will support museum operations, exhibits, youth and adult education programs and the development of a new education room featuring immersive experiences that bring Western history to life, a release from the museum said.
“The Black Hat Gala is an opportunity for our community to invest in the future of the Museum and ensure that Western history and traditions remain accessible to future generations,” Josie Molloy, executive director of the Old West Museum, said.
More information can be found at the
museum’s website
.
