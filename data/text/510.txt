2026 Wyoming Teacher of the Year Brian Cox is surprised with free Dunkin' coffee for a year Nov. 13, 2026 (Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Wyoming’s 2027 Teacher of the Year, Brian Cox, will be running on Dunkin’ for the next 365 days after being surprised with free coffee for a year Friday. The new Pershing Avenue Dunkin’s franchisees surprised Cox’s agricultural science class at South High with the news as well as free donuts and $5 gift cards for students.
“This is pretty darn special,” Cox said. He knew something was up when school administration asked him if he was going to be in class that day. He didn’t think he’d be getting such a surprise — one that he can share with his students.
2026 Wyoming Teacher of the Year Brian Cox’s agricultural science class is surprised with $5 Dunkin’ gift cards Nov. 13, 2026 (Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
As the store’s owners rolled the stacks of donuts in on carts, Cox had a chance to speak to his students about what a privilege it is to be their teacher and have his work recognized. He can never talk about it without tearing up, he said.
“This isn’t something you can plan for. You can’t set a goal of ‘Hey, I want to be Teacher of the Year.’ It just has to happen. It just has to fall in place,” Cox said. “The big one is getting nominated by a student and then winning the district first. My whole campaign has been about providing opportunities for the kids.”
“These kids here are incredible kids and I think the rest of the world doesn’t know how amazing South High School is. I think this is one of the neatest places,” he continued. “You get in the halls, they work extremely hard and they’re eager to learn, and that is what I absolutely love about these kids. They’re the most dedicated kids I’ve ever taught. Pretty amazing kids on the south side.”
2026 Wyoming Teacher of the Year Brian Cox in his class Nov. 13, 2026 (Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
Cox said he is a little worried about how much he is going to take advantage of his year of free coffee.
“I like the heck out of Dunkin’, so probably quite a bit,” he said.
