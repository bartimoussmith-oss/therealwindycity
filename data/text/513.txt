John West was chosen for the November LCSD1 employee spotlight (LCSD1)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Laramie County School District 1 has selected John West, an activity driver for the district, for its November employee spotlight. West has served the district for 21 years, faithfully transporting students and helping the district run.
West, LCSD1 said, was chosen for the spotlight because he is great with students, brings a positive attitude to the job and is always willing to assist his colleagues.
When district bus drivers are absent, West fills in; when buses experience mechanical problems, West is there to fix them. The district said it appreciates his attitude and the support he gives to students and staff.
He’ll be recognized by the LCSD1 Board of Trustees at its next meeting Monday, Nov. 16.
