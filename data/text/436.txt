CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Eric Barlow, chosen last week to lead the state as the next governor, has made his picks for his preferred leadership team. Among his selections is Walter Eggers, a Cheyenne attorney he nominated for attorney general.
Eggers, who graduated from the University of Wyoming College of Law in 1997, has been practicing law in Cheyenne for nearly 30 years. The Laramie native had served as both a prosecutor and defense attorney before joining law firm Holland & Hart in 2000.
He was a partner at the firm from 2008 to 2024, during which he lead the firm’s Cheyenne office and later its Energy, Environment and Natural Resources Practice Group. He has practiced administrative law, state and local taxation, litigation and appeals, Barlow’s team said in a statement. Since 2025, he has taught professional responsibility, evidence and oil and gas law at the University of Wyoming College of Law.
Eggers’s qualifications extend to civil rights and expanding access to legal assistance as well. He has worked with both the Wyoming Access to Justice Commission and Equal Justice Wyoming. His professional and community contributions have earned recognition, Barlow’s team said, including the Wyoming State Bar’s John M. Burman Extraordinary Service Award.
Unlike the other leadership selections
Barlow announced Tuesday
, including Erin Taylor as chief of staff and Eydie Trautwein as chief operating officer, Eggers’s selection is subject to Wyoming senate approval. He’ll need a simple majority vote in favor of his nomination to be confirmed.
