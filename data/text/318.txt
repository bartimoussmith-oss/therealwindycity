Rep. Bob Nicholas, R-Cheyenne. (Dan Cepeda, Oil City)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Incumbent Rep. Bob Nicholas defeated Democrat challenger Gavin Lobmeyer in Tuesday’s general election for the Wyoming House District 7 seat.
In Tuesday’s election, Nicholas received 1,993 votes to Lobmeyer’s 1,776.
Nicholas was challenged in August’s primary election
by Deb Mutter Shamley, defeating her 1,319 votes to 698.
Nicholas is a Cheyenne attorney and businessman who has served in the Wyoming House since 2011. He centered his campaign on state budget management, public education and infrastructure investment. A former chairman of the House Appropriations Committee, Nicholas said he prioritizes building state savings reserves, supporting funding for public schools and maintaining transportation networks.
Lobmeyer, a Cheyenne native with a background in public service, focused his campaign on lowering living costs, investing in education and retaining young workers in Wyoming. He advocated for expanding apprenticeships and local trades, modernizing infrastructure and reducing expenses for housing, groceries and healthcare.
