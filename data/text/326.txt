Election 2026: Gray to succeed Hageman as Wyoming’s next US House representative

Chuck Gray, Secretary of State (Dan Cepeda, Oil City News)
CASPER, Wyo. — Wyoming has chosen to send Secretary of State Chuck Gray to Washington, D.C., to fill the state’s lone U.S. House of Representatives seat being vacated by two-term Rep. Harriet Hageman.
In Tuesday’s race, called by the Associated Press at 9:40 p.m., Gray defeated three challengers, including Democrat Lisa Kinney, Libertarian Shawn Johnson and Jeff Haggit of the Constitution Party. Gray received 58,916 votes to Kinney’s 32,338, Johnson’s 8,215 and Haggit’s 4,586.
The final tally is subject to change as the race was called with 52% of the total votes cast. Polls closed at 7 p.m.
Gray advanced to the general election after earning 25.4% of the primary vote to become the Republican Party’s nominee in August, defeating a slew of challengers who included Jillian Balow, Bo Biteman, Kevin Christensen, Richard Dodson, Steve Friess, David Giralt, Keith Goodenough and Reid Rasner.
Gray hosted a Casper radio show and served three terms in the Wyoming House of Representatives before winning his current office seat in 2022 with the endorsement of President Donald Trump. As secretary of state, he enforced proof-of-citizenship voter registration rules and banned ranked-choice voting across the state.
Once Tuesday’s results are certified by county and state canvassing boards, Gray will succeed Hageman in January. Hageman vacated the seat to run for retiring Sen. Cynthia Lummis’s U.S. Senate seat.
Hageman defeated Democrat James Byrd on Tuesday to take that seat.
