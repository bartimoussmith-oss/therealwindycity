The Jackalope, center, is among several mythical creatures to be featured in a series of new stamps to be issued by the U.S. Postal Service in 2027, according to a release. (Courtesy USPS)
CASPER, Wyo. –– Jackalopes and mothmen and squonks, oh my!
Wyoming’s fabled and elusive jackalope is among several mythical creatures to be featured in the 2027 round of U.S. Postal Service stamps.
According to a release
from the USPS, the creatures will be joined by a number of other design themes, which will include sports legends, veggies, Halloween monsters and, as always, an American flag.
Along with the jackalope, the mythical creatures will include Mothman, the squonk, the sasquatch and Champ. Unicorns will also be featured in four different renderings.
“America’s many folkloric animals reflect our love of the unknown and odd,” said the release. “The Postal Service’s Mythical Creatures set features five different stamps depicting some regional favorites.” The bright and whimsical creatures were created by artist Nat Power, with USPS art director Antonia Alcalá credited with the stamp’s final designs.
According to Western Wyoming College
, the jackalope as we know it came about from a 1932 hunting trip by two Douglas taxidermists, who on a whim decided to attach antlers to a wild jackrabbit they had harvested. The resulting creation was sold to La Bonte Hotel owner Roy Hall, who the displayed it for all visitors to see and marvel. In 2005, the state moved to recognize the jackalope as the state’s official mythical creature.
While the jackrabbit with antlers is entirely fake, rabbits suffering from a deadly virus have been known to grow small protrusions or “horns” on their heads, leading some to believe that jackalopes are real.
The USPS did not announce a release date for the stamps, and added that some design elements may change before their final release. A full description and renderings of the 2027 stamps
