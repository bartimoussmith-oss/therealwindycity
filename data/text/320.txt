Erin Edwards (Submitted photo)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The Republican candidate who ousted incumbent Rep. Gary Brown will represent Wyoming House District 41.
In Tuesday’s election, Erin Edwards received 1,602 votes to Democrat challenger Stacy Becker’s 1,137.
In August, Edwards, a longtime volunteer firefighter and former Department of Defense contract firefighter who served during Operation Iraqi Freedom, defeated Brown
by a vote of 592 to 244.
Edwards advocated for protecting constitutional liberties; supporting agriculture, energy and mining industries; and preserving public lands for outdoor recreation. Her platform also emphasized constituent-first representation over legislative caucuses and special interests.
