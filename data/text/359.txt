Triumph High junior Ryker Wells is LCSD1 student of the week for Nov. 9, 2026 (LCSD1)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Triumph High School junior Ryker Wells is Laramie County School District 1’s student of the week for the week of Nov. 9. Chosen for his his academic achievement, excellent attendance and positive attitude toward learning and the Triumph community, Wells has all the qualities of a good student.
Wells maintains good grades and is well on his way to graduation next year, LCSD1 said. He is still making the most out of his time in high school, however. He has taken on a leadership role in his advisement group and serves as a teacher’s assistant in the library and media center. He also participates in the ASK program, working with elementary students after school.
Around Triumph, Wells is known for his kindness, generosity and his adherence to the principles of Triumph’s Discovery program. That means he is prompt, prepared and polite; maintains a positive attitude; and completes his work each day. He is an especially dedicated art student, creating works well beyond what the standard curriculum expects.
Wells selected Michael Hendrix as his favorite teacher because of his encouragement and support. LCSD1 said he credits Hendrix with challenging him and helping him recognize his own worth.
