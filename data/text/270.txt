Mild Halloween in Cheyenne before strong winds, cooler air move in

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Morning fog will yield to unseasonably mild conditions for Halloween across Laramie County on Saturday, but forecasters advise that a complex storm system will bring blustery winds and cooler temperatures to start the workweek.
According to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne, intermittent and locally dense fog that prompted advisories along portions of the Interstate 25 and Interstate 80 corridors early Saturday morning is expected to dissipate by midday. Another round of patchy fog remains possible late Saturday night into Sunday morning.
Despite the damp start, trick-or-treaters can expect pleasant conditions Saturday evening. Sunset temperatures across Cheyenne and surrounding areas are forecast to hover in the 50s before slipping into the mid- to upper 40s by 9 p.m., accompanied by relatively light winds. While isolated light showers cannot be ruled out along and west of the Interstate 25 corridor and the Laramie Range through early evening, most of Laramie County will remain dry.
Sunday will mark a transition as an approaching storm system draws deep subtropical moisture northward into Wyoming. Increasing cloud cover will hold temperatures slightly lower than on Saturday. While widespread shower activity is expected to spread across Carbon, Albany and Converse counties through Sunday afternoon, rainfall in far-southeast Wyoming and Laramie County will be much more spotty, with little to no accumulation anticipated before the initial wave lifts out Sunday evening.
Conditions are forecast to deteriorate on Monday as competing upper-level systems consolidate into a deepening surface low over the High Plains. Forecasters anticipate a tight pressure gradient that will generate widespread northwest wind gusts of 45–55 mph along and east of the Laramie Range from Monday through Monday night, with a 30%–40% chance that winds could approach high-wind criteria.
As winds aloft shift northerly Monday afternoon, a secondary round of precipitation is projected to develop across southeastern Wyoming and the adjacent plains. Downslope winds off the Cheyenne Ridge are likely to limit liquid precipitation totals in the immediate Cheyenne and Interstate 80 corridor compared to areas farther north toward Douglas and Chadron, Nebraska.
Colder air will gradually filter into the region Monday, dropping snow levels to between 5,000 and 8,000 feet by sunset. While official forecasts do not call for significant snow accumulation across lower elevations due to the lingering warmer boundary layer, forecasters note that localized bursts of heavier precipitation could bring a wet rain–snow mix to higher spots near the Interstate 80 summit.
Drier air will sweep into the area late Monday night, ending precipitation from north to south before Tuesday morning. Gusty northwest winds will linger into Tuesday, but temperatures across Laramie County are expected to rebound near seasonal averages for the rest of the week.

Partly sunny, with a high near 62. North wind at around 5 mph shifting to the south-southwest.
Tonight
:
Areas of dense fog after 3 a.m. Otherwise, partly cloudy, with a low around 38. South wind at around 5 mph.
Sunday:
A chance of showers between 9 a.m. and 3 p.m. Areas of dense fog before 9 a.m. Otherwise, mostly cloudy, with a high near 57. South-southeast wind at 5–10 mph increasing to 10–15 mph in the morning. Winds could gust as high as 25 mph. Chance of precipitation is 30%.
Sunday Night:
A 30% chance of showers, mainly after midnight. Mostly cloudy, with a low around 41. West-southwest wind at 5–10 mph shifting to the northwest at 10–15 mph after midnight. Winds could gust as high as 25 mph.
Monday:
A 50% chance of showers. Mostly cloudy, with a high near 49. Windy, with a north-northwest wind at 20–25 mph increasing to 25–30 mph in the afternoon. Winds could gust as high as 50 mph.
Monday Night:
A chance of rain showers before midnight, then a slight chance of rain and snow showers. Mostly cloudy, then gradually becoming mostly clear, with a low around 33. Windy. Chance of precipitation is 50%.
Tuesday:
Sunny, with a high near 55. Breezy.
Tuesday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 32.
Wednesday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 55.
Wednesday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 32.
Thursday:
Sunny, with a high near 55.
Thursday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 33.

Mostly sunny, with a high near 55.
