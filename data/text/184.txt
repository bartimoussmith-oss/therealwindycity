Lisa knows Wyoming
Lisa was born and raised in Laramie Wyoming. She is a librarian, lawyer, financial planner, lawmaker, writer, and photograher. She was the Albany County Public Library Director, conducted the Summit Bar Review, was a law partner in Corthell and King, and was a financial planner.
“Former Governor Mike Sullivan endorsed Democrat Lisa Kinney for House on Tuesday. ‘It’s important Wyoming have someone dedicated to public service, who works well with people and has proven their leadership like Lisa has and Chuck hasn’t,’ he said.”

Lisa knows how to get things done in government
Lisa represented District 9 in Albany County from 1984 to 1994. She passed legislation establishing the mechanism for durable power of attorney for health care and legislation to enable optometrists to write prescriptions for eye care medication. Her achievements in the Senate, even while always in the minority party, demonstrate her ability to achieve compromise and get work done.
Lisa knows how to make things happen in her community
Lisa has an extensive library background and she is a fierce advocate for public libraries. In her time with the Albany County Library, she passed a bond issue for a new library and then fund raised to close the budget gap.
Lisa knows how to make Washington work for Wyoming
She will ensure that Wyoming public lands remain public and accessible to all Wyomingites. She will work to make sure that all Wyomingites can afford health insurance and that everyone, including those in remote rural communities, can access the health care they need.
Lisa has been endorsed by those who came after her as Senators of State District 9, including Mike Massie and Chris Rothfuss. She has been endorsed by Republican legislators Rex Arney, Tom Jones, and Pattie Macmillan. Of course, she is also endorsed by former Governor Mike Sullivan.
