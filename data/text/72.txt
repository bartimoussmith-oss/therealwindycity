First responders help transport a heart attack victim out of the Snowy Range by helicopter Oct. 18, 2026 (Carbon County Sheriff)
CENTENNIAL, Wyo. — A man had to be transported by helicopter out of the Medicine Bow National Forest last weekend after suffering what appeared to be a heart attack, Carbon County Sheriff Alex Bakken said Thursday.
According to
a Facebook post by Bakken
, the man suffered the attack near Ryan Brothers Lake in the Snowy Range, an extremely rural area east of Medicine Bow Peak.
“Deputies and medical personnel responded, alongside air resources from Intermountain Air Ambulance,” Bakken wrote. “Despite being on vacation, Undersheriff Shawn Kelley happened to be in the area and didn’t hesitate to respond as well, and assisted loading the subject into the helicopter!”
Bakken did not comment on the status of the man, but said he was successfully transported out of the mountains.
