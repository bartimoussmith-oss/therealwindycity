Wyoming governor Republican candidate Eric Barlow is congratulated while watching results with family and supporters during an election party at the Wyo Sports Ranch on Tuesday, Nov. 3, 2026, in Casper.  (Dan Cepeda, Oil City News)
CASPER, Wyo. — Wyoming voters have picked Republican state Sen. Eric Barlow to succeed Gov. Mark Gordon in January. Gordon is stepping down due to term limits.
The Associated Press called the race at 7 p.m. with zero votes counted and zero percent of the votes in. Barlow was challenged by Democrat Kenneth Casner and Rebecca Bextel of the Constitution Party.
Vote totals will be available on the live results pages
here
.
Barlow became the Republican Party’s nominee to the seat in August after defeating Superintendent of Public Instruction Megan Degenfelder, Brent Bien and Curt Blake.
A U.S. Marine Corps veteran, veterinarian and Gillette native, Barlow has served in the state Legislature since 2013, including a tenure as Speaker of the House before joining the Senate. His platform prioritized property tax reduction, energy independence, expanded healthcare access and private property rights protections. In the recent legislative session, Barlow sponsored four signed bills addressing wildfire management, sage grouse mitigation, motor vehicle warranties and agritourism safety.
Barlow released the following statement shortly after the race was called:
“Kelly and I are deeply grateful to the people of Wyoming for the trust they have placed in us,” Barlow said. “We began this campaign by traveling the state, listening to our neighbors and working to earn their support. Those conversations shaped this campaign, and they will guide the work ahead. To our family, campaign team, volunteers and everyone who gave their time and support to this effort: thank you. I am proud of the campaign we ran together.”
“To those who supported another candidate, I will work to earn your trust, too. My responsibility as governor will be to every person who calls Wyoming home. I thank Ken Casner, Rebecca Bextel and the candidates who participated in the primary for stepping forward to serve. We will have differences, but we can approach them with respect and work together where we share common goals.”
“Wyoming families deserve a government that listens, spends responsibly and delivers dependable service. We have work to do to improve access to healthcare, support our schools and strengthen the industries that sustain our communities. We must protect our water and respect private property rights while creating opportunities for new businesses and investment. Above all, we need a Wyoming where our young people can choose to build careers, raise families and make their futures here.”
“I thank Governor Mark Gordon and First Lady Jennie Gordon for their service to Wyoming. As we begin this transition, I look forward to working with the governor’s team, legislative leaders and local communities. I will bring the same commitment to this office that I brought to the campaign: show up, listen and do the work.”
Results are unofficial until certified by county and state canvassing boards.
