Street sweeper driver killed in College Drive crash in south Cheyenne Thursday
by
Garrett Grochowski
October 23, 2026

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A car crash around the 400 block of College Drive on Thursday resulted in the death of a man driving a street sweeper, according to the Cheyenne Police Department. The Laramie County Coroner’s Office will identify the victim and notify the next of kin.
Traffic was shut down for over an hour Thursday as police responded to a crash in south Cheyenne. At the time, the department reported that the crash had resulted in injuries.
On Friday, the department reported that a 33-year-old man, the driver of a street sweeper doing work alongside College Drive, was killed.
Cheyenne police respond to a crash on College Drive on Oct. 22, 2026 (Cheyenne Police Department Facebook)
“Preliminary details show the crash occurred while a Dodge Durango and street sweeper were traveling north on College Drive,” a CPD release said. “The Durango reportedly rear-ended the machine in the lane of travel.”
The sweeper’s driver was ejected, sustaining life-threatening injuries. He was promptly transported to the Cheyenne Regional Medical Center, where he was later pronounced dead. The driver of the Durango was also injured and transported to the hospital.
The Cheyenne Police Department’s Major Crash Unit is now investigating the crash, working to determine what factors may have caused it.
