The Cheyenne Historic Preservation Board's 2026 Christmas ornaments are now for sale (City of Cheyenne)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — For the past eight years, the Cheyenne Historic Preservation Board has commissioned board member Gary Sims to create a unique Christmas ornament for residents to purchase. This year, the ornament celebrates Cheyenne’s newest public artworks: the murals on the Storey Boulevard water tanks.
The ornament, available for purchase for $25 at City of Cheyenne Planning and Development Department office, the Wyoming State Museum and the Cheyenne Depot Museum, also celebrates the United States’s 250th anniversary and features other unique hidden elements.
Past ornaments have featured the State Capitol, Union Pacific Depot, the Wyoming flag, the airport fountain, the Wyoming license plate, the Historic Governor’s Mansion and the Capitol Building dome.
All proceeds from the sales of the ornaments go to fund various Historic Preservation projects throughout the Cheyenne area.
