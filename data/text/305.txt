CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Incumbent Laramie County School District 1 Trustees Rene Hinkle, Marilyn Burden and Brooke Humphrey will be joined by a new face on the board in January.
Burden ran unopposed for her Area 1 seat. Hinkle defeated challenger Tanya Barcelona 14,797 to 8,832, for her at-large seat. Humphrey defeated two challengers, John Weber and Diane Wedl, for her Area 2 seat. Humphrey received 3,033 votes to Wedl’s 2,847 and Weber’s 2,277.
Hank Bailey will be joining the board in January to represent Area 3. With 4,059 votes, he defeated challengers Paul Bankes and Casi Crites. Bankes received 2,804 votes while Crites picked up 2,604.
