Petition challenging annexation falls short of signature requirement
Published on November 10, 2026
A petition filed in August challenging an ordinance that annexed land in southeast Cheyenne did not receive enough valid signatures, the City Clerk’s office has announced. As a result, the annexation will remain in place.
The petition concerned Ordinance No. 4687, which annexed approximately 3,460 acres in southeast Cheyenne. At least part of the property was planned for development as a Microsoft data center. Petitioners submitted 3,291 signatures across 227 pages. Of those, 61 were initially excluded for not meeting petition requirements, leaving 3,230 signatures for verification.
To trigger the referendum process, state law requires valid signatures from 10% of registered voters within city limits. For this petition, the threshold was 2,740 signatures. Signatories must be registered voters at the time the petition is submitted.
After reviewing the petition, the City Clerk’s office determined that 1,796 signatures belonged to registered voters in the City of Cheyenne. This number fell short of the threshold required by state law.
Among the remaining signatures, 773 belonged to Laramie County residents who lived outside city limits, and 539 belonged to city residents who were not registered to vote. Another 122 signatures were illegible, did not meet address requirements, or were duplicates.
Because the petition fell short of the required number of signatures, Ordinance No. 4687 will remain in place as passed by City Council.
