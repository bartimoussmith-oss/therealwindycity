Mild, dry conditions continue for Cheyenne ahead of weekend warmup

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Laramie County can expect mild, dry weather to wrap up the workweek before temperatures climb into the 60s this weekend, according to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne.
Sunny skies and light winds will push the high temperature in Cheyenne to near 55 degrees today. Cloud cover will increase late tonight as the low temperature settles around 33.
Partly sunny conditions will continue across Laramie County on Friday, with temperatures once again peaking near 55. Northwest winds will pick up slightly to between 5 and 10 mph Friday afternoon before skies clear overnight, accompanying a low around 34.
Across southeast Wyoming, high pressure centered over Montana will keep conditions quiet through Thursday. A weak upper-level system will slide east across the state on Friday, bringing a slight chance of light precipitation to the Snowy and Sierra Madre mountain ranges, meteorologists wrote in their forecast discussion. Plains communities will remain dry, with passing clouds serving as the only marker of a weak front.
Warmer air will return to eastern Wyoming over the weekend as a high-pressure ridge builds across the region. Cheyenne will reach a high near 59 under sunny skies on Saturday, climbing to near 65 on Sunday. Overnight lows through the weekend will hold in the 30s.
Dry conditions and mild air will persist through the middle of next week across southeast Wyoming. Cheyenne will see sunny skies with highs in the mid-60s on Monday and Tuesday, followed by sunshine and a high near 61 degrees for Veterans Day.
Detailed Forecast
Today:
Sunny, with a high near 55. West wind at around 5 mph calming.
Tonight:
Increasing clouds, with a low around 33. South wind at around 5 mph shifting to the west after midnight.
Friday:
Partly sunny, with a high near 55. Northwest wind at 5–10 mph.
Friday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 34. West wind at 5–10 mph.
Saturday:
Sunny, with a high near 59. West-northwest wind at 5–10 mph.
Saturday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 32.
Sunday:
Sunny, with a high near 65.
Sunday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 39.
Monday:
Sunny, with a high near 65.
Monday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 36.
Tuesday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 64.
Tuesday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 38.
Veterans Day:
Sunny, with a high near 61.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
