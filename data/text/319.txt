CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Incumbent Laramie County Community College Trustee Kathy Emmons has retained her seat on the board.
Three trustee seats were up for election this year, and there were six candidates vying for the role. Voters elected to send Joe Kelly and Kenneth Guille to the board alongside Emmons.
Emmons received 15,900 votes. Kelly netted 12,295 votes and Guille got 11,042 votes.
Challengers for the seats included Troy Rumpf with 9,402 votes, R. Larry Meuli with 6,963 votes and Michael Bechtel with 9,770 votes.
