15 cookie picks that made my sister take half before anyone else got a look in

When someone reaches for half the cookie plate, a range of flavors gives everyone else plenty to choose from. These 15 cookies cover chocolate caramel, oatmeal raisin, salted snickerdoodles, fruit, pecans, frosting, and mint, with crisp, soft, and chewy textures across the mix. Set out a few varieties for a get-together or pack extras for guests to take home.
Copycat Thin Mint Cookies. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Chocolate Caramel Cookies
Chocolate Caramel Cookies. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Rich, cocoa-forward layers give
Chocolate Caramel Cookies
a rich base, then chocolate frosting, caramel sauce, and crunchy toffee bits build a layered finish. The recipe yields 10 cookies, so this smaller batch can still claim plenty of attention on a dessert plate. Their chocolate-on-chocolate topping gives anyone reaching for another cookie a clear reason to do so. Set them beside coffee or pack one for an afternoon snack.
Get the Recipe:
Chocolate Caramel Cookies
Oatmeal Raisin Cookies
Oatmeal Raisin Cookies. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Chewy centers and crisp edges give
Oatmeal Raisin Cookies
their familiar contrast, while rolled oats and golden raisins make each bite hearty. The recipe yields 24 cookies in about 22 minutes, using brown sugar, white sugar, and butter in the dough. They suit lunchboxes, coffee breaks, or a simple after-dinner plate, making them an easy pick when a classic cookie is likely to disappear before the platter travels far.
Get the Recipe:
Oatmeal Raisin Cookies
Salted Caramel Brown Butter Snickerdoodles
Salted Caramel Brown Butter Snickerdoodles. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Soft, cinnamon-coated rounds hide caramel inside
Salted Caramel Brown Butter Snickerdoodles
have a familiar first impression, while browned butter and creamy caramel add toasted, sweet notes inside. A sprinkle of flaky salt balances the filling, and the recipe makes 36 cookies for sharing across a dessert tray or cookie exchange. Their soft, chewy texture and hidden caramel center make these a standout choice for anyone who usually reaches for a snickerdoodle.
Get the Recipe:
Salted Caramel Brown Butter Snickerdoodles
White Chocolate Crinkle Cookies
White Chocolate Crinkle Cookies. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Crackled tops make
White Chocolate Crinkle Cookies
easy to spot among the usual chocolate cookies. White chocolate gives the centers a mellow sweetness, and the layered sugar coating creates the signature crinkle as they bake. The recipe yields about 20 cookies and includes time to chill the dough, so plan for a cookie exchange or gift box. Their pale color and powdered finish bring contrast to a tray full of darker treats.
Get the Recipe:
White Chocolate Crinkle Cookies
Strawberry Shortcake Cookies
Strawberry Shortcake Cookies. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Soft, pink centers bring
Strawberry Shortcake Cookies
a fruit-forward look, with fresh strawberries, sweet cream, and white chocolate in the mix. The recipe makes 30 cookies and calls for chilling the dough before baking, helping prepare a generous batch for a spring gathering or dessert spread. Their shortcake-inspired flavor sets them apart from the chocolate-heavy options, while the tender, cakey texture makes them easy to serve with coffee or tea.
Get the Recipe:
Strawberry Shortcake Cookies
Zucchini Cookies with Chocolate & Pecans
Zucchini Cookies with Chocolate & Pecans. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
No-chill dough makes
Zucchini Cookies with Chocolate & Pecans
a quick option, with shredded zucchini, toasted pecans, oats, and semisweet chocolate chips in each batch. The recipe yields 24 cookies in about 30 minutes, so there is no long wait before they reach the cooling rack. Their nutty, lightly sweet profile adds a different texture to a cookie plate, and the chocolate gives each bite a familiar finish.
Get the Recipe:
Zucchini Cookies with Chocolate & Pecans
Pecan Butter Balls
Pecan Butter Balls. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Melt-in-your-mouth texture is the draw of
Pecan Butter Balls
, made with butter, finely ground pecans, and confectioners’ sugar. The recipe yields 24 cookies and takes about an hour with chilling included, giving the dough time to firm before baking. A powdered sugar coating gives them a classic finish suited to holiday tins, coffee breaks, or a mixed cookie platter. Their small, round shape makes them easy to pick up and pass around.
Get the Recipe:
Pecan Butter Balls
Copycat Crumbl Biscoff Cookies
Copycat Crumbl Biscoff Cookies. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Thick, bakery-style centers make
Copycat Crumbl Biscoff Cookies
stand out, with Biscoff cookie butter and crushed cookies folded into the dough. White chocolate chips add another sweet note, and the recipe yields 12 large cookies in about 20 minutes. Pressing the dough flat before baking helps create the soft middle and lightly crisp edges. Serve these when a standard-size cookie feels too small for the dessert plate.
Get the Recipe:
Copycat Crumbl Biscoff Cookies
Chocolate Linzer Cookies
Chocolate Linzer Cookies. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Star-shaped cutouts show the cream cheese frosting tucked between
Chocolate Linzer Cookies
. Cocoa-rich shortbread dough gets extra depth from espresso powder, while the filling adds a smooth contrast to the crisp cookie layers. The recipe makes 36 servings in about 40 minutes, so these can stock a holiday tray or gift tin. Their cutout tops give each cookie a bakery-style look, making them a natural choice when presentation matters as much as the chocolate flavor.
Get the Recipe:
Chocolate Linzer Cookies
Sweet Potato Cookies
Sweet Potato Cookies. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Lightly crisp edges and a maple glaze distinguish
Sweet Potato Cookies
, with cooked sweet potato and toasted pecans in the dough. The recipe yields 24 cookies in about 31 minutes, and the glaze adds a sweet finish without covering the nutty flavor. Bring them to a fall gathering, serve them after dinner, or add them to a mixed cookie plate for a different seasonal option. Their soft orange color also stands out beside traditional chocolate bakes.
Get the Recipe:
Sweet Potato Cookies
Peaches & Cream Cookies
Peaches & Cream Cookies. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Bright fruit notes distinguish
Peaches & Cream Cookies
a bright fruit flavor, while peach jam deepens it and whipped cream adds a soft topping. The recipe yields 24 cookies in about 28 minutes, with no dough chilling required. They bring a fresh, summery note to an assorted dessert plate and pair well with tea or coffee. Set them out when you want a fruit-centered cookie beside the richer chocolate and caramel choices.
Get the Recipe:
Peaches & Cream Cookies
Salted Dark Chocolate Cookies
Salted Dark Chocolate Cookies. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Crisp edges frame the deep cocoa flavor of
Salted Dark Chocolate Cookies
, made with dark chocolate, cocoa powder, and flaky sea salt. The recipe yields 30 cookies in about 27 minutes, with no dough chilling step, so they are an efficient option when a chocolate craving calls. Their bittersweet profile adds contrast to sweeter frosted cookies, and they work well beside coffee or in a lunchbox treat.
Get the Recipe:
Salted Dark Chocolate Cookies
Copycat Crumbl Cookies and Cream Milkshake Cookies
Copycat Crumbl Cookies and Cream Milkshake Cookies. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Vanilla dough, Oreo pieces, and fluffy cookies-and-cream frosting give
Copycat Crumbl Cookies and Cream Milkshake Cookies
their oversized bakery-style look. The recipe makes 18 cookies in about 30 minutes, with a soft base and a creamy topping. They fit birthday tables, bake sales, or any dessert spread where frosted cookies draw a crowd. Their milkshake-inspired flavor adds a familiar cookies-and-cream option alongside the fruit, nut, and chocolate bakes.
Get the Recipe:
Copycat Crumbl Cookies and Cream Milkshake Cookies
Cookie Frosted Chocolate Chip Cookies
Cookie Frosted Chocolate Chip Cookies. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Double cookie layers set
Cookie Frosted Chocolate Chip Cookies
apart: crisp chocolate chip cookies sit under vanilla frosting mixed with more crumbled cookies, then get another cookie crumb topping. The recipe yields 8 large cookies in about 27 minutes, making a small batch feel substantial. They suit bake sales or a dessert plate for serious chocolate chip fans, especially when an ordinary cookie needs a more elaborate finish.
Get the Recipe:
Cookie Frosted Chocolate Chip Cookies
Copycat Thin Mint Cookies
Copycat Thin Mint Cookies. Photo credit: Cook What You Love.
Crisp chocolate rounds carry the familiar mint finish of
Copycat Thin Mint Cookies
, their familiar chocolate-mint combination. The recipe yields 24 cookies in about 30 minutes, using mint extract and a smooth chocolate coating. They keep well in the freezer, so a batch can be saved for a later snack or added to a holiday cookie tray. Their crisp texture brings a welcome change from the soft, frosted, and chewy cookies elsewhere on the plate.
Get the Recipe:
Copycat Thin Mint Cookies
