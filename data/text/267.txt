CHEYENNE, Wyo. — This is one of those good news–bad news situations. The good news is that clocks “fall back” an hour on Sunday, allowing for one extra hour of sleep. The bad news is that it gets darker earlier.
Daylight saving time is used in most states, beginning in the spring, to take advantage of sunlight extended later in the evening. As days get shorter, crop harvesting concludes and the extra sunlight is no longer necessary.
On Nov. 1 at 2 a.m., clocks will reset to 1 a.m., which will give people an extra hour of sleep, but less evening sunlight. When they wake up — or the night before — people may need to wind back their analog clocks.
