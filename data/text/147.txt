Dense fog to clear for sunshine, 60-degree temps in Cheyenne

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Dense morning fog will lift across Laramie County today, clearing the path for afternoon sunshine and temperatures climbing near 60 degrees.
The National Weather Service in Cheyenne issued a
dense fog advisory
, in effect until 9 a.m. for the Interstate 80 Summit, Cheyenne and central and eastern Laramie County. Meteorologists said visibility was dropping below one-quarter of a mile.
A dense fog advisory is in effect until 9 a.m. today, mainly for the Interstate 80 corridor east of Laramie, along with the Interstate 25 corridor, including Cheyenne. (National Weather Service graphic)
Increasing winds will mix out the low-level moisture and end any lingering fog, according to the NWS in Cheyenne.
Along with the warmer air, breezes will pick up across southeast Wyoming. Cheyenne will see winds shift to the south at 5–15 mph in the afternoon. Wind-prone areas along I-80, including Arlington and parts of the Snowy Range face gusts of up to 55 mph under a high wind watch in place through noon.
Skies over Cheyenne will turn partly cloudy tonight as temperatures settle around 37 degrees.
Wednesday will bring morning clouds followed by afternoon clearing, with a daytime high near 63. Winds will gust at up to 30 mph.
Quiet conditions and mild temperatures in the low 60s will hold through Thursday and Friday. Meteorologists said the weather may shift for the weekend and early next week, when incoming systems could bring colder air along with rain and snow showers to southeast Wyoming starting Sunday night.
Detailed Forecast
Today:
Areas of dense fog before 11 a.m. Otherwise, mostly sunny, with a high near 60. West wind at 5–15 mph shifting to the south in the afternoon.
Tonight:
Increasing clouds, with a low around 37. South wind at 5–10 mph shifting to the west after midnight.
Wednesday:
Cloudy through mid-morning, then gradual clearing, with a high near 63. Breezy, with a west wind at 10–15 mph increasing to 15–20 mph in the afternoon. Winds could gust as high as 30 mph.
Wednesday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 34. West northwest wind at 5–10 mph shifting to the south-southeast in the evening.
Thursday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 61. South wind at around 10 mph.
Thursday Night:
Mostly cloudy, with a low around 36.
Friday:
Partly sunny, with a high near 63.
Friday Night:
Mostly cloudy, with a low around 38.
Saturday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 64.
Saturday Night:
A slight chance of showers before midnight. Mostly cloudy, with a low around 38.
Sunday:
A chance of showers. Partly sunny, with a high near 57.
Sunday Night:
Rain and snow showers likely. Mostly cloudy, with a low around 34.
Monday:
A chance of rain and snow showers. Partly sunny, with a high near 50. Breezy.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
