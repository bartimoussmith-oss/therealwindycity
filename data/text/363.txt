Investigators say concrete dust obscured view before fatal Oct. street sweeper crash in Cheyenne

CHEYENNE, Wyo. —
A Cheyenne man was killed Oct. 22 while driving a street sweeper on College Drive
. According to Wyoming Highway Patrol investigators, concrete dust kicked up by the machine obscured the view of another driver before he crashed into the sweeper, ejecting its driver.
A report published by the Wyoming Department of Transportation identified the fatality as 33-year-old Damon Shaw. He was reportedly not wearing his seat belt when the fatal accident occurred.
Both the sweeper and the other vehicle, a Dodge Durango, were headed north on College Drive the morning of Oct. 22. Shaw, driving the sweeper, was hugging the curb as he cleaned the street before the machine picked up concrete dust. Collecting in the air, the Durango driver, who the report said was likely not paying attention, entered the dust cloud and struck the rear of the sweeper.
The Durango driver was reportedly injured, but survived the crash.
Other potential contributing factors, such as weather, were not considered by investigators.
This story contains preliminary information as provided by the Wyoming Highway Patrol via the Wyoming Department of Transportation Fatal Crash Summary map. The agency advises that information may be subject to change.
