CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Incumbent Rep. Justin Fornstrom defeated Constitution Party challenger Tony Perry in Tuesday’s general election to retain his Wyoming House District 10 seat.
In Tuesday’s election, Fornstrom received 2,907 votes to Perry’s 578.
Fornstrom was elected to his first term in the Wyoming House of Representatives in 2024. A farmer, he serves on the House Agriculture, State and Public Lands & Water Resources Committee. He also serves on the House Special Investigative Committee and the Select Water Committee.
