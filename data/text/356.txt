Friday hearing scheduled for Cheyenne July grand theft suspect

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A Laramie County Circuit Court judge will hear the case against a man suspected of stealing thousands of dollars’ worth of silver, coins and $2 bills from his girlfriend Friday, determining if there is probable cause to send him to district court.
Craig Boulse, born in 1981, was arrested in July on one count of felony theft. According to police, he broke into his girlfriend’s safe and cleaned it of its valuables. Boulse is considered innocent until pleading or being found guilty.
Boulse’s criminal affidavit reports that police were dispatched to a home in Cheyenne on July 14. There, they contacted Boulse’s girlfriend, whom he started dating just a few weeks prior. Originally from Colorado, the girlfriend said she had let him stay at her home.
That day, she said, she went to work before 6 a.m. and was told by Boulse midday that he had to go to Casper with his boss. When she returned at the end of the day, she found her home safe — worth about $100 — pried open.
Stolen from it, police said, was a box containing around $1,100 worth of silver bars; $140 worth of silver bars not contained in the box; more than $200 worth of $2 bills; a jelly bean can full of half-dollars, liberty dollars and Eisenhower dollars; and two Crown Royal bags full of other miscellaneous coins.
No one else had access to the apartment besides Boulse, the girlfriend said. She also reported that he cut contact with her immediately after and never came back, leading police to believe that he was responsible for the theft.
He was issued a $10,000 cash bond, which may be reduced based on the outcome of the preliminary hearing Friday, Nov. 6.
