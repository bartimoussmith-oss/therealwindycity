CASPER, Wyo. — A
30-year-old woman reported missing late last week
was found dead Sunday morning after
her vehicle broke through a guardrail on Casper Mountain Road
, police said.
The Natrona County Coroner’s Office identified the driver as Kandice Gunter, who had been missing since Oct. 29.
(Photo courtesy of the Casper Police Department)
First responders were called to the scene along Wyoming Highway 251 near Lookout Point around 9:20 a.m. Responding crews found the vehicle down the hillside and discovered Gunter inside. She was pronounced dead at the scene and was the only person in the vehicle.
The crash forced authorities to reduce traffic on the mountain road to a single lane Sunday morning while recovery crews worked the steep slope.
The circumstances of the wreck and Gunter’s cause of death remain under investigation,
according to the Casper Police Department
. Police said there is no evidence to suggest foul play or the involvement of any other person.
Gunter had been the focus of a search since Thursday evening, when she was reported missing to Casper police. Officers had asked the public to watch for Gunter and her gray 2016 GMC Canyon pickup truck after her phone was last tracked Friday afternoon near the Natrona County line along Wyoming Highway 387.
Agencies responding to the mountain Sunday included the Casper Police Department, Natrona County Sheriff’s Office, Wyoming Highway Patrol, Casper Fire-EMS and the Natrona County Fire Protection District.
