Jonne Rae Moore
Jonne Rae Moore
: 1938 – 2026
Jonne Rae Moore went home to be with the lord on October 11, 2026. She was born in Mitchell, Nebraska to Bert and Vivian Kenzy, on July 24, 1938, and died of cancer in Cody, Wyoming at the age of 88. She was a devout Christian with strong beliefs.
Surviving family includes her children: Shannon Mangus (Que), Shelly Livingston, and Jay Moore; five grandchildren; and five great-grandchildren; her sister, Jacque Halsey; and brother, Curt Kenzy (Darlene).
She was preceded in death by her parents, Bert and Vivian Kenzy; sister, Maxine Romsa; and spouse, James Ray Moore.
A memorial service will be held in her life-long community of Albin at 2 p.m. on Thursday, October 22, 2026, at Albin Baptist Church, 316 Cheyenne Avenue, Albin, Wyoming 82050. Reception to follow.
In lieu of flowers, donations may be made to Operation Christmas Child Shoebox collection (through Albin Baptist Church), Samaritan’s Purse, or Operation Exodus.
Carol Dee Dingman:
1961 – 2026
Carol Dee Dingman was born on April 14, 1961, in Florida. She lived in Cheyenne, Wyoming, alongside her beloved husband, Mike Dingman, who brought her happiness, comfort, and a sense of safety and love.
Carol cherished the simple joys in life, especially taking little trips and making special memories with her husband. The time they spent together brought her great happiness, and their love was an important part of her life.
She also treasured the bond she shared with her son, Cody Noska, and her grandson, Cash Noska, who live in Texas. She loved talking with them and looked forward to visiting whenever possible. Even with the distance between them, the love she had for her family remained strong.
Carol was loved by many, and her kindness, love, and presence touched the lives of those around her. She will be remembered for the happiness she found in spending time with the people she loved most and for the special memories she leaves behind.
Carol is survived by her loving husband, Mike Dingman; her son, Cody Noska; and her grandson, Cash Noska.
Though she is no longer with us, the love she gave and the memories she created will live on in the hearts of her family and all who knew her.
She will be deeply missed and forever loved.
