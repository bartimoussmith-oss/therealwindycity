Dear Cheyenne,
How come the bid to repair the historic pumphouse didn’t follow the requirements for contracting selection, especially when the fire happened last May?
Being proactive in selecting bids prior to deciding the buildings future would seem appropriate, especially if keeping it was part of the debate. I think some better explanation is in order.
Billy Holmes
Cheyenne
