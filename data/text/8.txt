Council to hold work session on Cox Ranch annexation
Published on August 25, 2026
The Cheyenne City Council will hold a work session to receive information and discuss the annexation of a section of the Cox Ranch lands on Friday, Aug. 28, at noon. No votes will be taken during this work session.
An agenda for the meeting will be available at
www.cheyennecity.org/minutesandagendas
.
The proposed annexation is 1,197.76 acres and is located on the west side of Cheyenne on Happy Jack Road north of the existing North Range Business Park. The land is intended to be developed as a data center by Skybox Datacenters and ViaWest.
This annexation was postponed in April to allow for more time for the companies to conduct community engagement. It is scheduled to return to City Council’s Public Services Committee on second reading on Sept. 8 and will then go before the full Council on Sept. 14. If approved, the annexation will return to Public Services Committee and the full Council for consideration on third and final reading.
The work session is available to attend in-person in Council Chambers at the Municipal Building (2101 O’Neil Ave.) and will also be available to watch remotely. A Zoom link and information about watching remotely will be available at
www.cheyennecity.org/zoom
.
No public comment will be taken during the work session and no other business will be conducted. Public comment about the annexation will be taken at the Sept. 8 and Sept. 14 meetings as well as on third reading.
A map of the proposed annexation is available at
http://connect.cheyennecity.org/coxannexation
.
Information about the proposed zone change for this parcel is available at
http://connect.cheyennecity.org/coxzoning
.
