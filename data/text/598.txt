CHEYENNE – It took a photo finish, but Cheyenne East senior Maggie Madsen won the individual title in the Championship division at the Nike Cross Northwest Regional on Saturday at The Course in Spokane Valley, Washington.
Madsen made a late charge down the final stretch and nipped Ellery Lincoln of Portland, Oregon, at the finish line. Both runners’ final time was listed as 16 minutes, 41.5 seconds.
Madsen’s time breaks the meet record of 16:50.6 set by Rawlins’ Sydney Thorvaldson-Vaught in 2019.
The meet included runners from Alaska, Idaho, Hawaii, Montana, Oregon, Washington and Wyoming.
Cheyenne Central senior Adalyn Racines finished fourth in 16:53.8. The Indians were fourth in the team standings. Seniors Sofia Rose (50th), Malea Adams-Case (67th), Claire Coles (142nd), freshman Lyla Rose (111th) and sophomore Karley Dobbin (182nd) rounded out Central’s team.
Madsen and Racines will compete at Nike Cross-Country Nationals on Dec. 5 in Portland.
Laramie’s girls placed third in the team standings. Junior Dorothy Jensen placed seventh (17:05.5).
On the boys side, Central sophomore Cal Bibbey placed 22nd in 14:55.8.
