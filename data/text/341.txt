Cheyenne to see seasonable, quiet weather before weekend warmup

Klark Byrd

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Cheyenne will see calm conditions and seasonable temperatures through the workweek before a sunny weekend warmup pushes highs back into the 60s.
Highs across the capital city will hover in the 50s through Friday with minimal chances for moisture, according to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne.
For today, Cheyenne will see mostly cloudy skies, a high near 51 degrees and light winds. Cloud cover will linger overnight as the low drops to around 30.
Sunshine returns to central Laramie County on Thursday with an afternoon high near 54. Clouds will thicken again Thursday night ahead of another weak disturbance, with the overnight low dipping to around 31.
Meteorologists call for a 20% chance of light showers in Cheyenne after 11 a.m. Friday, though impacts will stay low. The high will reach near 57 under mostly sunny skies before clearing Friday night alongside a low around 30.
Across southeast Wyoming, morning commuters west of the Laramie Range are running into patchy valley fog this morning due to lingering moisture from earlier in the week. A compact low tracking south into Colorado will spread clouds across areas southwest of a Douglas–Sidney line, kicking off isolated rain and mountain snow showers over Carbon County, meteorologists said.
A ridge of high pressure will build over the region this weekend, driving dry air and much warmer weather across the plains.
Saturday will be sunny with a high near 58 in Cheyenne. Sunday will see temps jump to 63 degrees, with Monday rising to 65. Meteorologists expect highs east of the Laramie Range to sit roughly 10 degrees above seasonal averages through early next week, while areas west of the mountains reach the mid- to upper 50s.

A 20% chance of showers after 11 a.m. Mostly sunny, with a high near 57. Northwest wind at 5–10 mph.
