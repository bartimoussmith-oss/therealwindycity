CFR rescues driver stranded in floodwater in Cheyenne on Saturday

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — After a water main break trapped a person on top of their vehicle in a flooding street early Saturday morning, Cheyenne Fire Rescue utilized a ladder truck to get them out.
According to a Facebook post by CFR
, a man was driving along Gregg Way in Cheyenne at around 6 a.m. Saturday when he encountered a pool of water formed by a broken water main. He attempted to navigate through it but underestimated its depth and got his truck stuck.
CFR’s Battalion 1, Engine 4, and Ladder 1 were dispatched to the incident. Ladder 1’s aerial ladder was used to reach the driver, who had gotten on top of the vehicle to wait for rescue. No injuries were reported.
“This incident highlights the skill, teamwork, and adaptability of our firefighters as they work to protect our community, even in challenging and potentially dangerous conditions,” CFR said. “Great work by all crews involved in bringing this incident to a safe conclusion!”
