Warm, sunny conditions to continue across Cheyenne through Friday

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Above-average temperatures and sunny skies will linger across southeast Wyoming through the end of the workweek before cooler air moves in for the weekend.
The capital city is set to climb to a high near 61 degrees for Veterans Day under clear skies, according to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne. Winds from the northwest will blow at 10–15 mph, with occasional gusts reaching up to 25 mph before tapering off by late afternoon.
Tonight will stay clear as the temperature drops to a low around 38. High pressure is keeping conditions roughly 10 degrees above normal for this time of year, with temps in the low to mid-60s expected along and east of the Interstate 25 corridor and mid-50s west of the Laramie Range, meteorologists said.
Thursday and Friday will bring more mild fall weather. Meteorologists expect highs to top out near 65 in Cheyenne under mostly sunny skies both days. Overnight lows will hover between the low and mid-30s.
Clouds will increase Saturday, dropping Cheyenne’s daytime high to near 53.
While the highest chances for scattered rain and snow showers are over the Snowy and Sierra Madre ranges, Cheyenne will see a slight chance of showers late Saturday night. The low will fall to near 32.
Drier air returns Sunday with mostly sunny skies and a high near 52. Southeast Wyoming will remain mild and dry into early next week, with daytime highs rebounding into the mid- to upper 50s for Monday and Tuesday.
Detailed Forecast
Veterans Day:
Sunny, with a high near 61. Northwest wind at 10–15 mph.
Tonight:
Clear, with a low around 38. West-northwest wind at 5–10 mph.
Thursday:
Sunny, with a high near 65. West-northwest wind at 5–10 mph shifting to the southwest in the afternoon.
Thursday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 36. West wind at around 5 mph.
Friday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 65. West wind at around 5 mph shifting to the southeast in the afternoon.
Friday Night:
Mostly cloudy, with a low around 32.
Saturday:
Partly sunny, with a high near 53.
Saturday Night:
A slight chance of showers after 11 p.m. Mostly cloudy, with a low around 32.
Sunday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 52.
Sunday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 29.
Monday:
Sunny, with a high near 56.
Monday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 30.
Tuesday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 57.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
