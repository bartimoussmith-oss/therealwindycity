ROCK SPRINGS, Wyo. — Two people were killed Friday, Nov. 13 in a head-on crash near Rock Springs.
According to reports from the Wyoming Highway Patrol, a Jeep was traveling north along U.S. Highway 191 when it fully entered the southbound lanes near mile marker 33. The Jeep collided head-on with a van headed south, killing both drivers.
The WHP has identified the deceased as 29-year-old Wyoming resident Mario Lozoya and 19-year-old Utah resident Lilliani Serawop.
Driver inattention and fatigue are cited as possible contributing factors by the WHP.
According to data from the WHP, there have been 118 motor vehicle fatalities in Wyoming so far this year.
This story contains preliminary information as provided by the Wyoming Highway Patrol via the Wyoming Department of Transportation Fatal Crash Summary map. The agency advises that information may be subject to change.
