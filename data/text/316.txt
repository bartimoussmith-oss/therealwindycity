CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Incumbent Sen. Evie Brennan defeated Democrat challenger Shifa Hamid in Tuesday’s general election to retain her Wyoming Senate District 31 seat.
In Tuesday’s election, Brennan received 3,172 votes to Hamid’s 1,479.
Brennan was challenged in August’s primary election
by Rep. Daniel Singh, defeating him 1,753 votes to 894.
Brennan is a registered nurse from Cheyenne who chairs the Senate Labor, Health and Social Services Committee. She centered her reelection campaign on healthcare, education and housing access. First elected to represent Senate District 31 in 2022, Brennan focuses her legislative work on hospital pricing transparency, affordable housing initiatives and public school facility access.
