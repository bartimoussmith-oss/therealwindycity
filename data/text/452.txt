A Laramie County Sheriff's Office deputy poses with their vehicle. (LCSO photo)
LARAMIE COUNTY, Wyo. — The Laramie County Sheriff’s Office announced on Wednesday that at approximately 1:42 p.m., deputies with the LCSO were involved in a shooting off Interstate 80, near mile marker 377.
A social media post from the LCSO says that all deputies are safe and the suspect was pronounced dead at the scene.
“There is no ongoing threat to the public,” the post states. “The scene is large and complex; please avoid the area and follow any traffic directions or closures.”
The Wyoming Division of Criminal Investigation has been requested and is currently investigating. This remains an active and ongoing investigation.
Cap City News will update this story as more information becomes available.
