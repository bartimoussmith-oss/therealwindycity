Highway Patrol releases report for Sunday crash involving missing Casper woman
by
Garrett Grochowski
November 5, 2026
November 5, 2026

CASPER, Wyo. — 30-year-old Kandice Gunter, who had been reported missing days prior, was found among the wreckage of a Casper Mountain car crash Nov. 1. A report by the Wyoming Highway Patrol indicates that her truck traveled around 75 feet in the air before hitting the ground. The investigation into the crash is still ongoing.
First responders were called to the scene along Wyoming Highway 251 near Lookout Point on Casper Mountain around 9:20 a.m. Sunday, Nov. 1 Responding crews found a vehicle down the hillside and discovered Gunter inside.
She was pronounced dead at the scene and was the only person in the vehicle.
Reported missing on Thursday, Oct. 29, She had been the focus of a multi-day search before her discovery.
While the investigation into the circumstances of Gunter’s original disappearance remains open, a recently published report by the WHP includes new information about the crash itself.
According to the report, Gunter was heading northbound in the direction of Casper in her GMC Canyon when, upon passing the Lookout Point parking area, she turned left. She then turned her vehicle hard to the right, causing it to enter a high-speed slide.
The car’s front end hit the guardrail and the vehicle vaulted over it, traveling around 75 feet in the air before violently striking the ground. The vehicle bounced and rolled continuously, tumbling downslope and hitting a group of pine trees, which forced it upright. The vehicle continued until the driver’s side struck a 24-inch-diameter pine tree, forcing a stop.
The crash is still under investigation, and investigators have not yet determined at what approximate time it occurred. The window of time that Gunter could have crashed begins at around 10:30 p.m. Oct. 29 — the evening she was reported missing — and ends in the very early hours of Nov. 1 before the crash was discovered.
Agencies responding to the mountain Sunday included the Casper Police Department, Natrona County Sheriff’s Office, Wyoming Highway Patrol, Casper Fire-EMS and the Natrona County Fire Protection District.
Multiple agencies responded to a reported vehicle crash at Lookout Point on Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026. (Klark Byrd, Oil City News)
This story contains preliminary information as provided by the Wyoming Highway Patrol via the Wyoming Department of Transportation Fatal Crash Summary map. The agency advises that information may be subject to change.
