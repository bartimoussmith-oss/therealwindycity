Mild temperatures hold in Cheyenne today ahead of cooler weekend

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Warm, dry weather will stick around Cheyenne today ahead of a Pacific storm system bringing cooler temperatures and a slight chance of showers on Saturday.
Partly sunny skies and a high near 61 degrees are expected in the capital city  today, according to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne. West winds at 5–10 mph will shift to the south-southeast this afternoon. Tonight will be mostly cloudy with a low around 30 and a south-southeast wind at around 5 mph.
Across southeast Wyoming, highs will reach the upper 50s to mid-60s. Meteorologists expect the highest temperatures along and just east of the Interstate 25 corridor. Another round of low clouds and patchy fog could develop across the high plains and far-eastern Wyoming late tonight and early Saturday. Meteorologists said that fog may reach Laramie County after 2 a.m. Saturday, clearing later in the morning.
A Pacific storm system moving into the region Saturday is expected to split and stay mostly south of Wyoming. In Cheyenne, Saturday will be mostly cloudy with a high near 50, a south wind at 5–10 mph and a 20% chance of showers after 11 a.m.
Shower chances in Cheyenne continue before 11 p.m. under mostly cloudy skies. The overnight low will fall to around 31, with south winds at around 5 mph shifting to the west-southwest after midnight.
Dry weather returns Sunday as the storm moves east. Cheyenne will see sunny skies, a high near 53 and a west-southwest wind at 5–10 mph, followed by a mostly clear night with a low around 30.
Warmth and sunshine will hold through early next week. Cheyenne can expect sunny skies and a high near 58 on Monday, with a mostly clear night featuring a low around 31. Tuesday will be mostly sunny and breezy across the region, the mercury resting near 59 in Cheyenne before dropping  to an overnight low around 32 under partly cloudy skies.
Detailed Forecast
Today:
Partly sunny, with a high near 61. West wind at 5–10 mph shifting to the south-southeast in the afternoon.
Tonight:
Mostly cloudy, with a low around 30. South-southeast wind at around 5 mph.
Saturday:
A 20% chance of showers after 11 a.m. Mostly cloudy, with a high near 50. South wind at 5–10 mph.
Saturday Night:
A 20% chance of showers before 11 p.m. Mostly cloudy, with a low around 31. South wind at around 5 mph shifting to the west-southwest after midnight.
Sunday:
Sunny, with a high near 53. West-southwest wind at 5–10 mph.
Sunday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 30.
Monday:
Sunny, with a high near 58.
Monday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 31.
Tuesday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 59.
Tuesday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 32.
Wednesday:
A chance of rain and snow showers. Mostly sunny, with a high near 50.
Wednesday Night:
A chance of rain and snow showers. Partly cloudy, with a low around 27.
Thursday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 49.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
