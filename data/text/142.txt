University of Wyoming linebacker Steven Perez was named the Mountain West freshman of the week, the league announced Monday.
It marked the third consecutive week a Cowboys player was honored by the conference.
Perez led UW with 17 tackles in a 28-14 win over Air Force last Friday. It was the most tackles in a game for the Pokes since Chad Muma had 17 against Utah State in 2021.
Perez added a fumble recovery while tallying 10 solo stops.
Perez is second on the Cowboys this season with 54 total tackles. He has also recorded 5½ tackles for loss to rank second on the team. The redshirt freshman has 1½ sacks and two fumble recoveries.
The Cowboys (5-3 overall, 4-1 MW) are on a bye before taking on UNLV at 8:30 p.m. Nov. 7 in Las Vegas.
