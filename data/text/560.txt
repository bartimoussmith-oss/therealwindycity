CHEYENNE, Wyo. — On Friday, the Cheyenne Police Department announced that it was searching for suspects involved in the vandalism of multiple soccer fields.
A social media post from the CPD says that officers were dispatched to Cheyenne North Park on Friday morning.
“We know better than anyone that there are lots of ways to enjoy donuts — this certainly isn’t one of them,” the Cheyenne PD wrote. “This morning, Cheyenne Police Officers were dispatched to a report of vandalism at Cheyenne North Park. Tire tracks were discovered across multiple soccer fields, causing extensive damage.”
The CPD says those with any information about the incident may contact CSO Zabriskie at
307-637-6519
or via email at
dzabriskie@cheyennepd.org
.
Information can also be provided anonymously at
307-638-8477
or online at
silentwitnesslaramiecounty.com
.
Crime Stoppers Silent Witness is offering a reward of up to $1,000 for information leading to the identification of a suspect.
