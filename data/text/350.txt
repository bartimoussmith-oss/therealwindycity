Nov. 5 Thankful Thursday to benefit VOA Northern Rockies services

(VOA Northern Rockies)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Thankful Thursday, a weekly community fundraiser supporting local nonprofit organizations, will feature VOA Northern Rockies this week. From 4 to 8 p.m. today, visitors ages 21 and older can learn about the organization’s mission and have fun with the community.
Known as the party with a purpose, Thankful Thursday’s weekly fundraisers bring together the community to participate in activities like raffles and games. The proceeds from Nov. 5’s event will directly support VOA Northern Rockies’ programs in Cheyenne and across the region.
The organization supports more than 10,000 people across Wyoming, Montana and western South Dakota, helping them access outpatient mental healthcare, crisis stabilization and supportive housing, addiction treatment and other veterans services.
“Thankful Thursday shows what Cheyenne can accomplish when local businesses, donors and neighbors come together,” Cheryl Hackett, vice president of institutional advancement for VOA Northern Rockies, said in a Thankful Thursday release. “That support helps more people find mental health care, addiction treatment, stable housing and services for veterans when they need them.”
This week, each attendee will receive a free wristband and entry into a bottle drawing. Ten numbers are selected each week, and winners can receive up to $1,000 and qualify for the season’s final drawing. Attendees must be present to win. Raffle prizes include Colorado Eagles hockey tickets, University of Wyoming 2027 football tickets with pre-game field access, three Marky’s Meat Market meat bundles and a Little America overnight stay with golf for two. Laramie County Community College will serve a spaghetti dinner for a suggested donation of $10 per person.

VOA Northern Rockies website
and the
Thankful Thursday web page on the Cheyenne Greenway Foundation website
