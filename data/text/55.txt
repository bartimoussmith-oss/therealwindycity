Cool weekend in Cheyenne brings widespread rain chances Sunday, Monday
by
Nick Perkins
October 24, 2026

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A cold front that swept across southeast Wyoming early Saturday morning will keep temperatures well below normal this weekend before an incoming Pacific weather system delivers widespread rain to Cheyenne and Laramie County through Monday.
According to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne, daytime highs across the region will run 5–10 degrees below average Saturday and Sunday, topping out in the mid-40s to lower 50s. Overnight lows along the Interstate 80 corridor are expected to drop into the upper 20s to mid-30s.
A moisture-rich storm system that previously triggered flooding in the Desert Southwest will lift northeast across the Front Range over the weekend, bringing substantial precipitation chances to southeast Wyoming. Meteorologists forecast widespread rainfall totals between a fifth and a half of an inch, with precipitation probabilities topping 60% across Laramie County starting Sunday and lingering into Monday.
Because overnight temperatures east of the Laramie Range are forecast to remain in the mid- to upper 30s, the precipitation in Cheyenne is expected to stay primarily as liquid rain rather than snow. Higher elevations in the regional mountains could see light snow accumulations, generally under 5 inches.
The unsettled weather will gradually push east into the Nebraska Panhandle by Monday afternoon, giving way to a brief warming trend. High temperatures in Cheyenne and areas east of the Laramie Range are forecast to rebound into the lower 60s by Tuesday and Wednesday.
However, dry conditions may be accompanied by increasing winds midweek. Forecasters note that an approaching secondary trough off the Pacific Northwest will strengthen regional pressure gradients on Wednesday. The setup could generate gusty southwest winds and potential high-wind hazards, especially in wind-prone corridors along Interstates 80 and 25.
Detailed Forecast
Today:
Partly sunny, with a high near 48. North-northeast wind at 10–15 mph shifting to the east.
Tonight:
A chance of showers between 7 p.m. and midnight. Patchy fog after 2 a.m. Otherwise, cloudy, with a low around 33. South-southeast wind at around 10 mph. Chance of precipitation is 30%.
Sunday:
Patchy fog before 8 a.m. Otherwise, mostly cloudy, with a high near 48. South-southeast wind at around 10 mph shifting to the east in the afternoon.
Sunday Night:
Showers before 9 p.m., then showers and possibly a thunderstorm until midnight, followed by likely showers. Low around 35. East wind at 5–10 mph shifting to the north-northwest after midnight. Chance of precipitation is 80%.
Monday:
A 40% chance of showers before noon. Cloudy, then gradually becoming mostly sunny, with a high near 52. North-northwest wind at around 5 mph shifting to the south-southwest in the afternoon.
Monday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 32.
Tuesday:
Sunny, with a high near 60.
Tuesday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 34.
Wednesday:
Sunny, with a high near 61.
Wednesday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 34.
Thursday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 63.
Thursday Night:
Mostly cloudy, with a low around 38.
Friday:
Partly sunny, with a high near 63.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
