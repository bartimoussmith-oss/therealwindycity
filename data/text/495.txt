ROCK SPRINGS -- Sweetwater County School District No. 1 is providing an update regarding recent rumors and text messages circulating among students concerning a possible threat to Rock Springs Junior High School (RSJH).
School administration and Central Administration Building (CAB) personnel are aware of the messages and concerns being shared among students, according to a Nov. 12 press release from SCSD No. 1.
The Rock Springs Police Department (RSPD) began actively investigating the matter last night and has determined that there is no credible threat to students or staff at Rock Springs Junior High School.
School is in session today and operating on its normal schedule.
The district takes all safety concerns seriously and appreciates the prompt attention and investigation by the RSPD. Students are encouraged to report any concerning messages or information directly to a trusted adult or law enforcement rather than forwarding or sharing unverified information through text messages or social media.
Sweetwater County School District No.1 appreciates the continued partnership of families and the community in helping maintain a safe learning environment for all students and staff.
