LCSD1 renews dual, concurrent enrollment agreement with LCCC

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The Laramie County School District 1 Board of Trustees voted Monday to purchase seven new school buses, subscribe to a digital budget book software, and renew its dual enrollment memorandum of understanding with Laramie County Community College.
During the board’s Nov. 2 meeting, in which the trustees also approved
renewal of the charter for PODER Academy
and an update to its early retirement benefits plan, trustees made some quick, but impactful district decisions.
They moved to purchase seven new school buses, worth at most $1,452,095, from Elder Equipment Leasing. Seven of the district’s current school buses will be declared as surplus once the new fleet additions arrive.
They also unanimously approved a new $2.5 million memorandum of understanding with LCCC for the 2026–27 Concurrent and Dual Enrollment Program.
The agreement allows LCSD1 students to gain college credit while still in high school through two similar but distinct methods.
Concurrent enrollment means that students can take college-level classes for credit, but through the district. The classes are taught in district schools by district teachers, but the advanced curriculum earns them credit. The MOU helps support this by offsetting what the credits normally cost at LCCC through the teachers’ salaries.
Dual enrollment means students are taking high school courses in LCSD1 schools and college courses at LCCC at the same time. Dual enrollment costs are reimbursed through state data collection supported by a $10 million statewide pool.
The board also voted unanimously to purchase digital budget book software from ClearGov Inc. for an amount not to exceed $86,485.20. The software, according to the district, helps streamline district financial reporting, budget formatting and public transparency tools for future fiscal years.
