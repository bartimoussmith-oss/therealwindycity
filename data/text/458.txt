Mounted procession to decorate Cheyenne bronzes with wreaths on Nov. 21

CHEYENNE, Wyo. — A mounted procession will travel across Cheyenne’s downtown Nov. 21, decorating the many bronzes along Capitol Avenue with wreaths. Led my
the Mounted Marshals
, the Lighting the Legacy procession will honor the stories of those depicted by the statues as well as the community effort that led to their creation.
The procession will depart from the Wyoming State Capitol at 4:30 p.m. Nov. 21. It will travel south to the Depot Plaza, stopping as local leaders and business owners place a wreath at each bronze to honor their story.
“These sculptures tell the stories of leaders, railroad workers, Indigenous people, military heroes, ranchers and everyday residents who helped shape Cheyenne and Wyoming,” Visit Cheyenne’s Marie Margot said in a release.
The wreaths also honor the people who worked to make the bronzes possible. The Capitol Avenue Bronze Project began as an idea by Cheyenne gallery owner Harvey Deselms and was eventually realized by the Capitol Avenue Bronze Commission.
“Lighting the Legacy brings Cheyenne’s past and present together along Capitol Avenue,” Amber Trevizo, director of visitor experience for Visit Cheyenne, said. “The bronzes preserve the stories of those who shaped our community, while the people and businesses placing the wreaths represent those carrying that legacy forward today. We are grateful to Harvey Deselms and everyone whose vision and support made this meaningful connection possible.”
Members of the Cheyenne community are welcome to watch and carry battery-operated LED candles to help create a corridor of light for the procession. They can also stop by the Holiday Night Market, happening at the Depot Plaza the same day from 3:30 to 6:30 p.m.

Visit Cheyenne’s web page on holiday events happening in town
