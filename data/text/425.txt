Warm, dry weather and breezy winds expected in Cheyenne this week
by
Klark Byrd
November 9, 2026
November 9, 2026

(Garrett Grochowski, Cap City News)
CHEYENNE, Wyo. — Southeast Wyoming will see warm, dry weather continue through the week, with breezy conditions expected today and Tuesday.
High temperatures today will reach the mid- to upper 60s for areas east of Interstate 25, according to the National Weather Service in Cheyenne. Skies will be sunny, with wind gusts reaching up to 40 mph in wind-prone areas. Tonight’s low will be around 38 degrees.
Tuesday will remain dry and mostly sunny with a high near 65 degrees. Winds will increase, with meteorologists forecasting gusts of up to 50 mph in wind-prone areas by the afternoon. Tuesday night will be mostly clear with a low around 37.
Veterans Day on Wednesday will be the coolest day of the week, featuring sunny skies and a high near 60 degrees. Northwest winds could gust as high as 25 mph.
A warming trend will begin Thursday and carry into the weekend. High temperatures will range from the low to mid-60s, keeping the area 10–15 degrees above average for November.
Meteorologists expect no snow for the next seven days. The next chance for a storm system to enter the area is between Nov. 16 and Nov. 19, making a run for a spot on the top 10 latest first snowfalls on record. Last year, the first snow was Nov. 8, coming in ninth on the list.
The latest first snowfall on record for Cheyenne occurred on Nov. 20, 1900. No snow is expected for at least the next seven days.
Detailed Forecast
Today:
Sunny, with a high near 68. West wind at 5–10 mph shifting to the south in the afternoon.
Tonight:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 38. Southwest wind at 5–10 mph.
Tuesday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 65. Breezy, with a west wind at 10–15 mph increasing to 20–25 mph in the morning. Winds could gust as high as 40 mph.
Tuesday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 37. Breezy, with a west-northwest wind at 15–20 mph and gusts as high as 30 mph.
Veterans Day:
Sunny, with a high near 60. Northwest wind at 10–15 mph, with gusts as high as 25 mph.
Wednesday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 35.
Thursday:
Sunny, with a high near 64.
Thursday Night:
Mostly clear, with a low around 34.
Friday:
Sunny, with a high near 65.
Friday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 37.
Saturday:
Sunny, with a high near 63.
Saturday Night:
Partly cloudy, with a low around 35.
Sunday:
Mostly sunny, with a high near 63.
More on the weather is available from the NWS in Cheyenne.
