CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The Cheyenne Metropolitan Planning Organization will host a public open house Nov. 17 for residents to provide feedback on potential future extensions of Parsley Boulevard and Wallick Road.
The extensions, the MPO said, would run south of College Drive and west of U.S. Highway 85. The extensions would run approximately southwest of Orchard Valley.
The open house will be Tuesday, Nov. 17 from 5:30 to 7 p.m. at
Afflerbach Elementary School
. Feedback from the open house will be factored into the final plan for the two corridors, which is in development by the MPO.
See the
MPO website
for more information about the various projects the city is considering for future growth.
