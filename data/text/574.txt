CHEYENNE, Wyo. — The Laramie County Sheriff’s Office is gearing up to auction off its stockpile of abandoned vehicles this week. On Nov. 18 from 9 to 10 a.m., Wyomingites can place their bids on all sorts of vehicles, ranging from a Cadillac SRX to a homemade flatbed trailer.
The auction will take place at
13907 Bullseye Blvd.
in Laramie County’s Archer Complex.
The following vehicles are up for auction:
2011 Audi A6
1999 Suzuki Marauder
2015 Cadillac SRX-4
2011 Chevrolet HHR
2004 Chevrolet Cavalier
2010 Infiniti G37X
2008 Subaru Forester
2000 Lexus GS 300
1979 International Dump Truck
Homemade flatbed trailer
1994 Sportsmens Camper
25-foot Bumper Pull Camper
Elmers Pop-up Camper
“These vehicles have no guarantee whatsoever, and will be sold as is to the highest bidder,” the sheriff’s office warned. “Each person will be given bidding forms to record their bids. Turn in your form as you exit the sale, and the highest bidder will be notified by the sheriff’s office later. This is a cash only sale.”
(Laramie County Sheriff’s Office)
