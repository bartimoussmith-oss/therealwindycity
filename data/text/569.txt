RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
November 9, 2026
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in City Council Chambers and via electronic conference meeting. Present were:
MAYOR – Patrick Collins COUNCIL MEMBERS – Dr. Michelle Aldrich, Dr. Kathy Emmons,
Ken Esquibel, Pete Laybourn, Mark Moody, Dr. Mark Rinne, Tom Segrave, Jeff White and
Lawrence Wolfe. Also present: Kylie Soden, City Clerk. The pledge of allegiance was recited.
Consent Agenda. (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion. There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two
other members is received. Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its
normal sequence on the agenda). Mr. Segrave moved to approve, seconded by Mr. White. Motion
carried. Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body.
[CA] Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on October 26, 2026. Motion (per
Consent Agenda) carried.
Mayor Collins opened a public hearing to determine whether compliance of annexation
conditions required by Wyoming Statute §15-1-402 exist for about 5.06 acres of land located south
of E Fox Farm Road, north of E Allison Road and east of adjacent to S College Drive. Public
comment was made by: Tommie Kniseley and Tony Perry. There being no further comments or
input and the purpose of the public hearing having been met, Mayor Collins closed the public
hearing.
Mayor Collins opened a public hearing for a bar & grill liquor license application filed with
the City Clerk’s Office for Bally’s Wyoming, LLC d/b/a Bally’s Cheyenne, 1734 Meadowland
Dr., Cheyenne, WY. Public comment was made by: Kerry Smith, Bally’s Wyoming LLC. There
being no further comments or input and the purpose of the public hearing having been met, Mayor
Collins closed the public hearing and referred the application to Finance Committee.
Mayor Collins opened a public hearing for a restaurant liquor license application filed with
the City Clerk’s Office for Shack & Co LLC d/b/a Wing Shack, 5719 Yellowstone Rd., Cheyenne,
WY. Public comment was made by: Steve Murlowski, Shack & Co. LLC. Comment was made by
the following member of the governing body: Dr. Aldrich. There being no further comments or
input and the purpose of the public hearing having been met, Mayor Collins closed the public
hearing and referred the application to Finance Committee.
