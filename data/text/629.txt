University of Wyoming’s Eowyn Dalbec finished 171st in the women’s 6-kilometer race at the NCAA Division I Cross-Country Championships on Saturday at the LaVern Gibson Cross Country Course in Terre Haute, Indiana.
Dalbec finished in 20 minutes, 41.8 seconds.
She became the first Cowgirl since 2022 and the seventh individual Cowgirl to qualify for the race.
Dalbec entered with a personal-best of 20:01.4, which she set on the championship course during the Pre-National Invitational on Oct. 16.
South Carolina’s Salma Elbadra, who spent the 2024 season at Wyoming, finished as runner-up in 18:51.8. Elbadra surged from fifth in the final kilometer and nearly caught eventual champion Hannah Gapes (North Carolina State).
Gapes won in 18:44.3.
Alabama won the team title with 120 points.
