Cowgirls soccer embracing playoff mindset against Air Force

  • WyoSports staff Via UW Athletics

University of Wyoming soccer is in playoff mode.

The Cowgirls have two regular-season matches left, likely needing points from both to keep their postseason hopes alive.

The first of those matches is against Air Force at 1 p.m. Sunday at the Louis S. Madrid Sports Complex. The match will be streamed on MW+.

“It has been the same story the last two years, it’s going to come down to the last week,” head coach Josh Purdum said. “If we are focused one game at a time, and we give ourselves the best chance to win, we’ll go into the last week with the idea that we are playing as if we are in a quarterfinal.”

UW is 11-5-1 overall and 4-3-1 (13 points) in Mountain West play. The top of table includes: Colorado College (19 points), Air Force (18 points), Hawaii (18 points), New Mexico (16 points), Grand Canyon (13 points) and UC Davis (10 points). Those seven squads are battling for four conference tournament spots.

The Falcons come into Sunday’s contest as the hottest team in the league. They’ve won five in a row and, in addition to their conference mark, also own an 11-4-1 overall record.

The Cowgirls have dropped consecutive matches for the first time this season. They fell at Grand Canyon, 2-1, on Wednesday, which followed a 2-1 loss at Colorado College last weekend.

Sunday will feature two of the best offensive threats in the league. Wyoming boasts Alyssa Glover, who has netted seven goals and logged eight assists for 22 points, which is second in the conference. Air Force’s Annika Jost has eight goals and seven assists for 23 points, which is first in the league.

It will also be a stout battle in net. UW's Haley Bartel has 53 saves with 16 goals surrendered for a goals against average of 0.95, while the Falcons’ Sydney Nelms owns 60 saves with 14 goals allowed for a GAA of 1.04.

The Cowgirls own a slight 14-13-4 edge in the all-time series; however, the Falcons have gone 2-0-2 in the last four, including a 1-0 win in Laramie last season.

Following the match with the Falcons, the Pokes close out the regular season at home against Hawaii at noon Nov. 7.
