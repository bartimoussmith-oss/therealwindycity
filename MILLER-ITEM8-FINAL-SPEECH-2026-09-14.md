### SPEECH — READ VERBATIM — 410 WORDS — 3 MINUTES — RECORD-READY

Mayor Collins, members of the city council, Charles Miller, Laramie County, for the permanent administrative record — Item 8, second reading, amending the Official Zoning Map, establishing AG Agricultural and P Public for land annexed west of Roundtop Road and adjacent to Happy Jack Road, PUDC-26-37 Cox Ranch, postponed from April 27.

Three asks in the first 45 seconds, numbered for the record.

One: continue this item. Your own Section 8 says — quote — **"shall be in full force and effect upon its approval and publication, and upon approval and publication of the affiliated annexation."** [150419 Sec8]. Item 7 has not had third reading. You are assigning city zoning to land that is still county land.

Two: split this ordinance. Section 1 — quote — **"consists of multiple parcels under separate ownership, including parcels owned by the William S. Cox Revocable Trust and parcels owned in fee by the City of Cheyenne"** [150419 Sec1]. Section 5 then — quote — **"real property... under ownership of William S Cox Revocable Trust is hereby established as AG... and... under ownership of the City... is hereby established as P - Public"** [150419 Sec5]. One ordinance, two owners, two zones, two purposes. Same bundled-tract defect as Item 7 that voids it ab initio.

Three: produce the FLUM consistency finding. Staff says future land use is — quote — **"Urban Transitional Residential"** [150419 Staff p1]. You assign AG and P. Tonight's Item 29d admits — quote — **"resolution that was postponed in May 2026 will return to the Public Services Committee on Monday, September 21, 2026... Amending the Future Land Use Map... west of Roundtop Road and adjacent to Happy Jack Road"** — you are zoning now, amending the plan that justifies zoning later. Backward.

Now why this is a holding-zone fraud, from your own staff.

Staff Report — quote — **"AG – Agricultural zone is the most analogous zone for this land. The AG zone is also a holding zone which will allow the land to be rezoned more easily in the future as the land develops."** [150419 Staff p2-3]. That is not zoning. That is regulatory arbitrage. You assign AG tonight as placeholder so Item 9 can rezone 1,197.76 acres to BP — quote — **"To zone land BP for development"** [150421 p1] — next vote.

Staff also admits city parcels — quote — **"These parcels are operated by the BOPU and are owned by the City of Cheyenne. The current use of the land is a water treatment facility. Based on this information Staff believes that the P - Public zone is the most analogous zone"** [150419 Staff p2-3]. Facility already exists. Already operated by BOPU. Already public. Why does an existing city water treatment facility need annexation and P zoning? It proves Item 7 annexation is not needed to extend services. It destroys your W.S. 15-1-402(a)(iii) logical and feasible finding.

Notice — Memo April 8 — quote — **"As of the creation of this memo (April 8, 2026), Staff has received no inquires or comments from the public... project page... 92 views and no comments."** [150419 Memo]. Same pattern as Item 7 92/0, Item 9 84/0, Hitching Post 193/0 and 15/0. Not notice under 15-1-405.

Planning Commission heard April 6, 2026 [150419 Sec3] and recommended approval — before April 27 postponement when you discovered ViaWest Group and Skybox Datacenters secured energy capacity before public hearing. PC never reviewed the real reason. Recommendation stale.

If Item 7 is void ab initio for bundled tracts — 1,252.09 acres private under 15-1-403 plus 10.23 acres city non-contiguous under 15-1-407 in one ordinance, 5.26% contiguity, future occupants vs persons residing — then Item 8 is void ab initio as dependent. You cannot zone what you do not lawfully annex.

Once AG granted, Item 9 BP locks public out forever. Your own prior admissions: BP **"is intended for data center uses and allows development with flexibility and without further zone changes"** — Director White: **"No public process. No mailed notice."** Mayor Collins same meeting: **"Absence of future public process factually incorrect."** V-01.

I demand a no vote, or continuance until Item 7 split into separate ordinances, FLUM amended first, and holding-zone purpose disclosed with independent studies. The administrative record is now closed. The trap is sprung. Govern yourselves accordingly. See you in district court.
