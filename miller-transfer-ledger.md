## §1 — PRONG ONE: The 2% BOPU Assessment — **VERIFIED QUOTE, CONTRADICTED DATE**

**The dossier's attribution:** "Goal Setting Review, July 29, 2026."

**The record's location:** Collins's **first budget work session as mayor — FY2022 cycle, 2021** ("this is my first opportunity to do this as mayor"), in a transcript framed by pandemic-hangover sales-tax forecasts, FY2022 general-fund revenues of $53,795,619, a SAFER-grant fire hire, and the pending Bird scooter ordinance. Not 2026. Same defect class as the previously logged Irwin-quote mis-date.

**The quote itself — verbatim in the compilation [R]:**
> "…we're proposing to doing the similar thing uh with the board of public utilities which would be i call it a franchise style i don't think it's really a franchise agreement but basically a payment from the board of public utilities equal to two percent of the revenues so **this will not cost the board of utilities anything it's not coming out of the revenues it'll just be a pass-through from the ratepayers directly to the governing body** and that would equal 640 000 so that's 1.1 million dollars and that kind of meets our goal that we set of a million dollars…"

**What this verifies, precisely:**
1. The mechanism is real and admitted: a 2% assessment on BOPU gross revenues, $640K/yr (2021 dollars), explicitly described by the mayor as a **ratepayer pass-through to the general fund**, adopted to hit a self-set $1M goal-setting revenue target.
2. The mayor's own sentence — *"this will not cost the board of utilities anything… a pass-through from the ratepayers directly to the governing body"* — is the kill-quote, and it needs no dossier framing. Properly dated (2021), it is *stronger*: an admission at origin, not a 2026 slip.
3. Municipal-code section **13.04.141 appears twice in the compilation** [R] — the codified home of the assessment. **"Ordinance No. 28"** as the enacting number: **UNVERIFIED** (number not located; do not cite until pulled).
4. Same session, unprompted, the mayor ties the whole revenue philosophy to data centers [R]: *"gas and electric franchises that's why data centers are so important to our future."*

**Unverified specifics in this prong:** the 5% rate-hike figure (rate *increases* are press-verified [P-11/P-14]; 5% is not); the $100M total lead-crisis figure (defensible as an estimate — ~25–28K lines at industry replacement costs — but it is the movement's number, not the city's; the *documented* figures are $20M SRF borrowing [S] and ~25–28K unknown/lead-noticed locations [S]); whether the assessment's 2026 yield still equals $640K (a code percentage scales with revenue — the 2026 number is a records request, not an assumption).
