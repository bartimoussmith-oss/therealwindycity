## 4. The live endgame (post-July 13) — from the research tail (compiled Aug 13, 2026)

**Instruments:** the Highlands annexation = **Ordinance No. 4687**; the BP rezoning = **separate ordinance** (PUDC-26-85 is the case file, not the ordinance number — number still unknown).

**Three different clocks:**
1. **Annexation appeal — W.S. 15-1-409(c): 60 days from the ordinance's EFFECTIVE date**, "forever barred" after. Effective date = **publication** (W.S. 15-1-408) unless the ordinance specifies otherwise, with a floor of **≥20 business days after the §15-1-405(a) hearing**. If first publication was ~July 18, the bar runs to **~mid-September 2026** — still open as of Aug 23. **Standing under 15-1-409(a) is limited to:** a landowner in the annexed territory, an owner of real property in the annexing city, or a utility — which bites directly on the "Laramie County" standing riddle.
2. **Referendum — W.S. 22-23-1005: petition of 10% of qualified electors filed within 20 days of first publication** after adoption. **A referendum petition on Ordinance 4687 has been filed** (≈3 weeks of signature collection, submitted early August; reports say it is "close to forcing a public vote").
3. **The 30-day APA theory (W.S. 16-3-114 / W.R.A.P. 12.04)** — applies only if the action is quasi-judicial "agency action"; the conservative last day measured from the 12:30 a.m. July 14 vote was **~Aug 13, 2026 — already past**. The city attorney treats annexation/rezoning as legislative; if a court disagrees as to the BP map amendment, that count is now time-endangered.

**The city's counter-move:** at the **Aug 10, 2026 GB** (after a private executive session on the referendum petition), the council **voted to ask a court whether §15-1-409 is the exclusive remedy, crowding out referendum** — i.e., the city is trying to convert the referendum fight into "you should have sued within 60 days" (before the 60 days has even run). Same meeting: Roundtop/Happy Jack annexation 3rd reading on the agenda — the Cox Ranch war continuing.

**Also in the record:** W.S. 15-1-404's **landowner veto** — if over half of landowners in the territory object within 20 business days after the hearing, annexation is barred for two years (surfaced in the April fights).

**Still missing (the user's own checklist):** (1) Ordinance 4687 as adopted + any stated effective date; (2) the BP rezoning ordinance number; (3) **first-publication proof** — newspaper clipping + publisher's affidavit (Wyoming Tribune Eagle legal-notice archive; City Clerk, 2101 O'Neil Ave); (4) approved minutes showing when the July 13/14 votes were memorialized. *Do not assume the midnight vote started any clock — publication and the ordinance text do.
