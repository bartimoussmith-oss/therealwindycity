*(submit under the UDC/urban-farm project — the one with a stated delivery promise to test)*

> I am submitting this comment for the official record on the agriculture UDC package scheduled for introduction at the second September meeting, Public Services Committee Nov. 2, and third reading Nov. 9. I have read the full draft text posted on this platform. **I support this package** — Gardens and Homemade Foods permitted in every district, the home-occupation exemption, the electric-fence rules, and the pre-annexation grazing protection (§6.08.020.F.7) in particular. Support is exactly why I am asking for four fixes before introduction:
>
> 1. **The 45-day threshold.** At the Aug. 21 work session, staff said the number was "just what we found through our different searches of different codes" and "seemed to be a standard number." The draft's own markup shows the 45-day rule already existed in the UDC — applied only to swine — and the new definition extends it to all livestock. Please disclose this in the staff report and publish any comparative analysis, or restore the swine-specific scope.
> 2. **The site-plan exemption (§2.2.3.b.5(c))** adopts verbatim the standard described on the record Aug. 21 as "subjective" and "solely at your discretion," which Councilwoman Aldrich said she found "really problematic." Please add written, measurable criteria and an appeal path before introduction.
> 3. **The gravel-surfacing exception** gates on the Director's view of "the character of the surrounding area" — a second subjective standard. Please substitute objective criteria or a published administrative-adjustment standard.
> 4. **Process:** (a) the Board of Public Utilities' written position on the slaughter/processing distinction (Councilwoman Emmons' question) before introduction; (b) confirmation that all comments received before introduction are published and included in the packet the council votes from, as stated at the Aug. 21 session; (c) note that Gardens and Homemade Foods is defined by reference to W.S. 11-49-102 without printing it — please state in the staff report how future amendments to that statute will be tracked.
>
> — [NAME], Cheyenne. All quotes verbatim from the city's own video, work-session record, and posted draft text; section citations included.
