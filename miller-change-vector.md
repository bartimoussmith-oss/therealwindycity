## §0 — Method: What "Most Change" Means Here

Change is ranked by leverage, not by satisfaction: **(1) forced accounting > (2) ballot > (3) courts > (4) press > (5) speeches.** A speech he has already given ~30 times. A court can only reach what a claim reaches. A ballot reaches every ordinance. But *forced accounting* — making the council publicly reconcile discretionary spending against a federal mandate with its own documents — changes the budget conversation permanently, feeds every other track, and costs one records request. This volume is built around that ranking, and it opens with the integration the record missed entirely.

## §1 — THE RESEARCHED CORE: THE LEAD PINCER
### (The single highest-change integration available, verified this session)

**The four corners, all now documented, never before connected:**

1. **The mandate (federal).** EPA's Lead and Copper Rule Improvements, announced Oct. 8, 2024: every U.S. system must identify and **replace lead pipes by 2037** [S]. BOPU's inventory deadline was Oct. 16, 2024 [S].
2. **The numbers (local).** BOPU's federally required notices went to **~28,000 service-line locations in Nov. 2024** — "the majority of letters... stated that the status of service line material is **unknown**"; "a few... confirmed as lead" are being mitigated [S — WTE/KGAB, Nov. 15, 2024]. Annual notices continued Oct. 2025 to **~25,000 customers** (known lead / galvanized-requiring-replacement / unknown) [S — Cap City, Oct. 31, 2025]. A **public GIS map of every line's classification exists** (cheyennebopu.org/leadfree) [S]. Sampling is currently below the EPA action level [S] — this volume builds on the *unknown* and the *mandate*, never on a contamination claim the record can't carry.
3. **The money (state).** A public notice nobody in this record ever cited: **BOPU plans to borrow $20,000,000 from the Wyoming Drinking Water State Revolving Loan Fund** for the Lead Service Line Investigation & Replacement Project — potholing investigations and replacements — with an environmental Categorical Exclusion determination on file at DEQ's Cheyenne office (SRF contacts named: Crocker, Eisenhauer) [S — WYDEQ public notice].
4. **The discretionary side (council).** While that mandate loan was moving, the council: appropriated **$5M from reserves for the West Edge/Reed Avenue corridor** over Miller's "Fix the lead first" objection [P-7]; passed **utility rate hikes** over his subsidy warning [P-11]; and held **~$234M budget** with reserves he challenged as idle [P-14].

**The pincer, stated as the accounting the council has never given:**
> *The lead mandate was never funded by the city — it was **borrowed, $20M, in the ratepayers' name**, to be repaid through the very rate hikes passed in May–June 2026, while the same governing body wrote a $5M reserves check for a discretionary railroad corridor. The council did not lack money for lead. It lacked the preference.*

**The instruments this opens (five, in leverage order):**

- **L-1. The Left-on-the-Table Request.** The Bipartisan Infrastructure Law routes dedicated lead-service-line money through the DWSRF with substantial **principal-forgiveness/subsidization** requirements for it [C — flag exact % for verification]. One WPRA request — *all correspondence, applications, and subsidy determinations for BOPU's LSLR SRF borrowing, including any principal-forgiveness application* — prices whether the city took free federal money or borrowed repayable principal while raising rates. If the answer is bad, this is not an argument; it is a scandal with a document number. **Cheapest, fastest, highest-change item in this entire volume.**
- **L-2. The Categorical Exclusion pull.** The SERP file is "available for public review upon request" at DEQ Cheyenne [S]. Request it, read it, and if the exclusion is fragile (batched phases, no cumulative analysis), comment on the *next* SRF round. Miller has never entered a state permitting room; this door is open with his name nowhere on it.
- **L-3. The EPA Referral — no state filter.** **Wyoming is the only state in the nation without SDWA primacy: EPA Region 8 (Denver) directly implements and enforces the drinking-water program** [S — EPA Region 8; Wyo. DEQ; Wyo. LSO 2023 primacy handout]. There is no captured state agency between a citizen complaint and the federal enforcer. A Region 8 complaint on inventory-verification progress, or the SDWA citizen-suit channel (42 U.S.C. § 300j-8; 60-day notice; fee-shifting for prevailing parties [C]) on any noncompliance, converts "fix the lead first" from a slogan into a docket in Denver.
- **L-4. The Ratepayer Reconciliation.** Collins's enterprise-fund rebuttal [P-11] only holds if **transfers to the general fund are zero**. WPRA the BOPU→general-fund transfers, PILOT-equivalents, and indirect-cost allocations for five years, plus the fund-balance policy, from the ACFR. If BOPU has been exporting surplus while borrowing for the mandate, the mayor's own sentence becomes the exhibit against him.
- **L-5. The Map Overlay.** The public LSLI GIS map [S] × council wards × data-center load zones = the 2027 campaign atlas, free, already built by federal law. Every ward's unknown-line count is a door-knock number. **The federal government mailed the precinct data to the campaign.**

**What the pincer refuses (discipline):** no contamination panic — sampling is below the action level [S]; the honest frame is *unknown ≠ accounted-for*, the 2037 clock is real, the borrowing is real, and the discretionary contrast is real. A movement that overstates lead loses the corridor argument it is winning.

## §2 — THE BALLOT AXIS (change instruments that are not lawsuits)

1. **Rates are ordinances.** The May–June 2026 rate increases passed as ordinances [P-11, P-14] — and ordinances are what W.S. 22-23-1005 and **Wyo. Const. art. 13 § 1(b)** make referendable. A **rate-referendum** — conditioned publicly on a published lead-replacement schedule and the subsidy accounting — forces the discretionary-vs-lead reconciliation onto a citywide ballot. *Nuance to respect:* the 20-day post-publication window means the campaign must exist **before** the vote, not after; the May–June window closed while Miller was arguing, not organizing (see MO-7); and the city will argue rate ordinances are "appropriations"-adjacent — the municipal statute's silence on that exclusion, against art. 3 § 52(g)'s express state-level exclusions, is the textual reply (Vol. IX §V-8).
2. **Nov. 3, 2026.** The annexation referendum is the movement's ballot beachhead — 3,275 signatures proved the machine exists [P]. Not his to run (he cannot sign it [R]); his to supply.
3. **2027 municipal elections.** The record already registers Miller as an electoral variable [R — Vol. V P8]. The map overlay (L-5) is the targeting file; the ward-level unknown-line count is the literature.

## §3 — THE IMMUNITY PINCER (the theoretical unification)

**The doctrine the city cannot escape on both horns, formalized:**

- **Horn A — "It's legislative."** Then *Bogan* protects the votes [C], but the **referendum applies** (Title 22; art. 13 § 1(b)) — the people are the appellate court, and 3,275 of them already filed.
- **Horn B — "It's quasi-judicial / appeal-only."** Then W.S. 15-1-409's district-court channel governs (the city's own DJ theory), but *quasi* character imports **due-process findings duties** (*Wilson Advisory* 2012 WY 163; *Foster's* 718 P.2d 868 [C]) and shrinks the legislative-immunity shelter for adjudicative conduct — the V-1/V-2 counts strengthen precisely as the referendum weakens.

**The city's Aug. 10 declaratory-judgment action forces the election of a horn in a public pleading.** Whichever it pleads, it surrenders the other axis — and the movement's briefs should quote the city's own choice of words back at it. This is the "exponential evolution" of the register's three separate grades (V-1 B+, V-5 B, V-8) into one structural argument: **you may be immune or you may be unaccountable; you cannot be both.**

**The Three-Track Doctrine, restated as command:** ballots for *laws*; fees for *treatment*; findings for *process*. Never litigate what a ballot can repeal. Never ballot what a writ can reverse. Never speechify what a subpoena can discover.
