The question of the week

One page. One fight. Read it and you're ahead of half the room.

Does the city clerk certify the referendum — and does a judge let it stand?

Organizers turned in roughly 3,200 signatures; 2,739 valid Cheyenne-registered 2024 voters are required under the Title 22 referendum statute. The city attorney is simultaneously asking a district court whether annexations must be fought under W.S. 15-1-409 instead. Until both questions resolve, Ordinance No. 4687 — the largest annexation in city history — sits in suspension's doorway.

WATCH FOR: Clerk's final count; declaratory-judgment filing; any motion invoking W.S. 22-23-1005/1007.
