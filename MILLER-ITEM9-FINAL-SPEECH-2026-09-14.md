### SPEECH — READ VERBATIM — 425 WORDS — 3 MINUTES — RECORD-READY

Mayor Collins, members of the city council, Charles Miller, Laramie County, for the permanent administrative record — Item 9, second reading, AG Agricultural to BP Business Park, west of Roundtop Road and adjacent to Happy Jack Road, 1,197.76 acres, PUDC-26-38 Cox Lands, postponed from April 27.

Three asks in first 45 seconds, numbered.

One: continue this item. Your own staff admits current plan does NOT support BP. Quote — **"The current future land use designation does not support the BP zone."** [150421 Staff p3]. And — **"According to the comprehensive plan the optimal City zones... would be AR – Agricultural Residential or AG – Agriculture."** [150421 Staff p3]. You are rezoning to BP in violation of PlanCheyenne.

Two: produce the FLUM amendment approval. Staff says — quote — **"The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1 if the future land use map designation is approved."** [150421 Staff p3]. If. Tonight's Item 29d says FLUM amendment for this same land returns September 21 PSC and September 28 Council. You are finding compliance based on a future plan that does not exist. That's conditional zoning, illegal under Wyoming law.

Three: produce water, power, and Industrial Siting memos. You are rezoning 1,197.76 acres to data-center district with no aquifer study, no WAPA/Black Hills capacity letter, no Industrial Siting Act analysis. Continue.

Now why this is the data-center lockout, from your own documents.

Your staff report Purpose — quote — **"To zone land BP for development."** [150421 Staff p1]. Request — quote — **"A request to rezone approximately 1,197.76 acres of land from AG - Agricultural to BP Business Park."** [150421 Staff p1].

Your staff Background — quote — **"This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from AG Agricultural to BP Business Park for roughly 1,197.76 acres of land. The land is currently vacant. The land subject to the zone change request is currently outside of City limits."** [150421 Staff p2]. You are rezoning land outside city limits to BP. Contingent zoning.

Your staff admits AG is holding — quote — **"The BP zone provides more flexibility... The current zone of AG is considered a holding zone for the property and should it be developed with industrial or commercial use, it would need to be rezoned."** [150421 Staff p3] and — **"The AG zone was meant to be a holding zone for this property until it was ready for development."** [150421 Staff p4]. Item 8 AG was never real zoning. It was placeholder to get to this vote.

Your staff defines BP — quote — **"The Business Park (BP) district is intended to support small and large-scale industrial development... The BP district is applicable to areas... that are intended to support large-scale industrial, manufacturing, shipping, research and development, and data center uses."** [150421 Staff p4-5]. BP is data-center district by definition. April 20 you gavelled residents for saying data center while your own code says BP is for data centers. Chair Emmons: **"This issue is not about data centers."** Your code: **"data center uses."** Which is it?

Your staff compatibility — quote — **"The proposed BP zone can be seen as an extension of the business park that already exists... Surrounding this parcel is either vacant land or ranches. The Cheyenne Water treatment plant is adjacent"** [150421 Staff p3]. Vacant ranches and water plant — you call that compatible with data center?

Once BP granted, your own prior admission: BP **"is intended for data center uses and allows development with flexibility and without further zone changes"** — Director White: **"No public process. No mailed notice."** Mayor Collins same meeting: **"Absence of future public process factually incorrect."** V-01. Tonight is last public hearing ever for this land.

Notice — Memo April 8 — quote — **"project page... 84 views and no comments"** [150421 Memo] — Staff Report March 30 — quote — **"project page... 51 views and no comments"** [150421 Staff p5]. 84, 51, zero comments. Same pattern as 92/0, 193/0, 15/0. Not notice.

Planning Commission heard April 6 [150421 Sec2] and recommended approval — before April 27 postponement when ViaWest and Skybox secret power deals discovered. PC never reviewed energy pre-allocation. Stale.

Agent AVI P.C. again — same firm that drew 5.26% contiguity failure, same firm you gave $196,334.40 Contract #8415 on consent August 10 and $252,522 to study its own client's loophole to bypass Industrial Siting Act.

I demand a no vote. Or continuance until FLUM amended first, then BP, with water, power, and siting studies. If you grant BP tonight on land outside city, under plan that doesn't support it, based on future plan that doesn't exist, you have created void ab initio zoning that locks public out forever.

The administrative record is now closed. The trap is sprung. Govern yourselves accordingly. See you in district court.
