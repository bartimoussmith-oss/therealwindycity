Subject: A RESOLUTION EXPRESSING THE SUPPORT OF THE GOVERNING

LLC 
 
Date: April 21, 2025 
 
Background:  
The item for consideration is a Warranty Deed conveying property located along 
Parsley Boulevard south of Van Tassell Court to the City. The property is .03 acres +/-.  
This small parcel was requested as part of a Site and Engineering Plan review for 
PUDC-23-157, Parsley Clear Creek Warehouse, 445 Parsley Avenue, and is being 
donated in lieu of building sidewalk along the development.  In 2019, the Cheyenne 
MPO conducted a long-range planning study for future construction of Parsley Blvd 
and the small parcel will be incorporated when this project goes to the design phase. 
 
The Governing Body review schedule is as follows: 
• Introduction and Referral 04/28/2025 
• Finance Committee 05/06/2025 
• Final Reading 05/12/2025

WHEREAS, Phillip Regeski, as managing partner of Sloan Lake , LLC, is engaged in the 
business of developing and redeveloping real estate; 
 
WHEREAS, during a Site Plan and Engineering Plan review process for PUDC -23-157, 
Parsley Clear Creek Warehouse,  located at 445 Parsley Avenue, Mr. Regeski offered to donate 
certain real property  held by Sloan Lake, LLC, in lieu of building sidewalk s along the 
development; 
 
WHEREAS, Mr. Regeski on behalf of Sloan Lake, LLC, has since agreed to donate that 
certain real property to the City as described in a Warranty Deed , which deed is attached and 
incorporated herein as Exhibit A; and 
 
WHEREAS, in 2019, the Cheyenne Metropolitan Planning Organization conducted a 
long-range planning study for the future reconstruction of Parsley Boulevard and the parcel of land 
Sloan Lake, LLC, seeks to donate can logically be incorporated when that reconstruction project 
goes to the design phase. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING , that the City of Cheyenne accept this donation of real property  
from Sloan Lake, LLC; and 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Mayor of the City of Cheyenne is hereby 
designated as the authorized representative of the City to act on behalf of the Governing Body on 
all matters relating to the donation of this real property and is authorized to execute any necessary 
agreements and associated documents.
