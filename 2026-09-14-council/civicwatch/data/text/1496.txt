MEMO 
To: Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council 
From: Charles Bloom, AICP, Jeanie Anderson-Shrednik 
Subject: Grant of perpetual right-of-way and utility easement and temporary easement to Microsoft 
Corporation for underground fiber lines. 
Date: February 9, 2026 
______________________________________________________________________________________________________________ 
 
Background:  
 
The item for consideration is a Grant of Easement. The proposed Governing Body review 
schedule is as follows: 
• Introduction and referral  February 9, 2026 
• Finance Committee   February 18, 2026 
• Final consideration  February 23, 2026 
 
The proposed easement is generally located along the former CB & Q rail corridor southeast 
of the JL Ranch subdivision.    
 
 
 
The easement would grant Microsoft Corporation a permanent right-of-way on City of 
Cheyenne property southeast of the JL Ranch subdivision to install, operate, and maintain 
underground fiber optic lines within a 10-foot-wide corridor of approximately 1,000 square 
feet, along with a temporary construction easement extending 10 feet on each side for 
installation. The easement would also allow Microsoft to access the site for construction, 
maintenance, upgrades, and removal of its facilities and, with City approval, to share th e 
conduit with other telecommunications providers. Microsoft would be responsible for all 
related costs, burying the lines at least 48 inches deep, repairing any damage, restoring 
disturbed areas, and complying with applicable laws. The City would retain the right to use 
the area so long as it does not interfere with the facilities and may require removal of the 
lines if they are unused for two consecutive years.

 AS A FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF THIS AGREEMENT , the parties hereto agree 
as follows: 
 
1. Grantee has selected the route for said Facilities over and through said premises, 
and shall be responsible for all construction, reconstruction, operation, installation, use, repair, 
replacement, upkeep, monitoring, and maintenance of the Facilities; 
 
2. That Grantee shall bury all Facilities  a minimum of 48 inches below existing 
ground to allow for construction of future Greenway trail; 
 
3. That Grantee shall replace or rebuild any and all unusual or excessive damage to 
the easement property , and/or pay for any damage caused  which shall be occasioned by the 
construction, operation and maintenance of said Facilities under and through the above-described 
premises; 
 
4. That Grantee’s activities are subject to, and Grantee shall comply with, all 
applicable federal, state, and local laws, ordinances, and permitting requirements, including but 
not limited to environmental, safety, and land use regulations; 
 
5. That Grantee shall, within thirty (30) days of completion of any installation, 
maintenance, or removal activities, restore the surface of the Permanent Easement and any 
affected improvements to a condition equal to or better than that which existed prior to 
commencement of such activities, subject to City inspection and approval. Grantee shall 
promptly correct any deficiencies identified by the City; 
 
6. Grantee shall, to the fullest extent permitted by law, defend, indemnify, and hold 
harmless the City, its officers, employees, agents, and assigns from and against any and all 
claims, liabilities, damages, losses, costs, and expenses (including attorneys’ fees) arising out of 
or related to the exercise of rights under this easement or the presence, installation, construction, 
operation, maintenance, repair, or removal of Grantee’s Facilities, except to the extent caused by 
the gross negligence or willful misconduct of the City. 
 
7. If any stakeholder requires relocation of the Facilities  (including, but not limited 
to Grantor, Grantee , Utilities Franchisor , or Right-of-Way and Greenway  Administrator), 
Grantee agrees to relocate the Facilities at its own expense , provided that the Grantor supplies a 
suitable alternate location for such Facilities and grants the necessary easement rights at the new 
location upon the same terms and conditions herein provided. 
 
8. It is further understood and agreed that this agreement shall run with the land and 
shall inure to the benefit of, and be binding upon the legal representatives, heirs, devisees, 
successors and assigns of the respective parties, and by the execution and acknowledgment 
thereof, Grantors jointly and severally waive their homestead rights to the above -described lands 
so far as the same may be affected by this agreement.  
 
9. The City shall retain the full right to use and enjoy the easement area for any 
purpose not unreasonably interfering with the operation or maintenance of the Grantee’s 
Facilities. No new buildings may be constructed within the easement without Grantee’s prior 
written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed. In the event 
the Grantee and the City disagree as to whether a proposed use will cause interference, the 
parties shall engage in good faith efforts to resolve such dispute, and if unresolved , may pursue 
appropriate legal action to enforce or otherwise interpret the terms of this agreement. 
 
10. Abandonment. In the event the Facilities are abandoned, discontinued, or not used 
for a continuous period of twenty -four (24) months, the City may, upon written notice to 
Grantee, require Grantee to remove the Facilities and restore the easement area at Grantee’s sole 
cost. If Grantee fails to do so within a reasonable time specified by the City, the City may 
perform such work and recover its costs from Grantee. Upon abandonment and removal, this 
Easement shall automatically terminate as to the abandoned Facilities. 
 
11. Governmental Immunity.  The City does not waive, and expressly reserves, all 
rights, claims, immunities, and defenses available to it under the Wyoming Governmental 
Claims Act, W.S. § 1 -39-101 et seq., and any other applicable law. Nothing in this Agreement 
shall be construed as a waiver of the City’s governmental immunity or sovereign immunity, 
except to the limited extent necessary to allow the parties to enforce the express terms of this 
Agreement as provided by law. 
 
12. Grantor hereby represents, warrants and covenants that Grantor has sufficient 
legal authority and interest  in the above-described land, subject to existing liens and easement s 
of record and has all rights to grant this easement.
