The above referenced contract is amended or modified as set forth in the following and these 
amendments and modifications of the plans and specifications are agreed to by the parties. All 
other aspects of the original agreement remain unchanged and in full force and effect. 
 
I. Description of Changes 
a. Additional Pedestrian Pathway/Greenway upgrades abutting city-owned property along 
Windmill Road and connecting to Dell Range Boulevard. These upgrades will convert the 
existing pathway from deteriorating asphalt to concrete, improve drainage, and provide for 
safer pedestrian travel.  ($236,622.00). See Exhibit A, attached hereto and incorporated 
herein by reference. 
 
b. Additional concrete repairs throughout the Dell Range corridor due to unforeseen subgrade 
conditions, which have created a shortage of funds in the contract amount to complete all 
the scheduled work.  ($60,000.00). 
 
II. New Contract Items 
a. The f irst new contract item  regarding the additional $236,622.00 contract amount is 
detailed as provided by Exhibit A, attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference.  
 
b. The second new contract item  authorizes an increase in the contract value by $60,000.00 
to restore funds previously reallocated to address unanticipated conditions, including 
deteriorated concrete in deceleration lanes on Dell Range Boulevard and removal of buried 
concrete on Ridge Road. These additional costs impacted funding for remaining items such 
as thermoplastic crosswalks, curb and gutter, and asphalt patching. The added amount 
includes a contingency to cover any further miscellaneous needs as the project concludes. 
 
III. Schedule 
Due to the additional work included under this contract modification, the contract completion date 
is extended from May 31, 2025, to August 15, 2025.
