 
 
MEMORANDUM  
TO:  The Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and City of Cheyenne Council Members 
FROM:  Robin Lockman, City Treasurer 
DATE:  April 7, 2025 
SUBJECT: Reappropriation to be considered for approval 
 
 
On April 14, 2025, the Governing Body will refer a resolution to the Finance Committee seeking 
to reappropriate funds for the Fiscal Year (FY) 2025 City of Cheyenne budget, in accordance with 
W.S. §§ 16-4-112 and 16-4-113.  Detailed information regarding this reappropriation request  is 
provided below.  
 
COMPENSATION STUDY ADJUSTMENTS 
1. The Mayor is seeking approval to allocate funds to implement recommendations from a 
recent compensation stud y conducted by the Employer’s Council.  The study evaluated 
employee pay levels, benchmarking them against industry standards and market trends to 
ensure the City’s compensation practices are competitive, equitable, and  aligned with 
current labor market conditions. Implementing these recommendations for the final three 
months of FY 2025 will cost $106,823 from the General Fund and $ 20,941 from non-
General Funds. 
 
GENERAL FUND – ADDITIONAL REVENUE AND RESERVES 
1. The Police Department is requesting $3,57 7 from General Fund reserves to cover the FY 
2025 portion of their subscription service for ten Axon Fleet -3 in -car cameras. This 
includes associated peripheral hardware, software, licensing, and warranties.   
 
2. Planning and Development is requesting $103,200 from General Fund reserves to fund 
professional services contracts with Jones Land Surveying (#7916) and Steil Land 
Surveying (#7971) for county pocket annexations. These funds were originally 
appropriated in FY 2023 and rolled over into FY 2024 b ut were inadvertently omitted 
during the FY 2025 rollover process. 
 
 
 

OTHER FUNDS – ADDITIONAL REVENUE AND RESERVES  
The City of Cheyenne has received new grants and donations or is reallocating unspent funds from 
existing grants, which are included in this reappropriation. These funds will support various city 
projects and initiatives, ensuring efficient use of resources to address community needs. 
1. Local and State Grant Fund – new grants received: 
• $3,000 – Visit Cheyenne Marketing Grant for Community Recreation and Events (partial 
reappropriation). 
• $150,000 – Cheyenne Community Recreation District Grant for the construction of a new 
restroom and concessions building at the Dunbar Field. 
• $280,000 – Cheyenne Community Recreation District Grant for the construction of a new 
restroom and concessions building at the Dutcher Complex. 
 
2. Law Enforcement Grant Fund – new grants received: 
• $15,000 – Byrne Justice Assistance Grant for Community Policing  (partial 
reappropriation). 
• $66,826 – Wyoming Office of Homeland Security Grant for Bomb Team Equipment. 
 
3. Federal Grants Fund – new grants received: 
• $53 – Wyoming Department of Health Community Emergency Medical Services (CEMS) 
Grant for Cheyenne Fire Rescue (partial reappropriation). 
• $421,212 – American Rescue Plan Federal Recovery Funds  Grant through the State of 
Wyoming, for the Van Buren Storm Sewer Interceptor Project. 
• $486,736 – Wyoming Outdoor Recreation Grant , through Wyoming State Parks and 
Cultural Resources, for Belvoir Ranch Trailhead Phase 1.  
Other non-General Fund items being requested: 
1. Development Impact Fees  – An amount of $ 63,075 is being requested to fund the 
professional conceptual design services contract with Plan One Architects (#8074), for the 
development of new fire stations #2 and #6.  This will be paid for with public safety 
infrastructure fund reserves. 
 
2. Recreation Programs Fund – The following are new revenue requests pertaining to the 
Recreation Programs Fund: 
a. An increase of $16,500 to the concessions budget is being requested to purchase 
concession items for resale at the BEAST facility. The funding for this expense will 
come from the BEAST concessions revenue account.  This aligns with the facility's 
operational model, where concession sales contribute to its self-sustaining financial 
structure. 
b. An increase of $3,336 to the recreation supplies budget is being requested to 
purchase new youth volleyball jerseys. This expense will be covered by revenue 
generated from volleyball fees. 
c. An increase of $24,935 is being requested for the Botanic Gardens  to fund the 
Education, Agriculture, and Trial (EAT) Garden Design  Project. The Botanic 
Gardens will be converting the existing South Community Garden into an EAT 
garden. This increase will be paid for through the Botanic Gardens' miscellaneous 
donations account.  
d. An increase of $10,345 is being requested from the Botanic Gardens’ reserves to 
cover the City's matching requirement for the EAT Gardening Design Project. 
 
3. Belvoir Recreation Fund – A funding request of $394,240 from the Belvoir Recreation 
Fund reserves is being made to support the professional services contract with TPT Trails, 
LLC (#7975) for the Belvoir Ranch Phase 2 Design-Build Trail Project. This project aims 
to further develop the trail system on the Belvoir Ranch, enhancing recreational 
opportunities and continuing the City's vision for the property as outlined in its master plan. 
 
4. Real Property Revolving Fund – A funding request of $42,136 from reserves in the Real 
Property Revolving Fund is being made to cover infrastructure development expenses for 
the new gymnastics’ facility. These expenses include wireless access points, wireless 
networking, emergency radio signal testing, fire alarm testing, and the installation of a new 
lighted sign for the building. 
 
5. One Percent Sales Tax Funds – The Police Department is requesting $850,000 from their 
One Percent Sales Tax Fund reserves for the purchase of new police vehicles. 
 
6. Transportation Planning Fund – The MPO is requesting $122,347 for the professional 
services contract with Kimley Horn & Associates (#8079) to update the Cheyenne Connect 
2050 long-range plan.  This increase will be paid from the following revenue sources: 
a. $110,712 – US Department of Transportation Grant (90.49%) 
b. $5,818 – County match (4.755%) 
c. $5,818 – City match (4.755%) 
As always, if you have any questions, please give me a call at 637 -6336 or email at 
rlockman@cheyennecity.org.
