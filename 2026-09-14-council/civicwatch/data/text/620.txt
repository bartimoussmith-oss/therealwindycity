Please find attached a Drainage Easement Agreement between Tractor Supply Company and the 
City of Cheyenne for a recently constructed stormwater detention pond associated with the 
development of Lot 1, Block 1 of the WYODIGS Properties Subdivision, now addressed as 8418 
Hutchins Drive. 
 
The property owner will be responsible for the maintenance of the detention pond so that it 
functions as designed and constructed. The City is granted access to ensure maintenance is 
completed and for any emergency maintenance deemed necessary. 
 
There are no financial implications associated with this Drainage Easement Agreement. Your 
approval of this agreement is respectfully requested.

DRAINAGE EASEMENT AGREEMENT 
 
KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS that, Tractor Supply Company, a Delaware 
corporation (“Grantor”), whose address is 5401 Virginia Way, Brentwood, Tennessee 37027, in 
consideration of the sum of Ten Dollars ($10.00) and other good and valuable consideration, 
receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged by and between the parties, does hereby 
grant to the City of Cheyenne, a Wyoming municipal corporation (“City”), whose address is 
2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001, and to its successors and  assigns, a non -
exclusive drainage easement, legally described on Exhibit A, attached hereto and incorporated 
herein. 
 
RECITALS 
 
WHEREAS, the City desires to obtain a drainage easement for the purposes of providing, 
ensuring the maintenance of a storm water conveyance and/or drainage facility  (hereafter the 
“facility”) on and through the property more particularly described and depicted in Exhibit A 
(“Easement Area”); 
 
WHEREAS, Grantor acknowledges that they have the obligation to maintain the drainage 
facilities within the Easement Area in accordance with this agreement; 
 
WHEREAS, Grantor is willing to grant such easement to the City on and subject to the 
terms and conditions of this agreement; and 
 
WHEREAS, Grantor states it is the lawful owner of the real property described herein, that 
it has good and lawful right and authority to grant, sell, and convey said property, or any part 
thereof, and that it warrants the title of said property. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and the mutual agreement 
of the parties hereto, the parties hereby agree as follows: 
 
1. Drainage Easement. Grantor executes this easement in favor of City, its successors 
and assigns, the right and privilege to ensure on behalf of the public that the facility remains in 
Good Working Order as defined below. Such easement includes all rights and privileges necessary 
or convenient for the full enjoyment and use thereof for the above-mentioned purposes including, 
but not limited to, the right of ingress and egress over Grantor’s adjoining property in order to 
exercise the explicit right to remedy any drainage issues within the Easement Area. 
 
2. Modification. The Grantor shall be prohibited from altering or placing anything in 
the Easement Area which shall obstruct or impede the flow of storm water or surface drainage 
from the Grantor’s property. Grantor agrees not to build, or to convey to others permission to build, 
any structures or improvements on, over, across, in, through, or under the above -described 
Easement Area, or to develop or allow the development of the Easement Area in any manner which 
obstructs or interferes with the function or capacity of the drainage facilities. However, if approved 
in writing by the City, the Grantor may construct and/or install surface improvements to its 
property, including paved driveways, parking and/or walkways, landscaping, utilities and/or 
Page 2 of 3 
 
similar improvements, which improvements do not then or later interfere with the aforementioned 
purposes of the Easement Area. 
 
3. Maintenance. Grantor shall maintain the drainage facilities contained within the 
Easement Area in Good Working Order. “Good Working Order” is defined as the drainage system 
operating as designed and constructed in accordance with the as -built construction drawings on 
file with the City including, but not limited to, ensuring that the structures and Easement Area land 
surface are free of debris, impoundments, and/or alterations to the drainage flow. 
 
4. Shared Use. The Grantor reserves the right to make any use of the Easement Area 
which is not inconsistent with the rights herein conveyed to the City, and which does not interfere 
with the aforementioned purposes of the Easement Area. 
 
It is understood and agreed that the enforcement and conditions of this agreement, and all 
rights contained herein, shall be strictly reserved to the Grantor and the City. Nothing contained in 
this agreement shall give any claim or right whatsoever by any third person or entity. It is the 
express intention of the Grantor and the City that any such person or entity, other than the Grantor 
or the City, that receives a service or benefit under this agreement shall be deemed an incidental 
third-party beneficiary only. 
 
5. Governing Law Venue/Governmental Immunity . This Agreement shall be 
governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws of the State of Wyoming. Except for 
enforcement of the terms and conditions of this Agreement as between the parties, the City does 
not waive its  governmental immunity by entering into this Agreement and reserves all defenses 
and immunities under the Wyoming Governmental Claims Act, Wyo. Stat. § 1 -39-10 l, et seq., 
and other applicable constitutional and statutory provisions of the S tate of Wyoming. The venue 
for any legal proceedings related to this Agreement shall be the First Judicial District Court sitting 
in Laramie County,  Wyoming. Designations of venue, choice of law, enforcement actions, and 
similar provisions should not be constructed as a waiver of governmental immunity. The parties 
agree that any ambiguity in  this Agreement shall not be strictly construed, either against or for 
either party. 
 
5. Waiver. Waiver by either party of any breach of any term or provision of this 
Easement shall not be deemed a waiver of any subsequent breach of the same or other term or 
provision hereof. 
 
This grant of easement is binding upon the heirs, executors, administrators, successors, and 
assigns of the parties hereto.
