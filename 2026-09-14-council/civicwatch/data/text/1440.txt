RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION IN SUPPORT OF THE PROFESSIONAL RODEO 
COWBOY ASSOCIATION (PRCA) AND PROFESSIONAL RODEO HALL 
OF FAME AND MUSEUM OF THE AMERICAN COWBOY.” 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne, County of Laramie, and State of Wyoming’s legacy is 
built on the rich history of the American West; 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne, County of Laramie, and State of Wyoming embrace 
rodeo and cowboy culture as defining elements of their identities; 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne, County of Laramie, and State of Wyoming support the 
education of the public regarding the history, traditions, and impact of rodeo; 
 
WHEREAS, the State of Wyoming hosts hundreds of rodeos per year, with rodeo existing 
at every level —from ranch and youth rodeos to high school , collegiate, and elite professional 
competition; 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne recognizes rodeo’s historic and ongoing impact on 
Western American culture and its role in linking local communities to the national and 
international rodeo industry; 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne supports recognition of rodeo notables of the past and 
present; 
 
WHEREAS, rodeo serves as an inspiration for future generations, and communities 
throughout Wyoming host rodeos that serve as gathering places for residents and visitors alike, 
fostering shared heritage, civic pride, and intergenerational participation whil e strengthening the 
social and cultural fabric of towns and cities across the State; 
 
WHEREAS, the agricultural way of life is deeply engrained in the State of Wyoming; 
 
WHEREAS, tourism is the second largest industry in Wyoming , and  rodeos generate 
significant economic activity by attracting visitors who support local businesses, including hotels, 
restaurants, retailers, and service providers, thereby contributing to the vitality and sustainability 
of local economies;  
 
WHEREAS, Rodeo is the official sport of Wyoming and the City of Cheyenne is home to 
the largest outdoor rodeo in the world; and 
 
WHEREAS, the relocation of the Professional Rodeo Cowboy Association (PRCA) 
Headquarters and the Professional Rodeo Hall of Fame and Museum of the American Cowboy to 
Cheyenne would further solidify Cheyenne’s , and the State of Wyoming’s,  standing as the 
epicenter of professional rodeo and Western heritage, while generating substantial cultural and 
economic benefits for the community. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the City of Cheyenne stands in full support of the Professional 
Rodeo Cowboy Association ( PRCA) Headquarters and Pro fessional R odeo Hall of Fame and 
Museum of the American Cowboy relocating to Cheyenne, Wyoming; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Governing Body expresses its intent to pledge up 
to One Million Dollars ($1,000,000.00) in financial support for the relocation of the PRCA 
Headquarters and the Professional Rodeo Hall of Fame and Museum of the American Cowboy to 
Cheyenne, Wyoming; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that such pledge is expressly contingent upon the PRCA 
formally selecting and completing the relocation of its Headquarters and the Professional Rodeo 
Hall of Fame and Museum of the American Cowboy to Cheyenne, Wyoming, and shall not become 
effective unless and until such relocation occurs; and 
 
BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED, that any expenditure of pledged funds shall be subject to 
lawful appropriation by the Governing Body and compliance with all applicable municipal and 
state laws.
