MEMORANDUM 
TO: The Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Cheyenne City Council Members  
FROM:  Robin Lockman, City Treasurer 
DATE:  April 16, 2026 
SUBJECT: Reappropriation to be considered for approval 
 
On April 2 7, 2026, the Governing Body will consider referral of  a resolution to the Finance 
Committee to reappropriate funds within the Fiscal Year 2026 City of Cheyenne Budget, pursuant 
to W.S. §§ 16-4-112 and 16-4-113.  This memorandum summarizes the proposed adjustments. 
General Fund Reserves (Total: $118,336) 
▪ $18,336 - SeeClickFix ($17,886) and Granicus ($450)  software subscription expenses that 
were previously paid with ARPA grant funds, which are no longer available for these items.  
▪ $100,000 - Contract #8267 modification to pay for design services for the renovation of the 
former Laramie County Senior Activity Center and the Municipal Court’s second floor, as 
approved by the Governing Body on March 23, 2026. 
Local and State Grant Fund - Additional Revenue (Total:  $2,536,178) 
▪ $16,079 – BOPU joint project funding for the 18th Street Storm Sewer Interceptor Project.  
▪ $5,500 – Visit Cheyenne grant for out -of-state marketing efforts by the Botanic Gardens, 
Civic Center, and Ice and Events Center.  
▪ $2,366,424 – Wyoming State Parks and Cultural Resources funding for the High Plains 
Arboretum renovation and construction project.  
▪ $148,175 – Wyoming State Parks and Cultural Resources funding for the High Plains 
Arboretum demolition and abatement project. 
Recreation Programs Fund – Reserves (Total:  $125,000) 
▪ $75,000 – conceptual design for Childcare Program renovations and expansion.  
▪ $50,000 – additional part-time gymnastics coach salaries. 
Belvoir Ranch Recreation Fund – Reserves (Total:  $100,000) 
▪ Costs associated with Contract #7975 (Phase 2 design/build trail), Contract #8098 (trailhead 
and access roads), and necessary site items such as keys, locks, and signage.  
Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) Fund - Additional Revenue (Total:  $78,442) 
▪ Reverted FY2024 funds originally allocated to the Lincoln Park Project, now reassigned to 
the Optimist Park Project.

Real Property Revolving Fund – Reserves (Total:  $150) 
▪ Closing costs related to Resolution 6517 for the Holliday Park property sale. 
Law Enforcement Grant Fund - Additional Revenue (Total: $4,000) 
▪ U.S. Marshals Service funding for CPD task force overtime. 
Federal Grants Fund - Additional Revenue (Total:  $5,610) 
▪ Wyoming Office of Homeland Security pass -through funding for the 18th Street Storm 
Sewer Interceptor Project.  
Transit Fund - Additional Revenue (Total:  $1,414,924) 
▪ Federal Transit Administration (FTA) FY2026 funding to support operations, including 
interior and exterior renovations of the new Transit Facility on Westland Road. 
Juvenile Justice Fund - Additional Revenue (Total:  $78,175) 
▪ Laramie County contract funding for a new case manager and related operating expenses. 
Annexation Improvements Fund – Reserves (Total: $3,550) 
▪ Interest payment for an annexation deposit refund. 
Miscellaneous Agency Fund – Reserves (Total: $1,500) 
▪ Education expenses for the Community Risk Reduction (CRR) Program. 
Special Purpose Option Tax Fund – Reserves (Total:  $95,000) 
▪ Unanticipated building repairs and improvements at the Botanic Gardens. 
Civic Center Fund - Additional Revenue (Total: $17,542) 
▪ Artist payouts. 
Fiduciary Fund - Visit Cheyenne Reserves (Total: $850) 
▪ Replacement of a boot facilitated by Arts Cheyenne. 
Perpetual Care Fund – Reserves (Total: $17,000) 
▪ Interest transfers to the General Fund for Fiscal Year 2026. 
 
Requested Action 
Please review the above and consider adoption of the attached resolution authorizing these 
appropriations.
