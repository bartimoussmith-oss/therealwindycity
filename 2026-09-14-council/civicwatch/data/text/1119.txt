Subject: Downtown Development Authority Proposed Budget - Fiscal Year 2026

Background: 
A
ttached is the proposed budget for fiscal year 2026 (July 1 to June 30). The proposed Governing Body 
review schedule is as follows: 
• Introduction and Referral: May 12, 2025
• Finance Committee: May 20, 2025
• Final consideration: May 27, 2025
Th
e budget represents a $23,351.00 increase in mill levy assessment collections ($487,000.00) and a 
decrease in interest earned (-$400). The interest earned estimate is based on an estimated $1 million 
dollars being left in the Wyoming Government Investment Fund (WGIF) which as of May 5, 2025, has a 
seven-day yield of 4.21%. 
Th
e proposed budget is a decrease in the budget from the prior year. The proposed budget of $728,450 
is $349,599 less than fiscal year 2025, brings in $131,450 from reserves, accounts for a $30,000 grant 
from the Cheyenne-Laramie Co. Economic Development Joint Powers Board, and $5,000 in funding from 
Laramie County. The City contributes $251,025 covering all payroll, insurance, and benefits for 
employees. Additionally, other City staff provide support to DDA employees at no cost to the DDA. The 
City also provides physical office space and all things commonly associated with office space at no cost 
to the DDA board. 
H
ighlights of the budget are below.  
• 704 - Communications Plan: $10,000 total
o A Communications Plan has been added to the FY26 Budget due to stakeholder
feedback of poor historical communication. The amount may be amended in the future
depending on RFP responses.
• 708 - Event Management (Visit Cheyenne Sponsored Events) $30,000 total:
o July, Downtown Express Shuttle, $7,500 - The Downtown Express shuttle provides a
free, convenient, and stress-free way for attendees to travel between Downtown
Cheyenne and Frontier Park, reducing traffic congestion, impaired driving, and parking
challenges. By offering reliable transportation, we make it easier for visitors to fully
experience the best of Cheyenne, and provide them a convenient way to explore our
vibrant downtown businesses. Ridership has increased steadily over the last 3 years.

o July, Downtown Busker Program, $2,250 - Launched in 2024 during CFD, the Downtown 
Busker Program received tremendous positive feedback from business owners to 
visitors, and the musicians themselves. Designed to enhance the visitor experience, 
support local artists, and drive foot traffic to downtown businesses, this initiative helps 
to transform public spaces into engaging, lively destinations. 
o July, Cheyenne Day, $2,500 - Cheyenne Day provides a key opportunity to activate 
downtown with unique programming that encourages visitors to linger, explore, and 
experience what makes our city special. The variety of local events all contribute to an 
energetic, welcoming atmosphere that keeps people engaged throughout the day. By 
investing in Cheyenne Day, we create an environment where local businesses thrive, 
and residents take pride in their community. One of the most important aspects of 
Cheyenne Day is that it ensures everyone in our community can take part in the 
excitement of Frontier Days, regardless of their budget. While tickets to the rodeo, 
concerts, and carnival can be cost-prohibitive for some, Cheyenne Day offers a free and 
accessible way for locals and visitors to experience the spirit of the celebration and our 
incredible downtown. Attendance for this event has noticeably increased each year for 
the last 3 years. 
o December, Old West Holiday, $17,750 - Old West Holiday brings families, friends, and 
visitors together to enjoy a unique seasonal experience. With free and low-cost 
activities such as Santas Horses at the Stables, visits with Santa and Mrs. Claus, and 
adult-focused experiences like the Running of the Santas, this multi-weekend 
experience ensures that everyone can participate in the holiday fun. By strategically 
placing events and activations across multiple weekends and throughout Downtown 
Cheyenne, Old West Holiday drives foot traffic to local businesses, encouraging holiday 
shopping and dining. The Santa Stop Sweepstakes, and our new digital passport 
program, incentivizes participants to explore shops, restaurants, and attractions. 
• 739 - DDA Board Strategic Projects $100,000 total 
This line item replaces the previous “Large Projects” line item and provides clarity that this is a 
special fund to carry our unique Board projects outlined in the 2024 Cheyenne DDA Plan of 
Development. 
• 720 - Sponsorship $45,000 total 
This line item represents sponsorships to other events and organizations that may make 
requests throughout the year. Allocations will be made on a request basis and sponsorship 
funds must face public facing activities and programs that have direct public benefit. 
• 710 - Downtown Planter Program $45,000 total 
This program has the same proposed budget as FY25 based on discussions with potential 
providers.  
• 735 - Façade Improvement Program (FIP) $100,000 total 
This is a preservation-based facade improvement program aimed at revitalizing historical 
structures by restoring and enhancing their exterior appearance while fostering economic 
growth through increased property values and tourism. The program has not been used in that 
capacity recently and staff intends to further assess and develop the program in the upcoming 
year. For budgeting and accounting purposes, we recommend that this and the Capital 
Improvement Grant program be independent line items.  
• 736 - Capital Improvement Grant (CIG) Program $200,000 total 
This program has been commonly used in recent years, comprising a majority of the grants 
awarded in recent years. In fiscal year 2025, $250,000 was budgeted ($500,000 approved) and it 
is expected that all grants will be allocated prior to the end of the fiscal year. For budgeting and 
accounting purposes, we recommend that this and the Façade Improvement Program be 
independent line items.  
• 738 - Sidewalk Match Program $85,000 total 
This is a line item from FY 25 to carry out the “Sidewalk Repair Assistance Program,” a response 
to the City Engineering Department’s sidewalk repair initiative.  
 
The DDA Board approved this budget at their April 17, 2025 Regular Board meeting.

BEGINNING JULY 1, 2025, AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2026." 
 
WHEREAS, the Downtown Development Authority ("DDA") was created pursuant to 
Ordinance No. 2804 adopted by the Governing Body on July 9, 1984; 
 
WHEREAS, Wyo. Stat.§ 15-9-208(a) requires the DDA to propose a budget, which shall 
be administratively reviewed by the City prior to submission of the proposed budget to the 
Governing Body for approval; 
 
WHEREAS, Section 2.76.050(C) of the city code provides that the DDA budget shall be 
subject to alteration, modification and approval by the Governing Body in accordance with the 
policies of the City; 
 
WHEREAS, at its April 17, 2025, meeting the DDA Board approved the proposed budget 
for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2025, and ending June 30, 2026, a copy of which is appended 
to this Resolution and incorporated herein; and 
 
WHEREAS, City staff has reviewed the proposed budget received from the DDA. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the proposed budget of the Downtown Development 
Authority for the Fiscal Year beginning July 1, 2025, and ending June 30, 2026, as set forth in the 
attached information, entitled "Downtown Development Authority Proposed Budget - Fiscal Year 
2026" in the total amount of  seven hu ndred twenty -eight thousand fou r hundred fifty dollars  
($728,450.00), is hereby approved.
