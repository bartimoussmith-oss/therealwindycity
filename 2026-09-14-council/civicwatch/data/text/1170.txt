RESOLUTION NO._______________ 
ENTITLED: “ A RESOLUTION TO TEMPORARILY REDUCE THE MONTHLY 
PARKING FEE AT THE JACK C. SPIKER PARKING STRUCTURE, 
EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2025, THROUGH JUNE 30, 2026.” 
WHEREAS, the Jack C. Spiker Parking Structure (the “Spiker Parking Structure”) 
currently charges a monthly parking fee of $45.00; 
WHEREAS, community members, particularly downtown business owners, have 
requested a reduction in the monthly parking fee or free parking at the Spiker Parking Structure; 
WHEREAS, as of May 1, 2025, the Cox and Spiker Parking Structures have a combined 
outstanding bond debt of three million twenty -five thousand dollars ($3,025,000), which is 
scheduled to be fully retired by the end of Fiscal Year 2028; 
WHEREAS, given the outstanding bond  debt, offering free parking at the Spiker Parking 
Structure is not feasible at this time; 
WHEREAS, while free parking is not currently feasible, there is interest in increasing the 
usage of the Spiker Parking Structure by downtown business owners and their employees; 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body believes that temporarily reducing the monthly parking 
fee at the Spiker Parking Structure from $45.00 to $30.00, effective July 1, 2025, through June 30, 
2026, will help increase usage by downtown business owners and their employees; and 
WHEREAS, the purpose of this temporary fee reduction is to assess whether it leads to 
increased revenue and usage of the Spiker Parking Structure. 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the monthly parking fee at the Jack C. Spiker Parking Facility 
shall be temporarily reduced from $45.00 to $30.00, effective July 1, 2025, through June 30, 2026, 
after which the monthly fee shall revert to $45.00; and 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that by no later than August 1, 2026, the Parking 
Administration Manager of the Cheyenne Police Department shall provide a report to the 
Governing Body on the results of the one -year discounted fee period  for the Spiker Parking 
Structure. The report shall include: (1) the number of permits purchased during the discount period; 
(2) the number of those permits that remain active as of the date of the report; (3) a comparison of
revenue generated during the discount period and the previous five (5) years; and (4) any
recommendations for modifying the permit system to better serve the community.
