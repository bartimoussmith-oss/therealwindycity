RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE SUBMISSION OF AN APPLICATION 
BY THE CITY OF CHEYENNE FOR STATE FUNDING FOR THE OFFICE OF 
STATE LANDS AND INVESTMENTS UNMET HOUSING GRANT.” 
 
 WHEREAS, the Governing Body for the City of Cheyenne desires to participate in the Unmet 
Housing Grant Program administered by the Wyoming Office of State Lands and Investments (OSLI), to 
assist in funding the construction of twelve units of housing for low Area Median Income (AMI) 
participants transitioning from group housing to independent living in the Magic City Enterprise program 
for intellectually and developmentally disabled adults (the Project); 
 
 WHEREAS, the Governing Body recognizes the need for the Project; 
 
 WHEREAS, the Unmet Housing Grant program  requires that criteria be met  as described in the 
application, and the City of Cheyenne agrees to ensure satisfaction of all requirements;  
 
 WHEREAS, if any of the disbursed grant funds are later deemed to not comply with the OSLI 
criteria, Wyoming Statutes overseeing the use of State of Wyoming funds, and Article 16, § 6 of the 
Wyoming Constitution, the grant applicant agrees to repay the ineligible gr ant funds within fifteen (15) 
days of such finding to the OSLI; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the Governing Body intends that a request be submitted for a grant of $ 1,000,000 
from Unmet Housing Grant program for the cost of the construction of the proposed Project. 
 
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF 
CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that an application be submitted on behalf of the City of Cheyenne requesting 
one million dollars ( $1,000,000.00) in Unmet Housing Grant Program funding from the Office of State 
Lands and Investments, for consideration to assist in funding for the Project, and that the Mayor and City 
Clerk are authorized to execute all documents associated with the grant application; and 
 
 BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that Elizabeth Finochiaro, Economic Resource Administrator for 
the City of Cheyenne, is hereby designated as the Project Administrator for the City to act on all matters 
relating to this funding application and award.
