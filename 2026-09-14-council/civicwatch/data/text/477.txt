The applicant is requesting approval of a preliminary plat for roughly 1.02 acres of land. The proposed plat 
would subdivide the parcel into 5. These four of these lots would be considered DD7 Lot and the remaining lot

On the future land use map found in PlanCheyenne, the subject land and majority of surrounding 
land is designated as Urban Residential with an additional significant designation being Mixed-
Use Residential. The Urban Residential designation encourages a range of residential uses. The 
proposed plat is to provide additional single-family residential to the area. Mixed-Use Residential 
is meant for mixed-use purposes with an emphasis on residential development and supporting 
commercial developments..   
 
Foundation 1 of PlanCheyenne encourages community growth. This development is in response 
to market demands and is within the Urban Service Boundary. The development patterns 
proposed by this plat are compatible with the designations found on the future land use map.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.  
 
2) The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to 
existing and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration 
of current and future streets, open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or 
facilities. (UDC 2.1.2.d.2) 
 
The proposed plat subdivides a larger parcel into five parcels. Four of these parcels are proposed 
for residential development. For the remaining lot, potential development is undetermined at this 
moment. The four parcels are for residential have a smaller frontage than surrounding lots but 
had the same lot area as surrounding lots.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
   
3) The division of land meets the purposes, intent, and all applicable standards in 
Article 4. (UDC 2.1.2.d.3) 
 
Community Facility Fees (4.2): 
For fees based on acreage, the area of the property is pro-rated to the nearest tenth of an 
acre. Lands reserved as unbuildable (for right-of-way, detention, parks, remainder lots, etc), 
are exempt from fees. Per UDC Section 4.2.5, Table 4-2, Land acquisition, Infrastructure, and 
Enhancement Fees are all payable at the issuance of a building permit for each structure. 
 
Transportation Network (4.3): 
This plat is not creating any new streets. Existing access to the larger lot exists off Ridge R oad 
at two different access points. Each of the new, smaller lots would be accessed from Darnell 
Place.  
 
Open Space (4.4): 
Cahill Park is located less than 400 ft from property, which would allow for infill residential 
development to be exempt from Open Space requirement per UDC 4.4.2.b.3. 
 
Required Improvements (4.5): 
The plat provides a utility easement, currently the existing easement is 10 feet. The water and 
sewer lines need to be 10 feet apart by BOPU’s request. Otherwise, this plat appears to be 
able to provide all required utilities to the lots within this development.

Public Safety Fees (4.6): 
Public Safety Fees are required at the time of construction of any residential building on any 
of the proposed lots. Per UDC Section 4.6.3.b, Public Safety Fees are payable at the issuance 
of a building permit for each structure.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
 
4) Any phasing proposed in the application is clearly indicated and demonstrates a 
logical and well-planned development pattern. This may include consideration of 
amenities and public facilities of the subdivision within each phase compared to 
the anticipated timing or the likelihood of future phases being constructed as

No specific phasing is proposed with this project. 
 
Review criterion not applicable.   
 
5) The impacts identified in specific studies or technical reports that were prepared or 
requested to be prepared in association with the application, and whether the 
findings and recommendations of these reports, or any modifications or conditions 
on approval of the application, can mitigate these impacts. (UDC 2.1.2.d.5) 
 
Drainage: 
A drainage study was submitted and reviewed. There were no concerns with drainage at the plat 
level for the project as presented.  
 
Traffic: 
A transportation study was submitted and reviewed. There were no concerns with traffic at the 
plat level for the project as presented.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion. 
 
6) Lots proposed for development shall be buildable lots according to the existing or 
proposed zoning district standards, result in design and development patterns that 
meet the Intent and Applicability statements of the existing or proposed zoning 
district, and not aggravate nonconforming setbacks or currently adopted building 
and fire code access and separation requirements. (UDC 2.1.2.d.6) 
 
All lots proposed for development are buildable lots within the MR zone district. It is noted that 
the proposed lots are smaller than the surrounding lots. All the residential lots are located within 
the MR zone district. The Intent and Applicability of the MR zone district is for a mix of density 
and dwelling types in an area where more compact neighborhood development patterns are 
desired.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.

7) All parcels not proposed as buildable lots and which are not dedicated to and 
accepted by the City shall have a clearly defined entity responsible for ongoing 
maintenance.  (UDC 2.1.2.d.7) 
 
All proposed lots are proposed as buildable lots. The Plat does not propose any lots subject to 
this criterion. 
 
Review criterion not applicable. 
 
8) The application will not create any undue influence on existing or potential 
development for adjacent property considering the existing zoning and 
Comprehensive Plan policies for that property.  (UDC 2.1.2.d.8) 
 
This plat will not create any undue influence on existing or potential development. There are 
streets on two sides of the site. South of the subject site, there are two lots. One of the lots is 
residential and the other is an auto service shop. Properties to the north and west are single-
family houses.  
 
East of the property is vacant land with no development plans at this time. The vacant land is 
within the HR zone district with a Mixed-Use Residential designation from the Future Land Use 
map. Expected potential development for the site could be high-density residential. The only part 
of the plat facing the vacant land is remaining the same as it previously operated. This plat would 
not create any undue influence on potential development to the vacant land.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
 
9) The application is not harmful to the public health, safety, and welfare. (UDC 
2.1.2.d.9) 
 
City staff is satisfied that this proposed plat would not adversely affect the public health, safety, or 
general welfare. 
 
The application complies with this review criterion.
