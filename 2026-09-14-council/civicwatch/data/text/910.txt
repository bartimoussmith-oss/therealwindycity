 
 
 
RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION DIRECTING STAFF TO LET BIDS FOR STRIPING  
PARKING SPACES AND PAINTING CURBS  IN THE DOWNTOWN 
AREA." 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne and the Downtown Development Authority  
commissioned a study to determine the most efficient use of parking spaces in the downtown area;  
 
WHEREAS, parking has been under discussion since 2016 , when a professional parking 
study was conducted with a recommendation to delineate parking spaces and adequately mark 
accessible parking spaces; 
 
WHEREAS, substantial public and private investment has been made in the downtown 
area; 
 
WHEREAS, the Spiker Parking Garage has been recently remodeled to include restroom 
improvements, upgraded lighting, and interior painting to enhance safety and user experience;  
 
WHEREAS, the City also recently engaged a professional civil engineering firm to develop 
a plan for striping parking spaces and painting curbs in the downtown area; and 
 
WHEREAS, funding is available for downtown maintenance and improvement projects 
through the voter-approved one percent (1%) specific purpose sales and use tax (“6th Penny Tax”), 
which may be applied to parking striping and curb painting. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that City staff are hereby directed to let bids for the striping of 
parking spaces and painting of curbs in the downtown area, including and between 15th Street on 
the south, 23rd Street on the north, Pioneer Avenue on the west, and Warren Avenue on the east . 
Staff shall proceed with letting bids immediately upon completion of the plans currently in 
development for this purpose;  
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED , that funding for the striping and painting provided for 
herein shall be provided from funds available through the 6th Penny Tax fund.  City staff shall 
endeavor to ensure the work is completed as expeditiously as possible, and prior to the onset of 
inclement weather that may impede timely completion; and 
 
BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED, that, to the extent the engineering firm’s plan presently 
under development does not encompass the entirety of the area described herein, City staff may 
request that the consultant modify the plan as necessary to effectuate the intent of this Resolution.
