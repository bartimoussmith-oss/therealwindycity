______________________________________________
This is a request for an additional award of Mid-Size SUVs and a Full Size SUV from Bid E-
19-25.  The original bid consideration was approved at the June 23, 2025 City Council Meeting.
This request includes One (1) Mid-Size SUV for the Compliance Division, One (1) Full Size 
SUV & Two (2) Mid-Size SUVs for the Community Recreation and Events Department, and 
Two (2) Mid-Size SUVs for the Cheyenne Fire Rescue Department. 
Four (4) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.  The Four (4) bids 
were received from: 
1. Fleet Vehicle Source, Inc dba US Fleet Source (Las Vegas, Nevada)
a. Bid Rejected.  Did not comply with all Bid Requirement, and the Mid-Size SUV
Option did not meet the minimum wheelbase requirements.
2. Fremont Motor Company (Lander, Wyoming)
3. Ken Garff Cheyenne (Cheyenne, Wyoming)
4. PFVT Motors, LLC dba Peoria Ford (Peoria, Arizona
The bids were reviewed by  Bryan Bell (Fleet Maintenance), Craig LaVoy (Public Works),  and 
Vicki Nemecek (Public Works).   
Their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from Fremont Motor Company.  The 
total requested bid award, is for $274,676.00.
