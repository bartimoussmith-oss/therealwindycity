APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS: 
 
• UDC 2.2.8 Assigned Zoning 
 
BACKGROUND: 
The applicant has petitioned for roughly 34.46 acres of land to be annexed into city limits. Per UDC 2.2.8 a zone 
will be assigned based on allowed uses, current uses, or surrounding City zones. Should the applicant wish to 
have a different zone at the time of annexation, they will be required to submit a subsequent zoning map 
amendment, proposing a different zone.  
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1. The proposed zoning assignment is the most analogous City zone to the current zone 
when taking into consideration allowed uses, current uses, or surrounding City zones. 
Zoning shall be assigned a City zone based on: 
a. Permitted uses, 
b. Uses that could reasonably be anticipated for conditional use approval 
under the current zoning, 
c. Current uses, or 
d. Surrounding City zoning, 
as deemed most suitable for the property being assigned a zone in terms of allowing 
the landowner to continue the current use of the land while respecting the current and 
anticipated uses of surrounding property. 
 
Through the reviewing of the annexation petition area, the assigned zoning is fairly straightforward. 
 
The land is currently zoned LU-Land Use within the County. The land is surrounded by City BP – 
Business Park to the south and the east and City AG – Agriculture to the north. County LU is to the 
northwest and the west. The property currently has county approval for a site plan to allow a staging 
area with parking and office trailers for the construction of the existing data center locations within 
the North Range Business Park. This property is adjacent to North Range business park on three sides 
and is meant to integrate and become a part of the Business Park. Due to the current approved use, 
surrounding zoning and surrounding uses, Staff believes that the BP – Business Park zone is the 
most analogous zone for this land.  
 
This assigned zoning complies with review criterion 1.
