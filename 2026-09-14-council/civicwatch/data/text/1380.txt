Lease.

Cattle Company.

in place.

GRAZING LEASE AGREEMENT 
 
 This BELVOIR GRAZING LEASE (this “Lease”) is made and entered into this  _______ 
day of __________, 202 5, by and between CITY OF CHEYENNE,  WYOMING, the mailing 
address of which, for purposes of this Lease, is MUNICIPAL BUILDING, 2101 O’NEIL 
AVENUE, PUBLIC WORKS OFFICE, ROOM 207, CHEYENNE,  WYOMING 82001  
(“Lessor”) and WYOMING CATTLE COMPANY the mailing address of which, for purposes of 
this Lease, is 1251 COUNTY ROAD 157, PINE BLUFFS, WYOMING 82082 (“Lessee”). 
 
1. Leased Premises. For and in consideration of the promises, covenants, and agreements 
hereinafter set forth to be kept and performed by Lessee, its successors and assigns, Lessor, 
for the term hereinafter set forth, does hereby lease, demise, and let unto Lessee, for grazing 
purposes, the parcel of real estate situated in the County of Laramie, State of Wyoming, as 
shown on attached Pasture Map, Exhibit “ A” attached hereto and incorporated herein by 
this reference (the “Leased Premises”). The Leased Premises shall include the pastures 
Identified on the Belvoir Ranch map with the exception of the Rim Pasture and the Big 
Hole. The Leased Premises shall include improvements and fixtures of a permanent nature 
located on or appurtenant to the Lease Premises. 
 
Lessee shall be entitled to graze not more than one thousand and fifty ( 1,050) animal unit 
months ( AUM)’s annually on the Leased Premises pursuant to this Lease except as 
otherwise mutually agreed in writing by both parties. 
 
2. Exclusions. The Lease Premises shall not include, and Lessor expressly reserves, any and 
all hunting and other recreational privileges; provided, however, that the exercise of any 
such reserved privileges by Lessor or its assigns shall not unreasonably interfere with 
Lessee’s use of the Leased Premises as a grazing pasture. Similarly, the Leased Premises 
shall not include, and Lessor expressly reserves, all coal, oil, gas, other minerals and water 
located in, under and to be produced from the Leased Premises or any part thereof, it being 
mutually agreed the Lessee, its successors and assigns, grants to Lessor  the right to enter 
in and upon the surface of the Leased Premises for the purpose of operation, development 
and production of such minerals and water  and shall allow Lessor to use the Leased 
Premises or any part thereof as shall  be required for buildings, tanks, pipelines, fixtures, 
and equipment in connection herewith. Furthermore, Lessor, and any architects, engineers 
or other agents, independent contractors, or employees or Lessor, shall have the right, at 
any time during the term of this Lease, and from time to time, to enter  upon the Leased 
Premises, to show the Leased Premises, to make soil and water tests, to survey the Leased 
Premises, to acquire engineering and other data with respect to the Leased Premises, and/or 
to install utility lines or  facilities over, under, or across the Leased Premises. In the event

that damage is caused to the surface of the Leased Premises, or to Lessee’s livestock, as a 
result of Lessor’s activities, Lessor shall adjust the Lessee’s lease payment accordingly.  
 
A. The Belvoir Ranch was purchased by the City of Cheyenne in response to the need 
to site a new landfill and develop ground water resources. (A landfill will not be 
placed on the Belvoir Ranch for the foreseeable future since  the expansion of the 
current landfill.) While development of ground water resources is a future 
development (potentially years away), City investigative work could be ongoing on 
ranch property. These activities by the City and/or its agents will have priority for 
land use. Very little impact is anticipated to the grazing opportunities. 
 
B. Portions of the Ranch may be developed for non -motorized recreational activity.  
This would most likely take place in the southwestern portion of the Ranch and 
consist of a trail from the State section adjacent to Harriman Road to the “Big 
Hole.” Minimal impact on grazing is anticipated; however, the grazing lease may 
be renegotiated to accommodate this development.  
 
3. Term of Lease.  This Lease shall be for a term of three (3) years commencing on the date 
hereof and expiring October 31, 2027. 
Upon written request by the Lessee, Lessor may extend the Lease for  three (3) additional 
three (3) year  periods. Requests shall be submitted by written notice to Lessor 90 days  
before the scheduled expiration of the Lease. Any such extension shall be on the same 
terms and conditions as set forth in the Lease or renegotiated by mutual agreement. Any 
Agreement by the Lessor to so extend shall be conditioned upon Lessee not being in default 
at the time of giving such notice or at the commencement of the extended term and that 
Lessee has practiced good stewardship of the proper ty and facilities as determined by the 
Lessor. 
 
4. Rental.  Annual rental for this Lease shall be in the amount of Two Hundred Ten Thousand 
Dollars ($210,000.00) which rental shall be payable as follows: 
 
A. The sum of One Hundred, Five Thousand Dollars  ($105,000.00) shall be due and 
payable upon stock arrival or before June 15, each year.   (1/2 of total, adjusted as 
necessary to reflect actual number of head of stock allowed). 
B. The remaining sum of One Hundred, Five Thousand Dollars ($105,000.00) (1/2 of 
total amount) shall be due and payable upon stock removal or before October 31, 
each year. 
C. Annual Rental is based on a standard of 1050  AUMs annually at Forty Dollars 
($40.00) per AUM.

D. The Lessee will graze and pay for a minimum of 1050 AUMs annually unless the 
City determines a lower number or timeframe based on the condition of the pasture. 
Lessee will pay for actual use determined by AUM and time on the range.  
E. Lessor shall pay state lease fees and any real and personal property taxes which are 
assessed against the Leased Premises during the term of this  Lease, and all water 
assessments, fees and related charges. 
F. Lessee shall pay any personal property taxes with respect to Lessee’s personal 
property and chattels. In addition, Lessee shall pay all expenses normally associated 
with grazing operations incurred by Lessee in connection with its use of the Leased 
Premises. 
 
5. Ranching Methods. Lessee shall use the Leased Premises for grazing and related purposes 
only. Lessee agrees to follow the best grazing, ranching, and husbanding practices 
prevailing and shall ope rate the Leased Premises in good, first class, and workmanlike 
manner in such a way as to not damage or  harm the grass plants growing thereon by over 
grazing. Lessor shall bear the expense of chemicals for spraying of noxious weeds and shall 
be responsible for all spraying and application costs. Lessee shall conduct all of its  
operations on the Leased Premises in full compliance with all applicable federal, state or 
local laws, rules, regulations and ordinances. 
 
6. General Stipulations 
 
a. Summer grazing normally allowed May through October depending on range 
conditions except as otherwise mutually agreed in writing by both parties. 
b. Range conditions permitting, no more than 1050 AUMs total annually will be 
allowed on the property. This number could be less depending on range and water 
availability. 
c. Pasture use will follow a time frame as established by the Lessor  in consultation 
with the Laramie County Conservation District. Livestock will not be allowed to 
be moved without the express permission of the Lessor through the Ranch Manager. 
d. It is the sole responsibility of the Lessee to monitor and move cattle as required.  
The Ranch Manager will provide oversight only. 
e. Veterinary care, medicines, and supplies and any salt or minerals will  be the 
responsibility of the Lessee. 
f. The Lessor will not be responsible for any “death loss”. 
g. Lessor will provide access to water. However, it is the Lessee’s responsibility to 
check and ensure the availability  of water to cattle.  Lessee shall notify Lessor in 
case of a broken waterline in a timely manner. Hauling water is the responsibility 
of the Lessee until the Lessor is able to repair the waterline.

h. Maintenance of corrals, fences, stock tanks and any other ranch facilities relating 
to grazing operations will be the responsibility of the Lessee. However, major 
repairs of the water systems, pumps, and wells will be made by the Lessor.  Lessee 
is responsible for the full repair of any damage caused to ranch facilities by Lessee’s 
operations. Access to property will be provided at least 30 days prior to stock arrival 
for site preparation. Gates will be kept closed at all times. 
i. Lessee may operate ATVs for cattle management.  Lessee assumes all liability 
related to such activities. 
j. All dates of use, pasture locations, and maximum numbers of head of stock 
will be determined by the Lessor . The decisions will be based on range 
conditions at the time.  Lessor reserves the right to determine, under any 
contingent circumstances (such as flood, fire, drought, etc.), when and how 
many head of stock the range can carry at any given time and reserves the 
right to require immediate reduction or removal of livestock to those numbers 
deemed appropriate by the Lessor. 
k. A City employee functioning as a ranch manager will be living on- site and be the 
main point of contact between the Lessee and Lessor. This person will oversee the 
arrival and removal of all livestock and coordinate the lease agreement activities. 
 
7. Maintenance.  To the extent normally associated with grazing of livestock,  Lessee shall 
keep the Leased Premises and all improvements thereon,  including, but not limited to, 
fences, and corrals, in their current state of order and repair.  Lessee will control soil erosion 
as completely as practicable. Lessee shall commit no waste and shall surrender the Leased 
Premises at the end of termination of this Lease in as good a  condition as they are now, 
excepting wear and tear. 
 
8. Termination of Lease.  This lease may be terminated  by either party, with written notice 
provided not later than October 31 prior to the year the lease is to be terminated.  
 
9. Removal of Lessee’s Property. On or before OCTOBER 31, OF EACH YEAR, without 
regard to whether Lessee has requested an extension of the term of this Lease, Lessee shall 
remove from the Leased Premises all livestock, and all items of personal property  and 
equipment belonging to Lessee unless otherwise mutually agreed in writing by both parties. 
 
10. Insurance Requirements.  The Lessee must provide proof of the following insurance 
coverages, to be in force while occupying the Leased Premises:

Additional Insurance Information 
The Lessee shall name the City of Cheyenne as an Additional Insured by endorsement on

that the insurance may not be canceled, amended or allowed to lapse without at least thirty 
(30) days advance written notice to the City of Cheyenne. In addition, Lessee shall provide 
the City with copies of insurance  policies and /or policy endorsements listing the City of 
Cheyenne as an additional insured. The City’s failure to request or review such policies, 
endorsements, and certificates shall not a ffect the City’s rights or Lessee’s obligation 
hereunder. Any insurance company providing coverage under this agreement shall have a 
minimum A.M. Best rating of A- (excellent). 
11. Governmental Immunity. The City of Cheyenne expressly reserves the right  to invoke 
governmental immunity for any claim arising out of this Lease pursuant to the Wyoming 
Governmental Claims Act, Wyo. Stat. §1-39-101, et_seq. 
 
12. Mechanic’s Liens. Lessor shall not, under any circumstances, be liable for the payment of 
any expenses incurred or for the value of any work done or  materials furnished to the 
Leased Premises by Lessee. All such work shall be at Lessee’s expense, and Lessee shall 
be solely responsible to the contractors, laborers, and materialmen furnishing labor and 
material to the Leased Premises.

13. Right to Inspect. Lessor reserves the right for itself, its agents, employees, contractors and 
representatives, at any and all times to enter upon the Leased Premises, to inspect the 
Leased Premises, and to protect itself against loss occasioned by any negligence or neglect 
of Lessee.  
 
14. Illegal Use. Lessee agrees not to use or permit the Leased Premises to be used  for any 
unlawful or illegal business or purpose. 
 
15. Default by Lessee.  If Lessee shall default in its obligations to make due and punctual 
payment of any rent or additional payments required to be paid by Lessee under this Lease, 
or if Lessee shall fail to perform any other  obligations assumed by it under this Lease, 
Lessor may give written notice to Lessee, specifying the nature of the default, stating that 
this Lease shall expire on the date specified in such notice, which shall be at least ten (10) 
days after the giving of such notice; and upon the date specified in such notice, this  Lease 
and all of the rights of Lessee hereunder shall terminate, except as expressly provided 
otherwise herein. It is agreed that if Lessee shall fail to pay any installments or rent when 
due and such failure shall continue for a period of ten (10) days following the due date, 
said installment of rent shall bear interest at the rate of ten percent (10%) per annum until 
paid. Lessor  reserves the right to make any payments or perform any action required  
hereunder by Lessee (but shall not be required to do so); and all amounts so expended by 
Lessor, together with interest at the rate of ten percent (10%) per  annum, shall be 
immediately due and payable by Lessee to Lessor. In lieu of the right of Lessor to terminate 
this Lease in the event of a default by Lessee, Lessor shall have the right, at his election, to 
reenter and take possession of the Leased Premises; and Lessee shall peaceably vacate the 
Leased Premises. In such event. Lessee shall remain liable for all unpaid rentals for the 
remaining term of this Lease. If Lessor elects to reenter as provided herein  without 
terminating this Lease, Lessor may, from time to time, without terminating this Lease, relet 
the Leased Premises or any part thereof for such term or terms, and at such rental or rentals, 
and on such terms and conditions as Lessor may deem advisable. No such reentry or taking 
of possession of the Leased Premises by Lessor shall be construed as an election on 
Lessor’s part to terminate the Lease unless written notice of termination is delivered to  
Lessee by Lessor. In the event of such repossession, Lessee shall pay to Lessor, as current 
liquidated damages, the then value of the rent and other  sums payable under the terms of 
this Lease as if such repossession had not occurred; less the net proceeds, if any, of any 
reletting of the Leased Premises after deducting all of Lessor’s expenses in connection with 
such reletting, including, without limitation, all repossession costs, brokerage 
commissions, legal expenses, attorney’s fees, and expenses or repair and preparation of the 
Leased Premises for such reletting.
