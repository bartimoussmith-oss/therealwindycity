
RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CITY OF CHEYENNE TO 
ACCEPT AND IMPLEMENT CERTAIN PLEDGED FUNDS FROM 
FORMAK, A 501(c)(3) NON-PROFIT ORGANIZATION, IN SUPPORT OF 
SAFE ROUTES TO SCHOOL IMPROVEMENTS AND THE PURCHASE 
AND INSTALLATION OF RELATED TRAFFIC CONTROL DEVICES.” 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne has an ongoing interest in promoting safe and efficient 
traffic control around schools within the City; 
 
WHEREAS, the City has been awarded Community Development Block Grant funding 
and has identified City contributions in support of certain Safe Routes to School improvements; 
 
WHEREAS, ForMak, a 501(c)(3) non -profit organization, has submitted a written 
commitment pledging supplemental funding in the amount of One Hundred Fifty Thousand 
Dollars ($150,000.00) to support the inclusion of school crossing and safety improvements at Alta 
Vista Elementary School, Baggs Elementary School, Davis Elementary School, and Rossman 
Elementary School; 
 
WHEREAS, ForMak has also submitted a written commitment pledging funding in the 
amount of Fifty-Two Thousand Four Hundred Forty Dollars ($52,440.00) to support equipment 
upgrades that will allow the City to be more responsive to early school release schedu les and 
improve compliance and expectations of motorists; 
 
WHEREAS, the total amount pledged by ForMak for these purposes is Two Hundred Two 
Thousand Four Hundred Forty Dollars ($202,440.00); 
 
WHEREAS, ForMak’s written commitments state that the pledged funds are contingent 
upon the execution of a mutually acceptable funding agreement outlining the scope, timeline, and 
disbursement terms for the pledged funds; 
 
WHEREAS, the City desires to accept the pledged funds and proceed with the necessary 
agreements, purchases, and implementation steps consistent with the commitments made by 
ForMak and the City’s applicable procurement, budgeting, and financial requirements; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body finds that acceptance and implementation of these funds 
will serve a public purpose by supporting pedestrian safety, school -zone traffic control, and the 
safe movement of students and motorists near schools within the City of Cheyenne. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the City of Cheyenne is authorized to accept pledged funding 
from ForMak in the total amount of Two Hundred Two Thousand Four Hundred Forty Dollars 
($202,440.00), consisting of One Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($150,000.00) for Safe Routes 
to School crossing and safety improvements and Fifty-Two Thousand Four Hundred Forty Dollars 
($52,440.00) for the purchase and installation of related traffic control device upgrades; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Mayor  and other City staff and officials are 
authorized to take all necessary and appropriate actions to accept, receive, budget, account for, and 
expend the pledged funds for the purposes described in this Resolution, including the execution of 
any funding agreement  or other instrument necessary to implement the funding commitments, 
provided such documents are approved as to form by the City Attorney’s Office and comply with 
applicable City policies and procedures; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the City is authorized to use the pledged funds for the 
Safe Routes to School improvements and related traffic control device upgrades described in 
ForMak’s commitment letters, including the purchase and installation of necessar y device 
components anticipated to be installed prior to the 2026–2027 school year; and 
 
BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED that nothing in this Resolution shall be construed to require 
the City to expend funds in excess of amounts lawfully appropriated or otherwise available for the 
purposes stated herein.
