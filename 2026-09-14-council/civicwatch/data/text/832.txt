 
RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE A 
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING WITH PARTICIPATING 
COUNTIES, CITIES AND TOWNS, REGARDING THE ALLOCATION OF 
IMPACT ASSISTANCE FUNDS ASSOCIATED WITH THE 
CHUGWATER ENERGY PROJECT APPLICATION BEFORE THE 
INDUSTRIAL SITING COUNCIL.” 
 
WHEREAS, on or about September 3, 2025, Chugwater Energy, which consists of wholly 
owned indirect subsidiaries of NextEra Energy Resource, LLC , submitted to the Wyoming 
Department of Environmental Quality (DEQ), Industrial Siting Division (ISD) , a permit 
application in accordance with Wyoming Industrial Development Information and Siting Act (“the 
Act” to construct and operate the Chugwater Energy Project (hereinafter referred to as “Project”); 
 
WHEREAS, Chugwater Energy plans to construct and operate the Project on private and 
state leased lands in Platte County, Wyoming.  The Project site is located east of the Town of 
Chugwater and Interstate 25 and spans approximately 49,500 acres.  The Project anticipates the 
generation of approximately 300 megawatts of wind energy, 150 megawatts of solar energy, and 
includes a 150 megawatt 4-hour duration battery energy storage system; 
 
WHEREAS, a jurisdictional meeting was held on March 25, 2025, during which ISD staff 
determined the Project is subject to their jurisdiction based on the estimated construction cost, and 
Platte, Goshen, and Laramie County were defined as the study area; 
 
WHEREAS, Chugwater Energy proposes a 20-month construction period beginning in the 
second quarter of 2026. Chugwater Energy  estimates a twenty (20) month average  temporary 
workforce of two hundred fifty-three (253) workers with a peak of four hundred fifty (450) workers 
in July 2027; 
 
WHEREAS, the total estimated construction cost for the Project is nine hundred seventy-
seven million seven hundred fifty thousand dollars ($977,750,000.00) , including materials and 
labor; 
 
WHEREAS, W.S. 35-12-109(xiii) requires  that Chugwater Energy  provide in its 
Application "[a]n evaluation of potential impacts together with any plans and proposals for 
alleviating social and economic impacts upon local governments or special districts and alleviating 
environmental impacts which may result from the proposed facility[;]" 
 
WHEREAS, the evaluation of potential social and economic impacts identifies temporary 
increases in population, demand for services, and other community effects that may result in 
additional costs to local governments within the study area; 
 
Page 2 of 2 
WHEREAS, recognizing these potential impacts, the  Act and associated DEQ rules 
provide a process for the allocation of “impact assistance funds” to affected local governments to 
offset such costs; 
 
WHEREAS, Section 12 of DEQ’s Industrial Development Information and Siting Rules 
and Regulations, provides that as part of the permit application process, the affected counties, cities 
and towns in the area primarily affected can agree to an amount and schedule for the distribution 
of impact payment funds and submit their agreement to the Division for submission to the Council 
for its consideration; 
 
WHEREAS, the local governments that will be primarily affected by this project desire to 
enter into an agreement to determine the amounts and schedule for payment distribution of impact 
assistance funds for the Project; and 
 
WHEREAS, the counties, cities, and towns  affected by the project have prepared a 
Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) regarding funding amounts and schedule for impact 
payment distributions, which must be finalized  and executed by the parties before October 31, 
2025. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Mayor is hereby authorized and directed to execute, on 
behalf of the City, a Memorandum of Understanding with the participating counties, cities, and 
towns affe cted by the Chugwater Energy Project, regarding the allocation and distribution of 
impact assistance funds in accordance with the Industrial Development Information and Siting Act
