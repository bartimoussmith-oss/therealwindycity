BACKGROUND: 
 
This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from Agricultural and Light Industrial to Business 
Park for roughly 652.67 acres of land. The land is currently vacant with sewer and water extension in the area. 
The proposal is to allow a large-scale destination facility which may include offices, a museum, or other 
accessory uses. Although the facility itself will likely only occupy small portion of the region, uses which may 
want to locate near this use may follow and utilize other land in the area proposed to be rezoned.

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical 
patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1) 
 
These parcels are currently undergoing a Future Land Use Map Amendment to change 
designation from Agriculture/Rural and Rural Residential to Community Business to be in 
accordance with development and physical patterns. The Community Business designation 
supports a range of commercial activities, regional malls, and allows for offices, hotels, and service 
businesses. Intensive industrial activities are not appropriate. Secondary uses can open space and 
recreation, and other public or civic uses are appropriate. Multi-family housing may be 
appropriate if designed as part of an integrated mixed-use concept plan. Ideal locations for this 
designation are at interstate interchange or at the intersection of arterial or state highway. This 
designation aligns with the potential development Commercial/Regional Activity Centers.  
 
Foundation 1 of PlanCheyenne does allows for flexibility in the future land use map based on 
market. This same Foundation promotes working with local and regional economic development 
partners to position Cheyenne as a place to live and do business in the Northern Front Range 
region.  
 
Foundation 3 of PlanCheyenne focuses on Cheyenne becoming a favorable business environment 
through the development of regional activity centers. These commercial activity centers should be 
pedestrian-based and well designed.  
 
Based on the policies and future land use map, BP Business Park would be appropriate in this 
area.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1. 
 
2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering 
existing zoning, the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies 
created under the guidance of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2) 
 
The proposed BP zone district would extend existing BP zoning from the south. The southern 
property is adjacent to BP zoning, and the BP zone specifically anticipates intervening interstates 
or natural areas (like floodplains) – the AG zoned parcel between these two parcels area is located 
along a waterway with floodplain. The northern properties are zoned County LU Land Use and 
City CB Community Business; the County LU Land Use parcels are vacant, and the CB Community 
Business parcels have the use of Lodging, Hotel/Motel. The remaining parcel around the largest 
parcel is zoned AG Agricultural and is vacant. The eastern parcels surrounding the smaller parcel 
are zoned P Public and Overland PUD and have developed commercial uses on them.

The existing zones are AG Agricultural and LI Light Industrial. AG Agricultural zoning allows for a 
limited amount of development options. LI Light Industrial allows for select commercial 
development and industrial development. Neither of the current zones would appropriately 
support the proposed end use(s) for this parcel.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2. 
   
3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards 
and uses of the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject 
property for development according to the standards and uses of the proposed 
zoning district (UDC 2.2.1.d.3) 
 
The BP zone district allows for select service uses, a variety of commercial uses, and select 
industrial uses. The current use of the subject property is for vacant or agriculture proposed. In 
the County, the parcels were zoned within the Overland PUD. The Overland PUD created areas 
based on land use type. Within the annexed area, the land use types are Industrial, Business, and 
Open Space.  
 
When annexed into the City, the land will be assigned the City zones of AG Agricultural and LI 
Light Industrial based on the current use of the property and previous County zoning. Based on 
the proposed development for this parcel, would need to fall under the UDC 6.9 General 
Commercial Design Standards. There is reduced landscaping requirements that may be 
inappropriate for the proposed development along with potential future development. 
Landscaping is vital in creating aesthetic pleasing character of new development and reduce 
visual harshness and summer heat. With the BP zone, majority of the landscaping will be focused 
on the parking lot and street frontage.  
 
Running two weeks behind this zone change are various Ordinances that proposes changing 
parts of the Unified Development Code. The changes proposed in these ordinances would allow 
the BP Business Park zone district to include equestrian activities. The proposed end use for one 
of the platted parcel will involved equestrian activities.  
 
The proposed zone change largely complies with review criterion 3. 
 
4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed 
according to the Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed 
zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4) 
 
There are changes to the transportation network that will need to developed in accordance to 
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 and in coordination with Wyoming Department of 
Transportation future development plans.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4. 
 
5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of 
the proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific 
standards. (UDC 2.2.1.d.5) 
 
PUDC-26-207   September 8. 2026 
5 | P a g e 
 
The BP district is intended to support small and large-scale industrial development while 
providing essential commercial services along regional transportation routes in an industrial park 
setting. The district integrates manufacturing, processing, research and development, and 
shipping activities in addition to retail, dining, and convenience stores to enhance functionality 
and appeal for businesses and employees.  
 
These Business Park districts should be in area on the edge of the community that are intended to 
support large-scale industrial, manufacturing, shipping, research and development, and data 
center uses. These districts are typically found close to major transportation routes. The district 
has to be a minimum of 320 acres of land that can be interrupted by transportation routes and 
natural features. Large-scale areas within the future land use designations in the comprehensive 
plan of Community Business, Mixed-Use Employment, and Industrial – including 
Community/Regional Activity Centers are appropriate for BP zoning. The UDC also states that 
areas currently designated Agricultural/Rural may also be appropriate for this zone district if near 
to regional transportation routes and if development of the area extends appropriate 
infrastructure. 
 
With the change in agricultural uses, equestrian uses will be allowed in the Business Park zone 
district. This was an important addition to the Business Park as part of the proposed development 
is facilities that would be used for equine events. With this change to the BP zone, it would make 
BP a more appropriate zone district for this use.  
 
The future use of the area meets the intent and applicability of the BP zone district due to the 
location of the interstate interchange and the potential for a campus like development. The BP 
zone is meant for area of large area development, connective transportation routes, and the 
development of supportive uses.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5. 
    
6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for 
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options 
if any specific development proposed in association with the application is not 
executed after approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6) 
 
The BP zone does allow for a variety of uses with a concentration on non-residential 
development. This is increase in amount of uses from the previous zone district of Agricultural 
and Light Industrial, which allowed for very few uses. All these uses will be needed to meet the 
applicable standards within the UDC.  
 
The proposed use for this area are offices, a museum, and equine sport facilities. If for some 
reason this proposed use was unable to develop, it would still be able to develop into a 
commercial, service, or industrial use still. Due to access to the interstate, uses that could go into 
this area are restaurants, fast food or otherwise, hotels, and warehouse developments.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6. 
 
7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7)

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7.

City staff is satisfied that this proposed zone change would not adversely affect the public health,
