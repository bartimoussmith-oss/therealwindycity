RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE 
LIMITED SITE ACCESS AND DUE DILIGENCE AGREEMENTS FOR 
CITY-OWNED PROPERTY COMMONLY KNOWN AS BELVOIR 
RANCH.” 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne owns certain real property located in Laramie County, 
Wyoming, commonly known as Belvoir Ranch; 
 
WHEREAS, the Belvoir Ranch was purchased by the City of Cheyenne for the benefit of 
the citizens of Cheyenne, including for public, municipal, operational, recreational, utility, and 
other lawful public purposes; 
 
WHEREAS, from time to time, private companies and other entities may request limited 
access to portions of Belvoir Ranch for the purpose of conducting due diligence activities related 
to potential development, infrastructure, utility, energy, environmental, or other projects; 
 
WHEREAS, limited due diligence activities may include, without limitation, surveying, 
mapping, topographic review, wetlands evaluation, preliminary geotechnical investigation, 
environmental review, site observation, or other similar temporary activities approved by the City; 
 
WHEREAS, allowing limited, temporary, non-exclusive, and revocable access to Belvoir 
Ranch for due diligence purposes may assist the City in evaluating potential projects, gathering 
information, coordinating with interested entities, and determining whethe r any future proposal 
may warrant further consideration by the City; 
 
WHEREAS, any such access shall be subject to written agreement and include appropriate 
protections for the City, the Belvoir Ranch, Board of Public Utilities operations and infrastructure, 
existing ranch operations, cultural and archaeological resources, environmental compliance, and 
restoration obligations; 
 
WHEREAS, any site access and due diligence agreement authorized by this Resolution 
shall grant only a limited license to enter upon designated portions of Belvoir Ranch and shall not 
convey any leasehold, easement, ownership interest, development right, entitlement, or other real 
property interest; 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body intends that such agreements be limited to temporary 
due diligence access only, and that the Mayor’s execution of such agreements shall not constitute 
approval of any proposed development project or any future property interest in Belvoir Ranch; 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body further intends that the execution of any site access and 
due diligence agreement shall not constitute approval of, or bind the City to approve, any future 
project, development, lease, sale, easement, utility agreement, development agreement, land use 
approval, permit, or other project-related authorization; and 
 
WHEREAS, any future development project, property interest, land use approval, 
development agreement, lease, easement, sale, utility commitment, or other approval requiring 
action by the Governing Body shall remain subject to separate review and approval b y the 
Governing Body in accordance with applicable law. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Mayor is hereby authorized to execute site access and due 
diligence agreements allowing limited, temporary, non -exclusive, and revocable access to 
designated portions of Belvoir Ranch for due diligence activities, provided that each agreement is 
reviewed and approved as to form by the City Attorney’s Office prior to execution; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that any agreement executed under this Resolution shall 
be limited to due diligence activities and may include, as appropriate, activities such as surveying, 
mapping, wetlands evaluation, preliminary geotechnical investigation, topog raphic review, 
environmental review, site observation, and other similar temporary activities approved by the 
City; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that any agreement executed under this Resolution shall 
include terms determined necessary or appropriate by the City to protect the public interest, 
including provisions addressing the scope of permitted activities, excluded areas, coordination 
with City and Board of Public Utilities representatives, insurance, indemnification, environmental 
compliance, cultural and archaeological resources, restoration, termination, governmental 
immunity, and any other provisions deemed necessary by the City Attorney’s Office; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Mayor’s authority under this Resolution shall not 
include authority to approve any development project, grant any leasehold interest, easement, 
ownership interest, development right, entitlement, utility commitment, or other  property interest 
in Belvoir Ranch, except as may be separately authorized by the Governing Body; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the authority granted to the Mayor under this 
Resolution is limited to the Mayor’s current term of office and shall automatically expire at the 
conclusion of that term, unless renewed or extended by subsequent action of the Governing Body; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the execution of any site access and due diligence 
agreement under this Resolution shall not obligate the City to approve, support, negotiate, or enter 
into any future agreement related to any proposed project, development, lease, easement, sale, land 
use approval, permit, utility arrangement, or other project-related authorization; and 
 
BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED that any future project or development requiring approval 
by the Governing Body shall be presented to the Governing Body for separate consideration and 
approval in accordance with applicable law.
