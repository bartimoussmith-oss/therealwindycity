WHEREAS, on April 25, 1983, the City of Cheyenne (“City”), the County of Laramie 
("County"), the City of Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities ( "BOPU"), and the South Cheyenne 
Water and Sewer District (“District”) entered into an agreement entitled "Memorandum of 
Agreement: Intergovernmental Contract Agreeing to Participate in the Implementation of the 
Findings of the 201 Facilities Plan Final Report for the City of Cheyenne, the South Cheyenne 
Water and Sewer District and Laramie County"; 
 
 WHEREAS, the April 25, 1983 , agreement is commonly referred to as the "201 
Agreement"; 
 
 WHEREAS, the 201 Agreement defines the respective responsibilities and obligations of 
the signatory governmental entities with respect to the implementation of the “201 Facilities Plan” 
that was created in response to the requirements of Section 201 of the Federal Water Pollution 
Control Act; 
 
 WHEREAS, one of the principal purposes of the 201 Agreement is to ensure that 
wastewater (sewage) generated in the areas immediately surrounding the corporate limits of the 
City of Cheyenne would preferentially be disposed of through the sanitary sewer systems operated 
by BOPU; 
 
 WHEREAS, the 201 Agreement defines the “service area boundary” sometimes referred 
to as the “sewerable boundary” or “201 Area” as the area that “can be provided with Municipal 
Sewage Treatment Services via gravity flow”;

WHEREAS, the Applicant seeks to install a small wastewater system [septic system] at a 
cost of approximately $14,500.00 rather than pay an estimated cost of $316,650.00 to extend 
sanitary sewer service approximately 330 linear feet to the Applicant’s property; 
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 3.4(d) of the 201 Agreement, the County agreed to work 
with the City-County Health Unit to “ensure that no new permits for small wastewater treatment 
systems within the service area boundary that are inconsistent with the Facility Plan are issued”; 
 
WHEREAS, exceptions for the installation of small wastewater treatment systems may be 
granted pursuant to Section 4.1 of the 201 Agreement , and the Applicant has applied for such an 
exception; 
 
WHEREAS, Section 4.1 provides in relevant part that an exception  may be granted if it 
“will alleviate a clearly demonstrable hardship and compliance with the Facility Plan will 
exacerbate this hardship.” Section 4.1 further provides that a "hardship will be determined based 
upon a cost/effectiveness study of sewer service provision to the property seeking the exception[;]" 
and 
 
 WHEREAS, upon finding that a demonstrable hardship exists under Section 4.1 of the 201 
Agreement, on March 3, 2025, the City Planning Commission recommended that the Governing 
Body grant the Applicant's 201 Exception to permit the installation of a small wastewater system 
on the subject property. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the City of Cheyenne hereby consents to the granting of an 
exception pursuant to Section 4.1 of the 201 Agreement to permit  the installation of a small 
wastewater system, as the exception will alleviate a clearly demonstrable hardship and compliance 
with the Facility Plan will exacerbate this hardship; and 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the City of Cheyenne hereby consents to the granting 
of an exception pursuant to Section 4.1 of the 201 Agreement on the conditions that the property 
be required to connect to sanitary sewer should it become readily available  and the  Applicant 
connect to the Board of Public Utilities water system.
