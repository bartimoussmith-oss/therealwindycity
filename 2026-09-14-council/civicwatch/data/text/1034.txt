 
 
RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION DIRECTING THAT PLANS FOR THE 15TH STREET 
CORRIDOR REVITALIZATION  PROJECT PRESERVE THE 
CHEYENNE GUNSLINGERS ’ CURRENT LOCATION, LAYOUT, 
BUILDINGS AND LANDSCAPING AT WEST 15TH STREET AND 
PIONEER AVENUE." 
 
WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Gunslingers are situated at Gunslinger Square located on West 
15th Street  and Pioneer Avenue , Cheyenne, Wyoming, and represent the traditional Western 
history of the City, providing entertainment to the Downtown district; 
 
WHEREAS, the 15th Street Corridor Revitalization Project ( 15th Street Project ), as 
presented during the January 31st, 2025 , Work Session of the City Council  indicated that the 
Cheyenne Gunslingers and Gunslinger Square would remain on 15th Street but move from the 
present location; 
 
WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Gunslingers ’ mission is to bring citizens and visitors to the 
Downtown district during the months of June and July along with providing additional foot traffic 
to Downtown businesses; 
 
WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Gunslingers are also important during Cheyenne Frontier Days 
to attract citizens and visitors to the Downtown district during the afternoon and evening hours; 
 
WHEREAS, the Gunslinger Square attracts citizens and visitors year-round and maintains 
the City’s traditional Western history; and 
 
WHEREAS, during the Christmas Season the Gunslinger Square is decorated for the Old 
West Holiday by Visit Cheyenne.  
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Cheyenne Gunslingers and Gunslinger Square shall 
remain at their present location on West 1 5th Street and Pioneer Avenue in Cheyenne, Wyoming 
with their present landscaping, layout and buildings; and  
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that City staff take appropriate steps to enable the 
Cheyenne Gunslingers to remain as described above.
