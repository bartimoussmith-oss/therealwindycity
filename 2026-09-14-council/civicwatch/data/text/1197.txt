BACKGROUND: 
 
The applicant is requesting approval of a preliminary plat for roughly 5.59 acres of land. The proposed plat 
would subdivide the parcel into 36 Lots for single-family residential. These Lots would be considered DD7 
Lots within the MR zone.

must make the following findings required in UDC 2.1.2.d  – a detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns 
identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.2.d.1) 
 
On the future land use map found in PlanCheyenne, the subject land and surrounding area has 
been designated as Urban Residential. The Urban Residential designation encourages a range of 
residential uses. The proposed plat is to provide additional single-family residential to the area.

Transportation Network (4.3): 
This plat is creating two additional streets that will add to the street network in the area and 
provide access to the new subdivision. Proposed blocks meet the block size requirements of 
the UDC. 
 
Open Space (4.4): 
The applicant has stated that this plat is within ¼ mile buffer of an existing neighborhood 
park which is located at the corner of Council Bluff Road and Mountain Road. Additionally, 
during of hours there is available recreational open space and playground equipment 
located immediately south of Trailhead Estates 1st Filing within the Anderson Elementary 
School grounds. This 1st Filing will also be in close proximately to future improved open 
space areas, some of which are described in more detail associated with the 2nd Filing 
(PUDC-25-35). Staff is satisfied with this explanation.  
 
Required Improvements (4.5): 
This plat appears to be able to provide all required utilities to the lots within this 
development.
