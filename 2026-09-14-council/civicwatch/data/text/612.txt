BACKGROUND: 
The applicant is requesting approval of a preliminary plat for roughly 1.88 acres of land. The proposed plat 
would subdivide the parcel into 9 Lots for sale of the individual buildings and piecemealing the 1/8th of the 
common lot to each building owner. The buildings that would be available for sale have already been 
constructed.   
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Preliminary Plat, the Commission 
must make the following findings required in UDC 2.1.2.d  – a detailed analysis is below: 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical
patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.2.d.1).
On the future land use map, the subject site is designated as Mixed-Use Commercial. 
PlanCheyenne allows this category to promote a number of land uses with an emphasis on 
commercial activities but also other employment, public, and residential uses. Residential is 
considered a secondary use within this designation and should be a high-density residential

2) The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to 
existing and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration 
of current and future streets, open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or

This plat will have no impact of surrounding development. There is multifamily development to 
the east and to the north. To the west of this site is a high school. These sites are already 
developed and operating with similar or supportive uses of this plat.  
The application complies with this review criterion.

Transportation Network (4.3): 
This plat is not creating any new rights-of-way. The only access to the site is from Grand 
Harmony Boulevard. Access from College Drive will not be possible for any type of 
development on this lot. A pedestrian easement exists on the plat to allow for public sidewalk 
on the site.  
 
Open Space (4.4): 
This plat has an approved site plan with the expected open space requirements per the 
Unified Development Code.

Drainage:

Traffic: 
A transportation study was submitted and reviewed. There were no concerns with traffic at the

The application complies with this review criterion. 
 
6) Lots proposed for development shall be buildable lots according to the existing or 
proposed zoning district standards, result in design and development patterns that 
meet the Intent and Applicability statements of the existing or proposed zoning 
district, and not aggravate nonconforming setbacks or currently adopted building 
and fire code access and separation requirements. (UDC 2.1.2.d.6) 
 
The site that is being platted is located within the CB zone district. Multifamily residential 
development is a conditional use within the CB zone district. This Conditional Use was approved 
in November 2024 to allow for multi-family development within the CB zone. While conditional 
use approval could require multifamily to meet alternative lot types (such as the MD1 or MD3), 
the approval for this project contained no such requirement. The lot type for this development is

approved site plan and are buildable in the CB zone. 
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
 
7) All parcels not proposed as buildable lots and which are not dedicated to and 
accepted by the City shall have a clearly defined entity responsible for ongoing 
maintenance.  (UDC 2.1.2.d.7) 
 
Eight of the proposed lots are buildable and have existing buildings on them. The ninth lot is 
proposed to be unbuildable and it is indicated on the plat it will be sold as 1/8th piece parcels to 
the other lots. It has been noted on the final plat that Lot 9 owners will be mutually responsible 
for funding maintenance of the lot.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.  
 
8) The application will not create any undue influence on existing or potential 
development for adjacent property considering the existing zoning and 
Comprehensive Plan policies for that property.  (UDC 2.1.2.d.8) 
 
This plat will not create any undue influence on existing or potential development. There is 
existing multifamily development on the proposed plat. There are existing streets surrounding a 
majority of the plat. Residential development to the east of the proposed plat is already 
developed.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
 
9) The application is not harmful to the public health, safety, and welfare. (UDC 
2.1.2.d.9) 
 
City staff is satisfied that this proposed plat would not adversely affect the public health, safety, or 
general welfare. 
 
The application complies with this review criterion.
