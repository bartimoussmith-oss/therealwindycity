 
 
ORDINANCE NO. ________ 
 
ENTITLED: "AN ORDINANCE DEFINING THE TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF 
EMPLOYMENT FOR SWORN OFFICERS OF THE CHEYENNE POLICE 
DEPARTMENT FOR JANUARY 2026 UNTIL THE END OF THE FISCAL 
YEAR 2026." 
 
 BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, 
WYOMING: 
 
 Section 1.  Definitions. 
 
 EMPLOYEE/EMPLOYEES – “Employee” or “employees” as used in this ordinance  
means sworn police officers, below the rank of chief. 
 
 Section 2.  Applicability. 
 
 The terms and conditions of employment as set forth herein shall apply to all employees as 
defined herein. 
 
 Section 3.  Purpose. 
 
 The purpose of this ordinance is to define the terms and conditions of employment for  
employees. The rights contained herein are in addition to any rights an employee may have under 
the Cheyenne Police Department Policy Manual (as that document may be amended at any time,  
before or after passage of this ordinance), and applicable federal, state and local laws. In areas of  
conflict between this ordinance and established federal, state or local laws, the latter shall govern. 
This ordinance comprises the exclu sive statement of employee terms and conditions of  
employment. Any other agreement with, or representation made to, any employee by a supervisor, 
agent or officer of the City of Cheyenne which is in conflict with or in addition to this ordinance  
is specifically disclaimed by the City of Cheyenne, except for the Cheyenne Police Department  
Policy Manual and except for agreements entered into by and between supervisory personnel and 
employees establishing specific job expectations, requirements or discipline which are part of the 
employee’s official personnel file maintained by the Human Resources Department. 
 
 Section 4.  Cheyenne Police Department Policy Manual and Benefits Policy. 
 
 The Cheyenne Police Department Policy Manual, including the benefit policy set forth in 
Exhibit A as they exist or are amended from time to time, are incorporated herein and made part 
of these terms and conditions of employment and shall apply only to sworn police officers. 
 
 Section 5.  Compensation. 
 
 The compensation paid to employees is set forth in Exhibit B , which is  attached and 
incorporated by reference herein. The compensation appropriate for the aforementioned employees 
shall be computed with reference to the grade of the individual employee. Assignment of

employees to the appropriate grade under the new plan will be made on a case-by-case basis, based 
upon employee hire date or promotion date, by the chief with the assistance of the director of 
Human Resources Department. The governing body for the City of Cheyenne  reserves the right, 
in its sole discretion, to modify or change pay scales. Employees will receive, in addition to basic 
pay, specialty on-call pay as set forth in Exhibit C, which is attached hereto and incorporated by 
reference. Employees will also receive, in addition to basic pay, longevity pay as set forth in 
Exhibit D, which is attached hereto and incorporated by reference. 
 
For any employee called to active military duty, the City of Cheyenne will compensate the 
difference between the employee’s City base pay and total military monetary compensation 
(commonly listed on the military leave and earnings statement as the total entitlements), provided 
the military compensation is lower than the City base pay. “Base pay” is defined as the regular pay 
the employee would have received from the City had they not been called to active military duty , 
not including on-call or specialty pay. 
 
 Section 6.  Continuation of Employment. 
 
 Any employee covered by this ordinance who continues his or her term of employment 
during and beyond the term of this ordinance shall be entitled to advancement annually (subject to 
the provisions of Section 5), as set forth in Exhibit B, upon recommendati on of the employee’s 
supervisor, if the mayor and council approve funds to pay the increases. 
 
 Section 7.  Health, Medical, Dental, and Life. 
 
 Each employee who participates in the city health insurance program shall contribute ten 
percent (10%) of the monthly premium based on the coverage level they select. This contribution 
will be made through payroll deduction. 
 
Beginning in Fiscal Year 2027, the City will continue to cover ninety percent (90%) of the 
health insurance premium for employees who complete the 2025–2026 Wellness Program by June 
30, 2026. Employees who do not complete the 2025–2026Wellness Program by June 30, 2026, or 
who choose not to participate in the Wellness Program, will receive an eighty-five percent (85%) 
contribution from the City and will be responsible for the remaining fifteen percent (15%) of the 
premium. 
 
 The City will maintain dental insurance for employees pursuant to the dental plan on file 
in the human resources department. The City will pay premiums for employees. If an employee 
wishes to maintain dental insurance under this plan for eligible dependents as defined in 
Appendix B of the dental plan, he or she may do so by paying the premium for the additional 
person(s) insured. 
 
 The City will pay premiums for term life insurance on all employees in accordance with 
the term life insurance plan on file in the Human Resources Department and City Clerk’s Office.

 Section 8.  Term. 
 
 The terms and conditions of employment set forth in this ordinance shall be effective as of 
the City’s January 2026 payroll . This ordinance will terminate upon passage of a replacement 
ordinance. 
 
 Section 9.  Repeal of Other Ordinances. 
 
 All other ordinances establishing specific terms and conditions of employment are 
repealed. 
 
 Section 10.  Severability. 
 
 If any clause, sentence, paragraph, or part of this ordinance or application to any person or 
circumstance shall for any reason be adjudged by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid, 
such judgment shall not affect, impair, or invalidate the remainder of this agreement. 
 
 Section 11 .  This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its passage and 
publication.

EXHIBIT A 
 
EMPLOYEE WAGE AND BENEFIT POLICY 
 
THIS POLICY COVERS ALL COMMISSIONED OFFICERS OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE 
POLICE DEPARTMENT. 
 
1. MONTHLY WAGES. 
 
 a. Wages. 
 
 All commissioned officers are covered by the City of Cheyenne pay plan. 
 
 All step increases shall be on the officer’s original date of employment or promotion date. 
 
 b. Lateral Transfer Program. 
 
 Lateral entry candidates must be currently certified as a Wyoming peace officer or in a 
state with a reciprocity agreement with Wyoming. If not currently certified in Wyoming, lateral 
entry candidates must take and pass the Wyoming POST challenge test, as well as the Wyoming 
criminal law and domestic violence courses offered through the Wyoming Law Enforcement 
Academy. 
 
 Subject to these requirements, the department offers a lateral transfer program for officers 
with prior qualifying law enforcement experience. At the chief’s discretion, qualified officers will 
be placed in the step program according to their experience as follows upon date of hire: 
 
  2 to 4 years of experience: Grade 201 - Step 3 
  4 to 6 years of experience: Grade 201 - Step 4 
  6 + years of experience: Grade 201 - Step 5 
 
 c. Specialty On Call Pay. 
 
 Eligible employees are entitled to receive specialty on-call pay. Employees are limited to 
receiving compensation for a maximum of two specialties per month. 
 
 An eligible employee is one who serves as a first-line supervisor (field training officer) or 
is required to be available for callout and does not receive any other compensation for being on 
call. 
 
 d. Longevity Pay. 
 
 Eligible employees are entitled to receive longevity pay based on years of service.  
Longevity raises will be effective on the employee’s anniversary month. 
 
2. OVERTIME. 
 
a. Patrol Officers and Patrol Sergeants. 
 
 All sergeants and officers who are called in for overtime duties will receive a minimum of 
two and one -half hours of overtime pay, excepting court appearances , which are limited to two 
hours of overtime pay or compensatory time if the time spent is two hours or less. All court time 
in excess of two hours is to be paid at time and one-half the classification rate. All patrol officers 
and patrol sergeants shall be limited to eighty hours of accumulated compensatory time, at which 
time, they must take paid overtime until they reduce their compensatory time below the eighty -
hour level. 
 
 All officers assigned to acting sergeant duties will be compensated two hours of overtime 
for each shift. All sergeants assigned acting lieutenant duties will be compensated one -hour 
straight time for each shift. 
 
 Should the acting sergeant, lieutenant , or captain duties extend beyond thirty consecutive 
days, the department may temporarily promote the employee  and place the employee in the 
appropriate pay grade of the new position. 
 
3. HOLIDAYS. 
 
 The officers and sergeants of the City of Cheyenne Police Department will be entitled to 
those paid holidays that are set by Personnel Rules and Regulations of the City of Cheyenne, as 
amended from time to time, and as enjoyed by personnel of other depart ments of the City, to 
include “Special Holidays” or other paid time off afforded to other City employees. 
 
 The rate of pay for these holidays will be computed on the basis of 2,080 hours per year. 
The officer working a holiday has the option of taking overtime pay or compensatory time at one 
and one-half times the classification rate. If the holiday falls on an officer’s regular day off, he will 
be given eight hours of straight pay or compensatory time. Likewise, if the holiday falls on the 
officer’s vacation, or while on sick leave, the holiday will not be counted as vacation time or sick 
leave. 
 
4. SICK LEAVE. 
 
 Each officer shall accumulate sick leave time at the rate of ten hours for each month of 
continuous employment to a maximum of six hundred forty hours. 
 
 Officers who have completed four years of continuous service and terminate from the City 
shall receive 50% of their accrued sick leave at their regular base rate, not to exceed 240 hours. In 
the case of retirement, an employee will be reimbursed fully for his or her accrued sick time up to 
240 hours. 
 
 Officers who have not accumulated 480 hours by January 20th will be entitled to twenty -
four hours of goodie time each year, provided they use not more than forty hours of sick leave  
between January 2 1st of the prior year and January 20 th of the current year . Officers who 
accumulate 480 hours  by January 20 th will be entitled to forty hours of goodie time each year, 
provided they use no more than twenty-four hours of sick leave between January 21st of the prior 
year and January 20th of the current year; or thirty-two hours goodie time each year, provided they 
use no more than thirty-two hours of sick leave between January 21st of the prior year and January 
20th of the current year ; or twenty-four hours goodie time each year, provided they use no more 
than forty hours of sick leave between January 21st of the prior year and January 20th of the current 
year. Goodie days under this section shall not be accumulated. Officers entitled to the goodie time 
provided herein shall make application to the Chief of Police who shall schedule the goodie time 
having regard for the convenience of the department . Upon termination, the twenty -four, thirty-
two or forty hours of goodie time are not paid as vacation. 
 
 For a period of one year following the date of an injury as defined in W.S. § 27 -14-
102(a)(xi) sustained in the line of duty for the Cheyenne Police Department, an officer who is 
totally disabled from performing any duties for the Department shall receive  a monthly payment 
from the City in an amount equal to the difference between: (1) the amount of any monthly benefit 
received by the officer from any division of the Wyoming Department of Employment; and (2) 
the average amount of monthly pay received by th e officer during the twelve month period 
preceding the date of injury, provided however, that if the officer has less then twelve months of 
service prior to the injury, his or her average monthly pay shall be computed on the basis of actual 
months of service. Upon the expiration of a period of one year following the date of an injury as 
defined in W.S. § 27-14-102(a)(xi), an officer who is totally disabled from performing any duties 
for the Cheyenne Police Department shall be required to use accumulated sick leave.  
 
5. SICK BANK. 
 
 The Sick Bank is to be used by officers in the case of serious illness or injury which is not 
covered under Worker’s Compensation, and after they have exhausted their accumulated sick 
leave. 
 
 The Sick Bank is to be established on the effective date of this policy with a balance of 
1,200 hours contributed by the City. The Sick Bank will maintain a balance limited to 1,200 hours. 
This balance is to be renewed by the City, if any days are used, on January 1st of each year. 
 
 A contributory pool (Sick Pool) of 4,160 maximum sick hours will be established on the 
effective date of this policy. Sworn officers with excess sick leave (over 640 hours) may contribute 
their excess sick leave to the Sick Pool on January 1st of each year. 
 
 The Sick Bank and Sick Pool are to be administered by the Sick Bank Board. This Board 
will consist of the Chief of Police, the City Human Resources Director , and the President of the 
Police Protective Association or his designee. 
 
 Policies and procedures for administering the Sick Bank and Sick Pool are to be made by 
the Sick Bank Board, including renewing depleted balances. These policies and procedures are to 
be in writing and available, upon request, to all sworn officers. Request for Sick Bank/Sick Pool 
hours are to be made in writing to the Sick Bank Board, in accordance with their policies and 
procedures. All requests for Sick Bank/Sick Pool must be approved by the Board prior to use of 
those days. 
 
6. VACATION. 
 
 Each officer shall be entitled to a vacation of eighty -eight hours after one year of 
continuous service, and shall be entitled to an additional eight hours of vacation for each year of 
uninterrupted service , provided that an officer is entitled to earn no more than 216 hours of 
vacation in any one year. Insofar as possible, vacation time shall be determined by division and 
squad, with seniority rank having preference within each division and squad. 
 
 Not more than 296 hours of accumulated vacation leave can be carried from one calendar 
year to the next calendar year. Accumulated vacation leave in excess of 296 hours on January 1 of 
each year shall be forfeited. 
 
 On separation from service, an officer shall be paid for all accumulated vacation hours 
earned and not taken, including the year of separation, proportionate to the time employed in that 
year. 
 
7. PERSONAL DAY. 
 
 Each full -time uniformed police officer with one year of continuous service will be 
awarded one personal day each fiscal year. A personal day shall be defined as  one scheduled 
workday. 
 
 This personal day may be used by the employee at his or her discretion, with supervisory 
approval. Personal days must be used in the year in which they are awarded. Personal days cannot 
be broken into increments of less than a normal working day. They do not carry over from one 
year to the next. 
 
8. REDUCTION IN FORCE. 
 
 Officers laid off in accordance with the provisions of this section will be eligible for rehire 
or reinstatement into a position of the same classification in the inverse order of layoff. If rehired 
or reinstated, however, no officer shall accumulate time in service during the period of lay off. An 
officer laid off under these provisions shall be protected for a period of twenty -four months from 
the time of separation, pursuant to state statute. The department shall notify protected officers of 
any vacancy as soon as known to the department. The officer shall notify the department within 
five days of his or her receipt of notice of his or her intent and must report at least fifteen days 
after receipt of notice. 
 
9. CLOTHING AND EQUIPMENT ALLOWANCES. 
 
a. Detectives shall be entitled to $80.00 per month clothing allowance. 
 
b. The department will provide all employees with the following equipment: 
 
  i. Uniforms; 
 
  ii. Ballistic vest; 
 
  iii. Leather gear; 
 
  iv. Handcuffs; 
 
  v. Baton; 
 
  vi. Duty t-shirts; 
 
  vii. Handgun; and 
 
  viii. Duty footwear. 
 
10. MEDICAL AND LIFE INSURANCE. 
 
 All eligible commissioned officers may elect to participate in any of the health insurance 
plans offered by insurance companies contracted by the City to provide medical and life insurance. 
 
11. EXTENSION OF MEDICAL COVERAGE. 
 
 a. All retirees may elect to participate in any of the health insurance plans offered by 
the City’s health insurance provider. Premiums will be paid by retirees. 
 
 b. If an officer sustains an injury as defined in W.S. § 27 -14-102(a)(xi) in the line of 
duty for the Cheyenne Police Department and is awarded permanent total disability benefits as 
defined in W.S. § 27 -14-102(a)(xvi), or if an officer dies as a result of a n injury as defined  in 
W.S. § 27-14-102(a)(xi) sustained in the line of duty, the officer and his or her eligible dependents 
shall be offered health insurance at the expense of the City subject to the following terms and 
conditions: 
 
i. The City’s obligation to provide health insurance coverage for the officer 
shall terminate when the officer becomes eligible for coverage under Medicare or 
Medicaid; 
 
ii. The City’s obligation to provide health insurance for the officer’s spouse 
shall terminate when the spouse re-marries, becomes eligible for health insurance coverage 
under any employer-sponsored health insurance program, or becomes eligible for Medicare 
or Medicaid, whichever occurs first; and 
 
iii. The City’s obligation to provide health insurance coverage for an eligible 
dependent of the officer shall terminate when that dependent reaches the age of twenty-six 
years, or becomes eligible for Medicare or Medicaid, whichever first occurs. 
 
 c. For the purposes of this paragraph (¶ 11), the word “spouse” shall refer only to the 
person to whom the officer is legally married at the time the officer is awarded permanent total 
disability benefits as defined by W.S. § 27 -14-102(a)(xvi) and the phrase  “eligible dependent” 
shall refer only to a child of the officer, including any child adopted by the officer before the child 
reached the age of fourteen years. Step-children, foster children, and wards of the officer under 
any guardianship or conservatorship may be eligible dependents for coverage under the plan 
documents for health insurance coverage provided by the City.  
 
 In the case of a qualifying event, the eligible person may change his or her election within 
thirty days of said qualifying event or wait until open enrollment. 
 
 The City of Cheyenne may, from time to time, require verification of permanent disability 
of a member, of full-time student status, or mental or physical disability. 
 
12. PPA BUSINESS. 
 
 With prior immediate supervisor and division head approval, officers may conduct PPA 
business during working hours. 
 
13. DUES FOR MEMBERSHIP. 
 
 The dues for membership in the PPA shall be set forth by the PPA by-laws. The City agrees 
to deduct this sum, as a payroll deduction, from such member’s monthly paycheck and remit these 
dues to the PPA. 
 
14. NEW LANGUAGE. 
 
 All proposed changes to the wage and benefit policy will be distributed in writing a 
minimum of thirty days prior to the effective date. The staff officers of the department will be 
available to discuss and explain and take any comments relative to the proposed change. All other 
policies will be distributed in writing and once each year updated in this manual. 
 
15. PENSION CONTRIBUTIONS 
 
 All sworn employees of the Cheyenne Police Department will participate in the Wyoming 
Retirement System Law Enforcement Pension Plan. Sworn employees will contribute 8.6% of 
their monthly salary to the Wyoming Retirement System. The  City will contribute the remaining 
balance of the monthly required pension contributions.
