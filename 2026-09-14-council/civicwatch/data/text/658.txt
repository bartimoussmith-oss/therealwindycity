RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION NAMING A TRIANGLE -SHAPED PARCEL OF REAL 
PROPERTY LOCATED AT THE INTERSECTION OF RANDALL AVENUE 
AND CRIBBON AVENUE AS THE ‘DR. ROBERT M. FARR PARK.’” 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne owns real property located at the intersection of Randall 
Avenue and Cribbon Avenue, upon which an existing triangle-shaped park is located;  
 
WHEREAS, Dr. Robert M. Farr’s life reflects a rare combination of professional excellence, 
military service, historic preservation, and selfless community care;  
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body recognizes that naming the park in his honor would 
constitute a fitting tribute to a man whose decades of dedication have enriched the City of Cheyenne 
and preserved its past for future generations;  
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body wishes to acknowledge Dr. Farr’s many contributions to 
Cheyenne and has received a petition containing approximately 300 signatures demonstrating 
community support for naming the aforementioned park in his honor;  
 
WHEREAS, it is appropriate for governmental entities to name public facilities after civic 
contributors in recognition of their efforts; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body believes it is most fitting to name the aforementioned 
existing triangle park the “Dr. ROBERT M. FARR PARK” in recognition of Dr. Farr’s contributions 
to the enrichment of the City of Cheyenne and its residents. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that  the property owned by the City of Cheyenne located at the 
intersection of Cribbon Avenue and Randall Avenue is hereby named the “Dr. Robert M. Farr Park.”
