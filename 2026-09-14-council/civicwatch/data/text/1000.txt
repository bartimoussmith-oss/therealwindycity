 
 
 
RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION DIRECTING THE CITY OF CHEYENNE TO LIST THE 
CHEYENNE AUXILIARY PUMPING STATION LOCATED AT 1504 
DILLON AVENUE, CHEYENNE, WYOMING  FOR SALE OR LEASE TO 
THE PUBLIC AS SPECIFIED." 
 
WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Auxiliary Pumping Station (Pump House) located at 1504 
Dillon Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming , is a unique structure that has historical significance to the 
Cheyenne community; 
 
WHEREAS, the Pump House represents an important chapter in the city's development, 
and holds cultural and historic significance, warranting preservation in the public interest; 
 
WHEREAS, the Pump House is currently abandoned, and despite repeated efforts to secure 
the premises, the site continues to attract vagrants and present ongoing security and public safety 
concerns; 
 
WHEREAS, the Pump House also represents a health hazard due to pest infestation, animal 
waste and drug paraphernalia; 
 
WHEREAS, the Pump House structure has recently been found to be sound and deserves 
to be brought back to life; 
 
WHEREAS, it is estimated that it will cost approximately $1,000,000.00 to replace the 
roof and prepare the Pump House structure to be mothballed until it can be restored; 
 
WHEREAS, it is estimated that restoring the Pump House structure and preparing it for 
use by a potential tenant would cost approximately $4,000,000.00; 
 
WHEREAS, the City has had entrepreneurs and business owners express interest in the 
Pump House for future business opportunities; 
 
WHEREAS, the City does not currently have a private partner or potential tenant willing 
to sign a  Memorandum of Understanding  or other type of agreement to help restore the Pump 
House or utilize it for the benefit of the community; 
 
WHEREAS, the City does not currently have the funding available to restore the Pump 
House and complete it to the point where the City could reasonably secure a tenant; 
 
WHEREAS, because of the challenges surrounding the restoration and redevelopment of 
the Pumphouse, it is in the City’s best interest to offer the property for sale or lease to better ensure 
it may be put to a beneficial use; and  
 
 
 
WHEREAS, in accordance with Resolution No. 6039, adopted by the Governing Body on 
March 9, 2020, as part of the sale of the Pump House, the Governing Body must decide if the 
proceeds from the sale of the property should be deposited in the Revolving Real P roperty Fund, 
which fund helps support the acquisition and construction of new real property for the City of 
Cheyenne. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that  the City of Cheyenne  shall obtain an appraisal  or market 
assessment for the Cheyenne Auxiliary Pumping Station  parcel located at 1504 Dillon Avenue, 
Cheyenne, Wyoming, as deemed appropriate by the City Department or staff member customarily 
responsible for such matters , and shall list the parcel for sale to t he public consistent with the 
requirements of Wyoming Statute § 15 -1-112(a) or for lease, subject to final approval by the 
Governing Body;  
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that any existing agreements between the City of 
Cheyenne and other entities related to the Cheyenne Auxiliary Pumping Station parcel that may 
impede the sale or lease of the parcel, or are no longer necessary due to the pending sale or lease, 
of the parcel, be cancelled, allowed to expire, or completed as appropriate to facilitate a timely and 
cost-effective listing of the parcel; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that as a condition of sale or lease, the purchaser or lessee 
shall be prohibited from demolishing the structure, shall not modify or alter the exterior of the 
structure in any way that would render it ineligible for nomination to the National Register of 
Historic Places, and shall ensure that any exterior rehabilitation, renovation or restoration complies 
with the Secretary of the Interior’s Standards for the Treatment of Historic Properties. In the case 
of a sale, these requirements shall be expressly incorporated into the deed and shall run with the 
land as binding covenants enforceable by the City of Cheyenne; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that as a condition of sale or lease, the City of Cheyenne 
shall secure any necessary and appropriate easements over the property and include any other 
terms and conditions in the sale or lease agreement as are necessary to promote investment in and 
redevelopment of the downtown area; and 
 
 BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED, that, in the event of sale,  all proceeds of the sale of the 
property be deposited in to the Revolving Real Property Special Revenue Fund to be expended 
consistent with Resolution No. 6039;
