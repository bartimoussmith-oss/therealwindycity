While the configuration of this intersection as a modern roundabout has reduced the severity of 
collisions compared to the previous layout of the intersection, which included multiple traffic 
signals, the number of collisions remains a concern.

current operations of the roundabout. A summary of the findings and recommendations will be 
provided in a technical memorandum. Based on the findings and recommendations, design 
plans will be prepared, and the proposed improvements will be let for bidding. Ardurra staff will 
provide limited professional services during bidding and construction.

Converse Ave. – Pershing Blvd. – 19th St. Roundabout Operations Review & Modification Design  
Ardurra Group, Inc. Scope & Fee Proposal 
 
The Converse Ave. – Pershing Blvd. – 19th St. roundabout was completed in December 2013 to 
replace the former layout , which had 125 collisions in the five -year period reviewed for the 
Alternatives Analysis prepared in 2006.  While the roundabout reduced collision severity, the 
number of collisions per year has been an ongoing concern.  In 2017 , the roundabout was the 
subject of a Cheyenne Metropolitan Planning Organization  (MPO) safety campaign.  In 2019 , 
MTJ Engineering reviewed the roundabout and provided several recommendations  within a 
report that aimed at improving driver comprehension and reducing collisions.  Ardurra has been 
tasked to review the recommendations, update and/or modify them as necessary, and provide 
a bid ready set of contract drawings and specifications targeted for bid advertisement in 
February 2027. 
 
This Ardurra Exhibit A outlines a scope and fee proposal that addresses the following consultant 
actions:  
• Develop an independent review of current roundabout operations and compare 
findings with the 2019 MTJ report. Summarize findings in a technical memorandum.  
• Develop drawings, specifications, and construction cost opinion to 60% and 100% levels.  
• Permit the 100% drawings through the City Engineering Plan Review (EPR) process. 
• Support the project through advertisement and bidding.  
• Construction Management Services (optional additional service)  
SCOPE OF SERVICES 
Task 1. Project Management, Administration, and Quality Control 
This task acknowledges additional budget for Tasks 1-6 for the project manager and 
administration staff to coordinate the project . This  includes City correspondence, meetings 
(beginning July 2026), invoicing, schedule coordination, and progress reporting.  Ardurra 
anticipates nine monthly meetings with agencies , and two review meetings  to support the 
project.  Quality Control reviews will be provided by senior, specialized staff for all submittals.  
 
Task 1 Deliverables:  
Two sets of review meeting minutes, nine invoices and status updates.  
Task 2. Field Data Collection  
Obtaining topographic survey data in support of drawing development. Survey will include 
signage location, pavement markings, existing roundabout geometry and drainage, and any 
marked utilities and utility structures. Additionally, Ardurra will coordinate with the City for 
footage of traffic operations during a period of both daylight and night time hours. 
 
Task 2 Deliverables: None.

Task 2 Assumptions:  
Boundary survey is not necessary, and in -depth utility research and coordination will not be 
required for this project. The City will provide a drone and pilot to capture traffic flow footage 
for two, two-hour periods.  
Task 3. Project Development 
Review current traffic counts, collision history, observed performance, and Cheyenne MPO 
forecast data to summarize findings for recommended improvements in a technical 
memorandum. SIDRA will be used to perform operational analysis. Compare these findings to 
the 2019 MTJ report and compare conclusions within the technical memorandum. Submit this 
memorandum to City Engineering staff and attend a review meeting to discuss findings and 
recommendations and 60% design parameters. 
 
Task 3 Deliverables: 
Technical memorandum and supporting data (i.e., traffic counts, collision history, t urning 
movements analysis results).  
 
Task 3 Assumptions: 
City to provide as-built drawings and design parameters for original roundabout construction, if 
available. 
Task 4. 60% & 100% Design 
Advance project recommendations, incorporating City feedback on the technical 
memorandum from Task 3, to the 60% design level. This includes preparation of construction 
drawings, technical specifications, and an opinion of probable construction cost. Submi t the 
60% design package to the City and conduct a design review meeting. 
 
Incorporate comments received during the 60% design review to advance the project to the 
100% (final) design level, including updated drawings, specifications, and opinion of probable 
construction cost. Submit the 100% design package through the EPR portal for City review and 
respond to all City comments.
