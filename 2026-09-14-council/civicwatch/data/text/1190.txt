 BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, 
WYOMING: 
 
Section 1 .  That Section  10.24.190, Critical Traffic Control Areas ; Creation and 
Administration; Enhanced Penalties, of Chapter 10.24, Rules of the Road Generally , of Title 10, 
Vehicles and Traffic, of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, is amended to read: 
 
10.24.190 - Critical traffic control areas; creation and administration; enhanced penalties. 
 
A. There is created the critical traffic control program, which shall be empowered to designate 
one (1) or more critical traffic control areas in the city of Cheyenne as provided in this 
section. The program shall be administered by the program committee in accordance with 
this section, which committee shall be composed of the mayor or his or her designee, the 
city engineer or his or her designee, and the chief of police or his or her designee . The 
objective of the program shall be to identify areas in the city with unsafe or unlawful traffic 
speeds or other behaviors and to implement appropriate temporary remedial measures to 
address those behaviors. 
 
B. No A critical traffic control area shall may only be designated under the program except as 
provided in this in accordance with subsection (C) of this section and only after the receipt 
of a critical traffic control program  complaint. The complaint shall be signed by not less 
than five (5) residents of the area that is the subject of the complaint, which residents shall 
represent different businesses or households  affected by the alleged unsafe or unlawful 
traffic speeds or behaviors. Complaint forms shall be made available to the public by the 
city engineer. Any complaint submitted under this subsection shall specifically identify the 
unsafe or unlawful traffic speeds or behaviors that are of concern to the residents. 
 
C. The program committee shall review and evaluate the merits of any traffic control program 
complaint submitted in accordance with subsection (B) of this section . Should the 
committee determine the complaint has merit, the city engineer shall order a traffic study 
for the affected area, the results of which shall be submitted to the committee not less than 
fourteen (14) days after the date that the study was initiated. If the city engineer ordered a 
study to evaluate vehicle speeds, the study period shall include not less than two (2) 
weekdays and not less than one (1) weekend Saturday as data gathering points. In addition, 
a crash history for not less than the previous five (5) years shall be obtained and a crash 
data analysis shall be developed and summarized. If the results of the study substantiate 
the complaint, the program committee may designate the affected area as a critical traffic

control area. Nothing in this subsection shall be construed to prohibit the city engineer from 
commissioning a third -party professional engineer to complete the traffic study required 
under this subsection. 
 
D. Upon the designation of any critical traffic control area as provided by subsection (C) (B) 
of this section and subject to the requirements of  subsection (F), the program committee 
may implement appropriate temporary remedial measures to address the unlawful traffic 
speeds or other behaviors including: 
 
1. Establishing enhanced fines for speeding violations within the designated area, which 
fines shall be two (2) times the amount of the fine that would be imposed for the same 
violation in the same or similar area but for the  traffic control area designation. If 
enhanced fines are implement ed, proper signage shall also be installed to provide 
reasonable notice to drivers, which signage shall identify the beginning and end points 
of the critical traffic control area. An  enhanced fine imposed under this paragraph  is 
mandatory and may only be reduced by a municipal court judge as follows: 
 
a. after the court specifically finds Upon a specific finding that the defendant has no 
ability to pay the full fine amount and that no reasonable probability exists that the 
defendant will have an ability to pay the full fine amount; 
 
b. Upon completion of a defensive driving cour se but only if the defendant has 
previously maintained a motor vehicle record free of moving violations  and the 
prosecutor first consents; or 
 
c. The defendant is entitled to a fine reduction under W.S. 31-5-1402(e). 
 
2. Installation of additional traffic control devices, including driver feedback radar signs, 
traffic control signals or traffic calming devices; 
 
3. Increased law enforcement presence for the purpose of strictly enforcing traffic law s, 
which increased presence may be incentivized through the authorization of overtime to 
be offered to police officers; and 
 
4. Any other appropriate temporary measures the committee determines may address the 
unsafe or unlawful traffic behaviors. 
 
E. Any critical traffic control area designated under this section shall be reevaluated by the 
program committee not more than fourteen (14) thirty (30) days after the implementation 
date of any temporary remedial measures to determine if the measures are still appropriate 
under the circumstances. Should the committee determine the unsafe or unlawful traffic 
behaviors have been corrected, the critical traffic control area designation shall terminate 
and the measures implemented in accordance with this s ection shall be removed. Should 
remain appropriate or should be modified or terminated. Record of the program 
committee's revaluation shall be filed in accordance with subsection (F) of this section. The 
program committee’s failure to meet within the requi red thirty (30) days shall not affect

fine implemented under paragraph (D)(1) of this section.  If the committee determine that 
the temporary remedial measures should be modified or continued, modifies or continues 
the remedial measures, the area shall again be reevaluated by the program committee not 
more than fourteen (14) thirty (30) days after the implementation date that the measures

F. Upon the designation of a critical traffic control area and the implementation of any 
temporary remedial measures under subsection s (C) and  (D) of this section , or the 
modification or termination of an existing area under subsection (E), the program 
committee shall file a record of the action with the city clerk using a standardized form. 
The form shall specify the remedial measures to be implemented, continued or modified  
and further state the effective date for the implementation, continuance or modification of 
applicable remedial measures. If a designation is terminated, the form shall specify  the 
effective date of the termination.  Upon receipt of  a record of action  form under this 
subsection, the city clerk shall provide a copy to the chief of police or his or her designee 
for the purposes of enforcement. 
 
F. G. Nothing in this section shall be construed to limit or otherwise affect the authority 
granted under title 10, chapter 10.68 of the city code or the authority of the city traffic 
engineer under section 10.12.130 of the city code.  
 
 Section 2 .  Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases of this ordinance are 
severable. If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase of this ordinance is declared 
unconstitutional or otherwise unlawful, such declaration shall not affect any of the remaining 
sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases. 
 
 Section 3.  This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon approval and publication.
