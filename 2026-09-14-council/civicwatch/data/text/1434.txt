BACKGROUND: 
 
This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from MR – Medium-density Residential to NR-2 – 
Neighborhood Residential Medium-density for roughly 0.50 acres of land. The land is currently developed with 
multifamily development in a townhouse style. Each unit has its own entrance. A concurrent plat application 
has been submitted to create fee simple ownership of each unit (PUDC-26-8).  
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical 
patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1) 
 
PlanCheyenne encourages growth within the Urban Service Boundary and the maximization of 
existing infrastructure. This property is within the Urban Service Boundary, in an area with 
infrastructure that is already existing.

The Future Land Use Designation for this property is Urban Residential and the surrounding area 
is Urban Residential, Open Space, and Public. According to the comprehensive plan the optimal 
City zones for the subject property’s future land use designation is any one of the residential 
zones, including NR-2. 
 
Based on the policies and future land use map, NR-2 zoning is appropriate at this location. 
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1. 
 
2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering 
existing zoning, the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies 
created under the guidance of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2) 
 
Surrounding properties are zoned MR, MUB, and P. MR and NR-2 zones are medium-density 
residential zones. The difference is that NR-2 zoning anticipates alley-loaded development, allows 
smaller lots, more urban styles of development, and has a higher design standard. NR -2 zoning is 
compatible with surrounding zoning. When the NR-2 zone was created, the official zoning map 
was never updated to change certain areas of the community to this zone. This area would most 
likely be best zoned NR-2 based on the development pattern of the area.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2. 
   
3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards 
and uses of the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject 
property for development according to the standards and uses of the proposed 
zoning district (UDC 2.2.1.d.3) 
  
This property is already developed with existing multifamily development. This change in zoning 
will allow the property to be replatted to create fee simple lots for ownership of the individual 
townhouse units. The property would be considered Cottage Lots rather than multifamily after the 
change to NR-2 and the replatting of the land.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3. 
 
4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed 
according to the Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed 
zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4) 
 
No changes to the street network are proposed. Accesses will not be changed with the zone 
change. Infrastructure (streets and utilities) exist in the area.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4. 
 
5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of 
the proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific 
standards. (UDC 2.2.1.d.5)

The NR-2 zone is intended for a medium density and mix of dwelling types that promote a 
consistent neighborhood character. All uses require full access to public infrastructure and city 
services. The regulations are designed to protect the neighborhood character of the district and 
protect the primarily residential use of land. 
 
The proposed zone is in an area that has a mix of dwelling types and uses that are on smaller 
blocks and have alley access.   
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5. 
    
6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for 
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options 
if any specific development proposed in association with the application is not 
executed after approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6) 
 
The NR-2 zone is a residential zone that allows for slightly more dense development than the MR 
zone. The property is currently developed with multifamily residential. The applicant is proposing 
to subdivide the parcel creating fee simple lots for each of the residential units which would then 
make the property a cottage lot style of development which is a permitted use within the NR -2 
zone. Even if the plat was not approved or recorded, the existing multifamily development would 
be allowed in the NR-2 zone, but would require a conditional use to expand, similar to the MR 
zone.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6. 
 
7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7) 
 
No major comments received in regard to the proposed zone.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7. 
 
8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare. 
(UDC 2.2.1.d.8) 
 
City staff is satisfied that this proposed zone change would not adversely affect the public  health, 
safety, or general welfare. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 8.
