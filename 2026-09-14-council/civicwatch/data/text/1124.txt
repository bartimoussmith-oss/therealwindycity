BACKGROUND: 
 
The applicant is requesting connection to BOPU sanitary sewer services and water services for property 
outside City limits. There is currently existing water and sewer in the area and multiple other outside user 
agreements. City water and sanitary sewer is currently adjacent to the property along the northwest side.  
 
The standard Outside User Agreement contract includes language where the applicant consents to 
annexation at the Citys request, with no specific date to complete the annexation. 
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
The property is currently zoned a PUD Planned Unit Development in the County.  
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
The request for an outside user agreement has no formal review criteria; however, the following 
elements should be considered when reviewing outside user agreements: 
- BOPU capacity 
- Needed infrastructure improvements 
- Fiscal cost/benefit to the City and the BOPU 
- Population served 
- Considerations on the effect on future annexation(s), including proximity to current and future 
City services 
- Conformance with the Comprehensive Plan 
 
The requested outside user agreement has minimal impact on BOPUs system capacity. The proposed 
use of the property is for industrial use. Water and sanitary sewer is available within Clear Creek 
Parkway.  
 
Property tax would increase, but the City would not have any income from the property tax in the area 
at this time. The proposed use is not residential and would not change distribution of funds based on 
population. The BOPU may charge a higher rate for outside users.
