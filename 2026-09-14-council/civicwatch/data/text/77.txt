 
RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE A 
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING WITH PARTICIPATING 
COUNTIES, CITIES AND TOWNS, REGARDING THE ALLOCATION OF 
IMPACT ASSISTANCE FUNDS ASSOCIATED WITH THE LARAMIE 
RANGE WIND PROJECT  APPLICATION BEFORE THE INDUSTRIAL 
SITING COUNCIL.” 
 
WHEREAS, on or about June 7, 2026, ConnectGen Laramie County LLC (“ConnectGen”), 
an affiliate of Repsol Renewables North America, Inc., submitted to the Wyoming Department of 
Environmental Quality (DEQ), Industrial Siting Division (ISD), a permit application in accordance 
with the Wyoming Industrial Development Information and Siting Act  (“the Act”) to construct 
and operate the Laramie Range Wind Project (hereinafter referred to as “Project”); 
 
WHEREAS, ConnectGen plans to construct and operate the Project on private and state 
leased lands in Laramie County, Wyoming. The Project site is located in northwestern Laramie 
County, west of Interstate 25, and spans approximately 41,220 acres (35,350 acres of private land 
and 5,870 acres of land managed by the Wyoming Office of State Lands and Investments ). The 
Project anticipates the generation of approximately 650 megawatts of wind energy; 
 
WHEREAS, a jurisdictional meeting was held on March 22, 2024, during which ISD staff 
determined the Project is subject to ISD jurisdiction based on the estimated construction cost, and 
that Laramie County, including the City of Cheyenne, and Platte County were defined as the study 
area; 
 
WHEREAS, ConnectGen proposes a two-phase construction schedule, with the first phase 
consisting of 400 MW, and the  second phase comprising the remaining 250 MW.  Initial 
construction activities are expected to commence as soon as 2027, with both phases operational 
by the end of 2029; 
 
WHEREAS, ConnectGen estimates that the on-site construction workforce will range from 
17 to 305 , with an e stimated monthly average of 131 on-site workers over the 24 -month 
construction period; 
 
WHEREAS, W.S. 35-12-109(a)(xiii) requires that ConnectGen provide in its Application 
"[a]n evaluation of potential impacts together with any plans and proposals for alleviating social 
and economic impacts upon local governments or special districts and alleviating environmental 
impacts which may result from the proposed facility[;]" 
 
WHEREAS, the evaluation of potential social and economic impacts identifies temporary 
increases in population, demand for services, and other community effects that may result in 
additional costs to local governments within the study area;

WHEREAS, recognizing these potential impacts, the  Act and associated DEQ rules 
provide a process for the allocation of “impact assistance funds” to affected local governments to 
offset such costs; 
 
WHEREAS, Section 12 of DEQ’s Industrial Development Information and Siting Rules 
and Regulations provides that, as part of the permit application process, the affected counties, cities 
and towns in the area primarily affected can agree to an amount and schedule for the distribution 
of impact payment funds and submit their agreement to the Division for submission to the Council 
for its consideration; 
 
WHEREAS, the local governments that will be primarily affected by this project desire to 
enter into an agreement to determine the amounts and schedule for payment distribution of impact 
assistance funds for the Project; and 
 
WHEREAS, the counties, cities, and towns  affected by the Project are preparing  a 
Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) regarding funding amounts and schedule for impact 
payment distributions, which must be finalized, executed, and submitted by the parties to the ISD 
on or before August 13, 2026. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Mayor is hereby authorized and directed to execute, on 
behalf of the City, a Memorandum of Understanding with the participating counties, cities, and 
towns affected by the Laramie Range Wind Project, regarding the allocation and distribution of 
impact assistance funds  in accordance with the Industrial Development Information and Siting 
Act.
