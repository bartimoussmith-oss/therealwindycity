BACKGROUND: 
 
This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from LI Light Industrial to BP Business Park for 
roughly 419.06 acres of land. The land is currently vacant. The land is just south of North Range Business Park 
and is planned to be an extension of the business park. 
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns 
identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1) 
 
PlanCheyenne encourages opportunities for a range of industrial employment uses as well as 
encourages working with community partners such as LEADS. LEADS over sees much of North 
Range Business Park and this is an extension of it.  
 
The Future Land Use Designation for this property is Industrial, Mixed-use Employment, and 
Agricultural / Rural along the south end of the property. According to the comprehensive 
plan the optimal City zones for the subject property’s future land use designation is any of our

Based on the policies and future land use map, BP zoning is appropriate at this location.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1. 
 
2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning, 
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance 
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2) 
 
The proposed BP zone is an extension of the business park to the north. The business park to the 
north is zoned BP with a few parcels being zoned LI. This area has developed with mostly 
industrial and commercial uses which match the BP zoning.  Surrounding this parcel is mostly 
vacant land zoned County A-2, which could be rezoned in the future to also match the BP or 
other similar zone districts.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2. 
   
3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of 
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for 
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC 
2.2.1.d.3) 
  
The BP zone is essentially a mix of the LI, HI and CB zones, which allow for many different 
industrial and commercial uses. Almost all uses allowed in the current LI zone would also be 
allowed in the BP zone. The BP zone provides more flexibility for large sites in terms of 
landscaping. Additionally, if this lot was to be further subdivided, the BP zone would allow for 
more uses than the current LI zone and would not require the parcel to go through more zone 
changes.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3. 
 
4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the 
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4) 
 
No changes to the street network are proposed. Accesses will not be changed with the zone 
change. Infrastructure (streets and utilities) exist in the area. Water and sewer will likely need to 
be extended to the area. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4. 
 
5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the 
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC 
2.2.1.d.5) 
 
The Business Park (BP) district is intended to support small and large-scale industrial 
development while providing essential commercial services along regional transportation routes 
in an industrial park setting. The district integrates manufacturing, processing, research and 
PUDC-25-123   July 7, 2025 
development, and shipping activities in addition to retail, dining, and convenience stores to 
enhance functionality and appeal for businesses and employees.  
 
The BP district is applicable to areas on the edge of the community that are intended to support 
large-scale industrial, manufacturing, shipping, research and development, and data center 
uses. The district is typically found close to major transportation routes, making commercial 
uses appropriate. The district shall contain not less than 320 acres of land (interrupted only by 
roads, railroads, highways and natural features (such as ridges and waterways). Specifically, 
large-scale areas within the future land use designations in the comprehensive plan of 
Community Business, Mixed-Use Employment, and Industrial – including Community/Regional 
Activity Centers. Areas currently designated Agricultural/Rural (either inside or outside of the 
Urban Service Boundary) may also be appropriate for this zone district if near to regional 
transportation routes and if development of the area extends appropriate infrastructure. 
 
The proposed zone is to extend the North Range Business Park and develop it with industrial 
uses. The parcel on its own meets the applicability of the BP zone as it is over the 320 acre 
requirement to be zoned BP. The property is on the outskirts of the community and near 
regional transportation routes. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5. 
    
6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for 
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options if any 
specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after 
approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6) 
 
The BP zone is essentially a mix of the LI, HI and CB zones, which allow for many different 
industrial and commercial uses. The BP zone will allow this property to develop with more uses 
than what is allowed by the LI zone.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6. 
 
7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7) 
 
No major comments received in regard to the proposed zone.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7. 
 
8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare. (UDC 
2.2.1.d.8) 
 
City staff is satisfied that this proposed zone change would not adversely affect the public 
health, safety, or general welfare. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 8.
