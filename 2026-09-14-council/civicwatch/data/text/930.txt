 
RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE SUBMISSION OF A FEDERAL 
MINERAL ROYALTY CAPITAL CONSTRUCTION ACCOUNT GRANT 
APPLICATION TO THE STATE LOAN AND INVESTMENT BOARD ON 
BEHALF OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE FOR THE PURCHASE OF A 
TYPE 6 WILDLAND BRUSH TRUCK WITH EQUIPMENT.” 
 
WHEREAS, the State of Wyoming’s Mineral Royalty Grant (MRG) program 
provides that federal mineral royalties may be loaned or granted to incorporated cities and 
towns, counties, and special districts for various purposes, including to provide an essential 
public service; 
 
 WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne desires to apply for an 
MRG Federal Mineral Royalty Capital Construction Account Grant to assist in financing 
the purchase of a Type 6 Wildland Brush Truck with equipment , at a total estimated cost 
of three hundred seventy-five thousand dollars ($375,000.00); 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body recognizes the critical need for this acquisition 
in order to maintain essential public safety services; 
 
WHEREAS, applications for the Federal Mineral Royalty Capital Construction 
Account Grant must meet the eligibility criteria set forth in the State Loan and Investment 
Board’s Rules and Regulations, and to the best of the Governing Body’s knowledge, this 
application satisfies those requirements; and 
 
WHEREAS, the program requires a fifty percent (50%) local funding match, and 
the City plans to provide one hundred eighty -seven thousand five hundred dollars 
($187,500) from the two thousand twenty-one (2021) Special Purpose Option Tax/Grants 
Match Fund for this purpose. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF 
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that a grant application in the amount of one 
hundred eighty-seven thousand five hundred dollars ($187,500) be submitted to the State 
Loan and Investment Board for consideration at its February 3, 2026, meeting, to fund fifty 
percent (50%) of the total project co st of three hundred seventy -five thousand dollars 
($375,000.00) for the purchase of a Type 6 Wildland Brush Truck with equipment; and 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Mayor is hereby authorized to execute any 
grant award documents, agreements, or related instruments , including any and all 
subsequent grant award documents, on behalf of the City, without further action by the 
Governing Body, provided such documents are consistent with this Resolution and the 
terms of the approved grant.
