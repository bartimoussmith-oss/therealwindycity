RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING THE CITY OF CHEYENNE’S 
PRIORITIZED LIST OF PROJECTS PROPOSED FOR POTENTIAL 
INCLUSION ON THE ONE PERCENT (1%) SPECIFIC PURPOSE SALES 
AND USE TAX BALLOT PROPOSITION TO BE SUBMITTED TO THE 
QUALIFIED ELECTORS OF LARAMIE COUNTY AT THE PRIMARY 
ELECTION ON AUGUST 18, 2026.” 
 
WHEREAS, Wyo. Stat. § 39-15-204(a)(iii) and Wyo. Stat. §  39-16-204(a)(ii) authorize 
any county to impose a one percent (1%) sales and use tax for specific purposes, subject to approval 
by the qualified electors in accordance with Wyoming law; 
 
WHEREAS, Wyoming law authorizes the establishment of reserve accounts in connection 
with the one percent (1%) specific purpose sales and use tax, and provides that funds deposited in 
a reserve account may be invested as other public funds pursuant to Wyo. Stat. § 9-4-831 and may 
be expended for specific purposes previously authorized under the tax proposal and for the 
ordinary operations of local government; and further provides that a reserve account may be 
designated as a maintenance and sinking fund for one or more specific projects, or as an inviolate 
account constituting a permanent or perpetual trust fund, with the principal invested to preserve 
the corpus and any investment earnings deposited into a separate account and expended as 
authorized by law; 
 
WHEREAS, in anticipation of a potential one percent (1%) specific purpose sales and use 
tax election in Laramie County, the City of Cheyenne considered a broad range of potential capital, 
infrastructure, and facility projects, but, recognizing the practica l limits of available funding, 
determined that a focused and prioritized list of projects was necessary; 
 
WHEREAS, in selecting and prioritizing the projects identified in this resolution, the City 
was guided by policy objectives aimed at supporting core municipal services and community 
needs, including public safety, transportation and street maintenance, par ks and recreation, 
facilities serving the public, and downtown and economic development;  
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne intends to formally adopt and 
transmit its prioritized list of preferred projects for consideration by Laramie County in connection 
with preparation of a proposed ballot proposition; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body finds that it is prudent and in the best interests of the 
City and its citizens to establish a reserve account for the deposit of revenues collected from the 
one percent (1%) specific purpose sales and use tax in excess of the amount necessary to complete 
the approved City projects, should such excess revenues occur, with such reserve funds to be 
invested or expended as provided in this resolution and in accordance with Wyoming law. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the following prioritized list of specific projects be adopted 
and presented to Laramie County for its consideration and potential inclusion on the One Percent 
(1%) Specific Purpose Sales and Use Tax ballot proposition to be submitted to the qualified 
electors of Laramie County at the primary election to be held on August 18, 2026: 
 
1. Fire Station #2 Replacement and Relocation Project – Replacement of the existing Fire 
Station #2 on Fox Farm Road and relocation of the station to City -owned property at 
503 West College Drive to better serve current and projected growth areas of the City. 
The estimated cost of the project is Twelve Million Dollars ($12,000,000). 
 
2. Fire Apparatus Replacement Project – Replacement of three aging fire apparatus with 
two multi-purpose response vehicles and one new 107 -foot heavy-duty aerial ladder 
truck, replacing units originally placed in service in 1998, 2001, and 2008. The 
estimated cost of the project is Five Million Twenty -Three Thousand Three Hundred 
Sixty-Four Dollars ($5,023,364). 
 
3. City Street Maintenance Project – Continued investment in proactive street 
maintenance to preserve roadway infrastructure, enhance safety and ride quality, and 
reduce long -term repair costs. Work to include crack sealing, patching, surface 
treatments, and mill -and-overlay projects. The estimated cost of the project is Nine 
Million Dollars ($9,000,000). 
 
4. Police Digital Equipment and Technology Project – Replacement and modernization 
of in -car cameras, body -worn cameras, and electronic control devices, along with 
associated digital evidence storage and investigative software. The estimated cost of 
the project is Three Million Seven Hundred Thousand Dollars ($3,700,000). 
 
5. Fire Equipment and Communications Replacement Project – Replacement of outdated 
fire department equipment, including personal protective gear and department radio 
systems, to maintain safety and operational effectiveness. The estimated cost of the 
project is One Million Fifty -Two Thousand One Hundred Twenty Dollars 
($1,052,120). 
 
6. Greenway System Maintenance Project – Repairs, upgrades, and maintenance of the 
City’s Greenway system, including funding for staffing, to ensure the system remains 
clean, safe, and accessible and to prevent more costly future repairs. The estimated cost 
of the project is Three Million Dollars ($3,000,000). 
 
7. Downtown Improvements Project – Improvements to various downtown facilities and 
infrastructure, including replacement of the Jack R. Spiker Parking Garage elevator, 
renovation of the Cheyenne Depot Museum, construction of enclosed storage areas for 
trash and recycling containers, and curb and sidewalk improvements. The estimated 
cost of the project is Four Million Dollars ($4,000,000). 
 
8. Police Firearms Training Enhancement Project – Construction of an enclosed training 
building with modular interior walls for simunitions-based training and resurfacing of 
the pistol range deck to improve safety and usability. The estimated cost of the project 
is One Million Dollars ($1,000,000). 
 
9. Greenway New Construction and Rehabilitation Project – Leveraging these revenues 
with other funding sources to support new Greenway construction and major 
rehabilitation projects, including the Yellowstone Road underpass rehabilitation, 
Parsley Boulevard extension, and the Cribbon connection. The estimated cost of the 
project is Three Million Dollars ($3,000,000). 
 
10. Municipal Building Remodel Project – Completion of a full remodel of the existing 
Municipal Building, following design and planning work previously approved and 
funded by the voters. The project includes improvements to public service areas, office 
space reconfiguration, ADA accessibility upg rades, and associated furniture and 
equipment. The estimated cost of the project is Twenty -Two Million Dollars 
($22,000,000). 
 
11. Johnson Pool Replacement Project – Replacement of the former Johnson Pool facility 
in south Cheyenne. Project planning is approximately 65% complete and this project 
allows completion of final design and construction of a new pool facility with expanded 
amenities. The estimated cost of the project is Ten Million Four Hundred Seventy-Four 
Thousand Five Hundred Sixteen Dollars ($10,474,516). 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the City supports inclusion, within any ballot 
proposition prepared for the one percent (1%) specific purpose sales and use tax, of authority for 
the City to establish a reserve account for tax revenues collected in excess of the amount necessary 
to complete the approved projects, with such reserve funds authorized, as permitted by law, to be 
invested in the same manner as other public funds pursuant to Wyo. Stat. § 9-4-831, to be expended 
for specific purposes previously authorized as part of the one percent (1%) specific purpose sales 
and use tax proposals, or to be expended for the ordinary operations of government; and 
 
BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED that this resolution and the foregoing prioritized list of 
projects shall be transmitted to Laramie County for consideration in connection with the 
preparation of a potential one percent (1%) specific purpose sales and use tax ballot proposition.
