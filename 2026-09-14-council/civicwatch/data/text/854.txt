RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION OF THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF 
CHEYENNE, WYOMING, DECLARING A VACANCY IN THE OFFICE 
OF A CITY COUNCIL MEMBER FOR WARD I AND ESTABLISHING 
PROCEDURES FOR FILLING SAID VACANCY.” 
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to Wyoming Statutes § 15 -1-107, a vacancy on the city council 
occurs upon the death of a sitting member of the governing body; 
 
WHEREAS, the Honorable Scott Roybal, duly elected Councilmember representing 
Ward I of the City of Cheyenne, was discovered to have passed  away on September 2 6, 2025, 
thereby creating a vacancy in the office; 
 
WHEREAS, Councilmember Roybal was one of the longest tenured members in the City’s 
history, serving a total of fifteen (15) years on the Governing Body; 
 
WHEREAS, throughout his tenure, Councilmember Roybal was deeply passionate about 
his community and dedicated to serving all who call Cheyenne home, consistently demonstrating 
integrity, compassion, and commitment to public service; 
 
WHEREAS, he will be remembered not only for his distinguished record of service but 
also as an exemplary colleague, a strong advocate for Ward I, and a dedicated public servant whose 
contributions have left a lasting impact on the City of Cheyenne; 
 
WHEREAS, in light of his passing and the vacancy created in the office, it is incumbent 
upon the Governing Body to follow the provisions of law to ensure continued representation for 
the residents of Ward I; 
 
WHEREAS, Section 2.04.040 of the Cheyenne City Code provides that the Governing 
Body must adopt a resolution declaring that a vacancy exists before filling such office, and further 
prescribes the procedure by which the vacancy shall be filled; 
 
WHEREAS, Section 2.04.040 requires the councilmembers from the affected ward to 
nominate and submit a candidate for consideration and approval by majority vote of the Governing 
Body, with the President of the City Council responsible for establishing the timeframe for such 
submission; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body recognizes its duty to ensure continuity of representation 
for the residents of Ward I and to act in accordance with the procedures prescribed by law. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the office of City Councilmember for Ward I, previously held 
by the Honorable Scott Roybal, is hereby declared vacant, effective as of September 26, 2025; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that in accordance with Section 2.04.040 of the Cheyenne 
City Code: 
 
i. The remaining council members from Ward I shall select and submit a nominee from 
that ward for the approval and appointment by a majority of the Governing Body to fill 
the vacancy. 
 
ii. Should the Ward I representatives be unable to agree on a single nominee, each 
representative may submit a separate name, and the Governing Body shall appoint one 
of the nominees by majority vote. 
 
iii. Notwithstanding, all nominees must meet the legal qualifications required for holding 
the office of City Councilmember. 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that, in accordance with Section 2.04.040(C) of the 
Cheyenne City Code, the President of the City Council hereby establishes a period of not more 
than thirty (30) calendar days  from the effective date of this Resolution as the timeframe within 
which the name or names of proposed appointees shall be submitted to the Governing Body; and 
 
BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED, that upon timely submission, the Governing Body shall 
consider the nomination or nominations at a regular or special meeting and appoint by majority 
vote a qualified individual to serve as Councilmember for Ward I for the remainder of the 
unexpired term.
