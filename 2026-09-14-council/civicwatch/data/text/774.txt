ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF AN OUTSIDE 
USER WATER SERVICE AGREEMENT  BETWEEN THE CHEYENNE 
BOARD OF PUBLIC UTILITIES AND WYOMING KING GOLD MINING 
COMPANY, INC., FOR SECTION 36, TOWNSHIP 14 NORTH, RANGE 70 
WEST, OF THE 6TH P.M., LARAMIE COUNTY, WYOMING.” 
 
WHEREAS, State of Wyoming is the owner of the real property in Laramie County, 
Wyoming, more specifically described as: 
 
Section 36, Township 14 North, Range 70 West, of the 6th P.M., Laramie County, 
Wyoming. 
 
WHEREAS, Wyoming King Gold Mining Company, Inc. (“Gold King”) has leased the 
above-described property from the State of Wyoming for purposes of developing and operating a 
copper, gold, and silver mining operation; 
 
WHEREAS, Gold King previously submitted an application to the  Cheyenne Board of 
Public Utilities (“BOPU”) requesting water service outside of the corporate limits of the City of 
Cheyenne for the leased property;  
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to Resolution No. 3577, adopted by the Governing Body on 
September 26, 1994, BOPU presented the water service application from Gold King to the 
Governing Body for review and consideration, and the application was approved on October 24, 
2022; 
 
WHEREAS, following that approval, BOPU and Gold King executed an Outside User 
Water Service Agreement dated February 3, 2023, which identified the proposed water source as 
new wells to be developed on the Belvoir Ranch; 
 
WHEREAS, because the Governing Bdoy approved the application, BOPU and Gold King 
Corp executed an Outside User Water Services Agreement, dated February 3, 2023; 
 
WHEREAS, it has since been determined that the Belvoir Ranch wells are not a feasible 
source of supply, and Gold King has requested to instead receive water service from the Crystal 
Reservoir and South Crow Pipeline for its proposed mining operations; and 
 
WHEREAS, to accommodate the change in water source, BOPU has reviewed the request 
and determined that execution of a new Outside User Water Service Agreement identifying the 
alternative source is necessary and appropriate. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities and Wyoming King 
Gold Mining Company, Inc. are hereby authorized to execute a new Outside User Water Service 
Agreement for water service to the property described as Section 36, Township 14 North, Range 
70 West, of the 6th P.M., Laramie County, Wyoming, which agreement shall supersede and 
replace the February 3, 2023, Outside User Water Service Agreement previously executed between 
the parties.

In the event of a water shortage for any reason, (including but not limited to drought, prior

12. Supply of Replacement Water:  Upon written noti ce to GK of a shortage of water 
available to the BOPU or City of Cheyenne, Replacement Water (defined below) may be 
supplied to BOPU by GK at a location and via a means and manner agreed to by BOPU and GK 
to avoid, in whole or in part, reductions to GK’s water supply. GK shall have the option of 
supplying additional raw water from alternative sources approved by and consistent with BOPU 
system operation ("Replacement Water") to the BOPU system and still withdraw up to 600 
gallons of water per minute from the South Crow Pipeline or Crystal Reservoir infiltration 
gallery on a gallon for gallon basis for the amount of replacement water provided by GK to 
BOPU, up to 600 gallons per minute.  If Replacement Water is provided, GK shall be credited 
for payments made to BOPU on a gallon for gallon basis up to 600 gallons of water per minute at 
the BOPU’s water rate in effect at the time the Replacement Water is supplied to BOPU for each 
gallon of Replacement Water supplied to the BOPU system, minus any losses. Any costs, 
expenses, or water charges incurred by GK in obtaining Replacement Water that exceed the 
BOPU’s water rate in effect at the time the Replacement Water is supplied to BOPU shall be the 
sole responsibility of GK. Any Replacement Water arrangement shall be documented in writing 
by the Parties, and whether to accept any proposed source of Replacement Water offered by GK 
shall be at BOPU’s sole discretion.  A suitability and/or blending study will be required for all 
replacement water that could be delivered to the Sherard Water Treatment Plant and must be 
approved by Region 8 EPA.
