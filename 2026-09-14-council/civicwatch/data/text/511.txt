ORDINANCE NO. ________ 
 
ENTITLED: "AN ORDINANCE APPROPRIATING MONEY FOR THE REED AVENUE 
CORRIDOR PROJECT." 
 
 BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, 
WYOMING: 
 
 Section 1.  The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne finds and declares that: 
 
A. Portions of the Reed Avenue Corridor Project are currently funded through revenues 
generated by the Sixth Penny Sales Tax approved by voters in 2017;  
 
B. Extensive and expensive planning, design and communication efforts by the City have 
taken place since that time , in cooperation with the Burlington Northern Santa Fe 
Railroad, to achieve completion of the Reed Avenue Corridor Project; 
 
C. The City C ouncil during it s most recent goal setting  meeting, determined that the 
completion of previous Council priority projects was to be  a primary goal in 2026 , 
which previous projects includes the Reed Avenue Corridor Project;  
 
D. In developing the proposed Sixth Penny Sales Tax ballot for the August 18, 2026 
election, the City evaluated numerous worthy capital projects competing for a limited 
amount of available Sixth Penny funding . Due to those funding limitations, the Reed 
Avenue Corridor Project was not included among the projects recommended for 
funding under the proposed 2026 Sixth Penny Sales Tax ballot; and 
 
E. The Governing Body therefore finds that the Reed Avenue Corridor Project remains a 
worthy and necessary public infrastructure project and that sufficient General Fund 
reserves are available to fund a portion of the project without adversely affecting the 
City’s fiscal condition. 
 
Section 2.  That the sum of five million dollars ($5,000,000.00) or so much thereof that 
may be necessary, and the same is hereby committed from General Fund Reserves  to the Reed 
Avenue Corridor Project  for planning, design, right -of-way acquisition, construction, and other 
related project costs.  No funds shall be expended from this commitment until a final agreement 
with the Burlington Northern Santa Fe Railroad has been reached. 
 
Section 3.  No portion of the funds committed by this ordinance shall be transferred or 
expended for any purpose not authorized herein and any unexpended, unobligated funds remaining 
from this commitment shall be uncommitted from the City of Cheyenne General Fund Reserves 
account on June 30, 2027.
