Background:  
Attached is the amended budget for ﬁscal year 2026 (July 1 to June 30) for the Cheyenne DDA. The proposed 
Governing Body review schedule is as follows: 
• Introduction and Referral: April 13, 2026 
• Finance Committee: April 21, 2026 
• Final consideration: April 27, 2026 
The adopted budget for FY2026 was $728,450, established based on prior years' operating expenses. As DDA 
operations and key initiatives expanded throughout the year, additional resources were required beyond the 
original budget projections. 
Wyo. Stat. 15-9-218 requires a DDA to adopt a budget, with administrative review conducted in accordance 
with each municipality's policies. The DDA has historically managed budget adjustments through quarterly 
amendments. Going forward, the DDA will align its budgeting process to ensure full statutory compliance on 
an annual basis. 
The proposed changes to the FY2026 DDA Budget are summarized as follows:  
Revenue 
• $5,000.00 increase to (405) Laramie County to account for a clerical error at original adoption (grant of 
$10,000.00 was awarded for FY26). 
• $92,824 increase to (450) Revenue from Reserves to cover increase to FY26 expense items 
Expenses 
• $4,000.00 increase to (510) Accounting & Audit for 2026 1099 ﬁlings and FY26 contracted CPA services 
not previously budgeted 
• $37,250.00 increase to (710) Downtown Planter Program for FY26 (to June 2026) expanded vendor 
scope (additional ﬂowers and planters) 
• $5,624.00 increase to (711) Downtown Banner Initiative for design (depending on Board sourcing 
decision) and printing costs for summer seasonal banners

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION APPROVING AN INCREASE OF THE BUDGET OF 
THE CHEYENNE DOWNTOWN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY FOR 
THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING JULY 1, 2025, AND ENDING JUNE 30, 
2026." 
 
WHEREAS, on July 9, 1984, the Governing Body approved Ordinance No. 2804 creating 
the Downtown Development Authority (here after referred to as “Authority”) and Downtown 
Development District (hereafter referred to as “District”); 
 
WHEREAS, the proposed budget for the Authority for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 
2025, and ending June 30, 2026, was approved by the Governing Body on May 27, 2025 under 
Resolution 6468; 
 
WHEREAS, Section 2.76.050(C) of the city code provides that the DDA budget shall be 
subject to alteration, modification and approval by the Governing Body in accordance with the 
policies of the City; 
 
WHEREAS, the Authority has identified an unanticipated increase of  ninety-seven 
thousand eight hundred twenty-four dollars ($97,824.00) in operational costs for the current fiscal 
year, resulting from staff turnover and the expansion and addition of programs that were not 
reflected in prior assessments; 
 
WHEREAS, at its March 19, 2026, meeting the DDA Board approved the proposed budget 
increase of ninety-seven thousand eight hundred twenty-four dollars ($97,824.00) for the fiscal 
year beginning July 1, 2025, and ending June 30, 2026 , a copy of which is attached to this 
Resolution as Exhibit A and incorporated herein; and 
 
WHEREAS, City staff has reviewed the proposed budget increase received from the 
DDA. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED  BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE 
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the proposed budget increase of the Downtown 
Development Authority for the Fiscal Year beginning July 1, 2025, and ending June 30, 2026, 
as set forth in the attached Exhibit A , entitled “Downtown Development Authority Budget 
Amendment – Fiscal Year 2026” in the amount of  ninety-seven thousand eight hundred twenty-
four dollars ($ 97,824.00), increas ing the total budget amount to eight hundred  twenty-six 
thousand two hundred seventy-four dollars ($826,274.00), is hereby approved.
