BACKGROUND: 
 
This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from PUD to NR-3 Neighborhood Residential-
High Density for 44.16 acres of vacant land.  
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns 
identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1) 
 
PlanCheyenne actively promotes growth within the urban service boundary, optimizing the use 
of existing infrastructure. This particular property is situated within that boundary, benefiting 
from established infrastructure in the area. The Future Land Use Designation for this site is 
classified as Mixed-Use Residential and urban residential, both supporting higher-density 
housing. The NR-3 zoning designation further facilitates the development of such housing, 
aligning with the goals of enhancing community growth and sustainability. 
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning, 
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance 
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2) 
 
The proposed zone change application is fully compatible with the area's existing regulatory 
framework and planning objectives. The surrounding environment is predominantly residential 
and public, which aligns with the intended uses outlined in the Comprehensive Plan. 
 
Additionally, the application adheres to the current zoning regulations and supports the 
community's vision for development, ensuring that it complements the character and 
functionality of nearby properties. Therefore, this application is consistent with the established 
zoning, the Comprehensive Plan, and other official policies designed to guide land use in this 
area. 
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 2. 
   
3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of 
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for 
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC 
2.2.1.d.3) 
The proposed zoning change application is most suitable for development under the new 
classification. This change would enhance the property's potential for diverse development 
opportunities and align with current market demands and the objectives outlined 
in PlanCheyenne. 
 
By transitioning to the proposed zoning district, the property can better accommodate modern 
housing needs and community growth, ultimately contributing to a more vibrant and 
sustainable environment than what is permitted under the existing zoning regulations. 
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3. 
 
4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the 
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4) 
The proposed NR-3 zone is intended to have smaller blocks and significant restrictions on front-
loaded housing – encouraging rear-loading property. Due to the large area of land proposed 
with this zoning and due to the currently-vacant nature of that land, there is plenty of 
opportunity for this area to meet the applicable standards of article 4 applicable to the 
proposed NR-3. 
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 4. 
 
5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the 
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC 
2.2.1.d.5) 
 
The intent of the NR-3 zone is to create high-density residential neighborhoods. This district is 
applicable in areas where high-density residential development is desired within the Urban 
Residential future land use designation. The context encourages NR-3 zoning to be within a 
UDC-24-219   December 2, 2024 
quarter-mile of activity centers, and in areas with a highly-connected street network. The NR-3 
zoning applies the design standards of 6.6 – including Compact and Mixed-Density 
Neighborhood Design and Cottage Lots. 
 
The application for the proposed zoning change effectively supports the proposed district's 
Intent and Applicability statements while striving to achieve the Design Objectives outlined in 
the specific standards. 
 
By aligning with the goals of the proposed zoning district, the application promotes smart 
growth principles and enhances the area's overall design quality.  
 
Furthermore, the proposed zoning allows for flexibility in design, which is essential for meeting 
the specific standards outlined in the zoning regulations. This flexibility will enable the 
development to incorporate innovative design solutions that enhance the neighborhood's 
character while fulfilling the community's needs.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 5. 
    
6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for 
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options if any 
specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after 
approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6) 
The application demonstrates a strong ability for all potential future development to meet the 
applicable standards for development in the proposed zoning district. This includes ensuring 
compliance with regulations related to land use, density, design, and infrastructure, which are 
essential for any development project. 
 
The proposed zoning change allows for flexibility in development options. If the specific 
development initially proposed with the application is not executed after approval, the property 
can still accommodate alternative development plans that adhere to the zoning standards. This 
adaptability is crucial for maintaining the property's viability and ensuring that it can evolve with 
changing market conditions and community needs. 
 
Overall, the application is well-positioned to support a range of development possibilities while 
ensuring compliance with the necessary standards, thereby promoting sustainable growth 
within the community. 
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6. 
 
7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7) 
 
No major comments were received regarding the proposed zone.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 7.

STAFF REVIEW: 
 
The property in question features a combination of mixed-use and urban residential designations, 
indicating that a more intense residential zone would be suitable. While it may not be within convenient 
walking distance to commercial centers, it is in close proximity to several schools, enhancing its appeal 
to families. 
 
Additionally, the property is located adjacent to York Avenue, which is classified as a collector street, 
facilitating access to surrounding areas. It's important to note that this proposed zone change would 
involve dividing a Planned Unit Development (PUD); however, the existing PUD is primarily residential in 
nature, aligning with the intended use of the area. 
 
CONCLUSION: 
 
Staff is satisfied that the application for the NR-3 zone meets the review criteria outlined in UDC 
2.2.1.(d).  
 
ALTERNATIVES: 
 
1. Recommend approval of the zoning amendment as submitted by the applicant. 
2. Recommend approval of the zoning amendment subject to conditions or modifications.  
3. Recommend denial of the zoning amendment based on findings of denial. The Planning 
Commission must conclude that the project does not meet all the review criteria. As part of the 
motion, findings for denial must be stated. 
4. Postpone the zoning amendment until issues identified during the meeting can be resolved. 
 
General Information Regarding Alternatives: 
 
If the Commission acts contrary to staff’s recommendation or modifies conditions of approval, the 
Commission shall cite specific reasons (based on the review criteria) for approval or denial. 
 
STAFF RECOMMENDATION: 
 
Staff recommends approval of the rezoning of the property from PUD to NR-3 Neighborhood Residential 
-High Density.
