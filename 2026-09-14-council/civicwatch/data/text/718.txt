RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION DESIGNATING SPECIFIED LOCAL BANKS AS 
DEPOSITORIES FOR MUNICIPAL FUNDS." 
 
WHEREAS, Wyoming Statute § 9-4-817 requires that municipalities keep their funds, to the 
extent they are not otherwise invested, deposited in national and state banks, including credit unions; 
 
WHEREAS, in compliance with state law, the City of Cheyenne annually designates local 
depositories for its funds; 
 
WHEREAS, applications have been received from ANB, First Interstate Bank, FNBO, 
Pinnacle Bank, Platte Valley Bank, US Bank, Wells Fargo Bank, and Wyoming Bank and Trust to 
serve as depositories of City funds for a one-year period beginning January 1, 2026; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body desires to designate the foregoing financial institutions as 
depositories of City funds upon the delivery of appropriate security for the protection of said funds. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that ANB, First Interstate Bank, FNBO, Pinnacle Bank, Platte Valley 
Bank, U.S. Bank, Wells Fargo Bank, and Wyoming Bank & Trust are hereby designated as 
depositories of City funds. This designation shall commence on January 1, 2026, and continue for a 
period of one (1) year. No monies may be deposited by the City Treasurer except in the financial 
institutions so designated. 
 
 BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the City Treasurer is authorized to place investments on 
behalf of the City of Cheyenne in those instruments authorized under Wyoming Statute § 9-4-831(a), 
and to enter into agreements with the designated depositories and appropriate investment entiti es, 
subject to review by the City Attorney. 
 
BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED that this Resolution shall be in full force and effect from and 
after its passage in accordance with the laws of the State of Wyoming.
