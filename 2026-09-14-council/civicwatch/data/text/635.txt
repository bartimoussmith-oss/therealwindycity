MEMORANDUM  
 
TO:  Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council 
FROM:  TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager 
DATE:  April 8, 2026 
SUBJECT: Consideration of Bid #E-15-26 for furnishing 1.5 Cubic Yard Rear-Load 
Dumpsters for the Sanitation Division 
 
Four (4) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.   
 
The Four (4) bids were received from: 
1. Fleet Innovations LLC. 
2. Kois Brothers Equipment 
3. Royal Media Network Inc. 
4. Wastequip Manufacturing Company LLC (Rejected) 
 
The bids were reviewed by Austin Reiner (Sanitation), Craig LaVoy (Public Works), and 
Vicki Nemecek (Public Works).   
 
The bid submitted by Wastequip Manufacturing Company LLC has been rejected, as 
their Rear-Load Container did not meet all of the required specifications.   
 
Specifically:  
 
1. Their container was 47.75” tall, instead of the maximum of 45” tall as called out 
in the specifications.   
2. Their washer on the trunnion bar to keep the container in place while it is being 
dumped was only 2.125”, and the specification required 3” minimum.  
 
Kois Bros Equipment was the next lowest responsive bidder. 
 
The total requested bid award for Kois Bros Equipment is $88,472.00. 
 
Funding:   Solid Waste Fund / Small Equipment
