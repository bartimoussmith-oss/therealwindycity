BACKGROUND: 
 
This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from County A-1 to AG Agricultural for roughly 
14.47 acres of land. The land is currently vacant. This parcel was annexed to the City in 1890. Since then it has 
had a County zone and was never given a City zone when the City and County separated. The applicant is 
proposing the AG zone to allow a temporary Work Camp through the Conditional Use process. If a property 
with County A-1 zoning was annexed to the City, the assigned zoning upon annexation would be AG 
Agricultural. 
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns 
identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1) 
 
PlanCheyenne encourages growth within the Urban Service Boundary and the maximization of 
existing infrastructure. This property is within the Urban Service Boundary, in an area with 
existing infrastructure.

While the proposed AG zone is not consistent with the land uses identified by the Future Land 
Use Map, the AG zone is the rough equivalent of the current County zone and would not have 
an adverse effect on the potential for the property to develop in accordance with the plans and 
policies of the comprehensive plan in the future. The AG zone is also intended to be a ‘holding 
zone’. 
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1. 
 
2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning, 
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance 
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2) 
 
The future land uses anticipated by the Comprehensive Plan for this area anticipate mixed-use 
commercial types of development. While the proposed AG zone is not consistent with the 
Future Land Use Map, the AG zone is intended to be able to be used as a ‘holding zone’ until 
future development occurs, and the proposed AG zone is consistent with the existing County 
zoning.   
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 2. 
   
3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of 
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for 
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC 
2.2.1.d.3) 
  
The current zone is County A-1 which, outside of City limits, allows for limited development 
options and is intended for lower intensity uses. Work Camps are a conditional use in the 
County A-1 zone. The AG zone is the most analogues City zone to the current County zone. If this 
parcel was to be annexed under current UDC regulations it would have been assigned the AG 
zone.   
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3. 
 
4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the 
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4) 
 
The zone change is occurring in a pre-existing neighborhood. No changes to the street network 
are proposed. Infrastructure (streets and utilities) exist in the area. 
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 4. 
 
5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the 
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC 
2.2.1.d.5) 
 
The AG zone is intended to develop with primarily agricultural uses or to leave land in a natural 
state. This zone prohibits most of the typical urban-style uses.  The AG zone is applicable to 
areas where significant natural lands or open spaces are to be protected, or areas designated 
Agriculture/Rural or Rural Residential by the Future Land Use Map. The AG zone is also 
applicable as an interim or holding zone for areas that may be further developed in the long 
UDC-24-216   December 2 , 2024 
term, but where minimal development activity in the interim is acceptable and will not 
prematurely establish development patterns. 
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 5. 
    
6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for 
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options if any 
specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after 
approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6) 
 
The specific development proposal which is the impetus behind this request is a Work Camp. A 
Work Camp is a conditional use in the County A-1 zone and the City AG zone. The proposed AG 
zoning is the zone most similar to the existing zoning on the property. More intense 
development on the property zoned AG would require a future rezoning.   
   
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6. 
 
7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7) 
 
Planning and Development Department Staff would like to note that if this property was to be 
annexed and assigned a City zone under today’s code, it would have been assigned the AG zone.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 7. 
 
8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare. (UDC 
2.2.1.d.8) 
 
City staff is satisfied that this proposed zone change would not adversely affect the public 
health, safety, or general welfare. 
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 8.

The AG zone is the most analogous City zone to the current County zone and would have been assigned 
to the property if it were to be annexed under current code requirements.
