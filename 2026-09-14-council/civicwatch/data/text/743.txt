RECOMMENDED MOTION: 
 
Move to recommend the Governing Body approve the proposed zoning designation of AG Agriculture 
for the parcel of land being annexed, located generally east of Converse Ave and north of East Carlson St. 
 
APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS: 
 
• UDC 2.2.8 Assigned Zoning 
 
BACKGROUND: 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1. The proposed zoning assignment is the most analogous City zone to the current zone 
when taking into consideration allowed uses, current uses, or surrounding City zones. 
Zoning shall be assigned a City zone based on: 
a. Permitted uses, 
b. Uses that could reasonably be anticipated for conditional use approval 
under the current zoning, 
c. Current uses, or 
d. Surrounding City zoning, 
as deemed most suitable for the property being assigned a zone in terms of allowing 
the landowner to continue the current use of the land while respecting the current and 
anticipated uses of surrounding property. 
 
The underlying county zone is County LU. The property is currently used for agriculture and the 
adjacent City zones include CB community Business and NR-3 Neighborhood Residential – High 
Density. The CB zone is to the southeast and is undeveloped. The NR-3 zone is to the southwest and 
northeast and in both cases is developed with multifamily. A majority of the surrounding land is 
outside of City limits (to the west, north, and east) and the property is currently zoned LU in the 
county and used for agricultural purposes. While the LU zone allows a large variety of uses with 
administrative or conditional use approval, the current land use in the area is agriculture. Based on the 
current use of the subject property and surrounding County land, a City agricultural zone is 
appropriate. As the AG zone is also intended to be used as a ‘holding zone; Staff selected the City AG 
zone as the most analogous City zone to the current County zone and uses. 
 
Establishing the City zone matches the policy document, and the parcel is assigned the most 
analogous City zone based on the allowed uses; therefore, the item complies with review criterion.
