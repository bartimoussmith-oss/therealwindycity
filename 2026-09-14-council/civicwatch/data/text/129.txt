APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS: 
 
• UDC 2.2.8 Assigned Zoning 
 
BACKGROUND: 
This is land-owner petition to annex the property into the City. 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1. The proposed zoning assignment is the most analogous City zone to the current zone 
when taking into consideration allowed uses, current uses, or surrounding City zones. 
Zoning for standard county zones shall be assigned based on the policy document, 
while zones not listed on the policy document and terminated City zones shall be 
assigned a City zone on a case-by-case basis shall be assigned a City zone based on: 
a. Permitted uses, 
b. Uses that could reasonably be anticipated for conditional use approval 
under the current zoning, 
c. Current uses, or 
d. Surrounding City zoning, 
as deemed most suitable for the property being assigned a zone in terms of allowing 
the landowner to continue the current use of the land while respecting the current and 
anticipated uses of surrounding property. 
 
This parcel is currently zoned LU – Land Use within the County. City Rights-of-Way surrounds the 
property on three sides. Parcels surrounding the property to the east, west, and south is zoned 
County LU. City MR Medium-density Residential is located to the north of the property. Other City 
zoned lots in the area are zoned AR – Agricultural Residential. The lot is currently being used for 
residential use.  
 
Lots that are similar in size and location to the subject parcel have been zoned AR – Agricultural 
Residential to maintain the uses of the properties. While the current use might not involve agricultural 
activity, the parcel is large enough to sustain limited agricultural uses and should be zoned to 
accommodate such potential uses.  
 
This assigned zoning complies with review criterion 1.
