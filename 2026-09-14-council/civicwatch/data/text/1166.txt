RESOLUTION NO. _________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE A 
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING WITH CHEYENNE FRONTIER 
DAYS, INC., FOR THE PROVISION OF PUBLIC SAFETY SERVICES 
DURING THE ANNUAL FRONTIER DAYS EVENTS.” 
 
WHEREAS, Cheyenne Frontier Days (CFD) is a ten -day annual event that attracts 
significant attendance and increases activity throughout the City of Cheyenne; 
 
WHEREAS, the popularity of the event requires enhanced public safety services to ensure 
the health and safety of participants, spectators, and the community; 
 
WHEREAS, the City and CFD have previously entered into a Memorandum of 
Understanding (MOU) for the provision of such services, with compensation from CFD to cover 
the City’s costs; 
 
WHEREAS, under the proposed MOU, attached hereto, CFD will continue to reimburse 
the City for public safety services with increased payment amounts to reflect rising service costs;  
and 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body finds that entering into the MOU is in the best interest 
of the City to support a safe and successful Cheyenne Frontier Days event. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of the City of 
Cheyenne, Wyoming that the City is authorized to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding 
with Cheyenne Frontier Days, Inc. for the provision of public safety services during the Cheyenne 
Frontier Days events. 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that t he Mayor is authorized to act as the City’s official 
representative and to execute the MOU on behalf of the City.

MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING  
between Cheyenne Frontier Days and  
the City of Cheyenne for Public Safety Services at Frontier Park 
 
Cheyenne Frontier Days (“CFD”) and the City of Cheyenne (“City”) hereby enter into this 
Memorandum of Understanding for delivery and receipt of Public Safety Services at Frontier 
Park ( hereafter the “Agreement”) during the yearly Cheyenne Frontier Days Event held in 
Cheyenne, Wyoming. CFD and the City hereby agree as follows: 
 
 1. For purposes of this Agreement, the following definitions shall apply: 
 
  a. “Frontier Days” means the  yearly event held in Cheyenne, Laramie 
County, Wyoming that starts approximately mid-July and runs for ten (10) 
consecutive days. 
 
  b. “Frontier Park” means the location for Frontier Days , including rodeos, 
carnival, night shows  and other activities . Frontier Park is located 
immediately adjacent to Lions Park operated by the City and is  bounded 
on the East by Carey Avenue, on the North by Kennedy Road, on the West 
by Hynds Boulevard, and on the South by 8th Avenue. 
 
  c. “Effective Date” shall be the date of the last signature is affixed to this 
Agreement. 
 
 2. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for 
five (5) years from the Effective Date. 
 
 3. The City of Cheyenne shall provide public safety related services at Frontier Park 
during Frontier Days in accordance with the incident action plan (including, but 
not limited to, a malt beverage sales and control plan) mutually agreed to by the 
City and CFD. Public safety related activities shall include police, fire, and 
emergency protection for Frontier Days as dictated by the needs of each day of 
the event and the unique characteristics of the activities occurring on that day. 
This Agreement in no way limits the discretion of the City in supplying additional 
City police, fire or other emergency resources including specialized groups of 
Cheyenne Police Department officers dedicated as  bomb squads, S.W.A.T. 
Teams, or other law enforcement assets necessary to provide public safety at 
Frontier Days. While assigned to Frontier Park, City public safety personnel shall 
at all times remain employee s of the City acting within the performance of their 
duties and the scope of their employment with the City and shall be under the sole 
supervision and control of their respective commander (s). City employees shall 
not act a s agents of CFD. The sole responsibility of the public safety personnel 
while so assigned shall be to enforce the ordinances of the City and statutes of the 
 2 
State of Wyoming. The duties assumed by the City, the Cheyenne Police 
Department, and Cheyenne Fire Rescue are duties owed to the public. 
 
 4. In exchange for public safety services provided under this Agreement, CFD 
agrees to pay the City the following amounts for each year of the five  (5) year 
term: 
− $85,400.00 for 2025;  
− $90,400.00 for 2026;  
− $95,400.00 for 2027; 
− $100,400.00 for 2028;  
− $105,400.00 for 2029. 
CFD will pay one -half of the yearly amount on or before July 1 st of each year, 
and the balance of the outstanding amount on or before the commencement of 
Frontier Days each year.  The payments specified in this section help offset the 
added public safety costs caused by Frontier Days , including for additional 
personnel and equipment supplied to Frontier Park . The City shall ensure that the 
cost for providing public safety services meets or exceeds the total amount paid 
by CFD each year. Any requirements imposed by the City of Cheyenne Fire Code 
are separate from this Agreement  and any fees or additional services that may be 
required under the Fire Code are not subject to this Agreement. Any person 
seeking a permit for some activity requiring permit ting under the Fire Code  will 
pay the required fees or for the additional services.  
 
 5. The City shall be solely responsible for security and public safety functions 
related to the Pancake Breakfasts and Parades that are held by the City of 
Cheyenne during the Frontier Days event. 
 
 6. The City and CFD shall confer no later than June 15 th of each year of this 
Agreement to assess necessary public safety measures and reach an agreement on 
the incident action plan for that year.  Upon completion of Frontier Days each 
year, CFD and the City shall meet to discuss after -action planning that evaluates 
the efficacy of the public safety services and activities that were carried out that 
year. 
 
 7. The City hereby assumes sole and exclusive responsibility for any and all liability 
arising from police, fire, or emergency protection services   provided by its 
employees under this agreement, as long as those services are provided within the 
scope of their employment . This Agreement provides no indemnification to the 
City by CFD regarding the actions of the City during Frontier Days. 
 
 8. CFD hereby assumes sole and exclusive responsibility for any and all liability 
associated with CFD volunteers, contractors, employees or agents of CFD during 
Frontier Days. This Agreement provides no indemnification to CFD by the City 
regarding the actions of the CFD volunteers, employees, contractors or agents 
during Frontier Days.
