BACKGROUND: 
The current project, spanning 720.0 acres of land, is a mix of City HI: Heavy Industrial, City AG: 
Agricultural, and County A-2: Agricultural zoning. The County A-2 zoning portion is property which 
is currently being annexed and the property will be AG: Agricultural by the time the Ordinance for 
this zone change passes. The BP: Business Park zone is a new zone district and most of this 
business park was zoned prior to the existence of the BP zone. This zone change would allow most 
of this existing business park to become zoned BP.  
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns 
identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1)  
 
For the area in which the subject site is located, the proposed BP: Business Park zoning is in line 
with the Comprehensive plan policy Foundation 3-Fostering a vital economy and activity 
centers, as the plan is its focus on providing new employment business parks, regional 
commercial activity centers, and mixed-use commercial activity centers as well as its emphasis 
on breathing new life into established centers and neighborhoods throughout the Cheyenne

Area which each offer a unique identity and collection of amenities. A majority of the land 
within the zone change area is designated Industrial by the future land use map, which supports 
uses that require access to regional transportation corridors and require buffering from the 
community. 
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1. 
 
2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning, 
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance 
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2) 
 
The surrounding zones include Agricultural (County A-2 and City AG) and Industrial zones (City HI 
and LI). The proposed Business Park (BP) zoning is a suitable transitional use. To the north and 
west, there are existing and planned industrial zones, which align with the goals of a Business 
Park. While there are agricultural zones to the south and east, the industrial areas nearby 
suggest the Business Park will fit naturally into the area, rather than creating an isolated area. 
The Comprehensive Plan supports the development of new business parks, further confirming 
the plan's compatibility with the area's growth goals. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2. 
   
3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of 
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for 
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC 
2.2.1.d.3)  
 
The property is currently zoned for both Heavy Industrial and Agricultural use. While these 
zones permit some industrial and agricultural activities, the large 720-acre size and its proximity 
to existing industrial areas make it suitable for more productive uses. The proposed Business 
Park (BP) zoning would allow a broader range of activities, like offices, light manufacturing, 
research, and commercial services. This would lead to more job opportunities and increased tax 
revenue, making it a better fit for the property. The new zoning would enable more efficient and 
beneficial development, aligning with the city’s growth and economic objectives. 
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3. 
 
4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the 
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)  
 
The proposed BP: Business Park zoning district includes large acreages without maximum block 
sizes. Subdivisions created under the BP zone in this area should be able to meet all block size, 
access, and infrastructure standards.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4. 
 
 
 
 
PUDC-25-115   July 7, 2025 
5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the 
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC 
2.2.1.d.5)  
 
The Intent of the BP: Business Park district is to provide areas suitable for a variety of office, 
research, and development, as well as light manufacturing and supporting commercial uses, in a 
industrial park setting, promoting economic vitality and high-quality development. Given the 
large size of the subject property, it is well-suited to support the intent of the BP district by 
accommodating a range of compatible businesses. The scale of the project enables 
comprehensive site planning to achieve the design objectives, including the creation of 
attractive building facades, significant landscaping buffers, internal pedestrian and vehicular 
networks, and effective stormwater management. The applicant's proposal for BP zoning would 
align with these design principles, promoting a cohesive and aesthetically pleasing environment. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5. 
    
6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for 
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options if any 
specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after 
approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6)  
 
Even if the specific Bison Business Park Expansion project as currently envisioned does not 
proceed, the proposed BP: Business Park zoning district includes a comprehensive set of 
development standards, including use limitations, bulk regulations (setbacks, height, lot 
coverage), parking requirements, and landscaping standards. Any future development under 
this zoning would be required to comply with these established regulations. The BP district is 
designed to accommodate a variety of compatible uses, including warehousing, manufacturing, 
data centers, office, and commercial uses. 
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6. 
 
7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7) 
 
Through the review process, the only recommendation received was encouragement to extend 
the BP zoning to the rest of the business park to the north and west of the subject properties. 
This could be included in this item or with (a) subsequent request(s). 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7. 
 
8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare. (UDC 
2.2.1.d.8) 
 
City staff are satisfied that this proposed zone change would not adversely affect public health, 
safety, or general welfare. The proposed BP: Business Park zoning is designed to accommodate 
uses that are generally compatible with surrounding areas and that are subject to 
comprehensive development standards. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 8.

STAFF REVIEW: 
Staff have thoroughly reviewed the application for a Zoning Map Amendment from City HI: Heavy 
Industrial, City AG: Agricultural, and County A-2: Agricultural to City BP: Business Park. Based on the 
analysis of the application's conformance with the UDC review criteria (2.2.1.d.1 through 2.2.1.d.8), staff 
finds that the proposed zone change is consistent with the City's Comprehensive Plan, compatible with 
surrounding land uses and zoning, and suitable for the proposed development type. All required findings 
for the approval recommendation can be made. 
 
CONCLUSION: 
Based on the foregoing analysis, staff recommends approval of the Zoning Map Amendment for Bison 
Business Park Expansion, finding that the application meets all applicable review criteria outlined in UDC 
2.2.1.d. The proposed BP: Business Park zoning aligns with the Comprehensive Plan's vision for 
economic development, is compatible with the surrounding land, and provides a suitable framework for 
future growth while upholding public health, safety, and general welfare.
