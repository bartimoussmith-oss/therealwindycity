MEMORANDUM  
TO:  Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council 
FROM: TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager 
DATE: October 22, 2025 
SUBJECT: Consideration of Bid #E-5-26 for furnishing One (1) New Backhoe Loader for the 
City of Cheyenne Cemetery 
 ______________________________________________ 
 Two (2) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.   
 
The Two (2) bids were received from: 
1. RDO Equipment Co. 
2. Wyoming Machinery Company 
 
The bids were reviewed by Bryan Bell (Fleet Maintenance), Nathan Chapman (Cemetery), Bobby 
Lamb (Cemetery), Jason Sanchez (CRE), and Sam Payne (CRE). 
 
The Bid Submitted by RDO Equipment Company has been rejected due to the multiple items not 
meeting the minimum specification of the bid.  Some items include: 
1. Exceeded the allowable maximum length of the unit (Dimensions, #4) 
2. Did not meet braking system requirements (Braking, #1) 
3. Did not meet the control information system requirements (Operator Station, #8) 
4. Did not meet HVAC system requirements (Operator Station, #22) 
5. Did not meet stabilizer controls requirements (Backhoe, #16) 
6. Did not meet digging bucket rotation requirements (Backhoe, #20) 
7. Did not meet serviceability requirements (Serviceability, #1, #3) 
 
Their recommendation is to accept the  next lowest responsive bid  from Wyoming Machinery 
Company. 
 
The Bidding Documents contain a Surcharge and Escalator – Special Provision, that allows for  
the review of surcharges related to the following items: Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) 
and/or Body surcharges applied to the cost of the vehicles after the bid opening date & Model  
year escalators. The Surcharge & Escalator Contingency, is for an amount not -to-exceed Fifteen 
Percent (15%), of the total value of requested items on the Bid. 
 
 

The total requested bid award is for $174,437.50. This include the base cost of the unit  
($161,250.00), less trade-in (-$11,000.00), plus the 15% surcharge contingency ($24,187.50).
