RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: " A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CITY OF CHEYENNE BOARD 
OF PUBLIC UTILITIES TO DISCHARGE UNCOLLECTIBLE DEBTS 
PURSUANT TO WYOMING STATUTE 16-4-502." 
 
WHEREAS, Wyoming Statute § 16-4-502 provides for the discharge of uncollectible debts 
due and owing to a governmental entity upon proper certification to the governing body; 
 
WHEREAS, the Board of Public Utilities (BOPU)  has prepared a  certification for 
uncollectible debts on the books of BOPU, a copy of which is attached to and incorporated as part 
of this Resolution as Appendix A;  
 
WHEREAS, the debts have been reviewed by the appropriate BOPU staff and collection 
efforts have failed; 
 
WHEREAS, BOPU desires to remove the uncollectible debts from its books as expediently 
as possible; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne has reviewed the debts and 
determined to its satisfaction that the debts are uncollectible. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the City Treasurer is authorized to discharge the uncollectible 
debts reflected in Appendix A of this Resolution in the amount of twenty -six thousand eleven 
dollars and forty cents ($26,011.40) from the books of the City of Cheyenne Board of Public 
Utilities.
