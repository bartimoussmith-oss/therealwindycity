MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING  
BETWEEN THE CITY OF CHEYENNE  
AND CHEYENNE FRONTIER DAYS, INC. 
 
This Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is made and executed this _______ day of 
_________________, 2026, by and between the City of Cheyenne  (“City”), a municipality duly 
organized and existing under the laws of the State of Wyoming, whose address is 2101 O ’Neil 
Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001, and Cheyenne Frontier Days, Inc.  (“CFD”), a Wyoming 
corporation, whose address is P.O. Box 2477, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82003. The City and CFD may 
be referred to individually as a “Party” or collectively as the “Parties.” 
 
RECITALS 
 
1. The City owns certain land and buildings near Frontier Park  that have historically been 
used by CFD for parking, camping, and related activities during the annual Cheyenne 
Frontier Days celebration. These City-owned properties include: 
 
a. The land surrounding Lake Absarraca, generally described as the area bordered by 
Kennedy Road on the south, Interstate 25 on the west, the Governor’s Mansion 
property on the northwest, and the Airport Golf Course on the north, northeast, and 
east; and 
 
b. The “Brown Lot” parking area, formerly referred to in prior versions of this MOU 
as the “E Lot,” generally described as the area north of Lions Park Drive, south of 
the Municipal Aquatics Center, and east of Carey Avenue. 
 
2. In addition to the City -owned property described above, the overall parking plan for the 
Cheyenne Frontier Days celebration also involves the nearby “Orange Lot,” generally 
located between Volunteer Avenue on the west and Carey Avenue on the east, west of 
Sloan Lake in Lions Park. The general location of the Brown Lot and the Orange Lot areas 
are depicted on the map attached as Exhibit A and incorporated herein by reference. 
 
3. The City and CFD have previously entered into agreements regarding CFD’s use of City -
owned property and infrastructure in connection with the Cheyenne Frontier Days 
celebration. This MOU is intended to continue that general arrangement and is substantially 
similar to prior agreements between the Parties. 
 
4. For the 2026 Cheyenne Frontier Days celebration, CFD proposes to use certain City-owned 
property for the following purposes: 
 
a. To provide overnight camping at Lake Absarraca, beginning on the Sunday 
preceding the Cheyenne Frontier Days celebration and continuing through the 
Monday after the celebration ends;

b. To provide permit parking in the Brown Lot. No overnight parking shall be allowed 
in the Brown Lot. This additional parking for automobiles, in the confines of Lions 
Park, is intended to facilitate access to Frontier Park for volunteers and contractors. 
This will allow additional public parking in the Orange Lot. The intended purpose 
is to relieve  parking pressure in the Avenues neighborhood, as well as to protect 
the public pedestrian traffic from crossing Carey Avenue; and 
 
c. To provide public paid parking in the Lake Absarraca area, extending from Kennedy 
Road to the camping area described in Recital 1.a. 
 
WHEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises by the City and CFD made each 
to the other, the Parties agree as follows: 
 
1. CFD shall: 
 
a. Maintain control over the areas covered by this MOU; 
 
b. Provide portable toilet facilities for the areas covered by this MOU; 
 
c. Mark and designate camping and parking spaces to maximize use of available 
space; 
 
d. Provide staff to control access to areas managed by CFD; 
 
e. Allow for public access to Lions Park along Lions Park Drive during the Cheyenne 
Frontier Days celebration; 
 
f. Police and patrol the campground on a 24-hour basis; 
 
g. Provide sewer services for RV units; 
 
h. Provide access along Kennedy Road for day-use fishing at Lake Absarraca. Fishing 
access shall not be permitted on the north side of the lake; 
 
i. Keep all grounds clean and free from litter, and empty trash receptacles daily, or as 
needed. CFD shall coordinate with the City Sanitation Department for placement 
of additional dumpsters at Lake Absarraca; and 
 
j. Provide proof of insurance as follows: CFD agrees to file with the City, Certificates 
of Insurance verifying each type and amount of insurance coverage listed below. 
Certificates shall be submitted to the City prior to commencement of performance 
under this MOU.

It is understood and agreed that these policies are primary and not contributory. It 
shall be an affirmative obligation of CFD to immediately notify in writing the City 
Risk Manager, City Clerk, and City Attorney of any fact, circumstances, or 
occurrence t hat has resulted in or may result in the cancellation or substantive 
change of any insurance coverage required by this MOU. Failure to do so shall be 
construed to be a breach of this MOU.  
 
The City of Cheyenne shall be named as an additional insured on CFD's insurance 
policies, except Workers' Compensation, and CFD shall provide, upon request, a 
copy of an endorsement providing this coverage. 
 
In addition, CFD shall provide the City with copies of insurance policies and/or 
policy endorsements listing the City of Cheyenne as an additional insured. The 
City's failure to request or review such policies, endorsements, or certificates shall 
not affect the City's right or CFD’s obligation hereunder. 
 
The City has the right to reject a certificate of insurance if CFD's insurance 
company is widely regarded in the insurance industry as financially unstable. Any 
insurance company providing coverage under this MOU shall have a minimum 
A.M. Best rating of A- (Excellent); 
 
k. Provide daily cleanup and final cleanup of all areas covered by this MOU; 
 
l.  Restore the ground surface in the Brown Lot to substantially the same condition 
that existed immediately prior to the 2026 Cheyenne Frontier Days celebration; and 
 
m.  Provide payment to the City of Cheyenne in the amount of $15,000.00 prior to the 
start of the 2026 Cheyenne Frontier Days celebration.  
 
2. The City shall: 
 
a. Provide adequate dumpsters for the areas covered by this MOU and empty them 
daily, or as otherwise needed.

b. Provide daily maintenance of associated restroom facilities including all supplies, 
but excluding the facilities at Lake Absarraca, which will be maintained by CFD; 
and 
 
c. Assist with T-post installation for fencing of the areas, as mutually determined by 
the Parties prior to the Monday preceding the Cheyenne Frontier Days celebration. 
 
3.  The City agrees to allow CFD to use the Brown Lot and Lake Absarraca Campground for 
the purposes described in this MOU in exchange for payment to the City in the amount of 
fifteen thousand dollars ($15,000.00).  
 
4.  This MOU shall commence June 1, 2026, and terminate on August 31, 2026, unless sooner 
terminated by either Party upon thirty (30) days written notice. Notwithstanding the 
foregoing, CFD’s obligations under Subsections 1(k), 1(l) and 1( m) shall survive the 
expiration or termination of this MOU and remain in full force and effect until satisfied. 
 
5.  CFD agrees to comply with all applicable federal, state , and local laws, rules and 
regulations. 
 
6.  The City expressly invokes and reserves its governmental immunity for any claim arising 
out of this MOU pursuant to the Wyoming Governmental Claims Act, Wyo. Stat. § 1 -39-
101, et seq. 
 
7.  CFD agrees to indemnify, hold harmless and defend the City from and against any and all 
liabilities, claims, penalties, forfeitures, and suits, and the cost and expenses incident 
thereto including reasonable attorney's fees, which may hereafter arise as a result of death 
or bodily injury to any person, destruction, or damage to any property, contamination of or 
adverse effects on the environment, or any violation of governmental laws, regulations, or 
orders to the extent caused by: (1) CFD's breach of any term or provision of this MOU, or 
(2) any negligent or wrongful act, error, or omission by CFD, or its employees or 
subcontractors in the performance of this MOU. CF D acknowledges that it may incur a 
financial obligation to the City pursuant to the terms of this paragraph. 
 
8.  This MOU contains the entire understanding of the Parties. There are no other terms or 
conditions, written or oral, concerning or controlling this matter. The Parties agree that this 
MOU shall not become effective or enforceable until it has been approved by the Parties' 
respective governing bodies.
