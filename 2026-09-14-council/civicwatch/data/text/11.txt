MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING BETWEEN 
LARAMIE COUNTY SCHOOL DISTRICT NUMBER ONE AND 
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING 
 
1. Parties. This Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is made and entered into by and 
between Laramie County School District Number 1 (District), whose address is 2810 
House Avenue,  Cheyenne, WY 82001, and the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming (City) , 
through the Cheyenne Police Department,  whose address is 415 W. 18 th Street, 
Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001.  
 
2. Purpose. The purpose of this MOU is to delineate the terms under which the City will 
provide school resource officer services to District schools  within the recognized 
jurisdiction of the City and at school-related events during the academic year, including 
summer school of each year (Academic Year), under the School Resource Officer 
Program (SRO). The primary charge of the SRO Program is the reduction, prevention, 
and responsiveness to school-related violence and crime. The SRO Program creates and 
maintains a safe, secure, and crime -free learning environment for students, educators 
and the District. In addition, the SRO Program is designed to develop and enhance 
rapport between youth, parents, the surrounding school community, police officers, 
school leaders, and staff.  
 
3. Term of MOU . This MOU shall commence upon the day and date last signed and 
executed by the duly authorized representatives of the parties to this MOU and shall 
remain in full force and effect until August 31, 202 7. This MOU shall specifically 
supersede any prior MOU entered into by the Parties and in effect at the commencement 
of this MOU. 
 
4. Payment. The District agrees to pay the City for the services described herein. 
Specifically, the District shall pay as follows: 
 
A. The District shall reimburse the City for the salary and benefit costs identified in 
Attachment A, as adjusted for actual, annual, and incremental changes in salary 
and benefit personnel expenses, and for any Additional Costs incurred under this 
MOU. 
 
B. All costs of salary and benefits incurred by the City in the performance of duties 
under this MOU shall be paid by the City, and the District shall reimburse the 
City for all such costs. 
 
C. Amounts due under this MOU shall be invoiced to the District monthly and in 
arrears. 
 
D. The City will provide cellular phones for use in official communications with 
District employees. The phones shall remain the property of the City. The District 
agrees to be responsible for all the costs associated with the cellular service plan.

The City agrees to be responsible for the cost of the phones themselves, including 
any related hardware, as well as maintenance, upgrades, and replacements. 
 
5. Responsibilities of District.  
 
A. The District agrees that school-requested SRO attendance at events occurring 
outside of normal school hours or outside the Academic Year constitute s 
Additional Costs. For purposes of this MOU, "Additional Costs" means actual 
personnel costs incurred by the City for school -requested SRO services that are 
not included in the regular SRO salary and benefit costs identified in Attachment 
A, including overtime, premium pay, associated mandatory payroll costs, and the 
actual cost of any additional or replacement officer assigned by the City for the 
requested event or service. Additional Costs do not include ordinary City 
equipment or administrative overhead unless the parties agree in writing before 
the cost is incurred.  
 
B. The District agrees that SRO attendance at events occurring outside of normal 
school hours or outside the Academic Year is subject to the mutual agreement of 
the District and City. The District may request that the City flex an SRO’s 
schedule to assist at such events , but any schedule adjustment shall remain 
subject to City staffing, operational needs, applicable law, and City Police 
Department policies. 
 
C. The District shall designate and provide the City with contact information for a 
District point of contact responsible for coordinating the SRO program. 
 
D. The District agrees to coordinate with the City and share information to the extent 
allowed by law.  
 
E. The District agrees that it is responsible for student discipline for behaviors 
occurring on District property or at District activities.  
 
F. The District acknowledges that violations of District or school policies, student 
discipline issues, or other matters that do not rise to the level of criminal activity 
remain the general purview of the District. Accordingly, student behavioral 
problems or similar events, unless c riminal in nature or posing a threat of 
imminent physical danger to the student or others, will not generally result in the 
use of police force to restrain, detain, or otherwise restrict the activities o f a 
student or students engaged in such activity. The District agrees that it shall not 
involve the SRO in school discipline matters unless school or public safety is at 
risk or reporting is required by law.  
 
G. The District agrees that the school principal or principal’s designee, absent 
emergency circumstances, will determine whether to involve SRO or other City 
Police Department personnel in any matter.

H. The District agrees that SRO or City Police Department referrals may be made 
for the following:  
  
(i) Assaults or battery;  
  
(ii) Felony or violent criminal offenses, including but not limited to robbery, 
extortion, arson, or sexual assault;  
  
(iii) Use or possession of dangerous weapons by a student, including but not 
limited to: knives, guns, and explosive devices;  
 
(iv) Sale, possession, or use of illegal drugs, tobacco/nicotine product s, or 
alcohol;  
 
(v) Threats of physical harm to school employees or the public; and  
 
(vi) Destruction, damage, and vandalism of District property. 
  
I. The District agrees the SRO shall comply with City Police Department policies 
and procedures; where City Police Department policies conflict with District 
policies and procedures, SRO shall defer to the City Police Department policies 
and procedures.  
  
J. Except in emergency, urgent, or preliminary public safety inquiry circumstances, 
SROs shall follow these conditions for interviewing or searching students 
suspected of criminal activity:  
 
(i) SROs may question a student about subjects that may result in or identify 
a crime, threat, or concern utilizing the following process. Unless an 
emergency or urgent circumstance exists, any law enforcement officer 
(LEO) desiring to question a student at school must notify the school 
principal or the principal’s designee in advance so that permi ssion for 
questioning may be obtained from a parent or guardian of the child.  
  
(ii) Once notified that a parent or guardian asks that the student not be 
questioned until he/she or his/her designee arrives, the principal will 
advise the LEO and ask that the officer wait to question the student until 
the parent or guardian arrives.  
 
(iii) If the parent or guardian cannot be reached, the student may be questioned 
by the officer with the principal or the principal’s designee present. The 
principal or his/her designee must be present when any law enforcement 
interview of a child is conducted on school property.  
  
(iv) Pursuant to Wyoming Statute 14-6-206(c), after issuing any citation to a 
child for a violation of a state law or a municipal ordinance for  
   
 
 4 
which incarceration or a fine may be imposed, the law enforcement 
agency issuing the citation or its designee shall take reasonable actions to 
notify the child’s parent, guardian or custodian. SROs will only be 
responsible for issuing citations related to violations of law, not violations 
of school or district rules or policies. 
 
(v) If the parent or guardian is the subject of a potential abuse or neglect 
allegation, neither the LEO nor the Department of Family Services (DFS) 
is required to, nor may the principal or his/her designee, notify the parent 
or guardian prior to a LEO and/or DFS questioning the child. In such 
circumstances, the LEO and/or DFS representative shall question the 
student with the principal or his/her designee present.  
 
(vi) If there are exigent or urgent circumstances that prevent prior parental 
notification.  
 
a) Parent or guardian shall be notified as soon as reasonably 
possible. 
 
b) School personnel will serve ‘in loco parentis’ (in place of a parent 
or guardian). 
 
(vii) Absent exigent or urgent circumstances, a principal or designee shall be 
consulted before the SRO conducts questioning of a student or searches 
a student’s person, possessions , or locker to allow time to express any 
concerns about reasonableness and to notify a parent or guardian.  
  
(viii) When there is probable cause to believe the student has committed or is 
committing a felony offense or the offense at issue poses a threat of 
serious and immediate injury, the SRO may conduct questioning or a 
search at school without notice to school personnel, a parent or guardian.  
 
(ix) SROs may not request that school officials conduct a search of the 
student’s person, possessions, or locker to evade the probable cause 
standard required of law enforcement.  
  
(x) Nothing herein shall prevent the SRO from engaging students in casual 
conversation about subjects other than inquiries related to suspected 
criminal activity. 
 
K. The District agrees that SRO audio and/or video recordings constitute “peace  
officer recordings” under the Wyoming Public Records Act. See Wyo. Stat. § 16-
4-201(a)(xi). As such, SRO peace officer recordings are the sole property of the 
City and may not be released except as permitted by law.

L. The District will timely notify the SRO or the City Police of any student with 
disabilities that require specialized treatment or accommodation regarding any  
matter referred to the SRO by the District. 
 
M. The District shall provide each SRO a private and secure office space at the 
appropriate school with access to technology support. 
 
N. Camera System Access and Use. 
 
(i) Access to District Security Camera Systems . The District’s security 
camera systems are maintained as school safety and security tools. Access 
by SROs or other Police Department personnel shall be limited to 
legitimate school safety, emergency response, and specific incident -
response purposes, and shall not be used for generalized law enforcement 
surveillance, routine monitoring of students or staff, or exploratory 
searches for violations of law or District policy. The District shall provide 
access to the SRO assigned to the school where the camera is located. In 
addition, the SRO Supervisor shall have access to all school cameras. 
Temporary access to additional cameras may be granted to SROs for 
coverage, emergency response, or incident command. During an active 
threat, lockdown, active shooter incident, or other serious emergency, the 
District may temporarily expand access to additional Police Department 
personnel or incident command as reasonably necessary to protect health 
or safety. 
 
(ii) Copying, Recording, Reproduction, and Disclosure of Camera Footage . 
Unless exigent circumstances require immediate action to protect health 
or safety, SROs and City Police Department personnel shall not 
download, export, copy, photograph, screenshot, record with a body -
worn camera or personal device, transmit, or otherwise reproduce District 
camera footage without prior authorization from the Superintendent, or 
designee, or without a subpoena, warrant, court order, consent, or other 
lawful process. Any copy lawfully provided to the City shall be used only 
for the identified purpose and shall not be redisclosed except as permitted 
by the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), other 
applicable law, court order, or written authorization from the District.  
Nothing in this subsection shall be construed to limit access to or use of 
the District’s security camera systems as authorized under subsection (i). 
 
O. The District shall provide each SRO limited access to the District’s Infinite 
Campus system to include s tudent name, address, parent or guardian contact 
information, in accordance with local, state and federal law.  The SROs will be 
required to complete annual FERPA training. The training will be required prior 
to or at the beginning of the new school year and will be provided by the District 
at no cost to the City. The District will maintain the SRO’s training records 
depicting the FERPA training was completed. The SRO will not include any 
information from Infinite Campus in any report or formal record of a law 
enforcement investigation.

P. The District shall provide the SRO access to all school facilities for the purposes 
of official duties. 
 
Q. The District understands and agrees the SRO may be immediately recalled for 
public emergencies. The parties agree that SRO recall for public emergencies is 
not a material breach of this MOU and there is no penalty to the City. 
 
6. Responsibilities of the City. 
 
A. The City agrees to provide the District with seven (7) SROs and one SRO 
Supervisor for the 2026-2027 Academic Year. The SRO Supervisor's 
responsibilities will be to manage and coordinate the City’s performance under 
this MOU. 
 
B. The City agrees that SRO duties are to respond to and investigate criminal acts  
on District property and/or at District events during normal school hours, or as 
otherwise agreed to, during the Academic Year.  
 
C. The City agrees that the SRO may initiate appropriate law enforcement actions 
to address criminal matters, including matters that threaten the safety and security 
of the school or its occupants, and/or  intervene with staff or students when 
necessary to ensure the immediate safety of persons in the school environment in 
light of an actual or imminent threat to health or safety. Any such intervention 
shall be reasonably calculated to protect the physical safety of members within 
the school community, while minimizing, to the extent possible, any unintended 
negative effects on students. All law enforcement actions and interventions to 
protect the safety of others shall be consistent with all applicable laws, 
regulations, and policies. 
 
D. The City agrees that except for felony offenses or criminal offenses that 
constitute a threat to the safety of the school or public (“Serious Offenses”), the 
SRO will notify and consult with the school principal or his or her designee prior 
to questioning a student. 
 
E. The City agrees that except for Serious Offenses, the SRO will delay questioning 
a student until the parent or guardian arrives. 
 
F. The City agrees that except for Serious Offenses, the school principal or his or 
her designee shall be present for student interviews and searches on District  
property. 
 
G. The City will take reasonable action to notify the parent or guardian of any  
student issued a citation. See Wyo. Stat. § 14-6-206(c). 
 
H. The City agrees that the SRO s may not request that school officials conduct a  
search of the student’s person, possessions, or locker to avoid probable cause  
standards. 
   
 
 7 
 
I. The City agrees that an SRO may provide educational support, when applicable, 
for in-service training for school staff and students on emergency preparedness, 
crisis intervention, crime trends, law enforcement policies , and intervention 
methods. 
 
J. The City agrees that the school may request assistance of an SRO to conduct a 
search of a student’s person, possessions, or locker on behalf of the school when 
the school has a reasonable suspicion of a violation of school policy or law by 
the student. Any search will be conducted at the sole discretion of the SRO and 
in accordance with applicable law and City Police Department policies. 
 
K. The City acknowledges that District records, student information, and District 
security camera footage may be protected by FERPA, state confidentiality laws, 
District policy, or other applicable law. SROs and City Police Department 
personnel shall request, access, use, and redisclose such records or footage only 
as authorized by Section 5 (N), or with written District authorization, a valid 
FERPA exception, consent, subpoena, warrant, court order, or other lawful 
process. 
 
L. The City agrees to provide a replacement officer if the assigned SRO will be 
absent for ten (10) or more consecutive school days. The replacement officer will 
cover the SRO’s duty assignment at the actual cost of the replacement officer. 
Further, the District shall not be billed for periods when individual SROs are 
absent from their assigned duties/school(s), to include periods of professional 
training for collateral duty.  The SRO is encouraged, when possible, to take 
personal leave during scheduled school breaks. 
 
7. Special Provisions. 
 
A. SROs will be selected by means of a joint selection committee, comprised of 
representatives from the District who will be appointed by the Superintendent of 
Schools and representatives from the City appointed by the Chief of Police. The 
committee will make recommendations to the Chief of Police; however, final 
selection of any SRO is at the sole discretion of the City. 
 
B. SRO training specific to law enforcement duties shall be provided and paid for 
by the City. Training for SRO in areas that are specific to juvenile crimes and 
SRO duties may be provided by the City and District. The District will provide 
overview and familiarization with District techniques and procedures to SROs as 
necessary and with content relevant to SRO duties. The District’s Safety and 
Security Program Administrator will coordinate dates and times of this training 
with the SRO’s supervisor. Training requests will be made to the SRO Supervisor 
and the Superintendent of Schools or the District’s Safety and Security Program 
   
 
 8 
Administrator. At least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of this MOU, the 
City and the District will jointly evaluate the SRO program.  
 
C. The City will evaluate the effectiveness of each SRO. The City shall seek input 
from the District for each evaluation.  
 
D. SROs assigned to provide services under this MOU shall, at all times, be 
employees of the City. The City shall comply with all federal, state, and local 
laws and ordinances regarding the employment relationship between the City  
and SROs assigned to provide services under this MOU. 
 
E. Long Term Official School Closure: Should any non -weather-related event 
require long -term official closure of school locations for more th an five ( 5) 
consecutive school days, the District may request limited law enforcement 
services with the balance of SRO’s daily hours red irected to supervise schools 
and/or released back to the City as best represents the interests of both the City 
and the District. If such interests cannot be agreed upon, this MOU shall be 
suspended for the remainder of the period of the official closure, and payments 
vacated for duties not performed during such period. 
 
8. General Provisions. 
 
A. Amendments. Either party may request changes in this MOU. Any changes, 
modifications, revisions, or amendments to this MOU that are mutually agreed 
upon by the parties to this MOU shall be incorporated by written instrument, 
executed and signed by all parties to this MOU. 
 
B. Applicable Law. The construction, interpretation, and enforcement of this MOU 
shall be governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming. The courts of the State 
of Wyoming shall have jurisdiction over any action arising out of this MOU and 
over the parties, and the venue shal l be the First Judicial District, Laramie 
County, Wyoming. 
 
C. Availability of Funds. Each payment obligation of either party is conditioned 
upon the availability of government funds which are appropriated or allocated 
for the payment of this obligation. If funds are not allocated and available for the 
continuance of the services performed by either party, the  MOU may be 
terminated by either party  at the end of the period for which the  funds are 
available. Each party shall notify the other party at the earliest possible time of 
the services which will or may be affe cted by a shortage of funds. No penalty 
shall accrue to either party in the event this provision is exercised, and neither 
party shall be obligated or liable for any future payments due or for any damages 
as a result of termination under this section. This provision shall not be construed 
to permit either party to terminate  this MOU to acquire similar services from 
another source.

F. Indemnification. Each party to this MOU shall assume the risk of any liability 
arising from its own conduct. Neither party agrees to insure, defend or indemnify 
the other.  
 
G. Independent Contractor. Nothing in this MOU shall be interpreted as 
authorizing either party or its agents and/or employees to act as an agent or 
representative for or on behalf of the other party or to incur any obligation of any 
kind on the behalf of the other party.  
 
H. Severability. Should any portion of this MOU be judicially determined to be 
illegal or unenforceable, the remainder of the MOU shall continue in full force 
and effect, and either party may renegotiate the terms affected by the severance.  
 
I. Termination of MOU. This MOU may be terminated, without cause, by either 
party upon thirty (30) days written notice. This MOU may be terminated 
immediately for cause if the  City or District materially fails to perform in 
accordance with the terms of this MOU  and fails to cure the breach within a 
reasonable time after written notice, unless the breach cannot reasonably be cured 
or immediate termination is necessary to protect public safety or the lawful 
operation of the SRO Program. 
 
J. Third Party Beneficiary Rights. The parties do not intend to create in any other 
individual or entity the status of third party beneficiary, and this MOU shall not 
be construed so as to create such status. The rights, duties, and obligations 
contained in this MOU shall operate only betwe en the parties to this MOU and 
shall inure solely to the benefit of the parties to this MOU. The provisions of this 
MOU are intended only to assist the parties in determining and performing their 
obligations under this MOU.  
 
K. Titles Not Controlling. Titles of paragraphs are for reference only and shall not 
be used to construe the language in this MOU.
