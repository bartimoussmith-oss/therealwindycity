WHEREAS, the Governing Body previously reviewed the application for water services 
submitted by the Applicant , determined that approval was in the best interest of the City of 
Cheyenne, and adopted Resolution No. 6424, approving the Outside User Agreement subject to 
the following conditions: “1. the proposed development shall comply with City Standards, or 
applicable City relief processes; and, 2. the property owner initiates annexation pursuant to 
Wyo. Stat. § 15-1-403 within one hundred and eighty (180) days of approval of this resolution;”  
 
WHEREAS, the Applicant ’s development program has changed resulting in the inability 
to meet the conditions of approval; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Applicant wishes to extend the  approval subject to  the following 
conditions:  1. Any development of the land shall comply with City development standards , and 
relief to those standards may be sought via applicable procedures for relief (e.g. Administrative 
Adjustment, Variance, etc.); and 2. The property owner shall initiate annexation of the property

 NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that Resolution No. 6424 adopted on September 9, 2024, to allow 
water services outside of the corporate limits of the City of Cheyenne is hereby modified to only 
include the following conditions:  
 
1. Any development of the land shall comply with City development standards and relief to 
those standards may be sought via applicable procedures for relief; and 
 
2. The property owner shall initiate annexation of the property and adjacent rights -of-way 
pursuant to Wyo. Stat. § 15 -1-403 within eighteen (18) months of approval of this 
Resolution unless an extension is mutually agreed upon by the applicant and the Governing 
Body. 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities (BOPU) and 
the Applicant are authorized to  approve and execute Outside User Agreement (s), subject to the 
conditions imposed by the Governing Body.
