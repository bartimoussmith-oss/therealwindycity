ORDINANCE NO.      
 
ENTITLED: “AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 8.24.020 OF THE MUNICIPAL 
CODE OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, CLARIFYING AND 
FURTHER REGULATING THE INSTALLATION OF ABOVE-GROUND 
TANKS AND CONTAINERS AS SPECIFIED." 
 
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, 
WYOMING: 
 
 Section 1 .  That Section 8.24.020, Aboveground Tanks in City Limits, of Chapter 8.24, 
Flammable and Combustible Liquids , of Title 8, Health and Safety , of the City of Cheyenne, 
Wyoming, is hereby amended to read: 
 
8.24.020 Above-ground tanks and containers in city limits. 
 
A. It is unlawful to have aboveground storage tanks exceeding sixty (60) gallons except in a 
heavy industrial (HI), light industrial (LI) or public (P) zone with the approval of, and under 
conditions established by, the chief of the Cheyenne fire department. Such tanks exceeding 
sixty (60) gallons The following above-ground tanks and containers may only be installed 
in the city with the wr itten approval of, and subject to the conditions established by, the 
fire code official: 
 
1. Any above-ground tank with a capacity of more than sixty (60) gallons that stores or is 
intended to store any Class I or Class II liquid; and 
 
2. Any above-ground containers with a capacity of sixty (60) gallons or less that stores or 
is intended to store any Class I or Class II liquid. 
 
B. An above-ground tank located in any refinery complexes and complex or bulk plants plant 
shall be in compliance with the National Fire Protection Association Pamphlet No. NFPA 
30, and the International Fire Code  as adopted under section chapter 8.24, title 8, of the 
city code. 
 
 Section 2 .  Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases of this ordinance are 
severable. If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase of this ordinance is declared 
unconstitutional or otherwise unlawful, such declaration shall not affect any of the remaining 
sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases. 
 
 Section 3.  This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon approval and publication.
