BACKGROUND: 
 
The applicant is requesting approval of a final plat for roughly 3.17 acres of land. The proposed plat would 
create three lots, two of which have existing single-family residences on them. This plat also incorporates 
portions of recently vacated Frank Court. 
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of final plat, the Commission must make 
the following findings required in UDC 2.1.3.d a detailed analysis is below. The purpose of a final plat 
review is to ensure compliance with the approved preliminary plat and its conditions of approval.   
 
1) The layout and design of the final plat is in substantial compliance with the 
approved preliminary plat considering the number of lots or parcels, street and 
block layout, and access, and any deviations in the final plat brings the application 
in further compliance with the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.3.d.1)

The Final Plat submitted with the application is substantially similar to the Preliminary Plat that 
was approved on January 26, 2026.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
 
2) The application meets any conditions or modifications of the approved preliminary 
plat. (UDC 2.1.3.d.2) 
 
The preliminary plat was approved by Planning Commission on January 5, 2026 and 
acknowledged by City Council on January 26, 2026 with the following conditions:  
1. Concurrent with Final Plat Submittal: 
a. Provide a vacation map to the City to initiate the full vacation of Frank Court, OR 
include all owners adjacent to Frank Court in the platting action. 
b. Clarify/verify owner and dedication information. 
2. On the Final Plat: 
a. Provide necessary easements. 
b. Make minor technical corrections. 
 
The applicant has provided a vacation map for the north-south Frank Court. The vacation is 
currently in front of Council and is scheduled to have final consideration on April 13, 2026.  
 
Staff is still concerned that the ownership of the east-west vacated Frank Court has not been 
appropriately demonstrated. The County confirmed the vacation of east-west Frank Court by 
finally recording a vacation action that was approved by the County in the 1980s. However, this 
action did not specify land ownership. The applicant could provide a title commitment from a title 
company or quiet title demonstrating the signatories on the plat own all of vacated east -west 
Frank Court within the Plat boundary, which would then satisfy this condition.  
 
Easements and technical corrections have been made. 
 
The application complies with this review criterion, with conditions.   
 
3) The application meets the purposes, intent, and applicable standards of Article 4. 
(UDC 2.1.3.d.3) 
 
Community Facility Fees (4.2): 
For fees based on acreage, the area of the plat is pro-rated to the nearest tenth of an acre. 
Lands reserved as unbuildable (for right-of-way, detention, parks, remainder lots, etc), are 
exempt from fees. Park fees are calculated at $700 per acre. All fees are due at the time of 
building permit. 
 
Per UDC Section 4.2.5, Table 4-2, Enhancement Fees are payable at the issuance of a building 
permit for each structure. 
Residential Enhancement Fee $400 per dwelling unit 
 
Transportation Network (4.3): 
This plat is for an existing development in an area that is already developed. No additional 
streets are proposed.

Open Space (4.4): 
Open space is required at 750sqft per unit or 8% of the gross development area, whichever is 
less. However, due to the size of the property, plat-level open space is not required. The plat 
includes a common lot which serves as a common open area. 
 
Required Improvements (4.5): 
This plat appears to be able to provide all required utilities to the lots within this 
development. 
 
Public Safety Fees (4.6): 
Public Safety Fees are required at the time of construction of any residential building on any 
of the proposed lots. Per UDC Section 4.6.3.b, Public Safety Fees are payable at the issuance 
of a building permit for each structure.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.

relate to applicable review criteria. In the event that the Commission acts contrary to staff’s 
recommendation or modifies conditions of approval, the Commission shall cite specific reasons (based on 
the review criteria) for approval or denial.
