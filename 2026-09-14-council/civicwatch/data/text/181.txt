SUBJECT: Consideration of RFP #6 -26 for a Contract between the City of 
Cheyenne and FCI Constructors of WY, for the Construction Manager 
at Risk Services for Cheyenne Municipal Building Renovation & 
Potential City Council Building & Second Floor Municipal Court 
Renovation 
 
RFP #6-26 was solicited for the Construction Manager at Risk Services for Cheyenne 
Municipal Building Renovation & Potential City Council Building & Second Floor 
Municipal Court Renovation. 
 
Three (3) proposers responded to this RFP.  The proposals were received from: 
1. FCI Constructors of WY 
2. GH Phipps Wyoming LLC 
3. Sampson Construction Co Inc 
 
The Proposals were reviewed by Public Works and the group recommends award to 
FCI Constructors of WY. 
 
At this time, the Contract is for an amount not -to-exceed $30,000.00 for Pre -
Construction Services.  As the final plans are finalized, FCI Constructors of WY will 
prepare their Guaranteed Maximum Price (GMP) for the project.  The GMP will come 
before the Governing Body as a Contract Modification for future consideration.
