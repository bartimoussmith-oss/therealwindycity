MEMORANDUM  
TO:  Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council 
FROM:  TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager 
DATE:  September 18, 2025 
SUBJECT: Consideration of Bid #S-2-26 for an Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and  
Richardson Construction, Inc., for the Depot Renovation Project  
______________________________________________ 
Bid #S-2-26 was solicited for the Depot Renovation Project. 
 
The Project involves the renovation of a portion of the Second and Third Levels and 
Exterior Improvements at the Historic UP Depot building located at 121 West 15th Street, 
Cheyenne, Wyoming. The Project involves furnishing all labor, materials, services, and equipment 
required for the project, including, but not limited to selective demolition, site work, work on historic 
wood windows, finishes, electrical, and mechanical work. 
The Community Recreation and Events Department has elected to remove the Historic Masonry 
Repairs from the current Project Scope. As a result, the Contract value for Richardson Construction, 
the lowest responsive bidder, has been revised to $1,874,421.00, reflecting a cost reduction of 
$419,579.00. Following consultation with the project Architect, it has been confirmed that deferring 
the Historic Masonry Repairs will not result in significant deterioration concerns and can be addressed 
at a future date. 
Four (4) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tab ulation is attached.  The Bids were received 
from: 
1. Recco, Inc. dba Five-R Co. 
2. Reiman Corp. 
3. Richardson Construction, Inc 
4. Sampson Construction 
 
The bids were reviewed by Jason Sanchez (CRE), Sam Payne (CRE), and The Design Studio, Inc 
(TDSi), and their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from Richardson 
Construction, Inc. 
 
The total Bid Award is $1,874,421.00.
