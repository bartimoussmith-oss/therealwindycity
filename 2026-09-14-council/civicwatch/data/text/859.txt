Description of Work 
We propose to furnish and install the labor, materials, tools and supervision to perform the following work on the 
passenger elevator located at the George Cox Parking. 
 
Elevator Controller: 
 
Hydraulic Smartrise microprocessor Controller equipped with the following characteristics will be 
installed:  
  
- Solid state soft starters in lieu of y-delta starters  
- Controller cabinet outlet plug  
- Interface for voice annunciator  
- Group box interface so both controllers can  
communicate with each other on dispatching calls   
- Door interface to communicate with new GAL door equipment  
- Hall lantern interface to communicate with innovation signal 
 fixture equipment  
- Machine room to hoistway wiring package- 100 feet of wiring  
- Traveling cable package- Twenty-six conductor composite  
14 AWG, fourteen 18 AWG & four 20 AWG shielded pairs all 
jacketed in protective rubber coating. Includes kellum grips to 
hang onto cars.  
- Pre-wired car top inspection stations  
- Interface for car call security  
- Emergency battery lowering

Power Unit Replacement: 
 
We will furnish and install a new submersible hydraulic power unit. The motor and pump will be 
submersed under the oil inside the tank in order to provide for sound isolation. A muffler, designed to 
reduce pulsation and noise, which may be present in the flow of hydraulic oil, will be provided in the oil 
line at the top of the pump. 
 
Control valves, including a safety check valve, an up direction valve with high pressure relief including 
up leveling and soft stop features and a lowering valve, including down leveling and a manual leveling 
feature, will be mounted in a compact unit assembly. A shut off valve, designed to shut off the flow of oil 
between the cylinder and the power unit, will be provided in the oil line in the machine room. Automatic 
two-way leveling will be provided to automatically stop and maintain the car approximately level with the 
landing, regardless of change in load.

Hydraulic Shut Off Valve    
 
We will furnish and install a hydraulic oil line shut off valve in the elevator machine room which allows the 
technician to isolate leaks in troubleshooting, decreasing downtime during hydraulic leak repairs.  
 
 
 
 
Hydraulic Fluid Replacement 
We will provide a repair crew to hang the elevator, remove the old worn-out hydraulic fluid and clean the 
hydraulic system. We will replace with new hydraulic fluid throughout the system.    
Once this work has been completed, we will perform a safety test of the elevator to ensure the valve, pump, 
and all safety circuits are operable. Once this work has been completed it should alleviate the recurring issues 
being faced.
