MEMORANDUM  
TO:  Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council 
FROM: TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager 
DATE: October 22, 2025 
SUBJECT: Consideration of Bid #E -3-26 for furnishing One (1) New 2025 -2026 30 Ton 
Articulated Dump Truck for the City of Cheyenne Landfill 
 ______________________________________________ 
 Three (3) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.   
 
The Three (3) bids were received from: 
1. RDO Equipment Co. 
2. Tri-State Truck and Equipment 
3. Wyoming Machinery Company 
 
The bids were reviewed by Bryan Bell (Fleet Maintenance), Samuele Quattrini (Landfill), Craig 
LaVoy (Public Works), and Vicki Nemecek (Public Works).   
 
Their recommendation is to accept the  lowest responsive bid  from Wyoming Machinery 
Company. 
 
The Bidding Documents contain a Surcharge and Escalator – Special Provision, that allows for 
the review of surcharges related to the following items:  Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) 
and/or Body surcharges applied to the cost of the vehicles after th e bid opening date & Model 
year escalators.  The Surcharge & Escalator Contingency, is for an amount not-to-exceed Fifteen 
Percent (15%), of the total value of  requested items on the Bid. 
 
The total requested bid award is for $501,541.25.  This include the base cost of the unit 
($531,775.00), less trade-in (-$110,000.00), plus the 15% surcharge contingency ($79,766.25).
