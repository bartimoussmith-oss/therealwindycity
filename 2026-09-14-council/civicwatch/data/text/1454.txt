BACKGROUND: 
This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from AG Agricultural to NR-3 – High Density 
Residential for roughly 3.43 acres of land. The land is currently vacant. The land is projected to be used for high 
density residential development. There is an expedited plat accompanying this zone change.  
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical
patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1)

PlanCheyenne designates this area for future land development as Urban Residential. Urban 
Residential promotes a board variety of residential development types, including multi-unit 
residential developments. Within PlanCheyenne, it is outlined that these high -density 
developments are to be located near activity centers. The Frontier Mall area is designated as an 
activity center. Proposed development for the area is for senior housing development. This 
development is in line with development patterns described for future land use for urban 
residential. The zoning district of NR-3 – High Density Residential allows for a development of 
multi-unit senior housing development. The development is appropriate for the area as it 
continues to develop in the future.  
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1. 
2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering
existing zoning, the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies
created under the guidance of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)
The proposed NR-3 zone is compatible with nearby properties. Surrounding properties are zoned
LR, AG, or MUB. The MUB parcel is a parking lot with the reminder of the land being vacant. The
MUB zone is a mixed-use zone allowing for residential and commercial development. The
residential portion of the MUB zone is tied to the NR-3 zone. Land that is currently assigned AG
Agriculture will be used for agriculture purposes until rezoned for development. The LR is a low-
density residential development that sits to the west of the parcel. Further south is dense
commercial development that contain several businesses. Future land use map designates the
area for Urban Residential. The NR-3 zone allow for number of residential developments to be
built in the zone.
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2.
3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards
and uses of the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject
property for development according to the standards and uses of the proposed
zoning district (UDC 2.2.1.d.3)
The current zone for the area is AG Agriculture, which would not allow for the proposed
development to be built on the parcel. AG zone only allows for agricultural activities; activities
that are not in line with future development plans. The proposed zone of NR-3 zone allows for a
variety of residential uses, including multi-unit development, like an apartment complex. The
proposed development on this parcel is for high-density senior housing development, which is
allowed by right within the NR-3.
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.
4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed
according to the Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed
zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

This parcel is located within an oversize block. There is an expectation extension of Melton Street  
with be alongside the parcel. This extension will need up connecting with other street as the area 
continues to develop.  
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4. 
5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of
the proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific
standards. (UDC 2.2.1.d.5)
The NR-3 zone district is intended for high-density, urban residential uses. Developments within
the NR-3 zone should be more than 15 units an acre. This zone district should be used in areas
were more compact neighborhood development is desired. This zone district should be near an
activity center to maintain walking access to non-residential uses and other urban amenities. This
district should be supported by highly connected street network with street design types that
have a degree of pedestrian amenities. The location of this proposed zoning meets the intent and
applicability found in the UDC for the NR-3 zone.
The NR-3 zone is subject to the UDC residential design standards, including the Compact and
Mixed-Density Neighborhood Design standards. Staff believes that the developer can comply
with applicable UDC standards.
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5.
6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options
if any specific development proposed in association with the application is not
executed after approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6)
Potential development for the parcel is senior residential housing in the form of an apartment
complex. Within the NR-3 zone, this development would by allowed by right instead of needing a
conditional use (a conditional use might be needed in a different residential zone).
Staff is confident that developer can meet UDC standards for any development.
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6.
7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7)
No major comments received in regard to the proposed zone.
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7.
8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare.
(UDC 2.2.1.d.8)
