The applicant is requesting approval of an expedited plat for roughly 233.33 acres of land. The proposed plat 
would create four lots for industrial / commercial development. There is a proposed zoning map amendment 
that includes these lots to BP – Business Park (PUDC-26-86).

Per UDC Section 4.2.5, Table 4-2, Enhancement Fees are payable at the issuance of a building 
permit for each structure. The Commercial Enhancement Fee is $1,100 per acre.

This plat is creating an additional street that will add to the street network in the area and 
provide additional access to the lots. There are no block size requirements for this area.  
 
Open Space (4.4): 
Open space is not required at the platting level for this project.   
 
Required Improvements (4.5): 
This plat appears to be able to provide all required utilities to the lots within this 
development.
