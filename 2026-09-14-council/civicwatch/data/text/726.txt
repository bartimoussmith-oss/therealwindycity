SUBJECT: 
Approval of the 2027–2030 ESRI Small Government Enterprise Agreement (SGEA) Renewal 
and Multi-Agency Memorandum of Understanding 
 
Please ﬁnd the attached ESRI Small Government Enterprise Agreement (SGEA) Renewal 
Quote and the Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) between Laramie County, the City of 
Cheyenne, the Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities (BOPU), and the Cheyenne/Laramie 
County Board of Health for GIS software licensing and cooperative management. 
The renewal SGEA provides enterprise-level licensing for ESRI software products, 
extensions, online services, and support for all participating agencies. The new three-year 
term will run from January 20, 2027, through January 19, 2030, with an annual total 
agreement cost of $128,200, as identiﬁed in ESRI Quotation Q-550827. 
Funding responsibilities for each year of the term are as follows: 
• Laramie County: $36,186 
• City of Cheyenne: $36,185 (including MPO’s $8,400 share) 
• Board of Public Utilities: $36,186 

• Health Department: $11,243 
The attached MOU outlines the management structure for the SGEA, identiﬁes the County 
as the Lead Agency, and clariﬁes responsibilities for software deployment, reporting, and 
technical support across participating entities. 
Approval of this renewal is required to ensure uninterrupted GIS licensing, secure access 
to ArcGIS software products, and continued operational support for critical City, County, 
and BOPU mapping and data functions. 
We respectfully request approval of the ESRI SGEA renewal and authorization for the Mayor 
to execute the Memorandum of Understanding.

I.   PURPOSE 
 
 The purpose of this MOU is to define the management and coordination strategy in 
administering the  Small Government Enterprise Agreement  and the Addendum  (hereinafter 
referred to as “SGEA”) by and between the COUNTY and Environmental Systems Research 
Institute, Inc., 380 New York Street, Redlands, California 92373 -8100 (hereinafter referred to as 
“ESRI”).  This is a renewal of an existing SGEA that benefits the members of the Cheyenne and 
Laramie County GIS Cooperative (hereinafter referred to as “CLCGISC”) in coordinating and 
collaborating on ESRI software licensing and maintenance.   
 
 
II. SGEA MANAGEMENT  
 
A. The COUNTY will serve as the Lead agency for the EA. This includes acting as the 
contractual agency with ESRI in approval of the  SGEA Terms and Conditions; 
managing a budget line item for pooling of funds from all agencies participating in the 
EA; and payment of the annual ESRI SGEA invoice. 
 
B. The Cheyenne/Laramie County  GIS Cooperative Coordinator will be the point of 
contact with ESRI in managing the SGEA to include the following items: 
 
1. Act as single point of contact for orders and deliveries of software and redistribution 
to eligible users.  Total backup media will not exceed two (2).  Additional back up 
media is available for purchase by the requesting agency. 
 
2. Provide ESRI with annual report of GIS software installations of software products 
referenced in the EA. 
 
3. Account for the below amounts in the CLCGISC Operational Budget annually and 
invoice each agency as part of the normal invoicing process for the CLCGISC.

date.  Deployments of software covered by this  SGEA within agencies not named or
