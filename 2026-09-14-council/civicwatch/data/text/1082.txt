SUBJECT: Memorandum of Understanding Between the City of Cheyenne and Frog 
Creek Partners (FCP), LLC  
 
 
Please find attached the proposed Memorandum of Understanding between City of Cheyenne 
and Frog Creek Partners (FCP), LLC a Wyoming Limited Liability Corporation being initiated 
by the City Engineering.  
 
Reason for Agreement:  
The City of Cheyenne has formally committed to the Clean Crow Creek Initiative through the 
adopting of Resolution No. 6420 on August 12, 2024.  The resolution underscored the 
commitment of the Governing Body for the City to enhance the environmental integrity of Crow 
Creek.  Furthermore, the Wyoming Department of Environment Quality has listed Crow Creek 
on the impaired streams list.    
 
The agreement was initiated due to the generous donations of local corporate businesses which 
have provided for the purchase and maintenance of multitude of Gutter BinⓇ stormwater 
filtration systems from Frog Creek and for installation within the City’s contributory watershed 
basins.   
 
The purpose of this MOU is to define the responsibilities of each party associated with the 
installation and maintenance of the Gutter Bins installed within the City of Cheyenne. 
 
Cost and Funding: 
The MOU will have no direct impact on the current or proposed City of Cheyenne Budget.    
 
Term: 
This MOU agreement will terminate on December 31, 2035.

This MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (“MOU”) is made and entered into by and 
between the City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne, Wyoming, a municipal corporation, located at 2101 
O'Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, WY 82001 (hereinafter referred to as the “City”), and Frog Creek 
Partners (FCP), LLC , a Wyoming Limited Liability Co mpany, located at 800 West 
Yellowstone Highway, Casper, WY 82601 (hereinafter referred to as “Frog Creek”). The City 
and Frog Creek are  sometimes individually referred to herein as “Party” and collectively as 
“Parties.” This MOU shall become effective upon the date of the last signature affixed hereto. 
 
RECITALS 
 
WHEREAS, the City has formally committed to the Clean Crow Creek Initiative through 
the adopting of Resolution No. 6420 (Aug. 12, 2024). This resolution underscores the commitment 
of the Governing Body for the City to enhance the environmental integrity of Crow Creek; 
 
WHEREAS, stormwater pollution has a direct effect on the water quality in Crow Creek, 
a perennial stream listed for sediment and E. coli on the Wyoming Department of Environment 
Quality impaired streams list; 
 
WHEREAS, one or more corporate businesses (“Sponsors”) have committed to purchasing 
multiple Gutter BinⓇ stormwater filtration systems ("Gutter Bins") from Frog Creek and donating 
them for installation within the City’s contributory watershed basins; 
 
WHEREAS, the Parties understand the mutual benefit associated with this public-private 
partnership between local businesses ( “Sponsors”) seeking to foster goodwill by protecting the 
Crow Creek from stormwater pollution; 
 
WHEREAS, The Parties understand that monetary compensation is neither promised or 
required under this MOU considering the other good and valuable consideration  exchanged 
between the parties with the execution of this agreement; 
 
WHEREAS, the City, Frog Creek, and associated Sponsors have collaborated on this 
endeavor with the goal of establishing a long-term implementation and maintenance partnership 
that benefits the community and the environment; and 
 
WHEREAS, the purpose of this MOU is to define  the responsibilities of each party  
associated with the installation and maintenance of the Gutter Bins installed within the City of 
Cheyenne.

AGREEMENT 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the promises of the parties hereto and other good 
and valuable consideration, the receipt and adequacy of which are hereby acknowledged, the 
Parties agree as follows:  
 
1. Recitals Incorporated. The foregoing recitals are incorporated herein by reference 
into this MOU as though set forth at length, unless otherwise called out in this 
agreement. 
 
2. Responsibilities of Frog Creek.  Frog Creek shall: 
a. Provide the City with a Gutter Bin installation schedule as specified in Exhibit B, 
which schedule may be updated as necessary upon the preapproval of the City. 
b. Only install Gutter Bin s at locations within the rights -of-way and only upon the 
preapproval of the City. 
c. Be directly responsibl e for cleaning all Gutter Bins documented in Exhibit B at 
least once per year. Frog Creek shall not be responsible for Gutter Bins as specified 
by Exhibit A, except to the extent that exhibit provides otherwise. 
d. Frog Creek, or through its sub-contractor(s), agrees to transfer captured waste from 
installed Gutter Bins for this Project to the City of Cheyenne Landfill located at 
1461 Happy Jack Road or to properly dispose of all material captured in accordance 
with applicable EPA and State regulations.  All waste shall be disposed of at a 
regulated landfill. Alternatively, upon prior written approval by the City, captured 
pollutants may be delivered to a designated municipal location typically used for 
vacuum truck discharge or other stormwater -related waste, provided it meets 
applicable regulatory and operational standards. 
e. Shall notify the City at least forty -eight (48) hours prior to any Gutter Bin 
maintenance or installation activity , unless an emergency situation  prevents 
advance notice, in which case Frog Creek shall notify the City within forty -eight 
(48) hours after the activity. 
f. Provide a yearly Pollution Capture Report and Inlet Debris Report to assist the City 
with maintenance and documentation for compliance with the MS4 permit. For the 
purposes of the Inlet Debris Report , information should include the date  of the 
Gutter Bin inspection, the approximate depth of debris in inlet , a description of 
debris type, and any other information that would materially affect the operational 
condition of the conveyance. 
g. Reserve the right to engage third -party contractors  (“sub-contractor(s)”), 
preapproved in writing by the City, to conduct inspections, installation, 
maintenance, cleaning or other services for the Gutter Bins as necessary.

3. Responsibilities of the City.  The City shall: 
a. Subject to the conditions set forth in this MOU, permit Frog Creek and any s ub-
contractor(s) of Frog Creek to enter upon the right-of-way subject to this MOU for 
purposes of conducting installation, maintenance and other work relating to the 
Project. 
b. Agrees to consult with Frog Creek on matters relating to the Project. 
c. Promptly review all updated Gutter Bin Installation Schedules submitted to the City 
and prioritize providing a timely written response to any request for schedule 
approval or related review. 
d. Clean all stormwater catch basin locations where Gutter Bins are to be installed. A 
tentative inlet cleaning schedule shall be provided to Frog Creek prior to the final 
execution of this MOU.

e. Clean inlets, pipes and sumps below and/or downstream of any Gutter Bin as part 
of the City’s the annual storm sewer cleaning process. 
f. Accept captured pollutants from Gutter Bins, in accordance with Section 2(d) of 
this MOU, at no cost to Frog Creek or other approved sub-contractor(s). The

Gutter Bin. The notification shall include the reason for the removal. If approved 
by the City, the Gutter Bin may be repaired or replaced then reinstalled by Frog 
Creek at the same or other location(s). 
 
4. Special Provisions. 
a. The City or its representatives shall be authorized to inspect all work and materials 
furnished by Frog Creek and any sub-contractor(s) of Frog Creek. Inspections may 
extend to part or all of the work performed under this MOU and to the preparation, 
fabrication, or manufacturing of materials being used. 
b. At all times during the term of this MOU, Frog Creek and any sub-contractor(s) of 
Frog Creek shall be considered an independent contractor. Neither Frog Creek nor 
anyone employed by it shall represent, act, purport to act, or be deemed to be the 
agent, representative, employee, or servant of the City. 
c. The parties mutually agree to meet annually to exchange updates, conduct product 
training, and foster effective communication, thereby strengthening their 
partnership and ensuring alignment on project goals. 
d. At the time of this MOU, t here are approximately sixty-three (63) Gutter Bins 
installed in the city of Cheyenne, as provided in Exhibit A, which Gutter Bins are 
not part of this Project. The cleaning and maintenance of these existing Gutter Bins 
shall remain the responsibility of the City of Cheyenne. Additionally, 
approximately seven (7) Gutter Bins previously removed by the City of Cheyenne 
will be replaced with new and improved units as part of this Project. Frog Creek 
will maintain these replaced Gutter Bins as part of the Project. 
e. The Parties acknowledge and agree that, in the event a Gutter Bin is observed to be 
obstructing flow during a storm event, the City may, at its sole discretion, remove 
the Gutter Bin as necessary to prevent damage to public or private property. Frog
