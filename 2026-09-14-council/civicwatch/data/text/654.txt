BACKGROUND: 
 
This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from AG – Agricultural to BP – Business Park for 
roughly 73.87 acres of land. The land is currently vacant with sewer and water extensions in the area. No 
specific use accompanies the zone change request. 
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical 
patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1) 
 
This parcel has the Future Land Use designation of the Community Business. The Community 
Business designation is meant to focus on general retail and office, large tenant retail, and 
regional malls, along with offices, hotels, and service businesses. Intensive industrial activities are

This parcel is sits at intersection of a state highway and major collector/principal arterial. 
Development at this location will likely fall in line with similar uses in the area. The BP zone would 
allow for commercial, light industrial, and data center development at this location. At this time, 
staff has no knowledge of the end use for this parcel.    
 
Based on the policies and future land use map, BP Business Park is appropriate in this area.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1. 
 
2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering 
existing zoning, the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies 
created under the guidance of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2) 
 
The proposed BP zone district would be an extension of BP zoning which recently changed north 
of I-80. The northern properties are zoned CB Community Business and BP Business Park across 
Interstate 80. The southern parcel is zoned County LU Land Use and is currently used for 
agriculture and rural residential uses. The western parcels are zoned Agricultural and Heavy 
Industrial. The Agricultural parcel is vacant; the Heavy Industrial zone parcel has a general 
industrial use on it. The parcel to the east is split zoned County P Public and LU Land Use with a 
government facility on it.  
 
Existing zone for this parcel is AG Agricultural. AG Agricultural only allows for limited uses that are 
low intensity in nature. The BP zone would allow for development similar to the surrounding area 
to the north and west.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2. 
   
3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards 
and uses of the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject 
property for development according to the standards and uses of the proposed 
zoning district (UDC 2.2.1.d.3) 
 
The BP zone district allows for select service uses, a variety of commercial uses, light industrial 
uses, and data center development. The current use of the subject property is for vacant or for 
agricultural purposes. The current zone of AG Agricultural allows for limited uses. With the change 
to BP zone district, any development would have to follow the applicable standards to the 
development types. Commercial development would have to meet design standards typical for 
commercial development, while industrial uses would not have design standards. 
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3. 
 
4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed 
according to the Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed 
zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

There will be no changes to the existing roads surrounding the parcel. The parcel is large enough 
to provide a street network if development patterns require smaller lots and blocks. All types of 
zones on this parcel would be limited in street network development by the interstate. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4. 
 
5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of 
the proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific 
standards. (UDC 2.2.1.d.5) 
 
The BP district is intended to support small and large-scale industrial development while 
providing essential commercial services along regional transportation routes in an industrial park 
setting. The district integrates manufacturing, processing, research and development, and 
shipping activities in addition to retail, dining, and convenience store to enhance functionality and 
appeal for businesses and employees.  
 
These Business Park districts should be in area on the edge of the community that are intended to 
support large-scale industrial, manufacturing, shipping, research and development, and data 
center uses. These districts are typically found close to major transportation routes. The district 
has to be a minimum of 320 acres of land that can be interrupted by transportation routes and 
natural features.  
 
This parcel would be unable to qualify for the BP zone by itself based on the lack of acreage. Due 
to a recent zone change to the north to BP, this parcel can join the rest of the business park to the 
north.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property  complies with review criterion 5. 
    
6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for 
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options 
if any specific development proposed in association with the application is not 
executed after approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6) 
 
This parcel can meet all applicable standards of the BP zone district. Currently, there is no 
proposed end use for this property. The BP zone is appropriate for this area due to it’s location 
near an interstate interchange, The parcel can develop in a use that is often at these interchanges 
and support surrounding uses.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6. 
 
7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7) 
 
No major comments received in regard to the proposed zone.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7.
