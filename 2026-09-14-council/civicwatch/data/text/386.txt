ORDINANCE NO. ________ 
 
ENTITLED: "AN ORDINANCE IMPOSING A TEMPORARY TWELVE (12) MONTH 
MORATORIUM ON THE ESTABLISHMENT , CONSTRUCTION,  OR 
CONVERSION OF ANY BUILDINGS OR STRUCTURES FOR USE AS A 
DATA CENTER, INCLUDING ANY CHANGE OF USE  TO A DATA 
CENTER." 
 
 BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, 
WYOMING: 
 
Section 1.  The Governing Body finds and declares that: 
 
A. The City is responsible for adopting ordinances, resolutions and regulations necessary 
for the health, safety, and welfare of City residents; 
 
B. Current zoning and development standards may not adequately address the unique 
characteristics, demands, and potential cumulative effects of data centers , which are 
rapidly proliferating within and near the City; 
 
C. There is considerable public interest and concern among City residents regarding the 
potential impacts of data centers on their health, safety, and general welfare; 
 
D. The Governing Body finds that a temporary a twelve (12) month moratorium is 
necessary on the acceptance and processing of applications and permits related to the 
establishment or expansion of any data center uses, including, but not limited to, 
applications for building permits, site plan and architectural reviews, variances, 
conditional use permits, grading permits, and occupancy permits; and 
 
E. A moratorium will allow City staff the time necessary to study the impacts of data 
centers and develop recommendations for the Governing Body regarding potential 
revisions to the applicable ordinances, codes and regulations pertaining to data centers. 
 
Section 2.  The Governing Body hereby imposes a twelve (12) month moratorium on the 
processing, consideration, approval, or issuance of any licenses, permits, approvals, permitted 
uses, special uses, accessory uses, site plans, conditional uses, rezoning s, or variances that would 
enable or authorize the establishment , construction, or conversion of any building or structure to 
data center use within the City. 
 
Section 3.  This moratorium shall not apply to data centers currently under construction, or 
to the repair or replacement of existing data centers , but shall apply to data centers where on-site 
construction has not commenced. For purposes of this Section, “on -site construction” means the 
commencement of substantial physical site work, such as grading, excavation, or foundation 
installation, and does not include preconstruction activities such as design, engineering, permitting, 
financing, equipment procurement, or c ontractual commitments. Any applications pertaining to 
data centers that have not begun on-site construction shall be held in abeyance during the pendency

of this moratorium until the moratorium has expired, unless the applicant elects to withdraw their 
application. Any required timelines related to an application shall be tolled for the duration of the 
moratorium. City staff shall provide notice to any applicable applicants of the moratorium and 
tolling period. Any fees paid to the City shall be refunded to any applicant that elects to withdraw 
an application related to the establishment, construction, or conversion of a building or structure 
to a data center that is delayed as a result of this moratorium. 
 
Section 4.  City staff shall study the impact of data centers on the environment, electricity 
rates, the power grid, water usage, and any other factors related to the health, safety, and welfare 
of City residents. Staff shall report the results of the study to the Governing Body not later than 
April 1, 2027, and shall include recommendations to the Governing Body for any changes to  
applicable codes, rules, or regulations. 
 
Section 5.  For purposes of this Ordinance, “data center” means as defined by § 1.4.4.(f) of 
the Cheyenne Unified Development Code. 
 
 Section 6.  Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases of this ordinance are 
severable. If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase  of this ordinance is declared 
unconstitutional or otherwise unlawful, such declaration shall not affect any of the remaining 
sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases. 
 
 Section 7.  This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon approval and publication  
for twelve (12) months from the date of publication unless further extended by the Governing 
Body.
