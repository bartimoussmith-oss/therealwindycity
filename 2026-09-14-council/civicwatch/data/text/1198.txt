BACKGROUND: 
 
The applicant is requesting approval of a preliminary plat for roughly 11.92 acres of land. The proposed plat 
would subdivide the parcel into 86 Lots for single-family residential, one lot for open space, and one lot for 
future development. A portion of the proposed Lots would meet the requirements for Cottage Lot 
Development, while the other lots are slightly under the required Lot width for the smallest allowed Lot in 
this zone district.  
 
The smallest width allowed is the DD8 Lot, which is 30-feet. The applicant is proposing 27-foot wide Lots 
based on the specific type of development they would like to provide. Should the Planning Commission and 
the Governing Body find the development pattern acceptable and approve the plat with the understanding 
of the product proposed, an administrative adjustment would be granted by Staff for a reduction in Lot 
width.

1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns 
identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.2.d.1) 
 
On the future land use map found in PlanCheyenne, the subject land and surrounding area has 
been designated as Urban Residential. The Urban Residential designation encourages a range of 
residential uses. The proposed plat is to provide additional single-family residential to the area 
in a cottage lot format.  
 
Foundation 1 of PlanCheyenne encourages community growth. This development is in response 
to market demands and is within the Urban Service Boundary. The development patterns 
proposed by this plat are compatible with the designations found on the future land use map. 
 
The application complies with this review criterion.  
 
2) The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to existing 
and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration of current and future 
streets, open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or facilities. (UDC 2.1.2.d.2) 
 
The proposed plat subdivides a larger parcel into 86 residential Lots. This plat adds an additional 
road and extends the utilities in the area allowing for further development. The cottage lot style 
of development pattern provides a higher density in terms of units per acre than the traditional 
single-family development pattern to the east, but at a lower density than the multifamily 
development pattern to the west. 
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
   
3) The division of land meets the purposes, intent, and all applicable standards in Article 4. (UDC 
2.1.2.d.3) 
 
Community Facility Fees (4.2): 
For fees based on acreage, the area of the plat is pro-rated to the nearest tenth of an acre. 
Lands reserved as unbuildable (for right-of-way, detention, parks, remainder lots, etc), are 
exempt from fees.

Infrastructure Fee     $2,380 ($350 per acre)

Total Land Acquisition & Infrastructure Fees $0.00 
 
Per UDC Section 4.2.5, Table 4-2, Enhancement Fees are payable at the issuance of a 
building permit for each structure. 
Residential Enhancement Fee $400 per dwelling unit 
 
Transportation Network (4.3): 
This plat is creating an additional street that will add to the street network in the area and 
provide access to the new subdivision. There are also alleys proposed to allow access to the 
proposed Lots. The plat is taking place on a larger block than is typically allowed, but the 
development provides a street to the western border, allowing a through connection should 
the area to the west ever redevelop. An exception to block size will not be required, as this 
plat provides for potential future connections.

Open Space (4.4): 
Tract A and Lot 1, Block 5 are proposed to be open space. Cottage lot development requires 
open spaces to be developed with a site plan, and this project will have a site plan to 
formalize open space amenities after plat approval. 
 
Required Improvements (4.5): 
This plat appears to be able to provide all required utilities to the lots within this 
development. 
 
Public Safety Fees (4.6): 
Public Safety Fees are required at the time of construction of any residential building on any 
of the proposed lots. Per UDC Section 4.6.3.b, Public Safety Fees are payable at the issuance 
of a building permit for each structure.

Most lots proposed for development are buildable lots in this zone district. The proposed lots

7) All parcels not proposed as buildable lots and which are not dedicated to and accepted by the 
City shall have a clearly defined entity responsible for ongoing maintenance.  (UDC 2.1.2.d.7) 
 
Track A and Lot 1, Block 5 are not proposed to be buildable lots. Tract A is proposed to be for 
open space, utilities and drainage. Lot 1, Block 5 is noted as being for future development. Lot 1, 
Block 5 could be replatted in the future to allow that area to develop or it could be developed 
with open space. All open space is proposed to be maintained by an HOA.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
 
8) The application will not create any undue influence on existing or potential development for 
adjacent property considering the existing zoning and Comprehensive Plan policies for that

This plat will not create any undue influence on existing or potential development. There are 
existing streets to the north and to the east of the proposed development. The land to the west 
is already developed and the land to the south is owned by the same developer. Should the 
developer wish to develop to the south, they can always replat Lot 1, Block 5 as it is not 
proposed to have any development at this time.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.

City staff is satisfied that this proposed plat would not adversely affect the public health, safety,
