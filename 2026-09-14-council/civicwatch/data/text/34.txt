Dear Council Members:
Pursuant to W.S. 6-7-10l(a)(ii)(F)(VI), please accept this letter as notice that the Horse Nations
Indian Relay Council (HNIRC) will be holding a Calcutta in conjunction with the 2026 Indian
Relay Championship of Champions on Sunday, September 27, 2026, at Frontier Park in
Cheyenne, Wyoming.
The Horse Nations Indian Relay Council is aware of the statutory requirements governing
Calcutta wagering and will conduct the Calcutta in full compliance with Wyoming law. The
rules governing the Calcutta will be made available to all participants prior to the event. Proceeds
from the Calcutta will be distributed as follows:
75% to the successful bidder;
10% to bona fide charitable organizations;
10% to teams
 5% to offset the costs associated with producing and administering the Calcutta;
This notice is provided in accordance with the Wyoming Statutes referenced above. While no
formal action is required by the City Council, we would be pleased to answer any questions or
provide additional information regarding the Calcutta or the Indian Relay Championship of
Champions.
Thank you for your continued support of this unique cultural and sporting event. We appreciate
your consideration and look forward to another successful event in Cheyenne.
