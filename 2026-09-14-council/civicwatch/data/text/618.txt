 
 
RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION AMENDING CITY PROVIDED HEALTH 
INSURANCE RELATED  BENEFITS FOR SPECIFIED PERMANENT 
PART-TIME CITY EMPLOYEES AND MEMBERS OF THE CITY 
COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE." 
 
WHEREAS, members of the City Council of the City of Cheyenne serve in part-time 
capacities as members of the City Council and are currently eligible for paid term life insurance, 
health and dental insurance, supplemental life insurance, and flexible medical benefits 
reimbursement programs through the City;  
 
WHEREAS, part-time employees of the City of Cheyenne do not qualify for these 
benefits; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body finds that aligning the benefits available to members 
of the City Council with those available to other part-time personnel promotes fairness among 
those who work on behalf of the City in part-time roles. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE 
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that members of the City Council , by virtue of their 
status as members of the Council,  shall be eligible to enroll in, participate in, or receive  City 
provided paid term life insurance, health insurance, dental insurance, supplemental life 
insurance, or flexible medical benefits reimbursement programs offered by or through the City, 
whether such benefits are provided directly or indirectly.  
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that permanent part -time employees who have 
averaged not less than twenty (20) hours of work per week for not less than the previous three 
(3) consecutive years shall be eligible to enroll in, participate in, or receive City provided paid 
term life insurance, health insurance, dental insurance, supplemental life insurance, or flexible 
medical benefits reimbursement programs offered by or through the City, whether such benefits 
are provided directly or indirectly. 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that one hundred percent (100%) of the premium owed 
for the respective benefits shall be paid by the respective member of the City Council or part -
time employee. The City shall not pay, reimburse, subsidize, or otherwise bear any portion of 
premiums, costs, expenses, or other financial obligations associated with paid term life 
insurance, health insurance, dental insurance, supplemental life insurance, flexible medi cal 
benefits reimbursement programs, or any other similar or related benefit for any member of the 
City Council or eligible part -time employee, whether such benefits are provided directly or 
indirectly, or funded in whole or in part by the City; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that any portion of Resolution No. 3652, adopted April 
10, 1995, or other any other resolution, rule, regulation, or policy authorizing members of the 
City Council of the City of Cheyenne or eligible part-time employees to receive these benefits 
 
 
through the City or providing for any City contribution toward such benefits by virtue of their 
membership on the City Council or part-time employee status is hereby repealed to the extent 
they are inconsistent with this Resolution . City staff shall take all necessary administrative, 
contractual, and regulatory actions to amend any applicable rules, regulations, policies, benefit 
plans, and agreements in accordance with this Resolution; and 
 
BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED, that this Resolution shall be effective and applicable, 
with respect to City Council Members , only to City Council Members whose terms of office 
commence in 2027 and thereafter, and shall not affect the compensation or benefits of any 
member during a term of office already in progress as of the effective date of this Resolution . 
Nothing in this Resolution shall be interpreted to affect the benefits available or applicable to 
full-time employees.
