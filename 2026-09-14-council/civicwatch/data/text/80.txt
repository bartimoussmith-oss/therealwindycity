Public Services Committee  Recommendation:  
At Public Services Committee on July 20, 2026 there was discussion around staff recommendation 2.a 
and the timing of the requirement. The applicant requested that 2.a not be at the time of recording as 
the whole point of the plat is to donate the land for recreational purposes. There is no funding coming 
from this plat that would allow the applicant to build the roads at this time but the land cannot be 
donated until the plat is recorded. So rather than hold up the plat at recording, a plan for construction 
of the streets would be required prior to development actions taking place which would give the 
applicant time to coordinate how and when the roads will be built.   
 
Public Services Committee recommended approval with the following exceptions and conditions: 
1. The following exceptions be granted: 
a. Exceptions to block size for perimeter blocks due to open space and civic uses.  
2. Prior to recording of the final plat: 
a. Technical corrections be made. 
3. Prior to a development action: 
a. A plan for instillation of all necessary public and private improvements be in place 
and assurance by bond or certified check or a development agreement or other 
written legal obligations be approved by the City. The development agreement shall 
be drafted by the applicant and reviewed and agreed upon by the Planning and 
Development Director and the City Engineer.  
 
 
Staff and the applicant are agreeable to this recommendation.
