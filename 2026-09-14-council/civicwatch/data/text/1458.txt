ORDINANCE NO. ________ 
 
ENTITLED: “AN ORDINANCE REPEALING AND REPLACING ORDINANCE 
NO. 4603, SPECIFYING THE MONTHLY STORMWATER RUNOFF 
MANAGEMENT SYSTEM USER FEE IN ACCORDANCE WITH 
SECTION 13.32.050 OF THE MUNICIPAL CODE OF THE CITY OF 
CHEYENNE, WYOMING, AND PROVIDING A REVISED EFFECTIVE 
DATE.” 
 
 BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, 
WYOMING: 
 
Section 1.  That the prior ordinance specifying the monthly stormwater runoff management 
system user fee adopted pursuant to Section 13.32.050 of the Municipal Code of the City of 
Cheyenne, Wyoming, and previously providing for an effective date of no sooner than January 1, 
2026, is hereby repealed in its entirety and replaced by this Ordinance. 
 
Section 2.  That in accordance with Section 13.32.050 of the Municipal Code of the City 
of Cheyenne, Wyoming, a monthly user fee is hereby established to be imposed on each and every 
developed property within the City as provided in this section: 
 
A. Section 13.32.050(C) of the Municipal Code requires the Governing Body to establish 
by ordinance the fee to be imposed for use of and discharge to the City’s stormwater 
runoff management system; and 
 
B. In accordance with Section 13.32.050(B) of the Municipal Code, the fee shall be in the 
amount of two thousand four hundred ten-thousandths of a dollar ($0.0024) per square 
foot of hard surface. 
 
 Section 3.  This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon approval and publication 
but no sooner than March 5, 2027. 
 
 Section 4.  Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases of this ordinance are 
severable. If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase  of this ordinance is declared 
unconstitutional or otherwise unlawful, such declaration shall not affect any of the remaining 
sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases. 
 
 Section 5.  This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon approval and publication.
