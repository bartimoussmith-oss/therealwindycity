MEMORANDUM  
TO:  Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council 
FROM: TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager 
DATE: February 4, 2026 
SUBJECT: Consideration of Bid #E-12-26 for furnishing One (1) New Recycling Vacuum & 
High Pressure Water Jet Truck 66K GVWR for the Street and Alley Division 
 ______________________________________________ 
 Three (3) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.   
 
The Three (3) bids were received from: 
1. McCandless Truck Center, LLC 
2. Floyd’s Truck Center Inc. 
3. Kaiser Premier 
 
The bids were reviewed by Bryan Bell (Fleet Maintenance), Bill Gonzales (Street & Alley), Vicki 
Nemecek (Public Works), and Craig LaVoy (Public Works). 
 
The Bids submitted by McCandless Truck Center, LLC & Floyd’s Truck Center Inc, have been 
rejected, due to the bids not fully meeting the minimum specifications of the Bid.  Specifically: 
a. Both vendors (McCandless & Floyd’s) provided the same body style for the Sewer 
Jetter Body, and their proposed option did not meet over 100 of the line item 
specifications.   
b. There were other Truck specifications that both companies took exceptions to, but 
not meeting the Sewer Jetter Body specifications were the most substantial. 
 
Their recommendation is to accept the  next lowest responsive bid  from Kaiser Premier, who 
submitted the Bid closest meeting the minimum specifications. 
 
The total requested bid award is for $848,639.00. 
 
Funding:   020-26-2616-42-43102 (1% Sales Tax 19-22)
