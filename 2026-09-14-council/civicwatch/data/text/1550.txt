MEMORANDUM 
TO: The Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Cheyenne City Council Members 
FROM:  Robin Lockman, City Treasurer 
DATE:  January 5, 2026 
SUBJECT: Reappropriation to be considered for approval 
On January 12, 2026, the Governing Body will consider referral of a resolution to the Finance Committee 
to reappropriate funds within the Fiscal Year 2026 City of Cheyenne Budget, pursuant to W.S. §§ 16-4 -
112 and 16-4-113. This memorandum provides a summary of the proposed reappropriation. 
General Fund Reserves 
 On December 8, 2025, the Governing Body approved a resolution authorizing a 3% cost -of-
living salary adjustment for eligible full-time and part -time City of Cheyenne employees, 
effective with the December 31, 2025, payroll. To implement this action, this r eappropriation 
increases the applicable budget line items for full-time employees only for the remaining six 
months of Fiscal Year 2026 by $554,652 from General Fund reserves and $160,770 from all other 
funds. 
 The City Clerk’s Department requests $23,270 from General Fund reserves to pay for the 
Granicus Peak Agenda Management and FOIA Solution software subscriptions, replacing 
American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) funds that have expired and were previously used for this 
purpose. This item was inadvertently omitted from the City Clerk’s Fiscal Year 2026 budget 
request. 
 An additional $5,000 from General Fund reserves is requested for appraisal services related to 
the former Laramie County Senior Center at 2101 Thomes Avenue.  
 Finally, $58,250 from General Fund reserves is requested to reimburse Laramie County for rent 
and utilities for office space leased on the 3rd and 5th floors of the US Bank Building at 2020 
Carey Avenue from October through December 2025, of which $47,750 is for rent and $10,500 
is for utilities. 
Other Funds  
Local and State Grant Fund 
 $1,500,000 – Wyoming Business Council grant for the Project Bison Microsoft Data Center. 
 $150,855 – Board of Public Utilities joint projects funds for the 18th Street Storm Sewer 
Interceptor Project. 
 $150,000 – Cheyenne Community Recreation District grant for the Dunbar Ballfields Lighting 
Project. 
 $236,641 – Wyoming State Parks and Cultural Resources funds for the High Plains Arboretum 
Irrigation Construction Project. 
Law Enforcement Grant Fund 
 $6,400 – Wyoming Association of Chiefs of Police Alcohol Compliance Inspection contract 
Federal Grants Fund 
 $35,000 – Wyoming Energy Authority, Energy Audit and Retrofit grant for the Transfer Station. 
 $36,360 – Wyoming Office of Homeland Security grant for air purifying respirators for the 
Cheyenne Police Department. 
 
 $262,500 – Wyoming Office of Homeland Security grant for scoping and design for Clear Creek 
Flood Detention Storage. 
Solid Waste Fund 
 $12,769 from the Solid Waste Fund reserves to pay for the purchase of a new copier for the 
Transfer station. 
Special Purpose Option Tax Fund 
 $300,000 in grant match funds for the Dunbar Ballfields Lighting Project.  
 $3,600,000 for City Contract #8274 with Cheyenne LEADS for the administration of the Large 
Gap Finance Program to address financing gaps in eligible economic development projects. 
 $2,200,380 for carryover reserves that were not budgeted in the prior reappropriation ($14,046 
for expenses associated with the fire trucks ballot, $200,000 for Greenway maintenance and 
$1,986,332 for City roads projects). 
Fiduciary Fund 
 $16,760 from the Historic Preservation Board account to pay for 2025 Christmas ornaments and 
repairs made to the Historic Airport Fountain.  These funds were earned by and belong to the 
Historic Preservation Board, with the City acting solely in a fiduciary capacity.  
Miscellaneous Agency Fund 
 $83,750 for various line items including $20,000 for the Police forfeitures account, $35,000 for 
the Police projects account, $350 for the Car Seat Program, $4,100 for the Fire projects account, 
$4,300 for Community Risk Reduction (CCR), and $20,000 for the Police evidence account.  These 
amounts are covered by reserve balances in each separate account. 
Requested Action 
Please review the above and consider adoption of the attached resolution authorizing these 
appropriations.
