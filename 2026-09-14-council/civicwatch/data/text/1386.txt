Bid #E-10-25 was solicited for One (1) Front Load Refuse Truck, and Three (3) Dual Arm Refuse 
Trucks for the Sanitation Division. 
 
One (1) bidder responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.  The Bid was received from: 
1. Cheyenne Peterbilt Inc. Jackson Group Peterbilt, Cheyenne, Wyoming 
 
The bid was reviewed by Vicki Nemecek (Public Works), Craig LaVoy (Public Works), Austin Reiner 
(Sanitation), and Bryan Bell (Fleet Maintenance) , and their recommendation is to accept the lowest 
responsive bid from Cheyenne Peterbilt Inc. Jackson Group Peterbilt. 
 
Cheyenne Peterbilt Inc. Jackson Group Peterbilt submitted two (2) pricing options for the Front 
Load Style Trucks.  Their ‘Option 1’ submittal did not meet the minimum requirements for the 
specifications, so it will not be considered.   
 
The total Bid Award is for $2,038,492.00 (Purchase of 4 Trucks, Less Trades).
