Background:  
The item for consideration is a Resolution overruling a Planning Commission denial of a proposed   
Future Land Use Map Amendment (or Master Plan Amendment).  This is needed to fulfil Wyoming State 
Statute 15-1-506 and allow the Future Land Use Map Amendment to be considered by the Governing 
Body.    
 
The Planning Commission heard a future land use map amendment at their September 8, 2026, 
meeting and unanimously voted 6-0 to deny the amendment. Wyoming State Stature requires a 
majority of the Commission to vote in favor which would require four members to have voted ‘yes’ on a 
motion to approve. Wyoming State Statute allows the Governing Body to overrule the Planning 
Commission and consider plans or plan amendments the Planning Commission disapproves. 
 
The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as follows: 
• Introduction and Referral   September 14, 2026 
• Public Services Committee   September 21, 2026 
• Final Consideration  September 28, 2026
