BACKGROUND: 
 
The applicant is requesting approval of a preliminary plat for roughly 6.50 acres of land. The proposed plat 
would subdivide the parcel into 5 residential lots and 1 common lot. Five of the lots will be the buildings that 
will be sold to individual owners. The remaining lot is the common space in which building owner will be given 
1/5th interest in maintaining the common space to the approved site plan.  
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Preliminary Plat, the Commission 
must make the following findings required in UDC 2.1.2.d  – a detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical 
patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.2.d.1) 
 
The Future Land Use Designation for this property is Mixed-Use Commercial. According to the 
comprehensive plan, this designation supports more commercial, light industrial, or live -work 
developments that could be “activity centers”. Residential use may be added to these areas in

2) The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to 
existing and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration 
of current and future streets, open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or 
facilities. (UDC 2.1.2.d.2) 
 
This plat will have minimal impact on surrounding development. To the north and east, there is 
residential uses already developed. To the south and west there are industrial sites that have been 
developed. These sites are already developed and operating with similar or supportive uses of this  
plat’s expected development plans.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
   
3) The division of land meets the purposes, intent, and all applicable standards in 
Article 4. (UDC 2.1.2.d.3) 
 
Community Facility Fees (4.2): 
For fees based on acreage, the area of the property is pro-rated to the nearest tenth of an 
acre. Lands reserved as unbuildable (for right-of-way, detention, parks, remainder lots, etc), 
are exempt from fees. Per UDC Section 4.2.5, Table 4-2, Land acquisition, Infrastructure, and 
Enhancement Fees are all payable at the issuance of a building permit for each structure.  
 
Transportation Network (4.3): 
This plat is not creating any new Rights-of-Way. There will be two access points along Richard 
Martin Drive. There are sidewalks that will be connected to an existing system in the area. 
 
Open Space (4.4): 
There is currently a site plan undergoing review. It is stated on the site plan, the property will 
being using existing public open space credit with the addition of deck and balconies on the 
structures.   
 
Required Improvements (4.5): 
This plat appears to be able to provide all required utilities to the lots within this 
development.

Public Safety Fees (4.6): 
Public Safety Fees are required at the time of construction of any residential building on any 
of the proposed lots. Per UDC Section 4.6.3.b, Public Safety Fees are payable at the issuance 
of a building permit for each structure.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.

Drainage: 
A drainage study was submitted and reviewed. There were no concerns with drainage at the plat

Traffic: 
A transportation study was submitted and reviewed. There were no concerns with traffic at the 
plat level for the project as presented.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion. 
 
6) Lots proposed for development shall be buildable lots according to the existing or 
proposed zoning district standards, result in design and development patterns that 
meet the Intent and Applicability statements of the existing or proposed zoning 
district, and not aggravate nonconforming setbacks or currently adopted building 
and fire code access and separation requirements. (UDC 2.1.2.d.6) 
 
The proposed plat is in the NR-3 zone district. Multi-dwelling buildings are a permitted use in this 
district, meaning that all Multi-dwelling Building Lot Types are allowable in the NR-3 zone district. 
The Intent and Applicability of the NR-3 zone district is to promote high-density, urban residential 
neighborhoods where compact neighborhood developments are desired. Lots proposed on the 
plat are reflective of buildings on the site plan and are buildable in the NR-3 zone.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.
