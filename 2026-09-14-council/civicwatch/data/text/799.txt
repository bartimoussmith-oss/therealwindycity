BACKGROUND: 
 
The applicant is requesting annexing 20.41 acres of land to allow for residential development in the City. The 
land is currently vacant. The annexed area will be assigned a City zone based on the underlying County zone. 
No other applications have been submitted for this property at this time.   
 
Land annexed to the City requires a City zone. The petition to annex has been accompanied by a zone change 
request to NR-3 Neighborhood Residential High Density. 
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
To accept the petition to annex and approve the annexation Ordinance, the Governing Body must make 
the following findings required in WS 15-1-402; a detailed analysis of which is below: 
 
1) An annexation of the area is for the protection of the health, safety and welfare of 
the persons residing in the area and in the city or town.  [WS 15-1-402 (a) (i)] 
 
The annexation of this property will increase the health, safety, and welfare of the future 
occupants. By being in City limits, the property will receive access to City Fire, Police, water, 
sanitary sewer, and sanitation services.    
 
The application complies with this review criterion. 
 
2) The urban development of the area sought to be annexed would constitute a 
natural, geographical, economical and social part of the annexing city or town. [WS 
15-1-402 (a) (ii)]

These properties are currently vacant but are within a major commercial and residential area of 
the City. They are surrounded by urban development to the east and the south making these 
properties a natural, geographic, economic, and social part of the city. The properties are adjacent 
to the City to the south and west. The contiguity to the City and the proximity to surrounding 
urban-style development makes the parcel a natural geographic part of the City. The 
comprehensive plan encourages urban development in this area. The annexation of these 
properties will benefit those who live within the City and will be a part of the social and economic 
fabric that is Cheyenne. These parcels are part of a larger County Pocket.    
 
The application complies with this review criterion. 
   
3) The area sought to be annexed is a logical and feasible addition to the annexing 
city or town and the extension of basic and other services customarily available to 
residents of the city or town shall, within reason, be available to the area proposed 
to be annexed. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (iii)] 
 
The area is a logical and feasible addition to the City due to the reasons previously listed. All City 
services normally available to residents will also be made available. Police and fire protection will 
be extended upon annexation. Utilities are adjacent to the area and can be extended upon 
developmental action. 
 
The application complies with this review criterion. 
 
4) The area sought to be annexed is contiguous with or adjacent to the annexing city 
or town, or the area meets the requirements of W.S. 15-1-407. [WS 15-1-402 (a) 
(iv)] 
 
The parcel adjacent to Converse Avenue and East Carlson is roughly 62% contiguous.    
 
The application complies with this review criterion. 
 
5) If the city or town does not own or operate its own electric utility, its governing 
body is prepared to issue one (1) or more franchises as necessary to serve the 
annexed area pursuant to W.S. 15-1-410. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (v)] 
 
Utility franchises are in place to serve the annexed area.   
 
The application complies with this review criterion. 
    
6) The annexing city or town, not less than twenty (20) business days prior to the 
public hearing required by W.S. 15-1-405(a), has sent by certified mail to all 
landowners and affected public utilities within the territory a summary of the 
proposed annexation report as required under subsection (c) of this section and 
notice of the time, date and location of the public hearing required by W.S. 15-1-
405(a). [WS 15-1-402 (a) (vi)]

CONFORMANCE WITH APPLICABLE PLANS: 
 
The project is located in an area covered by the following plans: 
• PlanCheyenne 
 
The Future Land Use Map of PlanCheyenne, as updated by Connect 2045, anticipates this area of the 
community to develop with Urban Residential uses. These areas are expected to develop with full access 
to urban services – such areas are best served by being within City limits. The area is within the Urban 
Service Boundary.
