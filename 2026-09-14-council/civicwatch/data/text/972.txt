RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CITY OF CHEYENNE TO 
ENTER INTO A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING WITH 
CHEYENNE LEADS TO ESTABLISH AND ADMINISTER A LARGE 
PROJECTS GAP FINANCE PROGRAM, UTILIZING VOTER -
APPROVED 6TH PENNY SALES  AND USE  TAX FUNDS FOR 
ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT PURPOSES." 
 
WHEREAS, in accordance with state law, counties in Wyoming are authorized to impose 
a specific purpose excise tax , commonly referred to as the "6th Penny Tax ," upon approval by a 
majority of the county’s qualified electors, with the requirement that all revenues generated from 
the tax be expended solely for the purpose specified on the ballot; 
 
WHEREAS, as part of  the November 2021 election, the qualified electors of Laramie 
County approved Proposition 12, authorizing the allocation of $3,500,000.00, plus interest earned 
thereon, to the City of Cheyenne to fund  large project completion efforts, including through 
collaboration with outside partners such as the Cheyenne-Laramie County Corporation for 
Economic Development (“Cheyenne Leads”), to support large community projects; 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne intends to establish and implement a Large Projects Gap 
Finance Program ("the Program") to address capital financing gaps in high -impact projects that 
will provide significant benefit  to the city, including but not limited to  expanding the tax base, 
creating housing and employment opportunities, and attracting further private investment; 
 
WHEREAS, the Program is intended to allow for various types of funding, to include but 
not necessarily be limited to: (1) revolving loan funds that provide repayable financing for eligible 
projects; and/or (2) capital contribution funds, through which capital may be contributed to eligible 
projects under terms that allow for either deferred repayment or conversion into an equity interest, 
thereby offering greater flexibility to address varied financing needs while maintaining the long -
term viability and sustainability of the Program; 
 
WHEREAS, revolving loan  and capital contribution  funds are nationally recognized 
economic development tools that leverage public and private dollars to support significant projects, 
with repayments from early loan recipients used to support future applicants, thereby sustaining 
the funds over time;  
 
WHEREAS, Cheyenne Leads, a Wyoming nonprofit corporation recognized under Section 
501(c)(6) of the Internal Revenue Code, serves as the designated economic development 
organization for the City of Cheyenne and Laramie County and possesses extensive experience, 
longstanding industry relationships, and demonstrated success in administering complex economic 
development programs; 
 
WHEREAS, the 501(c)(6) designation allows Cheyenne LEADS to operate as a business 
association focused on promoting regional economic growth without the restrictions typical of 
charitable nonprofits, enabling it to engage directly in business recruitment, site development, and 
public-private partnerships on behalf of the community; 
 
WHEREAS, since its establishment in 1986, Cheyenne LEADS has successfully recruited 
over 80 companies to the region, resulting in the creation of more than 5,000 jobs, an estimated 
$170 million in annual payroll, and over $1.2 billion in capital investment, while also developing 
critical infrastructure such as the Cheyenne Business Parkway and the North Range Business Park; 
 
WHEREAS, Cheyenne LEADS, based on its qualifications, demonstrated performance, 
and alignment with the goals of the Program, has agreed to serve as the fiscal agent and 
administrator of the Program, and to be responsible for overseeing its implementation, ensuring 
accountability and compliance, and managing the Program’s financial and operational functions, 
while also contributing a substantial financial investment to support the Program’s success; 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body finds that a public -private partnership with Cheyenne 
LEADS is in the best interest of the City, and desires to formalize the structure and administration 
of the Program through a Memorandum of Understanding, which shall define the respective rights, 
responsibilities and obligations of the parties, and shall be consistent with the terms and 
requirements set forth in this Resolution; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Program shall adhere to the following  guidelines and limitations, 
consistent with generally accepted practices for economic development programs of this kind: 
 
1. Loans and capital contributions shall be used exclusively to support the completion 
of large -scale projects located within the City of Cheyenne that demonstrate a 
material public benefit, including, but not limited to, job creation, expansion of the 
tax base, and community revitalization. Loans and capital contributions under the 
Program are intended solely to bridge the financing gap between funding already 
secured for a project and the amount necessary to complete the project. No funds 
shall be disbursed until Cheyenne LEADS has received and reviewed 
documentation it deems sufficient to provide reasonable assurance that the project 
will be completed as proposed. 
 
2. Program participants shall be required to demonstrate a minimum equity 
investment in the project, in an amount determined by Cheyenne LEADS to be 
commercially reasonable and consistent with prevailing industry standards, taking 
into account  the size, scope, and nature of the project. In general, and unless 
otherwise specifically justified, such minimum equity investment shall not be less 
than twenty percent (20%) of total project costs. The participant’s equity 
contribution shall be fully committed, documented to the satisfaction of Cheyenne 
LEADS, and verified prior to the disbursement of any loan  or capital contribution 
funds under the Program. Acceptable forms of equity may include cash, land, or 
other assets directly attributable to the project, but shall not include prior sunk costs, 
in-kind services, or speculative valuations unless expressly approved by Cheyenne 
LEADS. 
 
3. Program participants may include developers, businesses, or public -private 
partnerships with verifiable financial capacity and a clearly articulated, actionable 
plan demonstrating a high likelihood of project completion. Loan amounts shall be 
based on documented project need and fund availability  but shall not exceed 
seventy-five percent (75%) of the project’s loan -to-value (LTV) or loan -to-cost 
(LTC) ratio, whichever is less, unless otherwise determined by Cheyenne LEADS 
to be commercially reasonable and prudent under the circ umstances, consistent 
with sound public lending standards. 
 
4. Cheyenne LEADS, shall conduct a formal review of each project that has received 
funding under the Program no later than ten (10) years following the initial 
disbursement of funds, or at such other time as may be appropriate based on the 
terms and conditions of the applic able funding agreement. This review shall take 
into account  such factors as  the character of the development, whether it be 
industrial, residential or other development type, the experience of the developer, 
the project’s financial condition, its operation al performance, and the long-term 
viability of the project. Such review is to be conducted to determine whether: ( 1) 
repayment of any or all Program funds, including repayment through a deferred 
balloon payment, is appropriate; (2) conversion of the Program’s financial interest 
into an equity position is warranted; or (3) an extension or other modification of the 
original funding terms is advisable. Cheyenne LEADS shall ensure that 
documentation of the review, including the basis for any determinations made , is 
maintained in the project file as part of the Program’s official records. 
 
5. All loans shall be adequately secured through collateral acceptable to Cheyenne 
LEADS, which may include but is not limited to liens, escrow accounts, debt 
limitations, cash sweeps, pledge or assignment of rights, corporate approval rights, 
personal guaran tees, or such other instruments as Cheyenne LEADS deems 
necessary to protect the public interest. 
 
6. Loan or capital contribution proceeds shall be used solely for direct investment in 
the approved project. Under no circumstances shall loan funds be used to refinance 
existing debt incurred prior to the commencement of the project. 
 
7. All Program applications shall be subject to a comprehensive risk assessment and 
review, which may be conducted, in whole or in part, by qualified third -party 
professionals designated by Cheyenne LEADS. Applicants must demonstrate, to 
the satisfaction of Cheyenne LEADS, the project’s viability and shall provide, at a 
minimum, a balance sheet , cash flow  statement, and other documentation as 
determined by LEADS, sufficient to support the Applicant’s ability to successfully 
complete the project.  
 
8. Loan agreements shall include clear performance benchmarks, progress reporting, 
and binding clawback provisions enforceable upon default, non -performance, or 
abandonment to safeguard public funds in the event of non -performance, default, 
or project abandonment. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that  the  City of Cheyenne is authorized to execute of a 
Memorandum of Understanding  (MOU) with Cheyenne LEADS to establish and administer the 
Large Project Gap Finance Program, which shall include but not necessarily be limited to revolving 
loan funds and/or  capital contribution funds, to address financing gaps in eligible economic 
development projects; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the MOU shall incorporate all guidelines, roles, 
responsibilities, and review processes set forth in this Resolution and may be amended from time 
to time by written mutual agreement of the parties to adapt to evolving program needs; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that  the City of Cheyenne commits $3,500,000.00, plus 
the interest earned thereon,  of the 6th Penny funds previously approved by the voters for large 
project completion to seed the Program in accordance with Proposition 12 and the approved ballot 
language; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that any repayments, returns, or proceeds received from 
loan or capital contribution s shall be reinvested into the Program to support the completion of 
future large projects in a manner that promotes the continued sustainability of the funds; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the City of Cheyenne acknowledges and appreciates 
the financial contribution and programmatic leadership of Cheyenne LEADS as a co -investor, 
fiscal agent and administrator; and 
 
BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED, that the Mayor is hereby authorized to take any and all 
actions necessary to implement this resolution and to finalize and execute the MOU, subject to 
approval by the City Attorney.
