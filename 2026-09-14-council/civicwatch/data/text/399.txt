BACKGROUND: 
 
The applicant is requesting approval of a preliminary plat for roughly 1.42 acres of land. The land is currently 10 
parcels around a cul-de-sac originally platted in the 1980’s, but the lots never developed. The proposed plat 
would subdivide the parcel into 9 lots. Eight of the lots will be the buildings that will be sold to individual 
owners. The remaining lot is the common space in which each building owner will be given 1/8th interest in 
maintaining the common space to the approved site plan.

On the future land use map found in PlanCheyenne, the subject land and surrounding area has 
been designated as Urban Residential. The Urban Residential designation encourages a range of 
residential uses. The proposed plat is to provide additional multi-family development in the area.  
 
Foundation 1 of PlanCheyenne encourages community growth. This development is in response 
to market demands and is within the Urban Service Boundary. The development patterns 
proposed by this plat are compatible with the designations found on the future la nd use map. 
 
The application complies with this review criterion.  
 
2) The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to 
existing and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration 
of current and future streets, open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or 
facilities. (UDC 2.1.2.d.2) 
 
This plat will have minimal impact on surrounding development. To the west, east, and south 
surrounding the site are several single-family residential dwellings. To the north of the property is 
school. These sites are already developed and operating with similar or supportive uses of  this 
plat’s expected development plans.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
   
3) The division of land meets the purposes, intent, and all applicable standards in 
Article 4. (UDC 2.1.2.d.3) 
 
Community Facility Fees (4.2): 
For fees based on acreage, the area of the property is pro-rated to the nearest tenth of an 
acre. Lands reserved as unbuildable (for right-of-way, detention, parks, remainder lots, etc), 
are exempt from fees. Per UDC Section 4.2.5, Table 4-2, Land acquisition, Infrastructure, and 
Enhancement Fees are all payable at the issuance of a building permit for each structure. 
 
Transportation Network (4.3): 
This plat is not creating any new rights-of-way as Nova Road is being vacant with this plat. 
There are two points of access to the property from E 12 th Street. There is attached sidewalk 
already existing in the area.  
 
Open Space (4.4): 
This plat has a site plan that is currently undergoing review process. It is expected that open 
space requirements will be meant upon approval of the site plan. 
 
Required Improvements (4.5): 
This plat appears to be able to provide all required utilities to the lots within this 
development. 
 
Public Safety Fees (4.6): 
Public Safety Fees are required at the time of construction of any residential building on any 
of the proposed lots. Per UDC Section 4.6.3.b, Public Safety Fees are payable at the issuance 
of a building permit for each structure.

The proposed plat is in the MR zone district. Multifamily residential development is a conditional

Multi-dwelling Building within the MR zone. All Multi-dwelling Building Lot Types are allowed 
within the MR zone upon conditional use approval. The Intent and Applicability of the MR zone is 
intended for mix of density and dwelling types, especially in areas that more compact 
neighborhood development patterns are desires. The lots proposed are reflective of buildings on 
an approved site plan and are buildable in the MR zone.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.
