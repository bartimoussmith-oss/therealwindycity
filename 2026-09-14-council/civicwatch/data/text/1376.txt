Section 1.  That Section 10.12.020, Incarceration for Certain Traffic Offenses, of Chapter

Wyoming, is hereby amended as set out in this ordinance. 
 
10.12.020 - Incarceration for certain traffic offenses. 
  
The municipal court may not impose a jail sentence or incarceration for any traffic-related 
offenses, offense except for the following offenses: 
 
A. Reckless driving (city code Section 10.08.010 and Wyo. Stat. Section W.S. 31-5-229); 
 
B. Driving or having control of vehicle while under influence of intoxicating liquor or 
controlled substances (city code Section 10.08.010 and Wyo. Stat. Section W.S. 31-5-
233); 
 
C. Driving while license cancelled, suspended or revoked (city code Section 10.08.010 
and Wyo. Stat. Section W.S. 31-7-134); 
 
D. Fleeing or attempting to elude police officers (city code Section 10.08.010 and Wyo. 
Stat. Section W.S. 31-5-225); 
 
E. Duty to stop vehicle where accident involves death or personal injuries (city code 
Section 10.08.010 and Wyo. Stat. Section W.S. 31-5-1101); .and 
 
F. Failure to maintain liability coverage (city code Section 10.08.010 and W.S. 31-4-103). 
 
The municipal court will shall advise the defendant that the maximum sentence on for any of 
the offenses listed in subsections (A) through E (F) of this section is six (6) months in jail, and/or 
a fine of seven hundred fifty dollars ($750.00), or both.
