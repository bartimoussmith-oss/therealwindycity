PURPOSE AND BACKGROUND 
The purpose of this agenda item is to request City Council approval of Amendment 
One to the Cooperative Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and the Wyoming 
Department of Transportation (WYDOT) for federally funded roadway improvements 
associated with the South Frontage Road and areas within the Whitney Ranch and 
Meadowlark Estates. 
 
SUMMARY OF AMENDMENT 
Amendment One updates the original agreement to reflect revised project costs, 
increased federal funding, updated federal award documentation, and clarified City 
responsibilities. The amendment replaces several exhibits in the original agreement, 
including the Summary of Project Costs and Federal Award Information, and formally 
incorporates updated federal requirements. 
 
Key changes include an increase in the total federal participation from $3.16 million 
to $6.61 million, reflecting updated cost estimates and additional federal STPU 
funding. The City’s participation remains proportional to construction costs and 
includes reimbursement to WYDOT for eligible project expenses, including 
construction engineering and indirect costs assessed through WYDOT’s approved 
Indirect Cost Allocation Plan (ICAP). 
 
COST 
Based on the revised cost estimates, the City’s total project obligation was increased 
from $3,986,396 to $7,335,032, which includes construction, engineering, and 
applicable indirect costs.  However, due to the increase in available federal funding, 
the City’s share of the federal match and overmatch was reduced from $824,464 to 
$721,867, subject to final bid prices and federal participation rates.  Actual costs may 
vary, and any increase exceeding 20 percent of estimated costs would require further

RECOMMENDED ACTION 
I respectfully request approval of Amendment One to City Contract #7777, 
Cooperative Agreement No. 70439 between the City of Cheyenne and the Wyoming 
Department of Transportation in the amount of $866,240. 
 
Approval of this amendment allows the City to remain eligible for federal 
transportation funding administered by WYDOT and ensures compliance with 
federal requirements under 2 CFR Part 200. The amendment does not change 
WYDOT’s responsibilities but clarifies the City’s obligation to reimburse costs not 
covered by federal funds and to comply with federal grant administration, audit, and 
reporting requirements  
 
Failure to approve the amendment would delay project delivery and could 
jeopardize the availability of federal funds for this corridor improvement.
