ORDINANCE NO. ________ 
 
ENTITLED: "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING  SECTION 6.08.180, FEEDING GEESE 
AND DUCKS PROHIBITED, OF CHAPTER 6.08, ANIMAL CARE AND 
CONTROL REGULATIONS GENERALLY, OF TITLE 6, ANIMALS, OF 
THE MUNICIPAL CODE OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, TO 
INCLUDE SEAGULLS AND EXTEND THE FEEDING PROHIBITION TO 
ALL PROPERTY WITHIN CITY LIMITS." 
 
 BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, 
WYOMING: 
 
 Section 1.   
 
 WHEREAS, The feeding of wild animals attracts more animals and can lead to habituated 
animals becoming aggressive to humans, a large concentration of animals increasing the spread of 
disease outbreaks, and the noise, animal droppings, and potential for property damage caused by 
a concentration of wild animals constituting a nuisance; 
 
WHEREAS, Wyoming Statute § 15-1-103(a)(xix) provides that the governing bodies of 
all cities and towns  may declare and abate nuisances and impose fines upon parties who create, 
continue or permit nuisances to exist; 
 
WHEREAS, Wyoming Statute § 15-1-103(a)(xli) authorizes municipalities to adopt 
ordinances necessary for the health, safety and welfare of the city or town; 
 
WHEREAS, feeding seagulls has created a public health concern and affects the quality of 
life of neighbors; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City Council should amend Cheyenne Municipal Code 6.08.180 as shown 
in section 2. 
 
Section 2.  That Section 6.08.180, Feeding geese and ducks prohibited , of Chapter 6.08, 
Animal Care and Control Regulations Generally , of Title 6, Animals, of the City of Cheyenne, 
Wyoming, is hereby amended to read: 
 
6.08.180 - Feeding seagulls, geese and ducks prohibited. 
 
A. No person may feed, provide or give food or any other edible item to any seagull, goose, 
or duck, or to any gaggle of geese or flock of seagulls or ducks, in or upon: within the 
city limits. 
 
1. Any property owned by the city of Cheyenne within the city limits; 
 
2. Any park, golf course or other recreation, open space, or public use area within 
the city limits which is operated by the city of Cheyenne; or 
 
Page 2 of 3 
 
 
3. Any park, golf course, or other recreation, open space, or public use area within 
the city limits which is owned by the city of Cheyenne and operated by any third 
person or entity pursuant to a lease or other use agreement with the city of 
Cheyenne. 
 
B. Any person who violates this section shall be guilty of a misdemeanor, punishable by 
a fine of not  less than ten dollars ($10.00) and not more than twenty -five dollars 
($25.00) more than seven hundred fifty dollars ($750.00). 
 
C. In lieu of a citation for a violation of this section, an animal control officer or police 
officer may issue one (1) written warning to any person who has not previously been 
convicted of violating this section. 
 
D. No more than one citation alleging a violation of this section may be issued to any one 
(1) person in any twenty-four-hour twenty-four (24) hour period. 
 
 Section 3.  Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases of this ordinance are 
severable. If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase  of this ordinance is declared 
unconstitutional or otherwise unlawful, such declaration shall not affect any of the remaining 
sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases. 
 
 Section 4.  This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon approval and publication.
