TO: The Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and City of Cheyenne Council Members 
FROM: Robin Lockman, City Treasurer

SUBJECT: Reappropriation to be considered for approval 
On October 13, 2025, the Governing Body will be referring a resolution to the Finance Committee 
to reappropriate funds for the Fiscal Year (FY) 2026 City of Cheyenne Budget per W.S. § 16-4-
112 and 16-4-113.  Below is additional information regarding this request.  
ALL FUNDS - ENCUMBRANCES AND ROLLOVER ACCOUNTS 
Each September, my department prepares a comprehensive budget reappropriation request.  This 
request carries forward funds from the previous fiscal year, including encumbrances and unspent 
project balances.  Encumbrances are purchase orders that  were issued for goods and services  
ordered prior to but not received and paid until after June 30, 2025.  
When the FY 2026 budget was approved in June, rollover amounts for unspent project balances 
were not included because final fiscal year -end balances were unknown. After the City’s books 
closed in early September and those balances were finalized, we added them to the FY 2026 budget 
through this reappropriation request. 
Purchase Order Encumbrances Rollover 
Attached are detailed reports of FY 2025 purchase order encumbrances, totaling: 
• $3,101,885 in the General Fund
• $13,045,327 across all other City funds

MEMORANDUM 
TO: The Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and City of Cheyenne Council Members 
FROM: Jason Sanchez, Community Recreation and Events Director 
DATE: September 30, 2025 
SUBJECT: Civic Center and Ice & Events Center Requests from General Fund Reserves 
At the close of last fiscal year, our team managed over 100 large-scale events within the 
Community Recreation & Events (CRE) Department. Nearly half of these events, including 
Fridays on the Plaza, Superday, Brew Fest, CFD Pancake Breakfast, Cheyenne Day, and more, 
were hosted outside of the Cheyenne Civic Center and were directly managed or facilitated by 
Civic Center staff.  
The Civic Center staff consists of five full-time staff members who are critical to the operations 
and success of these outside events due to their specialized skill sets in technical production, event 
management, booking, marketing and advertising, ticketing, and safety. With this team, we have 
seen our most successful Fridays on the Plaza to date, built stronger partnerships with third-party 
promoters and agents, cultivated deeper relationships with sponsors, and served as a critical 
resource for local groups and community organizations in the surrounding area.  
The continued growth of the caliber of events we are bringing to the downtown community has 
consequently garnered stronger support from businesses as these events are catalysts for driving 
tourism, economic growth, and overall vitality for the City of Cheyenne.  For example, Visit 
Cheyenne’s economic impact analysis of the 2025 Fridays on the Plaza season estimated that the 
concert series generated roughly $1.9 million dollars of economic impact for Cheyenne.   
The Cheyenne Civic Center holds a special place in the hearts of our citizens, serving as a home 
for a wide range of events, from performances by local artists to nationally renowned acts, 
community events, conferences, training sessions, and so much more. Its diverse offerings 
contribute significantly to the City's cultural fiber, vibrancy, and economic growth.  And CRE 
continues to evolve the ways in which its programming connects citizens and visitors alike with 
the Civic Center.  
However, like any facility, the Civic Center requires ongoing investments to maintain its 
infrastructure, attract top-tier talent, and provide the best experience for both performers and 
audiences. It is my belief that redirecting funds toward the Civic Center is a strategic move that 
Page 1 of 2
will yield substantial returns in terms of community engagement, economic activity and overall 
quality of life for our residents.  
I respectfully request your support to reappropriate $300,000 from the General Fund Community 
Recreation & Events $678,619 FY25 reversions to support the Civic Center. I am also requesting 
$36,371 be reappropriated to support Ice & Events Center expenses, as well as $30,000 toward 
Fridays on the Plaza expenses.   
I understand the allocation of funds is a complex process that requires careful consideration of 
various priorities. However, I firmly believe that supporting these programs aligns with our city’s 
commitment to quality of life and fostering a rich cultural landscape, supporting local businesses 
and community groups, as well as providing our residents with enriching experiences. 
Respectfully, 
Jason Sanchez 
Community Recreation & Events, Director
