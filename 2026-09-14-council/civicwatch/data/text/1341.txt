Evaluation and implementation of design alternatives related to widening the existing bridge 
structure. This will include re-evaluating the hydraulic and hydrologic analysis and modeling for 
the project.  Design plans will address associated revisions to the traffic signal design, 
intersection, bridge lighting, signing and striping, and modifications to water, sanitary sewer, and 
storm sewer utilities.  Additional description of the work is provided in Exhibit A , which is 
attached hereto and incorporated by this reference. 
 
II. New Contract Items 
 
The design of widening the existing bridge structure of 5th Street crossing Crow Creek.

BACKGROUND and ASSUMPTIONS 
 
The City of Cheyenne previously selected HDR Engineering, Inc. to provide Design and Construction 
Management services for the 5 th Street Bridge Over Crow Creek Project. HDR Engineering, Inc. (HDR) 
and the City of Cheyenne (City) negotiated a baseline Scope of Services, in October 2021. The original 
scope of services included the design and reconstruction of the intersection of 5 th Street and Deming 
Drive. After the review of the Final Design Plans Submittal the City approached HDR to revise the 
structure type over Crow Creek and make modifications to other work components to more closely 
match revised budget constraints. 
 
 
TASK 3.0 – Final Design (Revisions to Previous Subtasks) 
 
Task 3.1 – Traffic Engineering, Lighting, Signalization 
 
• HDR will revise traffic signal design, intersectio n, and bridge lighting sheets, signing, and striping 
sheets, and quantities to reflect the new structure design.  
• HDR will revise signing and striping design based on City of Cheyenne inputs about project limits. 
o This work will be in accordance with the MUTCD and  the City of Cheyenne Standards 
 
Task 3.2 – Utility Design 
 
• HDR will re-evaluate existing utilities and identify potential conflicts with proposed improvements.  
o Public utilities include water, sanitary sewer, and storm sewer.  
o It is assumed that relocation of water main will be removed from the project  
o It is also assumed that Sanitary Sewer Relocation will not be required.  
• Storm sewer design will be updated based on change s to roadway geometry and City of Cheyenne 
review comments.  
o It is assumed that storm sewer system designed pre viously is acceptable for City of 
Cheyenne needs, from a capacity and discharge standpoint.

TASK 7.0 – Existing Bridge Rehabilitation & Widening Work  
 
Task 7 includes the field bridge inspection, engineering design calculations and plan preparation for the 
project plans. HDR will produce a plans package including specifications to be included in the plan set & 
bid booklet for the work associated with upgrading of the structure. The bridge widening will follow 
AASHTO Standard Specifications for Bridge Design (Load Factor Design) and use the HS 20-44 live 
loading. The original bridge was designed to the specification. 
 
Task 7.1 –Detailed Bridge Inspection:   
• HDR will have a 2-person crew document bridge elem ents that have deteriorated and are in need 
of repair on the structure.  The deficient or deteriorated bridge elements will be discussed to help 
determine bridge elements that will be repaired with this project.

existing bridge, a load rating, plans package (plan set) and engineers cost estimate for the bridge
