MEMORANDUM  
TO:  Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council 
FROM:  TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager 
DATE:  October 8, 2025 
SUBJECT: Consideration of Bid #S-4-26 for an Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and  
Construction Specialists, Inc., for the Dunbar Concessions Restroom project 
______________________________________________ 
Bid #S-4-26 was solicited for the Dunbar Concessions Restroom project. 
 
Five (5) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tab ulation is attached.  The Bids were received 
from: 
1. Big Horn Contracting, LLC 
2. Construction Specialists, Inc 
3. Richardson Construction, Inc 
4. S&S Builders LLC 
5. Wasatch West Contracting, LLC 
 
The bids were reviewed by Jason Sanchez (CRE), Sam Payne (CRE), and The Design Studio, Inc 
(TDSi), and their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from Construction 
Specialists, Inc. 
 
The total Bid Award is $1,155,700.00.   
 
Project Completion is scheduled for April 2026. 
 
Funding:  Cheyenne Community Recreation District - Rec Mill Grant  
 6th Penny Grant Match (2021 Special Purpose Option Tax) 
 Community Facility Fees - Infrastructure
