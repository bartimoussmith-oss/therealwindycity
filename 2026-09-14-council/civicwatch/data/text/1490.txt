Further Staff evaluation of Parcel 4: 
 
The current use of the property is a residence and an urban farm with a farm stand. These uses would 
be classified as “Limited Agriculture” and “Agriculture Product Sales” in the Unified Development Code. 
The Limited Agriculture use is only allowed in four zone districts with two of those being special zone 
districts. The zone districts are as follows: AG – Agricultural, AR – Agricultural Residential, AD – Airport 
District, and SS – Support Services. The AD and SS zones are special purpose zones and not applicable 
to this area.  
 
The AG zone allows for all agricultural uses in some fashion, whether it is permitted as a use by right, 
administrative, or requires conditional use approval. This is the most flexible zone for agricultural uses 
and would allow the farm and farmstand to continue operations.  
 
The AR zone allows for limited agricultural uses. This zone would make the farm stand non-conforming, 
and may be more limiting on the expansion of the agricultural operations.

BACKGROUND: 
The City is annexing parcels that are 100% surrounded by current City Limits as part of Councils goals to annex 
county pockets. Annexation of a parcel requires a city zone to be established.  
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1. The proposed zoning assignment is the most analogous City zone to the current zone 
when taking into consideration allowed uses, current uses, or surrounding City zones. 
Zoning shall be assigned a City zone based on: 
a. Permitted uses, 
b. Uses that could reasonably be anticipated for conditional use approval 
under the current zoning, 
c. Current uses, or 
d. Surrounding City zoning, 
as deemed most suitable for the property being assigned a zone in terms of allowing 
the landowner to continue the current use of the land while respecting the current and 
anticipated uses of surrounding property. 
 
Through the reviewing of the County Pockets, the assigned zoning is somewhat straight forward. 
 
Parcel 1 
 
Parcel 1 is currently zoned LI – Light Industrial within the County. This pocket is mainly surrounded by 
City LI zoning and there is P – Public to the north. This pocket is designated Mixed-use Employment 
by the Future Land Use map. The current use of two of the properties is residential. The third property 
is owned by the City and used like an alley. The surrounding area is mainly commercial or industrial 
uses. Based on this information Staff believes that the LI – Light Industrial zone is the most 
analogues zone for this pocket. The LI zone does make the use of the residential properties non-
conforming, but they were already non-conforming within the County under the County’s LI zone.  
 
PUDC-25-278   February 2, 2026

Parcel 2 
 
Parcel 2 is currently zoned LI – Light Industrial within the County. This pocket is surrounded by the LI 
zone. This pocket is a portion of the railroad and is not a parcel but more of a right of way. Based on 
this information Staff believes that the LI – Light Industrial zone is the most analogues zone for this 
pocket. 
 
Parcel 3 
 
Parcel 3 is currently zoned CB – Community Business within the County. This pocket is surrounded by 
the City CB zone and the LI zone to the north. This pocket is a portion of the railroad and is not a 
parcel but more of a right of way. Based on this information Staff believes that the CB  – Community 
Business zone is the most analogues zone for this pocket. 
 
Parcel 4 
 
Parcel 4 is currently zoned LU – Land Use within the County. This parcel is surrounded by MR – 
Medium-density Residential to the north and west, HR – High-density Residential to the east, and A-1 
to the south. The Future Land Use map designates this property as Urban Residential. The current use 
of the property is residential and agriculture. The property owner has stated that they have livestock 
and grow and sell crops. This is a large parcel that could be redeveloped in the future should it ever 
be sold or subdivided. Based on this information Staff believes that the AG – Agricultural zone is the 
most analogues zone for this pocket. The AG zone will allow the continuation of the current uses on 
the land but also allow for easier rezoning in the future.  
 
Parcel 5 
 
Parcel 5 is currently zoned URLD – Urban Residential Low Density within the County. This pocket has 
City MR to the east, CB to the north, and AG to the west. County zoning exists to the south. All parcels 
within this pocket are residential. The Future Land Use map designates this pocket as Urban 
Residential. Based on this information Staff believes that the MR – Medium-density Residential zone 
is the most analogues zone for this pocket. These properties are developed similar to MR zoned areas 
within the City.  
 
Parcel 6 
 
Parcel 6 is currently zoned LI – Light Industrial within the County. This parcel is surrounded by the City 
NR-3 Neighborhood Residential – High-density. There is CB zoning further to the west, County LI 
across Fox Farm and County LU a little further to the east. The current use of the property is  limited 
industrial / commercial. The Future Land Use map designates this parcel as Mixed-use Residential. 
There are several zones that this property could be. Those zones are AG, NR-3, CB, MUR, MUB, MUE, 
or LI. The County LU to the east does allow for agricultural and residential uses, but the AG zone does 
not really fit for this parcel based on its current use and the potential uses of the surrounding land. 
The CB zone is to the west but is just vacant land, so CB zoning may not be the most appropriate. The 
future land use designation of MUR would point to MUR being the most analogues but it is a guiding 
document and not a set in stone when it comes to zoning. The four zones worth discussing are the 
NR-3, MUB, MUE and LI.  
 
PUDC-25-278   February 2, 2026 
4 | Page  
  
The NR-3 does surround the parcel and is being developed to the northwest and the north. The 
portions to the east and south are still vacant. This zone allows for mainly residential uses  and would 
make the current use non-conforming. This does not mean the use could not continue as is, but if the 
property was to expand its current use it would require a conditional use before the Board of 
Adjustment. The NR-3 zone would also be more in line with the future land use designation of Mixed-
use Residential.   
 
The MUB zone would allow for more commercial focused development and allow for some limited 
industrial uses while also allowing similar residential development to that of the NR -3. The MUB 
would have stricter design requirements and would expect smaller block sizes. There is currently no 
MUB zoning in the area. The closes zone similar to the City’s MUB is the County’s MU Mixed -use to 
the southwest. The MUB would allow for the warehouse component on the property, but would not 
allow for the limited outdoor storage that exists. This means that if they wish to expand the limited 
outdoor storage they would need a conditional use. 
 
The MUE zone would allow for more of the industrial and some commercial uses, but would only 
allow for multi-family development and not single-family like the MUB or NR-3. The MUE would give 
the balance of allowing the property’s current uses while still allowing for multifamily development.  
There is only one property within City Limits that is zoned MUE. MUE is meant for campus like 
development, which is not what currently exists on the property, and it is unlikely a campus like 
development would happen in this location in the future.  
 
The LI zone is the current County zone for the property and would have the least impact on the 
current use of the property. It would not allow for residential development in the future without a 
rezoning. To the south across Fox Farm is County LI zoning and LI uses. The NR-3 that is just south of 
the property is vacant and not owned by the same land owner to the north developing with 
multifamily. This land could be rezoned in the future to a commercial or industrial zone.     
 
Staff believes that either the NR-3 or the LI zone could work for this property. Based on this 
information Staff believes that the LI – Light Industrial zone is the most analogues zone for this 
pocket because it would allow the continuation of the current uses and the land to the south even 
though its zoned NR-3 could be rezoned and not actually developed with residential. This land could 
also be rezoned in the future if it was sold to match the NR-3 to the north, but for now the LI zone 
would allow the land to continue as is and allow it to be used as it has been with out too many 
restrictions.   
 
Parcel 7 
 
Parcel 7 is currently zoned LI – Light Industrial and LU – Land Use within the County. This pocket is 
surrounded by AG zoning to the north, south and east. County LI zoning is to the west. The Future 
Land Use map designates this pocket as Mixed-use Employment. Two of the parcels are developed 
with industrial uses and one is developed with a residence. Based on this information Staff believes 
that the LI – Light Industrial zone is the most analogues zone for the parcels zoned LI in the County 
as it allows continuation of the current uses and is similar to that across College Drive.  Staff believes 
that the AG – Agricultural zone is the most analogues zone for the parcel zoned LU in the County as 
it allows for the residential use and allows for easier rezoning in the future should the property ever 
redevelop.  
 
This assigned zoning complies with review criterion 1.
