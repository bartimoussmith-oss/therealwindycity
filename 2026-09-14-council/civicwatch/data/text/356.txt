PURCHASE AGREEMENT  
THIS AGREEMENT IS ENTERED INTO VOLUNTARILY BETWEEN 
America’s Freedom Tribute and the City of Cheyenne  (of Wyoming). The purpose of this 
agreement is to formalize the terms verbally agreed to for sale and transfer of the asset known 
as “80% Replica, The Vietnam Wall 140 Panels” b y America’s Freedom Tribute  to the City of 
Cheyenne. This Tribute was fabricated in Bullard, Texas by America Veterans Traveling Tribute 
in its Production Facility. 
 
TERMS: 
 
1. Sale price; $350,000.00 for 80% Replica Vietnam 140 Wall Panels all newly engraved. 
Transfer Date: TBD. Subject to contingency below.  
2. $75,000.00 Total Deposit was discussed and agreed upon between both parties. Balance 
of $5,000.00 is required to be paid on the date of signed Purchase Agreement, no later 
than May 25th, 2026. 
$70,000.00 from previous deposit was made by Veterans Remembrance Memorial  was 
discussed and agreed upon between all parties to transfer it over to the City of Cheyenne 
(of Wyoming) as part of the deposit required for the new Vietnam Wall purchase leaving 
deposit balance due of $5,000.00. 
3. Payment Schedule is as follows: 
2nd payment $75,000 due 30 days after deposit on June 25, 2026.  
3rd Payment $75,000 due on September 25, 2026. 
4th Payment $75,000 due on December 25, 2026.  
5th Final Payment $50,000 due on March 25, 2027. 
This transaction may be paid off at any time before due date without penalty. Various payments 
can be made towards the balance as long as they are consistent and staying with timeline for 
wall to be paid off within 365 days from date of signing contract. 
4. AFT agrees to deliver newly engraved 80% Replica Vietnam 140 Wall Panels at which time
