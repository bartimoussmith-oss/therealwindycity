RE: City Liquor License Renewals for 2025-2026
Attached is a listing of those applicants applying for renewal of existing City liquor licenses
(i.e.: retail, restaurant, limited retail, resort, bar & grill licenses, microbrewery, satellite distillery, 
special malt beverage and winery permits), liquor licensee actions that occurred during this past
licensing year and the Police Department’s compliance report.  Public hearing for the applications
is scheduled during the February 24, 2025 City Council meeting. Please note, the renewal for F &
G Corporation d/b/a The Green Door will have a separate approval schedule as the licensee
is also requesting to transfer the location of the parked license and the transfer must be done
before the renewal.
Due to the large volume of paperwork involved in renewing the liquor licenses, the attached
listing provides the “basics” from application information in a readable format.  If you would like
to review an individual application file, please contact me and I will make it available to you.
If you have any questions concerning applicant information or renewal requirements and
procedures, please do not hesitate to contact me at your convenience.
