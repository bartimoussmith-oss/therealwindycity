SUBJECT: Consideration to Purchase between the City of Cheyenne, and Ace Equipment &

Systems 
______________________________________________ 
Consideration to Purchase between the City of Cheyenne, and Ace Equipment & Supply Company, 
for the purchase of two (2) sets of Rotary Mobile-Column Lifting Systems.  (Product information 
attached) 
 
This purchase is for two (2) sets of lifting systems.  Each system has six lifting columns.  One set 
will be utilized for the Sanitation Division Shop, and one set will be utilized for the Fleet 
Maintenance Shop. 
 
The total amount of the purchase is $185,878.00.   
 
From the Manufacturer: 
Rotary’s wireless communication technology is proprietary and not compatible with other 
brands of mobile-column lifting systems. 
 
Justification for Purchase: 
Existing lifts are Rotary brand as well.  Staying with this brand allows for compatibility, 
expansion, and continued connectivity between old and new lifts.
