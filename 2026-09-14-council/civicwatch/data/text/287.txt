BACKGROUND: 
 
This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from JL Ranch PUD to NR-2 Neighborhood 
Residential – Medium Density for roughly 6.77 acres of land. The land is currently vacant with sewer and water 
extension in the area.  
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical 
patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1) 
 
The Future Land Use Designation for this property is Mixed-Use Residential. According to the 
comprehensive plan, this designation supports range of residential housing types from single-
family residences to live-work units. Commercial use may be added to these areas in order to 
bring in more activity, but their development should be secondary to potential residential 
development in the area. If commercial developments are within the Mixed-Use Residential area,

Based on the policies and future land use map, NR-2 is appropriate in this area.  
 
The proposed zone change does comply with review criterion 1. 
 
2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering 
existing zoning, the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies 
created under the guidance of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2) 
 
The proposed NR-2 zone would be isolated to this lots. To the north of the property is zoned HI 
and hosts a retail transport warehouse. The parcel zoned JL Ranch PUD is located directly to the 
south is vacant. East of this parcel is land zone CB which is currently vacant. To the west of this 
property is JL Ranch PUD and contains single family dwellings. A property further to the south 
and east is zoned LI and is developing with large-scale data center development. 
 
The JL Ranch was zoned PUD in 2006 and amended in 2008 prior to the adoption of the UDC and 
the establishment of the NR-2 zone. In many ways, the general development pattern anticipated 
by the PUD is similar to that of the NR-2 zone (e.g. smaller lots, alley access). The proposed zone 
change from JL Ranch PUD to NR-2 would have minimal impact on surrounding properties. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2. 
   
3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards 
and uses of the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject 
property for development according to the standards and uses of the proposed 
zoning district (UDC 2.2.1.d.3) 
 
NR-2 would allow for a variety of residential development types. The subject parcels are currently 
in the JL Ranch PUD, which allows for development similar to NR-2. The lot size permitted within 
the NR-2 zone district. The current PUD requires fences to not exceed 4’ in height and any areas 
facing streets to have sod. There are multiple properties with fences over 4’ high or which do not 
have sod in the front yard. At one point, after neighborhood meetings, Staff proposed the built 
area of the neighborhood come together to amend the PUD or remove the PUD zoning 
completely to remove the fence requirements of the PUD, but there was not enough of the 
neighborhood interested in pursuing the formal process. 
 
The depth of the lot will require the applicant to seek administrative adjustment for rear setback  
to reduce it from 20 feet to 10 feet (the PUD’s current rear-yard setback).  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3. 
 
4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed 
according to the Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed 
zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

No changes to the street network are proposed. Accesses will not be changed with the zone 
change. Infrastructure (streets and utilities) exist in the area. Water and sewer have been extended 
to the area. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4. 
 
5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of 
the proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific 
standards. (UDC 2.2.1.d.5) 
 
The NR-2 district is intended to be used for medium density and mix of dwelling types. All uses 
require full access to public infrastructure and city services. Overall density of this district should 
be between 10 and 18 dwelling units per acre. The applicability is in area where more compact 
neighborhood development patterns are desired, specifically within the Urban Residential 
Category in the comprehensive plan. 
 
This district should be used in area than ½ mile from any existing or planned Activity Center. 
Applications of this district without transactions to activity centers should be avoided so that 
significant populated areas must rely solely on automobile travel and which are remote from 
necessary municipal service are not created. This district should be supported by a highly 
connected street network that have low design speeds and residential character streetscapes.  
 
The area has been platted with a compact development pattern and has pedestrian access to 
employment opportunities. There is also commercial zoned property in the area and a major 
retailer within two miles.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5. 
    
6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for 
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options 
if any specific development proposed in association with the application is not 
executed after approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6) 
 
The NR-2 zone would limit this parcel to be strictly residential development. The previous zoning 
of JL Ranch PUD would have prominently been a residential development with allowances for 
home occupations..  
 
Due to previous platting, any alternative development pattern would likely require a replat.  Upon 
replat, the JL Ranch PUD would only really allow townhouses, while the NR-2 allows more variety 
in residential development (such as duplexes, small apartment buildings, or cottage lots). The NR -
2 also has more allowances for non-residential uses, but those would require conditional use 
approval. 
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6. 
 
7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7) 
 
No major comments received in regard to the proposed zone.
