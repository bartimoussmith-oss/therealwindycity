RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE SUBMISSION OF AN APPLICATION 
BY THE CITY OF CHEYENNE FOR STATE FUNDING THROUGH THE 
OFFICE OF STATE LANDS AND INVESTMENTS FOR A RODEO GROUNDS 
PEDESTRIAN TRANSPORTATION INFRASTRUCTURE  GRANT, AND 
AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION AND SUBMITTAL OF ALL NECESSARY 
DOCUMENTS IN SUPPORT OF THE APPLICATION.” 
 
WHEREAS, during the 2023 General Session of the Wyoming Legislature, as part of the 
State-funded capital construction bill, Original Senate File 146 (2023 Wyo. Sess. Ch. 187), the Legislature 
appropriated four million dollars ($4,000,000.00) to the Wyoming Office of State Lands and Investments 
(“OSLI”) for the purpose of providing grants for infrastructure related to qualifying special events 
conducted on the grounds of any indoor or outdoor rodeo facility with a total seating capacity of not less 
than seven thousand five hundred (7,500)  (hereafter referred to as the “Rodeo Grounds Pedestrian 
Transportation Infrastructure Grant Program” or the “Program”); 
 
WHEREAS, under 2023 Wyo. Sess. Ch. 187, grant recipients under the Program are to be selected 
by the State Loan and Investment Board, and any grant awarded is subject to the following conditions: (i) 
the grant may only be awarded for infrastructure projects that create or enhance pedestrian transportation 
infrastructure in order to increase safety and avoid pedestrian and vehicle interactions, collisions, and 
conflicts; and (ii) expenditure of grant funds by a recipient is conditioned upon a matching contribution 
of not less than one dollar ($1.00) from any other source, including  in-kind contributions, for every one 
dollar ($1.00) of grant funds received; 
 
WHEREAS, the appropriation authorized by 2023 Wyo. Sess. Ch. 187 reverts on June 30, 2026; 
 
WHEREAS, OSLI issued notice announcing the solicitation of grant proposals for the Rodeo 
Grounds Pedestrian Transportation Infrastructure Grant  Program, as authorized under 2023 Wyo. Sess. 
Ch. 187, with a submission deadline of February 9, 2026; 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body for the City of Cheyenne desires to participate in the Program 
administered by OSLI to assist in funding the construction of a fully enclosed prefabricated truss 
pedestrian and bicycle bridge between the proposed Park-N-Ride lot and Frontier Park, spanning Interstate 
25 and Hynds Boulevard (the “Project”); 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body recognizes the need for the Project to enhance pedestrian safety 
and reduce conflicts between vehicles and pedestrians during special events at Frontier Park; 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne intends to request grant funding in the amount of four million 
dollars ($4,000,000.00) from OSLI for the Project, and further intends to provide a local match in the 
amount of one million dollars ($1,000,000.00) toward the cos t of construction of the Project, consistent 
with the grant program requirements; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne understands and agrees that compliance with all applicable 
OSLI criteria, Wyoming statutes governing the use of State of Wyoming funds, and Article 16, Section 6 
of the Wyoming Constitution is required, and further understands that any grant funds later determined to 
be ineligible must be repaid to OSLI within fifteen (15) days of such determination.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF 
CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that an application be submitted on behalf of the City of Cheyenne requesting 
four million dollars ( $4,000,000.00) in Rodeo Grounds Pedestrian Transportation Infrastructure Grant 
Program funding from the Office of State Lands and Investments to assist in funding for the Project; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Mayor of the City of Cheyenne is hereby authorized to 
execute, submit, and deliver the grant application, together with any and all certifications, assurances, 
supporting materials, amendments, and other information that may be necessary or reasonably required 
by OSLI or the State Loan and Investment Board in connection with the application and any resulting 
grant award; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Mayor is further authorized to execute any ancillary 
documents related to the administration of the grant application and, if awarded, the grant itself, subject 
to compliance with applicable law; and 
 
BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED, that Elizabeth Finochiaro, Economic Resource Administrator for 
the City of Cheyenne, is hereby designated as the Project Administrator and authorized to act on behalf 
of the City on matters related to preparation, submission, and administration of the grant application and 
any resulting award.
