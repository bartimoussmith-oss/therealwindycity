MEMO 
To: Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council 
From: Jeanie Anderson-Shrednik, PLA 
Subject: Contract Modification #1 to Contract #7322 between the City of Cheyenne and Sweetgrass 
Homeowners Association and the Sweetgrass Commercial Owners Association, Cheyenne, 
to outline responsibilities for both construction and maintenance of the Greater Cheyenne 
Greenway as well as enhanced landscaping constructed within tracts of land owned by the 
City and for areas within the public right-of-way within Sweetgrass, 1st and 2nd Filings.    
Date: February 17, 2026 
______________________________________________________________________________________________________________ 
 
Background:  
 
The item for consideration is the city’s consent to assignment of the Sweetgrass Maintenance 
Agreement, City Contract #7322,  The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as 
follows: 
• Introduction and referral  February 23, 2026 
• Public Service Committee  March 2, 2026 
• Final consideration  March 9, 2026 
 
The proposed amendment will correctly identify one of the entities that comprise the 
Sweetgrass Association, Sweetgrass Master Association, eliminate the expiration date in the 
original agreement and provide for perpetual terms and add a termination provision.  The 
document will be recorded with the Laramie County Clerk.

 This First Amendment and Consent To Assignment of Maintenance Agreement 
(“Agreement”) is made as of  _________________ ______, 2026 (the “Effective Date”) by and 
between the Sweetgrass Homeowners Association, a Wyoming nonprofit corporation , and 
Sweetgrass Commercial Property Owners Association, a Wyoming nonprofit corporation 
(collectively, the “Sweetgrass Associations”), and the City of Cheyenne, a municipality organized 
and existing under the laws of the State of Wyoming (“City”). The Sweetgrass Associations and 
the City are sometimes referred to herein collectively as “the Parties”. 
 
WHEREAS, the Parties entered into an agreement titled “ Maintenance Agreement 
Between the City of Cheyenne and The Sweetgrass Owners Association and Sweetgrass 
Commercial Owners Association”, also known as City Contract No. 7322, on or about April 14, 
2021 (“Maintenance Agreement”); and, 
 
WHEREAS, Section 1 of the Maintenance Agreement failed to correctly identify one of 
the entities that comprises the Sweetgrass Associations; and, 
 
WHEREAS, the Parties also desire to amend the Maintenance Agreement to eliminate the 
previously stated expiration date of January 1, 2030, thereby providing for a perpetual term unless 
terminated pursuant to Section 8 of the Maintenance Agreement, as created herein; and, 
 
WHEREAS, the Maintenance Agreement failed to include an agreement termination 
provision, though one was referenced, which deficiency the Parties desire to correct; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Parties further agree that the Maintenance Agreement, as amended by 
this Agreement, should be recorded with the Laramie County Clerk to provide notice of the Parties’ 
respective rights and obligations; and, 
 
WHEREAS, for the sake of ease and convenience, the Sweetgrass Associations also now 
desire to assign a portion of their rights and responsibilities under the Maintenance Agreement to 
the Sweetgrass Master Association, a Wyoming nonprofit corporation (“Master Association”); 
and, 
 
WHEREAS, Section 13(i) —originally Section 12(i) —of the Maintenance Agreement 
requires all Parties’ consent to amend said Agreement; and, 
 
WHEREAS, Section 13(iii)—originally Section 12(iii)—of the Maintenance Agreement 
requires the Sweetgrass Associations to secure the City’s consent to assign their interests under 
the Agreement. 
 
NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing recitals and the mutual promises 
contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Section 1 of the Maintenance Agreement is hereby amended in its entirety to read as 
follows: 
 
“Parties. This Maintenance Agreement (“MA”) is made and entered into by and 
between the Sweetgrass Homeowners Association, a Wyoming nonprofit 
corporation (“SOA”), 1720 Carey Avenue, Suite 500, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82009, 
and the Sweetgrass Commercial Property Owners Association, a Wyoming 
nonprofit corporation (“COA”), c/o Sweetgrass Land Co. LLC, 1825 Campstool 
Road, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82007, herein collectively referred to as the Sweetgrass 
Associations (“SA”), and the City of Cheyenne, a municipality organi zed and 
existing under the laws of the State of Wyoming (“City”), 2101 O’Neil Avenue, 
Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001, (collectively referred to as “Parties”).” 
 
2. Section 3 of the Maintenance Agreement is also hereby amended in its entirety to read as 
follows: 
 
“Purpose. The purpose of this MA is to outline responsibilities for the maintenance 
of a ten (10) foot concrete trail (Greater Cheyenne Greenway or “Greenway”) and 
enhanced landscaping and irrigation that will be constructed within the tracts of 
land  owned by the City  and described above in Section 2 of this MA,  as well as 
the areas located within the public right-of-way located within Sweetgrass, 1st and 
2nd Filings. It is mutually understood and agreed that the Greenway, as well as 
enhanced landscaping a nd irrigation, will be constructed and installed at the 
expense of the Sweetgrass Developers or entities other than the City and shall be 
designed and constructed to City Greenway standards and other specifications as 
found in the City of Cheyenne and Boar d of Public Utilities’ Construction 
Specifications and Standard Drawings, 2014. 
 
This MA shall remain in full force and effect from the effective date of this MA 
and shall continue in perpetuity  unless and until terminated as provided under 
Section 8 (Termination).” 
 
3. A new Section 8 of the Maintenance Agreement is hereby inserted to read as provided 
below to address the absence of a termination provision in the Maintenance Agreement: 
 
“8. Termination.  
 
i. Termination for Cause.  Either Party may terminate this MA upon written 
notice if the other Party materially breaches any provision of this MA and fails 
to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice 
specifying the breach and the required cure; provided, however, that nothing 
herein limits the non -breaching Party’s rights and remedies under Section 
12.ii. [Default; Remedies]—originally Section 11.ii. 
 
ii. Termination by Mutual Agreement. The Parties may terminate this MA at any 
time by a written instrument executed by authorized representatives of both

Parties, specifying the effective date of termination and any agreed transition 
terms. 
 
iii. Termination for Convenience by Mutual Consent.  The Parties acknowledge 
there is no unilateral termination for convenience; however, the Parties may 
mutually agree in writing to terminate for convenience consistent with 
subsection (b), including agreed allocation of transition tasks and costs. 
 
iv. Termination Due to Force Majeure/Impossibility. If performance by a Party is 
rendered impossible or impracticable for a continuous period of one hundred 
(120) days due to Force Majeure as described in Section 1 3.v—originally 
Section 12.v, either Party may terminate this MA upon thirty (30) days’ written 
notice to the other Party, effective at the end of such notice period; provided 
that termination shall not relieve either Party of obligations accrued prior to 
the effective date of termination.  
 
v. Notice; Effective Date. Notices of termination shall comply with Section 13.vi 
[Notices]—originally Section 12.vi—and state the subsection relied upon, the 
effective date (which may not be earlier than the applicable cure or notice 
period), and a summary of required post-termination responsibilities. From and 
after the effective date of the assignment referenced herein, all notices required 
or permitted under this Agreement shall also be delivered to the Master 
Association at its principal address on record with the State of Wyoming, 
unless the Master Association provides written notice of an alternate address 
to the Parties. 
 
vi. No Waiver of Immunities.  Nothing in this Section waives or is intended to 
waive the City’s governmental immunity.  
 
vii. Cross-References. References in this MA to termination “for default” or 
“pursuant to Section 7” are deemed to refer to termination pursuant to this 
Section 8 and Section 1 2 (Default; Remedies )—originally Section 11 , as 
applicable.”

Maintenance Agreement.

documents for recording and for any associated fees.  The Sweetgrass Associations shall

Maintenance Agreement. Should any defect in recordation occur, the Sweetgrass 
Associations, at their sole cost, shall take all necessa ry steps to correct such defect and 
provide satisfactory evidence of correction to the City within thirty (30) days of notice. 
 
7. Except as expressly amended by this Agreement, all terms and conditions of the 
Maintenance Agreement shall remain in full force and nothing in this Agreement shall
