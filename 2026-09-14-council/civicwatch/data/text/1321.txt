SUBJECT: Consideration of Bid #E-6-25 for 95 and 65 Gallon  Residential Autocarts (Trash Cans) for 
the Sanitation Division 
 ______________________________________________ 
Bid # E-6-25 was solicited  for 95 and 65 Gallon Residential Autocarts (Trash Cans) for the Sanitation 
Division 
 
Four (4) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.  The Bids were received from: 
1. Duramax Holdings LLC (Submitted Responsive Bid) 
2. Rehrig Pacific Company (Submitted Responsive Bid) 
3. Schaefer Plastics North America, LLC (Disqualified, did not meet minimum requirements) 
4. Toter, LLC (Submitted a “No Bid”) 
 
The bids were reviewed by Vicki Nemecek (Public Works), Craig LaVoy (Public Works ), and Austing 
Reiner (Sanitation), and their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from Rehrig Pacific 
Company. 
 
The purchase is for 1900 new Autocarts, 850 replacement lids, and 400 spare parts kits. 
 
The total requested bid award, is for $132,611.00 
 
Funding:  023-14-1417-43-32201 (Solid Waste Fund / Small Equipment)
