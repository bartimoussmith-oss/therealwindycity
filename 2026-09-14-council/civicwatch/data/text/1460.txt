BACKGROUND: 
 
The applicant is requesting approval of a preliminary plat for roughly 4.18 acres of land.  The proposed plat 
would create a lot for a multi-unit senior housing. The lot would be classified as lot type MD1 – Mutli-Dwelling 
Building/Large Lot. 
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING:

Should the Planning Commission  wish to recommend approval of the Preliminary Plat, the Commission 
must make the following findings required in UDC 2. 1.2.d  – a detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical 
patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.2.d.1) 
 
On the future land use map found in PlanCheyenne, the subject land and surrounding area has 
been designated as Urban Residential. The Urban Residential designation encourages a range of 
residential uses. The surrounding area includes single family dwellings to the west, there is high -
density residential development to the east, and commercial developments to the south of the

2) The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to 
existing and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration 
of current and future streets, open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or

The proposed plat consists of 4.18 acres that will be used for senior  high-density residential

continues to develop in the future.  
 
Public utilities and facilities currently don’t exist  on the property but can be extended . 
 
The application complies with this review criterion .   
   
3) The division of land meets the purposes, intent, and all applicable standards in 
Article 4. (UDC 2.1.2.d.3) 
 
Community Facility Fees  (4.2): 
For fees based on acreage, the area of the plat is pro -rated to the nearest tenth of an acre. 
Lands reserved as unbuildable (for right -of-way, detention, parks, remainder lots, etc), are 
exempt from fees.  The Park Land Acquisition Fee and Park Infrastructure Fee total $700 per 
acre due at time of building permit   
 
Per UDC Section 4.2.5, Table 4 -2, Enhancement Fees are payable at the issuance of a building 
permit for each structure.  The Park Enhancement Fee for residential is $400 per dwelling unit.  
 
Transportation Network (4.3):  
This plat is creating an extension of Melton Street. This parcel is located within a n oversized 
block. The extension will eventually connect to other parts of the street connection as 
development continues in the area.  
 
Open Space (4.4):  
Due to the nature of this application, Open space is not required with the plat.  
 
Required Improvements (4.5):  
This plat appears to be able to provide all required utilities to the lots within this 
development.
