TO:  City of Cheyenne Governing Body  
FROM:  Robin Lockman, City Treasurer 
DATE:  November 4, 2025  
SUBJECT: Annual Write-Off Per Resolution 4970 
 
 
On November 10, 2025, the Governing Body will refer  a resolution to the Finance Committee to 
write off $27,636.80 in uncollectible City accounts and $33,568.23 in Board of Public Utilities 
sanitation billings. Additionally, $75,623 in unpaid parking tickets from 2021 are included. 
Per Resolution 4970, debts owed to the City that are at least four years old, or where the customer 
is deceased or the debt has been discharged in bankruptcy, are classified as “uncollectible.” 
The debts under consideration for write-off are as follows:  
 
Please note that approval of this resolution does not prevent continued collection efforts on these 
accounts. The collection agency will continue its efforts until the statute of limitations expires, 
even if the accounts are removed from the City’s financial records. 
If you have any questions about these policies or procedures, please call me at 637-6336 or email 
me at rlockman@cheyennecity.org.

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION AUTHO RIZING THE CITY TREASURER TO 
DISCHARGE UNCOLLE CTIBLE DEBTS PURSUANT TO W YOMING 
STATUTE § 16-4-502." 
 
WHEREAS, the City Treasurer and Board of Public Utilities Administration Manager are 
responsible for collecting debts owed to the City of Cheyenne using a process that includes internal 
efforts, legal remedies and the use of collection agencies; 
 
WHEREAS, once accounts reach a certain age without successful collection, generally 
accepted accounting principles (GAAP) require that accounts  be written off the financial 
accounting records when management determines the receivables to be uncollectible after diligent 
and reasonable collection efforts have been made; 
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to Resolution No. 4970, the Governing Body has deemed certain 
debts owed to the City to be uncollectible when the debts are four (4) or more years old, the debtor 
is deceased, or the debt has been discharged in bankruptcy proceedings;  
 
WHEREAS, collection efforts such as liens filed by the City and the use of a contracted 
collection agency have been unsuccessful;  
 
WHEREAS, Wyoming Statute § 16-4-502 authorizes the discharge of uncollectible debts 
upon proper certification to the Governing Body;  
 
WHEREAS, the required certification includes the name and last known address of the 
debtor, a description of the goods or services for which the debt was incurred, the amount of the 
debt and the date when the debt became due and payable, and an explanation what actions have 
been taken to collect the debt; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City Treasurer has prepared the required report of uncollectible debts to 
be written off, a copy of which is appended to and incorporated as part of this Resolution. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING , that the City Treasurer is authorized to discharge the  City of 
Cheyenne’s uncollectible debts including parking tickets in the amount of one hundred and three 
thousand two hundred and fifty -nine dollars and eighty cents ($103,259.80) and the Board of 
Public Utilities sanitation debts in the amount of thirty-three thousand five hundred and sixty-eight 
dollars and twenty-three cents ($33,568.23).
