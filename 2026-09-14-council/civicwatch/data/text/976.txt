FENCE INSTALLATION AND ACCESS LICENSE AGREEMENT 
 
 This Fence Installation, Access, and Maintenance Agreement  (“Agreement”) is by and 
between the City of Cheyenne, a municipal corporation  organized and existing under the laws of 
the State of Wyoming  (“City”), whose address is 2101 O ’Neil Ave nue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 
82001, and Richard Menkin  (“Licensee”), whose address is Post Office Box 243, Cheyenne, 
Wyoming 82003. The City and the Licensee are collectively referred to as “Party” or “Parties.” 
This Agreement shall become effective upon the date of the last signature affixed hereto. 
 
RECITALS: 
 
 WHEREAS, the Licensee holds fee title to approximately 2.23 acres of land located in the 
City of Cheyenne, County of Laramie, State of Wyoming (the “Property”), more particularly 
described as: 
Lot 8, Block 3, Campstool Addition, 3rd Filing, City of Cheyenne, Laramie County, 
Wyoming; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the Licensee desires to install chain link fencing and gates across adjacent 
City-owned property, more particularly described as: 
Lot 6, Block 3, Campstool Addition, 3rd Filing, City of Cheyenne, Laramie County, 
Wyoming 
 
WHEREAS, the City agrees to permit the Licensee to install and maintain such fencing 
and gates, and to perform mowing and related maintenance activities on the City Parcel, for the 
mutual benefit of the Parties. 
 
 NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises set forth 
herein, the Parties agree as follows: 
 
 1. Purpose.  The purpose of this Agreement is to set forth the rights and 
responsibilities of the Parties regarding the Licensee’s authority to access, install, and maintain 
fencing and gates, and conduct mowing and related maintenance activities on the City Parcel. 
  
2. Term.  This Agreement shall become effective upon the date of the last signature 
affixed hereto. Unless earlier terminated in accordance with the terms set forth herein, this 
Agreement shall remain in full force and effect in perpetuity.

 3. Responsibilities of City. The City shall: 
a. Permit the Licensee to access, install, and maintain fencing, gates, and 
perform mowing on the City Parcel. 
 
 4. Responsibilities of Licensee. The Licensee shall: 
a. Install and maintain fencing and gates on the City Parcel in a manner 
acceptable to the City; and 
b. Mow the City Parcel no fewer than two (2) times per summer season, or 
more frequently as reasonably necessary to maintain a neat and safe 
condition. 
 
5. License to Use City Property.  The City grants Licensee a nonexclusive, revocable 
license to access, install, and maintain fencing and gates, and to perform mowing and related 
maintenance activities on the City Parcel. This license conveys no real property interest and may 
be revoked by the City upon thirty (30) days’ written notice. Upon revocation, Licensee shall be 
afforded a reasonable period of time to remove any installed improvements at the Licensee’s sole 
cost and expense. 
  
 6. Binding Effect.  This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of 
the Parties hereto, their respective personal representatives, heirs, successors, grantees, and 
assigns. This Agreement shall run with the City-owned parcel identified above and be recorded in 
the official records of the Clerk of Laramie County, Wyoming. 
 
7. General Provisions. 
 a. Amendments.  Any changes, modifications, revisions, or amendments to 
this Agreement which are mutually agreed upon by the Parties to this Agreement shall be 
incorporated by written instrument, executed and signed by all Parties to this Agreement. 
 
b. Termination.  Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time by 
providing thirty (30) days’ written notice to the other Party.  Upon termination of this 
Agreement, the City shall record in the official records of the Clerk of Laramie County, 
Wyoming the written notice of termination to provide public notice that the license granted 
herein is no longer valid or in effect. The Licensee shall cooperate in executing any 
necessary documentation to facilitate such recording. 
 
c. Assignment.  No Party may assign or transfer the responsibilities or 
agreements made herein without the prior written consent of the non -assigning Party, 
which approval shall not be unreasonably withheld. 
 
d. Applicable Law.  The construction, interpretation, and enforcement of this 
Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming. The courts of the State 
of Wyoming shall have jurisdiction over this Agreement and the Parties, and the venue 
shall be in the First Judicial District, Laramie County, Wyoming.

with all applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations in the performance of this 
Agreement. 
   
f. Indemnification.  Each Party to this Agreement assumes the risk of any 
liability arising from its own conduct. Each Party agrees that it will be responsible for any 
loss, claim, damages, or demands resulting from its negligence and the negligence of its 
employees and agents. Neither Party agrees to insure, defend, or indemnify the other. 
 
g. Nondiscrimination.  The Parties shall comply with the Civil Rights Act of 
1964, the Wyoming Fair Employment Practices Act, Wyo. Stat. § 27 -9-105, et seq., the 
Americans With Disabilities Act (ADA), as amended, 42 U.S.C. § 12101, et seq., and the

color, race, religion, national origin, ancestry, pregnancy, or quali fying disability in

h. Governmental Immunity.  The City does not waive governmental immunity 
by entering into this  Agreement and specifically retain s all immunities and defenses 
available to it as a governmental entity pursuant to Wyo. Stat. § 1-39-101, et seq., and all

similar provisions should not be construed as a waiver of governmental immunity.

other individual or entity, the status of third-party beneficiary and this Agreement shall not 
be construed so as to create such status. The rights, duties, and obligations contained in this 
Agreement shall operate only between the Parties to this Agreement and shall inure solely 
to the benefit of the Parties to this Agreement. The provisions of this  Agreement are 
intended only to assist the Parties in determining and performing their obligations under 
this Agreement. The Parties to this Agreement intend and expressly agree that only parties 
signatory to this Agreement shall have any legal or equitable right to seek to enforce this 
Agreement, to seek any remedy arising out of a Party’s performance or failure to perform 
any term or condition of this Agreement, or to bring an action for the breach of this 
Agreement. 
 
j. Force Majeure.  The performance of this Agreement by the Parties shall be 
subject to force majeure including, but not limited to, acts of God, fire, flood, natural 
disaster, war or threat of war, acts or threats of terrorism, civil disorder, unauthorized 
strikes, governmental regulation or advisory, recognized health t hreats as determined by

similar occurrence beyond the control of the Parties, where any of those factors, 
circumstances, situations, or conditions or similar ones prevent, dissuade, or unreasonably 
delay the performance required by this Agreement. This Agreement may be cancelled by
