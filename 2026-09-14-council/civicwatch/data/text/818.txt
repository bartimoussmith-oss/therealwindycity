Memorandum 2025-10-22 
To: Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council 
From: Fire Chief Andrew Dykshorn 
Date: October 22, 2025 
Subject: Request for Consideration of Bid – Battalion Chief Vehicle Replacement 
Dear Mayor and Council Members, 
I am writing to respectfully request your consideration of a bid for the procurement of a new 
Battalion Chief vehicle for the Fire Department. 
The current vehicle assigned to the Battalion Chief of Support has exceeded its recommended  
front-line service life and is increasingly requiring maintenance that impacts operational readiness 
and incurs additional costs. As this vehicle serves a critical role in incident command and rapid 
response, ensuring its reliability is essential to maintaining the safety and efficiency of our 
emergency operations. 
We have solicited bids for a suitable replacement that meets the operational needs of the 
department, including enhanced communications capabilities, storage for command equipment, 
and improved safety features. The recommended bid offers the best value in terms of cost, 
durability, and functionality.  The funding for this request will come from our Fire Operations 
General Fund Vehicle line item and the remainder from our 2023-2026 fifth penny allowance. The 
expected delivery is 4-6 months.  
I respectfully request approv al for  the proposed bid from Fremont Motors for $102,083.00 to 
ensure continued support for our frontline leadership and emergency response capabilities. I am 
available to provide further details or answer any questions you may have regarding this request. 
Thank you for your continued support of Cheyenne Fire Rescue  and our mission to protect the 
community. 
Sincerely, 
 
Andrew Dykshorn 
Fire Chief
