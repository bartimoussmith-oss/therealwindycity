RECOMMENDED MOTION: 
 
Move to recommend the Governing Body approve the assigned zoning for northeast of the intersection 
of Frontier Mall Dr. and Rue Terre Avenue and west of Converse Avenue to CB Community Business as 
shown in the attached zone change map, noting that the project meets the review criteria for approval. 
 
APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS: 
 
• UDC 2.2.8 Assigned Zoning 
 
BACKGROUND: 
The applicant has petitioned for roughly 18.62 acres of land to be annexed into city limits. The land proposed 
to be annexed is Tract 2, Winco Subdivision and a 1.83 acre parcel west of Converse Avenue and north of E 
Carlson Street. Annexation of a parcel requires a city zone to be established. Per UDC 2.2.8 a zone will be 
assigned based on the underlying county zone and per the policy document adopted by the city. Should the 
applicant wish to have a different zone at the time of annexation, they will be required to submit a 
subsequent zoning map amendment, proposing a different zone. The applicant is not requesting a different 
City zone at this time. Prior to this annexation the applicant went through a zone change in the County where 
the County Commissioners approved the rezoning of these two parcels to CB Community Business. 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The assigned zone is the most analogous City zone to the current zone when considering 
allowed uses. Zoning for standard County zones shall be assigned based on the policy 
document, while zones not listed on the policy document and terminated City zones shall be 
assigned a City zone on a case-by-case basis. (UDC 2.2.8.d.1) 
 
The underlying county zone is CB Community Business. The most analogous city zone is CB 
Community Business according to the policy document. The Assigned Zoning policy document is 
attached to the staff report as attachment number two.  
 
Establishment of the City zone matches the policy document; therefore, the item complies with 
review criterion 1.
