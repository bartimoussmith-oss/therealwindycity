RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CITY OF CHEYENNE TO 
APPROVE AND SUBMIT THE WYOMING DIVISION OF VICTIM 
SERVICES FUNDING APPLICATION FOR STATE FISCAL YEARS 2027 
AND 2028." 
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to Wyoming Statute § 1 -40-201 et seq, the Wyoming Victim and 
Witness Bill of Rights ensures that victims and witnesses of crime are treated with compassion, 
respect, and sensitivity within the criminal justice system; 
 
WHEREAS, the primary goal of the Cheyenne Police Department Victim Assistance 
Program is to provide direct services to victims of crime after an incident is reported to law 
enforcement within the city’s jurisdiction; 
 
WHEREAS, it is the Cheyenne Police Department Victim Assistance Program’s mission 
to enhance the treatment of victims, witnesses, and survivors of criminal and non -criminal crisis 
situations by providing them with the assistance and services necessary to speed their physical and 
emotional recovery; to support and aid them through the progress of the criminal justice system; 
and to make referrals to appropriate agencies; 
 
WHEREAS, services provided by the Cheyenne Police Department Victim Assistance 
Program include the following: crisis intervention, emotional support, emergency assistance and 
shelter options, information and referrals to community resources, case status disposition, criminal 
justice advocacy and, assistance with offering and completing Crime Victim Compensation 
Program applications for the State of Wyoming Division of Victim Services. 
 
WHEREAS, during the last calendar year, of the individuals served by the Cheyenne Police 
Department Victim Assistance Program, eight hundred seventy-nine (879) victims of violent crime 
were provided with one -on-one advocacy support. This support included crime scene crisis 
response, hospital forensic examination accompaniment, emotional support, connection to 
community and mental health resources, assistance with funeral preparations, liaison assistance 
with City and County Prosecuting Att orneys’ offices, education about the criminal legal system, 
physical safety plan, coping skills and mental health safety planning, suicidality assessments and 
intervention, assistance with Crime Victim Compensation, short term financial stability assistance, 
intervention with landlords and employers, and referrals to specialized community support 
agencies; 
 
WHEREAS, the services provided by the Cheyenne Police Department Victim Assistance 
Program have been crucial to the long -term stabilization and well-being of the city’s community 
members; particularly people with lower financial stability and those with vulnerabilities, and they 
would not be possible without the funding and staffing necessary to provide those services and 
resources; 
 
WHEREAS, the services of the Cheyenne Police Department Victim Assistance Program 
are funded in part by state and federal grants established by the Victims of Crime Act ("VOCA"), 
Page 2 of 3 
the Violence Against Women Act ("VAWA"), and the Family Violence Prevention and Services 
Act ("FVSPA"); 
 
WHEREAS, these state and federal grants are awarded through one, or a combination of, 
State Domestic Violence and Sexual Assault funds, State Victim/Witness funds, State Child 
Advocacy funds, State Salary funds, Federal Violence Against Women Act ("VAWA") funds, 
Sexual Assault ( “SASP”) funds, Federal Victim of Crime Act ( “VOCA”) funds and Federal 
Family Violence Prevention Act (“FVPSA”) funds; 
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to Wyoming Statute § 1-40-118, award recipients of these state and 
federal funds  are required to submit subgrantee reports to  the Wyoming Division of Victim 
Services ("DVS") in accordance with deadlines established through the Office on Violence Against 
Women and the Family Violence Prevention and Services Program; 
 
WHEREAS, to further support its services, the City of Cheyenne Police Department 
Victim Assistance Program intends to apply for these funds to provide basic, direct services to 
victims of crime in compliance with the Wyoming Division of Victim Services Rules; 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne Police Department Victims Assistance 
Program intends to apply for these funds through the WyoGrants System .  Final applications 
submissions are due by Monday, April 13th, 2026, for State Fiscal Years 2027 and 2028; and 
 
WHEREAS, funding awarded through the State Division of Victim Services will be 
utilized by the Cheyenne Police Department Victim Assistance Program to cover approximately 
forty-one (41%) of the annual program costs to include employment costs for salary and benefits 
for one (1) full-time Victim Assistance Coordinator and two  (2) full-time Victim Advocates.  
Funding from these state and federal grants will also be utilized to cover costs of cellular phones 
for staff members, office supplies, training expenses, costs associated with educational and contact 
brochures for the department, casework tracking software, and emergency assistance funding to 
assist crime victims with immediate safety, housing, and medical access concerns. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Governing Body approves the funding application and 
application process through the Wyoming Division of Victim Services State Fiscal Year 2027 and 
2028 (July 1, 2026, to June 30, 2028). 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Mayor and the City Clerk are authorized to execute 
all documents relating to the Wyoming Division of Victim Services State Fiscal Year 202 7 and 
2028 (July 1, 2026, to June 30, 2028).

Emergency Assistance 
This funding covers expenses necessary for the emergency stabilization of victims of crime.  Priority is given to 
addressing the physical and financial security of victims.  The primary goal of this funding is to avoid long- term negative 
consequences such as job loss, loss of housing, physically insecure dwellings, or lack of access to food and medical 
needs.  Expenditures traditionally include such things as transportation-related assistance, food/clothing/cleaning 
supplies, temporary housing/shelter, limited rental assistance, utility assistance, and window or lock repair. 
 
Requested from DVS: $9,000
