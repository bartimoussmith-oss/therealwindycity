 ______________________________________________ 
Seven (7) Proposers responded to Request for Proposal (RFP) #9-25, for the Municipal Building 
Renovation Design project. 
 
The scope of work includes design services required to provide a complete renovation the City of 
Cheyenne Municipal Building.  These services include designs for a total removal of the existing 
HVAC system, electrical system, partition walls, water lines, and other site improvements.  
 
The proposals were received from:  
1. GSG Architecture, Inc. 
2. IMEG Consultants Corp. 
3. Iron Spur Architecture & Design 
4. Plan One/Architects 
5. TDSi - The Design Studio, inc. 
6. VFLA Architect + Interiors 
7. Winters Griffith Architects 
 
Upon review of the proposals by the evaluation panel, the group has recommended acceptance of 
the proposal submitted by Plan One Architects, for a project award amount of $1,474,550.00. 
 
Basis for award was based on a Qualifications based review and scoring of proposals.

This Professional Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is  made and entered into by and 
between the City of Cheyenne , located at 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001 
(“City”), and  Plan One Architects , located at  325 West 18 th Street, Suite 3, Cheyenne , 
Wyoming 82001 (“Consultant”). This Agreement shall become effective upon the date of the 
last signature affixed hereto. 
RECITALS 
In consideration of the mutual covenants and obligations herein expressed, it is agreed by 
and between the parties as follows: 
1. SCOPE OF WORK
The Consultant agrees to provide services as described for the Municipal Building
Renovation Design, in accordance with City of Cheyenne Request for Proposal (RFP)
#9-25, incorporated herein by reference, and the Consultant’s submitted proposal ,
incorporated herein by reference.
2. TIME OF PERFORMANCE
The work included in this Scope of Work will be completed by August 27, 2027.
3. COMPENSATION
In consideration of the services to be performed pursuant to this Agreement, the
Consultant will bill the City and the City agrees to pay Consultant as stated in Exhibit A
– Compensation Schedule, for a total not to exceed amount of one million, four
hundred seventy-four thousand, five hundred fifty dollars ($1,474,550.00).
4. CONSULTANT RESPONSIBILITY
In providing services under this Agreement, the Consultant shall perform in a manner
consistent with that degree of care and skill ordinarily exercised by members of the same
profession currently practicing under similar circumstances. Upon notice to the
Consultant, the Consultant will, without additional compensation, correct those services
not meeting such a standard.
5. COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS
The Parties agree that they will perform their obligations as provided in this Agreement
in accordance with all applicable laws and ordinances.
