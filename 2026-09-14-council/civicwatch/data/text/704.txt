Accident and Damage Risk Management Claims 
The City routinely incurs certain costs that are unpredictable and cannot be reliably estimated 
during the annual budget process. To keep the original budget realistic, these costs are 
addressed through year -end reappropriations from reserves once the actu al amounts are 
known. 
Accident-related repair costs are one example. The City is responsible for a $5,000 deductible 
for each covered incident and may also incur member surcharges depending on the severity and 
total cost of the damage. The City is responsible for the full cost of repairs below the deductible.

General Fund Increase in Natural Gas, Electricity and Fleet Costs 
State law allows departments to transfer budgeted amounts between line items within their 
General Fund department (i.e. Police, Fire, CRE, Compliance, Public Works) without Governing 
Body approval. However, the City Treasurer has established certain restrictions on these 
transfers. For example, payroll budgets, including wages and benefits, may not be used for non-
payroll purposes without prior approval. Because the C ity Treasurer’s Office develops the 
payroll budgets, shortfalls in certain payroll accounts are addressed at the end of the fiscal year 
rather than requiring individual departments to absorb the overages. 
The City Treasurer’s Office also develops the budgets for certain costs that departments have 
little control over, including natural gas, electricity, and fleet expenses. Natural gas and 
electricity budgets for City facilities are estimated based on historical usage and current pricing 
trends. These budgets are reserved for utility costs and may not be redirected by departments 
to cover other expenses. Likewise, departments are not expected to use other operating budget 
line items  to absorb utility shortfalls. A total of $120,382 from General Fund reserves  is 
requested to cover FY 2026 natural gas and electricity budget shortfalls. 
Fleet fuel, parts, and labor costs are handled in the same manner. Because these costs are 
estimated by the City Treasurer’s office and departments have limited control over them, 
departments are not expected to reduce other operating expenditures to cover year -end fleet 
shortfalls. An additional $141,893 from General Fund reserves is requested to cover FY 2026 fleet 
cost overages. 
Vacation and Sick Leave Accrual Payouts for Terminated Employees 
Sick and vacation leave payouts resulting from employee separations are unavoidable 
obligations that cannot be budgeted accurately because the timing and amounts are

unpredictable. The City’s budgeting approach holds managers accountable only for costs within 
their control. Therefore, I am requesting  $172,132 from General Fund reserves  to cover all FY 
2026 leave payouts. 
Vandalism/Theft Expenses 
During FY 2026, six City divisions (Golf, Parks, Clean and Safe, Facilities Maintenance, Compliance 
Administration, and Risk Management) incurred unplanned costs related to vandalism and theft 
of City assets. Because these incidents are unpredictable, the costs were not included in the 
original FY 2026 budget. A total of $35,898 from General Fund reserves  is requested to cover 
these expenses.
