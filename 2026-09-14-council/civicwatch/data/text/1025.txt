RECOMMENDED MOTION: 
 
Move to recommend the Governing Body approve the assigned zoning for land north of Dell Range 
Boulevard, east and west of Ridge Road and south of Storey Boulevard to AG Agriculture, MR 
Medium-density Residential, MUR Mixed-Use Residential, and CB Community Business as shown in the 
attached zone change map, noting that the project meets the review criteria for approval. 
 
APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS: 
 
• UDC 2.2.8 Assigned Zoning 
 
BACKGROUND: 
The City is annexing parcels that are 100% surrounded by current City Limits as part of Councils goals to 
annex county pockets. Annexation of a parcel requires a city zone to be established. Per UDC 2.2.8 a zone will 
be assigned based on the underlying county zone and per the policy document adopted by the city.  
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The assigned zone is the most analogous City zone to the current zone when considering 
allowed uses. Zoning for standard County zones shall be assigned based on the policy 
document, while zones not listed on the policy document and terminated City zones shall be 
assigned a City zone on a case-by-case basis. (UDC 2.2.8.d.1) 
 
The underlying county zones are A-1 Agricultural and Rural Residential, MR Medium-density 
Residential, MU Mixed-use and PUD Planned Unit Development. The most analogous city zones 
are AG-Agriculture, MR Medium-density Residential and MUR Mixed-use Residential according 
to the policy document for the corresponding county zones. The PUD was analyzed by staff and 
the closest city zone was determined to be the CB Community Business zone. The CB zone 
would allow most of the current permitted uses by the PUD.    
 
The Assigned Zoning policy document is attached to the staff report as attachment number two. 
The County PUD is also attached as attachment number three.  
 
Establishment of the City zone matches the policy document; therefore, the item complies with 
review criterion 1. 
 
STAFF REVIEW: 
 
Through the review process, there were no comments.  
 
CONCLUSION: 
 
Staff is satisfied that the application meets the review criteria set forth in UDC 2.2.8.(d).
