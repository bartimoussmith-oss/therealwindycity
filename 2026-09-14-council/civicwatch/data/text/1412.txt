 ______________________________________________ 
Four (4) Proposers responded to  Request for Proposal (RFP) #2-26, for Auditing the Annual 
Financial Statements of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming and its Board of Public Utilities .  The 
project will provide audit services and report preparation for a three (3) year term. 
 
The proposals were received from:  
1. CLA (CliftonLarsonAllen LLP) 
2. ClingerHagerman, LLC 
3. Eide Bailly LLP 
4. McGee, Hearne & Paiz, LLP d/b/a MHP Assurance Services, LLP 
 
Upon review of the proposals by the evaluation panel, the group has recommended acceptance of 
the proposal submitted by McGee, Hearne & Paiz, LLP d/b/a MHP Assurance Services, LLP. 
 
The Consultant shall bill the City and the Board separately for each entity’s respective share of the 
services performed under this Agreement, and each entity shall be responsible only for payment 
of the amounts attributable to its respective audit services.  
1. For the City, a total not to exceed amount of $352,800.00.  
2. For the Board of Public Utilities, a total not to exceed amount of $195,800.00. 
3. Total Contract amount, not to exceed $548,600.00. 
 
Basis for award was based on a Qualifications and Pricing based review and scoring of proposals.

This Professional Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is  made and entered into by and between 
the City of Cheyenne, located at 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001 (“City”),  the 
Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities, located at 2416 Snyder Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001 
(“Board”), and McGee, Hearne & Paiz, LLP d/b/a MHP Assurance Services, LLP , located at  
1509 Bent Ave , Cheyenne, W yoming 82001 (“Consultant”). This Agreement shall become 
effective upon the date of the last signature affixed hereto. 
 
RECITALS 
 
In consideration of the mutual covenants and obligations herein expressed, it is agreed by 
and between the parties as follows: 
 
1. SCOPE OF WORK 
The Consultant agrees to provide services as described for Auditing the Annual Financial 
Statements of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming , and the Cheyenne  Board of Public 
Utilities, in accordance with City of Cheyenne Request for Proposal #2-26, incorporated 
herein by reference, and the Consultant’s submitted proposal , dated February 26, 2026, 
also incorporated herein by reference. 
 
The services performed under this Agreement include audit services for both the City and 
the Board of Public Utilities, which are separate reporting entities, and the Consultant 
shall allocate and invoice services accordingly. 
 
2. TIME OF PERFORMANCE 
The work included in this Scope of Work will be completed by May 31, 2029. 
 
3. COMPENSATION 
In consideration of the services to be performed pursuant to this Agreement, the 
Consultant will bill the City and Board, and the City and Board agrees to pay Consultant 
as stated in Exhibit A – Compensation Schedule. 
 
The Consultant shall bill the City and the Board separately for each entity’s respective 
share of the services performed under this Agreement, and each entity shall be 
responsible only for payment of the amounts attributable to its respective audit services.  
For the City, a total not to exceed amount of three hundred fifty -two thousand, eight 
hundred dollars ($352,800.00). For the Board of Public Utilities, a total not to exceed 
amount of one hundred ninety -five thousand, eight hundred dollars ($195,800.00).  The 
total contract amount shall not exceed five hundred forty -eight thousand, six hundred

4. CONSULTANT RESPONSIBILITY 
In providing services under this Agreement, the Consultant shall perform in a manner 
consistent with that degree of care and skill ordinarily exercised by members of the same 
profession currently practicing under similar circumstances. Upon notice to the 
Consultant, the Consultant will, without additional compensation, correct those services 
not meeting such a standard. 
 
5. COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS 
The Parties agree that they will perform their obligations as provided in this Agreement 
in accordance with all applicable laws and ordinances. 
 
6. DRUG-FREE WORKPLACE 
In compliance with the Drug -Free Workplace Act of November 1988, t he City and  the 
Board have established an Alcohol and Controlled Substance Policy that pertains to 
alcohol and drug usage by City and Board employees. All architects, engineers, and other 
consultants under contract with City of Cheyenne, and their employees and 
sub-consultants, are required to comply with the provisions of the City’s  and Board ’s 
Alcohol and Controlled Substance Policy for drug and/or alcohol usage on City property 
or other sites occupied by the Consultant while performing the duties and responsibilities 
of the Agreement. It is the responsibility of the Consultant to familiarize themselves with 
the requirements of this policy and to inform all their employees and sub -consultants of 
the requirements and ensure their compliance. If the Consultant, their employees, or 
sub-consultants are found in violation of this policy, the Agreement may be terminated. 
 
7. INDEMNIFICATION/HOLD HARMLESS 
The Consultant agrees to indemnify, hold harmless , and defend the City and the Board 
from and against any and all liabilities, claims, penalties, forfeitures , and suits, and the 
cost and expenses incident thereto, including reasonable attorney’s fees, which may 
hereafter arise as a result the performance of the Consultant’s duties, including death or 
bodily injury to any person, destruction or damage to any property, contamination of or 
adverse effects on the environment, or any violation of governmental laws, regulations , 
or orders to the extent caused by (1) the Consultant’s breach of any term or provision of 
this Agreement; or (2) any negligent or wrongful act, error , or omission by the 
Consultant, or its employees or sub -consultants in the performance of this Agreement. 
The Consultant acknowledges that it may incur a financial obligation to t he City and the 
Board pursuant to the terms of this paragraph.

9. MINORITY AND DISADVANTAGED BUSINESS ENTERPRISES (49 C.F.R. Part 26) 
All parties to this Agreement assure that no person will be excluded from participation in, 
denied the benefits of, or otherwise discriminated against, in connection with the award 
and performance of this Agreement on the grounds of age, race, color, disability, national 
origin, or sex. 
 
10. MONTHLY REPORT 
With each monthly billing, the Consultant shall provide the City and the Board with a 
written statement describing the status of the work under the Scope of Work, together 
with applicable time sheets and an updated work schedule. The Consultant shall submit 
separate invoices to the City and to the Board for services performed for each entity. 
Failure to provide the required monthly report may delay processing of the corresponding 
invoice until the report is submitted. 
 
11. INDEPENDENT CONSULTANT 
The Consultant shall function as an independent consultant for the purposes of this 
Agreement. The Consultant shall assume sole responsibility for any debts or liabilities 
that may be incurred by the Consultant in fulfilling the terms of this Agreement. Nothing 
in this Agreement shall be interpreted as authorizing the Consultant or its agents or 
employees to act as an agent or representative of or on behalf of t he City or the Board, or 
to incur any obligation of any kind on the behalf of the City or the Board.

12. TAXES 
The Consultant agrees to pay all valid taxes, excises, license fees, permit fees, bills, 
debts, and obligations incurred by and in connection with its operations under this 
Agreement. 
 
13. DEFAULT 
Each and every term and condition in this Agreement shall be deemed to be a material 
element of the Agreement. In the event either party should fail or refuse to perform 
according to the terms of this Agreement, such party may be declared in default. 
 
14. REMEDIES 
In the event a party has been declared in default, such defaulting party shall be allowed a 
period of fifteen (15) days within which to cure the default. In the event the default 
remains uncorrected, the non-defaulting party may elect to: 
a. Terminate the Agreement and seek damages, which damages shall not exceed the 
contract amount; or 
b. Treat the Agreement as continuing and require specific performance. 
 
15. TERM AND TERMINATION 
The term of this Agreement shall be from date of execution to June 30, 2029. The parties 
may by mutual written agreement renew or extend this Agreement. The City or the Board 
may, without cause and upon thirty (30) days’ written notice to the Consultant, terminate 
this Agreement in whole or in part as it relates to the services being performed for that 
entity. Such termination by one entity shall not terminate the Agreement with respect to 
the other entity unless otherwise agreed in writing by the parties.  Upon receipt of such 
notice, the Consultant shall: 
a. Discontinue all services affected; and 
b. Deliver to the terminating entity within five (5) days all data, drawings, 
specifications, reports, estimates, summaries, and such other information and 
materials as may have been accumulated by the Consultant in performing this 
Agreement, whether completed or in process. 
c. In the event of termination for convenience, the City or the Board, as applicable, 
will pay the Consultant for accepted work done and expenses incurred for services 
performed for that entity to the date of termination. Such acceptance shall not be 
unreasonably withheld. 
 
16. ADDITIONAL REMEDIES 
In the event the Consultant fails to strictly perform in accordance with this Agreement, 
the City or the Board may, with respect to services performed for that entity, elect to 
correct the deficiencies and charge the Consultant for the reasonable costs incurred in 
doing so.  In the event of default of any of the conditions by either party which shall 
require the party not in default to commence legal or equitable action against the 
defaulting party, each party shall bear its own costs and expenses, including without 
limitation attorney’s fees.

17. GOVERNING LAW, JURISDICTION AND VENUE 
The construction, interpretation, and enforcement of this Agreement shall be governed by 
the laws of the State of Wyoming. The courts of the State of Wyoming shall have 
jurisdiction over any action arising out of this Agreement and over the parties, and the 
venue shall be the First Judicial District, Laramie County, Wyoming. 
 
18. GOVERNMENTAL IMMUNITY 
The City of Cheyenne and the Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities, along with their 
respective officials and employees, do not waive their governmental immunity by 
entering into this Agreement, except to the extent necessary for the parties to pursue a 
contract action to clarify or enforce the written terms of the Agreement. Furthermore, the 
City and the Board specifically retain all immunities and defenses available to them as 
sovereign or governmental entities pursuant to Wyo. Stat. § 1 -39-101, et seq ., and all 
other relevant state and federal law. Designations of venue, choice of law, enforcement 
actions, and similar provisions shall not be construed as a waiver of governmental 
immunity. The parties agree that any ambiguity in this Agreement shall not be strictly 
construed, either against or for either party, except that any ambiguity as to governmental 
or sovereign immunity shall be construed in favor of governmental immunity. 
 
19. MONITORING ACTIVITIES 
The City and the Board  shall have the right to monitor all activities related to this 
Agreement that are performed by the Consultant or its sub-consultants. This shall include, 
but not be limited to, the right to make site inspections at any time and with reasonable 
notice; to bring experts and consultants on site to examine or evaluate completed work or 
work in progress; to examine the books, ledgers, documents, papers, and records 
pertinent to this Agreement; and to observe personnel in every phase of performance of 
the related work. 
 
20. OWNERSHIP OF DOCUMENTS AND WORK PRODUCT 
All deliverables by the Consultant, all work product of the Consultant, all notes, 
calculations, memoranda, or any other documentation generated by the Consultant 
pursuant to the scope of this Agreement shall be the property of the City of Cheyenne or 
the Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities, as applicable to the services performed for that 
entity, and shall be provided to the City or the Board, upon request, for materials related 
to the services performed for that entity. 
 
21. NON-DISCRIMINATION 
The Consultant shall comply with the Civil Rights Act of 1964, the Wyoming Fair 
Employment Practices Act (Wyo. Stat. § 27 -9-105, et seq .), the Americans With 
Disabilities Act (ADA), as amended, 42 U.S.C. § 12101, et seq ., and the Age 
Discrimination Act of 1975 and any properly promulgated rules and regulations thereto, 
and shall not discriminate against any individual on the grounds of age, sex, creed, color, 
race, religion, national origin, ancestry, pregnancy or qualifying disability in connection 
with the performance under this Agreement.

beyond the control of the parties, where any of those factors, circumstances, situations, or

Agreement as it relates to that party’s services by written notice to the other parties , 
without liability, damages, fees, or penalty, and any unused deposits or amounts paid 
shall be refunded, for any one or more of the above reasons, by written notice to the other 
party. 
 
23. PROFESSIONAL REGISTRATION 
The Consultant shall endorse, as required by law, plans and reports prepared under this 
Agreement and shall affix thereto his or her seal of professional registration, showing that 
he or she is licensed to practice in the State of Wyoming, if necessary.

Schedule (two (2) pages); Request for Proposal RFP 2 -26 issued via the OpenGov

to the subject matter hereof. There are no other terms or conditions, written or oral,
