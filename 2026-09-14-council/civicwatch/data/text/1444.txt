MEMORANDUM  
 
TO:  Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council 
FROM:  TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager 
DATE:  February 18, 2026 
SUBJECT: Consideration of Bid #E -13-26 for the Lease of One (1) New In 
Stock 40 Yard Front Loader Refuse Collection Truck 
 
 
One (1) bidder responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.   
 
The One (1) bid was received from: 
 
1. Elliot Equipment Company 
 
The bid was reviewed by Bryan Bell (Fleet Maintenance), Austin Reiner 
(Sanitation), Vicki Nemecek (Public Works), and Craig LaVoy (Public Works). 
 
Their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from Elliot 
Equipment Company. 
 
The lease will be for a One (1) Year Term, at a total cost of $10 9,388.96 
(including f reight) for a one (1) time up front lease payment.  The bid also 
contained the option for the City to buy -out the lease at the end of the One 
(1) Year Lease Term at a cost of $300,000.00 – or the City can end the lease and 
return the unit if it meets the minimum Buy Back requirements. 
 
The total requested bid award is for $409,388.96 (One (1) Year Term Lease + 
Freight+ OPTION of lease buy-out). 
 
Funding for the One (1) Year Lease will be covered by the Wyoming Association 
of Risk Management (WARM), under an open Insurance Claim for a damaged 
City Front Load Refuse Truck.  The City will be required to pay a deductible of 
$20,000.00 from the Solid Waste Fund, and WARM will cover the remainder of 
the lease.  In the event the City of Cheyenne Sanitation Department desires to 
buy-out the lease at the end of the term, the funds to cover the buy -out will 
come from the Solid Waste Fund.   
 
Funding:   WARM Insurance Proceeds
