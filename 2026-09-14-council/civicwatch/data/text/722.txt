APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS: 
 
• UDC 2.1.2 Preliminary Plat Approval 
• UDC 2.1.3 Final Plat Approval

• UDC Article 5 Zoning Regulations

BACKGROUND: 
 
The applicant is requesting approval of a preliminary plat for roughly 0.70 acres of land. The proposed plat 
would subdivide the parcel into 8 Lots for duplex residential development. These Lots would be considered 
SD2 Lots within the MR zone.  
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Preliminary Plat, the Commission 
must make the following findings required in UDC 2.1.2.d  – a detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical 
patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.2.d.1) 
 
On the future land use map found in PlanCheyenne, the subject land and surrounding area has 
been designated as Urban Residential. The Urban Residential designation encourages a range of 
residential uses. The surrounding area includes duplexes and single-family residential. The 
proposed plat is to provide additional duplex residential to the area.  
 
Foundation 1 of PlanCheyenne encourages community growth. This development is in response 
to market demands and is within the Urban Service Boundary. The development patterns 
proposed by this plat are compatible with the designations found on the future land use map.

The application complies with this review criterion.  
 
2) The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to 
existing and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration 
of current and future streets, open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or 
facilities. (UDC 2.1.2.d.2) 
 
The proposed plat subdivides 4 larger parcels into 8 residential lots that meet the standards of 
the SD2 Lot type. This plat adds extends the utilities in the area allowing for further development. 
The surrounding area is largely already developed with residential uses and the site has access to 
all utilities.   
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
   
3) The division of land meets the purposes, intent, and all applicable standards in 
Article 4. (UDC 2.1.2.d.3) 
 
Community Facility Fees (4.2): 
For fees based on acreage, the area of the plat is pro-rated to the nearest tenth of an acre. 
Lands reserved as unbuildable (for right-of-way, detention, parks, remainder lots, etc.), are 
exempt from fees. The Park Land Acquisition Fee and Park Infrastructure Fee total $700 per 
acre due at time of building permit  
 
Per UDC Section 4.2.5, Table 4-2, Enhancement Fees are payable at the issuance of a building 
permit for each structure. The Park Enhancement Fee for residential is $400 per dwelling unit.  
 
Transportation Network (4.3): 
This plat is not creating additional streets. The proposed block does not meet the block size 
requirement of the UDC; however there is no opportunity for this plat to efficiently reduce the 
block size. Staff recommends the infill expectation to block size be granted.  
 
There will not be a vehicular access from Lot 8 to Dell Range Blvd. Dell Range is substandard 
in required width and this plat will need to dedicate an additional ten (10) feet of right-of-way 
on Lot 8 along Dell Range. 
 
Open Space (4.4): 
Due to the nature of this application, Open space is not required with the plat.  
 
Required Improvements (4.5): 
This plat appears to be able to provide all required utilities to the lots within this 
development. 
 
 
Public Safety Fees (4.6): 
Public Safety Fees are required at the time of construction of any residential building on any 
of the proposed lots. Per UDC Section 4.6.3.b, Public Safety Fees are payable at the issuance 
of a building permit for each structure.

logical and well-planned development pattern. This may include consideration of

5) The impacts identified in specific studies or technical reports that were prepared or 
requested to be prepared in association with the application, and whether the 
findings and recommendations of these reports, or any modifications or conditions

Drainage: 
A drainage study was submitted and reviewed. There were no concerns with drainage at the plat 
level for the project as presented.  
 
Traffic: 
A transportation study was submitted and reviewed. There were no concerns with traffic at the 
plat level for the project as presented.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion. 
 
6) Lots proposed for development shall be buildable lots according to the existing or 
proposed zoning district standards, result in design and development patterns that 
meet the Intent and Applicability statements of the existing or proposed zoning 
district, and not aggravate nonconforming setbacks or currently adopted building 
and fire code access and separation requirements. (UDC 2.1.2.d.6) 
 
All lots proposed for development are buildable lots in the MR zone district. 
 
The applicant proposes reducing rear setback by 10’ to make the proposed dwelling units fit. 
Since the applicant is limited in lot depth by previous development. Staff is amenable to granting 
the adjustment if the Governing Body approves the plat.  
 
Where development occurs without plat-level open space, the UDC requires all single-unit homes 
to provide 200sqft of open space that is at least 15’ in each dimension (UDC 6.6.3.a.2), This 
requirement is typically easily met by backyards with the 20’ rear setback. Even with the proposed 
reduction in rear yard setback to 10’, the front yards of these dwelling units may be able to 
provide the required space. If not, the reduction of the rear setback dimensional requirement may 
also necessitate an administrative reduction in the open space 15’ minimum dimension 
requirement.   
 
The application complies with this review criterion with conditions.

recommendation or modifies conditions of approval, the Commission shall cite specific reasons (based on 
the review criteria) for approval or denial.
