ORDINANCE NO. ________ 
ENTITLED: "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING  AND REPEALING MULTIPLE 
SECTIONS OF CHAPTER 9.24, WEAPONS, OF TITLE 9, PUBLIC PEACE 
AND WELFARE, OF THE MUNICIPAL CODE OF THE CITY OF 
CHEYENNE, WYOMING,  RELATING TO THE SALE, TRANSPORT, 
DISCHARGE, POSSESSION AND REGULATION  OF FIREARMS TO 
BETTER CONFORM TO STATE STATUTE AND AVOID DUPLICATION 
WITH FEDERAL LAW.” 
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, 
WYOMING: 
Section 1 .  That Section  9.24.020, Exceptions , Section 9.24.030, Discharging firearms 
prohibited, Section 9.24.090, Carrying deadly weapons on school property,  and Section 9.24.070 
Sale or possession of firearms without manufacturer’s identification mark prohibited,  of Chapter 
9.24, Weapons, of Title  9, Public Peace and Welfare,  of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, are 
hereby amended to read: 
9.24.020 - Exceptions. 
This chapter shall not apply to duly appointed officers of the city, county or state or of the 
United States in the lawful discharge of their duties as such officers, nor to transactions in which 
delivery is made of a pistol firearm by mail or other common carrier . to a destination not within 
the city. 
9.24.030 - Discharging firearms prohibited. 
A. No person shall discharge within the city any firearm, unless such discharge occurs
within the confines of an indoor range or in self-defense pursuant to Wyo. Stat. Section
6-2-602.
B. No minor shall wear or carry any air gun, paintball gun, bow, or crossbow in public
and not within a range. No minor shall discharge any projectile from an air gun,
paintball gun, bow, or crossbow unless under the supervision of an adult. No person
shall discharge any projectile from an air gun, paintball gun, bow, or crossbow, unless
such discharge occurs within a range utilizing procedures and structures to prevent the
discharged projectiles from causing damage to persons or property, both inside and
outside the confines of the range.
C. It shall be a violation of the provisions of this chapter for any person to discharge from
an air gun, paintball gun, bow, crossbow, or firearm any projectile that escapes the
confines of the range in which the projectile was discharged, whether or not the
discharged projectile causes damage to persons or property.

9.24.090 - Carrying deadly weapons on school property.  
 
A. School Bus Defined. For purposes of this section, "school bus" means every motor vehicle 
that is owned by or leased to or registered to a school district and is used to transport 
children to or from school in conjunction with school activities and is desig ned for and 
capable of carrying twelve (12) or more passengers, but not including buses operated by 
common carriers in transportation of school children or buses owned by a community 
college or the University of Wyoming. 
 
B. Deadly Weapon Defined. For purposes of this section, "deadly weapon" means, but is not 
limited to, a firearm, explosive or incendiary material or any other device, instrument, 
material or substance which, in the manner it is used or intended to be used, is reasonably 
capable of producing death or serious bodily injury.  “Deadly weapon” shall not include a 
firearm. 
 
C. Carrying Deadly Weapons Restricted. No person, except a sworn peace officer, shall 
possess, wear or carry a deadly weapon on a school bus as defined in this section or within 
the boundaries of real property owned or used by a school district primarily for the 
education of any student in any grade from kindergarten through twelfth grade. 
 
D. Application to Weapons in Motor Vehicles. This section shall not apply to any person 
possessing a deadly weapon inside a motor vehicle within the boundaries of real property 
owned or used by a school district when such deadly weapon is in plain view throug h a 
window at all times and such deadly weapon is securely placed in a rack whose use is 
principally for the storage of such deadly weapon and such weapon is not in a position as 
to be capable of being discharged. 
 
E.D. Application to Instructors of Courses. This section shall not apply to any person 
possessing a deadly weapon within the boundaries of real property owned or used by a 
school district at the specific time designated by the instructor of a course of study in  the 
use of such deadly weapon and which such course is approved by the school district. 
 
F. Application to Concealed Weapons. This section shall not apply to any person possessing 
a valid concealed weapon permit, as defined under Wyo. Stat. Section 6 -8-104 legally 
issued to that person by the state of Wyoming. 
 
9.24.070 - Sale or possession of firearms without with manufacturer's identification mark or 
serial number removed prohibited. 
  
No person shall receive, possess, sell, lease or otherwise transfer any firearm from which 
the manufacturer's identification mark or serial number has been removed. 
  
Section 2.  That Section 9.24.120, Records to be kept, of Chapter 9.24, Weapons, of Title 
9, Public Peace and Welfare, of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, is hereby repealed.

9.24.120 - Records to be kept. 
A true record shall be made by each licensed manufacturer or dealer of each firearm sold, 
pledged as security for a loan, transferred or otherwise disposed of at wholesale or retail. This 
record shall contain the information and be used as required by state law. 
Section 3.  Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases of this ordinance are 
severable. If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase  of this ordinance is declared 
unconstitutional or otherwise unlawful, such declaration shall not affect any of the remaining 
sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases. 
Section 4.  This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon approval and publication.
