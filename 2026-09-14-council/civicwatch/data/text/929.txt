RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE FILING OF APPLICATIONS 
WITH THE FEDERAL TRANSIT ADMINISTRATION, AN OPERATING 
ADMINISTRATION OF THE UNITED STATES DEPARTMENT OF 
TRANSPORTATION, FOR FEDERAL TRANSPORTATION 
ASSISTANCE AUTHORIZED BY 49 U.S.C. CHAPTER 53; TITLE 23, 
UNITED STATES  CODE, OR OTHER FEDERAL STATUTES 
ADMINISTERED BY THE FEDERAL TRANSIT ADMINISTRATION.” 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne, through the Cheyenne Transit Program, operates public 
transportation services that are vital to the mobility, accessibility, and  well-being of the City’s 
residents; 
 
WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Transit Program receives a significant portion of its operational 
and capital funding through federal transportation assistance programs administered by the FTA, 
including regular funding through the Urbanized Area Formula Program aut horized under 49 
U.S.C. § 5307, as well as other FTA programs such as the Bus and Bus Facilities Program (49 
U.S.C. § 5339), the Enhanced Mobility of Seniors and Individuals with Disabilities Program (49 
U.S.C. § 5310), and various discretionary or emergency grant opportunities; 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne has received written authority from the Governor of 
Wyoming, as the designated recipient, to apply for and receive Urbanized Area Formula Program 
assistance; 
 
WHEREAS, the Administrator of the Federal Transit Administration (FTA) has been 
delegated authority to award federal financial assistance for public transportation projects; 
 
WHEREAS, any grant or cooperative agreement for federal financial assistance will 
impose certain obligations upon the applicant, including compliance with federal requirements and 
the provision of matching funds for the project; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne has provided or will provide all annual certifications 
and assurances required by the FTA in connection with such applications. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Mayor, or his designee, is authorized to execute and file 
applications for federal assistance on behalf of the City of Cheyenne and the Cheyenne Transit 
Program with the Federal Transit Administration, for assistance authorized by 49 U.S.C. Chapter  
53, Title 23 of the United States Code, or any other federal statute administered by the FTA; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED,  that the Mayor, or his designee, is further authorized to 
execute and file with such applications all annual certifications, assurances, and other documents 
required by the Federal Transit Administration before awarding a federal assistance grant or 
cooperative agreement; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the City of Cheyenne affirms it has received authority 
from the designated recipient to apply for Urbanized Area Formula Program assistance under 49 
U.S.C. § 5307; and 
 
BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED, that the Mayor, or his designee, is authorized to execute 
grant agreements, cooperative agreements, and related documents with the Federal Transit 
Administration on behalf of the City of Cheyenne and the Cheyenne Transit Program, and to take 
any other actions necessary to carry out the purpose of this resolution.
