SUBJECT: Reappropriation to be considered for approval 
 
 
On September 8, 2025, the Governing Body will refer a resolution to the Finance Committee to 
reappropriate funds for the Fiscal Year (FY) 2025 City of Cheyenne Budget in accordance with 
W.S. § 16-4-112 and 16-4-113. Additional details about this final FY 2025 reappropriation request 
are provided below. 
GENERAL FUND RESERVES REQUEST 
The following General Fund reserve requests are included for your consideration: 
Accident and Damage Risk Management Claims 
The City routinely encounters expenditures that are inherently unpredictable and cannot be 
accurately forecasted during the annual budgeting process. This approach aligns with 
recommended municipal financial practices, emphasizing realistic, data- driven budgeting and 
cautious year -end fund balance evaluation. Accordingly, the City manages such expenditures 
through budget reappropriation at the close of the fiscal year. 
Repair costs resulting from accidents are prime examples of these variable expenditures. For each 
incident, the City is responsible for a $5,000 insurance deductible and may also incur additional 
member surcharges determined by the severity and cost of the damage. Any accident expenses 
below $5,000 are solely the City's responsibility. 
For FY 2025, a total of $62,602 from reserves is being requested to cover repair and related costs, 
allocated by department as follows: 
• Community Recreation and Events - $6,190 
• Compliance - $500 
• Fire - $11,415 
• Police - $39,497 
• Public Works - $5,000

Vacation and Sick Leave Accrual Payouts for Terminated Employees 
Sick and vacation  leave accrual payouts following employment terminations  represent 
unavoidable obligations that are not budgeted for due to the unpredictable nature of this expense . 
My budgeting philosophy holds managers accountable only for expenses within their control or 
influence.  As a result, I am requesting General Fund reserves in the amount of $188,480 to cover 
all FY 2025 accrual payouts. 
 
Increase in Natural Gas and Electricity and Fleet Fuel, Labor, and Parts Costs   
Departments are allowed, by state statute, to transfer budget amounts between line items within a 
General Fund department level without Governing Body approval.  However, I have placed certain 
restrictions on these transfers .  For example, I do not allow  the budget for  payroll line items, 
including wages and benefits, to be used for other items, such as office supplies or professional 
services, unless prior approval is given.   
The City Treasurer’s office estimates natural gas and electricity costs for City facilities by 
analyzing historical usage and current price trends during the annual budgeting process.  I require 
that the utility budgets approved by the Governing Body must be used exclusively for their 
intended purpose, and departments are not permitted to repurpose these funds for other needs. 
Likewise, departments are not held liable for utility budget shortfalls. To address projected 
shortages, a total of $62,056 from General Fund reserves is requested. 
Additionally, fleet expenses including fuel, parts and labor for several General Fund departments 
have exceeded the approved FY 2025 budget. These budgets are managed in the same manner as 
utility costs .  F unds are earmarked for fleet expenses  only and cannot be redirected, nor are 
departmental managers responsible for covering deficits.  Therefore, an increase of $247,638 from 
General Fund reserves is requested to reconcile this budgetary shortfall.

Vandalism/Theft Expenses    
Five City divisions, including Programs and Facilities, Golf, Parks, Clean and Safe, and Facilities 
Maintenance, incurred unplanned expenses from vandalism and theft affecting City assets over the 
past fiscal year. Due to the unpredictable nature of these incidents, such costs are difficult to 
forecast and were not included in the original FY 2025 budget. To ensure the City’s resources are 
protected and divisions can continue to operate effectively, an increase of  $33,950 from reserves 
is requested to cover these unforeseen expenditures.
