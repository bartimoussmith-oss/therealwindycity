Subject: County Pocket Annexation #10

Background:  
 
The item for consideration is a resolution to allow the City to proceed with a City-initiated annexation. 
Annexation of County pockets is a 2022 City Council priority.  A “County pocket” is an area of 
unincorporated land 100% surrounded by the City of Cheyenne.   
 
The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as follows: 
• Introduction and referral  January 12, 2026 
• Public Services Committee  January 20, 2026 
• Final consideration  January 26, 2026 
 
This annexation is comprised of 16 properties 100% surrounded by the City (See attached map). Of 
the 16 parcels, most are developed with residential, and a few are commercial / industrial. The City 
has maintained contact with the County Planning Office to coordinate any pending permits or 
development reviews.  Currently there are no active permits or development specific to the 
properties proposed for annexation.   
 
Annexation also requires establishing a City zone. Staff has created a tentative timeline for the 
annexation and establishment of City zoning districts for the subject properties. Zone districts will be 
established on the assigned zoning process of Unified Development Code (UDC) section 2.2.8. In the 
event of split zoning (where two zone districts divide an existing tract of land), the City intends to 
apply the more restrictive zone unless extenuating circumstances exist.

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne desires to initiate annexation proceedings for certain  
tracts of land  and rights -of-way located in western and south ern Cheyenne, comprising of 
approximately 48.38 acres, more or less, as depicted in Exhibit A, which is attached hereto and 
incorporated herein by this reference; 
 
WHEREAS, Wyoming Statute § 15 -1-404 specifies the requirements for initiating 
annexation proceedings by cities and towns and requires that a resolution be adopted by the 
Governing Body certifying compliance with Wyoming Statute § 15-1-402; 
 
WHEREAS, based on reasonable evidence, the Governing Body has determined that the 
territory to be annexed complies with Wyoming Statute § 15 -1-402 and meets the following 
criteria found in Wyoming Statute § 15-1-402(a): 
 
(i)  The annexation of the area is for the protection of the health, safety and welfare of the 
persons residing in the area and in the City of Cheyenne;  
 
(ii)  The urban development of the area sought to be annexed would constitute a natural, 
geographical, economical and social part of the City of Cheyenne;  
 
(iii)  The area s sought to be annexed is a logical and feasible addition to the City of 
Cheyenne and the extension of basic and other services customarily available to residents 
of the city or town shall, within reason, be available to the area proposed to be annexed; 
 
(iv)  The area sought to be annexed is contiguous with or adjacent to the City of Cheyenne;  
 
(v)  The Governing Body is prepared to issue one (1) or more franchises as necessary to 
serve the annexed area pursuant to Wyoming Statute § 15-1-410; and 
 
(vi)  The City of Cheyenne is prepared to, not less than twenty (20) business days prior to 
the public hearing required by Wyoming Statute § 15 -1-405(a), send by certified mail to 
all landowners and affected public utilities within the territory a summary of the annexation 
report as required under Wyoming Statute §  15-1-402(c) and notice of the time, date and 
location of the public hearing required by Wyoming Statute § 15-1-405(a).
