Please find the attached Contract Modification between the City of Cheyenne and Simon Contractors 
for the repaving and concrete services of the Public Works Improvements Project. 
Over the last two years while this project has been  in progress, extensive unforeseen problems in 
subgrade have been found and additional concrete fa ilures in areas outside the scope of the original 
project have occurred.  
The work that this modification includes, without limitation:  
I. City Hall 
a. Replacement of approximately (8) concrete slabs around the municipal building and its 
courtyard.  
b. Replacement of approximately 35 linear feet of curb and gutter. 
II. Fleet Maintenance Facility 
a. Replacement of 4” sidewalk along the north side of the building. 
III. Transfer Station 
a. Replacement of (4) 8” concrete slabs located near the west side of the building. 
IV. Street & Alley 
a. Replacement of 8” concrete pad in front of the ice slicer building. 
b. Replacement of (4) steel bollards in front of ice slicer building. 
c. Addition of an IT communication pole. 
d. Over-excavation for unforeseen soft spot repair. 
 
The pricing of these additional items accounts for an 8% cost escalation due to inflation over the past 
two years and additional mobilization costs, as stated by Simon in the attached document. 
 
The total additional cost proposed by this Contract Modification is $266,406.49   
 
Project Completion is scheduled for June 2026.

I. Description of Changes

modification will add the replacement of the following:  
• City Hall: Sidewalk concrete and curb and gutter in various places. 
• Fleet Maintenance: Entire sidewalk along the north side of the building. 
• Transfer Station: One area of concrete driveway on the west side of the building. 
• Street & Alley: Extended area of concrete pavement in front of the Ice Slicer building, 4 
additional steel bollards to replace existing bollards, and an IT communication pole. 
The additional work is further detailed in Exhibit A attached hereto and incorporated by 
reference.
