ORDINANCE NO. ________ 
 
ENTITLED: "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING  SECTION 10.04.050, OBEDIENCE 
REQUIRED, OF THE MUNICIPAL CODE OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, 
WYOMING, TO CLARIFY THE DEFAULT OFFENSE LEVEL FOR 
VIOLATIONS OF TITLE 10 OF THE MUNICIPAL CODE." 
 
 BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, 
WYOMING: 
 
Section 1 .  That Sectio n 10.04.050 , Obedience Required,  of Chapte r 10 .04, General 
Provisions, of Title 10, Vehicles and Traffic, of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, is hereby amended 
to read: 
 
10.04.050 - Obedience required. 
 
It Except as otherwise provided, it  is unlawful and a misdemeanor for any person to do any act 
forbidden or fail to perform any act required in this title. 
 
 Section 2 .  Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases of this ordinance are 
severable. If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase  of this ordinance is declared 
unconstitutional or otherwise unlawful, such declaration shall not affect any of the remaining sections, 
paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases. 
 
 Section 3.  This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon approval and publication.
