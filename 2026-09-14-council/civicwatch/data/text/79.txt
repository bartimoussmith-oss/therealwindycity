BACKGROUND: 
 
The applicant is requesting approval of a preliminary plat for roughly 91.29 acres of land. The proposed 
subdivision is to create two large parcels for recreational development, a parcel for the existing utility station, a 
parcel for public greenway and two remainder parcels for future development. This area is part of the 
Sweetgrass PUD and is planned for recreational development.

The Open Space and Parks designation encourages public and private open space, public and

Foundation 6 of PlanCheyenne encourages a system of connected parks throughout our 
neighborhoods and community. This plat proposes a large community park and additional open 
space tracts for future trails. 
 
Foundation 7 of PlanCheyenne support the use of existing infrastructure and timely provision of 
services (including emergency and education services). This development is largely surrounded by 
existing infrastructure. 
 
Other principles of PlanCheyenne encourage tying neighborhoods together and a compatible 
development pattern between neighborhoods. This development proposes tying into the 
surrounding neighborhoods to the extent possible. 
 
The application complies with this review criterion.

existing and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration 
of current and future streets, open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or

The proposed plat is largely surrounded by already platted land that is currently under

plat will provide additional ingress and egress to the subdivision through two East-West 
connections.   
 
The application complies with this review criterion.

Community Facility Fees (4.2): 
For fees based on acreage, the area of the plat is pro-rated to the nearest tenth of an acre. 
Lands reserved as unbuildable (for right-of-way, detention, parks, remainder lots, etc), are 
exempt from fees.  
 
Transportation Network (4.3): 
This plat proposes the missing connection of E. Murray Rd. to create an East-West connection 
into and out of the Sweetgrass Development. Loving Trail (formerly known as Avenue C) is 
proposed to extend further south to East Nation Rd. East Nation Rd. will provide a secondary 
East-West connection for the Sweetgrass Subdivision. A plan for construction of the rights-of-
way being platted with this subdivision has not been provided. The expectation is that this
