I. Description of Changes: 
This Modification adds additional compensation to the Agreement in an amount not to exceed 
Twenty-Five Thousand Dollars ($25,000.00) for continued on -call engineering services. The 
Consultant shall continue to provide services in accordance with the original Scope of Work, 
including preparation of design plans, construction support services, and other related 
engineering services as requested by the City, on an as -needed basis and subject to the 
availability of such funds. 
 
II. New Contract Items:  
None. 
 
III. Schedule:  
The Agreement term may extend beyond the original termination date set forth in Contract No. 
8218, if necessary, to allow for completion of services authorized under this Modification. Any 
such extension is limited to the period during which funds added by this Modification remain 
available and shall not exceed the time required to expend such funds. In no event shall the 
Contract continue after such funds have been fully expended or if the Agreement is otherwise 
terminated in accordance with its terms.
