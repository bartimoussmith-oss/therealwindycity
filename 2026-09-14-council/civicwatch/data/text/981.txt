This is a request for an additional award two (2) Mid-Size Trucks from Bid E-3 -25.  The 
original bid consideration was approved at the October 14, 2025 City Council Meeting. 
This request includes Two (2) Mid-Size Trucks for the Cheyenne Fire Rescue Department.  
(No additional Regular Cab or Crew Cab trucks are being ordered at this time). 
One (1) bidder responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.  The Bid was received 
from:

The bids were reviewed by Neil Durante (CFR), Andrew Dykshorn (CFR), and Bryan Bell 
(Fleet Maintenance), and their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from Ken 
Garff Cheyenne. 
The total requested bid award, is $144,614.00.
