Background:  
The item for consideration is a Future Land Use Map Amendment (or Comprehensive Plan 
Amendment). This is approved by Resolution.    
 
The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as follows: 
• Introduction and Referral   June 8, 2026 
• Public Services Committee   June 15, 2026 
• Final Consideration  July 13, 2026 
 
Planning Commission Recommendation:  
The Planning Commission heard this item at their June 1, 2026, meeting and voted 3-2 against the 
amendment. Wyoming State Statute requires a majority of the Commission (not just those present) to 
vote in favor for a recommendation of approval. This requires four ‘yes’ votes. Approval of this motion 
was essential for the associated zone change, and the Planning Commission used this to influence their 
recommendation on the associated zone change (see the zone change staff report for more 
information on that item). A Resolution overruling the Planning Commission’s disapproval is required 
and is a separate item on the Council agenda. 
 
At the Public Services Committee meeting on June 15, 2026, Staff will request that this item be 
postponed until the July 13, 2026 City Council meeting to line up with the annexation and zoning map 
amendment. The reason for introducing this item early is so that all items related to the Highlands 
development can be discussed at the same time.  
 
The Planning Commission Staff report is attached and unchanged. 
 
County Commissioner Comment: 
The amendment procedures in PlanCheyenne require proposed amendments entirely within City limits 
(or going to be within City Limits upon final consideration) to go to the County Commissioners for 
comment only. The County Commissioners heard this item at their meeting on June 2, 2026 and had no 
comments or questions.  
 
Public Comment 
Between the publication of the Planning Commission Staff Report and the publication of this memo 
(June 3, 2026), Staff has received no new inquires or comments from the public on this project. The 
project page on the website has received 77 views and no comments.

Move to approve the Resolution amending the Future Land Use Plan for roughly 3,238.8 acres of land 
east of South Greeley Highway and south of the Sweetgrass Development as shown in the Future Land

APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS: 
 
• PlanCheyenne (2014 Community Plan) 
 
BACKGROUND: 
 
The applicant is requesting amending the Future Land Use Map for roughly 3,238.8 acres of land to Mixed-use 
Employment to accommodate a zone change to the BP – Business Park zone. A portion of the land within this 
amendment is already annexed and is currently zoned BP. Another portion that is already annexed is zoned 
Sweetgrass PUD and currently designated ‘Urban Residential’ by the comprehensive plan. 
 
Most of this area is outside the Urban Services Boundary and has a Future Land Use Map designation of 
Agricultural/Rural. This area is proposed to be added to the Urban Services Boundary and changed to Mixed-
use Employment. 
 
A concurrent application for a zoning map amendment has been submitted. The zoning map amendment will 
accompany the future land use amendment at City Council. If the application for the zoning map amendment is 
approved, the applicant will be required to submit a site plan application for any new development. 
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING:

1) BENEFICIAL OR ADDRESSES ISSUES - The proposed amendment is beneficial to the 
community or addresses issues not foreseen during the planning process.    
 
The proposed amendment to Mixed-use Employment at the proposed location would be 
beneficial as it is on the very edge of the community. Mixed-use Employment areas should be 
located toward the fringe of the community and in areas that are not highly residential as they 
allow for more industrial and business park type developments. This area is mostly vacant or has 
industrial uses nearby. There is a small single-family neighborhood to the south that contains 
about 17 residences. The closest home is about 2,200 feet to the corner of the proposed area.  
 
When the 2014 amendment was adopted, it was not known that the subject property had the 
potential for industrial development. However, the market and property owners noted the prime 
location of the subject property on the periphery of the community. The development at this 
location warrants an extension of the Urban Services Boundary and amendment to the Natural 
and Cultural Resources area contained in the Plan. This amendment will facilitate further 
development in South Cheyenne and further several economic development goals contained 
within PlanCheyenne.  
 
The small portion currently designated Urban Residential is across the ridgeline from the rest of 
the development area and topographically speaking, it makes sense for this area to match the 
designation that appears on the side of the ridge it is on. 
 
The proposed Future Land Use amendment complies with review criteria 1. 
 
2) COMPATIBLY - The proposed amendment is compatible with the surrounding area, 
and the goals and policies of the Plan.  
 
The subject property is located in an area that is largely undeveloped. The subject property is 
adjacent to Industrial to west, Agricultural/Rural to the south and east, and Urban Residential to 
the north. The Agricultural/Rural would not allow for commercial or industrial type uses. Due to its 
isolated nature, the subject property is suitable for industrial development, noting the impacts of 
the industrial development will be minimal to the largely vacant surrounding properties.  The 
proposed amendment is consistent with the goals and policies of PlanCheyenne, as discussed in 
more detail below. 
 
The proposed Future Land Use amendment complies with review criteria 2. 
   
3) IMPACT MITIGATION - The proposed amendment will address and mitigate 
impacts on transportation, services, and facilities.  
 
Transportation: 
The transportation network in this area of the City is largely undeveloped with the exception of  
Bison Business Park to the west. As the property develops, the property owners and developers 
will be required to dedicate adequate right-of-way to service the area and mitigate the 
development’s impact on existing rights-of-way. Furthermore, the proposed development will be 
required to build any rights-of-way as required by the City Engineer through the platting process. 
 
Services:

The proposed change will require coordination with the Board of Public Utilities and Fire  
Department through the platting and site plan process. The proposed development will be  
required to meet the standards of these reviewing departments and agencies. 
 
 
Facilities: 
The proposed change may require additional facilities as determined by the platting or site 
plan processes. These changes will be addressed at these phases of the development process.  
 
The proposed Future Land Use amendment complies with review criteria 3. 
 
4) SERVICE PROVISION - The proposed amendment will have minimal effect on 
service provision, including adequacy or availability of urban facilities and services, 
and is compatible with existing and planned service provision  
 
A majority of this land is currently proposed to be annexed into City limits and urban facilities and 
services are available to the subject property. Water and Sewer would need to be extended from 
the west or the north. The future land use map amendment is proposing an amendment to the 
currently adopted Urban Services Boundary.  
 
The proposed Future Land Use amendment complies with review criteria 4. 
 
5) ANNEXATION - The proposed amendment does not jeopardize the City’s ability to 
annex the property. 
 
The subject property is currently proposed to be annexed into City limits. Two small portions have 
already been annexed, one adjacent to Bison Business park, the other as part of the large-scale 
Sweetgrass PUD.   
 
The proposed Future Land Use amendment complies with review criteria 5. 
 .   
6) URBAN SERVICES AREA - The proposed amendment is consistent with the logical 
expansion of the Urban Services Area. 
 
The proposed amendment suggests amending the Urban Services Boundary to encompass a 
majority of the proposed area to be annexed. The expansion of the Urban Services Boundary at 
this location is logical as it is adjacent to the current Urban Services Boundary and accounts for 
the continued development in South Cheyenne. 
 
The proposed Future Land Use amendment complies with review criteria 6. 
 
7) STRICT ADHERENCE - Strict adherence to the Plan would result in a situation 
neither intended nor in keeping with other key elements and policies of the Plan. 
 
Strict adherence to the Plan may result in unintended consequences. While the land could 
develop with very low density residential (large Lot development generally 10+ acres in size), it is 
on the edge of the community where providing services for residential development can be more 
costly and challenging to extend for a residential developer. Alternatively, the area could develop 
PUDC-26-116   June 1, 2026

with large-acre ranchette-style development in the County or Wind energy generation or other 
energy product facilities.  
 
The current Urban Service Boundary in this area appears to follow topography or parcel 
boundaries. Strict adherence to the current Urban Service Boundary in this area of the community 
may be unfairly limiting to land otherwise developable. 
 
The proposed Future Land Use amendment complies with review criteria 7. 
 
8) PUBLIC WELFARE AND CONSISTENCY – The proposed plan amendment will 
promote the public welfare and will be consistent with the goals and policies of 
PlanCheyenne and the elements thereof. 
 
Staff is satisfied that the proposed application will not have any adverse effects on public welfare. 
Furthermore, the application accomplishes the following policies contained within PlanCheyenne : 
 
• Policy 1.1.B: Flexibility to Respond to Market Demands 
The Future Land Use Plan does not predetermine all land uses on individual parcels and is 
intended to provide some flexibility for the market to help determine which uses are 
needed at a given time. However, when evaluating land uses for a proposed 
development, consider whether a diverse and integrated mix of uses is provided 
throughout the entire Cheyenne urbanized area. 
 
• Policy 3.1.B: Recruit New Employers 
Work with partners, such as the Chamber and LEADS, to continue to recruit larger 
employers that provide family wage jobs and that are predominantly clean, nonpolluting 
industries. 
 
• Policy 3.5.A: Locations for Heavy Industrial Uses 
Continue to leverage the Cheyenne Area’s unique location and physical assets, such as 
heavy rail and interstate access, by designating and maintaining areas for heavy industrial 
uses that may not be suitable within designated activity centers due to design and/or 
traffic impacts. Such uses may include trucking and distribution facilities, oil and gas 
support services, and warehousing among others. 
 
The proposed Future Land Use amendment complies with review criteria 8.
