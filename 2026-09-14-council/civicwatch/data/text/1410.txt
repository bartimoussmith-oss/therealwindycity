DRAINAGE EASEMENT AGREEMENT 
 
KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS that, CBH PROPERTIES CHEYENNE, 
LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company (“Grantor”), whose address is 17725 Katy Freeway, 
Suite 200, Houston, TX 77094, in consideration of the sum of Ten Dollars ($10.00) and other good 
and valuable consideration, receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged by and 
between the parties, does hereby grant to the City of Cheyenne, a Wyoming municipal corporation 
(“City”), whose address is 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001, and to its successors 
and assigns, a non-exclusive drainage easement, legally described on Exhibit A, attached hereto 
and incorporated herein. 
 
RECITALS 
 
WHEREAS, Grantor is the lawful owner of certain real property located in Laramie 
County, Wyoming, more particularly described and depicted on Exhibit A, attached hereto and 
incorporated herein (the “Easement Area”); 
WHEREAS, the City desires to obtain a drainage easement over the Easement Area for the 
purpose of providing and ensuring the maintenance of a stormwater conveyance and/or drainage 
facility; 
WHEREAS, Grantor acknowledges that it has the obligation to maintain the drainage 
facilities within the Easement Area in accordance with this Agreement; and 
WHEREAS, Grantor represents that it has good and lawful right and authority to grant the 
easement described herein and warrants title to the property. 
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other 
good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, 
the parties agree as follows: 
1. Grant of Easement .  Grantor hereby grants and conveys to the  City, its successors and 
assigns, the right and privilege to ensure on behalf of the public that the facility remains in Good 
Working Order. For the purposes of this agreement, “Good working Order” means the drainage 
system is operating as designed and constructed in accordance with the as -built construction 
drawings on file with the City including, but not limited to, ensuring that the structures and 
Easement Area land surface are free of debr is, impoundments, and/or alterations to the drainage 
flow. 
This easement is perpetual and shall run with the land unless released in writing by the 
City. This easement is binding upon and shall inure to the benefit of the heirs, executors, 
administrators, successors, and assigns of the parties hereto. 
Such grant includes all rights and privileges necessary or convenient for the full enjoyment 
and use thereof for the above-mentioned purposes including, but not limited to, the right of ingress 
and egress over Grantor’s adjoining property in order to exercise the explicit right to remedy any 
drainage issues on the property. 
 
2. Restrictions and Modifications.  Grantor shall not alter, construct, install, permit, or allow 
any structure, improvement, or development within the Easement Area that obstructs, impedes, or

interferes with the flow, function, or capacity of the drainage facilities. However, if approved in 
writing by the City, the Grantor may construct and/or install surface improvements to its property, 
including paved driveways, parking and/or walkways , landscaping, utilities and/or similar 
improvements, which improvements do not then or later interfere with the aforementioned 
purposes of the Easement Area. 
Should any approved or unapproved improvement subsequently interfere with the function 
or capacity of the drainage facilities, as determined by the City in its reasonable discretion, the 
City may require Grantor, at Grantor's expense, to promptly remove or modify such improvement 
as necessary to restore proper function, and if Grantor fails to do so after written notice and a 
reasonable opportunity to cure (not less than fifteen (15) days), the City may remove or modify 
such improvement and recover its reasonable costs from Grantor as provided in Section 3. 
 
3. Maintenance.  Grantor shall maintain the drainage facilities contained within  the 
Easement Area in Good Working Order. If the Grantor fails to perform required maintenance in 
accordance with this Agreement following written notice from the City specifying the deficiencies 
and providing a reasonable opportunity to cure of not less than thirty (30) days, the City shall have 
the right, but not the obligation, to enter the Easement Area to perform or contract for such 
maintenance. Grantor agrees to reimburse the City for all reasonable costs incurred, including, 
without limitation, labor, materials, administration, reasonable attorneys’ fees, and other expenses 
incurred in connection with the enforcement of this Agreement, within thirty (30) days of written 
demand, together with interest at the highest rate allowed by law from the date of expenditure until 
paid 
 
4. Shared Use.  The Grantor reserves the right to make any use of the Easement Area which 
is not inconsistent with the rights herein conveyed to the City, and which does not interfere with 
the aforementioned purposes of the Easement Area. 
 
5. No Third -Party Beneficiaries .  It is understood and agreed that the enforcement  and 
conditions of this agreement , and all rights contained herein , shall be strictly reserved to the 
Grantor and the City. Nothing contained in this agreement shall give any claim or right whatsoever 
by any third person or entity. It is the express intention of the Grantor and the City that any such 
person or entity, other than the Grantor or the City, that receives a service or benefit under this 
agreement shall be deemed an incidental third-party beneficiary only. 
 
6. Indemnification.  Grantor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the City, its elected 
officials, officers, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, demands, damages, 
liabilities, losses, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorney fees) arising out of or related 
to (i) Grantor’s failure to maintain the drainage facilities in Good Working Order, (ii) any 
obstruction, alteration, or improvement placed within the Easement Area by or at the direction of 
Grantor, or (iii) Grantor’s use of the Easemen t Area, except to the extent caused by the sole 
negligence or willful misconduct of the City. 
 
7. Recording.  Grantor agrees that it shall record this agreement in the real property records 
of Laramie County, Wyoming, and shall notify any successor in interest to all or any part of the 
property subject to this easement of the obligations herein. Failure to provide such notice shall not 
relieve any successor of its obligations under this agreement.
