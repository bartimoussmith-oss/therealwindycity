SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
BACKGROUND: 
This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from AG Agricultural and CB Community Business 
to NR-3 Neighborhood Residential – High Density for roughly 20.41acres of land. The land is currently 
vacant. The current AG zone was assigned by the City as a holding zone until the applicant determined a zone 
that would allow for development of the parcel.  
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns 
identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1) 
 
PlanCheyenne encourages growth within the Urban Service Boundary and the maximization of 
existing infrastructure. This property is within the Urban Service Boundary, in an area with 
infrastructure that is currently being built.

The Future Land Use Designation for this property is Urban Residential and the surrounding area 
is Urban Residential and Urban Transition Residential. According to the comprehensive plan the 
optimal City zones for the subject property’s future land use designation is any one of the 
residential zones, including NR-3. 
 
Based on the policies and future land use map, NR-3 zoning is appropriate at this location.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1. 
 
2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing 
zoning, the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the 
guidance of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2) 
 
Surrounding properties in the City are zoned NR-3 – Neighborhood Residential - High Density. 
There is also a property zoned NR-3 located south of East Carlson St. NR-3 zoning is compatible 
with surrounding zoning.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2. 
   
3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses 
of the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for 
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning dist rict (UDC 
2.2.1.d.3) 
  
The AG zone was assigned to this property as a holding zone and would only allow for 
agricultural type uses. NR-3 zoning is intended for smaller-block, compact development patterns. 
The NR-3 zone allows for a variety of residential type uses and allows multifamily as a use by 
right, which is a development style that has become more common recently.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3. 
 
4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to 
the Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)  
 
The subject property will need to be platted in the future and will need to meet the standards of 
Article 4. Due to the size of the parcel, staff believes the standards can be met.   
 
Infrastructure (streets and utilities) are in the area and new streets and utilities can be extended to 
this parcel. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4. 
 
5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the 
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC 
2.2.1.d.5) 
 
The NR-3 zone is intended for a high density and mix of dwelling types that promote a consistent 
neighborhood character, with a compact development pattern and highly connected street 
PUDC-25-138   November 3, 2025 
4 | Page  
  
network. Due to the size of the lot, staff believes the applicant will be able to plat the property in 
a way that meets the intent of the zone district proposed. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5. 
    
6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for 
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options if any 
specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed a fter 
approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6) 
 
The NR-3 zone allows a variety of residential uses. The NR-3 also has design standards required 
per UDC 6.6.4 which will need to be met for any residential development that is proposed for this 
area.   
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6. 
 
7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7)  
 
Proposal meets comprehensive plan principles. No other comments were received from reviewers.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7. 
 
8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare. (UDC 
2.2.1.d.8) 
 
City staff is satisfied that this proposed zone change would not adversely affect the public health, 
safety, or general welfare. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 8.
