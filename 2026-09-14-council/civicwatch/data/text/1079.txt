 
 
 
MEMORANDUM  
TO:  Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and City of Cheyenne Council Members 
FROM:  Robin Lockman, City Treasurer 
DATE:  May 19, 2025 
SUBJECT: Professional Services Agreement with MHP to Audit Annual Financial Statements 
 
 
On May 27, 2025, the Governing Body will refer to the Finance Committee a proposed one -year 
Professional Services Agreement between the City of Cheyenne, the Cheyenne Board of Public 
Utilities, and McGee, Hearne & Paiz (MHP) LLP to perform the annual audit for fiscal year 2025. 
The total cost to the City for the fiscal year 2025 financial and compliance audit is $88,200, plus 
$15,150 for report drafting, for a combined total of $103,350. This represents an increase of 
$10,675 (11.5%) over the fiscal year 2024 audit cost. 
If more than three major federal programs are required to be audited, an additional fee of $3,500 
per program will be billed to the City. For fiscal year 2025, MHP anticipates that the City of 
Cheyenne may have between three and four programs subject to au dit. The actual number of 
programs required will depend on the City’s final federal spending for the year. 
If you have any questions, please contact me at (307) 637-6336 or rlockman@cheyennecity.org.
