 
 
 
 
MEMORANDUM 
TO: The Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Cheyenne City Council Members 
FROM:  Robin Lockman, City Treasurer 
DATE:  February 17, 2026 
SUBJECT: Proceeds from Sale of Portion of Holliday Park 
 
Attached for your consideration is a resolution directing that the net proceeds from the 
sale of a portion of Holliday Park, pursuant to City Contract No. 7437, be deposited into 
the City’s Real Property Revolving Fund. 
As you may recall, the City entered into a Purchase and Sale Agreement with Brandon 
Swain on November 4, 2021, which has since been amended. The amended purchase price 
is $139,566.24. This resolution formally acknowledges the sale and directs the City 
Treasurer to deposit the net proceeds into the Real Property Revolving Fund upon closing.  
Closing occurred on February 13, 2026. 
The Real Property Revolving Fund was established five years ago to receive and manage 
proceeds from the sale of City -owned property and to ensure those funds remain 
available for future real property needs and strategic acquisitions. The resolution creating 
the fund requires the Governing Body to determine, on a case-by-case basis, whether sale 
proceeds are to be deposited into the fund. This action satisfies that requirement.

ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION DIRECTING THE DEPOSIT OF PROCEEDS FROM

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne (“City”) is the owner of certain real property commonly 
known as a portion of Holliday Park (the “Property”);  
 
WHEREAS, the City and Brandon Swain entered into that certain Purchase and Sale 
Agreement, City Contract No. 7437, dated November 4, 2021, together with all amendments 
thereto (collectively, the “Agreement”), which Agreement is on file in the Office of the City Clerk; 
 
WHEREAS, the Agreement, as amended, provides for a purchase price of One Hundred 
Thirty-Nine Thousand Five Hundred Sixty-Six Dollars and Twenty-Four Cents ($139,566.24); 
 
WHEREAS, the City has established a Real Property Revolving Fund for the purpose of 
receiving and managing proceeds from the sale of City-owned real property; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body desires to formally direct that the net proceeds received 
from the sale of the Property be deposited into the City’s Real Property Revolving Fund. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING , that the Governing Body hereby acknowledges the sale of the 
Property pursuant to City Contract No. 7437, as amended, including the amended purchase price 
of $139,566.24 and the closing date established therein. 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the City Treasurer is hereby authorized and directed 
to deposit the net proceeds received from the sale of the Property into the City of Cheyenne’s Real 
Property Revolving Fund upon closing. 
 
BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED, that the Mayor, City Clerk, City Treasurer, and other 
appropriate City officials are authorized to take such actions as are necessary or convenient to 
carry out the intent of this Resolution, which shall be in full force and effect immediately upon 
adoption.
