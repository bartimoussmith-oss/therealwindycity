MEMORANDUM  
TO:  Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council 
FROM: TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager 
DATE: February 18, 2025 
SUBJECT: Consideration of Bid #E-12-25 for As-Needed Emergency Snow Control for the Public 
Works Department 
______________________________________________ 
Bid #E-12-25 was solicited for As-Needed Emergency Snow Control for the Public Works Department. 
 
Three (3) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached. 
 
The bi ds were reviewed by Vicki Nemecek (Public Works), Craig LaVoy  (Public Works ), Bill 
Gonzalez (Street and Alley), and Moniet Flores (Public Works), and their recommendation is to accept 
the bids from all three (3) firms.  The City of Cheyenne will offer the snow control for that particular 
storm to the lowest responsive bidder first.  If that Contractor is unable to provide snow control services, 
the City will contact the next  lowest responsive bidder.   Additionally, more than one (1) Contractor 
may be utilized based on  the severity of the storm and snow accumulation. Each Contractor utilized 
shall bill the City in accordance with their submitted hourly rates. 
 
The award calculation was based on a 3 day snow event, with each Contractor responding for 8  hours 
per day (24 hours / per event).  Based on the number of Line Options (Machine Options) submitted, an 
overall hourly cost for each firm was calculated (Based on Year 1 Pricing). 
 
S&S Builders:  5 Options * 24 Hours * 120 Hours = Average Hourly Rate $151.20 
 
Full Tilt:  10 Options * 24 Hours * 240 Hours = Average Hourly Rate $174.20 
 
Knife River:  8 Options * 24 Hours * 192 Hours = Average Hourly Rate $221.88 
 
Based on this overall hourly cost, the recommended award preference would follow as listed below: 
1. S&S Builders 
2. Full Tilt 
3. Knife River 
 
The total Bid Award is for $100,000.00 per calendar year, for a three (3) year term, for a total of 
$300,000.00. 
 
Funding:  021-26-2616-42-21327 (Emergency Snow Removal / 1% Sales Tax 23-26)
