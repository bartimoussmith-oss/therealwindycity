The above referenced contract is amended or modified as set forth in the following and these 
amendments and modifications of the plans and specifications are agreed to by the parties. All 
other aspects of the original agreement remain unchanged and in full force and effect. 
 
I. Description of Changes: 
 
Additional concrete and asphalt repairs on Concord Road from Andover Drive to Newton Drive. 
These repairs will upgrade deteriorating curbs, gutters, sidewalks, and asphalt surfacing, 
improving pedestrian safety, street rideability, and drainage. This modification adds the next 
block of Concord Road extending to Newton Drive in order to connect the original project limits 
with planned work by the Street and Alley Division. The original construction plan to mill and 
overlay Cheshire Drive did not fully connect to the Street and Alley Division’s planned paving 
work on Concord Road from Pershing Drive to Newton Drive, which would have left a gap 
between projects. The addition of this roadway segment eliminates that gap and ensures 
continuity of improvements.  See Exhibit A, attached hereto and incorporated herein by 
reference. 
 
II. New Contract Items: 
 
Although the project is being expanded to include additional work, no new categories of work 
are being added to the contract; therefore, no new contract items are associated with this Contract 
Modification. 
 
III. Schedule: 
 
Due to t he additional work included under this contract modification, the time frame for  
completion date is hereby extended from November 15, 2025, to May 30, 2026.
