ORDINANCE NO. ________ 
 
ENTITLED: "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING  CHAPTER 15.20 , NATIONAL 
ELECTRICAL CODE ADOPTED —APPENDIX K, INTERNATIONAL 
BUILDING CODE —ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS ADOPTED , OF 
TITLE 15 , BUILDINGS AND CONSTRUCTION , OF THE MUNICIPAL 
CODE OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING,  UPDATING THE 
INCORPORATED NATIONAL ELECTRICAL CODE  PROVISIONS AS 
SPECIFIED." 
 
 BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, 
WYOMING: 
 
Section 1.  That Sections 15.20.010 and 15.20.030 of Chapter 15.20, National Electrical 
Code Adopted—Appendix K, International Building Code—Administrative Provisions Adopted, 
of Title 15, Buildings and Construction, of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, are hereby amended 
as set out in this ordinance. 
 
15.20.010 - National Electrical Code—Adopted. 
 
A document, being marked and designated as provided in this section and  of which one copy 
is on file in the office of the city clerk and open for inspection of the public, is adopted and made 
a part hereof as if fully set out in this chapter: 
 
 National Electrical Code, 2023 2026 Edition, as published by the National Fire Protection 
Association, Inc. 
 
15.20.030 - Appendix K, International Building Code, 2021 2024 Edition, Administrative 
Provisions—Adopted. 
  
A document, being marked and designated as provided in this section and  of which one copy 
is on file in the office of the city clerk and open for inspection of the public, is adopted and made 
a part hereof as if fully set out in this chapter: 
 
 Appendix K, International Building Code, 2021 2024 Edition—Administrative Provisions, 
as published by the International Code Council, Inc. 
 
 Section 2 .  Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases of this ordinance are 
severable. If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase  of this ordinance is declared 
unconstitutional or otherwise unlawful, such declaration shall not affect any of the remaining 
sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases.
