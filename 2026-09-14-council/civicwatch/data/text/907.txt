 
 
MEMORANDUM 
TO:  The Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and City of Cheyenne Council Members 
FROM:  Robin Lockman, City Treasurer 
DATE:  August 15, 2025 
SUBJECT: Capital Improvement Plan Resolutio n to be considered for approval 
 
On September 8, 2025, the Governing Body will re fer a resolution to the Finance Committee 
proposing updates to the City’s Capital Improvement Plan (CIP) process.  Details of the proposed 
amendment are outlined below. 
On November 25, 2013, the Governing Body adopt ed Resolution 5545, requi ring the City to 
prepare and submit a comprehensive, five-year Capital Improvement Plan each year. This plan 
was intended to include both proposed and ongoing projects, ranging from the acquisition of new 
capital assets, construction of facilities and infrastructure, and the repair, renovation, and 
maintenance of existing City assets. 
In accordance with the Government Finance Offi cers Association (GFOA) best practices, each 
project in the CIP was to identify: 
 The type(s) of project 
 The department responsible 
 A description of the project scope 
 Estimated costs 
 Proposed funding sources 
The original resolution required City staff to update and submit the CIP to the Governing Body 
annually, no later than January 2 of each year, starting in 2015.  The plan was intended to serve as 
a strategic decision-making tool for prioritizing projects and shaping the following fiscal year’s 
budget. 
However, since its adoption, the current CIP process has not functi oned effectively as a 
policymaking or budgetary planning tool.  Main taining an annual update has proven to require 
substantial staff time and City resources, without providing the intended level of benefit. 

To address these concerns, the proposed amendment would revise the update schedule from once 
a year to once every three years.  Under the new timeline, updated CIPs would be submitted to the 
Governing Body by December 31 of the applicable  year, beginning with the next update due 
December 31, 2027.

RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AMENDING RESOLUTION NO. 5545 , REQUIRING 
THE SUBMISSION OF AN UPDATED FIVE -YEAR CAPITAL 
IMPROVEMENT PLAN AND ESTABLISHING A THREE-YEAR REVIEW 
AND UPDATE CYCLE FOR THE PLAN THEREAFTER.” 
 
WHEREAS, capital expenditures constitute a substantial portion of the overall budget for 
the City of Cheyenne (“City”): 
 
WHEREAS, such expenditures include the acquisition of  new capital assets, the 
construction of new facilities and infrastructure, and the repair, renovation, and maintenance of 
existing City assets; 
 
WHEREAS, the Government Finance Officers Association (GFOA) is a nationally 
recognized professional association representing public finance officials throughout the United 
States and Canada, and provides best practices and guidance to promote sound financi al 
management in government; 
 
WHEREAS, the GFOA recommends that municipalities adopt and maintain a 
comprehensive Capital Improvement Plan (CIP) that specifies project types, identifies the 
responsible departments, defines the scope of each project, estimates associated costs, and outlines 
the proposed funding sources; 
 
WHEREAS, on November 25, 2013, the Governing Body of the City approved Resolution 
No. 5545, which directed the preparation and submission of a five-year Capital Improvement Plan 
for proposed and ongoing capital projects, to be presented to the Governing Body for approval on 
or before January 2, 2015; 
 
WHEREAS, Resolution No. 5545 further encouraged that the CIP be updated annually and 
that such updates be submitted to the Governing Body on or before January 2 of each year 
thereafter; 
 
WHEREAS, maintaining an annual update process has required substantial staff time and 
resources; and 
 
WHEREAS, the current annual CIP update process has not been effectively utilized as a 
decision-making tool for policymakers, and the Governing Body therefore finds it necessary and 
prudent to modify the process to ensure the plan serves as a more effective strategic tool. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that City staff shall prepare and submit to the Governing Body a 
comprehensive five-year Capital Improvement Plan, developed in accordance with the guidelines 
set forth in this Resolution, to serve as a strategic tool for prioritizing and coordinating capital 
investments; and 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that City staff shall update and revise the five-year Capital 
Improvement Plan at least once every three  (3) years, with each updated version to be submitted 
to the Governing Body on or before December 31 of the applicable year, beginning December 31, 
2027.
