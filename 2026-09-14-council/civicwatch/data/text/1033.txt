RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE SUBMISSION OF AN 
APPLICATION BY THE CITY OF CHEYENNE FOR FEDERAL 
FUNDING THROUGH THE TRANSPORTATION ALTERNATIVES 
PROGRAM ADMINISTERED BY THE WYOMING DEPARTMENT OF 
TRANSPORTATION FOR THE PURPOSE OF CONSTRUCTING SAFETY 
AND INTERSECTION GEOMETRIC IMPROVEMENTS FOR THE 
PERSHING BOUL EVARD AND WINDMILL ROAD INTERSECTION 
PROJECT.” 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne desires that the City participate 
in the Transportation Alternatives Program (TAP), administered by the Wyoming Department of 
Transportation, to obtain funding assistance for the construction of safety and intersection 
geometric improvements for the Pershing Boulevard and Windmill Road Intersection Project 
(“Project”);  
 
 WHEREAS, the Governing Body for the City of Cheyenne recognizes the need for the 
Project;  
 
 WHEREAS, TAP requires compliance with applicable federal funding criteria, and the 
City of Cheyenne agrees satisfy all such requirements; 
 
 WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne acknowledges that, if funded, the Project must be 
completed on or before August 30, 2025; 
 
 WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne is requesting a grant in the amount of seven hundred 
thirty thousand dollars ($730,000.00) from TAP for the cost of the construction of Project;  
 
 WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne agrees to provide sixty-nine thousand four hundred 
twenty-three dollars ($69,423.00) from the 5th Penny Sales Tax for the required local match; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne acknowledges TAP is funded on a reimbursement basis, 
requiring that all invoices be paid in full by the City of Cheyenne prior to reimbursement, and 
further acknowledges that failure to comply with this requirement may result in cancellation of the 
award and the repayment of all TAP funds. 
 
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that a funding application requesting seven hundred thirty 
thousand dollars ( $730,000.00) in Total Federal TAP funding be submitted to the Wyoming 
Department of Transportation, Transportation Alternatives Program, for consideration to assist in 
funding for the Project, and that the Mayor and City Clerk are authorized to execute all documents 
associated with the grant application.
