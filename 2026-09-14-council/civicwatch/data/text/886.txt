MEMORANDUM  
TO: Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council 
FROM: TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager 
DATE: September 3, 2025 
SUBJECT: Consideration of Bid #S-1-26 for an Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and  
Construction Specialists , Inc., for the High Plains Arboretum - Headhouse Roofing 
Replacement project 
______________________________________________
Bid #S-1-26 was solicited for the High Plains Arboretum - Headhouse Roofing Replacement project. 
Two (2) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tab ulation is attached.  The Bids were received 
from: 
1. Construction Specialists, Inc
2. Ponderosa Builders LLC
Ponderosa Builders’ bid  has been rejected due to  non-compliance with the requirements set forth in 
the Bidding Documents.  One of the mandatory conditions was that the awarded contractor hold a 
Class A Contractor’s License issued by the City of Cheyenne  Building Department.  At the time of 
the Bid Opening, Ponderosa Builders had not yet taken the required ICC (International Code Council) 
examination, which must be successfully completed prior to applying for a Class A License. Given 
the timelines  involved with testing,  license application,  and review by the  Contractor’s Licensing 
Board, there remains a possibility that Ponderosa Builders would not be  able to obtain the required 
license. Furthermore, the City cannot postpone the project schedule to accommodate the completion 
of these  licensing requirements, as  doing so would  result in an  unreasonably compressed project 
completion window. 
Construction Specialists, Inc. was the next  responsive  bidder, and they are an established Class A 
License holder.  The bids were reviewed by Jason Sanchez (CRE), Sam Payne (CRE), and The Design 
Studio, Inc (TDSi), and their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from Construction 
Specialists, Inc. 
The total Bid Award is $245,700.00.  
Project Completion is scheduled for December 2025. 
Funding: 011-21-2111-45-86971 (WY State Parks and Cultural Resources – Arboretum Grant)
