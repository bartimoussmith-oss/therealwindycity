CODE OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING,  FURTHER

PARKING IN CHEYENNE AND ABROGATING ANY ON-STREET 
PARKING DISTRICT DESIGNATED BY RESOLUTION OF THE 
GOVERNING BODY." 
 
 BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, 
WYOMING: 
 
Section 1.  That Section 10.52.220, Parking Time Limited, of Chapter 10.52, Stopping, 
Standing and Parking Generally , and Section 10.60.010, Signed Time Limitations, of Chapter 
10.60, Parking Limitations, of Title 10, Vehicles and Traffic, of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, 
is hereby amended as set out in this section: 
 
10.52.220 - Parking time limited.  
 
When parking time limitation signs are erected in each block giving notice thereof, no person 
shall park a vehicle for longer than the time specified on posted signs on any day except Sundays 
and public holidays, unless otherwise indicated upon any of the streets within the city as designated 
by the city traffic engineer. Any person who has parked a vehicle in an area where parking is 
limited by time shall before the expiration of that time move the vehicle as required by Section 
10.60.010 of the city code. The requirements imposed under this section shall not apply to paid 
on-street parking districts as provided by Section 10.60.015 of the city code. from the spot in which 
the vehicle was previously parked before the expiration of the time indicated on the posted signs. 
 
10.60.010 - Signed time limitations.  
 
Any person who has parked a vehicle in an area where parking is limited by time shall move 
from the block face of the vehicle's parked position the vehicle from the spot in which the vehicle 
was previously parked before the expiration of the time indicated on the posted signs. This 
requirement applies on any day except Sundays and public holidays or unless otherwise indicated 
upon any of the streets within the city as designated by the city traffic engineer. The requirements 
imposed under this section shall not apply to paid on-street parking districts as provided by Section 
10.60.015 of this chapter. As used in this section, "block face" means the one side of a specific 
city block where signed time limited parking is available. 
 
 Section 2 .  That Section 1 0.60.015, Paid On -Street Parking Districts; Establishment;  
Administration, of Chapter 10.60, Parking Limitations, of Title 10, Vehicles and Traffic, of the  
City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, is hereby repealed. 
 
10.60.015 - Paid on-street parking districts; establishment; administration.

A. There is created the paid on -street parking districts program, which shall be administered 
by the parking division within the Cheyenne police department in accordance with this 
section. The geographic area of any paid on-street parking district administered under this 
program shall be designated by resolution of the governing body. 
 
B. Within each paid on -street parking district as designated by the governing body, the 
parking administration manager for the division shall establish appropriate on -street 
parking zones and reasonable on -street parking rates. The purpose for establishing an y 
parking zone or parking rate shall be to positively influence parking behavior, prioritizing 
customer and visitor parking while emphasizing off-street options for longer-term parkers, 
such business employees and city residents. When establishing any p arking zone or any 
parking rate under this subsection, the parking administration manager shall take the 
following factors into consideration: 
 
1. The types of activities or businesses to be encompassed within the proposed zone and 
whether the proposed parking rate will support the efficient use of the available on -
street parking; 
 
2. Whether dynamic parking management is appropriate for a proposed zone in order to 
manage demand for on-street parking through variable parking rates and dissemination 
of real-time parking availability information; and 
 
3. Other parking conditions, such as availability and usage trends, for the purpose of 
tailoring the paid on-street parking districts program to better match the specific needs 
and dynamics of a parking zone and between zones to optimize parking management 
and resource utilization. 
 
C. Before any parking zone or parking rate is established under subsection (B) of this section, 
and before any parking zone or parking rate may be modified by the parking administration 
manager, the division shall provide public notice of the proposed parking  zone or parking 
rate, or modification thereof, by means of publication of the notice on not less than one (1) 
City of Cheyenne media platform. In addition, the notice required under this subsection 
shall be provided for a period of not less than thirty (30) days before the proposed parking 
zone or parking rate establishment or modification is set to take effect and shall seek to 
solicit public input on the proposal. 
 
D. Every parking zone established under this section shall include appropriate signage 
communicating the type of zone and the authorized payment methods as provided by the 
division. Authorized payment methods may include payments through electronic means 
including using webpages, smartphone applications or payment kiosks. The division shall 
require all persons wanting to park a vehicle within a paid on-street parking district to first 
register for a user account for the purposes of program administration and securing on -
street parking payments. 
 
E. Information regarding every current parking district and parking zone, along with all 
attendant parking rates, shall be made publicly available by the division. The information

required under this subsection shall include maps for any parking district and parking zone 
established under this section and shall be publicly accessible on a City of Cheyenne media 
platform. 
 
F. It shall constitute a violation of this section, punishable by a civil fine as provided by 
Section 10.60.040 of this chapter, for any person to park a vehicle in a designated parking 
location within a paid on-street parking district: 
 
1. Without first registering for a user account as required under subsection (D) of this 
section; or 
 
2. Having registered for a user account, and parked in excess of the time authorized for 
the person to park the vehicle as provided by the paid parking apparatus or time 
monitoring platform regulating vehicle parking is the particular parking zone. 
 
G. As used in this section: 
 
1. "Division" means the parking division within the Cheyenne police department; 
 
2. "On-street parking rate" or "parking rate" means the monetary charge levied by the City 
of Cheyenne for the privilege of parking a vehicle for a specified duration in a 
designated parking space within any paid on-street parking district; 
 
3. "On-street parking zone" or "parking zone" means the geographical area located within 
a parking district that is specified in accordance with this section, which exhibits 
consistent usage characteristics that allow for the assessment of parking patterns an d 
determination of parking rates based on parking demand; and 
 
4. "Paid on -street parking district" or "parking district" means the geographical area 
specified by resolution of the governing body for the implementation of paid on-street 
parking as provided by this section. 
 
Section 3.  Any paid on -street parking district designated by resolution of the governing 
body in accordance with the authority granted under Section 10.60.015 of the city code, including 
Resolution No.6441, adopted by the governing body on November 25, 2024 , which authority is  
hereby repealed by section 2 of this ordinance, shall be deemed void and otherwise considered 
repealed upon the effective provided herein. 
 
Section 4.  Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases of this ordinance are 
severable. If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase  of this ordinance is declared 
unconstitutional or otherwise unlawful, such declaration shall not affect any of the remaining 
sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases. 
 
 Section 5.  This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon approval and publication.
