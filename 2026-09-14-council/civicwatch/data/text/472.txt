Background:  
 
Attached is the proposed budget for fiscal year 2027 (July 1 to June 30). The proposed Governing Body 
review schedule is as follows: 
 Introduction and Referral: April 27, 2026 
 Finance Committee: May 5, 2026 
 Final consideration: May 11, 2026 
 
The budget represents a $14,610.00 increase in mill levy assessment collections ($501,610.00) and a 
decrease in interest earned (-$27,759.15). The interest earned estimate is based on an estimated 
$625,000 dollars being left in the Wyoming Government Investment Fund (WGIF) which as of April 7, 
2026, has a seven-day yield of 3.56% (versus $1 million in the WGIF account during FY2026). 
 
The proposed budget of $1,022,800 is $196,526 more than fiscal year 2026 (per the final FY26 budget 
increase in April 2026), brings in $434,190 from reserves, accounts for a $30,000 grant from the 
Cheyenne-Laramie Co. Economic Development Joint Powers Board, and $10,000 in funding from 
Laramie County. The City contributes approximately $290,900 covering all payroll, insurance, and 
benefits for employees. Additionally, other City staff provide support to DDA employees at no cost to 
the DDA. The FY2027 Proposed Budget includes costs associated with an independent office outside of 
the City Building, along with associated expenses. The City has provided physical office space and all 
things commonly associated with office space at no cost to the DDA board since March 2023.

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE BUDGET OF THE CHEYENNE 
DOWNTOWN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR 
BEGINNING JULY 1, 2026, AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2027." 
 
WHEREAS, the Downtown Development Authority ("DDA") was created pursuant to 
Ordinance No. 2804 adopted by the Governing Body on July 9, 1984; 
 
WHEREAS, Wyo. Stat.§ 15-9-208(a) requires the DDA to propose a budget, which shall 
be administratively reviewed by the City prior to submission of the proposed budget to the 
Governing Body for approval; 
 
WHEREAS, Section 2.76.050(C) of the city code provides that the DDA budget shall be 
subject to alteration, modification and approval by the Governing Body in accordance with the 
policies of the City; 
 
WHEREAS, at its April 23, 2026, meeting the DDA Board approved the proposed budget 
for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2026, and ending June 30, 2027, a copy of which is appended 
to this Resolution and incorporated herein; and 
 
WHEREAS, City staff has reviewed the proposed budget received from the DDA. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the proposed budget of the Downtown Development 
Authority for the Fiscal Year beginning July 1, 2026, and ending June 30, 2027, as set forth in the 
attached information, entitled "Downtown Development Authority Proposed Budget - Fiscal Year 
2027" in the total amount of  one million twenty-two thousand eight  hundred dollars 
($1,022,800.00), is hereby approved.
