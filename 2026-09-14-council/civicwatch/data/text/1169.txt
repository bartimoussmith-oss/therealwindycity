RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION ADOPTING A REVISED CHEYENNE -LARAMIE 
COUNTY BOARD OF HEALTH FEE SCHEDULE FOR VARIOUS 
SERVICES AND INSPECTIONS." 
 
WHEREAS, Wyo. Stat. § 35-1-305(a) authorizes local boards of health to fix and charge 
reasonable fees for services; 
 
WHEREAS, the Cheyenne-Laramie County Board of Health currently provides inspection 
services including for food service operations, body art, childcare facilities, campgrounds, small 
wastewater systems, well and septic systems, swimming pools/spas, and massage establishments 
and therapists; 
 
WHEREAS, the Cheyenne-Laramie County Board of Health seeks to amend its health fee 
schedule for inspection services, permit fees, and other various services as set forth in Exhibit A , 
attached to this resolution and incorporated by reference herein; and 
 
WHEREAS, prior to the fees being assessed, the health fee schedule must be approved by 
the City of Cheyenne and Laramie County Board of Commissioners for its respective jurisdictions. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the revised health fee schedule for inspection services, permit 
fees, and other various services provided by the Cheyenne-Laramie County Board of Health, which 
health fee schedule is set forth in Exhibit A, is hereby approved and any conflicting fees adopted 
by prior resolutions are superseded.
