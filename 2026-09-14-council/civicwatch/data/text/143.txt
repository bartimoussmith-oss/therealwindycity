BACKGROUND: 
 
This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from AG Agricultural to BP Business Park for 
roughly 387 acres of land. The land is currently vacant or used for agricultural purposes. The request to BP 
Business Park is for the purpose of data center development per the statement of the agent of the applicant. To 
zone land BP, other potential uses of BP zoned land should also be considered. The application has been 
submitted concurrently with a proposed future land use map amendment to change the subject property 
to Mixed-use Employment. Staff’s discussion and recommendation on the proposed future land use map 
amendment are found in that application’s staff report.

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical 
patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1) 
 
The property is currently designated Urban Residential, Mixed-use Residential, Mixed-use 
Commercial, Community Business and Mixed-use Employment by the Comprehensive Plan, but a 
comprehensive plan amendment has been submitted to change the designation of the entire 
property to Mixed-use Employment. 
 
The current designations anticipate development that is no more than a quarter industrial, and a 
majority residential. As the BP zone encourages industrial uses and practically prohibits residential 
uses, the BP zoning would not be in accordance with the comprehensive plan for this area. Th e 
Mixed-use Employment designation anticipates a mix of industrial and commercial uses, and if 
the entire property were designated this way the BP zone would be appropriate.  
 
Support for this application in policies in PlanCheyenne also hinge on the future land use map 
amendment proposal. For example, policies within PlanCheyenne encourage a balanced mix of 
uses in the Cheyenne region using the future land use map as a guide (1.1.A), the flexibility to 
respond to market demands (1.1.B), meeting community housing needs (2.5.A), dispersing 
housing throughout the community (2.5.B), a business-supportive environment (3.1.C), and 
providing opportunities for a range of industrial uses (3.5.A). 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property does not comply with review criterion 1 
under the current future land use map designations but would comply if the concurrent 
application for future land use map designation change is approved. 
 
2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering 
existing zoning, the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies 
created under the guidance of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2) 
 
Nearby property is zoned LI in the County to the northwest. Property directly north is zone AG 
Agriculture. County LU zoning exists to the east. To the south is the Sweetgrass PUD and to the 
southwest is the LCCC PUD in the County.  
 
The County LU zone allows for a wide variety of uses, including light industrial uses, but such 
industry would need conditional use approval in the LU zone. The Sweetgrass PUD allows for a 
variety of uses, but most of the area adjacent to this parcel is slated for residential use by that 
PUD. The AG zone is part of a floodplain and largely used for agricultural purposes.  
 
The proposed LI zoning is largely compatible with the current surrounding zoning.  
 
Nearby Future Land Use designations include Mixed-use Employment and Agriculture/Rural to 
the north, Urban Residential to the south and east, and Community Business and Public Quasi 
Public along College Drive. The property is roughly 50% surrounded by Urban Residential. Based

on the future land use plans for the area, BP zoning for the entire parcel would not be considered 
compatible with surrounding properties. However, the subject property includes both Mixed-use 
Employment and Urban Residential, should the future land use map be amended, extending the 
border between the two designations further south and east, than BP could be considered 
appropriate for the entire parcel. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property does not comply with review criterion 2 
under the current future land use map designations but would comply if the concurrent 
application for future land use map designation change is approved. 
   
3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards 
and uses of the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject 
property for development according to the standards and uses of the proposed 
zoning district (UDC 2.2.1.d.3) 
  
The property is currently zoned AG Agricultural. The AG zone is intended as a holding zone 
pending more specific development actions. Most urban-style zones will allow a wider variety of 
uses than the current zone district. The proposed BP zone has limited design standards and allows 
a wide variety of uses. 
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3. 
 
4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed 
according to the Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed 
zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4) 
 
Future development in the area will be constrained by Crow Creek and Allison Draw. The large 
acreages allows for a variety of street network possibilities. The BP zone allows more flexibility in 
street network than some of the more dense residential or commercial zones.  
 
Major streets are designated on the future land use plan running through and around this 
property. Future development will be expected to provide for any major streets planned in this 
area. 
 
No changes to the current street network are proposed. Accesses will not be changed with the 
zone change. Infrastructure (streets and utilities) exist in the area. Depending on the proposed 
use, traffic impacts could be significant.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4. 
 
5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of 
the proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific 
standards. (UDC 2.2.1.d.5) 
 
The UDC indicates that the BP zone is intended for small and large-scale industrial development 
while providing essential commercial services along regional transportation routes in an industrial 
park setting, and applicable to areas on the edge of the community and contain not less than 320 
PUDC-26-121   June 1, 2026 
5 | P a g e  
 
acres of land. The areas zoned BP should be designated Community Business, Mixed-Use 
Employment, or and Industrial. 
 
The subject property is over 320 acres and near regional transportation routes. It is in an area 
where the developed portions of the community transition to open land. However, a majority of 
land within the parcel is designated for future residential development. If the area is changed to 
be designated ‘Mixed Use Employment’, the BP zoning is fully compatible.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property does not comply with review criterion 5 
under the current future land use map designations but would comply if the concurrent 
application for future land use map designation change is approved. 
    
6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for 
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options 
if any specific development proposed in association with the application is not 
executed after approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6) 
 
The specific development of the site as proposed by the applicant is a data center. However, 
should the data center use not be executed, the site is in close proximity to I-80 and other 
industrial or commercial uses would be logical on at least part of the site. For example, a large 
truck stop, hotels, or warehouses may find this location appropriate.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6. 
 
7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7) 
 
No major comments were received in regard to the proposed zone change that have not been 
covered under other review criteria.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7. 
 
8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare. 
(UDC 2.2.1.d.8) 
 
Cheyenne has had documented housing challenges and taking land anticipated for majority 
residential development and zoning that land into a Business Park would adversely affect the 
general welfare of the community. However, if the future land use designation changes and the 
land is no longer anticipated for residential development, City staff is satisfied that this proposed 
zone change would not adversely affect the public health, safety, or general welfare.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property does not comply with review criterion 8 
under the current future land use map designation but would comply if the concurrent 
application for future land use map designation change is approved.

STAFF REVIEW: 
 
Through the review process, review comments were received expressing concerns about the 
appropriateness of the BP zone at this location. 
 
CONCLUSION: 
 
The application has been submitted concurrently with a proposed future land use map amendment to 
change the subject property to Mixed-use Employment. Staff’s conclusion on the proposal to zone land 
BP Business Park hinges on the appropriateness of the associated future land use map amendment 
proposal. Should the future land use map amendment be approved, the extension of ‘mixed -use 
employment’-style development would be considered appropriate and the application would meet all 
review criteria. On the other hand, if the future land use map amendment is denied, this request would fail 
to meet review criteria 1, 2, 5, and 8. 
 
Staff’s discussion and recommendation on the proposed future land use map amendment are found in 
that application’s staff report.
