To: Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council 
From: Connor White, Planner II 
Subject: County Pocket Annexation #6   
Date: July 14, 2025 
 
Background:  
 
The item for consideration is an Ordinance. Annexation of County pockets is a 2022 City Council 
priority.  A “County pocket” is an area of unincorporated land 100% surrounded by the City of 
Cheyenne.  A related “Assigned Zoning” pursuant to Unified Development Code (UDC) Section 2.2.8 
ordinance is also on the agenda.    
 
The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as follows: 
• Introduction and Referral  July 14, 2025 
• Public Services Committee  July 21, 2025 
• Second reading    July 28, 2025 
• Public Services Committee  August 4, 2025 
• Third and final reading   August 11, 2025 
 
This annexation is comprised of 38 properties 100% surrounded by the City (See attached map). Of 
the 38 parcels, a majority are developed with residential uses, several are developed with commercial 
uses and others are undeveloped or incorporated into adjacent parcels under similar ownership. The 
City has maintained contact with the County Planning Office to coordinate any pending permits or 
development reviews.  Currently there are no active permits or development specific to the 
properties proposed for annexation.   
 
On April 21, 2025, City staff mailed a courtesy notice by first class mail to all landowners included in 
this annexation area inviting them to a “Question and Answer” on Monday May 5, 2025. Discussion, 
comments, and questions primarily surrounded the annexation component and public improvements. 
During the meeting staff provided information regarding trash collection, water and sewer services, 
continuation of non-conforming uses and what to expect with the annexation.  
 
The existing use of any property annexed, including any livestock use, will be allowed to continue as is 
as a nonconforming use.  Nonconforming uses may continue until they are abandoned for a period of 
12-months. Residents within pockets have been encouraged to complete a Land Use Documentation 
form detailing the existing use of the land for documentation purposes.  At the time of drafting of this 
memo, one property owner within the area has completed a land use documentation form. Providing

City-initiated annexations do not require an owner’s signature. To initiate an annexation, the City must 
adopt a resolution certifying that the annexation complies with Wyoming State Statute § 15-1-402. After 
the City adopts this resolution, the annexation follows the typical schedule, including a public hearing and 
the approval of an Ordinance which annexes the land. The Governing Body adopted Resolution No. 6469 
initiating this annexation on May 27, 2025. 
 
As with any annexation, City services will be extended to service the land contained in the annexation. City 
utilities are already located in the vicinity. Police, fire, and sanitation services will be provided.  As several 
parcels of land contained in this annexation area are developed, City sanitation service will need to be 
established and landowners have been notified of this required change.  Infrastructure improvements 
required related to the annexation are minimal. Future development such as subdivision or new 
development will require on- and off-site infrastructure improvements. Existing infrastructure will remain 
as is upon annexation.  
 
The areas proposed for annexation are adjacent to land under City jurisdiction. Development in this area is 
a natural extension of the City. 100 % of the perimeter of the annexation area is contiguous to City limits.   
 
Due to meeting the review criteria found in Wyoming Statute § 15-1-402 and the annexation conforming 
with the 2022 City Council goal to annex County pockets, staff recommends approval of this annexation. 
 
Staff Recommendation: 
 
Staff recommends the Governing Body approve the Annexation Ordinance.

APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS: 
 
• UDC 2.4.2 Annexations 
• PlanCheyenne 
 
BACKGROUND: 
 
Annexation of County pockets is a 2022 City Council priority.  A “County pocket” is an area of unincorporated 
land 100% surrounded by the City of Cheyenne. City-initiated annexations do not require an owner’s 
signature. To initiate an annexation, the City must adopt a resolution certifying that the annexation 
complies with Wyoming State Statute § 15-1-402. After the City adopts this resolution, the annexation 
follows the typical schedule, including a public hearing and the approval of an Ordinance which annexes 
the land. The Governing Body adopted Resolution No. 6469 initiating this annexation on May 27, 2025. 
 
This annexation is comprised of 38 properties 100% surrounded by the City (See attached map). Of 
the 38 parcels, a majority are developed with residential uses, several are developed with 
commercial uses and others are undeveloped or incorporated into adjacent parcels under similar 
ownership. 
 
Land annexed to the City requires a City zone. An assigned zoning ordinance is accompanying this annexation 
to assign the AR, MR, HR, CB, and MUR zones.  
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
To approve the annexation Ordinance, the Governing Body must make the following findings required in 
WS 15-1-402; a detailed analysis of which is below: 
 
1) An annexation of the area is for the protection of the health, safety and welfare of the 
persons residing in the area and in the city or town.  [WS 15-1-402 (a) (i)] 
 
The annexation of these properties will increase the health, safety, and welfare of the future 
occupants. By being in City limits, the property will receive access to City Fire and Police and 
sanitation services. The properties will also have the option of connecting to City water and 
sanitary sewer instead of having wells and septic systems.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion. 
 
2) The urban development of the area sought to be annexed would constitute a natural, 
geographical, economical and social part of the annexing city or town. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (ii)] 
 
These properties constitute a natural, geographic, economic, and social part of the city. The 
properties are surrounded by City on all sides. The contiguity to the City and the proximity to 
surrounding urban-style development makes the parcels a natural geographic part of the City.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion. 
 
   
3) The area sought to be annexed is a logical and feasible addition to the annexing city or town 
and the extension of basic and other services customarily available to residents of the city or 
PUDC-25-70   July 14, 2025 
town shall, within reason, be available to the area proposed to be annexed. [WS 15-1-402 (a) 
(iii)] 
 
The area is a logical and feasible addition to the City due to the reasons previously listed. All City 
services normally available to residents will also be made available. There are properties that 
are already connected to City sewer within the area to be annexed. Police and fire protection 
will be extended upon annexation. Utilities exist in the area and will be expanded into the 
proposed area as they redevelop.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion. 
 
4) The area sought to be annexed is contiguous with or adjacent to the annexing city or town, or 
the area meets the requirements of W.S. 15-1-407. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (iv)] 
 
The area proposed for annexation is 100% contiguous on all sides.   
 
The application complies with this review criterion. 
 
5) If the city or town does not own or operate its own electric utility, its governing body is 
prepared to issue one (1) or more franchises as necessary to serve the annexed area pursuant 
to W.S. 15-1-410. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (v)] 
 
Utility franchises are in place to serve the annexed area.   
 
The application complies with this review criterion. 
    
6) The annexing city or town, not less than twenty (20) business days prior to the public hearing 
required by W.S. 15-1-405(a), has sent by certified mail to all landowners and affected public 
utilities within the territory and by first class mail to any persons owning property that is 
adjacent to or within three hundred (300) feet of the territory proposed to be annexed, 
regardless of whether the property is inside or outside the corporate limits of the annexing 
city or town and regardless of whether the city or town is exercising authority under W.S. 15-
3-202(b)(ii), a summary of the proposed annexation report as required under subsection (c) of 
this section and notice of the time, date and location of the public hearing required by W.S. 
15-1-405(a). [WS 15-1-402 (a) (vi)] 
 
A certified mailing was made to the landowners and public utilities on June 6, 2025. Neighbors 
within 300’ were mailed first-class letters on June 4 and 5. The public hearing for this annexation 
is scheduled for July 14, 2025. Twenty (20) business days (on the City’s calendar) prior to the 
announced public hearing is June 12, 2025. The earliest confirmation of delivery was dated June 
9, 2025. 
 
The application complies with this review criterion.

CONFORMANCE WITH APPLICABLE PLANS: 
 
The project is located in an area covered by the following plans: 
• Plan Cheyenne 
 
The Future Land Use Map of PlanCheyenne, as updated by Connect 2045, anticipates this area of the 
community to develop with urban residential uses and a little bit of Mixed-use Commercial uses. These 
areas are expected to develop with full access to urban services – such areas are best served by being 
within City limits. The area is within the Urban Service Boundary. 
 
STAFF REVIEW: 
 
Through the review process, no comments were received. 
 
CONCLUSION: 
 
Staff is satisfied that the application meets the review criteria and therefore recommends approval of 
the annexation.   
 
The proposed annexation is a natural extension of the City, is contiguous to the current City limits, and 
most services typically available to urban areas are available to be extended to this property.  
 
STAFF RECOMMENDATION: 
 
Staff recommends approval of the annexation.
