LEASE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING 
AND UNION TELEPHONE COMPANY 
 
1. Parties. This Lease is made and entered into this 1st day of January, 2025, by and 
between the City of Cheyenne ("City"), a municipal corporation organized and existing under the 
laws of Wyoming, whose address is 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001, hereinafter 
referred to as the "Lessor," and Union Telephone Company, a Wyoming corporation, whose 
address is P.O. Box 160, Mountain View, Wyoming 82939, hereinafter referred to as "Lessee." 
 
2. Purpose. The Purpose of this Lease is to allow Lessee to utilize the Parks Division 
Maintenance Shop, located at 4500 Dell Range Boulevard, Cheyenne, Wyoming, as more 
particularly described in Attachment A , for the purpose of maintaining a roof mount 
communications system, as described in Attachment B  (the “Premises”), to be used by Lessee to 
conduct telecommunications operations. Attachments A  and B are attached hereto, incorporated 
by reference and expressly made a part of this Lease. 
 
3. Term. The term of the Lease shall run from January 1, 2025 , through December 
31, 2029.  This Lease may be renewed for up to four (4) successive five (5) year terms, contingent 
upon Lessee's agreement to increase the annual rent by fifteen percent ( 15%) for the renewal 
period. Lessee shall furnish to Lessor, no later than sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the 
lease term, written notice of its intent to renew or terminate the Lease. 
 
4. Payment of Rent. Lessor and Lessee acknowledge and agree that a rental payment 
in the amount of Seventeen Thousand Eight Hundred Sixty Dollars and Fifty Cents ($17,860.50)  
was made and received on or about January 17, 2025.  Upon acceptance and full execution of this 
Lease Agreement, Lessee shall make a one-time payment to Lessor in the amount of One Thousand 
One Hundred Thirty-Nine Dollars and Fifty Cents ($1,139.50) as the balance of Rent due for 2025. 
On or before January 1, 2026, and in advance of the anniversary date of this Lease each year 
thereafter, Lessee shall pay to Lessor the sum of Nineteen Thousand Dollars ($19,000.00) as 
annual Rent for this Lease, increased each term in accordance with Paragraph 3 above. 
 
5. Responsibilities of the Lessor . Lessor agrees that it will allow the Lessee use of 
the Premises for the limited purposes set forth in this lease. 
 
6. Responsibilities of the Lessee. Lessee agrees that it shall: 
 
a. Use the Premises only for the express purpose of maintaining and operating 
a roof mount communications system  in strict compliance with the terms set forth in this 
lease. All other uses are expressly prohibited. 
 
b. Operate and maintain the roof mount communications system  on the 
Premises in a condition satisfactory to Lessor.

c. Maintain the Premises in a condition satisfactory to Lessor. 
 
d. Be responsible for  any and all utility construction and service charges in 
relation to said roof mount communications system. 
 
e. Erect no structures, fences, buildings, or structures of any type without the 
prior express written consent of Lessor. 
 
f. Observe all federal, state, and local laws, regulations, rules and ordinances 
in exercising any rights under this lease, including but not limited to compliance with all 
federal permitting requirements. 
 
g. Permit no pollution of the air, water or earth in exercising any rights under  
this Lease. 
 
h. Permit no waste of the Premises. 
 
i. Not encumber the Premises, or allow the Premises to be encumbered, in any 
manner due to Lessee's activities. 
 
j. Not interfere with the radio signals of Lessor in any manner. 
 
k. Upon completion of construction and upon the termination of this Lease or 
any renewal thereof, restore the demised Premises in a manner which is satisfactory to 
Lessor. 
 
7. Co-Location Provisions. 
 
a. Third-Party Co-Location Not Permitted without Lesso rs Express Written 
Consent. Lessee expressly agrees and understands that it may not co -locate, sublease, 
assign or othe rwise permit installation, attachment or placement of any equipment not 
expressly approved in this Lease on the roof mount communications system  without 
Lessor's express written consent; provided however, that Lessee may co-locate equipment 
owned or operated solely by its subsidiaries without Lessor's express written consent. 
 
b. No Conflict with Lessor Operations. Lessee expressly agrees that it, and its 
sub-lessees, shall conduct their operations in a manner to avoid any conflict with current 
and future Lessor operations. In the event of any conflict, Lessee, and its sub -lessees, 
expressly agree to modify their operations and procedures upon demand of the Lessor to 
accommodate Lessor’s reasonable needs. In the event Lessee is required to modify its 
operations or procedures from those in effect at the date of execution of this Lease as a 
result of changes in Lessor’s operations, relocation of communications system equipment 
shall be at the expense of Lessor.

8. Special Provisions. 
 
a. Insurance Required.  Lessee must provide proof of the following insurance 
coverages: 
 
i. COMMERCIAL GENERAL LIABILITY INSURANCE.  
For claims arising out of bodily injury, illness or death, or from damage to 
or destruction of property of others, including loss or use thereof, with 
minimum limits of $1,000,000 per occurrence, $2,000,000 aggregate for the 
entire term of the Lease. Such insurance shall include, but not be limited to 
coverage for any damage to Lessor’s property, operations, tower, 
equipment, supplies or materials, and any damages sustained by any third 
persons as a result of Lessee’s operations, tower and related equi pment.  
Lessee shall maintain such insurance coverage in effect during the initial 
term of this lease, and any renewal thereof.  
 
ii. BUSINESS AUTOMOBILE INSURANCE.  Including 
owned, non -owned and hired vehicles with minimum limits for bodily 
injury and property damage of $1,000,000 per accident for the entire term 
of the Lease. 
 
iii. WORKERS' COMPENSATION.  Workers' Compensation 
coverage shall be in effect for the entire term of the Lease, as required by 
Wyoming law, for all employees or agents providing services under this 
Lease. Lessee shall provide Lessor with proof of workers' compensation or 
employer's liability insurance coverage. 
 
iv. CRIME INSURANCE.  In an amount not less than $500,000 
per occurrence. 
 
v. ADDITIONAL INSURANCE INFORMATION.  If 
insurance coverage required under this agreement is provided pursuant to a 
"claims made" policy, Lessee shall, concurrently with the execution of this 
Lease, provide the Lessor with a certificate of insurance demonstrating that 
such coverage is or shall be in effect on or before the effective date of this 
Lease. Lessee shall maintain said "claims made" coverage for a period of 
five (5) years following the last date that Lessee has operated under this 
Lease. In the event Lessee or the insurer terminates "claims made" coverage 
prior to the expiration of the periods provided in this paragraph, Lessee shall 
provide to Lessor advance written notification of the termination of said 
coverage and shall provide Lessor with an endorsement for an extended 
reporting period ("tail coverage") which shall be in effect f or a period of 
time not less than five (5) years following the last date that Lessee operated 
under this Lease. 
 
In the event this Lease is extended into a future period, the Lessee shall,

of insurance demonstrating that such coverage is or shall be in effect during

Any insurance company providing coverage under this Lease shall

b. Facility Ownership . It is expressly agreed and understood that legal and 
equitable title to any improvements placed upon the property by Lessee under this lease, 
including but not limited to the roof mount communications system and any and all other 
related equipment, material, goods or supplies, shall at all times be in the name of Lessee. 
 
c. Facility Management. It is expressly agreed and understood that Lessee's 
facility, including but not limited to the roof mount communications system, and any and 
all other related equipment, material, goods or supplies, shall at all times be managed by 
Lessee and that this duty shall not be delegated, without Lessor's written consent. 
 
9. General Provisions. 
 
a. Americans with Disabilities Act . Lessee shall not discriminate against a 
qualified individual with a disability and shall comply with the Americans with Disabilities 
Act, P.L. 101-336, 42 U.S.C. § 12101, et seq., and any properly promulgated rules and 
regulations related thereto.

b. Amendments. No changes, modifications, revisions or amendments to this 
Lease may be made or shall be valid in the absence of the execution of written agreement 
by the parties hereto. No oral amendment or modification of this Lease shall be valid or 
have any force or effect. 
 
c. Applicable Law/Venue. The construction, interpretation and enforcement 
of this Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming. The courts of the 
First Judicial District, Laramie County, Wyoming shall have exclusive jurisdiction over 
any disputes arising under this Lease and any actions relating to the interpretation or 
enforcement of this Lease shall be brought only in the First Judicial District. 
 
d. Assignment. This Lease may not be assigned to any other party without the 
express written consent of Lessor. Any unauthorized effort to assign this lease shall result 
in its immediate termination. 
 
e. Assignment Upon Default to Lender. With Lessor's express written consent, 
this Lease may be pledged as collateral in connection with any financing obtained by 
Lessee to fund capital improvements or operations. Lessor reserves the right to refuse to 
approve the assignment of this Lease in the event the proposed assignee refuses to assume 
the Lessee's duties to indemnify the City of Cheyenne and Lessor as provided for such in 
this Lease. 
 
f. Compliance with Law . Lessee, its agents, employees and officers shall 
become informed of and comply with all applicable federal, state and local laws, rules and 
regulations in the performance of this Lease. 
 
g. Governmental Immunity. By entering into this Lease, the City of Cheyenne 
and the Lessor do not waive governmental immunity, and each specifically retains all 
immunities and all defenses available to them as governmental entities pursuant to Wyo. 
Stat. § 1 -39-101 et seq. and pursuant to any other state or federal law which provides 
immunity to the City of Cheyenne or the Lessor. 
 
h. Titles Not Controlling. Titles of paragraphs are for reference only and shall 
not be used to construe the language in this Lease. 
 
i. Waiver. The waiver of any breach of any term or condition in this Lease 
shall not be deemed a waiver of any prior or subsequent breach. 
 
j. Indemnity. Lessee agrees to indemnify, hold harmless and defend Lessor 
from and against any and all liabilities, claims, penalties, forfeitures and suits, and the cost 
and expenses incident thereto, including reasonable attorney's fees, which may hereafter 
arise as a result of death or bodily injury to any person, destruction or damage to any 
property, contamination of or adverse effects on the environment, or any violation of 
governmental laws, regulations or orders to the extent caused by (1) Lessee's breach of any 
term or provision of this Lease; or (2) any negligent or wrongful act, error or omission by 
Lessee, or its agents, employees, sub-lessees, or subcontractors arising in any manner out

of Lessee's use of the leased property or this Lease. Lessee acknowledges that it may incur 
a financial obligation to Lessor pursuant to the terms of this paragraph. 
 
k. Termination. Lessor may terminate this Lease for: (i) breach of its terms 
and conditions; (ii) for abandonment of Lessee's obligations under this Lease; and, (iii) for 
such other reasons as necessary to serve the public interest, by providing Lessee with thirty 
(30) days advance written notification. 
 
i. Upon termination due to breach or abandonment, Lessee agrees to 
remove the roof-mount communications system and any related materials and 
equipment from the leased premises at its own expense within a time determined to 
be reasonable by the Parks & Recreation Department . In such case, Lessee shall 
restore the site as nearly as reasonably possible to its original condition. 
 
ii. In the event of termination in the public interest, the Lessee shall be 
compensated based upon the initial cost of improvements located on the lease, less 
depreciation as allocated over the life of the improvements as evidenced by the 
Lessee's Federal Tax amortization schedule.
