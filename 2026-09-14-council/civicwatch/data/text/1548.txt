RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION APPROVING A SPONSORSHIP BY BISON 
BEVERAGE FOR CHEYENNE FRONTIER DAYS EVENTS OCCURRING 
JULY 1 7 THROUGH JULY 2 6, 202 6, JULY 23 THROUGH AUGUST 1 , 
2027, AND JULY 21 THROUGH JULY 30, 2028.” 
 
 WHEREAS, Cheyenne Frontier Days ("CFD") relies heavily on sponsorships to assist in 
producing the yearly Cheyenne Frontier Days rodeo events and has established various levels of 
sponsor relationships based on value , including Platinum Arrowhead, Titanium, Bronze 
Arrowhead, Gold Boot, Chute 9 Sponsor, Silver Boot, and Copper Boot sponsors; 
 
 WHEREAS, Bison Beverage is a local malt beverage industry representative for Molson 
Coors Beverage Company brands Miller and Coors and  is interested in establishing a three-year 
sponsorship relationship with CFD; 
 
 WHEREAS, the activities of alcohol industry representatives are strictly regulated under 
Title 12 of Wyoming Statute; 
 
 WHEREAS, Wyoming Statute § 12-5-402(a) provides in part that "industry representatives 
shall not furnish, give or lend money or other things of value, directly or indirectly, to any person 
engaged in selling products of the industry at retail under privileges of a license or permit to sell 
any beverage or liquor in W yoming." The  statute further provides that this  "prohibition shall 
extend to the giving to any person for the use, benefit or relief of the person engaged in selling the 
industry's products and to prohibit guaranty by the industry of a loan or other financial obligation 
of persons engaged at retail in selling these products."; 
 
 WHEREAS, Wyoming Statute § 12-5-402(a) also outlines an exception to the prohibition 
imposed upon industry representatives , providing that " with prior approval of the applicable 
licensing authority, malt beverage industry representatives may furnish, give or lend money or 
other things of value to a licensee to support annual community events open to the public if the 
licensee: (i) Is a nonprofit corporation organized under the laws of this state;  (ii) Is qualified as a 
tax exempt organization under the Internal Revenue Code; and  (iii) Has been in continuous 
operation for a period of not less than two (2) years."; 
 
 WHEREAS, Cheyenne Frontier Days holds a retail liquor license, which license the City 
of Cheyenne  granted as the local licensing authority  in accordance with Wyoming Statute 
§ 12-4-201; 
 
WHEREAS, in accordance with Wyoming law, CFD and Bison Beverage are seeking prior 
approval from the City of Cheyenne to enter into a three-year sponsorship relationship under which 
Bison Beverage may furnish, give or lend money or other things of value to C FD to support the 
annual community event of Cheyenne Frontier Days, occurring on July 17 through July 26, 2026, 
July 23 through August 1, 2027 and July 21 through July 30, 2028; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the Govern ing body finds the statutory requirements articulated under 
Wyoming Statute § 12 -5-402(a) are satisfied in this instance as CFD is a liquor licensee, is a 
nonprofit corporation organized under the laws of the State of Wyoming, is qualified as a tax  
exempt organization under the Internal Revenue Code, and has been in continuous operation for a 
period of not less than two (2) years. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the sponsorship relationship between C FD and Bison 
Beverage for the Cheyenne Frontier Days events, scheduled to occur on July 17 through July 26, 
2026, July 23 through August 1, 2027 and July 21 through July 30, 2028, is hereby approved.
