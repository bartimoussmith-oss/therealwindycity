ENTITLED: “AN ORDINANCE AMENDING AND UPDATING THE SOLID WASTE 
FEE SCHEDULE IN ACCORDANCE WITH SECTION 8.44.010 OF THE 
MUNICIPAL CODE OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, AND 
REPEALING ORDINANCE NO. 4621.” 
 
 BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, 
WYOMING: 
 
Section 1.  That Cheyenne City Ordinance No. 4621, adopted by the governing body on 
May 12, 2025, authorized the fee schedule currently applicable to solid waste services under Title 
8, Chapter 8.44, of the Cheyenne City Code. 
 
Section 2.  That, in accordance with Section 8.44.010 of the Municipal Code of the City of 
Cheyenne, Wyoming, the Public Works Director is required to present the solid waste fee schedule 
to the City Council for approval, and the governing body hereby approves and adopts the updated 
Solid Waste Fee Schedule attached hereto as Exhibit A and incorporated herein by reference. 
 
Section 3.  That the Solid Waste Fee Schedule adopted by this ordinance shall become 
effective on July 1, 2026. 
 
Section 4.  That effective July 1, 2026, the solid waste fee schedule previously adopted by 
Ordinance No. 4621 is hereby repealed. 
 
Section 5.  Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases of this ordinance are 
severable. If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase of this ordinance is declared 
unconstitutional or otherwise unlawful, such declaration shall not affect any of the remaining 
sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases. 
 
Section 6.  This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon approval and publication.

Note 1: The freon removal fee must be paid in addition to disposal fee for all refrigerators, freezers, and 
air conditioners unless freon removal has been certified by a licensed repairman.  
Note 2: A “building,” for the purposes of Section 8.44.010 D of City Code, is defined as a non-residential 
premises using a water meter.  Buildings will be assessed at least a “Basic Rate Fee” for each collection 
point (i.e., container or set of containers) or charges based on the type and quantity of containers and 
frequency of pickups as outlined in this Solid Waste Fee Schedule.  Multiple collocated, non-residential 
buildings owned by a single property owner will be assessed at least a “Basic Rate Fee” equal to the 
number of collection points.  If 1.5 Cubic Yard or larger city dumpsters are used, the Basic Rate Fee is 
included in the fees for those containers.  Charges are normally included in water and sewer bills issued 
to property owners or agents of such premises by the Board of Public Utilities. 
  
Note 3: If material is left outside of dumpsters or if residents fail to control their waste, requiring 
manual pickup, the owner of the property may be assessed this additional cleanup fee for manpower 
and equipment time.  
 
Note 4: City residents must show identification (driver’s license, etc.) and certify that material is their 
personal household waste to receive reduced Transfer Station rates.   
 
Note 5: Electronic Components: Charges to commercial entities for recycling of electronic components 
are based on the City’s cost for recycling plus 10% for handling and processing. Contact the Solid Waste 
Division for current prices.  Electronic components (computers, televisions, cell phones, etc.) contain 
hazardous materials and must not be disposed of in the Landfill. Recycling of these components is free 
to city/county residents at the Transfer Station. 
 
Note 6: Tires larger than 20” will be charged by weight. 
 
Note 7: Residents must present documentation from a doctor and certify that there is not an able-
bodied person living in the home to qualify for complimentary “Special Attention” services.  
Documentation must be updated on an annual basis to continue in the program. A sanitation division 
employee will move one trash and one recycle auto cart from and return it to a designated and 
approved location each collection day. Additional carts may be included if justification is provided, and 
an additional fee is paid. 
 
Note 8: Residential recycle containers will be equal to or larger than residential trash containers.  To 
qualify for the smaller 60-gallon recycle container, residents must also have a 60-gallon trash container.  
Residents may choose not to accept a recycle container; however, there will be no reduction in the 
residential automated collection fee. 
 
Note 9: Overweight or over-packed Roll-Off Containers will be charged double the disposal fee.  
Overloaded cardboard containers will be charged based on time required to empty the container. 
 
Note 10: Residential trash and recycle service may be suspended for full months only. Full month 
suspension dates are based on billing cycles as defined by the Board of Public Utilities. 
 
Note 11: The optional yard waste collection program may not be suspended, only discontinued. 
Residents who pay for the optional yard waste container receive pickup of up to one cubic yard of limbs, 
tree trimmings, hedge clippings and similar material.  Residents must cut items to less than four-foot 
lengths and contact the Sanitation Division to schedule pickup.  The Sanitation Division does not provide 
yard waste removal services to those who do not subscribe to the optional yard waste collection 
program.  
 
Note 12: Place automated trash, recycle and yard waste containers a minimum of two feet from any 
object, i.e., vehicles, trailers, fences, poles, trees, other containers, etc., and be sure the area in front of 
the container (toward the street) is clear of obstructions. Waste must flow freely from containers when 
tipped. A Container Placement Fee is charged each time a driver must exit the vehicle to move a 
container so it may be safely dumped without striking a nearby object. The fee will not apply if a 
container is blown over by the wind or other act of nature.   
 
Note 13:  These charges apply to customers who do not get a water bill.  Billing must be done manually 
by Solid Waste Staff.     
 
Note 14:  The Landfill Manager may request to inspect the origin of the soil and require testing to verify 
the soil is uncontaminated.  Petroleum contaminated soil has minimum testing requirements per WDEQ 
guidelines.  The customer will pay for any testing.  If requested, test results shall be provided to the 
Landfill Manager a minimum of 48-hours prior to disposal. 
 
Note 15:  The Public Works Director or Deputy Director may negotiate costs and terms for unusual 
requests to dispose of material at the Landfill.  Examples include but are not limited to:  material from 
large commercial demolition or remodeling projects; material such as dirt that may be needed by the 
City; and material from accidents or natural disasters.  Testing for hazardous materials may be required 
in these instances. 
 
Note 16: Waste containers and their contents placed within city limits in violation of city code Chapter 
8.44, Solid Waste, will be removed and disposed of and an appropriate fee assessed to the owner. Fee 
will be based on unscheduled contents disposal plus the Transfer Station disposal fee cost per ton.  
 
Note 17: Contact the Sanitation Division to arrange waste collection for special events at least 10-
business days prior to the event. Special event waste collection arrangements made less that 10-
business days prior to the event will be subject to a 25% short notice fee. Orders under $300 are subject 
to a $50 administration fee.
