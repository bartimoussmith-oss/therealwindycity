I. Description of Changes 
In anticipation of the City’s purchase of the former Senior Center building located east of the 
Municipal Building, Plan One will provide architectural and engineering design services for its 
renovation. The building is proposed to house new City Council chambers, City Council office 
space, and office space for an additional tenant. Services will include documenting existing 
conditions, preparing schematic and design development drawings, producing final construction 
plans and specifications, coordinating all necessary architectural and engineering disciplines, 
assisting with bidding, and providing construction administration services through project close-out. 
 
Plan One will also provide updated design plans for the Municipal Court’s second floor which will 
be used to house several departments during the renovation of the Municipal Building. The design 
process will evaluate alternative layout configurations, including open workspace with cubicles and 
temporary partitions, a hybrid layout incorporating permanent offices for the Prosecutors Division of 
the City’s Attorney’s Office with workspace for other staff . The consultant will prepare the 
construction documents necessary to implement the selected design and will provide bidding support 
and construction administration services consistent with the services described above.
