ORDINANCE NO. ________ 
 
ENTITLED: "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SPECIFIED SECTIONS WITHIN 
CHAPTER 8.68, CAMPING ON PUBLIC PROPERTY,  OF THE 
MUNICIPAL CODE OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING,  TO 
INCREASE THE ASSOCIATED PENALTIES AND REQUIRE REMOVAL 
FROM THE PREMISES IF IN VIOLATION OF THE CODE." 
 
 BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, 
WYOMING: 
 
 Section 1.   That Section 8.68.025, Sleeping on sidewalks, streets, alleys or within 
doorways prohibited, Section 8.68.030, Camping on campsites on public property prohibited; 
exception; penalty,  and Section 8.68.035, Camping allowed with lawful permit; permit 
administration, of Chapter 8.68, Camping on public property, of Title 8, Health and Safety, of the 
City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, are hereby amended to read: 
 
Chapter 8.68 - CAMPING ON PUBLIC PROPERTY 
 
8.68.025 - Sleeping on sidewalks, streets, alleys or within doorways prohibited. 
 
A. No person shall sleep on any public sidewalk, street or alleyway at any time as a matter of 
individual and public safety. 
 
B. No person may shall sleep in any pedestrian or vehicular entrance to public or private 
property abutting a public sidewalk. 
 
C. Any person who violates this section is guilty of a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of  
fifty dollars ($50.00) not more than seven hundred fifty dollars ($750.00), imprisonment 
for not more than six (6) months, or both . Any person found in violation of this section  
may also shall be immediately removed from the premises. Upon conviction for a violation 
of this section, if a defendant demonstrates that after receiving the citation and before the 
hearing, they meaningfully engaged with public or private resources or service providers 
to address the reasons that led them to b e in violation, the court shall consider that 
information when determining the appropriate sentence. 
 
8.68.030 - Camping and campsites on public property prohibited; exception; penalty. 
 
A. It is unlawful for any campsite to be situated on public property of the city. Every unlawful 
campsite shall be subject to abatement by the department in accordance with 
Section 8.68.040 of this chapter. 
 
B. No person shall use or otherwise occupy any public property of the city of Cheyenne for 
camping purposes unless the person holds a valid permit issued in accordance with 
Section 8.68.035 of this chapter.

C. Any person who violates subsection (B) of this section is guilty of a misdemeanor 
punishable by a fine of not more than  fifty dollars ($50.00)  seven hundred fifty dollars 
($750.00), imprisonment for not more than six (6) months, or both . Any person found in 
violation of subsection (B) of this section may also shall be immediately removed from the 
premises. Upon conviction for a violation of subsection (B) of this section, if a defendant 
demonstrates that after receiving the citation and before the hearing, they meaningfully 
engaged with public or private resources or service providers to address the reasons that 
led them to be in violation, the court shall consider that information when determining the 
appropriate sentence. 
 
8.68.035 - Camping allowed with lawful permit; permit administration. 
 
A. Notwithstanding the prohibitions imposed under Section 8.68.030 of this chapter, a person 
may camp on public property of the city upon the issuance of a camping permit by the city 
clerk and payment of the associated permit fee as provided by this section. All applications 
for a camping permit shall be submitted to the  city clerk on forms provided by the clerk 
and shall be submitted not less than ten (10) business days before the date the camping will 
occur. No permit may be issued under this section except upon the approval of the clerk 
and the director of the community recreation and events department. 
 
B. Before the issuance of any camping permit under this section, the director of the 
community recreation and events department shall: 
 
1. Determine and keep a record of all parcels of public property of the city that are 
appropriate for camping and for which a camping permit may be issued under this 
section. In identifying any parcel for camping under this paragraph, the director 
shall only select those parcels, and the locations in each parcel, that will not 
overburden the surrounding community in terms of noise, traffic and environmental 
impact; 
 
2. Prescribe the attendant camping permit fee for camping on a parcel of public 
property, which fee may vary by parcel of public property, but which shall only be 
in an amount sufficient to defer the costs and impacts associated with the camping 
activity; and 
 
3. Prescribe permit conditions, the intent of which shall be to provide for the proper 
maintenance and administration of the parcels upon which camping occurs and to 
protect public health and safety of the camping participants and the community. 
 
C. Any person camping on a parcel of public property without a permit as provided by this 
section shall be guilty of a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of not more than fifty dollars 
($50.00) seven hundred fifty dollars ($750.00), imprisonment for not more than six (6) 
months, or both . Each day a person camps without a permit shall constitute a separate a 
and distinct violation of this section.
