SUBJECT: Consideration of Bid #E -1-27 for furnishing Compost Facility Waste 
Hauling 
 
Bid #E-1-27 was solic ited for furnishing Compost Facility Waste Hauling .  The 
contractor will transport up to 18,500 cubic yards of compost materials to the Landfill 
and up to 2,000 cubic yards of clean soil from the Landfill to the Compost Facility. 
 
Two (2) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.   
 
The Two (2) bids were received from: 
 
1. Consolidated Resource LLC 
2. Eric Beasley LLC 
 
The bids were reviewed by Vicki Nemecek (Public Works), Craig LaVoy (Public Works), 
and Sam Quattrini (Landfill / Compost). 
 
Their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive Bid  from Consolidated 
Resource LLC.  The total requested bid award is $207,665.00 
 
Funding:   Solid Waste Fund
