MEMO 
 
TO:  Honorable Mayor and members of the City Council   
 
FROM:   Charles W. Bloom, AICP 
 
SUBJECT: DDA Plan of Development Public Hearing 
 
DATE:  February 10, 2025 
 
The purpose of this public hearing is to solicit public feedback in compliance with  
Wyo. Stat. §15-9-208(c) on the plan of development for the downtown development area.  
 
The updated Plan of Development (Plan) has been completed, incorporating and expanding on 
elements of the 1991 plan and its amendments from 1993, 2006, 2016, and 2018 into a new 
single, comprehensive document. In addition to this consolidation, the update incorporates new 
ideas and initiatives identified through public outreach sessions, ensuring the plan reflects 
current community priorities and opportunities. Included in this process was a thorough review 
of the prior plans and other area plans to remove redundant, completed, or no longer necessary 
goals to streamline the plan for greater clarity and focus.  
 
The Plan process also featured robust public engagement including three online questionnaires 
to gather community input and two in-person visioning events to discuss the downtown and 
plans. Two work sessions were held for property and business owners, and one joint work 
session brought together the Downtown Development Authority (DDA) Board and Planning 
Commission for discussion and understanding of the process for adoption of the Plan. 
Community participation was further encouraged through three in-person events (Friday’s on 
the Plaza, local eateries, and Superday) to identify public preference and two draft plan open 
houses. The process also included opportunities for public comment at DDA meetings and an 
opportunity for final comment at a joint meeting of the DDA Board and Planning Commission on 
January 21, 2025. Throughout the process the draft plan has been made available for public 
comments on the DDA’s Plan website.  
 
The Plan is available online here: https://plan.konveio.com/cheyenne-dda-plan-development-
adoption-draft. A brief presentation will be made at the Public Hearing.
