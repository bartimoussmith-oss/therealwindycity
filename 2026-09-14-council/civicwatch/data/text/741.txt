RECOMMENDED MOTION: 
 
Move to recommend the Governing Body approve the assigned zoning for land south of Laramie Street, 
east of T-Bird Drive, north of East Lincolnway and US 30, and west of Parsons Place to MR Medium-
density Residential, MUB Mixed-use Business, and CB Community Business as shown in the attached zone 
change map, noting that the project meets the review criteria for approval. 
 
APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS: 
 
• UDC 2.2.8 Assigned Zoning 
 
BACKGROUND: 
The City is annexing parcels that are 100% surrounded by current City Limits as part of Councils goals to annex 
county pockets. Annexation of a parcel requires a city zone to be established.  
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1. The proposed zoning assignment is the most analogous City zone to the current zone 
when taking into consideration allowed uses, current uses, or surrounding City zones. 
Zoning shall be assigned a City zone based on: 
a. Permitted uses, 
b. Uses that could reasonably be anticipated for conditional use approval 
under the current zoning, 
c. Current uses, or 
d. Surrounding City zoning, 
as deemed most suitable for the property being assigned a zone in terms of allowing 
the landowner to continue the current use of the land while respecting the current and 
anticipated uses of surrounding property. 
 
Through the reviewing of the County Pockets, the assigned zoning is fairly straight forward. There is 
one pocket that needed additional review and consideration, that being Parcel #8 per the attached 
zone change map. 
 
Parcel #8  
 
Parcel #8 is currently zoned LU – Land Use within the County. The City MR- Medium-density 
Residential zone exists to the north, west and a little bit to the east. To the south is City P –  Public and 
to the east there is also MUB – Mixed-use Business in addition to the MR zone. The Lot addressed as 
3710 Ostdiek Ct. is developed with a residence and what appears to be a business. The Lot addressed 
as 3720 Cheyenne St. is developed with a residence. The LU zone does allow for some business 
activity as conditional uses and due to the Lot at 3710 Ostdiek Ct having a business prior to the 
change in County regulations, there is some expectation that they would be able to continue to use 
PUDC-25-138   November 3, 2025 
3 | Page  
  
the property as it has been used. For this reason, Staff believes that the MUB – Mixed-use Business 
zone is the most  analogues zone for 3710 Ostdiek Ct. As mentioned, MUB zoning is to the east and 
this would be an extension of that zone district. 3720 Cheyenne St. only has a residence and Staff 
believes that the most analogues zone would be the MR – Medium-density Residential zone for this 
property based on the current use and the surrounding zones.   
 
Parcels #1 – 7 
 
Parcels #1 – 7 are currently zoned LU – Land Use within the County. All of the Lots within these 
pockets are developed with residential uses currently. These parcels are also surrounded by the MR –  
Medium-density Residential zone. The LU permits single-family residential and allows more intense 
residential as a conditional use. Based on this information Staff believes that the MR – Medium-
density Residential zone is the most analogues zone for these pockets as it has similar permitted 
uses, similar conditional uses and is the surrounding City zone.  
 
Parcel #9 
 
Parcel #9 is currently zoned CB – Community Business within the County. There is City CB zoning to 
the west and the south. To the north is MR zoning and to the east is MUB zoning. One Lot is vacant 
and the other is developed with an RV park. The designation of CB zoning in the County has not 
changed with their update to their zoning map. For these reasons Staff believes that the CB – 
Community Business zone is the most analogues zone for this pocket. 
 
Parcels #10 – 11 
 
These parcels are rights-of-way and will take on the adjacent City zone and do not require an 
assigned zone.  
 
Parcel #12 
 
Parcel #12 is currently zoned MU – Mixed Use in the County. This pocket is surrounded by the MUB – 
Mixed-use Business zone on all sides. Some of the area is vacant, two of the Lots are developed with a 
Church and a single lot is developed with warehousing / commercial business. The MU zone in the 
County changed with their zoning code update. The MU now resembles the City MUB zone based on 
allowed uses and conditional uses. Additionally,  the Church development that is within this pocket is 
part of a larger church complex that includes portions already in City limits. Thea p ortion in City limits 
is zoned MUB. Based on this information Staff believes that the MUB – Mixed-use Business zone is 
the most analogues zone for this pocket.  
 
This assigned zoning complies with review criterion 1.
