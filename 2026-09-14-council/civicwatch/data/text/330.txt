RESOLUTION NO. _____________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING SUBMISSION OF AN 
APPLICATION TO THE WYOMING STATE LOAN AND 
INVESTMENT BOARD  FOR A LOAN  FROM THE WYOMING 
DRINKING WATER STATE REVOLVING FUND FOR AN AMOUNT 
NOT TO EXCEED $20,000,000.00 FOR THE LEAD SERVICE LINE 
INVESTIGATION AND REPLACEMENT  PROJECT AND 
AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR, CITY CLERK, AND DIRECTOR OF 
THE BOARD OF PUBLIC UTILITIES  TO EXECUTE THE 
APPLICATION AND ALL RELATED DOCUMENTS.” 
 
WHEREAS, Wyoming Statute  § 15-7-401, provides for a Board of Public Utilities 
(BOPU) to manage, operate, maintain and control municipal waterworks, sanitary sewer system 
and sewage disposal plants; 
 
 WHEREAS, Cheyenne City Code, Section 2.68.010, established BOPU to have 
exclusive authority to manage, operate, maintain and control the Cheyenne Municipal 
Waterworks, Sanitary Sewage System and Sewage Disposal Plants of the City and to make all 
rules and regulations necessary for the safe, economical and efficient op eration and 
maintenance of the above facilities; 
 
 WHEREAS, the EPA Lead and Copper Rule Revisions (LCRR) and Lead and Cooper 
Rule Improvements (LCRI) require baseline inventories which are due in November 2027 and 
replacements must begin in 2030 with 10% of total lead, lead impacted, or unknown service 
lines replaced each year until 2040;  
 
WHEREAS, BOPU will submit an Investigation and Replacement Plan to  the EPA 
outlining how the awarded funds will be used to physically validate unknown service lines and 
complete required replacement;  
 
WHEREAS, the EPA provided funding to the Wyoming Drinking Water State 
Revolving Fund  (DWSRF) in 2022, and those funds must be distributed by DWSRF  to 
Wyoming water system operators or be at risk to be reverted to the EPA for redistribution to 
other states; 
 
WHEREAS, BOPU qualifies for zero percent interest and up to 25%  principal 
forgiveness on DWSRF Loan for lead service line related projects; 
 
WHEREAS, the Board of BOPU  desires to pursue  investigation contracts to identify 
unknown service materials and replace lead or lead impacted water service lines  per guidance 
of  the EPA LCRR and LCRI;  
 
WHEREAS, the Project cost estimate for pothole investigation , service line 
replacement, and other administrative costs is $20,000,000.00;  
 2 
 
WHEREAS, a loan from the Wyoming Drinking Water State Revolving Fund is subject 
to a loan origination fee of 0.5 percent, which is estimated to be $100,000.00;   
 
WHEREAS, the Board recognizes that the annual principal and interest payment on the 
proposed loan will range from approximately $750,000.00 to $1,000,0000.00 based on 
principal forgiveness amount awarded;  
 
WHEREAS, the Board and the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne are committed 
to repaying the loan from revenue from the Water Enterprise Fund; and  
 
 WHEREAS, the Board recognizes that the Project is necessary to meet EPA guidelines 
provided in the LCRR and LCRI. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE  GOVERNING BODY OF THE  
CITY OF CHEYENNE , t hat an application be submitted to the Wyoming State Loan and 
Investment Board for a loan from the Wyoming Drinking Water  State Revolving Fund for an 
amount not to exceed $ 20,000,000.00 for P roject costs including, but not limited to , water 
service line potholing and replacement, administration, and all related costs of the Lead Service 
Line Investigation and Replacement Project; and  
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Mayor, the City Clerk, and the Director of the 
Board of Public Utilities are hereby designated as the authorized representatives of the City of 
Cheyenne to act on behalf of the City of Cheyenne in all matters related to this application and 
related documents, including any amendments to the Project Agreement.
