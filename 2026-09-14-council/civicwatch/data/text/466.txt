BACKGROUND:
The applicant is requesting amending the Future Land Use Map for roughly 1,193.4 acres of land to Mixed-use 
Employment to accommodate a zone change to the BP – Business Park zone.
Part of this property is outside the Urban Services Boundary and has no Future Land Use Map designation. This 
area is proposed to be added to the Urban Services Boundary and designated Mixed-use Employment.
The portion of the property currently within the Urban Service Boundary is shown as Urban Transition
Residential in the Future Land Use Plan. The Urban Transition Residential category, along with the Rural 
Residential category, provides a gradual transition from the urbanized areas of the Cheyenne Area to the
rural areas on the periphery. It includes existing homes and neighborhoods, as well as some planned and 
recent new growth areas.
A concurrent application for a zoning map amendment has been submitted. The zoning map amendment will 
accompany the future land use amendment at City Council. If the application for the zoning map amendment is 
approved, the applicant will be required to submit a site plan application for any new development.

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:
Should the Planning Commission wish to approve an amendment to the Future Land Use Map of the 
Comprehensive Plan, the Commission must make the following considerations outlined in the 2014
Community Plan (page 18).  A detailed analysis is below:
1) BENEFICIAL OR ADDRESSES ISSUES - The proposed amendment is beneficial to the 
community or addresses issues not foreseen during the planning process.
The proposed amendment to Mixed-use Employment at the proposed location would be 
beneficial as it is on the very edge of the community. Mixed-use Employment areas should be 
located toward the fringe of the community and in areas that are not highly residential as they 
allow for more industrial and business park type developments. This area is mostly agricultural in 
use at this time and is at the edge of the urban service boundary. The nearest residential 
subdivision is a rural-residential style development a half-mile to the east.
The proposed Future Land Use amendment complies with review criteria 1.
2) COMPATIBLY - The proposed amendment is compatible with the surrounding area, 
and the goals and policies of the Plan. 
The subject property is adjacent to Mixed-use Employment to south, no designation to the west, 
Agricultural/Rural to the north and Urban Transition Residential to the east. The Urban Transition
Residential would not allow for commercial or industrial type uses. Higher intensity employment 
is encouraged in the core of Mixed-Use Employment Campus areas, or adjacent to collector or 
arterial roadways. The proposed amendment is consistent with the goals and policies of 
PlanCheyenne, as discussed in more detail below.
The proposed Future Land Use amendment complies with review criteria 2.
  
3) IMPACT MITIGATION - The proposed amendment will address and mitigate 
impacts on transportation, services, and facilities.
Transportation:
The transportation network in this area of the City is somewhat developed with a major street to 
the south. All impacts to the transportation network will be addressed through the platting and 
site plan processes. Additional rights-of-way could be required depending on the development 
pattern proposed for the property.
Services:
The proposed change will not have any adverse effects on the provision of services to the subject 
property. Service providers, such as the fire department, will be included in platting and site 
planning processes to assess service needs.
Facilities:
The proposed change will likely require sewer to be extended to the development area.
The proposed Future Land Use amendment complies with review criteria 3.

4) SERVICE PROVISION - The proposed amendment will have minimal effect on 
service provision, including adequacy or availability of urban facilities and services, 
and is compatible with existing and planned service provision 
This land is currently proposed to be annexed into City limits and urban facilities and services are 
available to the subject property. Sewer would need to be extended from the south.  
The proposed Future Land Use amendment complies with review criteria 4.
5) ANNEXATION - The proposed amendment does not jeopardize the City’s ability to 
annex the property.
The subject property is currently proposed to be annexed into City limits.  
The proposed Future Land Use amendment complies with review criteria 5.
.  
6) URBAN SERVICES AREA - The proposed amendment is consistent with the logical 
expansion of the Urban Services Area.
This land is about 70% within the Urban Services Area. The Urban Service Boundary in this area is 
a straight north-south line that does not follow topography or current parcel boundaries. This 
future land use amendment would extend the Urban Service Area to match the platted boundary 
of the land. 
The proposed Future Land Use amendment complies with review criteria 6.
7) STRICT ADHERENCE - Strict adherence to the Plan would result in a situation 
neither intended nor in keeping with other key elements and policies of the Plan.
Strict adherence to the Plan may result in unintended consequences. While the land could 
develop with residential, it is on the edge of the community where the higher level of services 
typically expected with urban residential development could be challenging to expand. 
Alternatively, the area could develop with large-acre ranchette-style development in the County; 
however, such development is not optimal for areas within the Urban Service Boundary.
The current Urban Service Boundary in this area appears to have been created by reaching the 
edge of the 2014 ‘Planning Boundary’. Everywhere else, the Urban Service Boundary follows 
topography or parcel boundaries. Strict adherence to the current Urban Service Boundary in this 
area of the community may be unfairly limiting to land otherwise developable.
The proposed Future Land Use amendment complies with review criteria 7.
8) PUBLIC WELFARE AND CONSISTENCY – The proposed plan amendment will 
promote the public welfare and will be consistent with the goals and policies of 
PlanCheyenne and the elements thereof.
Staff is satisfied that the proposed application will not have any adverse effects on public welfare. 
Furthermore, the application accomplishes the following policies contained within PlanCheyenne:

traffic impacts. Such uses may include trucking and distribution facilities, oil and gas 
support services, and warehousing among others.

RE:  FUTURE LAND USE AMENDMENT OF LAND ON HAPPY JACK ROAD 
Dear Mr. Bloom 
 
Please accept this request to amend the Future Land Use Amendment for property currently 
under consideration for Annexation within the City of Cheyenne. 
 
The property is currently a vacant parcel just west and south of 8802 Happy Jack Road.  It is 
requested to be changed from rural residential to mixed use employment.  With annexation of 
the property and the extension of water and sewer to the property rural residential would not be 
a logical designation.  The request for mixed use employment is consistent with the properties 
to the south and east and the continued development of business parks to the west of the 
current City of Cheyenne boundaries. 
 
The requested amendment is consistent with the broader vision of the City’s Comprehensive 
Plan by encouraging orderly development and thoughtful land use planning.  The area has seen 
growth in commercial development of the North Range Business Park which make the proposed 
designation appropriate. 
 
If you have any questions or would like to discuss this project in greater detail, please contact 
me directly.
