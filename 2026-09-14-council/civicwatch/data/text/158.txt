GROUND LEASE 
 
 THIS GROUND LEASE  (the “ Lease”) is made and entered in to this ___ day of 
__________, 2026 (the “Effective Date”), by and between FIRST & UNION  APARTMENTS, 
LP, a Utah Limited Partnership (“First & Union”); and the CITY OF CHEYENNE, a Wyoming 
municipal corporation (the “City”); and is based on the following: 
RECITALS: 
 A. First & Union is organized under the laws of the State of Utah and  is engaged in 
developing Affordable Housing utilizing the Section 42 (LIHTC) program. 
 B. The City owns real property located at approximately the northwest corner of the 
intersection of Converse Ave nue and Carlson St reet in Cheyenne, Wyoming, containing 
approximately 8.37 acres  as more particularly described on Exhibit “B” attached hereto (the 
“Property”). 
 C. First & Union and the City desire to enter into a lease for the Property upon which 
First & Union will construct 184  units of affordable housing restricted to 60% AMI  (the 
“Affordable Housing Community”).  
 D. First & Union and the City desire to enter into this Lease to facilitate the 
construction of the Affordable Housing Community on the Property. 
 
 NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises hereinafter set forth, and 
other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby 
acknowledged, First & Union and the City agree as follows: 
 1. Premises.  The City hereby leases and lets to First & Union  and First & Union  
hereby rents from the City the Property , the same being outlined and shown on Exhibit “A” 
attached hereto and by reference incorporated herein and the legal description of which is set forth 
on Exhibit “B” attached hereto and incorporated herein, together with all easements, rights-of-way 
and appurtenances in connection therewith or thereunto belonging (all of which are hereinafter 
referred to as the “Premises”). 
 2. Term.  The t erm of th is Lease shall commence on the Effective D ate and expire 
fifty (50) years after the Effective Date (the “Term”). 
 3. Rent.  Beginning on the date that is the earlier of: (a) the date that is two (2) years 
from the Effective Date; or (b) the date that the Affordable Housing Community is stabilized at 
95% occupancy for a period of two (2) consecutive months, and continuing through the remainder 
of the Term of the Lease , First & Union shall pay to the City the amount of Four Thousand Five 
Hundred Twenty-One and 64/100 Dollars ($4,521.64) per month as rent (“Rent”). The Rent shall 
be payable on or before the fifth (5 th) day of each month , in advance; provided, however, that to 
the extent First & Union lacks sufficient free cashflow after debt service to pay all or any portion 
2 
 
of the Rent when due, First & Union shall provide written notice to the City not less than five (5) 
business days before the date such Rent is due. The notice shall state the amount of Rent First & 
Union is unable to pay, briefly describe the cashflow basi s for the nonpayment, and include 
reasonably sufficient supporting documentation to verify the lack of available free cashflow after 
debt service. Any unpaid Rent shall not be waived, forgiven, or discharged, but shall be deferred 
and remain an obligation of First & Union until paid. Any deferred Rent shall accrue simple 
interest at a rate equal to the long -term Applicable Federal Rate, based on annual compounding, 
as published by the Internal Revenue Service under Section 1274(d) of the Internal Revenue Code, 
in effect on the original due date of the deferred Rent (the “Interest Rate”), from the original due 
date until paid. The Interest Rate on any deferred Rent payments shall increase by one percent 
(1%) on each anniversary of the original due date that the applicable deferred Rent was due.  
Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Interest Rate shall not increase above twenty percent (20%) on 
any amounts of deferred Rent unpaid hereunder. First & Union shall pay any deferred Rent, 
together with accrued interest, from available free cashflow after debt service before making any 
discretionary distribution, owner return, or similar p ayment from the Affordable Housing 
Community. First & Union may prepay any and all Rent amounts due hereunder without penalty. 
The Rent amount set forth above is based on a one-half of one percent (0.5%) carry for the City 
on a value of the Property of Two Million Four Hundred Thousand Dollars ($2,400,000) amortized 
on a straight-line basis over fifty (50) years. City shall have the right, upon ten (10) business days’ 
notice and not more than once per calendar year, to audit books and records reasonably necessary 
to verify Free Cash Flow and compliance with this Section 3. 
Notwithstanding the foregoing, all deferred Rent and accrued interest shall become immediately 
due and payable upon the earliest of: (a) any sale, assignment, or transfer of First & Union’s 
interest in this Lease or the Affordable Housing Community, other than a permitted collateral 
assignment or tax credit investor admission; (b) any refinancing or recapitalization of the 
Affordable Housing Community to the extent proceeds are legally available after payment of 
senior debt, required reserves, and approved transaction costs; (c) termination of this Lease; (d) 
transfer of the Property to First & Union; or (e) any uncured default by First & Union after 
expiration of applicable notice and cure periods. 
 4. Transfer of Property. Upon the earlier of: (a) the natural expiration  or termination 
of the Term, provided First & Union is not then in default under this Lease;  or (b) the prepayment 
of all Rent due by First & Union during the Term hereunder, provided that the Affordable Housing 
Community has been completed, placed in service, and operated in compliance with the 
affordability requirements of this Lease for not less than ten (10) consecutive years, the City shall 
transfer the Property to First & Union by Special Warranty Deed. The Special Warranty Deed for 
the period of 50 years to begin on the due date of the first payment shall require the Property to be 
used and operated as an affordable housing community consisting of one hundred eighty-four units 
restricted to households no t earning more than sixty percent of area median income, subject to 
temporary reductions in available units due to casualty, repair, rehabilitation, code compliance, 
force majeure, or other events beyond First & Union’s reasonable control, provided that Fi rst & 
Union promptly commences and diligently pursues commercially reasonable efforts to restore the 
affected units for affordable housing use. 
3 
 
Upon the transfer of the Property to First & Union pursuant to this Section 4, this Lease shall 
automatically terminate and be of no further force or effect, except that the affordable housing use 
restriction shall survive the transfer and termination of this Lease  for a period of not less than 50 
years, shall be included in the Special Warranty Deed, and shall run with the land and be binding 
upon First & Union and its successors and assigns. During the Term, the affordability covenant 
shall be evidenced by and recorded in a memorandum or regulatory agreement acceptable to the 
City, to ensure notice to and binding effect upon successors and lenders, and shall include City 
inspection and reporting rights as set forth herein. 
5. Land Use Restriction Agreement This Property shall have a Land Use Restriction 
Agreement recorded against it within one hundred twenty ( 120) days of execution of this 
agreement which shall include at minimum an affordable housing period of not less than fifty (50) 
years restrict ing affordable housing  to 60% area median income  for 100% of the units. The 
Property shall maintain compliance with the Land Use Restriction Agreement and the W yoming 
Community Development Authority or other applicable agency.   
Failure to record an appropriate land use restriction agreement within one hundred twenty (120) 
days of execution of this agreement shall allow the City in its discretion to reenter and retake title 
to the Property in accordance with Section 23. 
 6. Use of Premises .  During the Term , the Premises shall be used and occupied by 
First & Union solely for the purpose of the design, development, construction, operation, use, 
repair, and maintenance of the Affordable Housing Community and related improvements 
pursuant to the  terms set forth in this Lease and the plans and specifications provided for the 
Affordable Housing Community (the “Plans and Specifications”). First & Union shall at all times 
during the Term operate the Affordable Housing Community in compliance with income, rent, and 
occupancy restrictions for households earning not more than sixty percent (60%) of area median 
income, subject to temporary reductions in available units due to events described in Section 4, 
with diligent efforts to restore compliance. First & Union shall deliver to the City, no later than 
ninety (90) days after each calendar year-end, an annual compliance certificate with rent rolls and 
income verification summaries evidencing compliance. First & Union shall not use or permit the 
Premises to be used for any other purpose without the prior written approval of the  City, which 
consent may be withheld by the City in its reasonable discretion.  First & Union shall not use or 
permit the Premises to be used for any purpose inconsistent with the Affordable Housing 
Community, including market-rate conversion, short-term rentals (less than thirty (30) days), hotel 
or transient use , or non -residential uses other than customary accessory uses. City shall have 
reasonable inspection rights during normal business hours upon two (2) business days’ notice to 
verify compliance (no more than once every six (6) months).

secure, replace, remove and operate the Affordable Housing Community and related 
improvements on the Premises , including parking areas and to extend, enlarge, alter, remove, 
repair, replace or improve any structure or other improvement then existing on the Premises, as

state, and local laws, ordinances, rules, regulations, permits, approvals, and land use requirements.

taxes, assessments, if any. 
 11. Termination.  This Lease may be terminated without any further liability on sixty 
(60) days’ prior written notice  by either party upon a default of any covenant or term hereof by 
the other party, which default is not cured within ninety (90) days of receipt of writt en notice of 
default, except that this Agreement shall not be terminated if the default cannot reasonably be 
cured within such ninety (90) day period and the defaulting party has commenced to cure the 
default within such ninety (90) day period and diligent ly pursues the cure to completion . 
Notwithstanding the foregoing, in no event shall termination relieve First & Union of the 
affordable housing use and income restrictions set forth in Section 4, which shall survive and run 
with the land. If any lender, tax credit investor, housing agency, or other party with a recorded 
interest requires notice and cure rights, the City agrees to provide such notices and allow customary 
extended cure periods up to one hundred eighty (180) days (or longer if the default is n ot 
reasonably curable within such period and is being diligently cured), provided that affordability 
covenants remain in full force and effect.

 13. Damage or Destruction . Should any building on the Premises be damaged or 
destroyed by any cause whatsoever, First & Union  will either (i)  cause the commencement of 
reconstruction to the damag ed or destroyed building within ninety (90) days after such damage 
and destruction and will thereafter cause such reconstruction to be diligently pr osecuted to 
completion; or (ii) within ninety (90) days of the date of damage or destr uction, commence the 
removal of such damaged building and the reafter diligently prosecute to completion the removal 
of the damaged building, the removal of all debris from the Premises. If insurance or other recovery 
is available in an amount sufficient to rebuild improvements of comparable utility for affordable 
housing, First & Union shall, subject to lender consent, apply such proceeds to restoration and 
shall not elect to terminate under this Section 13 without the City’s prior written consent, not to be 
unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Use of proceeds and restoration plans shall be 
reasonably a cceptable to the City to preserve long -term affordability.  Notwithstanding the 
foregoing, should the buildings located from time to time on the Premises be damaged or destroyed 
in excess of fifty percent (50%) of the then existing replacement cost of such buildings, First & 
Union shall have the right within ninety (90) days from the date of such damage or destruction to 
elect to terminate this Lease by submitting written notice thereof to the City, in which event, First 
& Union shall remove the damaged building and remove all debris from the Premises within thirty 
(30) days after the date such notice of termination is given. Termination of this Lease pursuant to 
this Section 13 shall be effective as of the date notice is given.
