Project: Professional Services Contract Between the City of Cheyenne and 
Matzen Brandon LLC  
Project Number:  Contract No. 8286 
Contractor:   Matzen Brandon LLC 
Date Prepared:   May 26, 2026 
 
The above-mentioned contract is amended or modified as set forth in the following and these 
amendments and modifications of the plans and specifications are agreed to by the parties. All 
other aspects of the original agreement remain unchanged and in full force and effect. 
 
I. Description of Changes: 
This Contract Modification adjusts the level of services contemplated under Contract No. 8286  
from a quarter -time equivalent arrangement to slightly less than a half -time equivalent 
arrangement. To reflect that change, the monthly payment amount is increased from four thousand 
five hundred dollars ($4,500.00) per month to seven thousand five hundred dollars ($7,500.00) per 
month. The annual not -to-exceed amount is increased proportionately from fifty -four thousand 
dollars ($54,000.00) to ninety thousand dollars ($90,000.00). 
 
II. New Contract Items: 
Section 5 of Contract No. 8286, titled “Payment,” is hereby amended to read as follows: 
 
5. Payment. The City agrees to pay the Contractor for the services described in 
Section 6, below, at a flat rate of seven thousand five hundred dollars ($7,500.00) 
a month. The total payment under this Contract shall not exceed ninety thousand 
dollars ($90,000.00) f or any one (1) year period. Should the Contractor fail to 
perform in a manner consistent with the terms and conditions set forth in this 
Contract, payment under this Contract may be withheld until such time as the 
Contractor performs its duties and responsibilities to the satisfaction of the City.
