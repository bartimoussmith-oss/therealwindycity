Subject: County Pocket Annexation #6  
Date: May 12, 2025 
 
Background:  
 
The item for consideration is a resolution to allow the City to proceed with a City-initiated annexation. 
Annexation of County pockets is a 2022 City Council priority.  A “County pocket” is an area of 
unincorporated land 100% surrounded by the City of Cheyenne.   
 
The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as follows: 
• Introduction and referral  May 12, 2025 
• Public Services Committee  May 19, 2025 
• Final consideration  May 27, 2025 
 
This annexation is comprised of 38 properties 100% surrounded by the City (See attached map). Of 
the 38 parcels, a majority are developed with residential uses, a few are developed with commercial 
uses and others are undeveloped or incorporated into adjacent parcels under similar ownership. The 
City has maintained contact with the County Planning Office to coordinate any pending permits or 
development reviews.  Currently there are no active permits or development specific to the 
properties proposed for annexation.   
 
On April 21, 2025, City staff mailed a courtesy notice by first class mail to all landowners included in 
this annexation area inviting them to a “Question and Answer” on Monday May 5, 2025.   
 
Annexation also requires establishing a City zone. Staff has created a tentative timeline for the 
annexation and establishment of City zoning districts for the subject properties. Zone districts will be 
established on the assigned zoning process of Unified Development Code (UDC) section 2.2.8. In the 
event of split zoning (where two zone districts divide an existing tract of land), the City intends to 
apply the more restrictive zone unless extenuating circumstances exist.

WHEREAS, Wyoming Statute § 15 -1-404 specifies the requirements for initiating 
annexation proceedings by cities and towns and requires that a resolution be adopted by the 
Governing Body certifying compliance with Wyoming Statute § 15-1-402; 
 
Page 6 of 7 
WHEREAS, based on reasonable evidence, the Governing Body has determined that the 
territory to be annexed complies with Wyoming Statute § 15 -1-402 and meets the following 
criteria found in Wyoming Statute § 15-1-402(a):  
 
(i)  The annexation of the area is for the protection of the health, safety and welfare 
of the persons residing in the area and in the City of Cheyenne;  
 
(ii)  The urban development of the area sought to be annexed would constitute a 
natural, geographical, economical and social part of the City of Cheyenne;  
 
(iii)  The areas sought to be annexed is a logical and feasible addition to the City of 
Cheyenne and the extension of basic and other services customarily available to 
residents of the city or town shall, within reason, be available to the area proposed 
to be annexed;  
 
(iv)  The area sought to be annexed is contiguous with or adjacent to the City of 
Cheyenne;  
 
(v)  The Governing Body is prepared to issue one (1) or more franchises as 
necessary to serve the annexed area pursuant to Wyoming Statute § 15-1-410; and 
 
(vi)  The City of Cheyenne is prepared to, not less than twenty (20) business days 
prior to the public hearing required by Wyoming Statute § 15 -1-405(a), send by 
certified mail to all landowners and affected public utilities within the territory a 
summary of the  annexation report as required under Wyoming Statute 
§ 15-1-402(c) and notice of the time, date and location of the public hearing 
required by Wyoming Statute § 15-1-405(a).  
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne has created an annexation report, which contains, at a 
minimum, the required information from Wyoming Statute § 15-1-402(c); 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne shall prepare for each landowner and affected public 
utility so requesting in writing, the estimated cost of infrastructure improvements required of the 
landowner and affected public utility related to the annexation pursuant to Wyoming Statute 
§ 15-1-402(e); 
 
WHEREAS, the annexation of this territory will not create an area which is situated entirely 
within the boundaries of the City of Cheyenne, following criteria found in Wyoming Statute 
§ 15-1-406(d); and 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body finds the territory to be annexed does not meet the 
criteria specified under Wyoming Statute § 15-1-412 and thus does not require the written consent 
of the owner of the territory before being annexed by the City of Cheyenne.
