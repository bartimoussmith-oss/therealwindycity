RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION SUPPORTING THE CHEYENNE REGIONAL AIRPORT 
BOARD IN ITS APPLICATION FOR, AND ACCEPTANCE OF , FEDERAL 
ASSISTANCE FROM THE FEDERAL AVIATION ADMINISTRATION 
UNDER THE BIPARTISAN INFRASTRUCTURE ACT TO ASSIST IN THE 
RECONSTRUCTION OF  GENERAL AVIATION APRON AND 
TAXIWAY A1 AT THE CHEYENNE REGIONAL AIRPORT.” 
 
 WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Regional Airport Board desires to participate in the Federal 
Aviation Administration (“FAA”) grant program, under the Bipartisan Infrastructure Law (“BIL”) to 
assist in funding costs related to the reconstruction of General Aviation (GA) Apron and Taxiway A1 
(the “Project”); 
 
 WHEREAS, the concrete on the GA Apron and Taxiway A1 has degraded to a point where it 
is no longer compliant with applicable federal standards or safe for continued use; 
 
 WHEREAS, the Project will reconstruct the GA Apron and Taxiway A1 to meet FAA 
standards, thereby ensuring their continued safe and functional use at the Cheyenne Regional Airport; 
 
WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Regional Airport Board proposes to file an application with the 
FAA for grant funding under the BIL for costs associated with the design and reconstruction of the 
Project in the amount of three million one hundred thousand dollars ($3,100,000.00), to be expended 
over a five (5) year period; and 
 
WHEREAS, the grant is anticipated to be funded at ninety -five percent (95%) by the FAA, 
with the remaining five percent (5%) to be provided in equal shares by the Wyoming Department of 
Transportation (2.5% or $81,579.00) and the Cheyenne Regional Airport B oard (2.5% or 
$81,579.00). 
 
 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the City hereby supports the submission of a grant application 
by the Cheyenne Regional Airport Board to the FAA under the BIL, in the amount of three million 
one hundred thousand dollars ($3,100,000.00), for the purpose of funding the Project. 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that, upon award of the grant, the Mayor and City Clerk are 
hereby authorized and directed to execute all documents necessary to accept and administer the grant, 
including any agreements and certifications required by the FAA.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: 
For your review and consideration are two FAA Grant applications: 
1. FAA Grant No. AIP-071, for $3,032,478 in federal assistance through the Bipartisan Infrastructure Law 
(BIL).  CYS was granted approximately $1,300,000 annually, totaling roughly $5.05 million over five years 
to be used to modernize infrastructure.  These funds had to be used within certain time limits.  
 
$2.5 million of these funds were previously used to construct the passenger boarding bridge in 2024.      The 
amount of remaining IIJA funds, approximately $3,100,000 will be used to reconstruct the GA apron and 
taxiway that are typically lower priority for competitive funds and are unlikely to be funded through regular 
AIP funding.    Anticipated financial contributions are as follows: 
 
AIP 071 
FAA 95%:     $      3,100,000 
State 2.5%:    $   81,579 
CYS 2.5%:    $      81,579 
TOTAL:         $ 3,263,158 
 
2. FAA Grant No. AIP -072, for $2,600,000 in federal assistance under the Airport Improvement Program 
(AIP). The federal funds requested are entitlement funds (two years) .  Airports are entitled to a certain 
amount of AIP funding each year, based on passenger volume. The amount of AIP entitlement funds the 
Airport receives is $1, 300,000 each fiscal year for being a “non- hub primary airport” which is defined as 
more than 10,000 passengers but less than .05% of the annual national passengers.     Anticipated financial 
contributions are as follows: 
 
AIP 072 
FAA 95%:     $      2,600,000 
State 2.5%:    $   68,421 
CYS 2.5%:    $      68,421 
TOTAL:         $ 2,736,842
