BACKGROUND: 
 
This applicant is requesting connection BOPU water services for property outside City limits. The land is 
currently being developed as a staging area for construction equipment for data centers. The adjacent 
properties are data centers within City limits and those properties have full access to water and sewer. The 
subject property is looking to extend services from the adjacent property with City limits to this construction 
yard. The applicant has applied to annex the subject property (OUDC-26-124) – the Outside User Agreement 
allows access to services while the property goes through the annexation process.  
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
The request for an outside user agreement has no formal review criteria; however, the following elements 
should be considered when reviewing outside user agreements: 
- BOPU capacity 
- Needed infrastructure improvements 
- Fiscal cost/benefits to the City and the BOPU 
- Population served 
- Considerations in the effect on future annexation(s), including proximity to current and future City 
services. 
- Conformance with the Comprehensive Plan 
 
The requested outside user agreement has minimal impact on BOPU’s system capacity. The proposed use 
of the property is for multiple construction offices that would be serviced by a Microsoft tap.
