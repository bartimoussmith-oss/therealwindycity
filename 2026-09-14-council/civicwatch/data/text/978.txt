Access Roads project. 
 
Based on a request by the wind energy company at th e Belvoir Ranch, an existing roadway that 
will be used to access the trailhead that is curren tly under construction needs to be re-aligned to 
provide better separation between an existing wind turbine tower and the roadway. This contract 
modification addresses the additional costs for thi s work as well as relocating the entrance gate 
from the access point from County Road 102 (Harrima n Road) to the City’s property line along 
the access road the trailhead and additional work i tems to address comments from Laramie 
County. 
 
This contract modification also extends the contrac t deadline to September 30, 2025. This 
extension is needed due to the additional roadway work and delays associated with permitting by 
other agencies. 
 
Funding for this work will come from the Belvoir Ra ch Recreation Fund. The total construction 
costs associated with this contract modification wi ll be $120,556.50 and paid at unit prices 
described in the original contract documents as wel l as Memo 1 and Memo 2 for the work to be 
completed. We respectfully request your approval of this contract modification.

energy company at the Belvoir Ranch and address concerns about the entrance gate from the 
State and address comments from Laramie County.  See Memo 1 and Memo 2 , attached hereto

Re-align and construct an unpaved roadway to meet safety spacing requested by the wind energy 
company. Relocate the entrance gate from the County Road 102 (Harriman Road) from the State 
property to the City property. See Memo 1 and Memo 2.

Extend from June 30, 2025, to September 30, 2025.
