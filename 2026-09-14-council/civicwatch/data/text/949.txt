BACKGROUND: 
 
This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from AG Agricultural to NR-2 Neighborhood 
Residential – Medium Density for roughly 10.94 acres of land. The land is currently vacant. The current AG 
zone was assigned by the City as a holding zone until the Developer determined a zone that would allow for 
development of the parcel.  
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns 
identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1) 
 
PlanCheyenne encourages growth within the Urban Service Boundary and the maximization of 
existing infrastructure. This property is within the Urban Service Boundary, in an area with 
infrastructure that is currently being built.   
 
The Future Land Use Designation for this property is Urban Residential and the surrounding area 
is Urban Residential and Urban Transition Residential. According to the comprehensive plan the

optimal City zones for the subject property’s future land use designation is any one of the 
residential zones, including NR-2. 
 
Based on the policies and future land use map, NR-2 zoning is appropriate at this location.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1. 
 
2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning, 
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance 
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2) 
 
Surrounding properties in the City are zoned MR and AG. There is also a property zoned NR-3 to 
the northeast at the intersection of Ridge Rd. and Storey Blvd. MR and NR-2 zones are medium-
density residential zones. The difference is that NR-2 zoning anticipates alley-loaded 
development, allows smaller lots, more urban styles of development, and has a higher design 
standard. NR-2 zoning is compatible with surrounding zoning.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2. 
   
3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of 
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for 
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC 
2.2.1.d.3) 
  
The AG zone was assigned to this property as a holding zone and would only allow for 
agricultural type uses. NR-2 zoning is intended for smaller-block, rear-loaded, compact 
development patterns (like what is found in the Avenues). The NR-2 zone allows for a variety of 
residential type uses and allows Cottage Lots as a use by right, which is a development style that 
has become more common recently.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3. 
 
4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the 
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4) 
 
The subject property will need to be platted in the future and will need to meet the standards of 
Article 4. Due to the size of the parcel, staff believes the standards can be met.   
 
Infrastructure (streets and utilities) are in the area and new streets and utilities can be extended 
to this parcel. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4. 
 
5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the 
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC 
2.2.1.d.5) 
 
The NR-2 zone is intended for a medium density and mix of dwelling types that promote a 
consistent neighborhood character, with a compact development pattern and highly connected 
PUDC-25-151   August 4, 2025 
street network. Due to the size of the lot, staff believes the applicant will be able to plat the 
property in a way that meets the intent of the zone district proposed. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5. 
    
6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for 
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options if any 
specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after 
approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6) 
 
The NR-2 zone allows a variety of residential uses. The NR-2 also has design standards required 
per UDC 6.6.4 which will need to be met for any residential development that is proposed for 
this area.   
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6. 
 
7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7) 
 
Proposal meets comprehensive plan principles. No other comments were received from 
reviewers.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7. 
 
8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare. (UDC 
2.2.1.d.8) 
 
City staff is satisfied that this proposed zone change would not adversely affect the public 
health, safety, or general welfare. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 8.
