This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from AG to NR-3 Neighborhood Residential – 
High Density for roughly 14.47 acres of land. The land is currently vacant.

Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2. 2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical 
patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1)

PlanCheyenne encourages  opportunities for commercial and high-intensity residential growth 
within this area under this designation. 
 
The Future Land Use Designation for this property is  Mixed-Use Commercial . According to the 
comprehensive plan , this designation supports more commercial, light industrial, or live -work 
developments that could be “activity centers”. Residential use may be added to these areas in 
order to bring in more activity, but their development should be secondary to potential 
commercial development in the area. If residential developments are within the Mixed -Use 
Commercial area , there should be more than 8 dwelling units per acre. The ideal location for 
activity center is along principal arterial or minor arterial streets . This lot is located at the 
intersection of Allison Road and Walterscheid Boulevard , two high trafficked streets. This results in 
higher volume of traffic than might be desired in residential areas.  
 
The optimal zone for this area is MUB  Mixed-use Business Emphasis, although a mix of 
commercial and high -intensity residential zones may also accomplish the comprehensive plan 
objectives. Based on the policies and future land use map, NR-3 is not the most appropriate for 
this area . The intersection is lacking in commercial zoning, though commercial zoning exists  less 
than 200 feet to the northeast of the subject property . The MUB zone in the UDC is essentially a 
combination o f NR-3 and NB zones, the MUB even uses NR -3 lot types and residential design 
standards for purely residential development. Essentially, the proposed NR -3 zone is the 
residential half of the optimal MUB zo ne. 
 
Although the subject parcel is designated Mixed -use Commercial by the future land use plan, the 
only designation that is not for public property found in the surrounding area within 900 feet is 
Urban Residential.  
 
PlanCheyenne also supports flexibility to adjust to market demands and supports development of 
housing to meet demands, preferably with a variety of types and with access to amenities. 
Housing is needed in the Cheyenne, and this location has access to amen ities and would allow for 
a variety of housing options.  
 
The proposed zone change  complies with review criterion 1. 
 
2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering 
existing zoning, the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies 
created under the guidance of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2) 
 
The proposed NR -3 zone would only be on this property, unless a concurrent zone change 
request to NR -3 is approved on land to the southwest . To the north of the property is zoned as A -
1 and hosts a utility station on the land. The Harmony Meadows PUD is located directly to the 
south and contains single family  dwellings. East of this parcel  is land zone Public which is currently 
vacant. To the east of this property is County Urban Residential Low-Density zone and contains 
single family dwellings – however that property is currently going through the County pockets 
annexation process a nd is proposed to have city MR Medium -density Residential zoning upon 
annexation.  
 
The property is adjacent to major roadways which is not as optimal for low -intensity 
development. While not adjacent to this property, high -intensity residential development is 
nearby to the northeast  and west.

High-intensity residential development is compatible with the surrounding area’s anticipated 
development patterns.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2. 
   
3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards 
and uses of the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject 
property for development according to the standards and uses of the proposed 
zoning district (UDC 2.2.1.d.3) 
 
NR-3 would allow for a variety of residential development  types. With the location of the parcel 
along two roads that have high volume of traffic, the allowance for high density residential 
developments by right would be appropriate. The existing zoning district is AG, which is meant to 
be used for agricultural uses or as a holding zone. Most urban zones would give more options for 
development than the current AG zone.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3. 
 
4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed 
according to the Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed 
zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4) 
 
No specific development is proposed with this zone change. Any streets and accesses would be 
required to meet standards. As an infill property constrained by major roadways, topography, and 
a major utility station, exceptions to typical block standards would be expected for any 
development proposal.   Infrastructure (streets and u tilities) exist in the area . Water and sewer will 
likely need to be extended to the area.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4. 
 
5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of 
the proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific 
standards. (UDC 2.2.1.d.5) 
 
The NR-3 district is intended to be used for high -density, urban residential use with consistent 
neighborhood charact er. The regulations are meant to protect the neighborhood character of the 
district and protect the primarily residential use of land.  
 
The applicability to areas where more compact neighborhood development patterns are desired, 
specifically the Urban Residential category in the Cheyenne Comprehensive Plan.  
 
Typically, this district is located no more than ¼ mile or up to four blocks from any existing or 
planned Activity Center. Isolated application of this district without adjacency to activity center 
should be avoided so that significant concentrations of de nsity do not exist without walking 
access to non -residential uses and quality urban amenities. This district should be supported by a 
highly connected street network with street design types that have high degree of pedestrian 
amenities.

. This parcel is not located near any activity center , but has commercial development to the east 
and schools nearby. Pedestrian amenities including the Greenway are in the area.   
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5. 
    
6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for 
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options 
if any specific development proposed in association with the application is not 
executed after approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6) 
 
The NR-3 zone would limit this parcel to strictly residential development . The previous zoning of 
AG severely limited development, as the AG is an agricultural or holding zone.  The NR-3 provides 
multiple paths to development .    
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6. 
 
7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7) 
 
No major comments received in regard to the proposed zone.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7. 
 
8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare. 
(UDC 2.2.1.d.8) 
 
City staff is satisfied that this proposed zone change would not adversely affect the public  health, 
safety, or general welfare.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 8.
