MEMORANDUM 
 
TO: Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council 
FROM: Erin Johnson, Transit Administrator  
DATE: April 8, 2026 
SUBJECT: Consideration of #9925 SPARE contract modification for Transit dispatch and 
paratransit software services 
 
 
Requests for quotes were solicited for Transit Fixed route and ridership tracking for all Fixed 
Route Transit Vehicles for the City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne transit Program.  
 
Five (5) quotes were received in response to the request. The quotes were received from: 
1) SPARE Labs Vancouver, BC 
2) Switftly San Francisco, CA 
3) Peak Transit Orlando, FL 
4) ETA Transit Boca Raton, FL 
5) TripShot/Passio/Transit Technologies Knoxville, TN 
 
The quotes were reviewed by Erin Johnson, Transit Administrator, and the recommendation is to 
move to modify the current contract with SPARE Labs. SPARE Labs is the current provider and 
the staff at CTP are happy with the services provided. 
 
The current contract includes pricing with the SPARE Software Platform, Spare AI Base 
Platform fee and Monthly Spare AI Outcomes with an annual renewal period. The modification 
would include Spare Fixed Route Platform, Base dedicated to four (4) vehicles and the ability to 
add more at a fixed cost and the Spare One app that would include both the fixed and 
paratransit/on-demand service for the public. The modification wud renew on an annual basis at 
a 5% increase each year.  
 
The current contract amount is $54,525.00 (annually with a 5% increase). The addition of the 
Spare Fixed Route Platform and one time implementation fee at $78,000.00, for a grand total of 
$134,711.00
