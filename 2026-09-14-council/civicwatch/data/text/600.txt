BACKGROUND: 
 
This is a request to amend the Sweetgrass Planned Unit Development (PUD). This is an amendment to 
make changes regarding the community park and add a Land Use Amendment process. The current PUD 
has very specifically designated park parameters and the proposed amendment increases flexibility in the 
provisions of park land.   
 
The PUD language proposed to be amended is as follows: 
(Section 1) 
E. Community Parks and Parcel Parks  
1. Based on the maximum number of units allowed in this PUD, to maintain the desired Level of 
Service for the anticipated number of dwelling units across this PUD, 5.8 acres of Community 
Parks per 1,000 residents (at 2.25 residents per unit), the Sweetgrass PUD is required to set aside a 
minimum of 67.2 acres of usable land.  
2. The PUD shall dedicate to the City a 45-acre Community Park within or immediately adjacent to 
the first phases of development, and is within 1.5 miles of more than 90% of the dwellings 
proposed in this PUD. Subsequent public park dedication(s) to fulfill the remaining required area 
will be subject to applicable PUD regulations for the Community Park and will be determined by 
the rate at which the PUD develops, acceptance by the City of the proposed dedication , and funds 
available for appropriate improvements.  
3. P rivate parks are not generally  required within the Sweetgrass PUD except as stipulated in 
Subsections 4.A.6 & 4.A.11; nothing in this PUD is intended to prevent the developer of any 
individual Parcel from creating a private park. 
  
(Section 2.C) 
4. Community Park: Dedicated open space to be owned and maintained by the City of Cheyenne 
as a public park to serve the residents of the PUD and surrounding neighborhoods. The ±60-acre 
Tract of land initial Community Park dedication (see Subsection 1.E above) shall be located at the 
southeast corner of Loving Trail and Murray Rd. (within Parcel designation “A+H”). 
 
(Section 4.B.) 
9-1: Community Park [O/S-P] Specific Standards: (Parcel A+H) 
 
a. Intent: The A+H Parcel is intended primarily for a Community Park and other Civic or 
Recreational uses and a minimum of 50% of the Parcel is intended to be dedicated to the City of

include regional stormwater detention facilities and trails to serve the PUD and connect to LCCC. 
Detailed program and design of public facilities will be determined by the City. 
b. General Standards:  
i. All provisions of Section 3.B.1 through 3.B.3 above.  
ii. Table 9 AND the following:

Administrative Adjustment procedures outlined in UDC§2.3.4. Such amendments could include 
text amendments that do not substantially alter the nature of the PUD; including, but not limited 
to, adjustments to Parcel boundaries, trail alignments, and other similar items. 
2. Major Amendments: In the event that the Director finds that a proposed amendment is 
outside of the minor adjustments described within UDC Section 2.3.4.b, the Committee is 
authorized to act on behalf of Sweetgrass owners and residents pursuant to UDC§5.6.2.h. 
3. Land Use Amendments (Zoning): When development conditions or Parcel circumstances 
have changed with respect to the PUD Master Plan, a landowner or the Committee may submit an 
application pursuant to UDC§2.2.1 to change up to 25% of the Land Use Designation for the 
Parcel. Such applications shall meet the review criteria of UDC§2.2.1 and must receive Committee 
Endorsement prior to submittal to the City of Cheyenne.  
Amendments of more than 25% of the PUD Parcel area shall be considered Major and subject to 
PUD§1.H.2 above. All requests will be evaluated by the Committee based on the scope and 
character of the request and at the Committee’s sole discretion, amendments of less than 25% of 
the PUD Parcel area may be considered Major and shall follow PUD§1.H.2 above.  
 
Proposed changes include: 
1. Requiring the initial dedication to be 45-acres minimum (instead of the full 67.2 acres 
recommended by the analysis). 
2. Allowing future dedications to make up the difference as mutually agreeable. 
3. Allow for private recreation to count toward the minimum recreation area. 
4. Add a Land Use Amendment procedure.

Regarding 1 and 2 above, generally City staff is amenable to the reduction in area of the initial park.  Staff’s 
determination is that Community Parks must be a minimum of 45 acres. However, Staff would emphasize 
that current capabilities would make multiple parks difficult to develop and maintain and the most likely 
scenario (according to current trends) is that the only public park in Sweetgrass would be the initial park. 
The Annexation agreement also encourages only one park to be developed. Sweetgrass ha s a long-term 
anticipated build-out (circa 50 years), so adding flexibility to future City officials would be appropriate in 
case the future City would like to own and maintain another park in this area of the community. 
 
Regarding 3, City Staff is amendable to the proposed language to allow up to 50% of the Community Park 
parcel to be developed privately. The proposed amendment specifies that the total Public Park area can 
not be less than 45 acres.  
 
Regarding 4, City Staff is satisfied with the proposed language as it is more restrictive than the UDC’s 
Zoning Map Amendment.  
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the final Planned Unit Development 
(PUD), the Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.2.d – a detailed analysis of 
which is below. No conditions may be added to a final PUD.  The purpose of a final PUD review is to 
ensure compliance with the approved Preliminary PUD, its conditions of approval, and to establish the 
Ordinance officially changing the zoning to the PUD zone district.   
 
1) The application meets all threshold requirements for application of the PUD 
District or Form-based Code standards. (UDC 2.2.2.d.1) 
 
When a new PUD is established the UDC requires the PUD to meet certain thresholds; however, 
this application is proposing amending the text of an existing PUD, not to establish or expand the 
PUD.  
 
The Unified Development Code requires every PUD Ordinance to include 12 specific elements 
(outlined in UDC 5.6.2.f). These elements are: 
1. Uses by right and location 
2. Uses permitted by Board approval and location 
3. Lot and building standards (including frontage, size, coverage, building height, and 
setbacks/build-to lines) 
4. Building design standards 
5. Landscape, buffering, and screening requirements 
6. Common open space or facilities and design types 
7. Signage requirements and regulations 
8. Parking requirements 
9. Development performance standards 
10. Proposed ownership of common facilities  
11. A statement on how proposed deviations from otherwise required standards support the 
proposed plan, and result in design excellence and outstanding public amenity that is 
otherwise not allowable under general zoning standards 
12. Any other requirements and restrictions

The existing PUD addresses all of the above items and the amendments continue to address the 
requirements. 
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
 
2) The application presents clear and specific criteria on how the property and 
subsequent development will be reviewed, regulated and modified consistent with 
these regulations. (UDC 2.2.2.d.2) 
 
The proposed amendment to the PUD adds additional language to sections related to the 
Community Park. It provides additional clarification on what will be dedicated to the City and the 
ability for there to be multiple parks. This amendment also allows for a portion of the community 
park to be privately developed.   
 
An additional amendment process is also added to allow amendments to the land uses in the 
PUD. These amendments would need to follow the requirements of UDC2.2.1. The proposed 
language also restricts the area that can be change through this process to 25%. Anything over 
25% would need to be submitted as a Major amendment.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
  
3) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical 
patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.2.2.d.3) 
 
PlanCheyenne has several designations for this area. The future land use map designates a 
majority of the development as Urban Residential, but also has designations of Mixed -use 
Commercial, Open Space and Parks, and Mixed-use Employment.  
 
The Urban Residential category is intended to develop with largely residential uses with some 
neighborhood-serving commercial uses in developing areas. The proposed amendments do not 
affect the areas designated for commercial or residential. 
 
Open Space and Parks is intended for regional parks and open space areas. The proposed 
changes affect the community park area. A community park will still be dedicated to the City, but 
the size will be reduced and a portion would be allowed to be privately developed. It does not 
change the use as open space or park area.    
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
 
4) The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to 
existing and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration 
of current and future streets, open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or 
facilities. (UDC 2.2.2.d.4) 
 
The PUD amendment proposes changes to park dedication and the City’s responsibility for 
developing the community park. Keeping amenities scaled to available funding is good 
stewardship, so reducing the park size to a more manageable size for City Staff is appropriate.

However, privatization of recreational areas can create challenges for the opportunity for the 
community to use recreational spaces. Reduction in minimum park area and adding flexibility to 
have multiple parks is appropriate. 
 
Areas of the PUD to the northeast of the portion of the PUD subject to this amendment have 
already been developed. These properties were likely marketed and occupied based on the PUD 
plan to have a park nearby.  
 
This proposal does not propose changes to any street networks blocks or public utilities or 
facilities.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
 
5) The application meets the purposes, intent, and all applicable standards in Article 
4. (UDC 2.2.2.d.5) 
 
The amendment does meet the purpose, intent and all applicable standards in Article 4. This 
amendment does not change any accesses or street networks in this area. 
 
The application complies with this review criterion.

A PUD is a specialized zoning Ordinance that becomes the official zoning of the selected area of the 
community. Once published, the Ordinance becomes law. Conditions or modifications must be resolved 
prior to final Governing Body approval.

AMENDMENT TO SWEETGRASS PLANED UNIT DEVELOPMENT 
Amending Subsection 1.E Community Parks and Parcel Parks, AND Subsection 2.C DEFINITIONS 
“Community Park”, AND Subsection 4.B.9-1 9-1: Community Park [O/S-P] Specific Standards; 
clarifying private vs. public park and recreation areas and ownership/maintenance provisions; 
AND Adding Subsection 1.H(3) PUD Amendments “Land Use Amendments (Zoning)”; 
to allow for Zone Change Applications within the PUD 
 
On behalf of the Sweetgrass Planned Unit Development (PUD), and by the authority of the Sweetgrass 
Master HOA, the Declarant and Applicant proposes to amend the PUD Regulating Document as follows: 
 
A. Subsection E Community Parks and Parcel Parks of Section 1 of the Sweetgrass PUD, is amended as set 
out in this section: 
2. The PUD shall dedicate to the City a 45 -acre Community Park  within or immediately adjacent to the first 
phases of development, and is within 1.5 miles of more than 90% of the dwellings proposed in this PUD. 
Subsequent public park dedication(s) to fulfill the remaining required area will be subject to applicable PUD 
regulations for the Community Park and will be determined by the rate at which the PUD develops, acceptance 
by the City of the proposed dedication, and funds available for appropriate improvements. 
3. Private parks are not generally required within the Sweetgrass PUD except as stipulated in Subsections 4.A.6 & 
4.A.11; nothing in this PUD is intended to prevent the developer of any individual Parcel from creating a private park. 
 
B. Subsection C: Definitions, of Section 2, of the Sweetgrass PUD, is appended as set out in this section: 
Community Park: Dedicated open space to be owned and maintained by the City of Cheyenne as a public 
park to serve the residents of the PUD and surrounding neighborhoods.  The ±60-acre Tract of land  initial 
Community Park dedication (see Subsection 1.E above) shall be located at the southeast corner of Loving Trail and 
Murray Rd. (within Parcel designation “A+H”). 
 
C. Item 9-1 (a and c): Community Park  [O/S-P] Specific Standards: (Parcel A+H), of Subsection 4.B, of the 
Sweetgrass PUD, is amended as set out in this section: 
a. Intent: The A+H Parcel is intended primarily for a Community Park and other Civic or Recreational uses and a 
minimum of 50% of the Parcel is intended to be dedicated to the City of Cheyenne to be developed by the City as a 
public park to serve south Cheyenne. The park should include regional stormwater detention facilities and trails to 
serve the PUD and connect to LCCC. Detailed program and design of public facilities will be determined by the City. 
b. General Standards: 
i. All provisions of Section 3.A, B, C, & E  above. 
ii. Table 9 AND the following: 
(a) Athletic fields, stands, and gathering areas shall a minimum of 100’ from any residential lot. 
(b) Parking lots and drives shall be located a minimum of 50’ from any residential lot. 
c. Alternative Uses: 
i. In the event the designated area or portion(s) thereof are not accepted by the City of Cheyenne for Civic 
uses, or developed as open space, Community Park, or for recreational uses, the list of permitted uses in said 
portion(s) shall expand to include those allowed in the MDR – Medium Density Residential District per PUD§4.A.9  
ii. Up to 50% of the Community Park Parcel(s) [O/S -P] may be developed privately for allowed recreational 
or open space use(s) as long as the total Public Park area is not less than 45 acres. In such case, the area of the 
privately developed recreational use(s) may be counted towards the total Community Park/open space dedication 
required by PUD§1.E.1, however such area(s) shall be privately owned and maintained.. 
 
D. Subsection H: PUD Amendments of Section 1, is appended as set out in this section: 
3. Land Use Amendments (Zoning): When development conditions or Parcel circumstances have changed with 
respect to the PUD Master Plan, a landowner or the Committee may submit an application pursuant to UDC§2.2.1 
to change up to 25% of the Land Use Designation for the Parcel.  Such applications shall meet the review criteria 
of UDC§2.2.1 and must receive Committee Endorsement prior to submittal to the City of Cheyenne. 
Amendments of more than 25% of the PUD Parcel area shall be considered Major and subject to PUD§1.H.2 above. 
All requests will be evaluated by the Committee based on the scope and character of the request and at the 
Committee’s sole discretion, amendments of less than 25% of the PUD Parcel area may be considered Major and 
shall follow PUD§1.H.2 above.
