Four (4) Proposers responded to Request for Proposal (RFP) #5-25, for the Landfill Compliance 
Monitoring Services project. 
 
The scope of work will include groundwater monitoring, landfill gas monitoring, data analysis, 
and compliance reporting. 
 
The proposals were received from:  
1. Broadbent & Associates, Inc 
2. Sanborn, Head & Associates, Inc. 
3. Terracon Consultants, Inc. 
4. Trihydro Corporation 
 
Upon review of the proposals by the evaluation panel, the group has recommended acceptance of 
the proposal submitted by Trihydro Corporation, for a project award amount of $281,007.00 over 
a three (3) year term. 
 
Basis for award was based on a Qualifications based review and scoring of proposals.
