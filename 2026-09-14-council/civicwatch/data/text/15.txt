This Agreement is between Laramie County, Wyoming (“County”), 310 West 19 th Street, 
Suite 300, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82003 -0608 and the City of Cheyenne, Office of Youth 
Alternatives, (“Contractor”), 1328 Talbot Court, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001 . The parties hereto 
do mutually agree as set forth below. 
I. PURPOSE 
The purpose of this Agreement is to procure from Contractor crisis shelter services on 
behalf of County and for which the Contractor will be paid by the County. These are 
services that could otherwise be rendered directly by the County for the benefit of the 
public. This Agreement does not constitute a grant. It is a contract to purchase services. 
These services are further described in the proposal which is attached hereto as 
Attachment A and incorporated herein. 
 
II. TERM 
This Agreement shall commence on July 1, 2026, and shall remain in full force and 
effect through June 30, 2027 , unless terminated in accordance with the terms and 
conditions set forth herein. 
 
III. RESPONSIBILITIES OF COUNTY 
County shall pay to Contractor an amount to not to exceed Ninety -Five Thousand, 
Seven Hundred Ninety-Nine Dollars ($95,799.00). Payment will be made in a series of 
twelve (12) monthly payments, with each such monthly payment being for one twelfth 
(1/12) of the total amount of payment under this contract. Payments may be suspended 
for any period which services are not rendered. Each such monthly payment will be 
made only after receipt of Contractor’s monthly invoice to County. Payment shall be 
in accordance with W.S. § 16-6-602 (as amended). 
 
IV. RESPONSIBILITES OF CONTRACTOR 
a. Contractor shall provide on -call crisis center services on a twenty -four-hour per 
day, seven day per week basis. The Contractor may use existing or specifically 
hired staff to provide these services as long as licensure and other professional 
standards are met and services as provided as set forth in Attachment A. 
 
b. Contractor shall provide with their monthly invoice a report of the number of 
juveniles served during the month and cumulatively by the Contractor’s services.

ATTACHMENT A 
 
SERVICES TO BE PROVIDED BY YOUTH ALTERNATIVES TO THE LARAMIE 
COUNTY SHERIFF’S DEPARTMENT AT THE JUVENILE SERVICE CENTER 
 
The proposed agreement between Laramie County and the Office of Youth Alternatives for the 
provision of screening, counseling and supportive services to youth referred to the Laramie County 
Juvenile Services Center – Youth Crisis Center (JSC-YCC). 
 
The services would include: 
 
1. 24/7/365 availability for crisis support 
2. Phone assessment 
3. Crisis meeting assessment 
4. Support services for youth and families redirected from the JSC-YCC 
5. Family counseling and support for families in crisis 
6. Individual and family goal development for admitted youth 
7. Discharge planning 
8. Conduct discharge meetings 
9. Provide follow-up services for discharged youth, including referral to service providers if 
necessary 
10. Provide on-going family counseling or referral to appropriate service providers 
 
To provide these services, Youth Alternatives will use its current on-call system. Coverage would be 
provided by a family counselor hired to support these services and by other Youth Alternatives’ staff. 
 
Youth Alternatives will be responsible for assuring that all services are provided by practitioners 
with appropriate licensing or under licensed supervision. 
 
When Youth Alternatives receives a call for services, the on-call staff will contact the JSC-Youth 
Crisis Center to determine eligibility and bed space availability. Those situations involving physical 
altercations may not meet eligibility requirements for the Youth Crisis Center, but should still be 
discussed, as there may be mitigating factors. Parent or youth referrals in which there is a clear and 
imminent danger of physical altercation law enforcement should be contacted; if not already present. 
 
The procedures to be used to refer juveniles to the JSC - Youth Crisis Center and to other community 
services will be jointly agreed upon by Youth Alternatives and the JSC -Youth Crisis Center. The 
procedures are broadly described in “Admission Flow for Referrals to Juvenile Services Crisis 
Shelter” drafted by Youth Alternatives and approved by the Laramie County Juvenile Services 
Center.  
 
Both Youth Alternatives and the Laramie County Juvenile Services Center will keep the Laramie 
County Board of County Commissioners informed of changes in services to be provided under the 
proposed agreement or any conflict over the terms or requirements of the agreement.
