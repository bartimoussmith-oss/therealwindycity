BACKGROUND:
The applicant is requesting approval of a preliminary plat for roughly 0.50 acres of land. The proposed plat
would subdivide the parcel into 17 Lots to allow fee simple ownership of the individual townhouse units. This 
development would be considered Cottage Lots per the UDC with the approval of this plat. A concurrent zone 
change application has also been submitted to change the zoning from MR to NR-2 (PUDC-26-7).
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING:
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Preliminary Plat, the Commission
must make the following findings required in UDC 2.1.2.d – a detailed analysis is below:
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical 
patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.2.d.1)
On the future land use map found in PlanCheyenne, the subject land and surrounding non-public 
area has been designated as Urban Residential. The Urban Residential designation encourages a 
range of residential uses. The proposed plat is to adjust the ownership model of a townhouse-like 
development.

Community Facility Fees (4.2):
For fees based on acreage, the area of the plat is pro-rated to the nearest tenth of an acre. 
Lands reserved as unbuildable (for right-of-way, detention, parks, remainder lots, etc), are 
exempt from fees. Park fees are calculated at $700 per acre. All fees are due at the time of 
building permit.
Per UDC Section 4.2.5, Table 4-2, Enhancement Fees are payable at the issuance of a building 
permit for each structure.
Residential Enhancement Fee $400 per dwelling unit
Transportation Network (4.3):
This plat is for an existing development in an area that is already developed. No additional 
streets are proposed.
Open Space (4.4):
Open space is required at 750sqft per unit or 8% of the gross development area, whichever is 
less. However, due to the size of the property, plat-level open space is not required. The plat 
includes a common lot which serves as a common open area.
Required Improvements (4.5):
This plat appears to be able to provide all required utilities to the lots within this 
development.
