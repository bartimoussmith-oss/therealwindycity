SUBJECT: Resolution for WYDOT Transportation Alternatives Program 
Application for Pershing Boulevard and Windmill Road Intersection Project.  
 
 
Please find the attached proposed resolution and document for your approval and 
execution. The resolution is summarized below for your information. Please feel free 
to contact me prior to the council meeting or come by my office, room 311, if you 
have any questions or concerns about the enclosed packet.  
 
Purpose of Resolution: 
The purpose of this resolution is to meet the requirements of the Wyoming 
Department of Transportation (WYDOT), Transportation Alternatives Program 
(TAP) grant application to have a resolution approved by council authorizing the 
submission of the application.  
 
This project will be located at Pershing Blvd and Windmill Road near East High 
School and focus on providing much needed safety upgrades to the intersection 
including redesign of the roadway for better traffic flow, traffic signal upgrades and 
new ADA components to improve pedestrian safety.  
 
If awarded, the Federal Transportation Administration (FTA) via WYDOT will 
provide $868,000 in funding support for this project. The remaining balance of 
$91,222 will come from the 5th Penny Sales Tax.  
 
This application was approved by City of Cheyenne City Council in 2025 but was 
not awarded any funding by WYDOT. City of Cheyenne Engineers Office and the 
Grants Office have been working closely together to improve the original application 
and gather letters of support for the project.

ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE SUBMISSION OF A

ROAD INTERSECTION PROJECT.” 
 
WHEREAS, the Transportation Alternatives Program (“TAP”), administered by the 
Wyoming Department of Transportation, is a federal funding program that provides financial 
assistance for eligible transportation -related projects, including pedestrian and bicyc le facilities, 
safe routes to school infrastructure, and other community-based transportation improvements; 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne desires that the City participate 
in TAP to obtain funding assistance for the construction of safety and intersection geometric 
improvements for the Pershing Boulevard and Windmill Road Intersection Project (the “Project”); 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne recognizes the need for the 
Project; 
 
WHEREAS, TAP requires compliance with applicable federal funding criteria, and the 
City of Cheyenne agrees to satisfy all such requirements; 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne acknowledges that, if funded, the Project must be 
completed on or before August 30, 2027; 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne is requesting a grant in the amount of eight hundred 
sixty-eight thousand dollars ($868,000.00) from TAP for the cost of constructing the Project; 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne agrees to provide ninety -one thousand two hundred 
twenty-two dollars ($91,222.00) from the 5th Penny Sales Tax fund for the required local match; 
and 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne acknowledges that TAP is funded on a reimbursement 
basis, requiring that all invoices be paid in full by the City of Cheyenne prior to reimbursement, 
and further acknowledges that failure to comply with this requirement may result in cancellation 
of the award and the repayment of all TAP funds. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that a funding application requesting eight hundred sixty -eight 
thousand dollars ($868,000.00) in Federal TAP funding is authorized for submission to the 
Wyoming Department of Transportation, Transportation Alternatives Program, for consideration 
to assist in funding the Project. 
 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Mayor and City Clerk are authorized to execute 
all documents necessary or appropriate for submission of the grant application and acceptance of 
the grant award, if awarded.
