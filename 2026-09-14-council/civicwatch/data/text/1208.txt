 ______________________________________________ 
 Two (2) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.   
 
The Two (2) bids were received from: 
 
1. Floyd's Truck Center Inc.  Cheyenne, Wyoming. 
2. McCandless Truck Center, LLC.  Cheyenne, Wyoming. 
 
The bids were reviewed by Austin Reiner (San itation), Bryan Bell (Fleet Maintenance), Craig 
LaVoy (Public Works), and Vicki Nemecek (Public Works).   
 
Their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from Floyd’s Truck Center. 
 
The Bidding Documents contain a Surcharge and Escalator – Special Provision, that allows for 
the review of surcharges related to the following items:  Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) 
and/or Body surcharges applied to the cost of the vehicles after the bid opening date & Model 
year escalators.  The Surcharge & Escalator Contingency, is for an amount not-to-exceed Fifteen 
Percent (15%), of the total value of  requested items on the Bid. 
 
The total requested bid award is for $232,698.85 (Bid Option 1, 2026 Freightliner 114SD Plus, 
with NMTC Roll Off Body).  
 
This includes the 30 Yard R olloff Truck ($244,846.83), an Extended Warranty for Engine & 
Aftertreatment for 5 Years / 100K Miles ($1,125.00), and acceptance of the trad e-in offer ($-
50,000.00), PLUS 15% Contingency ($36,727.02).
