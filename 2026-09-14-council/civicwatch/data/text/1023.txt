MEMO 
To: Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council 
From: Seth Lloyd AICP, Senior Planner 
Subject: PLTA-25-8 – Amending UDC signs for Window Signs and Prohibited Signs – substitute 
Date: June 11, 2025 
 
Background:  
An Ordinance amending UDC 6.5 Signs was introduced on May 27, 2025 and discussed at the 
meeting of the Governing Body on June 9, 2025. In that meeting, a motion was made to amend the 
Ordinance with respect to window signs. Staff has created a substitute to reflect that amendment 
with one minor grammatical change. 
 
Substitute Language:  
• “Window Signs” to read as follows:  
“Window Signs shall be allowed in any street-level window. Window signs shall not exceed more 
than 25% of the window area between 2’ and 8’ above grade. be counted toward the allowed 
wall sign area. If 100% of the window area is to be covered, it must utilize a material that allows 
light to penetrate into the building. 
(black text is originally proposed UDC language)

ENTITLED: "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING  PORTIONS OF THE UNIFIED 
DEVELOPMENT CODE  REGARDING SIGNAGE , TO INCREASE 
ALLOWANCES FOR WINDOW SIGNS AND FURTHER CLARIFYING 
PROHIBITED SIGNS." 
 
 BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, 
WYOMING: 
 
Section 1.  That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne finds that the text amendment 
to the Unified Development Code (UDC)  that is the subject of this ordinance meets the  review 
criteria specified in Section 2.4.1.d of the UDC. 
 
Section 2.  That on May 19, 2025, the Planning Commission conducted a public hearing, 
reviewed the proposed text amendment, and recommended approval of said text amendment. 
 
Section 3 .  That Table 6 -16 of Section 6.5.4, Sign Allowances , and Section  6.5.7, 
Prohibited Signs , of Chapter 6.5, Signs, of Article 6, Design Regulations , of the Unified 
Development Code of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, are hereby amended as set out in this 
section (the amendment in context of the entirety of Section 6.5.4 Sign Allowances is attached as 
Exhibit A): 
 
TABLE 6-16: NON-RESIDENTIAL DISTRICT SIGN ALLOWANCES 
Temporary Signs 
▪ Up to 2 per lot. 
▪ No single sign shall be more than 32 square feet. 
▪ Signs may be placed up to 45 days prior to and 10 days following the 
event or distinct time period addressed by the sign. 
Building Signs 
▪ Wall Signs up to 20% of the wall area, but no more than 400 square 
feet total. The NB, MUR , and MUB districts shall count only 10% of 
the street facing walls up to the height of 20’ in calculating the Wall 
Sign allowance. Only one Wall Sign per each 50’ of building 
frontage is permitted in the CBD. 
▪ 1 Projecting Sign per street frontage, projecting no more than 5’ from 
the surface, and no greater than 32’ in area. Projecting Signs shall 
have a clearance of at least 10’, and shall project no higher than the 
highest point of the building. 
Ground Signs 
▪ 1 Freestanding or Low-profile Sign per street frontage or building 
complex. The height and area of Freestanding Ground Signs shall be 
based on Table 6-17. The area shall be limited to 8 square feet for 
each 25’ of lot frontage up to a maximum of 80 square feet. 
▪ Incidental signs may be provided at 12 square feet per acre, and no 
more than 6 square feet per sign. 
Page 2 of 4 
 
Pedestrian Signs 
▪ Awning Signs / Canopy Signs provided the awning or canopy 
provides at least 8’ clearance and the sign is limited to 3 square feet 
per linear foot of awning or canopy. 
▪ Hanging Signs shall be allowed one per every 50 linear feet of 
building frontage, or one for every building entrance, whichever is 
greater. Hanging Signs shall not exceed 12 square feet in area. 
▪ Window Signs shall be allowed in any window. Window signs shall 
be counted toward the allowed wall sign area. If 100% of the window 
area is to be covered, it must utilize a material that allows light to 
penetrate into the building. 
Neighborhood / 
District 
Identification 
Signs 
▪ Up to 2 per each street intersection with an arterial street and no more 
than 32 square feet for each sign. In industrial business parks in the 
MUE, BP, and HI zone districts, the allowance for neighborhood 
identification signs may be up to 200 square feet per sign. The area 
allowance for each arterial intersection may be allocated to more 
frequent signs at smaller intersections within the neighborhood. 
▪ No sign or structure supporting the sign shall exceed a height of 6’. In 
industrial business parks in the MUE, BP, and HI zone districts, sign 
structures may be up to 20’ high, and neighborhood identification 
signs may be placed on prominent structures having a non-sign 
primary function (such as a water tower, cell tower, electrical facility, 
or similar). 
 
6.5.7 Prohibited Signs 
 
The following specific application of sign types are prohibited in all zoning districts, regardless of 
whether they meet the sign allowances of this Section: 
 
a. Signs that have been abandoned for sixty (60) days or longer; 
 
b. Signs imitating or resembling government signs for traffic direction or any other public safety 
symbol, unless approved by the City for the purpose of traffic direction or public safety  in 
accordance with Chapter 10.20 of the Municipal Code of the City of Cheyenne; 
 
c. Signs placed on vehicles or trailers for the primary purpose of displaying the sign(s), when 
such vehicle or trailer is parked and visible from the public right-of-way and is not used in the 
normal day-to-day operations of the owner or a business; 
 
d. Signs placed in or encroaching upon a public right-of-way, except: 
1. Awnings, projecting canopies, and marquees which comply with the provisions of these 
regulations and are at least 8’ above the sidewalk; 
2. Bus stop benches and shelters, displaying signs on the backs of the benches, approved by 
the City through a request for proposal (RFP) process and a contract with a provider of the 
benches;  
3. Portable signs which comply with these regulations; and 
Page 3 of 4 
 
4. Signs located in the right-of-way directly between the property line  of the owner of the 
sign and the curb or edge of the roadway, with the review and approval of the City Engineer 
through applicable agreements or permits regarding use of the right-of-way. Any approved 
sign comes with the understanding that roadway or utility improvements may affect the 
sign and if the sign is required to be maintained, moved, removed or replaced it may be 
required to be done at the expense of the owner of the sign . If approved, such signs must 
meet code requirements for a sign placed 0’ to 5’ from the right-of-way. 
 
e. Building signs that extend vertically above the highest portion of the roof line or parapet, 
whichever is higher.  
 
f. Portable signs, except that one sign per building entrance may be allowed as a permitted 
Pedestrian Sign in any Mixed -Use, Commercial or Industrial zone district, subject to the 
following: 
1. The sign consists of non -illuminated chalkboards or similar devices mounted on tripods, 
easels or pedestals;  
2. The sign shall not exceed 12 square feet and shall not exceed 5’ height; 
3. The sign shall be located no more than 25’ from the principal entrance to the building and 
removed from the premises during non-business hours; and 
4. If located in the right -of-way it shall be located in a  manner that allows at least 6 ’ clear 
area for pedestrian flow and meet all sight line triangle requirements. 
 
g. Signs on corner lots within the sight line triangle, except:  
1. Signs which do not exceed 2.5’ above street grade, and  
2. Freestanding signs which have at least 12’ of clearance above street grade and which do 
not have a support pole larger than12” in diameter. 
 
h. Animated signs, except in the CB, BP, LI, or HI districts. 
 
i. Off-premises signs located on private property, unless approved as a billboard or as part of a 
temporary use permit (for off-premises temporary signage).  
 
j. Signs that are obscene or do not concern lawful activity. 
 
Section 4.  Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases of this ordinance are 
severable. If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase of this ordinance is declared 
unconstitutional or otherwise unlawful, such declaration shall not affect any of the remaining 
sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases. 
 
 Section 5.  This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon approval and publication.
