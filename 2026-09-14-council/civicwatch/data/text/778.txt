 
 
 
RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION REPEALING RESOLUTION NO. 6479 AND 
DIRECTING THE RELISTING OF THE CHEYENNE AUXILIARY 
PUMPING STATION LOCATED AT 1504 DILLON AVENUE, 
CHEYENNE, WYOMING  FOR SALE OR LEASE , WITH REVISED 
TERMS TO FACILITATE REDEVELOPMENT AND COMMUNITY 
BENEFIT." 
 
WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Auxiliary Pumping Station (Pump House) located at 1504 
Dillon Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming , is a historic structure of architectural and cultural  
significance within the Cheyenne community; 
 
WHEREAS, despite its historic value, the Pump House has long been abandoned and 
continues to pose ongoing security and public safety concerns; 
 
WHEREAS, while structural evaluations confirm that the building remains sound, 
restoration costs are estimated to exceed $5,000,000, and the City lacks available funding to 
complete such work; 
 
WHEREAS, the Governing Body previously adopted Resolution No. 6479 on July 14, 
2025, offering the property for sale or lease under certain restrictive redevelopment conditions 
and, as those requirements limited the pool of  potential purchasers or lessees, the Governing 
Body now seeks to broaden the terms to allow greater flexibility and encourage renewed interest 
while maintaining appropriate historic and aesthetic protections; 
 
WHEREAS, to promote investment, preserve the exterior character where feasible, and 
support downtown revitalization, the Governing Body finds it in the public interest to reoffer 
the property for sale or lease with greater flexibility for redevelopment whi le maintaining 
reasonable historic and aesthetic protections; and 
 
WHEREAS, it further serves the public interest to establish a clear timeframe for private 
acquisition or lease, after which the property may be demolished and repurposed for a beneficial 
community use consistent with Resolution No. 6479. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE 
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that  Resolution No. 6479, adopted by the Governing 
Body on July 14, 2025, is hereby repealed and in all respects superseded by this Resolution; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the City of Cheyenne shall relist the Pump House 
parcel for sale to the public consistent with the requirements of Wyoming Statute § 15-1-112(a) 
and with the terms and conditions set forth in this Resolution, or for lease under comparable 
terms, subject to final approval by the Governing Body; 
 
 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that as a condition of sale or lease, the purchaser or 
lessee shall, to the greatest extent practicable, preserve the historic character and architectural 
integrity of the Pump House. Full demolition of the Pump House is prohibited. The Governing 
Body strongly prefers that the purchaser or lessee retain the original building footprint and 
structural shell of the existing structure  and that any additions or modifications be compatible 
in design, scale, and materials with the existing structure to preserve its historic aesthetic; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that as a condition of sale or lease, the purchaser or 
lessee shall, as a first priority, maintain the existing facade of the Pump House to preserve the 
structure’s historic aesthetic. If the purchaser or lessee demonstrates to the satisfaction of the 
City that full preservation is infeasible, the purchaser or lessee shall reuse materials from the 
existing facade to the greatest extent practicable and, where reuse is not possible, use materials 
and design features substantially simil ar to the original structure to maintain a comparable 
historic appearance; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that upon sale, the deed conveying the property shall 
include a restrictive covenant running with the land requiring that the purchaser, and all 
subsequent owners, maintain the historic features and aesthetic character of the struct ure as 
represented in the purchaser’s accepted bid and as incorporated into the purchase and sale 
agreement. The covenant shall prohibit demolition or material alteration of the structure without 
the prior written consent of the City and shall be binding upon all future owners of the property 
and enforceable by the City; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that as a condition of sale  or lease, the City of 
Cheyenne shall secure any necessary and appropriate easements over the property and include 
any other terms and conditions in the sale  or lease  agreement as are necessary to promote 
investment in and redevelopment of the downtown area; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that, in the event of sale, all proceeds shall be deposited 
into the Revolving Real Property Special Revenue Fund to be expended consistent with 
Resolution No. 6039; 
 
 BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that if the Pump House has not been sold or leased, 
and no valid contract for sale or lease exists on or before March 27, 2026, the City shall cease 
further efforts to sell or lease the property and initiate procurement for demolition and site 
conversion in accordance with City policy.  All actions shall comply with applicable bidding 
and purchasing requirements, and the demolition contract shall be awarded to the lowest 
responsible bidder; and 
 
BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED, that any existing agreements between the City and other 
entities related to the Pump House parcel that impede or are no longer necessary for the sale, 
lease, or demolition of the structure shall be cancelled, allowed to expire, or c ompleted as 
appropriate to facilitate a timely and cost-effective listing or demolition of the property.
