REAL ESTATE PURCHASE AGREEMENT  
 
 THIS REAL ESTATE PURCHASE AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”) is made and 
entered into this ________ d ay of ____________________, 20 ____, by and between KT Land 
Holdings, LLC, a Utah limited liability company, with a principal address of 595 S. Riverwoods 
Parkway, Suite 400, Logan, Utah 84321, (the “Seller”), and the City of Cheyenne, a municipality 
organized under the laws of the State of Wyoming, 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 
82001 (the “City”). 
 
WITNESSETH: 
 
 WHEREAS, the City wishes to secure, for public purposes, the real property legally 
described as: 
 
Lot 1 Block One of Scenic Development 8th filing, a portion of Section 20, 
Township 14 North, Range 66 West of the 6th P.M. Laramie County, Wyoming. 
 
Approximately 8.37 acres, more or less 
 
together with the Seller’s rights, privileges, interests and appurtenances related to said real 
property, including, but not limited to, all right, title and interest in and to adjacent streets, alleys, 
and rights-of-way, all of which is hereinafter referred to as the “Real Property;” and 
 
WHEREAS, the City is acquiring the Real Property to support the development and 
operation of affordable housing, including through a long -term ground lease or similar 
development arrangement with a qualified developer; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City expects to use funds donated or otherwise provided to the City for 
the purpose of supporting affordable housing projects, and the City has determined, or will 
determine before Closing, that acquisition of the Real Property is consistent with the purpose, 
restrictions, and conditions applicable to such funds; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City has determined that acquisition of the Real Property for affordable 
housing purposes serves a valid public purpose; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Seller is the owner of the above -described Real Property which is 
generally located  at the c orner of Carlson Street and Converse Ave nue, City of Cheyenne, 
consisting of approximately 364,597 square feet, more or less; and 
 
 WHEREAS, the Seller is willing to sell to the City and the City is willing to purchase 
from Seller the Real Property upon the terms and conditions hereinafter stated; and 
 
 NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt of which is hereby 
acknowledged, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. Purchase and Sale. Seller agrees to sell to the City and the City agrees to purchase from 
Seller: 
 
 1.1. The Real Property, described above. 
 
 1.2. All licenses, permits and franchises issued by any governmental authority to or in 
favor of Seller and pertaining to the ownership, development or use of the Real Property, if any; 
and  
 
 1.3. All contract rights and general intangible rights pertaining to the ownership, 
development or use of the Real Property, if any; and 
 
 1.4. All improvements, structures and fixtures situated on the Real Property, which 
will be conveyed in an “as is” condition without any representations or warranties relating to 
their physical condition and without ascribing any value to them, except that nothing in this 
Section shall limit Seller’s representations, warranties, covenants, or obligations expressly set 
forth in this Agreement or the City’s right to terminate this Agreement as provided herein; and 
 
 1.5. All fixtures, equipment and other personal property of every kind and nature now 
installed, located, situated or used in, on, or about, or in connection with the operation, use, or 
enjoyment of the Real Property; and  
 
 1.6. All: (i) contracts or agreements affecting or relating to the Real Property, but only 
to the extent the City elects to take an assignment thereof; (ii) licenses, permits or similar 
documents; (iii) plans, drawings, specifications, surveys, engineering reports, and other technical 
reports or descriptions of the Real Property, to the extent Seller has any in its possession; and (iv) 
insurance contracts or policies which pertain to the Real Property, but only to the extent the City 
elects to take an assignment thereof. 
 
 1.7. Prior to closing, the Seller shall pay, when due and payable, all real property taxes 
and assessments asserted against the Real Property for the current year and for all prior years. All 
such taxes and assessments for the current year will be apportioned between the parties on a pro 
rata basis as of the date of closing. 
 
2. Purchase Price. 
 
 2.1. The purchase price to be paid by the City to Seller for the Real Property and all of 
the Seller’s interests in said Real Property, as described in Section 1 of this Agreement, is  two 
million six hundred forty-five thousand dollars ($2,645,000.00) (the “Purchase Price”), subject to 
proration and adjustments as provided herein. 
 
 2.2. The Purchase Price shall be paid to Seller or, with written authorization, to 
Seller’s designee, in full at the time of Closing, subject to pro -rations and adjustments, provided, 
however, that the City’s duty to pay the Purchase Price at Closing is subject to the requirement 
that the Seller complete an IRS Form W-9 and otherwise cooperate with the City in reporting the

Purchase Price and the other terms and conditions of this Agreement to the Internal Revenue 
Service as required by law. 
 
 2.3. Seller shall convey the Real Property to the City in accordance with the title 
requirements set forth in Section 3 and the closing conditions set forth in Section 6. The City 
shall have the right to cancel this Agreement if Seller is unable or otherwise fails, neglects, or 
refuses to convey title to the City in accordance with the terms and conditions of this Agreement. 
  
3. Title. 
 
 3.1. Prior to Closing, the City shall have the right to obtain a Title Commitment for the 
Real Property (the “Title Commitment”) at the City’s sole cost and expense.  In the event the 
Title Commitment shows that the Seller is not vested with good, marketable and insurable fee 
simple title to the Real Property, or that the Real Property is subject to liens, encumbrances, 
taxes or assessments which will not be discharged prior to Closing, the City shall have the right, 
in its sole and absolute discretion, to declare this Agreement null and void pursuant to Section 
3.2 of this Agreement.  Alternatively, the City may accept the Real Property, subject to such 
matters as set forth on Exhibit “A” attached to this Agreement (collectively, the “Permitted 
Exceptions”). The condition of title as described in this Section 3.1 shall be the standard of 
marketable title that Seller shall deliver at the Closing.  If Seller delivers such title and except for 
the Permitted Exceptions as set forth in Exhibit “A”, such title will be satisfactory and acceptable 
to the City under Sections 3.4 and 7.2 herein. 
 
 3.2. Prior to Closing, the City shall have the right to obtain a survey (the “Survey”) of 
the Real Property, which Survey shall be prepared at the City’s sole cost and expense.  Seller 
shall grant to the City, and its employees and agents, access to the Real Property sufficient for 
the preparation of the Survey, including without limitation:  (i) the actual dimensions of, and area 
within, the Real Property, (ii) the location of any easements, setback lines, encroachments, or 
overlaps thereon and the outside boundary lines of all improvements; (iii) all easements, setback 
lines, and other matters referred to on the Title Commitment; (iv) access to and from the Real 
Property from any publicly dedicated streets or roads; and (v) any areas within the Real Property 
that have been designated by any federal, state or other governmental agency or body as being 
subject to special, or increased flooding hazards.  If required by the City, the Survey shall be 
prepared and certified by a surveyor licensed in the State of Wyoming and, if required, shall be 
sufficient to allow issuance of an ALTA Extended Coverage Title Policy.

Section 3.3(a) and 3.3(b)), Seller shall deliver to the City the following: 
 
 (a) True, complete and legible copies of all documents referred to in the Title 
Commitment to the extent that any such document has not been filed  for record in the

4. Inspection Period. 
 
 4.1. The City shall have thirty (30) calendar days following the date of execution of 
this Agreement (the “Inspection Period”) to make such physical, zoning, marketing and other 
investigations, tests and studies, including but not limited to soil tests and borings, Phase I 
environmental surveys, utility availability surveys, and confirmation of subdivision map 
requirements and restrictions as the City may deem appropriate (“the City’s Inspections”). The 
City’s Inspections shall be at the City’s sole cost and expense. Subject to the Wyoming 
Governmental Claims Act, Wyo. Stat. § 1 -39-101 et seq., constitutional and statutory limitations 
applicable to Wyoming municipalities, and the availability of lawfully appropriated funds, the 
City shall be responsible for repairing physical damage to the Real Property caused by the City’s 
Inspections, reasonable wear and tear excepted. Nothing in this Section shall be construed as a 
waiver of the City’s governmental immunity or as creating liability beyond that provided by 
applicable law. Subject to the foregoing limitations, and only to the extent permitted by law and 
payable from lawfully appropriated funds, the City shall indemnify and hold Seller harmless 
from third-party claims for bodily injury, death, or physical damage to tangible property to the 
extent directly caused by the City’s Inspections, excluding claims or damages arising from the 
mere discovery of adverse facts or conditions, pre -existing conditions affecting the Real 
Property, or the act, omission, negligence, or willful misconduct of Seller or Seller’s agents, 
contractors, employees, tenants, invitees, or representatives. 
 
 4.2. The City, at its option, may conduct, at its sole cost, such independent 
investigation and inspection of the Real Property as the City shall deem reasonably necessary to 
ascertain the “environmental” condition of the Real Property ( “the City’s Environmental 
Investigation”). The City assumes no responsibility or liability as a result of any inaccuracies 
arising in or from the City’s Environmental Investigation, and makes no representations or 
warranties whatsoever regarding ( i) the completeness of the City’s Environmental Investigation, 
or (ii) the truth or accuracy of the City’s Environmental Investigation.  If Seller conducts any

5. Termination. 
 
 5.1. In the event the City, in the City’s sole and absolute discretion, determines that 
any matter shown in the City’s Inspections or Environmental Investigation is not satisfactory 
either during or subsequent to the Inspection Period, the City shall have the right, at any time 
prior to the Closing, to terminate this Agreement by giving Seller written notice thereof (the 
“Termination Notice”), in which event, both parties shall be released from all further obligations 
under this Agreement. 
 
 5.2. If the City determines, in its sole discretion, that the conditions described in 
Sections 6.1(d) or 6.1(e) cannot be satisfied, the City may terminate this Agreement by written 
notice to Seller. Upon such termination, neither party shall have any further obligation under this 
Agreement except for obligations that expressly survive termination.

(d) The City shall have determined, in its sole discretion, that the donated,
