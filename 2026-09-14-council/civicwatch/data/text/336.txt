Background:  
The item for consideration is a Resolution overruling a Planning Commission disapproval of a proposed   
Future Land Use Map Amendment (or Master Plan Amendment).  This is needed to fulfil Wyoming State 
Statute 15-1-506 and allow the Future Land Use Map Amendment to be considered by the Governing 
Body.    
 
The Planning Commission heard this future land use map amendment at their June 1, 2026, meeting 
and failed to obtain a second on the motion to approve. Wyoming State Statute allows the Governing 
Body to overrule the Planning Commission and consider plans or plan amendments the Planning 
Commission disapproves. 
 
The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as follows: 
• Introduction and Referral   June 8, 2026 
• Public Services Committee   June 15, 2026 
• Final Consideration  June 22, 2026
