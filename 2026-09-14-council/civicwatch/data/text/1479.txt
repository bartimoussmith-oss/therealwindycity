RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY 
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE 
Office of City Clerk 
 
January 23, 2026  
 
 
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in special session on this date beginning 
at 12:00 p.m. in City Council Chambers  and via electronic conference meeting. Present were: 
MAYOR – Patrick Collins , COUNCIL MEMBERS – Dr. Michelle  Aldrich (via Zoom), Ken 
Esquibel, Pete Laybourn, Mark Moody, Dr. Mark Rinne  (via Zoom, joining at 12:05 p.m.) , Jeff 
White (via Zoom)  and Lawrence Wolfe . Absent: Dr. Kathy Emmons and Mr. Segrave. Also 
present: Kylie Soden, City Clerk. The pledge of allegiance was recited. 
 
Application for a transfer of ownership of retail liquor license application filed with the 
City Clerk’s Office for Westby Edge LLC d/b/a Westby Edge Brewing, 714 W. 20th St., Cheyenne, 
WY (license currently held by Scooters Scoreboard Inc.). Mayor Collins advised the purpose of 
the meeting was to consider the application prior to the expiration of the in -operational (parked) 
status under the current license ownership which expires on January 24, 2026. Mr. Laybourn 
moved to approve, seconded by Mr. Esquibel.  Motion carried. Voting “yes” – all members of the 
governing body present. Public comments were made by the following: Darrin Westby, Westby 
Edge Brewing and Teri Cassidy, representative for Scooters Scoreboard Inc. Comment was made 
by the following member of the governing body: Mr. Laybourn.  
 
There being no further business to come before the governing body, the meeting was 
officially adjourned at 12:07 p.m. 
 
        Submitted by, 
 
              
        Kylie Soden, 
        City Clerk
