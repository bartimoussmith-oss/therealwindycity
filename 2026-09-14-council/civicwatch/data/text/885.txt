 
 
 
FIRST AMENDMENT TO GRANT AGREEMENT  
 
This First Amendment to Grant Agreement (the “Amendment”) is entered into and between 
Memorial Hospital of Laramie County d/b/a Cheyenne Regional Medical Center (“CRMC”) and 
the City of Cheyenne’s Office of Youth Alternatives (“Grantee”). 
 
RECITALS 
 
WHEREAS, CRMC and Grantee entered into that certain Grant Agreement (City Contract 
No. 8035) effective July 1, 2024 (the “Agreement”); and 
WHEREAS, the parties desire to amend the Agreement to increase the grant funding. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE , in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and 
other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby 
acknowledged, the parties agree as follows: 
 
A. Amendment to Exhibit A .  Exhibit “A” to the Agreement is hereby deleted in its 
entirety and replaced with the revised Exhibit “A” attached hereto and incorporated 
herein by this reference. Pursuant to the revised Exhibit “A,” CRMC shall pay Grantee 
a maximum amount not to exceed N inety-Eight Thousand Four Hundred Fifty -Eight 
Dollars ($98,458.00). 
 
B. Effect of Amendment.  Except as expressly modified by this Amendment, all terms, 
conditions, and provisions of the Agreement shall remain in full force and effect. In the 
event of any conflict between the terms of this Amendment and the Agreement, the 
terms of this Amendment shall control. 
 
C. Effective Date.  This Amendment shall be effective as of the date of the last signature 
affixed below (“Amendment Effective Date”). 
 
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Amendment as of the dates set forth 
below.

EXHIBIT A  
 
SPECIFIC GRANT REQUIREMENTS/DELIVERABLES 
 
• The Institute should enter into a contract with the Office of Youth Alternatives to provide the 
following deliverables in prevention in Laramie County by June 15, 2026: 
 
1. Attend a minimum of eighteen (18) out of the twenty-two (22) Behavioral Health 
Action Team meetings within the period of performance. 
2. Pay for services equivalent to that of a part-time master’s level clinician, primarily 
responsible for working on suicide prevention, intervention, and postvention. 
3. Pay for evidence-based staff development opportunities. 
4. Reporting 
5. Complete and submit quarterly reports within 5 business days of the dates indicated 
below:  
o August 30, 2024 
o November 29, 2024 
o February 28, 2025 
o June 30, 2025 
o August 29, 2025 
o November 28, 2025 
o February 27, 2026 
o June 15, 2026 
o Reports must include data by month on the following: 
a) Number of suicide evaluations completed and outcomes of 
assessments 
b) Number of suicide deaths for youth working with Youth 
Alternatives 
c) Percentage of youth seen on the same day of the referral  
d) Number of referrals made for family support after suicide 
e) Number of sessions conducted to support youth/families 
o Reports documenting the deliverables, benchmarks and any other requirement 
will be submitted by the Grantee to CRMC’s Office of Community Health.   
• Grantee will be paid monthly upon invoice, not to exceed the amount listed below.  
o Year 1 Budget (July 1, 2024 – June 30, 2025): thirty-one thousand seven hundred 
and twenty-nine dollars and fifty cents ($31,729.00) 
o Year 2 Budget (July 1, 2025 – June 15, 2026): sixty-six thousand seven hundred and 
twenty-nine dollars and fifty cents ($66,729.00) 
o Each Grantee submitted a budget for Year 1 and Year 2. Funds not spent in Year 1 
may be reallocated and not put towards Year 2. 
 
• Grantee will utilize the grant funding for those activities specifically outlined in this exhibit. 
Requests to redirect funding must be submitted in writing to the Office of Community 
Health.  The Office of Community Health will indicate their approval or denial of such 
requests in writing to the grantee.  Funds shall not be redirected or expenditures made until 
written approval is granted. 
• All invoices and reports should be sent to countyprevention@crmcwy.org.
