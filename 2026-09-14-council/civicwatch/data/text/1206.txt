MEMORANDUM  
TO:  Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council 
FROM: TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager 
DATE: April 8, 2025 
SUBJECT: Consideration of Bid #E -16-25 for furnishing a 2000 Gallon Single Axle Oil 
Distributor Truck for the City of Cheyenne, Street & Alley Division 
 ______________________________________________ 
 One (1) bidder responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.   
 
The One (1) bid was received from: 
 
1. Floyd's Truck Center Inc.  Cheyenne, Wyoming. 
 
The bids were reviewed by Bill Gonzalez (Street & Alley), Mike Schlabs (Street & Alley), Bryan 
Bell (Fleet Maintenance), Craig LaVoy (Public Works), and Vicki Nemecek (Public Works).   
 
Their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive Bid from Floyd’s Truck Center. 
 
The Bidding Documents contain a Surcharge and Escalator – Special Provision, that allows for 
the review of surcharges related to the following items:  Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) 
and/or Body surcharges applied to the cost of the vehicles after the bid opening date & Model 
year escalators.  The Surcharge & Escalator Contingency, is for an amount not-to-exceed Fifteen 
Percent (15%), of the total value of  requested items on the Bid. 
 
The total requested bid award is for $307,361.75 (Bid Option 2, 2026 Freightliner M2 106 Plus, 
with Leeboy /  Rosco Oil Distributor).  
 
This includes the 2000 Gallon Single Axle Oil Distributor Truck  ($266,165.00), an Extended 
Warranty for Engine & Aftertreatment for 5 Years / 100K Miles ($1,800.00), Extended Warranty 
for Transmission for 5 Years / 100K Miles ($637.00), Extended Warranty on the Distributor Body 
for 3 Years ( $2,335.00), and acceptance of the Trade -In Offer ($-3,500.00), PLUS 15% 
Contingency ($39,924.75).
