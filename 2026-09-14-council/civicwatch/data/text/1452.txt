ORDINANCE NO. ________ 
 
ENTITLED: "AN ORDINANCE CREATING CHAPTER 8.72, PRIVATE DISPLAYS ON 
PUBLIC PROPERTY, TO PROHIBIT THE UNAUTHORIZED USE OF 
PUBLIC PROPERTY FOR SPECIFIED PRIVATE DISPLAYS." 
 
 BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, 
WYOMING: 
 
 Section 1.  That Chapter 8.72, Private displays on public property, of Title 8, Health and 
Safety, is hereby created to read: 
 
Chapter 8.72 – PRIVATE DISPLAYS ON PUBLIC PROPERTY 
 
8.72.010 – Purpose – Authority – General provisions. 
 
A. The purpose of this chapter is to prohibit private displays on public property that are not 
otherwise expressly regulated by this code or other law. 
 
B. The provisions of this chapter are pursuant to authority granted cities and towns under 
Wyo. Stat. Section 15-1-101, et. seq. 
 
C. The Cheyenne compliance department shall have primary responsibility for enforcement 
of this chapter but may request assistance from the police department as necessary. The department 
of public works shall have primary responsibility for any removal of a private display required by 
this chapter and shall maintain an accurate record of the cost of removal. 
 
8.72.020 - Prohibitions. 
 
A. No person shall erect a display on  any public property  of the city of Cheyenne  for 
advertising, artistic, memorial, or other purposes except as otherwise permitted by law or as 
expressly authorized by the city. 
 
B. In addition to the penalty provided in subsection (C) of this section, a ny person violating 
this chapter shall remove the display within seven (7) days of being requested to do so. Notice of 
the request to remove the display shall be provided by placing written notice on or near the display. 
If the displa y is not removed within seven (7) days of the written notice , the display shall be 
deemed abandoned property, and the city shall remove the display not later than thirty (30) days 
after providing written notice.  
 
C. Any person violating the provisions of this chapter shall be subject to a fine not to exceed 
seven hundred fifty dollars ($750.00) and any costs incurred by the city for removal of the display.
