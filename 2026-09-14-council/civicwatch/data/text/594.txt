Subject: Downtown Development District Expansion 
Background: 
The item for consideration is a Downtown Development District Expansion. This is approved by Ordinance.  
The procedure for inclusion of additional land in the district is outlined in Wyoming Statute § 15-9-223 
which reads as follows: 
“15-9-223. Inclusion of additional property into development area; procedure. 
Subsequent to the organization of an authority, additional property may be included in the 
development area. Proceedings for inclusion shall be initiated by petition to the board of the 
authority signed by the owner or owners in fee of each parcel of land adjacent to the existing 
area sought to be included. The petition shall include evidence satisfactory to the board 
concerning title to the property and an accurate legal description thereof. If the board approves 
the application, it shall submit the application to the governing body of the municipality. If the 
governing body also approves the application, it shall then, at a regular or special meeting, by 
amendment to the ordinance treating the authority, redescribe the development area so as to 
include the additional property as described in the petition. From the eﬀective date of the 
amendment the additional property shall be included within the development area and shall be 
subject to any taxes thereafter imposed by the municipality for the use and beneﬁt of the 
authority.”

If the Downtown Development Authority Board approves the addition of the parcel to the District, the request 
will be sent to the Governing Body for consideration and resolution, and then again for an ordinance amending 
the Downtown District boundary. The Downtown Development District is described in section 2.76.040 of the 
municipal code.  
The procedure for inclusion of additional land in the district is outlined in Wyoming Statute § 15-9-223 
which reads as follows: 
“15-9-223. Inclusion of additional property into development area; procedure. 
Subsequent to the organization of an authority, additional property may be included in the 
development area. Proceedings for inclusion shall be initiated by petition to the board of the 
authority signed by the owner or owners in fee of each parcel of land adjacent to the existing

governing body also approves the application, it shall then, at a regular or special meeting, by 
amendment to the ordinance treating the authority, redescribe the development area so as to 
include the additional property as described in the petition. From the eﬀective date of the 
amendment the additional property shall be included within the development area and shall be 
subject to any taxes thereafter imposed by the municipality for the use and beneﬁt of the 
authority.” 
 
With the redescription of the Downtown Development District, staﬀ intends to draft a legal description including 
all adjacent rights-of-way to the existing district and added property. This would include all adjacent rights-of-
way including but not limited to Snyder Avenue on the east side, West 21st Street on the south side, and the 
adjacent alley on the north side. This would allow DDA funds to be used on improvements within the adjacent 
rights-of-way.

Impact and Beneﬁts: 
The inclusion of this property supports the DDA’s statutory purpose to stop and prevent the spread of blight and 
maintain and increase property values within the central business district (W.S. 15-9-201). Expansion of the 
Downtown Development District enables infrastructure and property upgrades that the DDA can help fund 
through the Capital Improvement Grant, Façade Improvement Program, and other improvement grants, 
enhancing the area’s overall functionality and visual appeal. Inclusion of this property would help stimulate 
economic activity, attract new businesses, and support job creation within the expanded District, particularly 
with the potential of the Reed Rail Project and the increased demand it will put on property investment and 
improvements for nearby areas.  
This inclusion contributes to a more cohesive and thriving urban environment. Expanding the District aligns with 
the long-term strategic vision for not only the 2024 Plan of Development (speciﬁc to Infrastructure & Land Use 
redevelopment goals), but also the West Edge by encouraging orderly, planned development and access to 
resources. Additionally, inclusion of this property creates an opportunity to collaborate with other property 
owners along Snyder Avenue from 22nd to 15th Street for future inclusion in the DDA District. This corridor-wide 
approach could unlock multiple opportunities to support West Edge revitalization (a signiﬁcant gateway to 
Downtown Cheyenne), advance the Area Wide Plan for brownﬁelds mitigation, and position commercial 
properties along Snyder to beneﬁt from potential progress on the Reed Rail Project. These all contribute to the 
purpose and mission of the Downtown Development Authority.
