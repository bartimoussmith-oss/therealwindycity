SUBJECT:  Right-of-Way License Agreement between First Natio nal Bank of Omaha and the 
City of Cheyenne   
 
 
Please find attached a Right-of-Way License Agreeme nt between First National Bank of Omaha 
and the City of Cheyenne for portions existing onsi te parking in the rights-of-way of East 
Pershing Boulevard and East 23 rd  Street, and an existing pole sign located in the r ight-of-way of 
Pershing Boulevard. 
 
It was identified during the site plan process asso ciated with minor revisions to the existing 
facility that the outside edges of the on-site park ing and the existing pole sign are within the 
public rights-of-way. Since the site plan process was considered a minor site plan, i.e. no change 
of use, limited modifications to the site and facil ity (internal and external of the building) were 
approved. Adjusting the curb line and relocating the pole sign would have added significant costs 
to the property owner, therefore, this agreement allows the described encroachments to remain as 
described herein.  
 
There are no financial implications associated with  this Right-of-Way License Agreement. Your 
approval of this agreement is respectfully requested.

RIGHT-OF-WAY LICENSE AGREEMENT 
 This Right-of-Way License  Agreement (“Agreement”) is by and between the City of 
Cheyenne, a municipal corporation organized and existing under the laws of the State of Wyoming 
(“City”), whose address is 2101 O ’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001, and First National 
Bank of Omaha (“Licensee”), whose address is 1601 Dodge Street 3120, Omaha, Nebraska 68197.  
The City and the Licensee are collectively referred to as “Party” or “Parties.”  This Agreement 
shall become effective upon the date of the last signature affixed hereto. 
RECITALS: 
 WHEREAS, the Licensee holds title to and has developed approximately 0.67 acres of land 
in the City of Cheyenne, County of Laramie, State of Wyoming  (“Property”), more particularly 
described as being: 
Lot 1, Block 1, Western States Bank Subdivision , City of Cheyenne, Laramie 
County, Wyoming; 
 
 WHEREAS, the Property is served by a parking lot facility and an existing pole sign which 
encroach into the public right s-of-way as shown on Exhibit A, attached hereto and incorporated 
by reference (“Facility”); and 
 WHEREAS, the City is prepared to grant a license to use a right -of-way (“License”), 
provided the Licensee agrees to certain terms and conditions. 
 NOW, THEREFORE, based on good and valuable consideration, the receipt and 
sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows: 
 1. Grant of License .  T he City grants to Licensee, its successors and assigns, the 
temporary, non-exclusive privilege of using the City right-of-way, commonly referred to as FNBO 
Pershing, covering a portion of the land described as Lot 1, Block 1, Western States Bank 
Subdivision (Township 14  North, Range 66  West of the 6 th P.M., Laramie County, Wyoming, 
NE1/4 of NW1/4 of Section 32) for an area of approximately 1,082 square feet, to be used as a 
parking lot, drive aisle, and an existing pole sign specifically subject to the remaining terms and 
conditions set forth herein.  The Licensee hereby accepts this License, subject to the terms and 
conditions contained herein, and subject to any requirements or limitations of law, now or in the 
future, which may restrict the use or enjoyment of this License. 
 2. Term; Termination.  This Agreement shall not be effective until it is approved by 
the Parties.  This Agreement is a covenant running with the Property.  Upon thirty (30) days written 
notice, the City may terminate this Agreement and revoke the License granted hereby.  Nothing in 
this Agreement, nor the act of entering into this Agreement, shall be construed as a limitation of 
the City’s authority to modify or abandon the right-of-way the City now has.

 3. Responsibilities of Licensee .  The Licensee, its successors and assigns, covenant 
and agree to perform and abide by each and every of the following: 
a. Licensee shall pay to the City, on each successive occasion of use, the costs 
of any and all damages which may result from Licensee’s use of th ese public rights-of-
way.  
b. Licensee shall b e solely responsible for any and all installations, repairs, 
maintenance, or other costs associated with its use of th ese public rights-of-way, whether 
such costs are specifically enumerated herein or not.  
c. Any and all utility lines, including pipelines, shall be placed in a manner so 
as to conform with recognized standards and applicable federal, state, or local laws, codes, 
and ordinances, and as directed by City. Any installation, alteration, or modification of any 
utility line shall be performed in compliance with the current standards  and applicable 
federal, state, or local laws, codes, and ordinances, as may from time to time be amended. 
Any future alteration or modification of the  Facility, utility, or device within the existing 
public right-of-way that is required, requested, or agreed to by the City shall be completed 
without delay and without cost to the City. 
d. If the City should for any reason construct, reconstruct, move, or abandon 
any portion of the City’s roads wherein the Facilities, utility, or device is located, Licensee 
shall, when required or requested by the City, move the Facility without delay and without 
cost to the City. 
e. Upon request, the Licensee agrees to locate and mark the Facility, utility, or 
device being licensed in the following manner: 
i. The Licensee, as a condition of approval for the License, agrees to 
locate the Facility identified by this License, when needed by the City for future 
construction and/or maintenance activities. This information will be the basis of 
plans prepared by or for the City for use by the City, its contractors, or the public. 
ii. Location information will include the marking of the Facility on the 
ground, as specified by Wyo. Stat. § 37-12-301, et seq., with the appropriate color.  
Location information will also include the nature and elevation of the Facility. For 
each location, the utility shall be tied both horizontally and vertically, by 
coordinates, to a datum determined by City and certified by a licensed surveyor. 
The information will be shown on plans created by Licensee, the utility company 
or Facility owner. A copy of the plans will be sent to the City. Costs associated with 
identifying and locating the Facility, as may be required from time to time, will be 
the responsibility of Licensee. 
f. The Licensee shall be responsible for all maintenance and repair of the 
Facility and all associated appurtenances within and/or exclusively serving the Property.

 4. Access Easement .  The City is granted a permanent easement for access to the 
Facility in the event the City requires access to the Facility for emergency maintenance of the 
City’s improvements.  If the City exercises this provision, the City will repair, replace, or rebuild 
any and all damage to the private property which shall be occasioned by the emergency 
maintenance or other access to the Facility at the City’s expense.  All other costs including the 
costs of maintenance activities (labor, material, equipment, etc.) shall be the responsibility of the 
Licensee as outlined in Section 2 above.

Wyoming.
