BACKGROUND: 
 
The applicant is requesting approval of a preliminary plat for roughly 0.31 acres of land. The proposed plat 
would subdivide the parcel into two Lots to create an MR Lot with the existing residence and an MUB Lot 
along Dell Range Blvd. for future development. The Lot currently has split zoning. Although a plat dividing one 
lot into two is typically eligible for the administrative plat process, the unique situation of this particular 
proposal requires Governing Body approval.

1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical 
patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.2.d.1) 
 
On the future land use map found in PlanCheyenne, the subject land and surrounding area has 
been designated as Urban Residential. The Urban Residential designation encourages a range of 
residential uses. PlanCheyenne supports the use of existing infrastructure and timely provision of 
services (including emergency and education services). This development is proposed in an area 
with a mix of residential uses and which has largely existing infrastructure with no expansion of 
infrastructure is necessary. This development is in conformance with the principles and future land 
uses identified in PlanCheyenne. The property is within the Urban Service Boundary and has 
access to all utilities. 
 
The application complies with this review criterion.  
 
2) The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to 
existing and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration 
of current and future streets, open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or 
facilities. (UDC 2.1.2.d.2) 
 
The proposed plat subdivides a single parcel into two lots and brings the property more in line 
with sound planning principles as it removes the split zoning of the single parcel. Each new lot will 
have a single zone, Lot 1 will be zoned MUB and Lot 2 will be zoned MR. Both lots will still have 
access to utilities and to a street.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
   
3) The division of land meets the purposes, intent, and all applicable standards in 
Article 4. (UDC 2.1.2.d.3) 
 
Community Facility Fees (4.2): 
For fees based on acreage, the area of the plat is pro-rated to the nearest tenth of an acre. 
Lands reserved as unbuildable (for right-of-way, detention, parks, remainder lots, etc), are 
exempt from fees. Park fees are calculated at $700 per acre. All fees are due at the time of 
building permit. 
 
Per UDC Section 4.2.5, Table 4-2, Enhancement Fees are payable at the issuance of a building 
permit for each structure. The Park Enhancement Fee for residential is $400 per dwelling unit.  
 
Transportation Network (4.3): 
This plat does not proposed to add any additional streets to the network. The proposed block 
is too large for current block sizes, but this plat would be unable to provide a meaningful 
connection to reduce block size. Limited usefulness of truly reducing block size, the amount 
of area required to put in a street, and the restrictions of accesses on Dell Range make street 
dedication infeasible. Staff recommends that an exception to block size be granted.  
 
This subdivision proposes a lot which only has access to Dell Range. Regulations in Article 4 
would prevent an access to this parcel as the minimum access spacing along an arterial is 
660’, and there is are access points only a few feet to the east and west of this parcel. The 
PUDC-25-226   December 1, 2025 
4 | Page  
 
parcel currently has a residential driveway access and the City Engineering department is 
willing to allow the residential access to remain if the property develops with a single -family 
residential use (and this plat is approved). More intense development would require the 
closure of the current access and shared access with a neighborign parcel (preferably the 
parcel to the east, but details would depend on the timing of the proposed development and 
the context of the area at the time that development occurs.  
 
Open Space (4.4): 
Open space is required at 750sqft per unit or 8% of the gross development area, whichever is 
less. However, due to the size of the property, plat-level open space is not required. 
 
Required Improvements (4.5): 
This plat appears to be able to provide all required utilities to the lots within this 
development. 
 
Public Safety Fees (4.6): 
Public Safety Fees are required at the time of construction of any residential or commercial 
building on either of the proposed lots. Per UDC Section 4.6.3.b, Public Safety Fees are 
payable at the issuance of a building permit for each structure.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion, with conditions.   
 
4) Any phasing proposed in the application is clearly indicated and demonstrates a 
logical and well-planned development pattern. This may include consideration of 
amenities and public facilities of the subdivision within each phase compared to 
the anticipated timing or the likelihood of future phases being constructed as 
planned. (UDC 2.1.2.d.4) 
 
No specific phasing is proposed with this project. 
 
Review criterion not applicable.

Drainage: 
A drainage study was submitted and reviewed. There were no concerns with drainage at the plat 
level for the project as presented.  
 
Traffic: 
A transportation worksheet was submitted and reviewed. Depending on the type of development 
that is proposed for Lot 2, access to Dell Range Blvd. may be limited do to UDC 4.3.4.c "Standard 
Roadway: Principal Arterial", "Minimum Access separation”. An access easement may need to be 
obtained from the property owner to the east to allow for shared access.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.

6) Lots proposed for development shall be buildable lots according to the existing or 
proposed zoning district standards, result in design and development patterns that 
meet the Intent and Applicability statements of the existing or proposed zoning 
district, and not aggravate nonconforming setbacks or currently adopted building 
and fire code access and separation requirements. (UDC 2.1.2.d.6) 
 
The land as currently developed will become nonconforming upon recording. The existing garage 
is currently to the rear of the house while the front of the parcel is to the south; when proposed 
Lot 1 is created, the garage will become in front of the house (front will be north), which is not 
legal under current code (UDC 5.1.5). The proposed lots do not create any further non -
conforming setbacks other than with the garage as mentioned.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion, with conditions. 
 
7) All parcels not proposed as buildable lots and which are not dedicated to and 
accepted by the City shall have a clearly defined entity responsible for ongoing 
maintenance.  (UDC 2.1.2.d.7) 
 
All proposed lots are proposed as buildable lots. The Plat does not propose any lots subject to 
this criterion. 
 
Review criterion not applicable. 
 
8) The application will not create any undue influence on existing or potential 
development for adjacent property considering the existing zoning and 
Comprehensive Plan policies for that property.  (UDC 2.1.2.d.8) 
 
This plat will not create any undue influence on existing or potential development. This plat would 
allow the front of the lot to develop in some fashion, whether it be with residential or a small 
commercial use. This area consists of a mix of uses such as commercial retail, office, single-family, 
and multifamily.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
 
9) The application is not harmful to the public health, safety, and welfare. (UDC 
2.1.2.d.9) 
 
As mentioned under criteria 3 and 5, depending on the type of development that is proposed for 
Lot 2, access to Dell Range Blvd. may be limited do to UDC 4.3.4.c "Standard Roadway: Principal 
Arterial", "Minimum Access separation”. An access easement may need to be obtained from the 
property owner to the east to allow for shared access. 
 
City staff is satisfied that this proposed plat would not otherwise adversely affect the public 
health, safety, or general welfare. 
 
The application complies with this review criterion.
