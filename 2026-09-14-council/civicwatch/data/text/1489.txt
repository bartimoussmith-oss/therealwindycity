Background:  
 
The item for consideration is an Ordinance. Annexation of County pockets is a 2022 City 
Council priority. A “County pocket” is an area of unincorporated land 100% surrounded by 
the City of Cheyenne. A related “Assigned Zoning” pursuant to Unified Development Code 
(UDC) Section 2.2.8 ordinance is also on the agenda.    
 
The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as follows: 
• Introduction and Referral  February 9, 2026 
• Public Services Committee  February 17, 2026 
• Second reading    February 23, 2026 
• Public Services Committee  March 2, 2026 
• Third and final reading   March 9, 2026 
 
This annexation is comprised of 16 properties 100% surrounded by the City (See attached 
map). Of the 16 parcels, most are developed with residential uses, one is a farm, and others 
are developed with commercial or industrial uses. The City has maintained contact with the 
County Planning Office to coordinate any pending permits or development reviews.  
Currently there are no active permits or development specific to the properties proposed for 
annexation.   
 
On December 2, 2025, City staff mailed a courtesy notice by first class mail to all landowners 
included in this annexation area inviting them to a “Question and Answer” on Tuesday 
December 16, 2025. During the meeting staff provided information regarding trash 
collection, water and sewer services, continuation of uses and what to expect with the 
annexation. Only one resident attended the meeting. Their main concern was about their 
farm/farm stand and the continuation of the use once annexed. Staff is proposing that their 
property be assigned the AG Agricultural zone which will allow the farm to continue as a use 
by right and would not change the use of the property.  
 
The existing use of any property annexed, including any livestock use, will be allowed to 
continue as is as a nonconforming use.  Nonconforming uses may continue until they are 
abandoned for a period of 12-months. Residents within pockets have been encouraged to 
complete a Land Use Documentation form detailing the existing use of the land for 
documentation purposes. The form is simply a tool to assist future City staff members to 
determine if a use was in existence at the time of annexation.   
 
 
City-initiated annexations do not require an owner’s signature. To initiate an annexation, the 
City must adopt a resolution certifying that the annexation complies with Wyoming State 
Statute § 15-1-402. After the City adopts this resolution, the annexation follows the typical 
schedule, including a public hearing and the approval of an Ordinance which annexes the 
land. The Governing Body adopted Resolution No. 6511 initiating this annexation on January 
26, 2025. 
 
As with any annexation, City services will be extended to service the land contained in the 
annexation. City utilities are already located in the vicinity. Police, fire, and sanitation services 
will be provided. Infrastructure improvements required related to the annexation are minimal. 
Future development such as subdivision or new development will require on- and off-site 
infrastructure improvements. Existing infrastructure will remain as is upon annexation.  
 
The areas proposed for annexation are adjacent to land under City jurisdiction. Development 
in this area is a natural extension of the City. 100 % of the perimeter of the annexation area is 
contiguous to City limits.   
 
Due to meeting the review criteria found in Wyoming Statute § 15-1-402 and the annexation 
conforming with the 2022 City Council goal to annex County pockets, staff recommends 
approval of this annexation. 
 
Staff Recommendation: 
 
Staff recommends the Governing Body approve the Annexation Ordinance.

Responses to questions of David Kniseley regarding WYfresh Farms regarding the affects of annexation. 
 
Stormwater Fee 
The estimated stormwater fee is approximately $38.00 per month, based on impervious surface 
calculations. The fee is assessed at $0.0024 per square foot. A link to the stormwater fee map and 
Engineering Department contact information has been provided to him if he has questions or concerns.  
 
Property Taxes 
The estimated annual tax increase is $203.80. The County Assessor may reassess the property upon 
annexation. The Assessor's contact information has been provided to him to obtain additional 
information. 
 
Drainage 
The Planning and Development Department is not aware of any current drainage projects affecting the 
property. Engineering Department contact information has been provided to the inquirer o him to 
obtain additional information. 
 
Use of Land for Livestock 
The property is proposed to be zoned Agricultural (AG), which permits livestock. Minor adjustments in 
animal numbers within existing pastures do not require approval. Expansion of livestock use areas 
requires site plan review. Rotating or designated animal areas should be documented using a Land Use 
Documentation Form and a web link to that form has been provided to him. 
 
Right to Farm and Ranch Act 
The Act generally protects agricultural operations from nuisance claims when standard management 
practices are followed. It does not exempt property owners from compliance with local regulations. 
 
Expansion of Farming or Livestock Areas 
Expanding the area used for farming or livestock requires submission of a site plan. Infrastructure 
improvements may be considered but are evaluated in proportion to the scope of the proposed 
expansion. 
 
Guard Dog Vaccinations 
Vaccination requirements apply within both City and County jurisdictions. Animal Control contact 
information has been provided to him to obtain additional information.. 
 
Farmstand Licensing 
A $75 annual license is required for operation of a mobile food cart, along with compliance with public 
health requirements. City Clerk's Office contact information has been provided to him if he desires 
further information. 
 
Discharge of Firearms and Livestock Protection 
Discharge of firearms within the City is prohibited except at designated ranges. Alternative wildlife and 
nuisance animal control measures are available. Contact information for the police department and the 
Wyoming State Game and Fish Department has been provided to him for alternatives. 
 
Jefferson/Walterscheid Intersection 
Engineering staff have been notified of visibility and signage concerns at this intersection. Additional 
signage is anticipated to address traffic movement restrictions after annexation. 
 
 
Building Permits 
Existing structures will be recognized as constructed and are not required to be upgraded. New 
structures, including hoop houses, will require permits. Property owners are encouraged to document 
existing structures prior to ordinance adoption. 
 
Land Use and Agricultural Operations 
Annexation will not require the discontinuation of agricultural operations, livestock activities, farmstand 
operations, or existing driveways. Current uses may continue under the proposed zoning.

BACKGROUND:
Annexation of County pockets is a 2022 City Council priority.  A “County pocket” is an area of unincorporated 
land 100% surrounded by the City of Cheyenne. City-initiated annexations do not require an owner’s 
signature. To initiate an annexation, the City must adopt a resolution certifying that the annexation 
complies with Wyoming State Statute § 15-1-402. After the City adopts this resolution, the annexation
follows the typical schedule, including a public hearing and the approval of an Ordinance which annexes 
the land. The Governing Body adopted Resolution No. 6511 initiating this annexation on January 26, 2026.
This annexation is comprised of 16 properties 100% surrounded by the City (See attached map). Of the 16
parcels, most are developed with residential uses and a few are commercial / industrial in nature. 
Land annexed to the City requires a City zone. An assigned zoning ordinance is accompanying this annexation 
to assign the AG, MR, CB and LI zones. 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:
To approve the annexation Ordinance, the Governing Body must make the following findings required in 
WS 15-1-402; a detailed analysis of which is below:
1) An annexation of the area is for the protection of the health, safety and welfare of 
the persons residing in the area and in the city or town. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (i)]
The annexation of these properties will increase the health, safety, and welfare of the future 
occupants. By being in City limits, the property will receive access to City Fire and Police and 
sanitation services. The properties will also have the option of connecting to City water and 
sanitary sewer instead of having wells and septic systems.
The application complies with this review criterion.
2) The urban development of the area soug ht to be annexed would constitute a 
natural, geographical, economical and social part of the annexing city or town. [WS 
15-1-402 (a) (ii)]
These properties constitute a natural, geographic, economic, and social part of the city. The 
properties are surrounded by City on all sides. The contiguity to the City and the proximity to 
surrounding urban-style development makes the parcels a natural geographic part of the City. 
The application complies with this review criterion.

3) The area sought to be annexed is a logical and feasible addition to the annexing 
city or town and the extension of basic and other services customarily available to 
residents of the city or town shall, within reason, be available to the area proposed 
to be annexed. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (iii)]
The area is a logical and feasible addition to the City due to the reasons previously listed. All City 
services normally available to residents will also be made available. Police and fire protection will 
be extended upon annexation. Utilities exist in the area and will be expanded into the proposed 
area as they redevelop.
The application complies with this review criterion.
4) The area sought to be annexed is contiguo us with or adjacent to the annexing city 
or town, or the area meets the requirements of W.S. 15-1-407. [WS 15-1-402 (a)
(iv)]
The area proposed for annexation is 100% contiguous on all sides.  
The application complies with this review criterion.
5) If the city or town does not own or operate its own electric utility, its governing 
body is prepared to issue one (1) or more franchises as necessary to serve the 
annexed area pursuant to W.S. 15-1-410. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (v)]
Utility franchises are in place to serve the annexed area.  
The application complies with this review criterion.
6) The annexing city or town, not less than twenty (20) business days prior to the 
public hearing required by W.S. 15-1-405(a), has sent by certified mail to all 
landowners and affected public utilities within the territory and by first class mail 
to any persons owning property that is adjacent to or within three hundred (300) 
feet of the territory proposed to be annexed, regardless of whether the property is 
inside or outside the corporate limits of the annexing city or town and regardless 
of whether the city or town is exercising authority under W.S. 15-3-202(b)(ii), a 
summary of the proposed annexation report as required under subsection (c) of 
this section and notice of the time, date and location of the public hearing required 
by W.S. 15-1-405(a). [WS 15-1-402 (a) (vi)]
A certified mailing was made to the landowners and public utilities on January 8, 2026. Neighbors 
within 300’ were mailed first-class letters the same day. The public hearing for this annexation is 
scheduled for February 9, 2026. Twenty (20) business days (on the City’s calendar) prior to the 
announced public hearing is January 9, 2026. The earliest confirmation of delivery was dated
January 12, 2026.
The application complies with this review criterion.
