BACKGROUND: 
 
This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from Harmony Valley PUD to NR-3 
Neighborhood Residential  – High Density for roughly 25.17 acres of land. The land is currently vacant.  
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA : 
 
Should the Planning Commission  wish to recommend approval of  the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2. 2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical 
patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1) 
 
The Future Land Use Designation for this parcel is Urban Residential on the majority of the parcel 
and Mixed-Use Residential  on a small southern portion of the parcel. According to 
PlanCheyenne, the Urban Residential portion of the parcel ’s designation creates an emphasis on 
residential development with secondary uses being complementary uses to the neighborhood. 
The Mixed-Use Residential designation is meant for an emphasis on residential developments

with secondary uses being retail, offices, and light trade that are related to the neighborhood

The NR-3 zone district allows for a variety of residential developments  with an emphasis on high 
density residential uses .  
 
Based on the policies and future land use map, NR-3 zoning is appropriate at this location .  
 
The proposed zone change  complies with review criterion 1. 
 
2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering 
existing zoning, the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies 
created under the guidance of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2) 
 
The proposed NR -3 zone would be an extension of the existing NR -3 zone to the abutting to the 
parcel to the west. The proposed NR-3 is surrounded by parcels that are zoned P Public to the 
west, Harmony Meadow PUD to the eas t, and MUB to the south. There is a parcel to the north 
that is currently undergoing a zoning change from AG to NR -3. Much of the current development 
in the area is residential development along with associated neighborhood  uses. 
 
The existing zone (the Harmony Valley PUD ) has designated this parcel as Medium Density 
Residential. T he PUD Medium Density permitted uses are similar to the NR -3 permitted uses. Both 
plans promote the use of more intense residential developments . The NR -3 allows cottage lots by 
right.   
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2. 
   
3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards 
and uses of the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject 
property for development according to the standards and uses of the proposed 
zoning district (UDC 2.2.1.d.3) 
  
The existing zone is Harmony Vall ey PUD. In accordance to  the existing PUD, this parcel is listed 
as Medium Density Residential, which a compatible with the NR -3 allowed uses . It is to be noted 
that there are certain uses that are permitted within the NR-3 that are not allowed within PUD 
parameters.   
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3. 
 
4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed 
according to the Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed 
zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4) 
 
The NR-3 zone encourages small block development. The property will be required to connect to 
existing or future development. Pedestrian accesses will be encouraged where street access is not 
ferasible.  Infrastructure (streets and u tilities) exist in the area . Water and sewer will likely need to 
be extended to the area.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4. 
5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of
the proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific
standards. (UDC 2.2.1.d.5)
The NR-3 district is intended to be used for high -density, urban residential use with consistent
neighborhood character. The regulations are meant to protect the neighborhood character of the
district and protect the primarily residential use of land.
The applicability to areas where more compact neighborhood development patterns are desired,
specifically the Urban Residential Care in the Cheyenne Comprehensive Plan.
Typically, this district is located no more than ¼ mile or up to four blocks from any existing or
planned Activity Center. Isolated application of this district without adjacency to activity center
should be avoided so that significant concentrations of density do es not exist without walking
access to non -residential uses and quality urban amenities. This district should be supported by a
highly connected street network with street design types that have  a high degree of pedestrian
amenities.
This parcel is located between a school and existing neighborhood. There is not a nearby activity
center, but there is commercial development to the northeast and east , with limited non-
residential developments to the south along College . There is an existing pedestrian network in
the area. This would be an appropriate zone to all the desired compact neighborhood
development.
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5.
6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options
if any specific development proposed in association with the application is not
executed after approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6)
The NR-3 zone is a high -density residential zone that is similar to the existing PUD designation.
All the residential uses that are allowed by right within the PUD designation of this property are
allowed in the NR-3 zone.
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6.
7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7)
No major comments received in regard to the proposed zone.  
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7. 
8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare.
(UDC 2.2.1.d.8)
