RECOMMENDED MOTION: 
 
Move to recommend the Governing Body approve the assigned zoning for land south of Storey 
Boulevard, east of Powderhouse Road, North of Dell Range Boulevard, and west of Ridge Road  to AG 
Agricultural and MR Medium-density Residential as shown in the attached zone change map, noting that 
the project meets the review criteria for approval. 
 
APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS: 
 
• UDC 2.2.8 Assigned Zoning 
 
BACKGROUND: 
The City is annexing parcels that are 100% surrounded by current City Limits as part of Councils goals to annex 
county pockets. Annexation of a parcel requires a city zone to be established.  
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1. The proposed zoning assignment is the most analogous City zone to the current zone 
when taking into consideration allowed uses, current uses, or surrounding City zones. 
Zoning shall be assigned a City zone based on: 
a. Permitted uses, 
b. Uses that could reasonably be anticipated for conditional use approval 
under the current zoning, 
c. Current uses, or 
d. Surrounding City zoning, 
as deemed most suitable for the property being assigned a zone in terms of allowing 
the landowner to continue the current use of the land while respecting the current and 
anticipated uses of surrounding property. 
 
Through the reviewing of the County Pockets, the assigned zoning is fairly straightforward. 
 
Pocket A 
 
Pocket A is currently zoned LU – Land Use within the County. This pocket has a variety of City zone 
districts that surround it. There is commercial zoning to the south and mainly residential zoning to the 
east and west. To the north is County LU. This pocket is very large and a majority is currently under 
the ownership of one family. There is one parcel owned by the City and one by the School District. 
The land is currently used for grazing and is all vacant. Staff is unsure how this area will develop and 
due to the size and current use, Staff believes that the AG – Agricultural zone is the most analogous 
zone for this pocket. The AG zone is also a holding zone which will allow the land to be rezoned more 
easily in the future as the land develops.  
 
PUDC-25-258   January 5, 2026 
3 | Page  
  
Pocket B 
 
Pocket B is currently zoned LU – Land Use within the County. There are three different parcels in this 
pocket that are all under the same ownership. These parcels are currently vacant. They are surrounded 
by residential and public zoning. To the west, northwest, and southwest, the surrounding land is 
zoned NR-3 – Neighborhood Residential-High Density. The other zoning to the north if NR-2 – 
Neighborhood Residential-Medium Density. The zoning to the southeast is MR- Medium-density 
Residential. P – Public zoning is to the southwest and northeast. Two of the parcels are designated 
Urban Residential by the future land use map and the parcel along Converse is designated Mixed -use 
Residential by the future land use map. More than likely, these parcels will develop with some type of 
residential use. Based on this information Staff believes that the MR – Medium-density Residential 
zone is the most analogous zone for this pocket. Although NR-3 and NR-2 zoning make up a majority 
of the adjacent zoning, Staff does not believe a higher-intensity residential zone is appropriate for the 
assigned zoning and should the developer of the land wish to upzone to a higher intensity residential 
zone, they can go through the Zone Change process which would review the higher -intensity zone on 
its merits and  require neighbor notification.  
 
Pocket C 
 
Pocket C is currently zoned LU – Land Use within the County. This pocket is surrounded by MR – 
Medium-density Residential on all sides and has a little bit of LR – Low-density Residential to the 
southwest. The land is currently vacant. Surrounding land uses are single-family residential on three 
sides and vacant land to the north. More than likely, this parcel will develop with some type of 
residential use. Based on this information Staff believes that the MR – Medium-density Residential 
zone is the most analogous zone for this pocket.  
 
This assigned zoning complies with review criterion 1.
