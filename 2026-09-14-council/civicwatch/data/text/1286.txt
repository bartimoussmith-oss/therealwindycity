BACKGROUND: 
 
The applicant is requesting connection to BOPU sanitary sewer services and water services for property 
outside City limits. It is City Staff’s understanding that the property is intending to develop using a funding 
source that is unavailable if the property is within City limits. Subsequent to securing funding, the owner 
would then annex into the City. This property is currently mostly vacant but is proposed to have multiple 
warehouses. There is currently no existing sewer in the area or other outside user agreements. City water is 
currently adjacent to the property along the north side.  
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
The property is currently zoned a PUD Planned Unit Development in the County.  
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
The request for an outside user agreement has no formal review criteria; however, the following 
elements should be considered when reviewing outside user agreements: 
- BOPU capacity 
- Needed infrastructure improvements 
- Population served 
- Considerations on the effect on future annexation(s) 
- Fiscal cost/benefit to the City and the BOPU 
- Conformance with the Comprehensive Plan 
 
The requested outside user agreement has minimal impact on BOPU’s system capacity. The proposed 
use of the properties is for industrial warehousing. Sewer will need to be developed in the area and 
connected into South Cheyenne Water and Sewer district at the intersection of High Plains Road and 
South Greeley. Water is available within High Plains Road, but would need to be extended into the 
development.  
 
Property tax would increase, but the City would not have any income from the property tax in the area 
at this time. The BOPU may charge a higher rate for outside users. The sewer infrastructure will need to
