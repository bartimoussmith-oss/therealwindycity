This Professional Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is made and entered into by and 
between the City of Cheyenne, located at 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001 
(“City”), and AVI PC, located at 1103 Old Town Lane, Ste. 101, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82009 
(“Consultant”). This Agreement shall become effective upon the date of the last signature affixed 
hereto. 
 
RECITALS 
 
In consideration of the mutual covenants and obligations herein expressed, it is agreed by 
and between the parties as follows: 
 
1. SCOPE OF WORK 
The Consultant agrees to provide services as described for the above -mentioned project, 
in accordance with the Scope of Work attached hereto as Exhibit A, incorporated by 
reference and expressly made a part hereof. 
 
2. TIME OF PERFORMANCE 
The work included in this Scope of Work will be completed by December 31, 2026. 
 
3. COMPENSATION 
In consideration of the services to be performed pursuant to this Agreement, the 
Consultant will bill the City and the City agrees to pay Consultant a not to exceed amount 
of One Hundred Ninety -Six T housand Three Hundred Thirty -Four Dollars and Forty 
Cents ($196,334.40) as stated in Exhibit A.  
 
4. CONSULTANT RESPONSIBILITY 
In providing services under this Agreement, the Consultant shall perform in a manner 
consistent with that degree of care and skill ordinarily exercised by members of the same 
profession currently practicing under similar circumstances. Upon notice to the 
Consultant, the Consu ltant will, without additional compensation, correct those services 
not meeting such a standard. 
 
5. COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS 
The Parties agree that they will perform their obligations as provided in this Agreement 
in accordance with all applicable laws and ordinances.

6. DRUG-FREE WORKPLACE 
In compliance with the Drug -Free Work place Act of November 1988, the City of 
Cheyenne has established an Alcohol and Controlled Substance Policy that pertains to 
alcohol and drug usage by City Employees. All architects, engineers , and other 
consultants under contract with City of Cheyenne, and their employees and 
sub-consultants, are required to comply with the provisions of the City’s Alcohol and 
Controlled Substance Policy for drug and/or alcohol usage on City property or other sites

Agreement. It is the responsibility of the Consultant to familiarize themselves with the 
requirements of this policy and to inform all their employees and sub -consultants of the 
requirements and ensure their compliance. If the Consultant, their employees or 
sub-consultants are found in violation of this policy, the Agreement may be terminated.

15. TERM AND TERMINATION 
The term of this Agreement shall be from date of execution to provisions of the scope of 
work as presented in Exhibit A have been met . The parties may by mutual written 
agreement renew or extend this Agreement. 
 
The City may, without cause, and upon thirty days’ written notice to the Consultant,

Upon receipt of such notice, the Consultant shall: 
  a. Discontinue all services affected; and 
  b. Deliver to the City within five (5) days all data, drawings, specifications,

Consultant for accepted work done and expenses incurred to the date of 
termination. Such acceptance shall not be unreasonably withheld.

its own costs and expenses, including without limitation, attorney’s fees.

Dear Mr. Cobb, 
AVI is pleased to submit the following proposal for construction administration services for the 
Storey/Summit Construction Project.   
PROJECT UNDERSTANDING  
As you are aware, the final construction drawings have been approved through Laramie County 
and the City of Cheyenne, the project was bid and awarded to Reiman Contractors, and 
construction is underway. 
The design phase was strenuous, and the project required extra coordination efforts by the City 
of Cheyenne and AVI due to multiple jurisdictional agencies, multiple consultants involved, and 
many other design and construction projects in the immediate vicinity.  We anticipate these 
extraordinary coordination efforts being required through construction.  Due to this, we believe 
it essential for AVI to maintain services through construction as we have an intimate knowledge 
of design, conversations and directives, and coordination efforts that have occurred to date.  
This will facilitate a more efficient construction schedule and mitigate costs. 
AVI proposes to keep the design team on this project through construction, this includes; 
- Tristan Cordier – Project Manager
- Thomas Dehoff – City Coordinator and Easement Facilitation
- Zach Posten – Project Designer
- Rich Taylor – Onsite Construction Manager
- Aaron Cvar – Drainage Coordinator for parcel adjacent Whitney/Gysel property.
Additionally, AVI will coordinate directly with Laramie County Public Works, WYDOT for College 
Dr. improvements, Civilworx for drainage improvements along Summit Dr./Arthur Ave. and 
support efforts for the required offsite borrow area within Whitney Ranch.  AVI will also work 
closely with Black Hills Energy, High West Energy, and Spectrum for any utility conflicts 
encountered during construction.
