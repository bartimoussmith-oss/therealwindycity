BACKGROUND: 
 
The applicant is requesting approval of a preliminary plat for roughly 0.30 acres of land. The proposed plat 
would subdivide the parcel into 5 lots for residential development.  
 
SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING: 
 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Preliminary Plat, the Commission 
must make the following findings required in UDC 2.1.2.d  – a detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical 
patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.2.d.1) 
 
On the future land use map found in PlanCheyenne, the subject land and surrounding area has 
been designated as Urban Residential. The Urban Residential designation encourages a range of 
residential uses. The proposed plat is to provide additional single-family residential to the area.

2) The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to 
existing and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration 
of current and future streets, open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or 
facilities. (UDC 2.1.2.d.2) 
 
The proposed plat is for residential development. Surrounding this proposed area is residential 
and future development for area is projected to be residential as well considering the Future Land 
Use map designates this area as Urban Residential. Any residential development with this 
designation is appropriate. It will not create any impact in the area.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion.   
   
3) The division of land meets the purposes, intent, and all applicable standards in 
Article 4. (UDC 2.1.2.d.3) 
 
Community Facility Fees (4.2): 
For fees based on acreage, the area of the property is pro-rated to the nearest tenth of an 
acre. Lands reserved as unbuildable (for right-of-way, detention, parks, remainder lots, etc), 
are exempt from fees. Per UDC Section 4.2.5, Table 4-2, Land acquisition, Infrastructure, and 
Enhancement Fees are all payable at the issuance of a building permit for each structure. 
 
Transportation Network (4.3): 
This plat is not creating any changes to the transportation network. Accesses will be limited 
unto 5th Street as the street is designated a Major Collector on the Major Street Plan – 
however all lots have access to either O’Neil or the Alley.  
 
Open Space (4.4): 
There is city park a little over 1/4 of a mile from the proposed plat site. There are also pieces 
of the Greenway path within ¼ of a mile of the site. During development process of this site 
the applicant will need to meet open space requirements as prescribed by the UDC. 
 
 
Required Improvements (4.5): 
This plat will need to provide a sewer and water utility service line easement to the lots to 
demonstrate that utilities will be able to extend to all buildable lots.  
 
Public Safety Fees (4.6): 
Public Safety Fees are required at the time of construction of any residential building on any 
of the proposed lots. Per UDC Section 4.6.3.b, Public Safety Fees are payable at the issuance 
of a building permit for each structure.

provide a necessary sewer and water service line easement for extension of these utilities to each 
property.
