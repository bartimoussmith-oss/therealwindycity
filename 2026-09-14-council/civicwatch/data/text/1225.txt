MEMO 
To: Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council 
From: Charles W. Bloom, AICP, Planning and Development Director  
Subject: PLTA-24-6: An ordinance repealing Chapter 12.28, Downtown Graffiti Abatement Pilot 
Program of Title 12, Streets, Sidewalks and Public Places, of the municipal code of the City 
of Cheyenne, Wyoming. 
Date: April 14, 2025 
 
Background:  
 
The item for consideration is an amendment to municipal code and is approved by ordinance. The proposed 
Governing Body review schedule is as follows: 
• Introduction and Referral:  April 14, 2025 
• Public Services Committee:  April 21, 2025 
• Second reading:   April 28, 2025 
• Public Services Committee:  May 5, 2025 
• Third and final reading:   May 12, 2025 
 
The ordinance was passed on February 11, 2019, to improve the City's graffiti abatement process after then 
Downtown Development Authority (DDA) staff believed the police-run program was ineffective. That prior 
program required property owners to contact the Police Department, sign a damage waiver, and volunteers 
removed graffiti when able which resulted in inconsistent and unpredictable removal times. The DDA proposed 
a more proactive pilot program, run by the DDA, to streamline removal timelines and committed funding to 
graffiti removal ($5,000 initially and $15,000 in future funding). The program was a pilot program with an 
expiration date of November 1, 2020 (Section 3 of the ordinance). Presently, $3,000 is budgeted annually for 
graffiti removal and removal is typically limited to vulgar/obscene messages or images on sidewalks and 
occasionally on buildings.   
 
After a recent review, City staff concluded that detailing this program specifically for the DDA by ordinance is 
unnecessary. A City-wide ordinance and a separate memorandum of understanding (MOU) between the City 
and the DDA would be more appropriate. Staff also has concerns about the appropriateness of a City ordinance 
directing the DDA to perform specific tasks with the DDA’s independently managed funds, as they are a quasi-
governmental entity funded outside the City by a mill levy, with exclusive oversight of their finances.

Chapter 12.28 DOWNTOWN GRAFFITI ABATEMENT PILOT PROGRAM 
 
12.28.010 - Creation. 
 
There is hereby created the "Downtown Graffiti Abatement Pilot Program" (pilot program).  
 
12.28.020 - Definitions. 
 
The following definitions shall apply in the interpretation and enforcement of this chapter:  
 
"Downtown Development Authority (DDA)" means the body corporate created by the city of 
Cheyenne pursuant to Chapter 2.76 of the Municipal Code of the city of Cheyenne.  
 
"Graffiti" means any unauthorized inscription, word, figure, painting or other defacement that 
is written, marked, etched, scratched, sprayed, drawn, painted or engraved on or otherwise affixed 
to any surface or public or private property, to the extent th at the graffiti was not given written 
authorization in advance by the property owner, or, despite written advance authorization, is 
otherwise deemed a public nuisance by the downtown development authority.  
 
"Property" means any real property, including structures and fixtures.  
 
"Property owner" means a person, real or corporate, partnership or other business venture 
owning any property within the boundaries of the downtown development district as set forth in 
Section 2.76.040.  
 
"Structure" means any improvement to real property and shall include, but not be limited to, 
buildings, walls, doors, utility boxes, sidewalks, windows, fixtures, sheds, storage units, semi -
tractor trailers, mobile storage and masonry.

12.28.030 - Prohibited acts. 
 
It shall be unlawful for any person to apply graffiti to any natural or man-made surface on any 
city-owned property or, without the permission of the property owner on any non -city-owned 
property.  
 
12.28.040 - Program creation and opt-out. 
 
A. The DDA shall develop and implement the pilot program inside the boundaries of the 
downtown development district designated in Section 2.76.040.  
 
B. The pilot program shall include at least the following elements: 
 
1. Written notification sent to all property owners by certified mail of the scope and 
operation of the pilot program;  
 
2. An option to opt-out of the pilot program by responding in writing to the DDA within 
thirty (30) days of the postmarked date of the DDA's written notification of the pilot 
program. Any property owner failing to respond to the DDA's notice as set forth in this 
section is presumed to consent to inclusion in the pilot program; and  
 
3. A property owner's failure to respond to the opt -out notice of the pilot program as set 
forth above shall be deemed a release of the city and DDA from liability for property 
damage and personal injury as it relates to graffiti abatement performed in accordance 
with the provisions of this chapter.  
 
C. Written notification to all property owners shall be addressed to the mailing address of the 
property owner as identified by the Laramie County Tax Assessor on the Laramie County 
Cooperative GIS interactive parcel map site.  
  
12.28.050 - Removal of graffiti by DDA. 
 
A. Whenever the DDA determines that graffiti exists within the boundaries of the downtown 
development district on property which is visible to the public, the DDA may remove such 
graffiti to the extent allowed within their annual line item budget.  
 
B. Graffiti may be removed pursuant to the following provisions:  
 
1. Use of Public Funds. Whenever the DDA becomes aware or is notified and determines 
that graffiti is located on publicly or privately owned property viewable from a public 
or quasi-public place, the DDA shall be authorized to use DDA funds for the removal 
of the graffiti, or for the painting over, power cleaning, or repairing of the graffiti, but 
is not authorized to provide for the painting or repair of any more extensive an area 
than where the graffiti is located.

2. Right of Entry on Private Property. All property located within the boundaries of the 
downtown development district owned by property owners who fail to opt -out of the 
pilot program as set forth in Section 12.28.030 will be subject to graffiti abatement by 
the DDA. 
 
3. The DDA may only abate graffiti existing on property within the downtown 
development district owned by property owners who did not opt -out of the pilot 
program.  
 
4. No graffiti determined to exist on property owned by property owners who elect to opt-
out of the pilot program shall be abated by the DDA.
