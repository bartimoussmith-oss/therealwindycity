MEMORANDUM  
TO:  Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council 
FROM: TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager 
DATE: April 8, 2025 
SUBJECT: Consideration of Bid #E-17-25 for furnishing a 2000 Gallon Water Truck for the 
City of Cheyenne, Street & Alley Division 
 ______________________________________________ 
 Three (3) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.   
 
The Three (3) bids were received from: 
 
1. Floyd's Truck Center Inc.  Cheyenne, Wyoming. 
2. McCandless Truck Center, LLC.  Cheyenne, Wyoming. 
3. Dawson Infrastructure Solutions.  Denver, Colorado.  Bid Rejected 
 
The bids were reviewed by Bill Gonzalez (Street & Alley), Mike Schlabs (Street & Alley), Bryan 
Bell (Fleet Maintenance), Craig LaVoy (Public Works), and Vicki Nemecek (Public Works).   
 
The Bid from Dawson Infrastructure Solutions has been rejected, due to not meeting many of the 
required Bid Specifications: 
1. General, Item 33, Does Not Meet Spec for Road speed with cruise to be 65 m.p.h. 
2. Tires, Item 3, Not Providing One wheel and open block tire for spare. 
3. Manuals, Items 1-5, Not Providing Required Manuals 
4. Training, Item 1, Not Provided Required Training 
5. Engine Programming, Item 2, Does Not Meet Spec for Engine should regen at idle. 
6. Engine Programming, Item  4, Does Not Meet Spec  for Engine Altitude 
Programming. 
7. Pump, Item 1, Does Not Meet Spec for Water Pump 
 
Their recommendation is to accept the next lowest responsive bid from Floyd’s Truck Center. 
 
The Bidding Documents contain a Surcharge and Escalator – Special Provision, that allows for 
the review of surcharges related to the following items:  Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) 
and/or Body surcharges applied to the cost of the vehicles after the bid opening date & Model 
year escalators.  The Surcharge & Escalator Contingency, is for an amount not-to-exceed Fifteen 
Percent (15%), of the total value of  requested items on the Bid. 
 

The total requested bid award is for $416,227.70 (Bid Option 1, 2026 Freightliner M2 106 Plus, 
with Ranco Water Body).  
 
This includes the 2000 Gallon Water  Truck ($359,818.00), an Extended Warranty for Engine & 
Aftertreatment for 5 Years / 100K Miles ($1,800.00), Extended Warranty for Transmission for 5 
Years / 100K Miles ($637.00), PLUS 15% Contingency ($53,972.70).
