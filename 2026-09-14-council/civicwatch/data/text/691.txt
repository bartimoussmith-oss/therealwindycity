BACKGROUND: 
This is a landowner petition to annex the property into the City. 
 
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1. The proposed zoning assignment is the most analogous City zone to the current zone 
when taking into consideration allowed uses, current uses, or surrounding City zones. 
Zoning for standard county zones shall be assigned based on the policy document, 
while zones not listed on the policy document and terminated City zones shall be 
assigned a City zone on a case-by-case basis shall be assigned a City zone based on: 
a. Permitted uses, 
b. Uses that could reasonably be anticipated for conditional use approval 
under the current zoning, 
c. Current uses, or 
d. Surrounding City zoning, 
as deemed most suitable for the property being assigned a zone in terms of allowing 
the landowner to continue the current use of the land while respecting the current and 
anticipated uses of surrounding property. 
 
This annexation contains two different parcels. The larger parcel is currently zoned Overland PUD in 
the County. The smaller parcel is currently zoned County Light Industrial. The Overland PUD was 
zoned for residential, commercial, business, and industrial development in this area. These zones 
include land use allowances for Industrial, Commercial, Residential, and Open Space. County Light 
Industrial allows for lighter industrial uses. The City zoned parcels that are around these two parcels 
are AG Agriculture, Overland PUD, P Public, and County LI Light Industrial. The Future Land Use Map 
designates most of this land as Agricultural/Rural, Open Space and Parks. A small portion of the 
southern parcel is designated Mixed-Used Employment.  
The Overland PUD land use designations are outlined in its Ordinance. The Open Space is for area 
with slopes over 15% and natural drainage systems with the intent that developers dedicate the 
public the primary open space. Business is the location for offices and service-oriented uses. Major 
uses will be non-retail business facilities, with some retail being secondary. Industrial is for

manufacturing and warehousing activities, with intended for businesses that need truck access, along 
rail, or near interstate highway or interchange access. 
 
The parcels are both vacant with limited agriculture use, mainly grazing. 
 
Staff considered various options for the assigned zoning, including:  
- BP Business Park 
- LI Light Industrial 
- HI Heavy Industrial 
- CB Community Business 
- AG Agriculture 
After analyzing the potential options, Staff choose the AG Agriculture zone as the assigned zone for 
the northern parcel and LI Light Industrial for the southern parcel. The rationale behind each zone is 
outlined below. 
 
The BP Business Park zone district Intent and Applicability is primarily for small and large industrial 
development with retail and office uses that support the industrial developments, Business Parks are 
typically located near major transportation routes, and the district has to contain 320 acres. The 
associated annexation is roughly 650 acres. While it fits for the Industrial section of the PUD and 
applicable to the Business due to the supporting nature of commercial development in this zone 
district. It conflicts with Open Space designation of PUD. Thus, this does not make BP wholly 
appropriate for the assigned zone with the annexation. This zone district also does not cover the 
current use of the either parcel. The BP zone is also one of the City’s more intense zone districts and 
there is little on or around the property that is currently developed with this high intensity of uses. 
 
The LI Light Industrial zone district Intent and Applicability is for environmentally controlled 
manufacturing, or intensive employment uses that may have accessory commercial, service, office 
uses and cannot readily fit into other general business or mixed-use area due to nature or intensity of 
operations. Light Industrial zones are located in areas that provide substantial buffers to surrounding 
sites to minimize the potential for incompatible and negative impacts of uses in the district. This is 
appropriate for all of the Industrial based areas, but this does not cover the Business or Open Space 
areas. This is not appropriate as the assigned zoning for the whole annexed property but rather 
chunks of it, with other being assigned other zones. For the smaller parcel, Light Industrial would be 
the most appropriate considering the County zoning was Light Industrial. This zone district also does 
not cover the current use of the largest parcel. 
 
The HI Heavy Industrial zone district Intent and Applicability is for very high intensity industrial uses 
that should be isolated and buffered to protect the community to ensure unimpeded operation of the 
use. They cannot fit in general business or mixed-use areas due to the nature or intensity of 
operations. Larger land areas that are remote from other differing or incompatible to minimize the 
potential for incompatible and negative impacts of the uses. This is appropriate for all of the Industrial 
based areas, but this does not cover the Business or Open Space areas. This is not appropriate as the 
assigned zoning for the whole annexed property but rather chunks of it, with other being assigned 
other zones. This zone district also does not cover the current use of either parcel. 
 
The CB Community Business zone district Intent and Applicability is intended for Retail, Employment, 
Service and Civic uses that may serve a number of residential neighborhoods or other districts in 
meeting daily needs, discretionary needs, or other business functions. It is applicable in area where 
moderate- or large-scale business uses are appropriate, specifically larger Mixed-use Commercial

Activity Center. This zone would cover the Business portion of the designations of the PUD but not 
the Industrial or Open Space. Within the Community Business zone, select Industrial uses are 
Conditional Use in the CB zone district. This zone district also does not cover the current use of either 
parcel. 
 
The AG Agricultural zone district Intent and Applicability is for agriculture uses or other use of land in 
a natural state. Agriculture-related residential uses and agriculture-related businesses are included 
provided they support the primary use of the land for farming, ranching, or agriculture uses.  All uses 
require little to no public infrastructure in the short term. It is applicable in areas with significant 
natural lands or open spaces. The Agricultural zone district is also applicable as interim or holding 
zone for areas that may be further developed, re-subdivided and reclassified in the long term. This 
would cover the Open Space designation within the PUD but does not cover the Business or 
Industrial. Agriculture would be appropriate based on existing use of vacant/grazing lands as and due 
to the zone being a short-term holding zone for future development. It would also be appropriate 
due to the zoning of the touching City parcel is Agricultural.  
 
For the larger parcel, the most appropriate assigned zone is AG Agricultural based off of the current 
use and the variety of uses of the land under the Overland PUD creates ambiguity on a direct City 
zone. The smaller parcel is currently zoned County LI Light Industrial and City zone that is most 
analogous is LI Light Industrial. 
 
This assigned zoning complies with review criterion 1.
