## F. The 3-minute version for the room

1. **Four single-bid lines, $4.86 M, 2025.** Surface Seal, thermoplastic, Safe Routes, HVAC maintenance. Show up = win or reprice.
2. **Verticals are a 4-firm club.** Transit Facility and Botanic Gardens drew the same four names. Fifth chair is open.
3. **Muni Building is the whale.** FCI holds CMAR; GMP not set; PlanOne's scope says sub pre-bid is coming; $22 M more on the November ballot. Be a sub *now*.
4. **$118 M ballot + 1% renewal** = the pipeline for 2027–2030 is already written. Section D is the list.
5. **Spreads of 16–189%** on trail/drainage/painting/asbestos work — the low bidder isn't always the sharp one; there's real room.
