## 1. RED-TEAM FINDINGS (ranked by attack value)

### 🔴 #1 — Item 23: Resolution OVERRULING the Planning Commission's 6-0 denial (Orchard Hills FLUM, PUDC-26-176)
- Planning Commission voted **6-0 unanimously to deny** on Sept 8 (memo 150451).
- **City staff ALSO recommended denial** — staff report says the request fails review criteria **1, 2, 7 and 8** (150453, p.2 & p.7). Staff conclusion: the area "was anticipated to be residential"; the 2022 change to the south was done "to open the door for residential… to the north."
- Staff explicitly cites **Policy 2.5.A (Community Housing)**: this amendment "will reduce the ability for Cheyenne to provide available permanent housing connected to existing residential neighborhoods."
- Applicant "has not specified a proposed use" on paper but "indicated verbally in meetings that **data center development** is anticipated" (150453 p.2).
- The ONLY "public comment" in the packet labeled "Neighbor Comment Letter" is **written by the applicant's own engineer, Trey Rinne PE of AVI**, on AVI letterhead (150453 p.~1000). That is not a neighbor letter.
- Staff memo says the FLUM item will be **postponed to Oct 12** to "line up" with annexation/zoning — i.e., bundling so all Orchard Hills items pass in one vote.
- Existing-condition note: the parcel is next to an electrical substation and the Meta campus — applicant's own argument for why housing "isn't appropriate" is that they've already surrounded it with industry.
- **Attack line:** "When your own professional planners AND your own appointed Planning Commission both say no, unanimously, and the only letter of support is from the developer's engineer — what exactly is Council overruling them *with*?"

### 🔴 #2 — Item 22: Hitching Post FLUM (652 acres) — removing Natural & Cultural Resources + Open Space designations
- Resolution title literally says: change "Agricultural/Rural, **Open Space and Parks**, and Mixed-Use Employment to Community Business and **removing the Natural and Cultural Resources Area designation**" (150449).
- Planning Commission split **4-2** (not unanimous).
- Staff's own criterion-8 analysis: "Policies in PlanCheyenne are mixed on this proposal, but, **on the balance, probably would encourage maintaining the current land use designation.**" Yet staff still marks it "largely complies." (150449 p.5)
- Staff report admits the intended end use includes **equestrian activities** which "are not allowed in the BP zone district" — they're zoning it for a use the zone doesn't permit, meaning a text amendment or a bait-and-switch is coming.
- Transportation: "no existing transportation network"; requires Berwick Drive extension and **"changes to the interstate interchange"** — who pays? No fiscal analysis in the packet.
- **Document error:** memo says County Commissioners "are anticipated to hear this item at their meeting on **June 16, 2026**" — a date three months in the past. Memo also says PC heard it Sept 9 while the resolution says Sept 8. Sloppy packet = grounds to postpone.
- **Attack line:** "You cannot 'remove' a Natural and Cultural Resources designation on 650 acres on a consent-agenda referral with a packet that cites a County hearing date that already passed."

### 🟠 #3 — Item 13: Campstool/HR Ranch AG→BP — Planning Commission recommends DENIAL
- Memo (150430): "The Commission **recommends denial** of the zone change from AG Agricultural to BP Business Park."
- Staff report: "**staff has no knowledge of the end use** for this parcel" but notes BP "would allow for… data center development."
- Adjacent south: County rural residential. Adjacent west: Heavy Industrial pipeline.
- **Attack line:** "A spot rezone to Business Park with no disclosed user, against the Planning Commission's recommendation, next to rural homes."

### 🟠 #4 — Item 20: FY2026 Re-appropriation — $1,631,979 from General Fund reserves (+$3,335,391 non-GF)

- **Attack line:** "$1.6M out of reserves after the year closed, including a new Council Chambers — while we're told there's no money for [X]. And a pattern of at-fault City vehicle accidents that nobody is asked to explain."

### 🟠 #5 — Item 25: Gaming Center + Historic Horse Racing terminals, 1734 Meadowland Dr
- Same operator **already applied and was DENIED by the Board of Adjustment** as a Conditional Use; re-filed under the "Special Use" path where Council, not the BOA, decides (150455 p.~703).
- Nearest preschool ~1,300 ft on Stillwater Ave; report notes it's "less than 1,000 feet" from something (line 907 — read carefully; likely another existing gaming/liquor site).
- Applicant letter is from **Bally's**. No traffic or noise study required. Coupled with **Item 28a** — the liquor license currently held by "1734 Partners LLC" at the same address is being transferred out to a new location, which clears the decks for the gaming use.
- Referral only tonight — but on **consent**.

Also noteworthy: consultant **Lotus Engineering** (who wrote it) also bid on the $351k Comp Plan contract (26d) and lost.

### 🟡 #7 — Process/Transparency issues (usable in any speech)
- Every land-use memo boasts "**no public comments**" — the Orchard Hills page had 191–208 views, Hitching Post 15–85 views. Mailed notice went out Aug 13; PC hearing Sept 8; Council Sept 14. Six days between the PC vote and Council introduction.
- Staff openly states items are being **introduced early "so that all items related to [the] development can be discussed at the same time"** — that's bundling to reduce individual scrutiny.
- Items 7–9 (Cox Ranch, 1,260 acres) were **postponed from April 27** — 4½ months — yet packet says "Staff report attached and unchanged" (dated March 30). No update on what changed in five months.
- **Item 6a/6b public hearings are on statutory annexation compliance** (W.S. 15-1-402) — this is the ONLY place the public formally gets a hearing on the annexations; the zoning/FLUM public hearings already happened at Planning Commission.
- Item 26d: paying **$351,790** for "CheyenneNext" comp plan update (adoption Nov 2027) while approving 2,000+ acres of FLUM amendments that pre-empt it.
- Item 31: Executive session under §16-4-405(a)(x) — that's the "**consideration of information classified as confidential by law**" exception; ask which item it relates to.

### SPEECH A — Orchard Hills / Item 23 (and 14, 15, 16, 24, 6a)
*Use this if you get only ONE comment slot. It's the strongest.*

Mayor Collins, members of Council — my name is Charles Miller, I'm a Cheyenne resident, and I'm speaking on Items 23, 24, 14, 15 and 16 — the Orchard Hills package on South Greeley Highway.

Item 23 asks you to *overrule* the Planning Commission. I want you to hear what you'd be overruling.

On September 8th, the Planning Commission voted **six to nothing** to deny this land-use change. Not four-two. Six to nothing.

And they didn't go rogue — they followed **your own staff**. The staff report in tonight's packet recommends **denial**. It says the request fails review criteria one, two, seven, and eight. It says — and I'm quoting — this change "will reduce the ability for Cheyenne to provide available permanent housing connected to existing residential neighborhoods."

One hundred sixty-two acres that PlanCheyenne set aside for homes, going to Light Industrial. For what? The applicant hasn't written a use on any application. Staff says they "indicated verbally" that it's a data center.

Now look at the one "Neighbor Comment Letter" in the packet. It's on AVI letterhead. It's signed by the applicant's own engineer. That is not a neighbor. That's the developer writing himself a reference.

So here's the picture: your planners say no. Your Planning Commission says no, unanimously. The public got six days between that vote and tonight. And the only support letter comes from the engineer being paid to file the application.

If Council overrules both its professional staff and its appointed commission under those facts, then I'd ask honestly — what is the Planning Commission for?

Three asks.

First — do **not** overrule the Commission. Vote no on 23, or at minimum pull it off consent so the public can see each of you vote on it individually.

Second — do not bundle. Staff has told you in writing they want all of these items lined up for one vote on October 12th. Housing policy for 162 acres deserves its own night.

Third — you're about to spend three hundred fifty-one thousand dollars tonight, on Item 26d, to update the Comprehensive Plan. Let that process decide where our housing goes — not a verbal data-center promise.

Cheyenne says housing is a crisis. Tonight is the test of whether you mean it. Thank you.

*(≈400 words)*

### SPEECH B — Hitching Post District / Item 22 (and 10, 11, 12, 6b) + Item 13
*Use for a second slot, or swap in if Council already pulled Item 23.*

Mayor, Council — Charles Miller, Cheyenne resident. I'm speaking on Item 22 and the related Items 10, 11 and 12 — the Hitching Post District — and on Item 13.

Item 22 is 652 acres south of I-80. Read the title. It changes "Open Space and Parks" to "Community Business" and — quote — "removes the Natural and Cultural Resources Area designation."

You don't *remove* a natural-resources designation from 650 acres as a consent-agenda referral. That's a decision the whole community should hear about, and right now the packet says the project page had **fifteen views**. Fifteen.

Your own staff report, criterion eight, says PlanCheyenne's policies — quote — "on the balance, probably would encourage maintaining the current land use designation." The Planning Commission split four to two. That is not a consensus. That is a coin flip on 650 acres.

And the packet has errors. The memo says the County Commissioners are "anticipated" to hear this on **June 16th, 2026** — that date was three months ago. One page says the Commission heard it September 8th; another says September 9th. If the paperwork can't get the dates right, it isn't ready.

The staff report also admits the intended use needs equestrian activity — which the Business Park zone **does not allow**. So you'd be zoning land for a use that's prohibited in that zone, and somebody is going to be back here asking for a text amendment. Tell us that now.

Nowhere in this packet is a fiscal analysis. Staff says it needs a Berwick Drive extension and "changes to the interstate interchange." Who pays for an interchange? Not in the packet.

Quickly, Item 13 — Campstool and HR Ranch Road. Your Planning Commission recommended **denial**. Staff writes that it "has no knowledge of the end use." Seventy-four acres, no user, next to rural homes, Business Park zoning that allows data centers. That's a spot rezone on faith.

My asks: Pull Items 22 and 13 from the consent agenda. Postpone 22 until the County has actually commented and the packet is corrected. And commit — on the record — that no annexation, no rezone, and no land-use amendment for these 2,000 acres gets a final vote until a public fiscal and water-demand analysis is published.

Cheyenne has water, land, and a plan. Tonight you're being asked to give away all three in one motion. Slow down. Thank you.

*(≈410 words)*

### SPEECH C — Money & Transparency: Items 20, 26d, 25 and process
*Use if the land-use items get pulled/postponed early and you want a fiscal-accountability angle instead.*

Mayor, Council — Charles Miller, Cheyenne resident. I want to talk about money and process — Items 20, 26d, and 25.

Item 20 re-appropriates one million, six hundred thirty-one thousand dollars out of General Fund reserves for a fiscal year that's already over. Some of it is unavoidable — fuel, utilities. But look at what else is in there.

Three hundred seventy-eight thousand for the Communications Center overrun. A hundred forty-nine thousand for jail housing we didn't budget. Three hundred fifty thousand for a property purchase. A hundred fifty-five thousand toward designing the **new Council Chambers** in the old senior center.

And ninety-seven thousand in accident and damage claims — and I counted: at least eight of those are listed as "City employee was at fault." Nobody in this packet is asked why.

This is being referred on consent. A million-six out of reserves should not be a consent item. Pull it. Let Finance Committee ask the questions out loud.

Item 26d — three hundred fifty-one thousand dollars for CheyenneNext, the comprehensive plan update, adoption in November 2027. I support planning. But on the same agenda you're amending that very plan for over two thousand acres — Orchard Hills, Hitching Post, Campstool, Cox Ranch. Why pay a consultant to write a plan you're rewriting tonight, one developer at a time? Either the plan matters or it doesn't.

Item 25 — the gaming center and historic horse racing terminals at 1734 Meadowland. This same site was already denied by the Board of Adjustment this year. Now it's back through a different door — a Special Use where Council decides instead of the Board. Item 28a on the same agenda transfers the liquor license *out* of that address. That's a coordinated package, and it deserves to be discussed as one, in public, not referred on consent.

Last thing — process. Every land-use memo tonight says "no public comment received." The public hearing at Planning Commission was September 8th. Tonight is September 14th. Six days. Then staff wrote — in the packet — that items are being introduced early so everything can be "discussed at the same time" on October 12th. That's not efficiency. That's bundling.

My asks: pull Items 20, 22, 23 and 25 from consent. Give each an individual roll-call vote. And before you spend a dollar on a new Council Chambers, spend it on notice — so the people in those chambers actually know what's being decided. Thank you.

*(≈415 words)*
