## Honest assessment of tonight's testimony
**Landed:** 6(a) speech — clean, on-statute, notice/contiguity/vacant-land points all got repeated by other speakers and staff had to address the notice dates on the record.

**Backfired:**
- Item 7 speech: Rule 65 TRO / writ of prohibition / "fatal liability" / "void ab initio" language. Nobody with standing was named. Read as a threat, not an argument; cut off mid-sentence. Case cite ("Town of Worland v. Lang") was garbled on delivery.
- Item 8: "holding zone fraud" — the AG-as-holding-zone quote was good, framing as "fraud" wasn't.
- Item 9: procedural objection to the debate limit — the limit applied to **council members only**, so the objection was misdirected and the mayor ruled it out of order. Developer "tag-team" point was valid but landed inside a ruling against you.
- Voucher report: specific dollar claims and "decorative controls" — Aldrich/Cosgrave immediately answered with "annual outside audit, zero findings, credit-card audit matched every receipt." That exchange cost credibility for the rest of the night.
- Other business: cut off again.

**Net:** first speech built credibility; the last four spent it. Two councilmembers (Wolf, Laybourn) are making the same substantive points inside the rail — align with them, don't out-shout them.

## What actually moves votes now
Votes are 6–3. You need **two** flips for a 5–4. Realistic targets: **White** (sat on comp-plan committee, cares about process) and **Rainey** (local-control instinct; voted no on 18 for "stay in our lane" reasons). Esquibel/Cosgrave/Aldrich/Collins are locked.

Arguments that can flip White/Rainey:
- **Sequencing:** approving BP zoning + FLUM amendments for ~2,150 acres while paying $351,790 to rewrite the plan that governs them. (Laybourn already teed this up.)
- **Written commitments before 3rd reading:** Skybox admitted nothing is signed — no grazing lease, no "Commitment to Cheyenne." Trammell Crow promised binding conditions. Ask Council to condition 3rd reading on executed development agreements. Wolf said exactly this ("less than a month… to develop agreements").
- **Orchard Hills:** PC 6‑0 + staff denial. This is the one item where the City's own record is against it.
