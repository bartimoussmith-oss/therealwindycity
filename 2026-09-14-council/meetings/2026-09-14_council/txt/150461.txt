AGREEMENT FOR JUVENILE PROBATION SERVICES 
between 
LARAMIE COUNTY, WYOMING and  
CITY OF CHEYENNE, OFFICE OF YOUTH ALTERNATIVES 
 
This Agreement is between Laramie County, Wyoming (“County”), 310 West 19 th Street, 
Suite 300, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82003 -0608 and the City of Cheyenne, Office of Youth 
Alternatives, (“Contractor”), 1328 Talbot Court, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001 . The parties hereto 
do mutually agree as set forth below. 
I. PURPOSE 
a. To deliver services for juveniles appearing in the juvenile division of Laramie 
County Circuit Court and the Municipal Court of the City of Cheyenne. 
 
b. To implement a supervised probation program for juveniles appearing in the 
juvenile division of Laramie County Circuit Court and the Municipal Court of the 
City of Cheyenne. Juveniles are defined as those individuals under the age of 
eighteen (18). 
 
c. To provide coordination of drug and alcohol services to Juveniles appearing in 
the Laramie County Circuit and City of Cheyenne Municipal Courts. 
 
II. TERM 
This Agreement shall commence on July 1, 2026, and shall remain in full force and 
effect through June 30, 2027 , unless terminated in accordance with the terms and 
conditions set forth herein. 
 
III. RESPONSIBILITIES OF COUNTY 
County shall pay to Contractor an amount to not to exceed Eighty-Six Thousand, Three 
Hundred Four Dollars ($86,304.00). Payment will be made in a series of twelve (12) 
monthly payments, with each such monthly payment being for one twelfth (1/12) of 
the total amount of payment under this contract. Payments may be suspended for any 
period which services are not rendered. Each monthly payment will be made only after 
receipt of Contractor’s invoice to County. Payment shall be in accordance with W.S. 
§ 16-6-602 (as amended). 
 
IV. RESPONSIBILITIES OF CONTRACTOR 
a. Attendance and participation in the Juvenile Screening Committee and pre-
court assessment and screening of juveniles scheduled to appear in the Courts.

b. Coordination of Juvenile Diversion and Probation Services, which includes: 
 
i. monitoring and tracking of all youth assigned to the program; 
ii. schedule and notify all parents and youth for court attendance; 
iii. schedule random law enforcement home checks; 
iv. coordinate treatment program and client case reviews; 
v. ensure all other orders of the court(s) are adhered to; 
vi. provide consultation to the Circuit and Municipal Court Judges as requested; 
 
c. Serve as the probation officer(s) to all cases where a juvenile is placed on 
probation. Probation officers will: 
 
i. provide direct supervision of assigned cases; 
ii. ensure random urinalysis tests are conducted; 
iii. monitor school attendance and performance; 
iv. review misconduct and recommend appropriate graduated sanctions; 
v. conduct regular (at least monthly) meetings with parents; 
 
d. Provide probation supervision of all assigned cases from the Municipal  and 
Circuit courts consistent with the services listed in section 3 above. 
 
e. Contractor shall maintain complete case records of the services provided 
pursuant to this agreement. Contractor shall retain all records generated while 
performing service pursuant to this agreement for three (3) years after the 
County makes final payment and all other matters relating to this Agreement 
are concluded. Contractor shall provide quarterly reports detailing level of 
services to the Laramie County Grants Manager on a timely basis. Contractor 
shall permit access by the County or any of its duly authorized representatives 
to any books, documents, papers and records of the Contractor which are 
directly pertinent to this specific Agreement for purposes including but not 
limited to audit, examination, excerpts, and transcriptions.
