SUBJECT:  Right-of-Way Use Agreement between CSRE Properties  Wyoming, LLC and the 
City of Cheyenne 
 
 
Please find attached a Right-of-Way Use Agreement between CSRE Properties Wyoming, LLC 
and the City of Cheyenne for two connections of storm sewer pipe from the newly constructed 
detention ponds on the site to the open channel storm water network in the Venture Drive right 
of way. 
 
In order to properly release storm water from the site at 9010 Venture Drive, these outlet pipes 
need to extend into the public right-of-way to connect to the existing stormwater network. The 
property owner will be responsible for the maintenance of the pipes from their system to the 
open channel storm network in the Venture Drive Right-of-Way. 
 
There are no financial implications associated with this Right-of-Way Use Agreement. Your 
approval of this agreement is respectfully requested.

RIGHT-OF-WAY USE AGREEMENT 
 This Right-of-Way Use Agreement (“Agreement”) is by and between the City of 
Cheyenne, a municipal corporation organized and existing under the laws of the State of Wyoming 
(“City”), whose address is 2101 O ’Neil Ave nue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001 , and  CSRE 
Properties Wyoming, LLC  (“Licensee”), whose address is 10624 S. Eastern Ave Ste A638, 
Henderson, NV 8 9052.  The City and the Licensee are collectively referred to as “Party” or 
“Parties.”  This Agreement shall become effective upon the date of the last signature affixed hereto. 
RECITALS: 
 WHEREAS, the Licensee holds title to and has developed approximately 10.01 acres of 
land in the City of Cheyenne, County of Laramie, State of Wyoming  (“Property”), more 
particularly described as being: 
Lot 1 , Block 1, Campstool Addition, 7th Filing, City of Cheyenne, Laramie County, 
Wyoming;  
and 
 
 WHEREAS, the Property is served by an exclusive underground drainage facility which 
encroaches into the public right-of-way as shown on Exhibit A, attached hereto and incorporated 
by reference (“Facility”); and 
 WHEREAS, the City is prepared to grant a license to use a utility right-of-way (“License”), 
provided the Licensee agrees to certain terms and conditions. 
 NOW, THEREFORE, based on good and valuable consideration, the receipt and 
sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows: 
 1. Grant of License .  T he City grants to Licensee, its successors and assigns, the 
temporary, non -exclusive privilege of using the C ity right-of-way, commonly referred to as 
Venture Drive, covering a portion of the lands described as Township 14  North, Range 65  West 
of the 6 th P.M., Laramie County, Wyoming, NE1/4NE1/4 of Section 31, for  a distance of 
approximately 34 feet and 29 feet for the western and eastern facilities, respectively,  as shown in 
Exhibit A to be used as a detention pond  outlet conveyance pipe , specifically subject to the 
remaining terms and conditions set forth herein.  The Licensee hereby accepts this License, subject 
to the terms and conditions contained herein, and subject to any requirements or limitations of law, 
now or in the future, which may restrict the use or enjoyment of this License. 
 2. Term; Termination.  This Agreement shall not be effective until it is approved by 
the Parties.  This Agreement is a covenant running with the Property.  Upon thirty (30) days written 
notice, the City may terminate this Agreement and revoke the License granted hereby.  Nothing in 
this Agreement, nor the act of entering into this Agreement, shall be construed as a limitation of 
the City’s authority to modify or abandon the right-of-way the City now has. 
 3. Responsibilities of Licensee .  The Licensee, its successors and assigns, covenant 
and agree to perform and abide by each and every of the following:
