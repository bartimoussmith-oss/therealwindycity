(c) That the annexation of said area is for the protection of health, safety, and welfare

(d) That the urban development of the area sought to be annexed will constitute a 
natural, geographical, economical and social part of the City of Cheyenne; 
 
(e) That the area is a logical and feasible addition to the City and that the extension of

services customarily available in the City of Cheyenne can , within reason , be 
furnished;

Overlands PUD, LU – Land Use, and LI – Light Industrial by the Laramie County 
Commissioners; and

governing body is prepared to designate a utility to serve the annexed area pursuant 
to W.S. 15-1-410, and to authorize the designated utility to serve the entire annexed 
area.

map attached as Exhibit A is hereby annexed to the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, and shall become 
a part thereof.

the official City Map of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming.

1) An annexation of the area is for the protection of the health, safety and welfare of 
the persons residing in the area and in the city or town.  [WS 15-1-402 (a) (i)] 
Annexation of these two parcels would increase the health, safety, and welfare of future 
development as being within City limit would give them access to centralized water and sewer 
line through the Board of Public Utilities, which have existing lines located at southeast Clear 
Creek Parkway. . Access to accredited fire department would improve the standards of persons 
living and working within the parcel. These parcels would also be subject to City development 
standards, which typically require a higher standard than County development standards. Since 
this is a landowner petition annexation, the landowner has determined that it would be beneficial 
to the welfare of the parcels to have access to these services.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion. 
 
2) The urban development of the area sought to be annexed would constitute a 
natural, geographical, economical and social part of the annexing city or town. [WS 
15-1-402 (a) (ii)] 
 
The parcels are currently vacant but are located southwest of the interchange of Interstate 80 & 
25. There is City land surrounding both parcels. The largest parcel is surrounded by land that is 
zoned Agriculture. That parcel was zoned Agricultural due to the multiple flood zones within the 
area that affect its development ability. Adjacent to the smaller parcel are commercial and public 
developments that connect to more commercial and industrial development along the I-80 and I-
25 interchange, and I-25 corridor. The proximity to City development, urban uses, and 
infrastructure make the area a natural extension of the City.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion. 
   
3) The area sought to be annexed is a logical and feasible addition to the annexing 
city or town and the extension of basic and other services customarily available to 
residents of the city or town shall, within reason, be available to the area proposed 
to be annexed. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (iii)] 
 
The area is a logical and feasible addition to the City due to the reasons previously listed. All City 
services normally available to residents will also be made available. Police and fire protection will 
be extended upon annexation. Utilities are within the area and are anticipated to be extended 
with development.  
 
The application complies with this review criterion. 
 
4) The area sought to be annexed is contiguous with or adjacent to the annexing city 
or town, or the area meets the requirements of W.S. 15-1-407. [WS 15-1-402 (a) 
(iv)] 
 
Parcel 1 adjacent to Otto Road of the Hitching Post District is roughly 64.71% contiguous. The 
second parcel adjacent to Clear Creek Parkway is roughly 80.68% contiguous.    
 
The application complies with this review criterion.
