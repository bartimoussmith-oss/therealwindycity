 
 
 
RESOLUTION NO. __________ 
 
ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION OPPOSING FEDERAL LEGISLATION OR 
REGULATIONS THAT INCREASE MAXIMUM TRUCK SIZE OR 
WEIGHT LIMITS ON NATIONAL HIGHWAYS." 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne recognizes the critical role that freight transportation 
plays in supporting Wyoming's economy, interstate commerce, and the movement of goods 
throughout the United States; 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne is committed to maintaining a safe, efficient, and 
fiscally responsible transportation system for residents, businesses, visitors, emergency 
responders, and the goods movement industry alike; 
 
WHEREAS, proposals have been introduced in Congress from time to time to increase the 
allowable weight or size of commercial motor vehicles operating on the National Highway System, 
including proposals to increase the federal gross vehicle weight limit fro m 80,000 pounds to 
91,000 pounds; 
 
WHEREAS, heavier commercial vehicles would place additional stress on roads, bridges, 
intersections, and other transportation infrastructure that was not originally designed for 
substantially greater vehicle weights; 
 
WHEREAS, local governments are responsible for maintaining significant portions of the 
transportation network used daily by commercial trucks, and increased infrastructure deterioration 
resulting from heavier trucks would create additional maintenance and replacement costs without 
a dedicated federal funding source; 
 
WHEREAS, commercial trucks travel billions of miles annually on local roads, and 
communities such as Cheyenne experience substantial truck traffic that is vital to commerce but 
also contributes to ongoing infrastructure maintenance needs; 
 
WHEREAS, winter weather conditions, wind -gusts, steep grades, and periodic traffic 
congestion in and around Cheyenne already create operational and public safety challenges 
involving commercial vehicles; 
 
WHEREAS, the U.S. Department of Transportation has previously reported higher crash 
involvement rates for trucks weighing between 91,000 and 97,000 pounds compared to today's 
federally permitted 80,000-pound trucks, and has found that longer combination ve hicles require 
greater stopping distances and experience higher out-of-service violation rates; 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne believes any proposal to increase truck size or weight 
should fully account for its impacts on public safety, local infrastructure, and taxpayer costs before 
being considered by Congress; and 
 
 
WHEREAS, protecting the safety of Cheyenne residents, preserving public infrastructure, 
and avoiding unfunded federal mandates are fundamental responsibilities of the City. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY 
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the City of Cheyenne formally opposes federal legislation or 
administrative action that would increase the allowable size or weight of commercial motor 
vehicles operating on the National Highway System unless and until it can be demonstrated that 
such increases will not adversely impact public safety, local infrastructure, or impose additional 
unfunded costs on state and local governments; 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the City encourages Congress to preserve existing 
federal truck size and weight limits and to reject proposals that would authorize heavier or larger 
commercial vehicles without comprehensive safety analysis and dedicated funding to address 
resulting infrastructure impacts; and 
 
BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED, that the City Clerk is directed to transmit copies of this 
Resolution to: 
 
• Wyoming's Congressional Delegation; 
• The Governor of Wyoming; 
• The Wyoming Department of Transportation; 
• The Wyoming Association of Municipalities; and 
• Other appropriate federal and state officials.
