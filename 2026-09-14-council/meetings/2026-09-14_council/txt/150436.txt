BACKGROUND: 
 
This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from AG Agricultural which will be assigned upon 
annexation to LI Light Industrial for roughly 162.42 acres of land. The land is currently vacant or used for 
agricultural purposes. The request to LI Light Industrial is to allow for future industrial development. No specific 
use has been identified at this time. The application has been submitted concurrently with a proposed future 
land use map amendment to change the subject property to Mixed-use Employment. Staff’s discussion 
and recommendation on the proposed future land use map amendment are found in that application’s 
staff report (PUDC-26-176).

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA: 
 
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the 
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below: 
 
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical 
patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1) 
 
The property is currently designated Mixed-use Residential by the Comprehensive Plan, but a 
comprehensive plan amendment has been submitted to change the designation of the entire 
property to Mixed-use Employment. 
 
The current designations anticipate development that is a mix of residential and commercial. As 
the LI zone encourages industrial uses and practically prohibits residential uses, the LI zoning 
would not be in accordance with the comprehensive plan for this area. The Mixed-use 
Employment designation anticipates a mix of industrial and commercial uses, and if the entire 
property were designated this way the LI zone would be appropriate based on the future land use 
designation for the subject property. 
 
Support for this application in policies in PlanCheyenne also hinge on the future land use map 
amendment proposal. For example, policies within PlanCheyenne encourage a balanced mix of 
uses in the Cheyenne region using the future land use map as a guide (1.1.A), the flexibility to 
respond to market demands (1.1.B), meeting community housing needs (2.5.A), dispersing 
housing throughout the community (2.5.B), a business-supportive environment (3.1.C), and 
providing opportunities for a range of industrial uses (3.5.A). 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property does not comply with review criterion 1 
under the current future land use map designations but would comply if the concurrent 
application for future land use map designation change is approved. 
 
2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering 
existing zoning, the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies 
created under the guidance of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2) 
 
Nearby property is zoned LI in the City to the south. Property directly north is zone County Urban 
Residential Medium-density. County LU Land Use zoning exists to the east, west and north.  
 
The County LU zone allows for a wide variety of uses, including light industrial uses, but such 
industry would need conditional use approval in the LU zone.  
 
The proposed LI zoning is compatible with the current surrounding zoning.  
 
Nearby Future Land Use designations include Mixed-use Employment to the south, Urban 
Residential to the north, and Mixed-use Residential to the east and west. The property is roughly 
25% surrounded by Urban Residential and 50% surrounded by Mixed-use Residential. Based on 
the future land use plans for the area, LI zoning for this parcel would not be considered 
compatible with surrounding properties. However, should the future land use map be amended, 
then LI could be considered appropriate:  even though the property would still be surrounded on

three sides by Mixed-use Residential and Urban Residential designations, the approved 
amendment would indicate a changing use pattern for the area. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property does not comply with review criterion 2 
under the current future land use map designations but would comply if the concurrent 
application for future land use map designation change is approved. 
   
3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards 
and uses of the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject 
property for development according to the standards and uses of the proposed 
zoning district (UDC 2.2.1.d.3) 
  
The property will be zoned AG-Agricultural upon annexation. The AG zone is intended as a 
holding zone pending more specific development actions. Most urban-style zones will allow a 
wider variety of uses than the current zone district. The proposed LI zone has limited design 
standards and allows a wide variety of uses. 
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3. 
 
4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed 
according to the Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed 
zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4) 
 
The large acreages allows for a variety of street network possibilities. The LI zone allows more 
flexibility in street network than some of the more dense residential or commercial zones.  
 
Major streets are designated on the future land use plan running around this property. Future 
development will be expected to provide for any major streets planned in this area. 
 
No changes to the current street network are proposed. Accesses will not be changed with the 
zone change. Infrastructure (streets and utilities) exist in the area. Depending on the proposed 
use, traffic impacts could be significant.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4. 
 
5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of 
the proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific 
standards. (UDC 2.2.1.d.5) 
 
The UDC indicates that the LI district is intended for environmentally-controlled manufacturing or 
intensive employment uses that may have accessory commercial, service or office uses. The LI 
district is applicable to larger land areas that are capable of providing substantial buffers to 
adjacent sites. This district is most appropriate in areas with access to major regional 
transportation infrastructure, specifically areas designated Industrial in the Comprehensive Plan, 
and in limited applications within Mixed-use Employment areas. 
 
The subject property is a larger area capable of providing buffers and could be designated Mixed-
use Employment by the proposed comprehensive plan amendment. However the only current 
PUDC-26-175   September 8, 2026 
5 | Page  
 
access to the site is through a residential neighborhood and no ready access to major regional 
transportation infrastructure is currently available. This site would need to construct or expand 
roadways or accesses with development to be connected to major regional transportation 
infrastructure.  If the area is changed to be designated ‘Mixed Use Employment’, the proposed 
zoning may be able to meet the intent and applicability of the LI zone in the UDC.. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property does not comply with review criterion 5 
under the current future land use map designation but largely complies if the concurrent 
application for future land use map designation change is approved.   
  
6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for 
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options 
if any specific development proposed in association with the application is not 
executed after approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6) 
 
There is no specific development proposed for this parcel. The LI zone allows both industrial and 
commercial uses. The property is a large lot that would allow for many different uses.  
 
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6. 
 
7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7) 
 
No major comments were received in regard to the proposed zone change that have not been 
covered under other review criteria.  
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7. 
 
8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare. 
(UDC 2.2.1.d.8) 
 
Cheyenne has had documented housing challenges and taking land anticipated for majority 
residential development and zoning that land Light Industrial would adversely affect the general 
welfare of the community. However, if the future land use designation changes and the land is no 
longer anticipated for residential development, City staff is satisfied that this proposed zone 
change would not adversely affect the public health, safety, or general welfare. 
 
The proposed zone change for the subject property does not comply with review criterion 8 
under the current future land use map designation but would comply if the concurrent 
application for future land use map designation change is approved.

CONCLUSION: 
 
The application has been submitted concurrently with a proposed future land use map amendment to 
change the subject property to Mixed-use Employment. Staff’s conclusion on the proposal to zone land LI 
Light Industrial hinges on the appropriateness of the associated future land use map amendment 
proposal. Should the future land use map amendment be approved, the extension of ‘mixed-use 
employment’-style development would be considered appropriate and the application would largely meet 
all review criteria. On the other hand, if the future land use map amendment is denied, this request would 
fail to meet review criteria 1, 2, 5, and 8. 
 
Staff’s discussion and recommendation on the proposed future land use map amendment are found in 
that application’s staff report.
