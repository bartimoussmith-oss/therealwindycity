## 2. Pattern — the "Sweetgrass template" (July 13, 7h43m meeting)
July 13 is the playbook Cox/Orchard Hills are following:
- Annex (Clear Creek/Gannett Peak) 3rd reading: **9–1** (Moody no).
- Microsoft/Wallick BP rezone: **7–3** (Laybourn, Moody, Wolfe). Moody's motion to delay effective date to Jan 2027 failed; recommit failed; **Wolfe's motion to adjourn failed 3–7.**
- **Resolution overruling Planning Commission** (Sweetgrass, S. Greeley) adopted **7–3.**
- Miller ruled out of order by mayor.
→ Council has already overruled the Planning Commission once this summer on a FLUM for a data-center site, 7–3. Item 23 (Orchard Hills) is the second time. Precedent cuts against you; use it as "pattern," not as "unprecedented."

## 6. What this history hands you for Sept 21 / Oct 12
1. **"Same staff report as March 30."** Five months, a private settlement, a new grazing arrangement, a "Commitment to Cheyenne" — none of it in the record Council voted on. Ask that every commitment be reduced to an executed development agreement *attached to the ordinance* before 3rd reading. Wolfe already said this from the dais.
2. **Urban Service Boundary amendment (Sept 28)** is the real gate — Cox is *outside* the USB today. That's the FLUM vote where the plan itself is being moved to fit the project. Comp-plan contract just approved unanimously makes the timing indefensible.
3. **Moratorium failed 1–9 in May** — don't relitigate it. Council has voted; argue conditions, not pause.
4. **Overrule precedent (July, 7–3)** — frame Item 23 as "second time in 90 days you've overruled your own Planning Commission for the same industry."
5. **Rinne absence:** Cox passed 6–3 with one member absent. A 6–3 with 9 present is not the same as 6–4 with 10. Note it; don't lean on it.
