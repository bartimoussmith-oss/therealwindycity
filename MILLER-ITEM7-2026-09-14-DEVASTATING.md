## LEGAL APPENDIX — FOR FILING / PRA / APPEAL

**A. Bundled-Tract Defect — Two Statutes, One Ordinance — Void Ab Initio**

- **W.S. 15-1-403** — annexation by petition of landowners (Cox Trust) — requires contiguity, 20-day notice, landowner consent.
- **W.S. 15-1-407** — annexation of non-contiguous city-owned land by city — separate authority, no petition, no contiguity required.
- **This ordinance** merges them: Sec1 = 1,252.09ac private under 403, Sec2 = 10.23ac city under 407, Sec3 = single enactment. No Wyoming statute authorizes omnibus annexation under two distinct procedures with different notice/contiguity standards. Violates unified purpose doctrine of 15-1-402 and severability cannot cure because contiguity for city parcel depends entirely on private parcel (staff admission).
- **Exhibit:** Ordinance Sec1(a) + Sec2(a) + Sec3 + Staff Report p1 + p3.

**B. 15-1-402(a)(i) Finding — Future vs Residing — Void**

- Statute: "for the protection of health, safety and welfare of persons residing in the area"
- Finding: "future occupants" [Staff p2]. No persons reside — land vacant per staff. Cannot satisfy. This is not harmless; it's jurisdictional.

**C. 15-1-402(a)(iv) Contiguity — 5.26% + Laundering**
