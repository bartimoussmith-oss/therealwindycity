RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY 
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE 
Office of City Clerk 
 
February 23, 2026 
 
 The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in special session on this date beginning 
at 5:30 p.m. in Council Chambers and via electronic conference meeting, for the purpose of 
collective labor bargaining with the Cheyenne Professional Firefighters, International Association 
of Firefighters, Local No. 279.  Present were: MAYOR – Patrick Collins, COUNCIL MEMBERS 
– Dr. Michelle Aldrich, Dr. Kathy Emmons, Ken Esquibel, Pete Laybourn, Mark Moody, Dr. Mark 
Rinne, Tom Segrave (via Zoom), Jeff White and Lawrence Wolfe .  Also present: Kylie Soden, 
City Clerk , Amber Ash, Chief of Staff, John Brodie, City Attorney, Robin Lockman, City 
Treasurer and Chief Andrew Dykshorn, Deputy Chief Don Wood and Deputy Chief of 
Administrative Services, Glenn Rodgers,  Cheyenne Fire Rescue . The pledge of allegiance was 
recited. 
 
Mayor Collins provided opening remarks and emphasized the importance of the labor -
management discussions that take place in advance of formal negotiations. The proposed Ground 
Rules were reviewed, agreed upon, and signed by both the City and the Union. [Clerk’s Note: The 
City’s spokespersons are Mayor Patrick Collins, City Attorney John Brodie, and Chief of Staff 
Amber Ash. The Union’s spokespersons are Union President Chris Thoms, Union Vice President 
Jason Lindsay, and Union Grievance Representative James Lewis.]  
 
Mayor  Collins announced that additional negotiation meetings will be scheduled in the 
coming weeks to review City and Union proposals. 
   
  There bein g no fur ther business to come before the governing body, the meeting was 
officially adjourned at 5:33 p.m.  
 
 
       Submitted by, 
        
        
       Kylie Soden, City Clerk
