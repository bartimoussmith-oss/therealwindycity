“AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTERS 17.04 INTRODUCTORY PROVISIONS
AND DEFINITIONS AND 17.128 SIGNS OF TITLE 17, ZONING, OF THE CHEYENNE CITY
CODE 2002.”  Mr. Brown moved to approve on third and final reading, seconded by Mr. Pierson.
Upon inquiry, Martin Matsen, Development Director, provided information on the 150 linear ft.
distance for placement of billboards from single family residential properties as proposed in Sec.
17.128.010, G., stating if someone applied for a sign permit a plot plan would be required to reflect
the distance from the property line of the closest residence to the proposed sign pillar, and explained
the use is different than for a multi-family residence.  He confirmed that duplexes and townhomes
are not considered single family residences and if a single family residence is located within a CB
(commercial business) zone and billboards are allowed, the residence would not be afforded the 150
ft. distance protection.  Upon inquiry, Pat Crank, attorney with Speight, McCue and Crank law firm,
representing Ne xtMedia, a dvised N extMedia wo uld c onsider a nd a nalyze a ny re quest f rom a
neighbor involving night time light pollution.  He stated digital billboards are less likely to present
light pollution issues.  Comments followed on neighborhood light pollution concerns, and extending
the 150 ft. distance protection to those zones containing residential housing uses.  Dr. Rinne moved
to amend Sec. 17.128.010, G. to include duplexes and townhouses, seconded by Ms. Ash.  Upon
inquiry reg arding the amendme nt, Mr. Crank cla rified his client would be  ac ceptable to t he
amendment, a nd stated that duplexes and townhouses ar e spec ifically defined in city  code.
Comments followed in support of the amendment.  Motion to amend carried.  Voting “yes” – all
members of the governing body present in Council Chambers.  Upon inquiry regarding placement
of billboards near apartment buildings, Mr. Crank responded with information concerning limited
viable commercial billboard locations relative to the 150 ft. buffer zone restriction and desired high
volume visibility of signs for the benefit of sign advertisers and for public safety messages.  He also
discussed the net reduction in the number of his client’s billboards if the ordina nce is approved.
Main motion as amended carried. Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body present in
Council Chambers.  Following announcement of the vote, Mr. Collins and Mr. Valdez returned to
Council Chambers. (#3859)
