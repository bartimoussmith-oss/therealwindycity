Report on the On-Street Bicycle Striping and Signage Project involving Contract #6270  with
Kolbe Striping, Inc.  Barbara Kloth, Jordan Evans and Travis Kolbo spoke in support of the bike lane
striping proposed for Carey and Pioneer Avenues, and in support generally for the overall project.
Comments included the following topics:  Safety for bicyclists and pedestrians; extensive studies and
work that ha d been a chieved on the appr oved proje ct; alternative modes of tra nsportation;
community importance of a bicycle striping/signage program, and amenities affiliated with attracting
and retaining young professionals and adults in the community.  Upon inquiry, Jeff Wiggins, Parks
and Recreation Department, provided information on a past report, including a "gap analysis", that
had been conducted to review different routes, and potential and identified corridors, and provided
information relating to old signage, and streets used by bicyclists that are not necessarily delineated
to motorists and bicyclists for that use.  Mayor Kaysen stated request for the report had been met.
