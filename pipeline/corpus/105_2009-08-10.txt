Mayor Kaysen opened a Public Hearing to identify the need and opportunity, and to explore
funding and alternative solutions for development of the Saddle Ridge Park.  Teresa Moore, Parks and
Recreation Dept., provided information on the proposed park, stating grant funding is being pursued
through the Wyoming Business Council, under its Business Ready Communities - C ommunity
Enhancement program, for grant funding to assist with building the neighborhood park, located within
Saddle Ridge subdivision in east Cheyenne, as an urban quality amenity.  She stated the developer has
donated 14 acres for the project of which seven acres are proposed for park development at this time
and as part of the grant application.  Ms. Moore advised the park will serve residents and workers in
the workforce housing and business area, and will be located in the vicinity of the Cheyenne LEADS
business park and the new Saddle Ridge school, and also near the Greenway path system, making the
area an attractive amenity to businesses and individuals looking to locate in Cheyenne, enhancing the
area’s quality of life and promoting recreational values.  She indicated the developer is also donating
assistance with site grading and utility stubs.  Ms. Moore clarified the grant, if approved, is a 50/50
matching grant request for $285,820.00 for utilities, pathways, plazas, restrooms, shelter, landscaping,
and two pedestrian bridges to cross drainage ways, and stated the City’s match will come from private
donations, in-kind labor, 5th penny funds, community facility fees, and value of the donated land.  Ms.
Moore stated another grant request has been submitted for $105,000.00 from the Recreation Mill Levy
administered th rough th e school d istrict, w hich, if a pproved, w ill c over th e City’s p ortion for site
grading, playground, 2 multi-purpose sports fields and a park sign. There being no further comments
or input and purpose of the hearing having been met, Mayor Kaysen closed the public hearing.
