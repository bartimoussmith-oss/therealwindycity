City Clerk, offered a suggestion that in lieu of reading the list of renewal applicants for the 2016-
2017 term, as shown on the meeting agenda, the listing itself be recorded in its entirety in the official
minutes of the meeting together with any motions and votes.  She noted, pursuant to state statute
requirements, publication of the notice of renewal applications and posting of notices on respective
licensee premises have been achieved, and the holding of a public hearing before the governing body
has been conducted.  She further stated that the City Attorney has advised that this statement on the
record provides sufficient documentation to identify those individual renewal applications that may
be acted upon at the current meeting.  Upon query by Mayor Kaysen to City Council members, there
was no objection voiced to recognize the renewal applicants as listed on the agenda in lieu of the
Clerk re ading the individual applicants, and May or Ka ysen announc ed the r eading of re newal
applicants’ information would be waived.  Reporting for Finance Committee, Mr. Luna moved to
approve the renewal applications “en masse”, seconded by Ms. Williams.  Mr. Luna stated that at
the Finance Committee meeting staff advised there was one applicant, Nagle-Warren Mansion, LLC,
a restaurant liquor licensee, who has not met rene wal compliance as they have not yet submitted
proof of purchase of at least $500.00 of a lcoholic or malt beve rages during the current licensing
term, which is a state statute requirement.  He reported due to lack of compliance, a motion to divide
the question to allow for a separate vote on this application was made and approved at the Finance
Committee meeting.  Committee members unanimously voted “no” on the motion to approve this
application.  He further reported that following the Finance Committee meeting, and pursuant to
compliance information provided by City Clerk staff at the Committee meeting, a suggestion has
been made that, following a motion to divide the question at the current meeting, a motion be made
to approve the renewal application with conditional wording.  Mr. Luna moved to divide the question
to allow for a separate vote on the renewal application filed by Nagle-Warren Mansion, LLC,
seconded by Ms. Williams.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body.
Mr. Luna moved to appr ove the r enewal liquor license a pplication submitted by Nagle-Warren
Mansion, LLC on the condition the  applicant submits proof of the  statutory minimum $500.00
purchase requirement to the City Clerk’s office no later than 5:00 pm on March 31, 2016, seconded
by Mr. Shanor.  Up on inquiry, Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk, explained that the applicant was in
disagreement with the State Department of Revenue and Taxation regarding payment of a past sales

requirement.  She noted communication ef forts with the applica nt by City Clerk staff during the
licensing renewal process to remedy the lack of compliance.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all
members of the g overning body.  Main motion to appr ove remaining renewal applications “en
masse” carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body.
