 RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
April 14, 2009
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in special session beginning at 12:15 p.m.
in Council Chambers.  Those present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL MEMBERS -
Ms. Ash, Ms. Broyles, Mr. Brown, Ms. Case, Mr. Collins, Mr. Pierson and Mr. Valdez.  Absent: Dr.
Rinne and Mr. Spiker. 
Also present were Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Barb Dorr, City Treasurer, and Dan White, City
Attorney.
Purpose of the special session was to take action on “Contract Modification No. 1 to Contract
No. 5127 between the City of Cheyenne and Wyoming Department of Transportation to include use
of American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009 (ARRA) Federal Funds for the I-180 Project.”
Following the pledge of allegiance, Mr. Valdez, reporting for Finance Committee, moved to approve,
seconded by Ms. Broyles.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” - all members of the governing body present.
