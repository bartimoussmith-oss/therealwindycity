EXPRESSION OF APPRECIATION TO MAYOR’S YOUTH COUNCIL AND STATE OF
THE YOUTH REPORT.  Joseph Chenchar, Youth Council Chair, provided a State of the Youth
Report and described various projects the Youth Council participated in during the past school year,

positive interactions and learning experiences through involvement with local government issues.  He
provided suggestions for future Youth Council members, and expressed appreciation to those who
provided assistance to the group. Mayor Spiker and Jan Spires, Mayor’s Youth Council coordinator,
commended members of the 2007-2008 Mayor’s Youth Council and presented each member present
with a certificate and gift of appreciation. Youth Council members recognized were: Zack Anderson,
Joseph Chenchar, Zeb Glantz, Bryant Jackson, Kelsey James, Ryan Kolb (not in attendance), Ryan
Ragaglia, Tanya Salih, Luke Seebaum, Roxanne Vigil (not in attendance), and Ashley Woods.
