“AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 2.04.160(D)(6), READINGS-COMMITTEE
REPORTS-AMENDMENTS, OF CHAPTER 2.04, MAYOR AND CITY COUNCIL, OF TITLE
2, ADMINISTRATION AND PERSONNEL, OF THE CODE OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING.”  Reporting for Finance Committee, Mr. Segrave moved to approve on second reading,
seconded by Mr. Brown. Upon inquiry as to the purpose of the ordinance, Mayor Spiker advised the
state legislature at its last session increased the amount required for advertising of contract bids or
response from $20,000.00 t o $35,000.00 and t he o rdinance is in co njunction with t he state law
change.  Upon inquiry, City Treasurer Barb Dorr stated the change factors in inflation and increased
costs for public improvement projects; provided information on oversight of those projects involving
costs up to the $35,000.00 threshold, including governing body approval of vouchers, and indicated
the last ordinance involving this topic ($20, 000.00 threshold) was approved in 1995.  Discussion
followed on  p urchasing r equirements, p rocedures involving c alls f or b ids a nd/or r equests f or
proposals/quotes, governing body involvement and oversight in purchasing process, City Council
departmental bu dget a pproval a uthority, a nd C ity Attorney staff w ritten o pinion indicating t he
ordinance sponsor did not have a conflict of interest in sponsoring the ordinance.  Upon inquiry
regarding whether other cities have implemented similar ordinance changes, Mayor Spiker advised
it was unknown if other cities/towns had or were enacting similar changes, and stated the Wyoming
Association of Municipalities (WAM), whose members consist of all 99 communities within the state,
supported and lobbied for the change during the legislative session.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes”
– all members of the governing body with the exception of Mr. Laybourn and Dr. Rinne voting “no”.
