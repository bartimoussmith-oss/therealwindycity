Consideration of Bid #M-22-08 for a contract between the City of Cheyenne and Reiman
Corp., for t he Norris Viaduct Replacement Project (1% Specific Purpose Sales & U se Tax and
Federal Grant funds).  John Rogers, representing Scott Contracting, Inc., Henderson, CO, a bidder
on the project, expressed concerns regarding recommendation for approval relative to selection of
the lowest base bidder and staff proposal involving alternates. He provided a brief summary of bid
differences as submitted by Scott Contracting and Reiman Corporation; discussed a 2002 Wyoming
Supreme Court case; provided copies of a worksheet comparing bids between the two companies,
with alternates, and explained Scott Contracting’s reason for submitting an alternate concrete staining
material other than what was specified in the bid document.  He stated that although Reiman may
have submitted the lowest base bid, Scott Contracting had the lowest overall bid when including the
alternates.  Barton Puryear, in-house staff counsel for Scott Contracting, advised their company’s bid
would provide savings to the City; reviewed bid criteria; indicated that several supplies and manpower
would be obtained locally, and encouraged the bid be awarded to Scott Contracting.  John Fafoutakis
spoke in favor of Scott Contracting, Inc.’s bid due to overall cost savings and use of taxpayers’
dollars.  Gwendolyn Connell and Debbie Brennan spoke in favor of awarding the bid to a Wyoming
company that will assist with the local economy.  Mr. Rogers addressed the local contractor bid vs.
out-of-state bid and explained as there is a substantial amount of Federal funding involved, the bid
was conducted without consideration of the in-state bidder process.  He referenced recent projects
(Laramie County Library and St. Mary’s School) involving out-of-state companies that have hired
subcontractors locally and purchased supplies locally, as Scott Contracting would do.  Mr. Segrave
