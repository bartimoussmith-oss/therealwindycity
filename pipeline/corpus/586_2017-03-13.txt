Intlekofer, City Clerk, offered a suggestion that in lieu  of reading the list of renewal applicants 
for the 2017 -2018 term, as shown on the meeting agenda, the listing itself be recorded in its 
entirety in the official minutes of the meeting together with any motions and votes.  She noted, 
pursuant to state stat ute requirements, publication of the notice of renewal applications and 
posting of notices on respective licensee premises have been achieved, and the holding of a 
public hearing before the governing body has been conducted.  She further stated that the Ci ty

Attorney has advised that this statement on the record provides sufficient documentation to 
identify those individual renewal applications that may be acted upon at the current meeting.  
Upon query by Mayor Orr to City Council members, there was no obje ction voiced to recognize 
the renewal applicants as listed on the agenda in lieu of the Clerk reading the individual 
applicants, and Mayor Orr announced the reading of renewal applicants’ information would be 
waived.  Scott Turner, Marketing Manager, Sam’s  Club, Cheyenne, stated Sam’s Club had been 
notified last week regarding signage at the Club indicating that non -Club members were eligible 
to purchase products from the alcohol dispensing room, and verified the signage had been placed 
in error and that pu rchasers do need to be a Sam’s Club member pursuant to verbal information 
Club representatives had provided the governing body when its retail liquor license application 
was being considered for approval last year.  Reporting for Finance Committee, Mr. Luna moved 
to approve the renewal applications, seconded by Mr. Johnson.  Upon inquiry, Mr. Turner 
provided additional information verifying only those with a Sam’s Club membership card could 
purchase alcohol products at the store, noting that several states where other Sam’s Club stores 
operate liquor package stores require that sales not be restricted to members only.  Upon further 
inquiry, he confirmed that the Cheyenne store is the only one that restricts sales to Club members 
only pursuant to verbal agree ment with the governing body when the license application was 
reviewed.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body with the 
exception of Dr. Rinne voting “no”.
