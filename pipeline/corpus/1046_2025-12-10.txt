RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY 
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE 
Office of City Clerk 
 
December 10, 2025  
 
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in special session on this date for the 
purpose of a public hearing beginning at 6:00 p.m. in City Council Chambers  and via electronic 
conference meeting. Present were: MAYOR – Patrick Collins , COUNCIL MEMBERS – Dr. 
Michelle Aldrich (via Zoom, joining at 6:08 p.m.), Dr. Kathy Emmons, Ken Esquibel (via Zoom), 
Pete Laybourn, Mark Moody, Dr. Mark Rinne , Tom Segrave, Jeff White and Lawrence Wolfe . 
Also present: Kylie Soden, City Clerk. The pledge of allegiance was recited. 
 
REVIEW OF PROPOSED 6 TH PENNY PROJECTS. Mayor Collins advised the purpose 
of the meeting was to review the proposed 6th Penny projects and receive public comment. Chief 
of Staff Amber Ash provided a brief overview of the following proposed projects: Cheyenne Fire 
Rescue - Station 2 Replacement/Relocation, Station 6 Replacement/Relocation, Replacement of 3 
Fleet Apparatus & Loose Equipment -2 Type -1 Engines & 1 Straight Stick Ladder Truck and 
Miscellaneous Equipment; City Council - Downtown Improvement Projects, Reed Avenue 
Corridor and Remodel of Cheyenne Auxiliary Pumping Station; Community Recreation & Events 
- Greenway Maintenance and Johnson Pool Renovation; Engineer’s Office - Road Maintenance; 
Outside Entity – Airport and Arts & Aviation Center; Planning & Development - Greenway 
Reconstruction; Police Department - Digital Equipment and Software for Operations, Firearms 
Training Facility Improvements, Multipurpose Rescue and Response Vehicles and Multi -Bay 
Storage Building and Public Works - Addition & Remodel of Existing Municipal Building. Public 
comments were made by the following: Jim Ridgeway, Wendy Volk, Brian Bau, Dave Teubner, 
Sophia Maes, Downtown Development Authority Executive Director, Jack ie Suntrup, Carla 
Gregorio, Brennan Westby, Randy Byers, TDSi and Lee Filer. Comments were made by the 
following members of the governing body: Dr. Aldrich, Mayor Collins, Dr. Emmons, Mr. 
Laybourn, Mr. Moody, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Segrave, Mr. White and Mr. Wolfe.  
 
There being no further business to come before the governing body, the meeting was 
officially adjourned at 7:53 p.m. 
 
        Submitted by, 
 
              
        Kylie Soden, 
        City Clerk
