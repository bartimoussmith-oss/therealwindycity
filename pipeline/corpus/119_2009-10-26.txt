by substitute #2, seconded by Ms. Broyles.  City Attorney Dan White clarified the substitute provides

whether  temporary storage of landscaping materials in front of a residence could occur without having
to obtain a permit, and wording of current City Code prohibiting obstructions as reviewed earlier in
discussion by Mr. White.  Mr. Pierson moved to postpone for two weeks (to Nov. 9, 2009) to further
review ordinance wording to address storage of temporary landscaping materials, seconded by Mr.
Valdez.  Mr. Melko spoke in support of postponement and requested consideration be given to allow
storage of siding and roofing materials for construction jobs.  Comments followed on the intent of the
ordinance, which is to address storage of materials and containers in public rights-of-way; potential
cumbersome permit process affecting residents; dedicated streets that are not yet built; protecting the
usage of public rights-of-way for pedestrians, bicyclists, drivers, etc., and when a permit and fee may
not be required.  M otion to postpone carried.  V oting “yes” – all members of the governing body.
Mayor Kaysen advised the ordinance will be reviewed at the next Finance Committee meeting to be
held on Monday, Nov. 2, 2009 at 11:00 a.m. in Room 122 of the Municipal Building.
