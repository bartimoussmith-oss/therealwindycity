Mayor Spiker opened a public meeting to discuss the Cheyenne Transit Program’s boarding
transition to signed “Designated Boarding Areas,” amending the fixed routes of Cheyenne transit
buses, and recognizing the Five-Year Transportation Development Plan, as a flexible planning tool.
Joe Dougherty, Transit Director, provided a report on the updated routes and changes to the routes
involving a t ransition to designated boarding areas. He explained that currently Transit utilizes a
“wide open boarding” system which allows individuals to board the bus anywhere along the route that
is determined that safe boarding could be provided, and stated as the system has grown there have
become more and more demands for stops.  Mr. Dougherty reviewed a map of Transit service areas,
proposed changes, routes and number of designated boarding areas assigned to each route, and stated
the proposed changes are posted on the City’s website and in various community locations for the
public’s information.  H e noted the proposed changes are still o pen to r ecommendations. Upon
inquiry, Mr. Dougherty provided information on curb-to-curb service for individuals with special
needs, potential future pilot programs, number of diesel vs. gasoline buses in service, and ridership
levels and affiliated costs.  T here being no further comments or input and purpose of the public
meeting having been met, Mayor Spiker closed the public meeting.
