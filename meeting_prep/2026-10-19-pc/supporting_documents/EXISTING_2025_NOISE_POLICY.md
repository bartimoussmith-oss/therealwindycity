# Baseline being replaced: UDC Zoning Interpretation & Policy Statement, Data Center Sound Levels (August 20, 2025)

**Source PDF:** https://www.cheyennecity.org/files/sharedassets/public/v/2/departments/planningdevelopment/website-documents/zi-data-center-discernable-noise-v2.pdf
(Linked from the City's Unified Development Code page, under "Zoning Interpretations and Policies": https://www.cheyennecity.org/Your-Government/Departments/Planning-and-Development-Department/Unified-Development-Code)

This is the **current, already-in-effect interpretive policy** that PLTA-26-5 would formally codify into the UDC text — with lower (tighter) numbers, per staff's own account that the 2025 policy's limits are "likely too high."

---

Authorized by Charles Bloom, AICP, Planning and Development Director.

**Data Center definition (unchanged by PLTA-26-5):** "A use consisting of buildings or structures specifically designed or modified to house networked computers and data and transaction processing equipment and related infrastructure support equipment, including power and cooling equipment... A Data Center shall not include activities which consistently produce noise discernable at the property line. Should a Data Center produce noise discernable at the property line, it shall be classified as an Industrial use."

**Not considered "discernable noise at the property line" (2025 policy):**
- Sound (including data-center operation) discernable only at interior property lines between developments under common ownership or a common site plan / data-center development site.
- Emergency generator testing, provided it doesn't exceed 30 minutes/week (per single generator) — *(no time-of-day restriction in the 2025 version; PLTA-26-5 adds a 9am–4pm window)*.
- Emergency generator operation during a utility/energy-source outage, up to 144 hours (6 days) per event.

**2025 Sound Level Limits at Property Lines (the numbers being tightened):**

| Zone Adjacency | Daytime (7am–10pm) | Nighttime (10pm–7am) |
|---|---|---|
| Adjacent to Non-Residential Zone Districts (City: AG, CB, BP, P, LI, HI, non-res. PUDs; County: CB, LI, HI, P, MU, non-res. PUDs, undeveloped/ag LU tracts over 10 acres, utility/non-res. LU tracts) | ≤ 70 dBA | ≤ 65 dBA |
| Adjacent to All Other Zone Districts (i.e., residential) | ≤ 60 dBA | ≤ 55 dBA |

"For the purposes of this section, any property that is affected or potentially affected shall be considered adjacent."

**Methodology (2025 policy, largely carried forward into PLTA-26-5):**
- Sound studies submitted with site-plan applications to verify compliance.
- Measurements taken at 5 feet above ground, at or near the property line.
- Measurements reflect typical full-load conditions, including mechanical/HVAC equipment.
- Measurements reflect peak operating conditions: 85°F daytime ambient, 60°F nighttime ambient.

## Side-by-side: 2025 policy vs. PLTA-26-5 proposal

| | 2025 Policy (current) | PLTA-26-5 (proposed) |
|---|---|---|
| Non-residential property line, day | ≤ 70 dBA | ≤ 65 dBA |
| Non-residential property line, night | ≤ 65 dBA | ≤ 60 dBA |
| Residential property line, day | ≤ 60 dBA | ≤ 55 dBA |
| Residential property line, night | ≤ 55 dBA | **≤ 45 dBA** |
| Low-frequency (dBC) limit | none | dBC may not exceed dBA by more than 20 dB |
| Generator testing window | any time, ≤30 min/week/generator | **9am–4pm only**, ≤30 min/week/generator |
| Rooftop fans | not separately addressed | must be fully enclosed/acoustically attenuated |
| Cooling system type | not addressed in this memo (separate BOPU practice) | closed-loop required unless BOPU/Council grants exception |

Note: press coverage of the Oct. 5, 2026 meeting (Wyoming Tribune-Eagle/Laramie Boomerang) described the "before" non-residential numbers as "70 for both day and night" and the residential numbers as "60 and 55, respectively" — a slight simplification of the City's own 2025 policy document (which lists 70 day/65 night for non-residential). The residential figures match exactly (60/55 → 55/45). Use the City's own 2025 PDF (above) as the authoritative "before" baseline; the 45 dBA nighttime-residential headline number is confirmed verbatim in both the staff report and the Director's on-record quote.
