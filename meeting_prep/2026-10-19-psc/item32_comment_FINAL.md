# Item 32 — Gaming Center Special Use + Simulcasting Permit, 3307 Nationway
# ("Cadillac Ranch") — FINAL script (live-updated)

Madam Chair, members of the committee, Charles Miller for the record, item 32.

I'd like it noted that the applicant, Wyoming Downs, LLC, is not new to this
community. A Wyoming Downs off-track-betting operation has held a City liquor
license at 1345 Dell Range Avenue since at least 2022 — that's on the record
from this body's own October 2022 liquor license docket. This isn't a new
operator coming to town; it's an existing one expanding to a second site.

Staff just told this committee this property already received Board of
Adjustment conditional use approval on June 18th of this year, before the text
amendment that created the special use process for gaming centers even existed,
and that the applicant is coming in now — well ahead of the December 31st, 2027
deadline — to convert that into a special use approval rather than operate
under the old conditional use and come back later. Staff also described
conditional use and special use review criteria as "similar, but not
identical." I'd ask staff to state for the record exactly which criteria
differ between the two, since this applicant's original approval was reviewed
under the old standard, not the one in front of this committee tonight.

To the applicant's credit, staff also described a landscape buffer being added
along the south property line, facing the Medium-Density Residential parcels on
Belaire Avenue and 11th and 12th Streets, along with a revised parking layout,
added open space, and a tree lawn with street trees. I'd ask that those
specific mitigations be made conditions of approval in writing, not just
described verbally tonight, so they're enforceable regardless of what changes
in a future site plan.

On the numbers that are still unresolved: the site plan lists 124 "games," a
required-parking calculation of one stall per five seats yielding 25 stalls,
and 42 stalls provided — but games and seats aren't the same unit, and I'd ask
staff to clarify which was actually used to size that lot, and whether that
calculation was part of the original June 18th conditional use approval or is
new to this special use application. The facility is approved for 10 a.m. to
2 a.m. operation on a 0.8-acre site zoned CB, directly adjacent to that
residential block to the south. Staff's memo discloses 139 views and exactly
one public comment, "generally not in favor." Given the new buffer and
landscaping being added, I'd ask that the parking-math question get the same
level of attention before this resolution reaches final consideration.
