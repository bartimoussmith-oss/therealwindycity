# Item 34(f) — Grant of Easement, City of Cheyenne / Dept. of the Air Force,
# F.E. Warren AFB — Frontier Days Park-N-Ride — FINAL script (v2, live-updated

Charles Miller, for the record, item 34(f).

Engineer Cobb just told this committee this parking lot will have 2,868 stalls
— and that 39 of them are ADA stalls, which are concrete paved. I'd ask
directly: what are the other 2,829 stalls going to be surfaced with? If this
committee only needed to say the ADA spaces are paved, that tells me the rest
of this lot is not necessarily paved — and this committee passed an ordinance
earlier on this very agenda, item 19, that creates a brand-new exception for
exactly that kind of lot.

Item 19's "Low-Usage or Overflow Parking" exception applies to parking used
fewer than 55 times a year, and explicitly names overflow parking for
"Entertainment venues... and similar outdoor and assembly uses." Item 19 also
creates a new "Regional Stormwater Management" exception. A 2,868-stall lot
used roughly ten days a year for Frontier Days, sitting on the perimeter of an
Air Force base, is close to a textbook example of what those two exceptions
were written for. I'd ask staff directly, on the record: is this lot relying on
the exceptions this committee just approved in item 19, and if so, which
stalls are paved and which are gravel?

I'd also note the easement's own terms, which Engineer Cobb just read into the
record. The City takes this property "as is" and assumes responsibility for
insurance, compliance, and any required site restoration. In exchange, the Air
Force can restrict access or terminate this easement with 120 days' notice for
mission requirements or national defense needs. I'd ask this committee to
weigh that before final approval: the City is taking on full insurance and
restoration liability for nearly 2,900 parking stalls on federal land it could
lose access to on four months' notice.

Finally, the timing: this easement was approved as to form on October 7th —
five days before item 19 was even introduced to Council on October 12th. I'm
not suggesting one caused the other. I am asking whether these two items were
ever discussed together internally, because nothing in either packet says they
were, and the numbers staff just gave this committee make that question more
relevant, not less.
