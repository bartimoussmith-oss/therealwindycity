# Item 34(f) — Grant of Easement, City of Cheyenne / Dept. of the Air Force,
# F.E. Warren AFB — Frontier Days Park-N-Ride — FINAL script

Charles Miller, for the record, item 34(f).

This is a one-year Grant of Easement with F.E. Warren Air Force Base for a brand
new 36.5-acre Park-N-Ride on the old base golf course, accessed off Patriot
Drive at Gate 1. I'd ask the committee to look closely at the easement's own
Exhibit A, which states this is granted, quote, "exclusively for construction
activities in anticipation of and support for the 2027 Cheyenne Frontier Days
season." In other words, this committee isn't just approving a parking
easement tonight — it's approving the construction window for a parking lot
that will see heavy use roughly ten days a year, and sit empty the rest of the
time.

I'd ask the committee to connect that directly to item 19 tonight, the unpaved
parking ordinance this body already passed earlier on this same agenda. Item 19
creates a new "Low-Usage or Overflow Parking" exception for lots used fewer than
55 times a year, explicitly including, quote, "overflow parking for Civic Uses,
Entertainment venues... and similar outdoor and assembly uses." It also creates
a new "Regional Stormwater Management" exception. A 36.5-acre lot used roughly
ten days a year for Frontier Days is close to a textbook example of what that
exception was written for. I'd ask staff directly, on the record: is the
Frontier Days Park-N-Ride at F.E. Warren going to rely on the exceptions this
committee just approved in item 19, and if so, will it be paved or gravel?

I'd also note the timing. This easement was approved as to form on October 7th
— five days before item 19 was even introduced to Council on October 12th. I'm
not suggesting one caused the other. I am asking the committee to find out,
before a 36.5-acre lot gets built, whether these two items were ever discussed
together internally, because nothing in either item's packet says they were.

Finally, I'd note this easement runs exactly one year, November 1st, 2026 to
November 1st, 2027 — this is an annual-renewal instrument, not a standing
grant, and nothing in the nine pages I reviewed states whether this lot will
actually be paved or gravel. I'd ask that be put on the record before the City
commits Air Force land and City resources to building it.
