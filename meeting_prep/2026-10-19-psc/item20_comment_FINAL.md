# Item 20 — Municipal Code §6.08.020, livestock-keeping exceptions — FINAL script
# (as delivered live 10/19/2026 ~21:40 on the meeting clock)

Charles Miller, item 20.

For the record, staff's own memo on this item states that of the ten comments
received on the four-ordinance agriculture package, none address this specific
section — and the memo itself says the comment log includes, quote, "a list of
requests to amend other sections of 6.08, though none of the requested changes
are to 6.08.020." Staff doesn't name what that list is. I'd ask that it be
named, because the only Chapter 6.08 request I can find in tonight's packet is
the October 5th letter from WyFresh Farms' attorney, which asks the City to
define "livestock" under the Right to Farm Act, and to extend protection to
guardian dogs, terrier dogs for rodent control, and barn cats. That is a direct
request to amend Chapter 6.08, Animal Care and Control — the same chapter this
ordinance amends — yet it was logged under a different item's comment file
tonight, not this one.

This committee just spent several minutes on item 19 sorting out which document
a parking amendment actually belonged to. I'd ask that the same care be applied
here. If a livestock definition request got filed under the wrong item, the
record should say so, not just that "none of the requested changes are to
6.08.020."

On the substance: this ordinance is supposed to let livestock continue on
property that receives conditional use or site plan approval, and to protect
livestock operations already underway at the time of annexation. I'd ask the
committee to connect that directly to items 15 through 17 tonight — the Havel
Property annexation, which the staff report itself describes as currently "a
residence and agricultural" use. If this ordinance's annexation protection is
meant to cover exactly that kind of situation, I'd like it on the record whether
it actually applies to the parcel this committee is annexing tonight, or only to
future annexations going forward.

## Outcome (confirmed live)
Passed 2nd reading, motion by Dr. Emmons, second by Councilman LaBar, no
discussion, unanimous. Staff gave live detail not in the written memo: livestock
is clarified as allowed in AG, AR, and RR zone districts, plus in conjunction
with other permitted uses (stables, veterinary clinics); pre-existing grazing
lawful within the year prior to annexation is allowed to continue, per state
statute, which is relevant to the Havel Property question raised above.
