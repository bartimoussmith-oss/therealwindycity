## §1 — Headline Findings

1. **The 2025 press record is empty. Zero mentions.** Despite a founding event of record scale (the Sept. 20–21, 2025 pipeline rupture/fire — covered nationally by AP, CPR, US News, Denver7, Cowboy State Daily), and despite Miller's own 2025 activity (authorship self-disclosure May 2025; page founding), no 2025 press item names him. The scanner night produced 60-mile-visible coverage with no Miller in it. **The press discovers Charles Miller on Jan. 12, 2026 — through the warrants fight, the campaign the record says he won.** His press identity is born from the institution's overreach, not his own.
2. **Fifteen original mentions, three outlets, ~7 months.** Cap City News 7, Wyoming Tribune Eagle 5, Cowboy State Daily 3. No letters, no op-eds, no bylined interviews. Every single mention is the same genre: the public-comment quote.
3. **Total invisibility at the climax.** From July 13, 2026 forward — the ordinance votes, the referendum, the 3,275 signatures, the Aug. 10 declaratory-judgment action, the Secretary of State's intervention — **zero mentions.** The press narrates his campaign entirely through other people (Hasenauer, McAdams, Madrid, Moody, Gray). The man whose record-built machinery ran the summer is a ghost in the coverage of it.
4. **The descriptor arc** (the press minting him a public figure in stages): "Resident Charles Miller" (Jan.) → "Cheyenne resident Charles Miller" (Jun.) → **"a frequent critic of the city's planning process"** (WTE, Jun. 24) — by mid-summer the press no longer introduces him; it files him.
5. **External validation of the script factory (Vol. IV).** Every press-quoted line is a Miller podium line, and the four Cox-annexation quotes lifted by three outlets are verbatim matches to the Apr. 20 scripted-testimony format. The press index is a blind replication study of the scripting thesis: reporters selected exactly the scripted, quotable, laconic lines.
6. **The one meeting where they quoted everyone but him.** Apr. 27, 2026 GB — the Granicus record shows Miller speaking amid repeated points of order; Cap City's story that night quotes Woodhouse, O'Neal, Keslar, Isley, Emmons, Rinne, White — not Miller. The more procedural friction he generated, the less quotable the press found him.
7. **Fire-coverage contradiction locked.** All pipeline-fire reporting (AP: "no injuries or fatalities") contradicts the movement's "2 babies died / 7 elderly" amplification — now contradicted by contemporaneous national wire copy, not just the record's own audit. Chekhov's gun (Vol. VII narrative finding #4) is now loaded on the record side too.

## §2 — The Ledger: Every Original Mention, Chronological

### P-1 — Cap City News, Jan. 13, 2026 (meeting Jan. 12)
**"Cheyenne City Council advances controversial inspection warrant ordinance, plans to make amendments"** — Garrett Grochowski
> "Resident Charles Miller argued the measure is fundamentally flawed, stating it violates the law by placing warrant authority in the hands of a municipal judge rather than a district court judge. He cited Wyoming State Statute 35-7-1046 as the necessary standard, arguing that the city's proposal lacks the required 'pre-notice to the owner' and essentially facilitates secret searches for aesthetic code violations."

Maps to: Jan. 12 GB, warrant 2nd reading (7–3). Note: his statute cite (35-7-1046) printed unchallenged. Fellow commenters named: Gary Pugh, Caleb Wilkins. **His first-ever press mention.**

### P-2 — Cowboy State Daily, Jan. 27, 2026 (meeting Jan. 26)
**"Cheyenne Council Delays Expansion Of Inspection Warrants Amid 4th Amendment Fears"** — staff
> "Charles Miller, addressing the council remotely, said he was submitting an amendment. Similar to the amendment Patricia McCoy submitted, Miller said his amendment would move authority to a circuit court judge."

Maps to: Jan. 26 GB, postponement to Feb. 9 (unanimous). **First press corroboration of the record's warrant-war citizen cast: McCoy confirmed.** Also confirms Miller drafted and submitted formal ordinance amendments — legislating from the Zoom queue.

### P-3 — Cap City News, Feb. 10, 2026 (meeting Feb. 9)
**"Cheyenne City Council approves administrative warrant ordinance with scenario limits"** — Grochowski
Lede: *"In a battle over three words, 'verify' was the only survivor."*
> "'Verify' is a viable legal standard. It means you have evidence and are confirming it. 'Discover' is a constitutional non-starter,' said resident Charles Miller during public comment. 'It creates a general warrant, a fishing expedition authorized by the government to find a crime it doesn't know exists.' In response, Councilor Michelle Aldrich moved to strike the words 'determine' and 'discover' from the ordinance."

Maps to: Feb. 9 GB, third reading (7–3; Moody/Rinne/Esquibel no). **The press attribution of the record's central warrants win: his "verify/discover" distinction → Aldrich's strike motion, in consecutive sentences.** Vol. V's "warrants = his theater" finding is now newspaper-of-record fact.
